* **Study Design:** Single-center cross-sectional clinical metagenomic next-generation sequencing (mNGS) study of CT-guided percutaneous lung biopsy specimens from 130 patients presenting with undiagnosed pulmonary opacities: 50 lung cancer, 53 pulmonary infection, and 27 non-infectious benign pulmonary diseases.

## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study performs shotgun metagenomic sequencing (mNGS) on CT-guided percutaneous lung core biopsies from 130 patients categorized into lung cancer ($n = 50$), pulmonary infection ($n = 53$), and non-infectious benign pulmonary disease ($n = 27$). Using Kraken2 against a custom RefSeq database, the authors compare taxonomic relative abundances across groups, test associations with 10 clinical/pathological variables within the lung cancer cohort, and run univariate and multivariate binary logistic regressions on the 130 patients (50 lung cancer vs. 80 non-cancer) to identify "independent microbial risk factors" for lung cancer. They conclude that the phylum *Bacillariophyta*, the order *Alteromonadales*, and the genus *Shewanella* (alongside patient age $>65$) are independent risk factors for lung cancer development.

**Editorial Verdict: Reject (Fundamentally Invalid).** The study's microbial profiles and "independent lung cancer risk factors" are catastrophic artifacts of unblunted low-biomass reagent contamination ("kitome"), uncurated Kraken2 RefSeq database false positives, and a flawed logistic regression control group design: (1) percutaneous lung biopsies were subjected to multi-step enzymatic/bead host depletion (saponin, BGI host-removal mix, TIANGEN Lyticase, glass beads, magnetic beads) with **zero negative extraction or reagent blank controls**, yielding profiles where the #1 bacterial genus across all lung biopsies is the marine metal-reducing bacterium *Shewanella* and other top/differential taxa are hot-spring hyperthermophiles (*Aquificales*, *Hydrogenobacter*) and extreme acidophilic mine-drainage bacteria (*Acidibacillus*); (2) uncurated Kraken2 RefSeq k-mer matching misidentifies eukaryotic host/contaminant reads as **photosynthetic marine diatoms (*Bacillariophyta*, misclassified in Results line 123 as a bacterial phylum and declared the sole phylum-level "independent risk factor for lung cancer"!)**, cellular slime molds (*Heterostelium*, *Acytostelium*), agricultural plant rust/blast fungi (*Melampsora*, *Pyricularia*, *Botrytis*), arbuscular mycorrhizal soil root fungi (*Rhizophagus*), and tick/tsetse-borne protozoan parasites (*Theileria*, *Trypanosoma*); and (3) the logistic regression pools 53 acute pulmonary infection patients with 27 non-infectious patients as the 80-patient "non-lung cancer" reference group, so that high pathogen loads in the 53 infected lungs dilute background reagent kitome fractions, artificially making reagent/database contaminants (*Shewanella*, *Alteromonadales*, *Bacillariophyta*) appear "enriched" in low-biomass lung cancer biopsies.

### Specific Conclusions and Assessment of Support

* **Microbial alpha diversity is similar between lung cancer ($n=50$) and non-infectious benign lung disease ($n=27$), whereas beta diversity separates acute pulmonary infections ($n=53$) from both groups: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Acute bacterial/fungal pneumonia biopsies contain high biomass of true respiratory pathogens, naturally separating them in Bray-Curtis PCoA (Fig. 1G–H) from low-biomass tumor and non-infectious interstitial lung biopsies, whose mNGS reads are dominated by shared background reagent DNA.
* **Lung cancer biopsies are specifically enriched for *Acidibacillus*, *Aquificales*, *Hydrogenobacter*, *Rhizophagus*, *Dothidotthia*, *Theileria*, *Trypanosoma*, and *Heterostelium* across clinical and molecular subgroups: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  These organisms are biologically impossible residents of human lung tumors: *Aquificales* and *Hydrogenobacter* are obligate hyperthermophiles growing at $70\text{--}85^\circ\text{C}$ in volcanic hot springs; *Acidibacillus* is an extreme acidophile ($p\text{H } 1.5\text{--}3.0$) from acid mine drainage; *Rhizophagus* is an obligate plant-root mycorrhizal fungus; *Melampsora*, *Pyricularia*, and *Botrytis* are plant-pathogenic fungi; *Heterostelium* and *Acytostelium* are soil dictyostelid slime molds; and *Theileria* and *Trypanosoma* are vector-borne blood parasites. Their detection in Kraken2 is a well-known artifact of human repeat/low-complexity DNA fragments matching contaminated draft eukaryotic/microbial RefSeq genomes after incomplete host depletion, compounded by conducting dozens of unadjusted Wilcoxon/Kruskal-Wallis tests across 10 clinical variables without FDR correction.
* **Multivariate binary logistic regression proves that the phylum *Bacillariophyta*, order *Alteromonadales*, and genus *Shewanella* are independent risk factors for lung cancer development: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  First, *Bacillariophyta* are **diatoms** (unicellular photosynthetic stramenopile algae with silica frustules), not bacteria, yet Results (line 123, Fig. 4A–B) treats *Bacillariophyta* as a bacterial phylum alongside *Firmicutes* and declares it the sole phylum-level independent risk factor for human lung cancer. Second, *Shewanella* (*Alteromonadales*) is an environmental aquatic/marine bacterium and a documented reagent contaminant in commercial DNA extraction kits and lyticase preparations. Third, although the Abstract (line 5) states that the 27 non-infectious patients served as the primary control, the binary logistic regression in Section "Potential microbial risk factors for lung cancer" (line 123) compares 50 lung cancer cases against all 80 non-cancer cases (53 of whom have acute pulmonary infections where high pathogen read counts suppress the relative percentage of kitome/diatom background reads).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 1. Biologically Impossible Taxa (Diatoms, Slime Molds, Plant Rusts, Hot-Spring Hyperthermophiles) from Uncurated Kraken2 RefSeq Matching and Missing Reagent Blanks (Pillars 2, 5 & 6)
* **WHY:** Saponin host-depletion followed by enzymatic lysis (`Lyticase RT410-TA`, which is fungal-derived and frequently carries environmental microbial DNA) leaves residual human pericentromeric/simple-repeat reads and reagent kitome DNA. When classified with Kraken2 using default confidence (`0.0`) against an uncleaned RefSeq database containing draft eukaryotic/protozoan/plant-fungal assemblies contaminated with human k-mers, residual human reads are misassigned to *Bacillariophyta* (diatoms), *Theileria*, *Trypanosoma*, *Toxoplasma*, *Heterostelium*/*Acytostelium* (slime molds), and plant fungi (*Pyricularia*, *Melampsora*, *Botrytis*, *Rhizophagus*), while kit reagents contribute *Shewanella*, *Burkholderia*, *Acidibacillus*, *Herbaspirillum*, *Massilia*, *Stenotrophomonas*, *Cupriavidus*, *Variovorax*, and *Achromobacter*.
* **WHAT:** Retract the claims that *Bacillariophyta*, *Shewanella*, *Alteromonadales*, slime molds, plant fungi, and protozoan parasites are lung cancer risk factors or clinical correlates, and re-run taxonomic profiling with stringent host-read removal and false-positive filtering.
* **HOW:** (1) Deplete human reads against both GRCh38 and the telomere-to-telomere T2T-CHM13 reference (using `Bowtie2 --very-sensitive-local` + `Kraken2` human masking); (2) remove non-human photosynthetic algae (*Bacillariophyta*), plant pathogens, slime molds, and vector-borne protozoa from the custom Kraken2 database; (3) apply a Kraken2 confidence threshold ($\ge 0.2$) and verify putative hits (*Shewanella*, etc.) by mapping reads directly to reference genomes to check breadth of genome coverage (distinguishing uniform genome coverage of real bacteria from stacked k-mer artifacts).

### 2. Confounded Control Group Pooling ($n=53$ Acute Infections + $n=27$ Non-Infectious) and Uncorrected Multiple Testing in Logistic Regression (Pillars 3, 4 & 5)
* **WHY:** In the Abstract (line 5) and Methods (line 28), the authors state that *"The non-infectious group served as the primary control."* However, in Section "Potential microbial risk factors for lung cancer" (line 123), the binary logistic regression pools all 80 non-cancer patients (53 acute pulmonary infections + 27 non-infectious diseases) into one control group against the 50 lung cancer patients. Because compositional relative abundances sum to 100%, the massive pathogen load in the 53 pulmonary infection samples depresses the relative percentage of background reagent contaminants in the pooled 80-patient control group, creating a spurious compositional "enrichment" of reagent contaminants in the 50 low-biomass lung cancer samples. In addition, screening hundreds of taxa across 6 taxonomic ranks and 10 clinical subgroups (Figs. 2–4) without Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction guarantees dozens of false-positive associations.
* **WHAT:** Exclude the 53 acute pulmonary infection patients when modeling baseline microbial differences between lung cancer ($n=50$) and non-infectious benign lung disease ($n=27$), and apply FDR multiple-testing correction across all tested taxa.
* **HOW:** Re-run the differential abundance and logistic regression analyses strictly comparing lung cancer ($n=50$) vs. non-infectious benign disease ($n=27$) adjusting for age, sex, smoking, and COPD (note in Table 1 that 7/50 [14%] of lung cancer patients had COPD vs. 0/80 in controls, and 8/27 [29.6%] of non-infectious controls had autoimmune disease), and report Benjamini-Hochberg FDR $q$-values for all comparisons.

### 3. Pervasive Numerical, Statistical Notation, and Typographical Errors (Pillar 7)
* **WHY:**
  1. In Results (line 89), the Simpson diversity p-value is printed as `simpson' diversity (p = 284)` instead of `p = 0.284`, and Shannon is misspelled as `shannan's diversity`.
  2. In the legends for Figures 1, 2, and 3 (lines 92, 102, 114), non-significance is repeatedly written as `ns > 0.5` instead of `p > 0.05`, and test names are misspelled as `Wilcoxun rank sum test` and `Kruskal-Wallis tes`.
  3. Taxonomic and clinical names are repeatedly misspelled: `Fimicutes` and `Pseudomonasales` (line 99), `Shewanellla` (lines 111, 137), `Clostridiodes` (line 116), `Hypertention` and `Coronory heart disease` (Table 1, lines 80–81).
* **WHAT & HOW:** Correct `p = 284` to `p = 0.284`, change `ns > 0.5` to `ns, p > 0.05` in all figure legends, fix all taxonomic and clinical spelling errors, and deposit the human-depleted microbial read files or Kraken2 abundance matrices in a public repository.
