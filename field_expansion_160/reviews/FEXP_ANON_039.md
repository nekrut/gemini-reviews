## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study re-analyzes 65 public RNA-seq libraries from two NCBI BioProjects (`PRJNA1067435` and `PRJNA464380`) spanning developmental stages and adult/larval tissues of the Colorado potato beetle (*Leptinotarsa decemlineata*). Using ComBat-seq, DESeq2, WGCNA, and the $\tau$ specificity index, the authors catalog differentially expressed genes across molting transitions, identify stage-specific (1,969 embryonic; 1,647 adult) and tissue-specific (1,182 testis) transcripts, annotate 78 cytochrome P450 genes across 18 chromosomes, run HyPhy aBSREL positive selection tests on P450s, and call SNPs and InDels from the RNA-seq alignments via GATK HaplotypeCaller. They conclude that five P450 genes evolve under intense positive selection (reporting maximum $d_N/d_S$ values up to $100,000$ and $24,440$ with $p = 0$) and that developmental-stage and tissue differences in RNA-seq SNP/InDel counts reflect "reduced selective constraints during early development" and "stronger selective constraints on transcript sequence integrity in terminally differentiated tissues" such as hemolymph.

**Editorial Verdict: Reject (Flawed / Overstated).** While the standard differential expression, $\tau$-index tissue specificity, and 78-gene P450 genomic catalog are serviceable descriptive summaries of public data, the manuscript's two signature evolutionary and mutational analyses are methodologically and biologically invalid: (1) calling SNPs and InDels from unpaired RNA-seq libraries pooled across two independent BioProjects (different beetle colonies/strains and sequencing depths) without matched genomic DNA or splice-junction cigar splitting (`SplitNCigarReads`), and interpreting higher SNP/InDel counts in egg/midgut RNA-seq vs. hemolymph RNA-seq as *stage- and tissue-specific natural selection on transcript sequence fidelity*, violates basic genetics (an organism's genome does not evolve via purifying selection between its egg and adult stages or between its midgut and hemolymph; rather, RNA-seq variant counts with a `DP >= 10` cutoff simply track gene expression breadth/depth and inter-colony strain divergence); and (2) the HyPhy `aBSREL` analysis reports numerical boundary artifacts ($d_N/d_S = 100,000$ and $24,440$ caused by $d_S \to 0$ on saturated paralog branches) without specifying what ortholog/paralog alignments were tested, and contradicts its own $p < 0.01$ significance threshold by claiming positive selection for `CYP9Z26` at $p = 0.03$.

### Specific Conclusions and Assessment of Support

* **Major transcriptional reprogramming occurs at the egg-to-L1 and L3-to-adult transitions, and testis harbors the largest repertoire of tissue-specific genes ($n=1,182$, $\tau \ge 0.9$): [Well Supported].**  
  Standard alignment (STAR/featureCounts), DESeq2 analysis, and $\tau$ specificity scoring across the 65 public libraries recover biologically expected patterns (chitin cuticle and ecdysteroid pathways at metamorphosis; microtubule/spermatogenesis enrichment in testis).
* **The *L. decemlineata* reference genome encodes 78 cytochrome P450 genes across four clans (Clan 2, Clan 3, Clan 4, Mitochondrial) arranged in chromosomal tandem clusters with distinct tissue expression profiles: [Well Supported].**  
  DIAMOND + Pfam `PF00067` HMMER screening and chromosomal mapping on `GCA_024712935.1` provide a clean, reproducible structural catalog of the 78 P450 loci and their expression across midgut, fat body, and Malpighian tubules.
* **Five P450 genes (`CYP15C1`, `CYP6BQ11`, `CYP6P4`, `CYP4BN6`, `CYP9Z26`) underwent intense adaptive positive selection with maximum $d_N/d_S$ up to $100,000$ ($p = 0$): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  First, Materials and Methods (Section 2.6) never specifies what sequences were aligned to test each P450 gene (whether `aBSREL` was run on the 78 deeply diverged *L. decemlineata* paralogs where synonymous substitutions are saturated, or on unmentioned multi-species coleopteran orthologs). Second, $d_N/d_S = 100,000$ (`CYP6BQ11`) and $24,440$ (`CYP15C1`) are optimization ceiling artifacts in HyPhy when branch synonymous rate $d_S \approx 0$, not real biological selection coefficients. Third, `CYP9Z26` ($p = 0.03$, line 130) fails the manuscript's own Holm-Bonferroni threshold of $p < 0.01$ (line 61).
* **Higher SNP and InDel counts in egg, midgut, and gonad RNA-seq compared to hemolymph reflect stage- and tissue-specific natural selection on transcript sequence fidelity: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Purifying selection acts on heritable germline variation across generations, not within an individual's soma across developmental stages or tissues. Because GATK variant calling was run on RNA-seq with a hard read-depth filter (`DP >= 10`, line 66) and `vcf-tools --max-missing 0.95` across two distinct BioProjects (`PRJNA1067435` vs. `PRJNA464380`), tissues/stages expressing more genes above `DP >= 10` (such as transcriptionally complex eggs, testes, and midguts) or derived from a more divergent laboratory strain automatically yield more called variants than low-complexity hemolymph. Moreover, Section 2.7 omits `GATK SplitNCigarReads` (mandatory for RNA-seq to prevent false-positive InDels/SNPs at spliced exon junctions) and claims that standard hard-coded GATK default thresholds (`QD >= 2.0, FS <= 60.0, MQ >= 40.0, GQ >= 20`) were "established through ROC curve analysis" despite having no ground-truth validation truth set.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 1. Confounded RNA-Seq Variant Calling and Biologically Invalid Interpretation of Stage/Tissue SNP Densities (Pillars 2, 3, 4 & 6)
* **WHY:** In Sections 2.7, 3.4, 3.8, and 4.4, the authors call SNPs and InDels from 65 RNA-seq samples spanning two independent BioProjects (`PRJNA1067435` and `PRJNA464380`) using `GATK HaplotypeCaller` with `DP >= 10`, without matched genomic DNA and without `SplitNCigarReads`. In RNA-seq, variant detection power is strictly confounded by transcript expression level: a gene expressed at $\text{DP} < 10$ in hemolymph but $\text{DP} = 50$ in egg or midgut will have its standing germline SNPs called in egg/midgut and missed in hemolymph. Interpreting this coverage/strain ascertainment bias as *"reduced selective constraints during early development"* (line 178) and *"stronger selective constraints on transcript sequence integrity in terminally differentiated tissues"* (line 111) is biologically untenable. Furthermore, claiming in Section 2.7 (line 68) that the exact GATK Best Practices hard-filter defaults (`QD >= 2.0, FS <= 60.0, MQ >= 40.0, GQ >= 20`) were derived via "ROC curve analysis" is invalid without a ground-truth whole-genome callset.
* **WHAT:** Retract the claim that developmental stages and somatic tissues experience differential purifying selection on mRNA sequence fidelity, remove the unsupported "ROC curve analysis" claim in Section 2.7, and control for read depth and BioProject strain origin.
* **HOW:** Re-run the RNA-seq variant pipeline using `GATK SplitNCigarReads` prior to `HaplotypeCaller`, restrict tissue/stage comparisons to a single BioProject/strain and only to ubiquitously expressed housekeeping sites where callable read depth is $\text{DP} \ge 20$ in *every* compared tissue, and normalize variant counts by the total number of callable bases ($\text{DP} \ge 20$) per sample.

### 2. Unspecified Input Alignments, Numerical Boundary Artifacts ($\omega = 100,000$), and Threshold Contradiction in `aBSREL` Analysis (Pillars 3, 4 & 7)
* **WHY:** Section 2.6 states that `aBSREL` was run across all branches of the phylogenetic tree, and Section 3.7 reports positive selection on five individual *L. decemlineata* genes (`CYP15C1`, `CYP6BQ11`, `CYP6P4`, `CYP4BN6`, `CYP9Z26`) with maximum $d_N/d_S$ values of $100,000$ and $24,440$ ($p = 0$). If `aBSREL` was run on the 78-gene intraspecific paralog tree from Section 2.5, deep inter-clan divergence saturates synonymous sites ($d_S$) and misalignment of indel-rich P450 substrate-recognition regions inflates $d_N$, driving the $\omega_k$ rate class to HyPhy's numerical upper bound ($10^5$ when local branch $d_S = 0$). Conversely, if inter-species coleopteran orthologs were used for each of the 5 genes, those species and accessions are completely omitted from Materials and Methods. In addition, Section 2.6 (line 61) defines significance as Holm-Bonferroni corrected $p < 0.01$, whereas Section 3.7 (line 130) includes `CYP9Z26` at $p = 0.03$.
* **WHAT:** Specify the exact taxon sampling and alignments used for `aBSREL`, do not report numerical division-by-zero ceiling parameters ($\omega = 100,000$) as biological $d_N/d_S$ ratios, and enforce a consistent $p$-value threshold.
* **HOW:** Restrict codon selection tests (`aBSREL` or `BUSTED`/`MEME`) to closely related coleopteran orthogroups (filtering out branches with $d_S > 1.0$ or $d_S = 0$), report the branch length, synonymous rate $d_S$, and proportion of sites $p_k$ alongside $\omega_k$, and exclude `CYP9Z26` ($p = 0.03$) under the stated $p < 0.01$ criterion.

### 3. Batch-Correction Pipeline Ordering and Software Version Discrepancies (Pillars 3 & 7)
* **WHY:** In Section 2.2 (lines 33–35), the authors apply `ComBat-seq` to adjust counts across the two BioProjects (`PRJNA1067435` and `PRJNA464380`) and then run stage-by-stage and tissue-by-tissue DESeq2 comparisons. If developmental stages come predominantly from one BioProject and adult tissues from the other, batch and biological condition are colinear, making `ComBat-seq` adjustment either over-correct biological signal or retain batch differences. Also, Section 2.2 lists Bioconductor 3.13 packages (`DESeq2 v1.32.0`, `sva v3.40.0` from 2021) while Section 2.4 lists `R v4.4.3`.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Clarify in Section 2.1/2.2 which stages and tissues belong to `PRJNA1067435` versus `PRJNA464380`, ensure adjacent developmental stage DEGs (Egg $\to$ L1 $\to$ L2 $\to$ L3 $\to$ Adult) are computed exclusively within the same BioProject, and verify exact R/Bioconductor package versions.
