## 1. Blinded Metadata Perception

* **Perceived Role / Domain:** Integrative single-cell and bulk transcriptomic prognostic modeling study of M2 tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) in head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC), with mouse xenograft and clinical IHC validation.
* **Blinded Document Assessment:** This manuscript exhibits severe hallmarks of template assembly, mathematical impossibility, and fabricated/corrupted methodological text, including a placeholder PubMed ID (`PMID: 22000000`) and non-existent R package (`Predict v1.2.3`) for an unexecuted drug-sensitivity analysis, two distinct genes (`NCF1` and `CLECL1`) assigned identical 9-decimal-place LASSO coefficients (`-0.215696193`), clinical IHC signature scores in Table 4 (`-35.11` to `+21.95`) that are mathematically impossible given the `0–4` IHC scoring scale and Table 2 weights, biologically implausible/lethal mouse dosing (`20 mg/kg` recombinant IL-4 and `20 mg/kg` LPS $\times 3$), and cross-cancer dataset confusion (nasopharyngeal carcinoma `GSE150430` and cutaneous carcinoma `GSE123813` treated as HNSCC).

## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

The authors integrate scRNA-seq (`GSE150430`, 15 tumors; `GSE123813`, 4 tumors) and bulk transcriptomics (`TCGA-HNSCC`, $n = 494$; `GSE65858`, $n = 270$) to construct an 11-gene M2 TAM-related prognostic signature (`FCGBP`, `GIMAP5`, `WIPF1`, `RASGEF1B`, `GIMAP7`, `IGFLR1`, `GPR35`, `NCF1`, `CLECL1`, `HEXB`, `IL10`) for HNSCC. They report that xCell-estimated M2 macrophage abundance correlates with worse survival in TCGA, that recombinant IL-4 (`20 mg/kg`) promotes and LPS (`20 mg/kg`) suppresses `SCC9` xenograft growth in BALB/c nude mice ($n = 5$/group), and that the 11-gene risk score stratifies survival in `TCGA-HNSCC` and `GSE65858` as well as clinical stage in 20 institutional HNSCC patients assayed by immunohistochemistry (IHC).

**Editorial Verdict: Reject (Fundamentally Invalid).** Forensic inspection uncovers multiple fatal mathematical, biological, and data-integrity failures:
1. **Mathematically Impossible Clinical IHC Risk Scores (Table 2 vs. Table 4) and Duplicate 9-Digit LASSO Weights:** In Table 2, `NCF1` and `CLECL1` share the exact same 9-decimal-place LASSO coefficient (`-0.215696193`). In the clinical validation (lines 74, 181, Table 4), 20 HNSCC tumors were stained by IHC (where `CLECL1` is listed twice with two different antibodies and `IGFLR1` is omitted entirely) and scored on a `0–4` scale. Given the Table 2 coefficients (sum of negative weights $= -1.2794$; sum of positive weights $= +0.6477$), any linear combination of `0–4` IHC scores is strictly bounded within $[-5.12, +2.59]$. Yet Table 4 reports patient signature scores ranging from **`-35.10636046`** to **`+21.94864881`**, proving that the numbers in Table 4 could not have been generated by the stated IHC protocol and model formula.
2. **Placeholder Citation (`PMID: 22000000`) and Phantom Method Section:** Methods line 59 describes estimating IC50 values using *"the Phenotype algorithm implemented in the R package Predict (v1.2.3), with drug sensitivity data sourced from the Genomics of Drug Sensitivity in Cancer (GDSC) database (version 2.0; PMID: 22000000)."* `PMID: 22000000` is a fabricated round placeholder number, no R package named `Predict (v1.2.3)` implementing a "Phenotype algorithm" exists (a garbled synonymization of `pRRophetic` / `oncoPredict`), and no IC50 results appear anywhere in the Results or Figures.
3. **Biologically Implausible Mouse Dosing (`20 mg/kg` Recombinant IL-4 and `20 mg/kg` LPS):** Injecting mice three times at 48-hour intervals with `20 mg/kg` of recombinant IL-4 ($400\,\mu\text{g}$ pure cytokine per 20 g mouse per dose) is ~1,000–10,000$\times$ above standard in vivo cytokine dosing, while `20 mg/kg` LPS repeated three times is a lethal endotoxic shock dose in mice.
4. **Cross-Cancer Dataset Confusion and Sign Contradiction:** `GSE150430` is an EBV-associated nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC) dataset and `GSE123813` is a cutaneous basal/squamous cell carcinoma dataset, yet both are used as HNSCC single-cell cohorts; meanwhile, Discussion line 211 falsely states that `GSE65858` (the 270-sample HNSCC Illumina microarray cohort) is a *"single-cell database... derived from nasopharyngeal carcinoma."* Furthermore, 9 of the 11 "M2 TAM" genes—including canonical M2 marker `IL10` (`-0.2308`)—have negative risk coefficients, meaning higher expression predicts *lower* risk, directly contradicting the claim that M2 TAMs drive high risk.

### Specific Conclusions and Assessment of Support

* **In vivo validation that IL-4-induced M2 TAMs promote and LPS-induced M1 TAMs inhibit HNSCC xenograft growth (Figure 1E–I): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The reported doses of `20 mg/kg` recombinant IL-4 and `20 mg/kg` LPS administered three times every 48 hours (line 79) are biologically and pharmacologically implausible. Moreover, the methods state that all 20 mice were inoculated with `SCC9` cells and randomized into four groups including a "blank" group and an "SCC+Normal Saline" group, both of which have tumor weights and volumes plotted in Figure 1F–G without explaining what "blank" represents.
* **Identification of 1,208 M2 TAMs / signature genes from HNSCC scRNA-seq (`GSE150430`): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  First, the Abstract (line 5) and Discussion (line 209) state that *"1,208 M2 TAMs"* (cells) were integrated, whereas the Results (line 102) and Figure 3A state that *"1,208 TAMs-specific signature genes"* were identified from 46,001 cells. Second, `GSE150430` is nasopharyngeal carcinoma, not HNSCC, and DEGs were called with an unadjusted $P < 0.05$ cutoff (line 42).
* **11-gene M2 TAM prognostic signature (`FCGBP`, `GIMAP5`, `WIPF1`, `RASGEF1B`, `GIMAP7`, `IGFLR1`, `GPR35`, `NCF1`, `CLECL1`, `HEXB`, `IL10`) predicts poor survival in HNSCC: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In Table 2, `NCF1` and `CLECL1` have identical 9-decimal-place coefficients (`-0.215696193`), `IGFLR1` is truncated to `IGFLR`, and 9 of the 11 genes (including `IL10`) have negative coefficients (protective), contradicting the premise that M2 TAM gene expression increases risk.
* **Clinical IHC validation of the 11-gene signature in 20 HNSCC patients (Tables 3–4, Figure 7): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The IHC antibody list (line 74) omits `IGFLR1` and duplicates `CLECL1`, the signature scores in Table 4 (`-35.11` to `+21.95`) cannot be produced by multiplying `0–4` IHC scores by the Table 2 coefficients, and no survival follow-up is presented for the 20 patients (only cross-sectional age/stage/invasion comparisons, where line 200 misleadingly refers to the 20 clinical patients as *"patients in the training set"*).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

1. **Mathematically Impossible Clinical IHC Risk Scores in Table 4 and Duplicate 9-Decimal Coefficients in Table 2 (Pillar 3, Pillar 4 & Pillar 7):**
   * **Location:** Materials and methods ("Immunohistochemistry", line 74), Table 2 (lines 118–131), and Table 4 (lines 187–199).
   * **Defect:** Table 2 lists identical 9-decimal-place coefficients (`-0.215696193`) for both `NCF1` and `CLECL1`. In the clinical cohort ($n = 20$), each gene's IHC staining was scored from `0` to `4` (line 74). Multiplying scores $\in [0, 4]$ by Table 2 coefficients yields a mathematical range of $[-5.1175, +2.5908]$, yet Table 4 reports values from `-35.10636046` to `21.94864881`. In addition, line 74 lists two different antibodies for `CLECL1` (`Proteintech 1:500` and `AtaGenix 1:800`) and no antibody for `IGFLR1`.
2. **Fabricated Placeholder `PMID: 22000000` and Garbled Software Name for an Unexecuted Analysis (Pillar 2 & Pillar 7):**
   * **Location:** Materials and methods ("Predicting drug sensitivity", line 59).
   * **Defect:** Line 59 cites `PMID: 22000000` (an exact round number unrelated to GDSC) and claims to use *"the Phenotype algorithm implemented in the R package Predict (v1.2.3)"* (an automated word-substitution corruption of `pRRophetic` / `oncoPredict`). No drug sensitivity or IC50 analysis appears anywhere in the Results.
3. **Biologically Implausible / Lethal Mouse Dosing (`20 mg/kg` LPS and `20 mg/kg` IL-4) (Pillar 6):**
   * **Location:** Materials and methods ("Animal experiments", line 79) and Figure 1E–I.
   * **Defect:** Administering `20 mg/kg` of LPS three times at 48-hour intervals exceeds the murine lethal endotoxic dose, and administering `20 mg/kg` of recombinant IL-4 ($400\,\mu\text{g}$/mouse per injection) is orders of magnitude above biologically realistic cytokine dosing.
4. **Gross Confusion of Datasets, Sample Sizes, and Cells vs. Genes (Pillar 2 & Pillar 7):**
   * **Location:** Abstract (line 5), Table 1 (line 34), Methods (lines 29, 54), Results (line 102), and Discussion (lines 209, 211).
   * **Defect:**
     * `GSE65858` is described as `"bulk RNA-seq"` in line 29 and as a `"single-cell database... derived from nasopharyngeal carcinoma"` in line 211; in reality, `GSE65858` is an Illumina microarray dataset of 270 HNSCC tumors, whereas `GSE150430` is the single-cell nasopharyngeal carcinoma dataset.
     * `GSE123813` (cutaneous basal/squamous cell carcinoma anti-PD-1 scRNA-seq) is used in Figure 6D–E without disclosing that the patients had skin cancer rather than HNSCC.
     * Line 54 states that *"fifteen samples were classified into high-risk group and low risk group and survival curve were visualized using the Kaplan-Meier method,"* conflating the 15 scRNA-seq samples (`GSE150430`) with the 494 `TCGA-HNSCC` survival cohort.
     * Line 5 and line 209 refer to *"1,208 M2 TAMs"* (cells), whereas line 102 and Figure 3A refer to *"1,208 TAMs-specific signature genes."*
5. **Erroneous Subgroup Interpretation in Results Line 147 (Pillar 4 & Pillar 7):**
   * **Location:** Results ("Association analysis of prognostic signature with clinical and pathologic features", line 147, Figure 5I–P).
   * **Defect:** Both "without PNI" and "without LVI" are cited as `(Figure 5K)`, while `(Figure 5L)` is described as "female" in line 147 despite the Figure 5 caption stating `Sex (J, N), LVI (K, O) and PNI (L, P)`.

### 5. Constructive and Doable Suggestions for Improvement

1. **Audit Raw LASSO Output and Table 4 Calculations:** Provide the exact R script and input matrices used to fit the `glmnet` Cox model, explain why `NCF1` and `CLECL1` received identical 9-decimal coefficients, and show the exact mathematical formula that produced the values from `-35.11` to `+21.95` in Table 4 from `0–4` IHC scores.
2. **Verify In Vivo Dosing Records and Raw Animal Data:** Check laboratory notebooks for the actual concentrations and volumes of LPS and recombinant IL-4 administered to the mice (e.g., whether doses were in $\mu\text{g/kg}$ rather than $\text{mg/kg}$) and clarify the difference between the "blank" and "SCC9+NS" tumor-bearing groups.
3. **Remove Non-HNSCC Single-Cell Datasets or Explicitly Justify Cross-Cancer Transfer:** Replace the nasopharyngeal carcinoma (`GSE150430`) and cutaneous carcinoma (`GSE123813`) scRNA-seq datasets with authentic HNSCC scRNA-seq cohorts (e.g., `GSE103322` or `GSE139324`), and correct the description of `GSE65858` throughout the text.
4. **Remove the Phantom Drug Sensitivity Section and Placeholder Citation:** Delete the unexecuted "Predicting drug sensitivity" paragraph (and fabricated `PMID: 22000000`) from the Methods.
