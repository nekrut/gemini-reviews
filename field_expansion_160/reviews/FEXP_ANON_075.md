## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript performs a bidirectional two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR) screen between 731 circulating immune cell phenotypes (Orrù et al.) and breast cancer (`ukb-b-16890`, $10,303$ cases and $452,630$ controls in the UK Biobank), using genetic instruments selected at $P < 1 \times 10^{-5}$ ($r^2 < 0.1$ within $500\text{ kb}$) and declaring both Weighted Median (WM) and random-effects Inverse Variance Weighted (IVW) as co-primary estimators at an uncorrected nominal $P < 0.05$ threshold. The authors claim that 27 immune cell traits causally influence breast cancer onset (with Odds Ratios such as $\text{OR} = 1.002$ and $\text{OR} = 0.999$) and that breast cancer onset causally alters 17 immune cell populations (with reported Odds Ratios of $\text{OR} = 4,328.998$ [95% CI: $11.80\text{--}1,588,733.71$] and $\text{OR} = 7,345.265$ [95% CI: $13.51\text{--}3,994,622.93$]).

**Editorial Verdict: Reject (Fundamentally Invalid).** A forensic audit reveals catastrophic statistical, mathematical, and biological failures that render every number and conclusion in this paper invalid:
1. **Unmistakable AI/Automated Abbreviation Hallucination ("University of New South Wales Memory T Cells"):** In the Figure 1 caption (Lines 55 and 58), the authors define the standard B-cell flow cytometry phenotype `IgD on unsw mem` (IgD expression on **unswitched memory B cells**) as: *"IgD on unsw mem refers to the expression of the IgD molecule on **University of New South Wales Memory T Cells (unsw mem)**"*! T cells do not express surface IgD, and expanding `unsw` (unswitched) to `"University of New South Wales"` is an egregious hallucination.
2. **Catastrophic Scale Failure (`ukb-b-16890` Linear Probability Model Treated as Log-Odds in Both Directions, Producing $\text{OR} = 4,328.998$ and $\text{OR} = 7,345.265$):** The outcome dataset `ukb-b-16890` was generated by `BOLT-LMM` on the raw $0/1$ binary scale (case prevalence $\mu = 10,303 / 462,933 = 2.23\%$), where linear SNP effects $\beta_{\text{linear}}$ are $\sim 45.9$-fold smaller than log-odds ($\beta_{\text{logit}} \approx \frac{\beta_{\text{linear}}}{\mu(1-\mu)}$). In Forward MR, exponentiating unscaled linear probability slopes yields nonsensical "Odds Ratios" of $1.002$ and $0.999$. In Reverse MR (`Breast cancer -> Continuous immune cell level`), dividing continuous immune SD effects ($\beta_Y$) by the tiny $0/1$ linear probability slopes ($\beta_X \propto \mu(1-\mu)$) inflates the Wald ratio by $\sim 45.9\times$ to $\hat{\beta}_{\text{IVW}} \approx 6\text{--}9$, and the authors then **exponentiate this continuous linear slope** ($\exp(8.37) = 4,328.998$; $\exp(8.90) = 7,345.265$) and misreport it as an **"Odds Ratio per 1% increase"** for a continuous outcome!
3. **Complete Absence of Multiple-Testing Correction ($27$ and $17$ Nominal Hits Out of $731 \times 2 = 1,462$ Tests $\le$ Pure Chance Expectation):** Testing $731$ traits at $P < 0.05$ yields an expected $731 \times 0.05 = \mathbf{36.55}$ false positives under the global null in each direction—exceeding both the 27 forward and 17 reverse associations reported.
4. **Sex-Combined Breast Cancer GWAS (`ukb-b-16890` Includes ~210,000 Male Controls) and Falsely Claimed Unit Scaling:** Line 45 claims *"Immune cell levels were analyzed in their original percentage units, and ORs reflect the odds of breast cancer per 1% absolute increase"*, which is false because Orrù et al. inverse-normal transformed all 731 traits (including MFI, absolute counts, and morphological parameters, which are not percentages).

### Assessment of Specific Conclusions
* **Claim 1: Breast cancer onset causally increases activated CD4 regulatory T cell levels with $\text{OR} = 4,328.998$ ($95\% \text{ CI}: 11.80\text{--}1,588,733.71, P = 0.005$ in WM; $\text{OR} = 459.355$ in IVW; $\text{OR} = 7,345.265$ in weighted mode) and 16 other immune traits:** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  These astronomical "Odds Ratios" ($73.86$ to $7,345.27$ with confidence intervals spanning six orders of magnitude up to $3,994,622.93$) are mathematical artifacts of two compounded errors: (1) using unscaled linear probability effects ($\beta_X$ on the $0/1$ scale with $2.23\%$ prevalence in `ukb-b-16890`) as the denominator of the reverse-MR Wald ratio, which multiplies the slope by $\frac{1}{\mu(1-\mu)} \approx 45.9$, and (2) exponentiating a linear regression slope for a continuous quantitative outcome (inverse-normal transformed immune cell levels) and calling $\exp(\hat{\beta})$ an "Odds Ratio."
* **Claim 2: Twenty-seven immune cell traits causally trigger or protect against breast cancer (e.g., $\text{OR} = 1.002, P = 0.011$ for `CD127 on CD28+ CD45RA- CD8br`; $\text{OR} = 0.999, P = 0.024$ for `CD28- DN %DN`):** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  First, finding 27 traits at nominal $P < 0.05$ out of 731 tested exposures ($3.69\%$) is *less* than the 5% false-positive rate ($36.55$ hits) expected under the null hypothesis when no multiple-testing correction is applied. Second, the reported "Odds Ratios" of $1.002$ and $0.999$ are actually $\exp(\beta_{\text{BOLT-LMM}})$ on the linear probability scale rather than true logistic odds ratios. Third, the authors clump instruments at a liberal $r^2 < 0.1$ ($500\text{ kb}$) at $P < 1 \times 10^{-5}$ without accounting for LD correlation.
* **Claim 3: `IgD on unsw mem` refers to "expression of the IgD molecule on University of New South Wales Memory T Cells" (Fig. 1 caption, Lines 55 and 58):** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  In human immunology and in the Orrù et al. dataset, `IgD on unsw mem` denotes surface Immunoglobulin D (IgD) median fluorescence intensity on **unswitched memory B cells** ($\text{CD19}^+ \text{IgD}^+ \text{CD27}^+$). T cells do not express B-cell receptor heavy chains (IgD), and `unsw` stands for "unswitched," not "University of New South Wales."

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 1. [Critical] Comical Hallucination in Figure 1 Legend: `"University of New South Wales Memory T Cells (unsw mem)"`
* **Location:** Results Section (Line 55) and Figure 1 Caption (Line 58).
* **Defect:** Lines 55 and 58 state verbatim: *"IgD on unsw mem refers to the expression of the IgD molecule on University of New South Wales Memory T Cells (unsw mem)"*. In the Sardinian immunophenotyping GWAS (Orrù et al. 2020, *Nature Genetics*), `unsw mem` is the standard abbreviation for **unswitched memory B cells** ($\text{IgD}^+ \text{CD27}^+$ B lymphocytes). Calling B cells "Memory T Cells" and expanding `unsw` as "University of New South Wales" demonstrates unvetted LLM text generation and a complete absence of domain review.
* **Remediation:** Replace `"University of New South Wales Memory T Cells (unsw mem)"` with `"unswitched memory B cells (unsw mem)"` in Lines 55 and 58.

### 2. [Critical] Linear-Probability vs. Log-Odds Scale Failure in `ukb-b-16890` Producing Absurd Reverse-MR "Odds Ratios" up to $7,345.27$
* **Location:** Methods Section "Statistical analysis" (Line 45), Results Sections (Lines 53, 63), and Figures 1–2.
* **Defect:**
  1. **Unscaled `BOLT-LMM` linear probability effects in `ukb-b-16890`:** The MRC-IEU dataset `ukb-b-16890` ($10,303$ breast cancer cases and $452,630$ controls; case fraction $\mu = 0.02226$) reports linear mixed model (`BOLT-LMM`) effect sizes on the $0/1$ case-control scale, not logistic regression log-odds.
  2. **Reverse MR scale explosion and invalid exponentiation of continuous outcomes:** In Section 3.2 (Line 63), when breast cancer (`ukb-b-16890`) is the exposure and continuous Sardinian immune traits are the outcomes, the Wald ratio $\hat{\beta}_{YX} = \hat{\beta}_Y / \hat{\beta}_X$ divides an SD-scaled continuous immune effect by a tiny linear probability slope ($\hat{\beta}_X \approx 0.0218 \times \hat{\beta}_{\text{log-odds}}$), inflating $\hat{\beta}_{YX}$ by $\sim 45.9\times$. The authors then exponentiate these inflated continuous linear slopes ($\exp(\hat{\beta}_{YX})$) and report them as Odds Ratios: e.g., $\text{OR} = 4,328.998$ ($95\% \text{ CI}: 11.796\text{--}1,588,733.707$), $\text{OR} = 7,345.265$ ($95\% \text{ CI}: 13.506\text{--}3,994,622.925$), $\text{OR} = 1,980.835$, $\text{OR} = 882.946$, and $\text{OR} = 852.602$! Continuous immune cell phenotypes are not binary disease outcomes and do not have Odds Ratios.
  3. **False unit claim in Methods (Line 45):** Line 45 claims *"Immune cell levels were analyzed in their original percentage units, and ORs reflect the odds of breast cancer per 1% absolute increase"*, and Line 65 claims *"ORs correspond to absolute percentage changes in immune cell levels"*. In reality, Orrù et al. applied rank-based inverse normal transformation (SD units) to all 731 traits, and 539 of the 731 traits are median fluorescence intensities (MFI), absolute counts (AC), or morphological parameters (FSC-A/SSC-A)—not percentages!
* **Remediation:** Replace the sex-combined linear-scale `ukb-b-16890` dataset with a female-specific logistic-regression breast cancer GWAS (e.g., BCAC, Michailidou et al., $N = 228,951$ females), report continuous reverse-MR effects as linear $\beta$ (SD change in immune trait per unit log-odds of breast cancer) rather than exponentiated "Odds Ratios," and remove the false claim that all 731 traits are measured in raw 1% percentage units.

### 3. [Critical] Uncorrected Multiple Testing Across $1,462$ Bidirectional Tests (Hit Rate Below Null Expectation)
* **Location:** Methods (Lines 40, 45) and Results (Lines 53, 63).
* **Defect:** The authors perform $731$ forward MR tests and $731$ reverse MR tests ($1,462$ total tests) using a nominal $P < 0.05$ cutoff without any FDR or Bonferroni adjustment, while also clumping at $r^2 < 0.1$ ($500\text{ kb}$) at $P < 1 \times 10^{-5}$. Under the global null hypothesis, $731 \times 0.05 = 36.55$ false positives are expected per direction by pure chance, which is *greater* than the 27 forward hits ($3.69\%$) and 17 reverse hits ($2.33\%$) reported. Furthermore, the authors declare both WM and IVW as "primary" methods, yet include traits in their 27 forward hits where WM is non-significant (e.g., `CD28- DN %DN` has $P_{\text{WM}} = 0.065$, Line 53).
* **Remediation:** Apply Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction ($\text{FDR} < 0.05$) across all 731 traits in each direction, tighten LD clumping to $r^2 < 0.001$ within $10,000\text{ kb}$, and designate a single primary estimator (random-effects IVW).

### 4. [Minor] Incorrect Sample Size Citation and Duplicated Supplementary Captions
* **Location:** Section "Data sources for GWAS" (Line 35) and Section "Electronic supplementary material" (Lines 93–135).
* **Defect:** Line 35 states that the Orrù et al. Sardinian immune GWAS included `3514 individuals` (the actual sample size of Orrù et al. 2020 is $3,757$ individuals). In addition, every single supplementary table and figure caption from `Supplementary Table 1` through `Supplementary Fig. 2` (Lines 93–135) is printed twice consecutively, and no quantitative tables are provided in the main text.
* **Remediation:** Correct the Orrù et al. sample size to $3,757$, remove the duplicated supplementary captions, and include a summary table in the main text.
