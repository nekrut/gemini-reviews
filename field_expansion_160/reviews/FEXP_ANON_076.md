## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict
This manuscript claims to identify three circulating plasma microRNAs—`hsa-miR-130a-3p` and `hsa-miR-196b-5p` as causal risk factors and `hsa-miR-339-3p` as a causal protective factor—for chronic rhinosinusitis (CRS) via two-sample Mendelian Randomization (MR) across "two independent CRS datasets," and claims to validate their downstream signaling mechanisms (activation of MAPK and PI3K-AKT pathways and promotion of apoptosis) in clinical CRS nasal polyp specimens. Forensically, both the statistical genetics pipeline and the molecular mechanism linking the MR results to the wet-lab experiments are fundamentally flawed. First, the MR instrument selection clumped cis-mirQTL SNPs at a permissive linkage disequilibrium threshold of $r^2 < 0.5$ within a miniature $10\text{ kb}$ window (`Line 39`) and entered correlated cis-variants into standard uncorrelated Inverse Variance Weighted (`IVW`) regression (`TwoSampleMR`), violating the independence assumption of standard IVW and severely inflating false-positive rates. Second, the two CRS GWAS datasets presented as "two independent CRS datasets" (`ebi-a-GCST90018823` from Sakaue et al. 2021 and `ukb-d-J32` from Neale Lab UK Biobank) share massive unacknowledged sample overlap because `ebi-a-GCST90018823` is a trans-biobank meta-analysis predominantly composed of UK Biobank participants (`ukb-d-J32` is also a linear-probability `BOLT-LMM` GWAS on a sparse binary ICD-10 phenotype with $1,908$ cases and $359,286$ controls, introducing severe scale compression). Third, the manuscript omits all quantitative MR estimates (`OR`, `95% CI`, `P`-values, and instrument counts) for its three lead miRNAs from the main text and tables. Fourth, the mechanistic argument contains a direct directional contradiction: the authors state that `miR-339-3p` is *protective* against CRS and *downregulated* in CRS patients (`Line 98`), yet simultaneously assert that `miR-339-3p` exerts its protective effect by *activating* the PI3K-AKT pathway (`Abstract Line 11`, `Figure 6 caption Line 114`) while showing via Western blot and IHC that PI3K-AKT phosphorylation (`p-PI3K`/`p-AKT`) is *elevated* in diseased CRS patients (`Line 111`) alongside elevated apoptosis (`BAX`/`BCL-2`). No direct target mRNA of `miR-130a-3p`, `miR-196b-5p`, or `miR-339-3p` was ever assayed or perturbed.

* **Claim 1:** *"By analyzing two independent CRS datasets, we identified two miRNAs (hsa-miR-130a-3p and hsa-miR-196b-5p) positively associated with CRS, which are upregulated in CRS patients and increase the risk of developing the condition. Conversely, one miRNA (hsa-miR-339-3p) was found to be negatively associated with CRS, downregulated in CRS patients, and serves a protective role against the disease."* — **`[Methodologically Flawed]`** (`ebi-a-GCST90018823` and `ukb-d-J32` are not independent because `ebi-a-GCST90018823` incorporates the UK Biobank cohort; instruments were clumped at $r^2 < 0.5$ in a $10\text{ kb}$ window and analyzed with uncorrelated IVW; and zero numerical MR estimates—OR, 95% CI, $P$-value, or $N_{\text{SNP}}$—are reported in the text or main tables).
* **Claim 2:** *"Functional enrichment analysis and experimental validation demonstrated that the MAPK and PI3K-AKT pathways are predominantly activated by the target genes of the positively and negatively associated miRNAs, respectively, and these pathways promote apoptosis in nasal mucosal epithelial cells."* — **`[Overstated]`** (No direct target genes of any of the three miRNAs were experimentally measured or perturbed; furthermore, claiming that a *protective* miRNA [`miR-339-3p`] protects against CRS by *activating* PI3K-AKT while showing that PI3K-AKT is hyper-activated in CRS disease tissue and drives epithelial apoptosis is internally contradictory).
* **Claim 3:** *"RT-qPCR showed that compared to the control group, hsa-miR-130a-3p and hsa-miR-196b-5p were significantly upregulated in CRS patients, whereas hsa-miR-339-3p was downregulated (Figure 4E)."* — **`[Moderately Supported]`** (Supported by small clinical tissue RT-qPCR of $n = 10$ controls vs. $n = 16$ CRSwNP patients, though the study conflates circulating plasma mirQTLs from Framingham whole blood/plasma with inflamed nasal polyp vs. inferior turbinate tissue heterogeneity).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Major Methodological, Statistical, and Mechanistic Defects

1. **Correlated cis-mirQTL Instrument Selection ($r^2 < 0.5$, Window $= 10\text{ kb}$) Applied to Uncorrelated IVW (`Section 2.2`, `Line 39`):**
   * In `Section 2.2` (`Line 39`), the authors state: *"A strict threshold (P < 6.6 × 10^-5) was applied to select single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) strongly associated with miRNAs. Linkage disequilibrium (LD) analysis was performed using data from the European samples of the 1000 Genomes Project (r2 < 0.5, clustering window = 10 kb) to ensure the independence of the selected SNPs."*
   * Clumping within a $10\text{ kb}$ window at $r^2 < 0.5$ leaves substantial short- and medium-range linkage disequilibrium ($0.001 < r^2 < 0.5$, as well as any LD beyond $10\text{ kb}$) intact among cis-mirQTL variants located in the same locus (`Huan et al., 2015`, $N = 5,239$). Feeding correlated SNPs into standard `TwoSampleMR` IVW, weighted median, and MR-Egger estimators—without accounting for the inter-instrument LD covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Omega}$—artificially deflates standard errors and produces severe Type I error inflation.

2. **False Claim of "Two Independent CRS Datasets" (`ebi-a-GCST90018823` and `ukb-d-J32`) and Linear Probability Scale Distortion (`Abstract Line 9`, `Section 2.1 Line 28`):**
   * The manuscript repeatedly claims to validate its MR findings across *"two independent CRS datasets"* (`Abstract Line 9`, `Line 66`, `Line 156`): `ebi-a-GCST90018823` ($8,871$ cases and $461,025$ controls; Sakaue et al. 2021) and `ukb-d-J32` ($1,908$ cases and $359,286$ controls; Neale Lab UK Biobank).
   * In reality, `ebi-a-GCST90018823` (Sakaue et al. 2021 European CRS meta-analysis) is a composite of UK Biobank (`~360,000` individuals) and FinnGen (`~110,000` individuals). Consequently, `ukb-d-J32` ($361,194$ UK Biobank individuals) is **nearly a 100% proper subset** of the discovery dataset `ebi-a-GCST90018823`, not an independent replication cohort.
   * Furthermore, `ukb-d-J32` is a Neale Lab linear regression (`BOLT-LMM` on $0/1$ binary ICD-10 code `J32`) with a case prevalence of $\mu = 1,908 / 361,194 = 0.00528$ ($0.53\%$), whereas `ebi-a-GCST90018823` is on the logistic log-odds scale.

3. **Complete Absence of Numerical MR Results in the Manuscript Text and Tables (`Sections 3.1–3.2`, `Lines 66–81`):**
   * Remarkably, the manuscript contains **zero main tables** (`Table 1` does not exist; only `Supplementary Tables S1–S5` are referenced) and never reports a single numerical Odds Ratio, 95% confidence interval, $P$-value, $F$-statistic, heterogeneity $Q$ statistic, or SNP count in the text for any of the $25$ or $5$ nominal miRNAs or for the three overlapping miRNAs (`hsa-miR-130a-3p`, `hsa-miR-196b-5p`, and `hsa-miR-339-3p`).
   * In addition, testing $76$ circulating miRNAs across outcomes without any multiple-testing correction (`FDR` or Bonferroni $0.05 / 76 = 6.58 \times 10^{-4}$) is unaddressed; under the null hypothesis at $\alpha = 0.05$, intersecting a discovery cohort and an overlapping subset cohort yields expected false-positive overlaps by chance alone.

4. **Internal Mechanistic Contradiction Regarding `hsa-miR-339-3p`, PI3K-AKT Activation, and Epithelial Apoptosis (`Abstract Line 11`, `Sections 3.3–3.6`, `Lines 98–125`):**
   * Follow the authors' causal chain for `hsa-miR-339-3p`:
     1. In `Section 3.1` (`Line 76`) and `Section 3.4` (`Line 98`), `hsa-miR-339-3p` is identified as a **protective** factor against CRS (`OR < 1`) that is **downregulated** in CRS patients.
     2. In `Section 3.3` (`Line 86`), KEGG analysis of predicted target mRNAs of `hsa-miR-339-3p` highlights the `PI3K-AKT` pathway. Because microRNAs canonically *repress* their target mRNAs, downregulation of `hsa-miR-339-3p` in CRS patients would *de-repress* (upregulate) its target genes.
     3. In `Section 3.5` (`Line 111`), Western blot and IHC show that `p-PI3K` and `p-AKT` are **significantly elevated (activated)** in CRS patients compared to controls, and `Section 3.6` (`Line 123`) states that this activation promotes nasal mucosal epithelial cell apoptosis (`increased BAX`, `decreased BCL-2`).
     4. Yet in `Abstract Line 11` and `Figure 6 caption Line 114`, the authors write that *"the MAPK and PI3K-AKT pathways are predominantly activated by the target genes of the positively and negatively associated miRNAs, respectively, and these pathways promote apoptosis in nasal mucosal epithelial cells"* and in `Discussion Line 147` claim that *"hsa-miR-339-3p may exert a protective effect by regulating the PI3K-AKT pathway"* (`Line 149`: *"activating the PI3K-AKT pathway helps inhibit inflammation"*).
   * If `p-PI3K`/`p-AKT` is **already hyper-activated** in CRS patients where `miR-339-3p` is **downregulated**, and if `PI3K-AKT` activation drives pathogenic epithelial apoptosis (`Line 123`), then `miR-339-3p` cannot simultaneously be "activating the PI3K-AKT pathway to inhibit inflammation." Moreover, for the risk miRNAs (`miR-130a-3p` and `miR-196b-5p`), which are *upregulated* in CRS, canonical miRNA repression would *downregulate* their target mRNAs rather than activate them.

5. **Confounding of Tissue Type in Wet-Lab Comparison (`Section 2.6`, `Line 53`):**
   * In `Section 2.6` (`Line 53`), CRS group samples were harvested from **inflamed nasal polyps** (`CRSwNP`), whereas control group samples were harvested from **inferior turbinate mucosa** of nasal septum deviation patients. Inferior turbinate mucosa and ethmoidal nasal polyp tissue have well-known baseline differences in cellular composition (e.g., eosinophil/lymphocyte infiltration, goblet cell hyperplasia, and vascularity), confounding bulk tissue qRT-PCR and Western blot comparisons when no cell-type normalization or direct miRNA mimic/inhibitor perturbation in cultured nasal epithelial cells was performed.
