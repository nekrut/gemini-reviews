## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict
This brief communication reports the cloning of two `Mx` dynamin-like GTPase cDNAs (`Lj-MxA`, 1,959 bp / 652 aa; and `Lj-MxB`, 2,193 bp / 730 aa) from the Japanese lamprey (*Lampetra japonica*), characterizes their conserved GTPase motifs, constructs a chordate Neighbor-Joining tree, applies codon-level $d_N/dS$ ($\omega$) models to identify 9 putative positively selected sites, and measures basal and `Poly I:C`- or `LPS`-induced mRNA expression in heart and gill tissues by qRT-PCR. While cloning two `Mx` paralogs in a jawless vertebrate and demonstrating their transcriptional induction by `Poly I:C` ($P < 0.001$ at 8 h) provides useful primary evidence of interferon-stimulated gene conservation in cyclostomes, the manuscript exhibits major analytical and structural defects: (1) the coordinates of the positivamente selected sites reported for `Lj-MxA` (`139K, 252R, 376S, 398K, 416R, 423W... 651A`) and `Lj-MxB` (`217K, 330R, 454S, 476K, 494R, 501S... 730L`) are shifted by **exactly $+78$ amino acids** (`730 - 652 = 78`) starting already at residue 139 in the N-terminal GTPase domain (`139 + 78 = 217`), proving that `Lj-MxB` carries a 78-residue N-terminal extension relative to `Lj-MxA` and directly contradicting the claim in line 7 that the structural difference between `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` is a 40-amino-acid region in loop L4 of the C-terminal stalk; (2) the `M3 vs M0` likelihood ratio test is misapplied as a test of positive selection (line 16), and branch/branch-site $\omega$ models are run across deep bilaterian/chordate divergences (hemichordate *Saccoglossus kowalevskii* to mammals, $>500\text{ MYA}$) where synonymous substitutions ($d_S$) are completely saturated; and (3) no Materials and Methods section or experimental/computational parameters are provided anywhere in the manuscript.

### Claim-by-Claim Evaluation
* **Claim 1:** *Lampetra japonica possesses two Mx paralogs, `Lj-MxA` (1,959 bp, 652 aa) and `Lj-MxB` (2,193 bp, 730 aa), retaining the canonical N-terminal GTPase triad (`GDQSSGKS`, `DLPG`, `TKPD`), `LPRGSGIVTR` tag, bundle signaling elements, stalk, and C-terminal leucine zipper.* **[Well Supported]** — Supported by full-length ORF cloning and conserved domain sequence alignment (line 7).
* **Claim 2:** *`Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` are constitutively expressed across lamprey tissues (highest in heart and gill) and are significantly upregulated in heart and gill at 4–8 h post-stimulation with `Poly I:C` and `LPS` ($n = 3$, Fig. 2).* **[Moderately Supported]** — The qRT-PCR time-course profiles (Fig. 2B–E) show clear induction peaking at 8 h after `Poly I:C` challenge, though the specific statistical test used to generate the asterisk $p$-values (`*P < 0.05`, `**P < 0.01`, `***P < 0.001`), animal dosing protocols, and primer amplification efficiencies are entirely omitted.
* **Claim 3:** *The structural difference between `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` is a 40-amino-acid region in the disordered loop L4 of the stalk, and `Lj-MxA` is localized primarily in the cytoplasm whereas `Lj-MxB` is present in both the cytoplasm and the nucleus (line 7).* **[Methodologically Flawed]** — First, no subcellular localization experiment (immunofluorescence, GFP tagging, or nuclear/cytoplasmic fractionation) or prediction tool is described or shown to support the cytoplasmic vs. nuclear localization claim. Second, `Lj-MxB` (730 aa) is **78 amino acids** longer than `Lj-MxA` (652 aa), and as proven by the residue numbering in line 18 (`139K -> 217K`, `252R -> 330R`, `376S -> 454S`, `398K -> 476K`, `416R -> 494R`, `423W -> 501S`), the entire 78-aa length offset occurs upstream of position 139 in the N-terminal extension, not as a 40-aa insertion in the C-terminal L4 loop.
* **Claim 4:** *Branch, site (`M3 vs M0` and `M8 vs M7`), and branch-site models demonstrate that lamprey `Mx` is driven by positive selection ($\omega > 1$) at 9 specific codons (`139K, 252R, 376S, 398K, 416R, 423W, 456A, 499A, 651A` in `Lj-MxA`) that drive functional divergence between `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` (lines 16–18, 27).* **[Methodologically Flawed]** — (a) `M3 vs M0` tests rate heterogeneity among sites, not positive selection; (b) fitting codon $\omega = d_N/d_S$ models across an alignment spanning hemichordates (*Saccoglossus kowalevskii*), cephalochordates (*Branchiostoma floridae*), cyclostomes, and gnathostomes suffers from extreme $d_S$ saturation across $>500\text{ MYA}$ branches; and (c) 7 of the 9 "positively selected sites" are identical between `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` (`K, R, S, K, R, A` at homologous positions shifted by $+78\text{ aa}$), meaning they cannot explain functional divergence between the two paralogs.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1. Structural & Arithmetic Contradictions
1. **Exact $+78\text{ aa}$ N-Terminal Coordinate Offset Refutes the Claimed C-Terminal Loop L4 Structural Difference and Scrambles Site Pairs (line 7 vs. line 18):**
   * Line 7 states that `Lj-MxA` is 652 aa (`1,959 bp`) and `Lj-MxB` is 730 aa (`2,193 bp`), a length difference of exactly $730 - 652 = \mathbf{78\text{ aa}}$ (`234 bp`). Line 7 claims: *"The structural difference between Lj-MxA and Lj-MxB is a region of 40 amino acids in the disordered loop L4 protruded from the compact stalk."*
   * However, inspect the coordinates of the 9 positively selected sites reported for `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` in line 18:
     * GTPase domain: `Lj-MxA` `139K, 252R` vs. `Lj-MxB` `217K, 330R` ($217 - 139 = \mathbf{78}$; $330 - 252 = \mathbf{78}$).
     * CID (stalk): `Lj-MxA` `376S, 398K, 416R, 423W` vs. `Lj-MxB` `454S, 476K, 494R, 501S` ($454 - 376 = \mathbf{78}$; $476 - 398 = \mathbf{78}$; $494 - 416 = \mathbf{78}$; $501 - 423 = \mathbf{78}$).
     * C-terminal BSE: `Lj-MxA` `651A` vs. `Lj-MxB` `730L` ($730 - 652 = \mathbf{78}$, with `651` vs. `730` off by 1 residue at the C-terminus).
   * Because the $+78\text{ aa}$ coordinate shift is already present in full at residue 139 of `Lj-MxA` (`217` in `Lj-MxB`) and remains constant (`+78`) all the way to the C-terminus (`652` vs. `730`), the 78-residue extension in `Lj-MxB` is located at the **N-terminus** (upstream of residue 139), not in loop L4 of the C-terminal stalk!
   * Furthermore, for the fifth and sixth CID sites in `Lj-MxA` (`456A` and `499A`), adding $+78$ yields positions `534` ($456 + 78 = 534$) and `577` ($499 + 78 = 577$) in `Lj-MxB`, which line 18 prints out of numerical order as `"577A, and 534V"`—swapping the order of the two sites so that `456A` aligns with `534V` while `499A` aligns with `577A`.

### 3.2. Methodological & Statistical Defects
1. **Misuse of `M3 vs M0` as a Positive Selection Test & Deep-Divergence $d_S$ Saturation (line 16):**
   * Line 16 states: *"Likelihood ratio tests between the two nested models (M3 vs M0 and M8 vs M7) revealed that an alternative hypothesis that allowed for positive selection fit the data significantly better than neutral models."* In `PAML`, `M3` (discrete) vs. `M0` (one-ratio) tests only for among-site variation in $\omega$, not positive selection (Yang et al. 2000). Moreover, applying codon models across an alignment spanning hemichordates (*S. kowalevskii*), cephalochordates (*B. floridae*), cyclostomes, and jawed vertebrates violates synonymous rate reliability due to complete $d_S$ saturation along deep branches.
2. **Complete Omission of Experimental, Statistical, and Computational Protocols:**
   * The manuscript lacks any Methods section or caption-level specification of: (a) GenBank accession numbers for the newly cloned `Lj-MxA` and `Lj-MxB` cDNAs; (b) `Poly I:C` and `LPS` concentrations, injection volumes, animal sample sizes, and housing conditions; (c) the statistical hypothesis test used in Figure 2B–E; and (d) how subcellular localization (`"Lj-MxA is primarily localized in the cytoplasm, whereas Lj-MxB is present in both the cytoplasm and the nucleus"`, line 7) was determined.

### 3.3. Minor Textual & Terminology Errors
* **Tissue Count Mismatch (`"seven"` vs. 8 tissues, line 20):** Line 20 states: *"MxA and MxB expression levels were studied in **seven** L. japonica tissues: liver, gill, kidney, intestine, heart, leukocytes, muscle, and supraneural body."* Eight tissues are listed and plotted in Figure 2A.
* **Misnomer `"leucine zinc finger region"` (line 7):** Line 7 refers to the C-terminal leucine zipper (`LZ`) motif of Mx as a `"leucine zinc finger region"`; Mx GTPases contain a leucine zipper coiled-coil motif (correctly termed `leucine zipper` in line 3 and `LZ` in line 18), not a zinc-coordinating finger domain.
