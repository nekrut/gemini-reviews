## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict
This manuscript claims to construct and validate a 9-gene exosome-related prognostic signature in esophageal carcinoma (`TCGA-ESCA`, validated in `GSE53625` and `IMvigor210`), paired with single-cell RNA-seq analysis (`GSE196756`, `n = 6` samples) and pan-cancer `GSVA` profiling of `121` exosome-related genes.

Forensic audit reveals **irrefutable evidence of unedited LLM placeholder fabrication, cross-cancer template plagiarism ("bladder cancer"), fabricated post-hoc Results in the Discussion, and complete omission of the 9 signature genes**, making this manuscript a textbook paper-mill / synthetic text product:
1. **Smoking-Gun Unedited LLM Placeholders (`"e.g., GeneA, GeneB, GeneC"`) and Fabricated Analyses in the Discussion:** In the Discussion (`line 204`), the authors write:
   > *"By mapping each of the nine signature genes onto our annotated scRNA-seq clusters, we observed that seven genes (**e.g., GeneA, GeneB, GeneC**) were predominantly expressed in tumor-derived epithelial cells with high exosome activity, while the remaining two were enriched in cancer-associated fibroblasts. Leveraging these cellular-origin profiles, we applied CIBERSORTx deconvolution to TCGA-ESCA bulk samples and found a strong positive correlation between estimated epithelial cell fraction and the nine-gene risk score ($r = 0.62, p < 0.001$). We further refined the prognostic model by focusing on epithelial-specific markers (e.g., EPCAM, KRT8) and reran LASSO-Cox regression on epithelial-filtered expression data; this 'epithelial-filtered' model retained six of the original nine genes and achieved a comparable 5-year AUC (0.78 vs. 0.80)."*
   The literal placeholder string **`(e.g., GeneA, GeneB, GeneC)`** was generated by an LLM prompt asking how to integrate scRNA-seq with the bulk signature, and was pasted verbatim into the manuscript without the authors even replacing `GeneA, GeneB, GeneC` with real gene symbols! Moreover, **none of the analyses described in that paragraph** (`CIBERSORTx` deconvolution, `$r = 0.62, p < 0.001$`, or the 6-gene "epithelial-filtered" LASSO-Cox model with `AUC = 0.78 vs. 0.80`) exist anywhere in the Methods, Results, or Figures!
2. **Copy-Pasted "Bladder Cancer" Template Text Inside an Esophageal Carcinoma Paper:** In the Methods (`Selection of exosome-related gene sets`, line 40), the manuscript states:
   > *"By incorporating the exosome gene set into our analysis, we aim to explore the potential involvement of exosomes in **bladder cancer** and investigate their association with immune escape mechanisms and genomic alterations. This approach will provide us with valuable insights into the underlying biology of **bladder cancer** and potentially uncover novel biomarkers or therapeutic targets..."*
   The authors copied their Methods text directly from a bladder cancer template and forgot to find-and-replace "bladder cancer" with "esophageal carcinoma."
3. **Complete Omission of the 9 Signature Gene Names, Coefficients, and Formula:** Nowhere in the text, tables, or figure captions are the 9 genes of the "nine-gene exosome-derived risk signature" or their LASSO-Cox coefficients ever listed (only `HAPLN3` is ever named in the Results text!). Even `Table 1` (`Predicted drug–gene interactions for components of the nine-gene exosome-derived risk signature`, lines 23–25) is printed as a two-sentence caption stub with **no table body at all**!
4. **Severe Internal Numerical Contradictions:**
   - In the Results (`line 157`), the authors claim `TCGA-ESCA` has a sample size of **`N = 520`** (and `GSE53625` has `N = 179`), whereas the entire `TCGA-ESCA` cohort in GDC contains only `~160–185` tumors (`N = 520` is the sample size of `TCGA-HNSC` or `TCGA-LUAD`!).
   - In the Discussion (`line 204` / offset `line 1`), the authors claim their model yielded *"time-dependent ROC AUCs exceeding 0.80 for 1-, 3-, and 5-year overall survival, and it showed comparable AUCs (>0.78) in the independent GSE53625 validation set,"* whereas the Results (`line 157`) explicitly state that in `GSE53625` *"the signature achieved AUCs of **0.65, 0.63, and 0.61** at 1, 3, and 5 years"*!
   - Between two adjacent paragraphs in the Results (`line 145` vs. `line 153`), the number of differentially expressed genes in the epithelial cluster (`Cluster 3`) jumps from `1,343 + 374 = 1,717` (`line 145`) to `1,987` (`line 153`).
