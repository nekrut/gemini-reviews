## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study applies Voxel-Mirrored Homotopic Connectivity (VMHC) to resting-state fMRI data from $32$ patients with acute acquired concomitant esotropia (AACE) and $31$ healthy controls (HCs). The authors conclude that AACE patients exhibit significantly lower VMHC in the superior frontal gyrus (SFG) and anterior cingulate gyrus (ACG), and that VMHC in the left caudate and bilateral olfactory cortex correlates with near-vision work duration, strabismus deviation angles, and Beck Depression Inventory (BDI) scores.

Forensic audit reveals multiple foundational defects across the preprocessing description, anatomical labeling, mathematical properties of VMHC, and clinical tables:
1. **Complete Omission of the fMRI Preprocessing Pipeline and Symmetrical Template Registration Required for VMHC:** Sections "Resting-state fMRI parameters" (lines 55–62) and "VMHC statistical analysis" (lines 64–69) omit the entire fMRI preprocessing workflow. Neither the total number of acquired EPI volumes (or scan duration), discarded dummy volumes, slice-timing correction, head-motion realignment/exclusion criteria, spatial smoothing kernel, bandpass filter frequency range, nor nuisance covariate regression (white matter, CSF, motion parameters) are reported anywhere in the manuscript. Even more fundamentally, calculating valid VMHC requires non-linearly registering normalized brain images to a **hemispherically symmetric MNI template** so that left/right voxels $(-x, y, z)$ and $(+x, y, z)$ are anatomically homologous despite human cerebral asymmetry (Zuo et al., 2010); no symmetric template registration is mentioned.
2. **Mathematical Violation of Hemispheric Symmetry in Table 3:** By mathematical definition, VMHC at voxel $(x, y, z)$ is the Pearson correlation between the timeseries at $(x, y, z)$ and its mirror-image voxel $(-x, y, z)$. Consequently, every VMHC statistical map is strictly symmetric across the mid-sagittal plane ($x = 0$): every cluster at $(+x, y, z)$ must have an identical mirror cluster at $(-x, y, z)$ with the exact same peak $t$-value and the exact same voxel extent. Yet in **Table 3** (lines 127–134), the right orbitofrontal cluster at $(15, 66, -12)$ has cluster size **`14 voxels`** while its mirror peak at $(-15, 66, -12)$ has cluster size **`12 voxels`**, and the third row (`Olfactory_R/Olfactory_L/Caudate_L`, peak at $(9, 24, -3)$, $t = -3.7192$, size `17 voxels`) has **no left-hemisphere mirror entry at $(-9, 24, -3)$** and paradoxically labels a right-hemisphere coordinate ($x = +9$) as `Caudate_L` (left caudate)!
3. **Wholesale Contradiction Between the Brain Regions Claimed in the Text (`SFG` and `ACG`) and Those Reported in Table 3 and Correlations (`Orbitofrontal`, `Olfactory`, and `Left Caudate`):** Throughout the Abstract (lines 9, 11), Results (line 121), and Discussion (lines 148, 150–158), the authors claim that VMHC was reduced in the **superior frontal gyrus (SFG)** and **anterior cingulate gyrus (ACG)**, devoting the Discussion to frontal eye fields (BA 6, 8, 9, 32) and limbic cingulate mechanisms. However, **Table 3** (lines 131–133) contains neither `Frontal_Sup` nor `Cingulum_Ant`: it lists only **`Frontal_Med_Orb` / `Frontal_Sup_Orb`** (gyrus rectus / medial orbitofrontal cortex, BA 11) and **`Olfactory_R/Olfactory_L/Caudate_L`**! Furthermore, while Results line 140 reports that BDI depression scores negatively correlated with the **left olfactory cortex** ($r = -0.3588, p = 0.0475$), Discussion line 158 contradicts the Results by stating: *"In our study, the VMHC signal values in the anterior cingulate gyrus were significantly lower in the AACE group compared to the control group and exhibited a negative correlation with BDI depression scores."*
4. **Template Copy-Paste Artifacts and Arithmetic / Sample-Size Errors in Tables 1 and 2:**
   - The caption of **Figure 1** (line 124) reads: *"Interhemispheric connectivity in the **CSA** versus **NCs**"*—an unedited copy-paste artifact from an unrelated manuscript studying `CSA` instead of `AACE`.
   - In Section "Participants" (line 36), the inclusion criteria state: *"Subjects in **both groups** exhibited symptoms of diplopia or acute episodes of esotropia"*—erroneously stating that healthy controls had diplopia or acute esotropia.
   - In Results line 81, the authors state that AACE patients had a *"mean age of onset of 33.28 ± 9.15 years"*, which is actually their **current age** (`33.28 ± 9.15`, Table 1 line 90), whereas Table 1 line 105 reports `Age of onset/year` as **`29.66 (9.65)`**.
   - In Table 1 (line 99), `Fusion range (°)` is $19.97 \pm 8.52$ vs. $25.38 \pm 4.66$, for which a two-sample $t$-test with $N_1 = 32, N_2 = 31$ yields $t = -3.140, p = 0.0026$, contradicting the reported $t = -1.717, p = 0.095$ (indicating substantial unreported missing synoptophore data).

### Claim-by-Claim Audit Summary
* **Claim 1:** *"VMHC levels in the superior frontal gyrus and anterior cingulate were significantly lower in the AACE group."* — **`[Methodologically Flawed]`** (First, no fMRI preprocessing or symmetrical template registration is described. Second, Table 3 does not list the superior frontal gyrus or anterior cingulate gyrus, listing instead `Frontal_Med_Orb`/`Frontal_Sup_Orb` and `Olfactory`/`Caudate_L` with asymmetric voxel counts across hemispheres. Third, the GRF correction in line 73 specifies only a single $p < 0.001$ value without distinguishing voxel-level and cluster-level thresholds, yielding tiny clusters of 12–17 voxels.)
* **Claim 2:** *"In the AACE group, the VMHC values of the left caudate positively correlated with near vision work duration (r = 0.381, p = 0.034), the deviation angles at near (r = 0.428, p = 0.015) and at distance (r = 0.416, p = 0.018). The VMHC values in the bilateral olfactory cortex also positively correlated with the near vision work duration... while BDI scores negatively correlated with the VMHC values of the left olfactory cortex (r = −0.359, p = 0.048)."* — **`[Fragile]`** (All six correlations have marginal uncorrected $p$-values between $0.015$ and $0.048$ across multiple AAL regions and clinical variables without multiple-testing correction; none survive Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction. Moreover, Discussion line 158 misattributes the BDI correlation to the anterior cingulate gyrus instead of the left olfactory cortex.)
* **Claim 3:** *"The dysfunction of the medial frontal gyrus and anterior cingulate gyrus is the underlying neuropathological mechanism of AACE, and these dysfunctions may be related to poor eye habits and the severity of deviation."* — **`[Overstated]`** (Cross-sectional observational correlations in tiny 12–17 voxel subcallosal/orbitofrontal clusters cannot establish causal neuropathological mechanisms, especially when near-vision work duration did not significantly differ between AACE and HC groups [$8.15 \pm 2.54$ vs. $7.05 \pm 3.40\text{ h/day}, p = 0.119$, Table 1 line 104] and BDI-II scores did not differ between groups [$5.42 \pm 6.91$ vs. $6.11 \pm 3.48, p = 0.690$, Table 2 line 113].)

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1. Complete Omission of the fMRI Preprocessing Pipeline, Symmetrical Brain Registration, and Software Contradictions (Pillar 3 & Pillar 5 — Critical)
* **Location:** Sections "Resting-state fMRI parameters" (lines 55–62), "VMHC statistical analysis" (lines 64–69), and "Statistical analysis" (lines 71–74).
* **The Defect:**
  1. **Missing Preprocessing Steps:** The manuscript contains no description of fMRI preprocessing. Essential acquisition and processing parameters are entirely omitted: total number of functional volumes acquired (and scan duration), number of discarded initial volumes, slice-timing correction, head-motion realignment and motion exclusion thresholds, spatial normalization method and voxel size, spatial smoothing FWHM, temporal bandpass filter cutoffs, and nuisance regression of white matter, CSF, and head-motion covariates.
  2. **Missing Symmetrical Template Registration for VMHC:** Standard MNI152 templates are anatomically asymmetric between left and right hemispheres (e.g., Yakovlevian torque and petalia). Valid VMHC computation requires creating a group-specific symmetric T1 template (by averaging the normalized T1 images with their left–right flipped versions) and re-normalizing the functional images to this symmetric template before computing homotopic Pearson correlations (Zuo et al., 2010). No symmetric registration is described.
  3. **Software Version Contradiction:** Line 66 states that data were analyzed using `DPARSFA` and `SPM12`, whereas line 73 states that two-sample $t$-tests were run in the `SPM8 toolkit`. Additionally, line 73 states `(p < 0.001 for multiple comparisons using Gaussian Random Field theory)` without specifying both the voxel-level cluster-forming threshold and the cluster-level FWE/GRF threshold, resulting in clusters of only $12\text{–}17\text{ voxels}$ (Table 3).

### 3.2. Violation of Mathematical Midline Symmetry in VMHC Table 3 and Anatomical Mismatch Between Text and Tables (Pillar 1, Pillar 4 & Pillar 5 — Critical)
* **Location:** Abstract (lines 9, 11), Results (lines 121, 127–134, 140), Table 3, and Discussion (lines 148–164).
* **The Defect:**
  1. **Asymmetric VMHC Cluster Reporting (Table 3, lines 131–133):** Because VMHC correlates each voxel $(+x, y, z)$ with its exact mirror $(-x, y, z)$, the resulting 3D statistic map is identically symmetric across $x = 0$. Yet Table 3 reports `14 voxels` for the right-hemisphere cluster at $(15, 66, -12)$ and `12 voxels` for the left-hemisphere mirror cluster at $(-15, 66, -12)$, and reports a single cluster of `17 voxels` at $(9, 24, -3)$ (right hemisphere) labeled `Olfactory_R/Olfactory_L/Caudate_L` without any symmetric left-hemisphere peak at $(-9, 24, -3)$.
  2. **Mismatch Between Text (`SFG` / `ACG`) and Table 3 (`Frontal_Med_Orb` / `Frontal_Sup_Orb` / `Olfactory` / `Caudate_L`):** Results line 121 states that AACE patients had significantly lower VMHC in the **superior frontal gyrus (SFG)** and **anterior cingulate gyrus (ACG)**, and Discussion lines 150–159 discuss BA 6, 8, 9, and 32 (FEF/SEF) and anterior cingulate depression circuits. However, Table 3 lists only `Frontal_Med_Orb` / `Frontal_Sup_Orb` (BA 11, orbitofrontal cortex) and `Olfactory_R/Olfactory_L/Caudate_L` ( labeled BA 24 at $(9, 24, -3)$). Then, in Results line 140, correlations are reported for the `left caudate` and `bilateral olfactory cortex`, which were never mentioned in the VMHC Results text (line 121), and Discussion line 158 falsely claims that the BDI correlation was with the `anterior cingulate gyrus` rather than the `left olfactory cortex`!

### 3.3. Copy-Paste Template Artifacts (`CSA versus NCs`) and Demographic / Clinical Table Inconsistencies (Pillar 5 — Major)
* **Location:** Section "Participants" (line 36), Section "Results" (line 81), Table 1 (lines 84–109), Table 2 (lines 110–118), and Figure 1 caption (line 124).
* **The Defect:**
  1. **Unedited Acronym from Another Disease (`CSA versus NCs`, line 124):** The caption to Figure 1 states: *"Interhemispheric connectivity in the **CSA** versus **NCs**."* Neither `CSA` nor `NCs` is defined anywhere in the paper (which uses `AACE` and `HC`), indicating unedited reuse of figure captions from another manuscript.
  2. **Contradictory Age of Onset (Line 81 vs. Table 1 Line 105):** Results line 81 states that AACE patients had a *"mean age of onset of 33.28 ± 9.15 years"*, which is their current age at scanning (line 90), whereas Table 1 line 105 reports `Age of onset/year` as `29.66 (9.65)` (consistent with current age $33.28$ minus disease duration $2.99\text{ years}$).
  3. **Test Statistic Discrepancies in Tables 1 and 2:**
     - In Table 1 (line 99), `Fusion range (°)` is $19.97 \pm 8.52$ in AACE ($N = 32$) vs. $25.38 \pm 4.66$ in HC ($N = 31$), which yields $t = -3.140$ ($p = 0.0026$) under a two-sample $t$-test, yet Table 1 reports $t = -1.717$ ($p = 0.095$). Similarly, `Collective fusion (°)` ($8.21 \pm 7.29$ vs. $-4.88 \pm 10.36$, line 97) yields $t = 5.78$ for $N = 32, 31$, yet Table 1 reports $t = 4.095$, and `Diffuse fusion (°)` ($28.86 \pm 6.77$ vs. $13.50 \pm 9.20$, line 98) yields $t = 7.53$, yet Table 1 reports $t = 5.258$. Working backwards from the standard errors shows that synoptophore fusion measurements were only collected on approximately half of the cohort ($\text{df} \approx 28\text{–}30$ instead of $61$), without disclosing the missing data!
     - In Table 2 (lines 114, 117), AACE patients had significantly lower MoCA scores ($27.33 \pm 1.17$ vs. $28.67 \pm 1.54, p = 0.004$) and significantly lower Trait Anxiety scores ($42.07 \pm 5.18$ vs. $45.75 \pm 5.07, p = 0.011$) than HCs, yet neither difference is mentioned anywhere in the Results or Discussion, nor controlled for as a covariate in the fMRI analysis.

## 4. Quantitative Rubric Scoring

* **Rigor (0.6 / 3.5):** Complete omission of the fMRI preprocessing pipeline and symmetrical brain template registration required for VMHC; violation of mathematical midline symmetry in Table 3; unspecified GRF voxel/cluster thresholds yielding 12–17 voxel clusters; unadjusted multiple correlation testing; and undisclosed missing data in Tables 1 and 2.
* **Claims (0.6 / 2.5):** Severe anatomical contradictions between the text/abstract (`superior frontal gyrus` and `anterior cingulate gyrus`) and Table 3/correlations (`orbitofrontal`, `olfactory`, and `left caudate`), plus misattribution of the BDI correlation to the anterior cingulate in the Discussion.
* **Biology (0.8 / 2.0):** The clinical AACE cohort ($n = 32$) is prospectively registered and evaluated with synoptophore and prism cover tests, though key clinical differences in Table 2 (MoCA and STAI) are ignored and the inclusion criteria (line 36) erroneously state that both groups had diplopia/esotropia.
* **Reproducibility (0.8 / 2.0):** Impossible to reproduce the neuroimaging analysis due to complete omission of preprocessing parameters, scan length, filter band, smoothing kernel, and symmetrical registration steps, compounded by copy-paste errors (`CSA versus NCs`) and inconsistent $t$-statistics in Tables 1 and 2.
