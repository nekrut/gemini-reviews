## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This multi-modal pilot study characterizes acute (14-day) SARS-CoV-2 (USA-WA1/2020, $1.3 \times 10^6\text{ TCID}_{50}$) co-infection in seven female rhesus macaques chronically infected (4–8 months) with untreated SIVmac251 (median plasma SIV viral load $5.00\,\log_{10}\text{ copies/mL}$, CD4/CD8 ratio $<1$ in all $7/7$, peripheral $\text{CD4} < 500\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$ in $4/7$). Across longitudinal sampling at $-7, 3, 5, 7, 10,$ and $14\text{ DPI}$, the authors quantify genomic and subgenomic (`Sg-N`, `Sg-E`) viral RNA, infectious virus (`TCID50` and VeroE6-AT culture), lung histopathology (`HALO AI` and `MPO` IHC), 30-plex plasma/BAL cytokines, Spike IgM/IgG ELISAs, D614G pseudovirus neutralization (`ND80`), IFN-$\gamma$/IL-4 T-cell ELISpots, 16S rRNA V3–V4 nasal/tracheal/rectal/stool microbiomes (`QIIME2`/`DADA2`/`ANCOMBC2`), and viral whole-genome sequencing of culture-positive BAL/swab isolates. They report mild clinical disease and clearance of infectious virus from the lower respiratory tract (BAL) by $14\text{ DPI}$, but persistent infectious virus ($3/7$) and subgenomic RNA in nasal/tracheal swabs at $14\text{ DPI}$, blunted IgG class-switching ($2/7$), neutralizing antibody ($1/7$), and T-cell responses ($1/7$ moderate), transient tracheal alpha-diversity elevation at $5\text{ DPI}$, sustained gut *Streptococcus* depletion and *Succinivibrio* enrichment, and recurrent nonsynonymous viral SNPs across animals (`M H125Y`, `ORF6 T10A`, `ORF6 Q56X`, `N A251S`).

**Editorial Verdict: Minor Revision (Strong / Sound).** This is a comprehensive, technically well-executed NHP pilot study with exemplary open-science compliance (all raw viral WGS and 16S rRNA reads deposited in NCBI SRA under `PRJNA1260684` and `PRJNA1260606`, and analysis code on GitHub at `O-Connor-Lab/SIV-COVID-Pilot`). While the authors transparently acknowledge the absence of contemporaneous SIV-negative SARS-CoV-2 control animals in their Discussion (line 31), three specific methodological artifacts in the virological and evolutionary analyses must be addressed: (1) **viral WGS was performed on VeroE6-TMPRSS2/ACE2 *cultured* virus isolates rather than direct clinical swab/BAL RNA** (Methods line 86, Results line 9), meaning that the recurrent mutations highlighted as "intrahost SARS-CoV-2 viral evolution in SIV-infected macaques"—especially the C-terminal truncation `ORF6 Q56X` (present in 6/7 macaques up to 47%) and `ORF6 T10A` (5/7 macaques)—are classic *in vitro* Vero cell-culture adaptation / passage selection artifacts or pre-existing low-level inoculum quasispecies amplified during Vero culture; (2) cross-study comparisons of raw qRT-PCR $\text{copies/mL}$ (genomic N1 and `Sg-E`) at $7$ and $10\text{ DPI}$ against published external cohorts without shared calibration standards or batch-effect controls; and (3) absence of correlation analyses testing whether within-cohort variation in SIV immunosuppression ($\text{CD4}$ counts spanning $274$ to $1,090\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$, a 4-fold range) predicts day-14 nasal viral persistence or antibody generation.

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (SIV-infected rhesus macaques exhibit impaired anti-SARS-CoV-2 adaptive immunity at 14 DPI, with intact IgM [6/7] but severely blunted anti-Spike IgG class switching [2/7], pseudovirus neutralization [1/7], and peripheral IFN-$\gamma$ T-cell responses [1/7]): [Well Supported].**  
  Crucially, unlike the virological qRT-PCR comparisons, the serological and neutralization comparisons in Figure 3D–F were run *side-by-side on the same assay plates* using banked day-14 serum specimens from $n = 15\text{–}21$ SIV-negative WA1-infected control macaques, providing a well-controlled internal benchmark.
* **Claim 2 (SARS-CoV-2 clears from the lower respiratory tract [BAL: 0/7 infectious virus at 14 DPI] but persists in the upper respiratory tract [nasal swabs: 3/7 infectious virus and 7/7 genomic RNA at 14 DPI]): [Moderately Supported].**  
  Within the 7-animal cohort, the contrast between lower-respiratory clearance and upper-respiratory recovery of live infectious virus at 14 DPI in 3/7 animals is solid. However, the claim that 7–10 DPI nasal viral RNA loads are "significantly higher" than in SIV-negative macaques relies on comparing qRT-PCR $\text{copies/mL}$ directly against external published datasets (Chandrashekar et al. and elsewhere, line 121) assayed on different PCR instruments/extractions without shared international standards.
* **Claim 3 (SIV+/SARS-CoV-2+ co-infection induces sustained gut depletion of *Streptococcus* and enrichment of *Succinivibrio*, alongside a transient 5 DPI increase in tracheal Shannon diversity): [Moderately Supported].**  
  The 16S workflow uses appropriate negative extraction controls, `DADA2` denoising, and mixed-effects `ANCOMBC2` with animal random intercepts and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR adjustment across both rectal swabs and stool. However, without an unchallenged SIV+ control group undergoing repeated ketamine sedation and serial swabbing/BAL every 2–3 days (`-7, 0, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14 DPI`), effects of repeated ketamine anesthesia, handling stress, and hospital diet on gut *Streptococcus*/*Succinivibrio* cannot be separated from viral co-infection.
* **Claim 4 (Convergent emergence of `ORF6 Q56X`, `ORF6 T10A`, `M H125Y`, and `N A251S` across 5–6 of 7 macaques demonstrates SIV-driven intrahost SARS-CoV-2 evolution): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Look closely at Methods lines 86 and 91 and Results line 9:
  * Swabs and BAL were first inoculated onto `VeroE56AT cells` (Vero E6 expressing hACE2 and TMPRSS2) to recover live virus, and **"Virus-positive cultures were collected for whole genome viral sequencing"** (line 86).
  * Furthermore, in Methods line 91, the authors state: *"SNPs found in only one sample or those present in the inoculating virus and indels were removed."* Yet in Results line 9, they state that two silent SNPs *present in the inoculating virus* (`C4897T` at $5.2\%$ and `A27924C` at $7.8\%$) were retained and enriched in $6/7$ and $7/7$ animals!
  * Even more importantly, `ORF6` is an accessory interferon-antagonist protein that is dispensable and rapidly selected *against* in interferon-deficient Vero E6 cell culture (where C-terminal stop codons like `ORF6 Q56X` and missense variants already present at $<1\%\text{–}3\%$ in BEI Resources WA1 Vero stocks amplify in a single culture passage!). Observing the *exact same four nucleotide mutations* (`M H125Y`, `ORF6 T10A`, `ORF6 Q56X`, `N A251S`) arising "independently" by day 3 and day 14 across 5 to 6 out of 7 animals (when adaptive immunity was absent!) is the classic signature of parallel amplification of pre-existing sub-3% inoculum quasispecies during *in vitro* Vero cell culture recovery, not *de novo* SIV-driven host evolution.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

1. **Pillar 2, 3 & 6 Violation — Sequencing of *In Vitro* Vero-Cultured Virus Instead of Direct Clinical Specimens Confounds "Intrahost Evolution" with Inoculum Quasispecies & Culture Adaptation (Methods lines 86, 91; Results line 9; Discussion lines 28–29):**  
   Sequencing viral RNA harvested from VeroE6-ACE2-TMPRSS2 cell culture supernatants (after up to several days of *in vitro* cytopathic expansion) rather than sequencing RNA extracted directly from the primary nasal/throat swabs and BAL fluid introduces severe *in vitro* bottlenecking and positive selection for Vero-adapted variants (such as `ORF6` truncations). Moreover, the BEI Resources USA-WA1/2020 stock (`NR-53872`) is a polyclonal clinical isolate passaged in Vero cells that is well-known to harbor minor sub-consensus variants at $0.1\%\text{–}5\%$ frequency. Because the variant caller cutoff was set to $\ge 3\%$ (`minimum allele frequency of 0.03`, line 91), any stock variant present at $1\%\text{–}2.9\%$ in the inoculum (below the $3\%$ filter in the inoculum sequencing run) that reaches $\ge 3\%$ after Vero culture of animal swabs is falsely classified as a *de novo* mutation arising independently in 6 of 7 animals!
2. **Pillar 5 Violation — Uncalibrated Cross-Study qRT-PCR Viral Load Comparisons (Results line 121, Supplementary Figure 6):**  
   In line 121, the authors compare absolute $\text{copies/mL}$ of genomic N1 RNA and `Sg-E` RNA at $7$ and $10\text{ DPI}$ against external published datasets (`ref 25, 26`) that used different swab elution volumes, RNA extraction kits, and standard curves. Unlike the ELISA/neutralization assays (where historical control sera were re-assayed side-by-side on the same plates), cross-laboratory qRT-PCR $\text{copies/mL}$ values frequently differ by $0.5\text{–}1.5\,\log_{10}$ due to standard-curve quantification differences alone.
3. **Pillar 4 Violation — Omission of Within-Cohort Immunosuppression Stratification ($\text{CD4}$ Count vs. Viral Persistence):**  
   Although all 7 animals had $\text{CD4/CD8} < 1$, baseline peripheral $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell counts varied nearly 4-fold across the cohort, from $274$ to $1,090\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$ ($3/7$ animals had normal CD4 counts $>500\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$, line 114). The manuscript never tests or reports whether the 3 animals with infectious nasal virus at $14\text{ DPI}$ (or the 5 animals that failed to make anti-Spike IgG) were the animals with low $\text{CD4}$ counts ($<500\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$) and high SIV viremia ($\sim 10^6\text{ copies/mL}$).
4. **Pillar 7 Violation — Minor Typos and Internal Contradictions:**  
   * **Contradiction on inoculum SNP filtering:** Line 91 states *"SNPs found in only one sample or those present in the inoculating virus and indels were removed,"* whereas Results line 9 and Figure 6 explicitly retain and highlight two silent SNPs present in the inoculating virus (`C4897T` at $5.2\%$ and `A27924C` at $7.8\%$).
   * **Amino acid mutation typo:** Discussion line 29 refers to `The A215S mutation in the nucleocapsid phosphoprotein` in the first sentence and `the A251V mutation... This suggests that the 251 site...` in the next sentence (Results line 9 writes `N, A251S`).
   * **Cytokine and p-value typos:** Line 138 writes `CXCL/IL-88, a neutrophil chemoattractant` instead of `CXCL8/IL-8`. Figure 5 legend (line 4 of second chunk) writes `* p <0.5` instead of `* p < 0.05`.

### Constructive and Doable Suggestions for Improvement

1. **Distinguish Vero Culture / Inoculum Quasispecies Amplification from *De Novo* Intrahost Evolution:**  
   * **WHY:** Sequencing virus after recovery in VeroE6-ACE2-TMPRSS2 culture (line 86) selects for *in vitro* culture adaptations (such as `ORF6 Q56X` C-terminal truncation) and amplifies pre-existing sub-3% variants in the BEI `NR-53872` WA1 stock, explaining why the exact same 4 mutations appeared across 5–6 of 7 macaques as early as 3 DPI.  
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Inspect the un-filtered BAM files of the inoculating virus (`PRJNA1260606`) at positions corresponding to `M H125Y`, `ORF6 T10A`, `ORF6 Q56X`, and `N A251S` down to $0.05\%$ read depth (below the $3\%$ `iVar` filter) to determine if they pre-existed in the challenge stock, and revise the Results/Discussion to explicitly note that sequencing was performed on Vero-cultured isolates rather than unpassaged primary swabs.
2. **Correlate Baseline SIV Immunosuppression ($\text{CD4}$ Count and SIV Viremia) with Day-14 SARS-CoV-2 Persistence:**  
   * **WHY:** Because the cohort lacks contemporaneous SIV$-$ controls, the 4-fold internal gradient in baseline $\text{CD4}$ count ($274\text{–}1,090\text{ cells}/\mu\text{L}$) and 100-fold range in SIV viral load ($4.08\text{–}6.03\,\log_{10}$) provides a direct internal dose-response test of the immunosuppression hypothesis.  
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Report Spearman rank correlations (or Mann-Whitney comparisons between the 4 animals with $\text{CD4} < 500$ vs. 3 animals with $\text{CD4} > 500$) against 14 DPI nasal viral load, infectious virus recovery, and anti-Spike IgG titer.
3. **Fix Editorial Typos:**  
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Correct `CXCL/IL-88` $\to$ `CXCL8/IL-8` (line 138), `A215S` $\to$ `A251S` (Discussion line 29), `* p <0.5` $\to$ `* p < 0.05` (Figure 5 legend), and clarify the inoculum SNP filtering statement on line 91.
