Open-Review Megajournals (n = 50: 5.01 ± 1.69 / 10)

Despite open peer review (126 human reviewers, median 2,462 words; 48.0% strict failure <5.0), 38% of human review records are Superficial/Boilerplate and 58% (29/50) exhibit anomalies: generic/LLM-leaked boilerplate (19), coercive citation demands (6; e.g., OPEN_ANON_001, OPEN_ANON_011), or single-reviewer acceptances (4; e.g., OPEN_ANON_036), missing 213 critical flaws (4.3/paper).

1. First-Wave Pandemic Gold-Rush Papers (100–156 Cites)

Example: TIME_ANON_0224 (Pathogens 2020, 3.7/10, 156 cites) claimed SARS-CoV-2 is a MERS-CoV recombinant from a 7-taxon ClustalW alignment; TIME_ANON_0922 (2.2/10, 139 cites) docked Lopinavir+Ritonavir simultaneously as a single 1,300 Da molecule.

2. Useful Gene Catalog + Broken Downstream Math (73–158 Cites)

Example: TIME_ANON_08 (BMC Evol Biol 2005, 4.8/10, 158 cites) curated relaxin genes but plotted PAML's dN/dS = 99 division-by-zero code (`dS = 0`) as real ω = 99 positive selection; TIME_ANON_0605 (4.9/10, 73 cites) cataloged 209 millet MYB genes with a copy-pasted "MAPK" conclusion.

3. Prestige-Halo Provocative Claims (62–556 Cites)

Example: SP_ANON_109 (Nature 2016, 4.1/10, 556 cites) inferred ongoing HIV replication under ART from the first 6 months of viral decay in 3 patients; TIME_ANON_0867 (Mol Cancer 2004, 4.5/10, 111 cites) claimed cisplatin induces de novo methylation despite 0/18 genes methylated in vitro.

4. Modern Buzzword-Combo Templates (15–39 Cites/Yr)

Example: 2024–2025 papers combining scRNA-seq + Mendelian randomization + LASSO Cox + molecular docking (e.g., C21 at 2.8/10, 39 cites; C86 at 4.4/10, 36 cites) cited rapidly as introduction filler by other template papers.
