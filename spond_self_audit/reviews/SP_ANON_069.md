# Blinded Review: SP_ANON_069
**Composite Score:** 7.2 / 10.0 (Rigor: 2.4/3.5 | Claims: 1.8/2.5 | Biology: 1.4/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.6/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Unsampled Tissue Reservoir Confounding in Discrete-Trait Phyloanatomy & Small Sample Size ($N=3$)
**Verdict:** Minor Revision
**Study Modality:** Hybrid Computational + Wet-Lab Functional Validation
**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
This hybrid experimental and computational study investigates the role of circulating monocytes in within-host HIV-1 CRF01_AE diversity and peripheral blood viral dispersion. From a cohort of 50 cART-naive Thai individuals (CD4 $<350$ cells/$\mu$l), the authors sort peripheral blood CD14+/CD16+ monocytes and CD3+ CD16- T lymphocytes to $>98\%$ FACS purity in 13 of 22 tested participants, amplify cell-associated and plasma viral *env* gp120 RNA via limiting-dilution Single Genome Sequencing (SGS) in 10 participants, and obtain $\ge 10$ sequences per compartment (plasma, monocytes, T cells) in 3 high-viral-load individuals (`P01`, `P02`, `P13`; VL $>10^5$ copies/ml). Using compartment-subsampled BEAST discrete-trait asymmetric diffusion models (BSSVS with Hierarchical Phylogenetic Models across 3 subsampling replicates) and a branch-partitioned Fixed Effects Likelihood codon test (`contrast-FEL`), the authors report lack of strict phylogenetic compartmentalization among peripheral blood compartments, statistically supported viral transitions (Bayes Factor $>3$) from monocytes to plasma and from plasma to peripheral T cells across all 3 participants, and 0 to 5 sites per patient under differential selection across compartments.

The wet-lab protocols—FACS sorting to $>98\%$ purity, limiting-dilution SGS of viral RNA (rather than proviral DNA, avoiding archived defective proviruses), and strict physical amplicon-free room separation—are executed to a high technical standard. However, the phyloanatomic inference rests on $N = 3$ individuals (two of whom, `P02` and `P13`, are cross-sectional single-timepoint samples with a fixed external evolutionary rate prior) and suffers from a classic discrete-trait phyloanatomy confounder: because productive lymphoid and tissue-resident reservoirs (lymph nodes, gut lamina propria, bone marrow) were not sampled, transitions originating from unsampled tissue CD4+ T cells or macrophages into plasma and circulating cells must be absorbed by the three observed peripheral blood states.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Discrete-Trait BSSVS Phyloanatomy with Unsampled Source Compartments ("Ghost Demes") [Fragile]:** The BEAST BSSVS discrete-trait model restricts ancestral node states to the three sampled peripheral blood compartments `{Plasma, Circulating Monocytes, Circulating T cells}`. However, $>98\%$ of productive HIV-1 replication occurs in lymphoid tissues (lymph nodes and gut-associated lymphoid tissue), whereas circulating peripheral blood CD3+ T cells are predominantly quiescent/resting. When cell-free plasma virus is produced by unsampled lymphoid tissue CD4+ T cells and circulating monocytes have longer lifespans or distinct archival kinetics (coalescing deeper in the tree, as seen in P13 where the root is assigned to monocytes), the discrete-trait CTMC is forced to assign deep internal nodes to one of the three sampled blood states and infer directed flow (`Monocytes -> Plasma -> Peripheral T cells`). While the authors acknowledge in the Discussion that peripheral T cells are mostly quiescent and deep tissues were not sampled, claiming in the Abstract that "Bayesian phyloanatomic analysis of three of these individuals demonstrated a measurable role for these cells, but not the circulating T-cell population, as a source of cell-free virus in the plasma" overstates what a 3-state closed-system transition matrix can prove in the presence of unsampled primary tissue reservoirs.
* **Cross-Sectional Clock Calibration in `P02` and `P13` and Lenient Bayes Factor Threshold [Moderately Supported]:** Only `P01` had longitudinal sequences at 0 and 12 months (due to cART non-adherence), passing a clustered tip-date randomization test. `P02` and `P13` had monocyte sequences only at baseline (Visit 1, single timepoint), requiring tree times to be calibrated entirely from an external prior rate ($6.88 \times 10^{-4}$ subs/site/month) and a constant population size prior (caption to Fig. 1 states "constant population size over time" whereas Section 2 states "Bayesian skyline demographic models"). Furthermore, the threshold for calling directed migration rates "statistically significant" is $\text{BF} > 3$ (Section 2), which represents only "positive/substantial" rather than strong ($\text{BF} > 10$ or $> 20$) Bayesian evidence.
* **Subsampling Replicates and `contrast-FEL` Permutation Control [Well Supported]:** Subsampling compartments down to the minimum compartment size ($n=15, 20, 10$ for P01, P02, P13) across 3 replicates with an HPM prior mitigates tip-state frequency bias in BSSVS. Likewise, requiring `contrast-FEL` sites to pass both Holm-Bonferroni corrected LRTs and 20 branch-label permutations appropriately controls false positives, yielding 0 sites in P01, 5 in P02, and 1 in P13.
* **Typographical Error in Statistical Methods [Minor]:** In Section 2 (line 39), the text states: "Statistical significance for Critchlow's correlation coefficient (Slatkin 1989) was determined using a null distribution of permuted sequences (1,000 permutations). A P-value of $\ge 0.05$ was considered significant." (Should be $\le 0.05$). In Table 1 (line 52), P01's 12-month SE is formatted as `3,6435`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Rigorous Wet-Lab Isolation of Monocyte HIV-1 RNA via SGS [Well Supported]:** Isolating cell-associated HIV-1 gp120 RNA via limiting-dilution SGS from $>98\%$ pure sorted CD14+/CD16+ monocytes across 10/13 viremic patients and demonstrating that stop-codon frequency in plasma RNA (1/657 = 0.15%) is tenfold lower than in cellular RNA (~1.8–2.0%) reflects careful experimental execution.
* **Alternative Biological Explanations for Monocyte-Associated Viral RNA [Moderately Supported]:** As the authors candidly discuss in Section 4, detecting gp120 RNA inside sorted monocytes does not by itself prove productive reverse transcription, integration, and virion budding from monocytes *in vivo*, because monocytes/macrophages can bind or phagocytose virions or infected cell debris. The authors' topological argument against phagocytosis (that T-cell-to-monocyte flow was only supported in 1 of 3 patients, whereas plasma-to-monocyte and monocyte-to-plasma flows were supported in all 3) is consistent with virion uptake from plasma, but bidirectional `Plasma <-> Monocyte` exchange is also what would be expected if monocytes capture cell-free plasma virions without productive replication.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Exemplary Open Data and Code Availability:** All 1,079 SGS sequences are deposited in GenBank (`MK842155–MK843233`), annotated alignments, ML trees, and `contrast-FEL` 3D structural mappings are hosted at `thai.hyphy.org`, and BEAST XML/prior configurations are available on GitHub (`https://github.com/brmagalis/HIV_THAI_COHORT`).
