### 1. Executive Summary
This study sequences 94 complete coding genomes of Venezuelan equine encephalitis (VEE) complex alphaviruses and combines them with 36 published genomes (126 VEE complex isolates and 4 EEEV outgroup sequences) to investigate the molecular clock dating, purifying selection bias, and phylogeographic structure of the VEE complex. Using SLAC ($d_N/d_S = 0.057$), MEME (identifying 23 codons at nominal $p < 0.05$), and branch-site random effects likelihood models (BSREL and adaptive aBSREL) compared against GTR$+\Gamma_4$ branch lengths, the authors show that strong purifying selection causes nucleotide substitution models to severely underestimate deep branch lengths across the VEE complex. By iteratively pruning basal clades until GTR$+\Gamma_4$ and aBSREL tree lengths converge, the authors restrict BEAST relaxed-clock tMRCA interpretation to shallow, un-saturated terminal clades (e.g., Clade K in ID/IAB/IC and Clades R and T in subtype IE), corroborate the post-1943 vaccine-origin hypothesis for subtype IAB outbreaks, and identify an enrichment of structural polyprotein synapomorphies along the subtype IE stem branch potentially linked to specialization on *Culex (Melanoconion) taeniopus*.

This is a valuable genomic dataset paired with a thoughtful application of codon-based branch-length expansion (BSREL vs. GTR$+\Gamma_4$) to diagnose molecular clock compression under purifying selection. However, several statistical and methodological issues temper the quantitative conclusions: (1) an arithmetic discrepancy in the reported subtype IE synapomorphy counts ($39 + 37 = 76 \ne 63$); (2) absence of a formal statistical threshold for when GTR$+\Gamma_4$ vs. aBSREL branch-length underestimation is deemed "unbiased" for BEAST dating; (3) reporting 23 MEME sites at nominal $p < 0.05$ across $\sim 3,700$ codons without false discovery rate correction; and (4) alignment availability restricted to "available upon request."

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Ad Hoc Criterion for "Unbiased" Subtree Dating via BSREL vs. GTR$+\Gamma_4$ (Pillars 3 & 4):** The authors' core methodological strategy is to prune basal branches iteratively and compare total subtree length under BSREL/aBSREL against GTR$+\Gamma_4$ (Figure 2), asserting that subtrees lying near the $x = y$ diagonal can be reliably dated in BEAST under standard nucleotide models. However, no quantitative criterion (e.g., percent relative error, likelihood ratio test, or overlap of 95% HPDs) is defined to classify a clade as "supported by BSREL" (emboldened in Figures 3 and 4). Furthermore, BEAST analyses were still run on the broader subtype alignments (ID/II/IAB/IC together, and all IE together) where basal branches *were* distorted, meaning the inferred relaxed-clock mean rate and demographic prior across the tree remained influenced by the deeper branches.
* **Arithmetic Contradiction in Subtype IE Synapomorphy Test (Pillar 7):** In the Results (line 70) and Discussion (line 134), the authors state that VEEV subtype IE (Group H) exhibits a statistically significant excess of synapomorphic mutations in the structural protein genes ($p < 0.05$, Fisher's exact test). However, line 134 states: *"we observed 63 synapomorphic mutations associated with the IE subtype (Group H). Of these, 39 were in the non-structural protein genes and 37 in the structural genes."* Not only does $39 + 37 = 76 \ne 63$, but the text also does not state whether "synapomorphic mutations" refers to all nucleotide substitutions or only non-synonymous amino acid substitutions (given that the non-structural ORF is $\sim 7.5\text{ kb}$ and structural ORF is $\sim 3.8\text{ kb}$, $39$ vs. $37$ is roughly a $1:1$ count on a $2:1$ target size, which would be significant, whereas $39$ non-structural out of $63$ total leaves $24$ structural, which is proportional to ORF length $24/63 = 38.1\%$ vs. $\sim 33.6\%$ structural length and gives Fisher's exact $p \approx 0.45$). This numerical contradiction directly affects a headline claim of the paper.
* **Uncorrected Multiple Testing in Site-Level Selection Scan (Pillar 4):** Table 2 reports 23 codons under episodic positive selection via MEME at nominal $p < 0.05$ across the concatenated non-structural and structural polyproteins ($\sim 3,750$ codons). Only two sites (nsP4 position 2397 at $p < 0.001$ and E2 position 547 at $p = 0.001$) have $p \le 0.001$; under Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control Across $\sim 3,750$ codons, almost all of the 23 sites (19 of which have $0.01 < p < 0.05$) are indistinguishable from expected false positives under the null ($3750 \times 0.05 \approx 187$ expected under complete uniformity, or $\sim 15\text{--}25$ at variable sites).
* **Phylogenetic Bootstrap Mis-specification (Pillar 3):** In the Methods (line 53), the authors infer a maximum likelihood tree in PAUP* under GTR$+\Gamma_4+$I, but assess topological support using *"1,000 replicate neighbor-joining trees"* rather than ML bootstrapping.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Purifying Selection Bias in Deep Alphavirus tMRCA Estimation [Well Supported]:** The demonstration that GTR$+\Gamma_4$ severely underestimates internal branch lengths relative to codon-based aBSREL models across deep VEE complex splits is methodologically sound and consistent with known saturation of synonymous sites under strong purifying selection ($d_N/d_S = 0.057$).
* **Single Vaccine-Associated Origin of Subtype IAB Outbreaks [Well Supported]:** Complete genome phylogeny confirms that post-1943 IAB isolates cluster tightly ($96.08\text{--}99.23\%$ identity) with basal 1942/1950 Peruvian strains and lack interspersed enzootic ID progenitors, unlike polyphyletic IC emergence events.
* **Subtype IE Structural Protein Adaptation to *Culex taeniopus* [Fragile]:** While the vector specificity of subtype IE for *Cx. (Mel.) taeniopus* is well grounded in prior experimental midgut-infection studies, the genomic enrichment claim rests on the unresolved numerical contradiction ($63$ total vs. $39 + 37 = 76$) and lacks ancestral vs. derived codon-model testing (e.g., branch-site $d_N/d_S$ on the IE stem branch).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Data & Code Availability:** The 94 newly sequenced genomes are deposited in GenBank (accessions in S1 Table). However, the trimmed codon alignment (from which the hypervariable 3' nsP3 and 5' capsid regions were manually excised) is stated as *"available upon request"* (line 51), and exact coordinates of the trimmed nsP3/capsid regions and BEAST XML / HyPhy batch files are not archived.
* **Reference Strain Numbering:** Table 2 header states amino acid positions are defined using *"prototype VEEV strain 3880"* in the text (line 88) but *"Genome sequence 3908 (Accession number U55350)"* in the Table 2 caption (line 91).
