### 1. Executive Summary
This study evaluates whether partitioning HIV-1 and SIV `gp120` nucleotide alignments by experimental RNA secondary structure reactivity (`SHAPE` from reference strains $\text{HIV-1}_{\text{NL4-3}}$ and $\text{SIV}_{\text{mac239}}$) and/or phylogenetic comparative pairing probabilities (`RNA-Decoder` $P_{\text{prob}}$) improves evolutionary model fit (`BEAST` marginal likelihoods via stepping-stone/path sampling) and clarifies site-wise patterns of evolutionary rate, transition/transversion ratio ($\kappa$), and GC content across intra-host ($n=8$ HIV-1 patients, $n=8$ SIV macaques) and inter-host datasets. The authors report that structure-informed partitions alone perform substantially worse than a standard structure-agnostic 4-category gamma ($\Gamma_4$) rate model, whereas restricting trend analyses to sites where `SHAPE` and $P_{\text{prob}}$ agree ("Combined" concordant quartiles) recovers expected negative relationships between structural stability and substitution rate. The study is thoughtfully executed and commendably honest about the negative Bayes factor results for structure-based partitioning, though the "Combined" concordant analysis introduces selection bias by discarding discordant sites.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Rigorous Model Comparison Demonstrating Inferiority of Structure-Only Partitions:** Using path sampling (`PS`) and stepping-stone (`SS`) marginal likelihood estimation in `BEAST` across 200 empirical posterior trees per subject, the authors benchmark `SHAPE`-quartiles, $P_{\text{prob}}$-quartiles, paired/unpaired (`Fold`), and intra-helical stacking (`Stack`) partition models against an uninformed $\Gamma_4$ partition model and a random-quartile control (`Rand`). They transparently show that $\Gamma_4$ outperforms all RNA structure partition models (`BF > 10`), and that `SHAPE` and $P_{\text{prob}}$ partitions barely outperform random site partitioning in several patients—appropriately concluding that protein-level selection and site-to-site rate heterogeneity dominate over RNA secondary structure alone.
* **Non-Identical Site Subsets in the "Combined" Concordant Model (Attrition/Selection Bias):** To reconcile discrepancies between biochemical `SHAPE` reactivity and evolutionary `RNA-Decoder` pairing probabilities, the authors define four "concordant" structural stability categories (where `SHAPE` and $P_{\text{prob}}$ quartiles agree) and explicitly discard all remaining "discordant" nucleotide sites (`Category 5`, comprising the majority of positions in variable loops and several constant regions; page/line 17). Because `RNA-Decoder` $P_{\text{prob}}$ is itself inferred from sequence conservation and compensatory covarying substitutions in the alignment, filtering for sites with high $P_{\text{prob}}$ and low `SHAPE` automatically selects for evolutionarily conserved codons and compares non-identical site subsets against the full-alignment `SHAPE` and $P_{\text{prob}}$ models.
* **Extrapolation of Single-Strain *In Vitro* `SHAPE` Reactivities Across Divergent Subtypes and Longitudinal Quasispecies:** Experimental `SHAPE` profiles were measured on single reference clones ($\text{HIV-1}_{\text{NL4-3}}$ and $\text{SIV}_{\text{mac239}}$). While reasonable for macaques inoculated directly with the $\text{SIV}_{\text{mac239}}$/$\text{SIV}_{\text{mac251}}$ swarm, extrapolating $\text{NL4-3}$ base-by-base `SHAPE` reactivities across divergent inter-host HIV-1 group M subtypes (and across hypervariable `V1–V5` loops undergoing indels and local refolding) assumes structural invariance that breaks down outside conserved elements like the `RRE`.
* **Internal-Branch `HyPhy` Parameter Estimation:** Estimating partition-specific evolutionary rates and $\kappa$ (`Ts/Tv`) along internal branches of the MCC tree using profile likelihood confidence intervals in `HyPhy` is a sound strategy to mitigate transient deleterious mutations on terminal branches.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *Partitioning `gp120` solely by `SHAPE` reactivity or `RNA-Decoder` pairing probability provides a poorer fit to sequence evolution than standard uninformed gamma rate variation ($\Gamma_4$).* **`[Well Supported]`** — Consistently demonstrated across intra-host and inter-host HIV-1 and SIV datasets via `PS`/`SS` Bayes factors.
* **Claim 2:** *Concordant sites (where both chemical `SHAPE` probing and comparative $P_{\text{prob}}$ indicate high stability) exhibit lower substitution rates, higher $\kappa$ (transition bias), and higher paired GC content.* **`[Moderately Supported]`** — The linear trends across the four concordant categories are statistically significant, but partly circular because `RNA-Decoder` uses sequence conservation and transition-mediated base-pair maintenance (`G:U` intermediates) to assign high $P_{\text{prob}}$ in the first place.
* **Claim 3:** *Sliding-window correlation between $1 - \text{SHAPE}$ and $P_{\text{prob}}$ identifies conserved functional RNA structures across `gp120` constant/variable regions analogous to the Rev-Response Element (`RRE`).* **`[Moderately Supported]`** — Validated on the `RRE` positive control, though window correlations in `gp120` are modest ($r_s < 0.39$ across most regions) and lack direct mutagenesis validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Sequence alignments and custom `HyPhy` scripts are deposited in public GitHub repositories (`[GITHUB_REPO_REDACTED]` and `pubs/tree/master/HIV-RNA`), with full GenBank accession lists and `IQ-TREE` likelihood-mapping quartet diagnostics in the Supplement.
* **Deficiencies:** The exact proportion and coordinates of discarded "discordant" sites in each patient alignment should be tabulated in the main text alongside the concordant category results.
