### 1. Executive Summary
This study applies a comprehensive suite of comparative codon and amino acid selection models (`GARD`, `Contrast-FEL`, `BUSTED-PH`, `RELAX`, `MEME`, `FEL`, `FADE`, and `PCOC`) to full-length feline coronavirus (`FCoV`) coding regions (`Spike-1` $n=39$, `Spike-2` $n=8$, `ORF3a/b/c`, and `ORF7a/b`) from clinically annotated enteric (`FECV`) and lethal feline infectious peritonitis (`FIPV`) isolates, supplemented by `AlphaFold2` structural modeling of `ORF3c`. The authors identify two `Spike-1` sites evolving under significantly different selective pressures between `FIPV` and `FECV` (`Contrast-FEL` $q \le 0.20$: site `789` at P4 of the S1/S2 furin cleavage site and site `1,058` near the fusion domain, which also shows convergent evolution by `PCOC`), relaxation of purifying selection across the S1/S2 furin cleavage partition in `FIPV`, and 24 positively selected sites in `ORF7b` associated with `FECV`. The evolutionary pipeline is thoughtfully designed (accounting for recombination via `GARD` and small-sample asymptotics via parametric bootstrap in `FEL`/`MEME`), with caveats centered on the tiny `Spike-2` sample size ($n=8$) and the conditional post-selection logic of `BUSTED-PH` gatekeeping.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Proper Recombination Partitioning (`GARD`) and Parametric Bootstrap Calibration:** Recognizing that `FCoV` undergoes extensive homologous recombination, the authors screen all alignments with `GARD` (identifying 13 recombination-free partitions in `Spike-1` and 8 in `Spike-2`) and infer separate `RAxML-NG` trees per partition. Furthermore, to guard against asymptotic $\chi^2$ false positives on small alignments, site-level `MEME` and `FEL` tests use parametric bootstrap ($N=500$) likelihood ratio tests ($p \le 0.05$).
* **Conditional Gatekeeping by `BUSTED-PH` Prior to Site-Level Scans:** In the analysis workflow (lines 37–51), the authors first run gene/partition-level `BUSTED-PH` to classify whether episodic selection is statistically associated with `FIPV` ($p_{\text{FIPV}} \le 0.05$, $p_{\text{FECV}} > 0.05$, $p_{\text{diff}} \le 0.05$), `FECV`, or neither ("FCoV-wide"). Only when a partition passes `BUSTED-PH` for `FIPV` are site-level `MEME`/`FEL`/`FADE` tests restricted to `FIPV` branches and reported as "sites under positive selection associated with the `FIPV` phenotype" (`15` sites in `Spike`, `11` novel). However, for those specific individual sites within a `BUSTED-PH`-positive partition, `MEME`/`FEL` on `FIPV` branches only tests whether $\omega_{\text{FIPV}} > 1$ (against $\omega_{\text{FIPV}} \le 1$), *not* whether $\omega_{\text{FIPV}} \neq \omega_{\text{FECV}}$ at that site (which is what `Contrast-FEL` tests, finding only 2 sites: `789` and `1,058`). Indeed, inspection of Table 2 shows that several "FIPV-associated" or "FECV-associated" sites identified by `MEME`/`FEL`/`FADE` have nearly identical amino acid compositions in both biotypes (e.g., `Spike-1` site `64`: `FIPV G14 A3 D1 S1` vs. `FECV G17 S1 D1 A1`; site `744`: `FIPV T18 N1` vs. `FECV T18 N2`; site `808` "FECV div. sel.": `FIPV Y19` vs. `FECV Y20`, where the site is $100\%$ invariant `Y` at the amino acid level!). Flagging an amino-acid-invariant codon (`808: FIPV Y19, FECV Y20`) as undergoing positive diversifying selection ("`FECV div. sel.`") in Table 2 indicates either a coordinate off-by-one mapping error when projecting alignment positions onto ungapped reference `FJ938054` or a numerical artifact when $d_S = 0$.
* **Severely Underpowered `Spike-2` Sample Size ($n=8$):** The `Spike-2` dataset contains only 8 unique full-length sequences total across both biotypes, split across 8 `GARD` partitions. Inferring trees, branch-site random-effects distributions (`BUSTED-PH`, `RELAX`), and directional selection (`FADE`) on 8 tips has minimal statistical power and high topological uncertainty.
* **`FADE` Empirical Bayes Factor Threshold ($\text{EBF} \ge 10$):** For directional selection (`FADE`), the authors use a lenient threshold of $\text{EBF} \ge 10$ (compared to the more standard $\text{EBF} \ge 100$), which allows two substitutions along a clade (e.g., `E -> V(2)` at site `516`, or `T -> N(2)` at site `744`) to be called significant directional selection.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *`Spike-1` codon `789` (P4 of the S1/S2 furin cleavage site) and codon `1,058` (fusion domain–HR1 connector) evolve under significantly different selective pressures between `FIPV` and `FECV`, with `1,058` also exhibiting strong convergent evolution (`M1058L`) in `FIPV`.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Concordantly supported by `Contrast-FEL` ($q \le 0.20$), `PCOC` ($PP > 0.80$), `RELAX` on `RFP 7`, and prior clinical literature.
* **Claim 2:** *Purifying selection on the S1/S2 furin cleavage region (`RFP 7`) and `ORF7b` is relaxed in `FIPV` relative to `FECV`, consistent with loss of requirement for furin cleavage and enteric immune evasion following macrophage tropism shift.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Supported by partition-level `RELAX` and amino acid diversity distributions across the P6–P1' furin motif.
* **Claim 3:** *Specific novel sites listed in Table 2 (such as `Spike-1` site `808`, which is listed as `Y19` in `FIPV` and `Y20` in `FECV`) are undergoing diversifying positive selection associated with `FECV` or `FIPV`.* **`[Fragile]`** — Conditional `MEME`/`FEL`/`FADE` calls inside `BUSTED-PH` partitions do not prove site-level differential selection between biotypes, and Table 2 contains at least one site (`808`) that is completely monomorphic (`Y`) in the reported amino acid count column.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Exemplary open-science reproducibility: all partitioned alignments, labeled Newick trees (`data.hyphy.org/web/FCOV/data/`), `BUSTED-PH` batch files, and interactive 3D structural notebooks (`observablehq.com/@jzehr/fipv1-sites-mapped`) are publicly archived.
* **Deficiencies:** The amino acid composition vs. positive diversifying selection call at `Spike-1` site `808` (`FIPV: Y19, FECV: Y20`) in Table 2 should be audited for index-mapping accuracy.
