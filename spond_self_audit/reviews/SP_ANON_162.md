# Blinded Review: SP_ANON_162
**Composite Score:** 5.4 / 10.0 (Rigor: 1.6/3.5 | Claims: 1.2/2.5 | Biology: 1.0/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.6/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Alignment-Gap Denominator Inflation, Unaligned Disordered-Tail Confounding in AlphaFold2 Comparisons & Genomic Coordinate/Table Errors
**Verdict:** Major Revision
**Study Modality:** Dry-Lab Secondary Cohort/Database Re-Analysis
**Technology Maturity:** Commodity/Hype-Cycle Template
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
`SP_ANON_162` applies an automated molecular evolution and protein structure prediction workflow (`HyPhy` selection suite `FEL`, `MEME`, `SLAC`, `BUSTED+S+MH`, `BGM` coevolution, and an `AlphaFold2`/`ColabFold` + `TM-align` ancestral structure pipeline termed "SPA") to 175 mammalian orthologs of `Rem2` (Rad and Gem-like GTP-binding protein 2). While the open-source data/code sharing on GitHub is commendable and the general conclusion that the core RGK GTPase domain of mammalian `Rem2` evolves under purifying selection is uncontroversial, the manuscript exhibits major analytical flaws: (1) an indel-inflated alignment (`627 codon sites` for a ~340-aa human protein) where gap-dominated columns are misclassified as "251 (40%) invariant nucleotide sites"; (2) global `TM-score` and backbone `RMSD` comparisons across full-length `AlphaFold2` models dominated by unaligned, low-confidence intrinsically disordered regions (`pLDDT < 50`); (3) mixed `GRCh37`/`GRCh38` genomic coordinates in `Table 3` labeled as `hg38`; and (4) internal contradictions and typographical errors in reporting `BUSTED` and `FEL` results.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Indel-Inflated Alignment Denominator & Misclassification of Gap Columns as "Invariant Nucleotide Sites" (`Methodologically Flawed`):** Human `Rem2` (`NM_173527.3`) is only **340 amino acids** long (with a 169-aa core RGK GTPase domain in crystal structure `3CBQ`), yet the 175-species `MAFFT` codon alignment spans **627 codon sites** (`Lines 72, 88`) due to lineage-specific insertions and RefSeq isoform/annotation indels across 175 mammalian assemblies. In `Line 72`, the authors report *"251 out of 627 (or 40%) sites to be invariant at the nucleotide level, suggesting a highly conserved evolutionary importance for these loci."* Across 175 mammals spanning ~200 million years of divergence, a true core coding site is almost never 100% invariant at the third-position synonymous nucleotide level; rather, the ~251 "invariant" columns are predominantly lineage-specific insertion columns where 170+ of the 175 species contain alignment gaps (`---` or masked codons from `find-outliers`) and only 1–2 species have nucleotides! Treating gap-dominated alignment columns as "invariant nucleotide loci of high evolutionary importance" and using $627 - 251 = 376$ as the denominator to claim *"purifying selection in 70% of non-invariant codon sites"* (`Abstract`, `Line 70`) conflates alignment sparsity with sequence conservation.
- **Confounding of `TM-align` and `RMSD` by Unstructured `AlphaFold2` Intrinsically Disordered Regions (`Lines 49–58, 141–149`):** Over half of `Rem2` outside the 169-aa RGK core consists of N- and C-terminal intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) where `AlphaFold2` produces low-confidence (`pLDDT < 50`, colored red/orange in `Figure 4`), physically meaningless random coil orientations. Computing global backbone `RMSD` (`mean 3.61 Å, SD 0.54`) and `TM-scores` (`mean 0.62`) on the full-length structures—and furthermore using `SLAC` column-wise ancestral character states without a phylogenetic indel model to reconstruct 159 "extinct" sequences—measures stochastic variation in `ColabFold`'s placement of disordered tails and indel length differences rather than genuine structural divergence of the RGK fold. Consequently, the claim that *"for even small genetic distances (<0.05) we observe a large variance in TM-Score... [which] could spur evolutionary innovation in Rem2"* (`Line 149`) is an artifact of comparing unaligned disordered tails.
- **Mixed Genome Builds (`hg19` vs `hg38`) in `Table 3` (`Lines 129–135`):** `Table 3` lists human `Rem2` variants from `gnomAD` under the column header `Genome Position (hg38: chr14)`:
  - Codon 48 is listed at `23,353,918` and Codon 64 at `23,353,969–23,353,971` (which are **GRCh37 / hg19** coordinates for `REM2`!), whereas Codon 98 is listed at `22,884,863` (which is the **GRCh38 / hg38** coordinate, ~469 kb away!). Mixing `hg19` and `hg38` coordinates in the same table column under the label `hg38` is a serious curation error. Additionally, the `Table 3` caption (`Line 127`) states that positions `23,353,970` (`G->T`, `Arg64Ile`) and `23,353,971` (`A->T`, `Arg64Ser`) form an in-phase MNV altering `aGA to aTT` (`R to I`), failing to note that `G->T` alone (`AGA -> ATA`) already encodes `Ile`, and `AGA -> ATT` is synonymous with `ATA` (`Ile`).
- **Misreporting of `BUSTED` Model Averaging (`Table 2` & `Line 80`) and Self-Contradiction on `FEL` Positive Selection (`Line 114` vs `2nd half Line 3`):**
  1. In `Table 2` (`Lines 89–91`), the single Akaike-weighted model-averaged $p$-value (`0.2272`, derived from weighting `BUSTED+S` [$p=0.0000$, $\text{cAIC}=46,351.64$, weight $0.545$] and `BUSTED+S+MH` [$p=0.5000$, $\text{cAIC}=46,352.00$, weight $0.455$]) is pasted identically across all four columns, and the caption erroneously describes `LRT p-value` and `Averaged p-value` as *"unadjusted and adjusted... p-values"*.
  2. `Line 80` reports the `BUSTED+S` $\omega_3$ estimate as **"−50"** (a negative rate ratio, instead of `50.75` as shown in `Table 2`), and `Conclusion Line 16` similarly writes **"−200 million years"**.
  3. `Line 114` states *"we found a single positively selected site using FEL, after multiple tests correction, corresponding to alignment codon site 170 (human Rem2 site 148)"*, whereas the Discussion (`2nd half, Line 3`) directly contradicts this: *"Unfortunately, new sites under positive diversifying selection were not found in the Rem2 gene... after multiple tests correction."*

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Mammalian `Rem2` evolves predominantly under purifying selection across its core RGK domain, with no statistically significant gene-wide episodic diversifying selection after accounting for synonymous rate variation and multi-nucleotide substitutions (`[Well Supported]`).** Both `FEL` and `BUSTED+S+MH` support strong purifying constraint across the core GTPase domain.
- **Claim 2: 251 of 627 codon sites (40%) are invariant at the nucleotide level due to strong evolutionary constraint (`[Methodologically Flawed]`).** As noted above, this count is an artifact of alignment gaps across the 627-codon alignment relative to the ~340-codon mammalian gene length.
- **Claim 3: `SPA` ancestral structure prediction reveals substantial structural divergence (`mean RMSD = 3.61 Å`) and high `TM-score` variance at small genetic distances (`<0.05`) that could drive evolutionary innovation (`[Overstated]`).** The apparent structural variance is driven by low-confidence (`pLDDT < 50`) disordered N/C-terminal regions and gappy ancestral reconstructions rather than conformational divergence of the folded RGK domain.
- **Claim 4: 50 `BGM` coevolving codon pairs form an epistatic network linking the proximal disordered region and RGK domain with clinical implications for Huntington's disease, Long QT syndrome, and Timothy syndrome (`[Fragile]`).** With a permissive posterior probability cutoff of $0.5$ (`Line 156`) on a tree with normalized Robinson-Foulds distance $0.535$ from the mammalian species tree (`Line 78`) and extensive indel masking in the disordered region, many correlated changes in the disordered tail may reflect alignment/isoform artifacts rather than physical epistasis, and no experimental validation is performed.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Code & Data Availability:** All alignments, trees, `HyPhy` JSON output files, `AlphaFold2` PDB structures, and custom Python/Snakemake scripts are publicly archived in two dedicated GitHub repositories (`AOC-Rem2` and `SPA-REM2`) and an interactive ObservableHQ notebook.
