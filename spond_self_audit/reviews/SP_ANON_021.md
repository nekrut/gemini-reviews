### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript analyzes 4.48 million SARS-CoV-2 genomes from GISAID (January 2020–October 2021) to predict which viral coding and non-coding mutations will "spread" over a subsequent 3-month window. The authors construct an empirically weighted rank metric ("Epi Score") combining a mutation's current frequency, country count, and unique haplotype fraction in the prior 3-month window, reporting AUROC values of 0.96–0.99 for forecasting spread, alongside a Baron & Kenny mediation analysis linking sequence-based protein language model (BiLSTM) and evolutionary selection (FEL) scores to variant expansion, and a VSV-SARS-CoV-2 pseudovirus neutralization assay for S494P across 49 convalescent sera. While the empirical curation of GISAID dynamics and the S494P neutralization characterization are clear, the flagship forecasting claim rests on severe target-predictor autocorrelation and AUROC inflation under extreme class imbalance, mistaking ongoing logistic trajectory continuation for *de novo* mutation emergence prediction.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Autocorrelation and Target-Predictor Circularity in "Spread" Forecasting:** A mutation is defined as "spreading" in window $[t+1, t+3]$ if it achieves a fold-change $\ge 10$ in $\ge 1$ country, a fold-change $\ge 2$ in $\ge 3$ countries, and a global frequency $\ge 0.1\%$ in that window. The predictor ("Epi Score") in window $[t-2, t]$ is an exponentially weighted rank score of the exact same epidemiological state variables: (i) maximum country-level mutation frequency, (ii) fraction of total haplotypes carrying the mutation, and (iii) number of countries where the mutation has been observed $\ge 2$ times. Because global viral selective sweeps (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta) unfold over 3 to 9 months, a mutation that has already reached multi-country prevalence and high haplotype diversity at month $t$ is already in the middle of its epidemic expansion. Predicting that an already-expanding lineage continues to cross a $0.1\%$ global prevalence threshold over the next 90 days measures epidemic momentum rather than forecasting *de novo* functional driver emergence.
* **Severe Class Imbalance and Misleading AUROC Metrics:** Among 221,850 observed mutations (6,466 non-rare), ~97% never reach the $0.1\%$ global threshold and sit near zero frequency. Ranking mutations by current frequency against a vast pool of near-zero singletons automatically yields near-unity AUROC (0.96–0.99) because true negatives are trivially separated at the bottom of the rank distribution. By contrast, Figure 3A shows that the Positive Predictive Value (PPV) at the top 5% cutoff fluctuates between 20% and 60%, dropping sharply whenever a dominant VOC sweep replaces competing lineages. Precision-Recall (AUPRC) and lead-time conditioned on initial detection at ultra-low frequency (e.g., $<0.01\%$ global prevalence before multi-country dissemination) are omitted.
* **Retrospective Collection Date Lookahead Bias:** The sliding-window backtesting partitions sequences by *sample collection date* rather than *GISAID submission/deposition date*. Because GISAID reporting lags ranged from weeks to several months across different countries in 2020–2021, aggregating country-level fold-changes and global haplotype fractions by collection date introduces retrospective lookahead leakage unavailable to a real-time surveillance system at month $t$.
* **Invalid Independence Assumption and Hitchhiking Neglect:** Every mutation is scored and evaluated as an independent unit. In the Discussion, the authors argue that pairwise linkage ($>8$-fold enrichment) occurs in $<5\%$ of mutation pairs to justify ignoring haplotype structure. However, during Alpha and Delta sweeps, tens of synonymous, non-coding, and passenger missense mutations hitchhiked to $>50\%$ global prevalence on a single clonal backbone. Counting every co-segregating passenger mutation on a sweeping VOC haplotype as an independent "true positive" forecast inflates both sensitivity and PPV, and explains why non-coding and non-Spike mutations appear to have high "spread potential."
* **Misapplication of Linear Mediation Analysis:** The authors apply classic Baron & Kenny linear mediation models to test whether Epi Score mediates the effect of FEL ($dN/dS$) and BiLSTM DMS scores on future spread. Both FEL and BiLSTM are static or whole-pandemic summary metrics (and FEL was computed on GISAID sequences overlapping the evaluation period), violating temporal precedence, linearity, and independence of errors across phylogenetically linked sites.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim:** *The Epi Score accurately anticipates emerging mutations months in advance and captures intrinsic fitness effects mediated by evolutionary and language-model features.* **[Overstated / Methodologically Flawed]** — As shown in Table 1 and Figure 4, when evaluated at initial low prevalence without multi-country expansion, purely intrinsic sequence models (BiLSTM, FEL) have low predictive power, and Epi Score only flags mutations once they are already geographically dispersed or hitchhiking on an established VOC clade.
* **Claim:** *Spike S494P confers partial immune escape from convalescent sera while maintaining human ACE2 binding.* **[Well Supported]** — The wet-lab pseudovirus neutralization assays across 49 convalescent sera (Figure 2F–G) demonstrate a statistically significant ~2-fold reduction in neutralization titer ($p < 0.001$, Wilcoxon signed-rank test) for S494P relative to WT (D614G), consistent with structural proximity in the RBD. However, S494P never swept globally on its own, illustrating the disconnect between the wet-lab vignette and the global epidemiological forecasting claims.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Code and Data Availability:** No repository link, source code, trained BiLSTM weights, or processed mutation-by-window Epi Score matrices are provided in the manuscript. GISAID accession lists or EPI_SET identifiers are not included.
* **Parameter Specification:** The exact exponential transformation parameters used to convert raw feature ranks into the composite Epi Score (Methods, "Construction of the Epi Score") are described qualitatively ("exponentially weighted") without an explicit closed-form equation or decay constant.
