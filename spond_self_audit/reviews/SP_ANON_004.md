### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates the anatomic and cellular source of sexually transmitted HIV-1 in men who have sex with men (MSM) by sequencing *env* C2V3 across six transmission pairs (including a unique cluster where one index donor transmitted to three recipients during acute and chronic infection stages). Comparing recipient blood plasma HIV-1 RNA (via single-genome amplification, SGA) against source blood plasma RNA (SGA), seminal plasma cell-free RNA (clonal PCR), and seminal cell proviral DNA (clonal PCR) using maximum-likelihood phylogenetics, $F_{ST}$ compartmentalization tests, Bayesian coalescent dating (`BEAST`), and `HyPhy` selection models (`FEL`, `iFEL`, `GA-branch`), the authors demonstrate that transmitted viruses consistently trace to cell-free seminal plasma RNA rather than seminal cell proviral DNA. The manuscript stands out for its exceptional statistical self-awareness, explicitly demonstrating how naive non-phylogenetic tests create false-positive glycosylation/isoelectric differences due to pseudoreplication.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Exemplary Correction of Phylogenetic Pseudoreplication:** A major strength of this manuscript is its handling of sequence-level traits (isoelectric point `pI` and potential N-linked glycosylation sites `PNGS`). The authors explicitly show that a naive Mann–Whitney test pooling all clonal/SGA sequences across pairs yields highly significant $p$-values ($p < 0.0001$), correctly diagnose this as statistical pseudoreplication driven by within-host clonal expansion, and re-evaluate the hypothesis using phylogenetically independent subtree comparisons and sequence-level permutation tests—properly reporting a null result when phylogeny is accounted for (lines 48–50).
* **Multi-Test Compartmentalization and Selection Framework:** Compartmentalization is rigorously evaluated using three complementary metrics in `HyPhy`—Wright's $F_{ST}$ (Hudson, Slatkin–Maddison, and Hudson–Boos–Kaplan estimators with 1,000 permutations), nearest-neighbor $S_{nn}$, and the tree-based Slatkin–Maddison test—alongside `GARD` recombination screening, `FEL`/`iFEL`, and `GA-branch` lineage-level $\mathrm{d}N/\mathrm{d}S$ modeling.
* **Methodological Asymmetry Between SGA (Blood) and Clonal TOPO-TA PCR (Semen):** As the authors candidly discuss in the Discussion (line 69), blood plasma RNA was amplified via limiting-dilution SGA whereas seminal plasma RNA and seminal PBMC proviral DNA were amplified via bulk PCR followed by TOPO-TA cloning. Bulk PCR cloning can introduce *in vitro* PCR recombination and template resampling when viral loads in semen are low. While the authors mitigated resampling by reporting input template copy numbers and checking phylogenetic tree topologies against blood SGA sequences, this technical asymmetry remains a minor experimental limitation.
* **Small Effective Sample Size ($n=6$ Pairs from 4 Unique Sources):** Because Source 1 (`S1`/`S2`/`S3`) transmitted to three recipients (`R1`, `R2`, `R3`), the six transmission pairs represent four independent source individuals. Consequently, population-level binomial confidence intervals around the 6/6 concordance with seminal plasma RNA remain wide ($95\%\text{ CI} \approx [54\%, 100\%]$ across 6 events, or $[40\%, 100\%]$ across 4 independent donors).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *In this cohort of 6 MSM transmission pairs, the earliest recipient blood plasma virus is phylogenetically closest to cell-free HIV-1 RNA in the source's seminal plasma rather than seminal cell proviral DNA.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Supported across all 6 transmission pairs by minimum genetic distance, ML monophyly, and `BEAST` coalescent $T_{\text{MRCA}}$ Ordering, including in compartments displaying significant RNA vs. DNA compartmentalization.
* **Claim 2:** *A single mucosal founder variant established infection in all 6 MSM recipients despite diverse viral populations in the source.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Confirmed by recipient blood SGA diversity ($<0.66\%$), Poisson-consistent star-like phylogenies, and `BEAST` $T_{\text{MRCA}}$ estimates aligning with estimated exposure dates.
* **Claim 3:** *Putative C2V3 transmission signature motifs (e.g., PNGS count or isoelectric point) do not significantly differ between compartments once phylogenetic non-independence is controlled.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Directly demonstrated by contrasting naive vs. phylogenetically corrected tests.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Sequence data are deposited in GenBank (`GU063195`–`GU063864`), clinical metadata (days post-EDI, viral loads, CD4 counts, Fiebig stages) are fully tabulated in Table 1, and `BEAST` / `HyPhy` model specifications are thoroughly documented.
* **Deficiencies:** Custom `HyPhy` scripts used for the subtree `pI`/`PNGS` permutation tests are not linked to an external code repository.
