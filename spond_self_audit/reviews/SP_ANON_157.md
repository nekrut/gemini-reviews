# Blinded Review: SP_ANON_157
**Composite Score:** 7.6 / 10.0 (Rigor: 2.5/3.5 | Claims: 1.9/2.5 | Biology: 1.6/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.6/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Intra-Partition Recombination Confounding in Selection Tests & Unadjusted Single-Branch Site Testing
**Verdict:** Minor Revision
**Study Modality:** Dry-Lab Secondary Cohort/Database Re-Analysis
**Technology Maturity:** Mature/Classical
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
`SP_ANON_157` performs a molecular evolutionary and recombination analysis of the spike (`S`) gene of `CCoV-HuPn-2018` (and the closely related `HuCCoV_Z19Haiti`), a novel canine-feline-porcine recombinant *Alphacoronavirus 1* isolated from hospitalized pneumonia patients in Malaysia and a traveler to Haiti. Using GARD and RDP5 for recombination screening, HyPhy (`MEME` and `FEL` with 100 parametric bootstrap replicates on the terminal human branch, `aBSREL`, `BUSTED`, `RELAX`), and BEAST2 tip-dating with date randomization (`TipDatingBeast`), the authors examine adaptive evolution, relaxed selection in the 0-domain, and the divergence date of `CCoV-HuPn-2018`. The study commendably employs parametric bootstrapping for single-branch site selection tests and transparently reports negative branch-site (`aBSREL`/`BUSTED`) results, though unsplit recombination within the 0-domain partition and unadjusted multiple testing across single-branch codon tests warrant caution.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Unsplit Recombination Inside `GARD Partition 1` Confounding `RELAX` (`Lines 28, 40–41`):** For Alignment Set I (the N-terminal 0-domain, roughly 265 amino acids), `GARD` identified no breakpoints (`GARD partition 1`). However, `RDP5` (`Table 1` and `Line 40`) detected a strongly supported recombination event (`Event 1`, supported by 6/7 RDP methods) spanning from amino acid position 223 to the 3' end of Alignment Set I, involving `CCoV-HuPn-2018` as the recombinant and a `CCoV2a`-like minor parent. Notably, this recombinant segment (`aa 223–265`) is the exact region implicated in sialic-acid binding where Tajima's relative rate test found an elevated nonsynonymous substitution count (`Line 41`). Running `RELAX` across the unsplit `GARD partition 1` (`K = 0.07, p = 0.007` vs `CCoV2b`; `K = 0.081, p = 0.04` vs `TGEV`) mixes two distinct evolutionary histories (`CCoV2b`-like in `aa 1–222` and `CCoV2a`-like in `aa 223–265`), which can distort branch lengths and branch-site $\omega$ distributions.
- **Unadjusted Multiple Testing on Single-Branch `MEME`/`FEL` Scans (`Lines 25, 42–45`):** While the authors properly use 100 parametric bootstrap replicates to calibrate site-level $p$-values on the single terminal branch leading to `CCoV-HuPn-2018` (`Line 25`), no false discovery rate (FDR) correction is applied across the ~1,400 codons tested. On a single short terminal branch, any site that happens to experience a nonsynonymous substitution (especially a multi-nucleotide or clustered change) can yield a nominal $p \le 0.05$; thus, the 9 positively selected sites reported on the `CCoV-HuPn-2018` branch are expected to contain false positives, consistent with the fact that gene-wide tests (`aBSREL` and `BUSTED`) found no statistically significant episodic positive selection on that branch (`Line 42`).
- **Low Precision of Molecular Clock Dating on `GARD Partition 7` (`Lines 33–35, 49`):** Because only 16 sequences in `GARD partition 7` had collection dates and passed the date-randomization test (CR2 criteria), the BEAST2 strict-clock skygrid analysis yields a 95% HPD for the `CCoV-HuPn-2018` ancestor spanning 130 years (`1846–1976`, median `1957`). The authors appropriately report the full HPD interval and avoid over-interpreting the point estimate.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: `CCoV-HuPn-2018` spike has a complex mosaic recombination history involving `CCoV2b`, `FCoV2`, and a `CCoV2a`-like segment in the 0-domain (`[Well Supported]`).** Concordant GARD and RDP5 analyses across the full spike alignment and separate 0-domain alignment establish clear mosaicism, including the newly identified `Event 1` breakpoint at residue 223 of the 0-domain.
- **Claim 2: There is no evidence of strong gene-wide episodic positive selection along the terminal branch leading to `CCoV-HuPn-2018` (`[Well Supported]`).** Both `aBSREL` and `BUSTED` across all GARD partitions were non-significant on the `CCoV-HuPn-2018` branch, supporting the conclusion that prior adaptation in canine/feline hosts largely pre-adapted the virus.
- **Claim 3: Nine specific codon sites in `CCoV-HuPn-2018` (and 14 in `Z19Haiti`) evolved under positive selection (`[Fragile]`).** Because gene-wide tests are negative and single-branch site tests across >1,400 codons are unadjusted for multiple comparisons, individual site calls on a single terminal branch remain tentative hypotheses rather than confirmed adaptive substitutions.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** All sequences are public GenBank accessions (listed in Supplementary Table S1), and GARD partition coordinates, RDP5 breakpoint tables, and selection tables are provided in the text and supplement.
- **Code & Model Specifications:** Software versions (`MAFFT v7.481`, `GARD`, `RDP5`, `HyPhy v2.5.31`, `IQ-TREE2`, `TempEst`, `TipDatingBeast`, `BEAST v2.6.3`) and prior distributions (`1/X` clock rate prior, coalescent skygrid) are well documented.
