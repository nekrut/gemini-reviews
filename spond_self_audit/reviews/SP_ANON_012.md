### 1. Executive Summary
This software and methods manuscript presents `RASCL` (`Rapid Assessment of Selection within CLades`), an automated pipeline (implemented as a `Snakemake` command-line workflow and `Galaxy` workbooks with an `ObservableHQ` visualization dashboard) that subsamples viral gene alignments via `TN93` genetic distance clustering, reconstructs gene trees (`IQ-TREE`), and runs a battery of nine `HyPhy` selection and coevolution methods (`BUSTED[S]`, `SLAC`, `BGM`, `FEL`, `MEME`, `RELAX`, `Contrast-FEL`, `FADE`, `FMM`) comparing a query clade against a background reference set. The authors demonstrate `RASCL` on SARS-CoV-2 Omicron (`BA.1`, `BA.2`, `BA.5`) and `Mu` (`B.1.621`). While `RASCL` is a convenient software wrapper for `HyPhy`, the empirical biological demonstrations on `BA.1` and `BA.5` uncritically report severe sequencing/assembly artifacts (amplicon-dropout reversions to Wuhan-Hu-1 yielding impossible $\omega_3 > 64,000$) as genuine positive diversifying selection, and the pipeline omits recombination screening.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Uncritical Reporting of Amplicon-Dropout / Reference-Imputation Artifacts as Positive Selection:** In Table 2 (`BUSTED[S]` results on `BA.5`) and the `BA.1`/`BA.2`/`BA.5` case study (lines 118–132), `RASCL` reports extreme episodic diversifying selection in `BA.5` Spike ($\omega_3 = 64,042.68$ on $0.1\%$ of sites, $p < 0.0001$) and `BA.5` leader ($\omega_3 = 8,003.22$, $p < 0.0001$), and highlights `MEME`/`FEL`/`FADE` hits at Spike codons `339`, `371`, `440`, `493`, `505`, and `764`. Inspection of the amino acid state compositions reported by the authors in Table 3 (`BA.2`: `D339, F371, K440, R493, H505, K764`; `BA.5`: `D213/G14, F206/S9/L7, K174/N33, Q224/R6, H230/Y2, K226/N3`) reveals that the "positively selected substitutions" inside `BA.2`/`BA.5` are actually clustered *reversions* to the ancestral Wuhan-Hu-1 amino acids (`G339, S371, N440, Y505, N764`) in a small subset of `GISAID` sequences. It is well established in SARS-CoV-2 genomics that `ARTIC` primer dropouts across the Omicron RBD/S1 cause poorly configured consensus callers to fill low-coverage amplicons with Wuhan-Hu-1 reference sequence, creating clustered artificial "reversions" on internal and terminal branches that inflate $\omega_3$ into the tens of thousands. An automated surveillance pipeline intended for clade-level selection assessment must filter out reference-imputation/dropout tracts or flag clustered reversions to reference as probable assembly artifacts rather than reporting them as biological adaptation.
* **Sensitivity of `TN93` Genetic-Distance Subsampling to Sequencing Errors and Dropout:** `RASCL` uses `TN93` pairwise distance thresholds to downsample tens of thousands of `GISAID` sequences down to $\sim 200$–$300$ representative sequences per clade before tree building. Because `TN93` clustering maximizes diversity by retaining at least one sequence from every distance cluster, rare sequences containing sequencing errors, frameshifts, contamination, or amplicon-dropout reference fills are *preferentially enriched* in the subsampled alignment compared to clean consensus sequences, directly amplifying false-positive signals in `MEME`, `BUSTED[S]`, and `FMM`.
* **Absence of Intragenic Recombination Screening:** Unlike standard phylogenomic best practices for coronaviruses, `RASCL` constructs a single `IQ-TREE` topology per gene (`Spike`, `N`, `ORF1ab` peptides) without running `GARD` or filtering recombinant genomes (`X*` lineages or within-clade recombinants), which can further create spurious internal-branch homoplasies.
* **Multiple Testing Across 16 Genes $\times$ 9 `HyPhy` Methods:** Running nine distinct gene-wide and site-level tests across 16 ORFs/peptides per clade generates hundreds of p-values and empirical Bayes factors without a unified cross-pipeline false discovery rate framework.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *`RASCL` provides an automated, reproducible workflow and interactive dashboard for running comparative codon selection analyses on viral clades.* **`[Well Supported]`** — The `Snakemake`, `Conda`, `Galaxy`, and `ObservableHQ` components are functional, modular, and openly accessible.
* **Claim 2:** *Omicron sublineages `BA.1`, `BA.2`, and `BA.5` exhibit strong ongoing positive selection and directional evolution at signature RBD/S1 residues (`339`, `371`, `440`, `505`, `764`) and extreme gene-wide $\omega_3 > 64,000$ in `BA.5` Spike.* **`[Methodologically Flawed]`** — Driven predominantly by `GISAID` consensus assembly artifacts (amplicon dropout imputed with ancestral reference bases) enriched by `TN93` diversity subsampling.
* **Claim 3:** *`RASCL` analysis of the `Mu` (`B.1.621`) variant identifies clade-specific selection signals across `Spike`, `N`, and `ORF1ab`.* **`[Fragile]`** — Vulnerable to the same `TN93` outlier-enrichment and unscreened recombination artifacts without read-level quality verification.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Source code (`https://github.com/veg/rascl`), `Galaxy` workflows, `ObservableHQ` notebooks, and JSON output archives are publicly available under open-source licenses.
* **Deficiencies:** The pipeline lacks pre-alignment quality control against reference-backfill / amplicon-dropout artifacts and recombination filtering.
