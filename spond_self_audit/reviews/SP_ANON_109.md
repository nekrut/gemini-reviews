# Blinded Review: SP_ANON_109
**Composite Score:** 4.1 / 10.0 (Rigor: 1.1/3.5 | Claims: 0.7/2.5 | Biology: 1.0/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.3/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Evolutionary Rate / Clock Extrapolation Over-Interpretation on Short Time Series & Uncontrolled Founder/Clonal Diversity
**Verdict:** Reject
**Study Modality:** Hybrid Computational + Wet-Lab Functional Validation
**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Translational/Preclinical/Biomarker

### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates whether active HIV-1 replication persists within lymphoid tissue sanctuaries during combination antiretroviral therapy (ART) in $N = 3$ infected individuals sampled at day 0, month 3, and month 6 after treatment initiation (peripheral blood PBMCs, inguinal lymph node biopsies, and day 0 plasma RNA). Using 454 pyrosequencing of three ~509–587 bp amplicons (*gag*, *pol* PR [`pol1`], and *pol* RT [`pol2`]), computationally reconstructed viral haplotypes (`Segminator II` / `PREDATOR` and `QuRe` down to $0.04\%$ frequency), root-to-tip linear regression (`Path-O-Gen`), Bayesian time-calibrated phylogeography (`BEAST` v2.1.2 strict clock + constant population size + discrete trait / structured coalescent migration), and a two-compartment ODE mathematical model, the authors claim that HIV-1 continues to replicate and evolve at pre-treatment intra-host substitution rates ($0.47\text{--}1.87 \times 10^{-3}\text{ substitutions/site/month}$, equivalent to $\sim 0.6\text{--}2.2 \times 10^{-2}\text{ subs/site/year}$) in lymph node sanctuaries of patients with undetectable plasma viremia, continuously migrating from lymph nodes to blood without selecting for drug resistance due to low sanctuary drug concentrations.

Despite its ambitious multi-disciplinary design, the manuscript's central claim—that ongoing *de novo* viral replication and clock-like divergence occur in well-suppressed patients on ART—is not supported by the underlying experimental design or data in Extended Data Tables 1–5. Specifically: (1) all three patients were sampled only during the **first 6 months after starting ART** (day 0, month 3, month 6), a transient decay phase during which pre-existing diverse proviral populations established prior to ART (especially in Subject 1774, infected for 17 years and still viremic in plasma at month 3, and Subjects 1727/1679, treated 3–4 months after primary infection) undergo differential cellular clearance and clonal redistribution; (2) the reported linear regression substitution rates in Extended Data Table 2 ($\mu \approx 1.5\text{--}1.87 \times 10^{-3}\text{ subs/site/month}$) are **higher than or equal to untreated HIV-1 replication rates** and show glaring internal inconsistencies (including a statistically significant *negative* evolutionary rate in Subject 1774 LN `pol1`, missing PB `gag` data in Subject 1727, and no significant divergence in 4 of 6 regions in Subject 1679 in Extended Data Table 1); (3) forcing computational haplotype reconstructions (`PREDATOR`/`QuRe` on 500 bp 454 reads after 70 cycles of nested PCR) into a `BEAST` strict-clock constant-population tree over a 6-month window confuses standing pre-ART genetic diversity and PCR recombination/resampling with forward evolutionary divergence; and (4) the two-compartment ODE model is parameterized post-hoc ($z_1 = 0.3$) to reproduce the absence of drug resistance rather than independently testing whether replication is occurring.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Confounding of transient first-6-month decay kinetics and standing pre-ART diversity with ongoing replication ([Methodologically Flawed]):** The abstract and main text frame the study around *"well-suppressed patients on treatment"*, yet day 0 is the **day ART was initiated** (when plasma viral loads were high and massive standing genetic diversity was already present in blood and lymphoid tissue). Subject 1774 had been infected for **17 years** prior to day 0 and *still had detectable plasma viremia at month 3* (Extended Data Fig. 1a), meaning at least half of the 6-month window in Subject 1774 occurred before plasma suppression was even achieved! Even in Subjects 1727 and 1679 (infected 3–4 months prior to ART initiation), sampling at 0, 3, and 6 months of ART captures the steep first- and second-phase decay of productively and latently infected cell populations formed *before* ART. Shifting haplotype frequencies across compartments as short-lived productively infected cells die off and older archived proviral clones become proportionally more visible can increase pairwise $p$-distance from the day-0 dominant haplotype without a single round of *de novo* replication during months 3–6.
* **Contradictory and biologically implausible evolutionary rate estimates in Extended Data Tables 1 & 2 ([Methodologically Flawed]):**
  * In Extended Data Table 1, for Subject 1679 (one of the two "well-suppressed" subjects), 4 of the 6 compartment-gene combinations show **no significant increase** in $p$-distance at month 6 at $p < 0.05$ (`LN gag` actually *decreases* from $6.68\times 10^{-4}$ to $3.85\times 10^{-4}$, $p = 0.505$; `PB gag` $p = 0.880$; `PB pol1` $p = 0.099$; `PB pol2` $p = 0.238$). In Subject 1727, `PB gag` failed to amplify (`NA`) and `PB pol1` was non-significant ($p = 0.328$). Notice that the caption of Extended Data Table 1 lowers the significance threshold to $P < 0.1$ to claim that *"Most comparisons were statistically significant"*.
  * In Extended Data Table 2, the regression slopes $\mu$ (substitutions per site per month) are reported with implausibly tiny standard errors ($\sim 10^{-5}$, likely treating thousands of non-independent read/haplotype pairs from a single patient as independent data points in ordinary least squares regression!). Worse, Subject 1774 `LN pol1` has a highly significant **negative** evolutionary rate ($\mu = -4.87 \times 10^{-4} \pm 2.17 \times 10^{-5}\text{ subs/site/month}$, a 22-standard-error negative slope), while the positive slopes ($\sim 1.5\text{--}1.87 \times 10^{-3}\text{ subs/site/month} \approx 1.8\text{--}2.2 \times 10^{-2}\text{ subs/site/year}$) are $2\text{--}3\times$ *faster* than the main text itself claims on line 81 (*"range, $6.24 \times 10^{-4}$ to $1.02 \times 10^{-3}$ substitutions per site per month; Extended Data Table 2"*—numbers that do not match the actual values in Extended Data Table 2!).
* **70-cycle nested PCR + computational haplotype reconstruction (`PREDATOR`/`QuRe`) artifacts ([Fragile]):** The library preparation (lines 153–155) performs **35 cycles** of outer PCR followed by **35 cycles** of inner PCR (70 cycles total) without unique molecular identifiers (UMIs) before 454 pyrosequencing. Seventy cycles of bulk PCR on multi-template proviral DNA inevitably generates substantial *in vitro* PCR chimerism (recombination) and polymerase errors, and `PREDATOR`/`QuRe` probabilistic k-mer/multinomial haplotype assembly down to $0.04\%$ frequency cannot prevent *in vitro* chimeras of pre-existing variants from appearing as novel tips on a phylogeny. Furthermore, collapsing reads into unique deduplicated haplotypes and feeding them into `BEAST` under a **constant population size** coalescent prior (when viral load drops by 3–5 orders of magnitude between day 0 and month 6!) severely misspecifies the demographic process and forces tips sampled at month 6 to appear at the end of clock-calibrated branches.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Implausibility of rapid, uninhibited clock-like evolution under potent triple ART without selection ([Overstated]):** If HIV-1 were truly replicating in lymph nodes during months 0–6 of ART at $\sim 1.5 \times 10^{-3}\text{ subs/site/month}$ (~1–2% divergence per year, faster than typical untreated chronic infection) with zero drug resistance emergence and near-zero positive selection (Extended Data Table 4 shows $P = 1.0$ on internal branches for 8 of 9 gene-patient combinations), then ART would be exerting virtually zero antiviral effect in the sampled lymph nodes ($z_1 \approx 0.3$ in the ODE model), yet plasma viral load simultaneously dropped to $<48\text{ copies/mL}$ despite high inferred migration from lymph node to blood ($0.57\text{--}1.70\text{ emigrants/month}$, Extended Data Table 5).
* **Circular parameterization of the spatial ODE model ([Overstated]):** The two-compartment ODE model (Figs. 2–4) is a theoretical illustration showing that *if* a tiny sanctuary ($<0.01\%$ of body volume) had $z_1 = 0.3$ drug efficacy while the main compartment had $z_0 = 1.0$, drug-sensitive virus could replicate locally without selecting for resistance mutations whose fitness cost exceeds $0.3$. However, this is a post-hoc mathematical rationalization, not empirical proof of ongoing replication, and the model fit in Extended Data Fig. 5 simply fits bulk viral decay curves over 200 days—which are equally well explained by classic biphasic/triphasic decay of long-lived and latently infected cells without ongoing *de novo* infection.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Mismatch between main text and Extended Data Table 2:** Line 81 states *"the evolutionary rate estimates (range, $6.24 \times 10^{-4}$ to $1.02 \times 10^{-3}$ substitutions per site per month; Extended Data Table 2)"*, whereas the actual values in Extended Data Table 2 range from $-4.87 \times 10^{-4}$ to $+1.87 \times 10^{-3}$.
* **Missing sequence accession numbers:** No NCBI SRA or GenBank accession numbers are provided in the manuscript text for the 454 deep sequencing reads or reconstructed haplotypes from the three study subjects (only the HXB2 reference `K03455` is listed on line 164).
