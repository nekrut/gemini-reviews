### 1. Executive Summary
This brief comparative genomics study surveys the length and sequence conservation of the `ORF1a`/`ORF1b` programmed `-1` ribosomal frameshift element (`PFE`) overlap region across 4,904 NCBI *Coronaviridae* genomes (spanning $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and $\delta$ genera), evaluates consensus and intrahost sequence variation across $>1.52\text{ million}$ `GISAID` SARS-CoV-2 assemblies and 55,163 `COG-UK` deep-sequencing samples, and tests codon-level selection in the non-overlapping loop/stem regions of the SARS-CoV-2 `PFE` (`FEL` on internal branches). The authors show that *Coronaviridae* `ORF1a/1b` overlaps fall into five discrete length classes (`7, 16, 22, 25, 31 nt`) and that the SARS-CoV-2 `PFE` exhibits very low consensus and intrahost variability. While the descriptive genomic survey is clean and useful, the manuscript contains glaring numerical and sign errors in Tables 1 and 2 that directly contradict the main text, and its evolutionary model of `GTA` insertions across coronavirus genera is based on visual inspection of consensus logos rather than formal ancestral state reconstruction.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Direct Numerical and Sign Contradictions Between Main Text, Table 1, and Table 2:**
  1. In the main text (line 79) and Table 1 (line 90), the authors state that only a single codon in the non-overlapping `PFE` loop region (`Codon 1`, nucleotide position `13,443` in `Wuhan-Hu-1`, coding for `G` in `nsp11`/`nsp12`) shows statistically significant evidence of **purifying** selection ($p \le 0.01$, indicated by a downward arrow `↓` in Table 1). However, in Table 2 (line 103), the row for `Codon 1 (13,443)` reports $\alpha = 0.000$, $\beta = 0.000$, $\omega = 4.286$, $p = 0.002$. Not only is $\omega = 4.286 > 1$ the opposite of purifying selection ($\omega < 1$), but $\alpha = 0.000$ and $\beta = 0.000$ cannot yield $\omega = \beta/\alpha = 4.286$ or a significant likelihood ratio test ($p = 0.002$) for purifying selection unless the columns or decimal scaling in Table 2 were corrupted during table transcription.
  2. In Table 1 (line 90), the fourth nucleotide coordinate in the header is truncated to `13,43` instead of `13,435` (or `13,436`).
  3. In Table 2 (line 106), `Codon 31` is listed at reference nucleotide position `13,352`, which is 91 nucleotides *upstream* of `Codon 1` (`13,443`); because `30` codons downstream of `13,443` is $13,443 + 90 - 1 = 13,532$, the digits `5` and `3` were transposed (`13,352` instead of `13,532`).
* **Heuristic Indel Scenario Across Unresolved Genera Trifurcation:** To explain the five discrete overlap lengths (`7, 16, 22, 25, 31 nt`), the authors propose a stepwise sequence of `GTA` insertions and deletions (Fig. 2) based on visual comparisons of `WebLogo` consensus motifs across $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$, and $\delta$ coronaviruses. No outgroup-rooted `RdRp`/`ORF1ab` phylogeny, statistical indel model (e.g., `PRANK`, `MAFFT` ancestral gap reconstruction, or `Bali-Phy`), or formal ancestral state reconstruction is performed to test whether `7 nt` ($\delta$-CoV) or `16/22/25/31 nt` is ancestral, and the diagram depicts a polytomy (`trifurcation`) from $\delta$-CoV to $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$.
* **Appropriate Excluding of Dual-Coding Overlap from Single-Frame `FEL`:** Unlike `SP_ANON_005`, the authors correctly exclude the dual-coding `ORF1a/1b` overlap (`13,462`–`13,480`) from standard single-frame `FEL` $\mathrm{d}N/\mathrm{d}S$ testing (lines 77–79), restricting `FEL` to the adjacent single-frame stem-loop region (`Codons 1–33`, `13,443`–`13,541`) on internal phylogenetic branches.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *Across 4,904 RefSeq/GenBank coronavirus genomes, the `ORF1a/1b` programmed frameshift overlap takes one of five conserved lengths (`7, 16, 22, 25, or 31 nt`) that partition strongly by genus and subgenus.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Comprehensively documented across all available `NCBI Virus` *Coronaviridae* genomes.
* **Claim 2:** *The SARS-CoV-2 `PFE` (`13,462`–`13,541`) is exceptionally conserved at both the consensus (`GISAID` $>1.52\text{M}$ genomes) and intrahost (`55,163` `COG-UK` deep-sequencing runs) levels.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Directly demonstrated by the extreme rarity of consensus substitutions ($<0.2\%$ at all sites except `C13536U` at $0.18\%$) and intrahost minor alleles ($<0.05\%$ of samples).
* **Claim 3:** *Stepwise `GTA` insertions and deletions beginning from a 7-nt $\delta$-coronavirus-like ancestor generated the 16-, 22-, 25-, and 31-nt overlaps in $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ coronaviruses.* **`[Fragile]`** — Plausible homology hypothesis, but presented via manual `WebLogo` alignment inspection without formal phylogenetic ancestral indel reconstruction.
