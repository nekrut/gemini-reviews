## 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript introduces `B-STILL` (Bayesian Significance Test of Invariant Low Likelihoods), a hierarchical empirical Bayes framework implemented in `HyPhy` (`v2.5.95+`) that resolves evolutionary constraint at and near the zero-substitution boundary (`0/0` origin) in protein-coding alignments. Building on `FUBAR`, `B-STILL` discretizes synonymous ($\alpha$) and nonsynonymous ($\beta$) rates over a quadratically spaced near-zero grid ($\alpha_k, \beta_k \propto (k/(K-1))^2$), estimates a gene-wide empirical prior $\pi$ via 0th-order variational Bayes (`VB0`) or collapsed Gibbs sampling, and computes Empirical Bayes Factors (`EBF`) for exact invariance ($\alpha=0, \beta=0$), synonymous stasis ($\alpha=0$), and proximal stasis ($E[S] = (\alpha d_s + \beta d_{ns})T \le X$, default $X=0.5$ expected substitutions across tree length $T$). The site-level `EBF` calls (Evolutionary Stasis Anchors, `ESAs`) are paired with an exact nonparametric Hypergeometric Scan Statistic (`infer_stasis_clusters.py`, calibrated with 10,000 permutations per gene for FWER control) to detect regional Stasis Clusters. Validated across 1,800 simulations, 175,000 HIV-1 RT sequences, ~19,150 mammalian genes (120-mammal alignments), `gnomAD v4.1.1`, `ClinVar` (achieving $\text{AUROC} = 0.88$ for pathogenic synonymous variants), `REVEL` ($N=34,206$ variants, $\rho = 0.40$), `FRESCO` viral overlapping ORFs, `phyloP`, and 815 uncharacterized "dark proteome" genes, `B-STILL` is a mathematically principled, fast ($19.52$ codons/sec/core), and well-validated advance over nucleotide-level conservation scores.

## 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Overcoming the Frequentist `0/0` Plateau via Codon-Aware Opportunity and Tree-Scaled Proximal Stasis [Well Supported]:** Standard site-wise likelihood ratio tests (`phyloP`, `FEL`) assign identical maximal conservation scores to all completely invariant sites in an alignment, failing to distinguish a 4-fold degenerate Proline codon (`CC*`) with high transition-driven synonymous opportunity from a 1-fold Methionine (`ATG`) or 2-fold transversion-restricted codon, or a shallow tree ($T = 0.12$ in SARS-CoV-2 Spike) from a deep tree ($T > 10$). By defining the proximal stasis region in terms of expected substitutions $E[S] \le X$ (so the rate boundary $(\alpha d_s + \beta d_{ns}) \le X/T$ automatically tightens with tree depth $T$ while compressing EBFs toward $1.0$ in shallow trees) and concentrating grid points quadratically near $(0,0)$, `B-STILL` achieves a null false positive rate $\ll 1\%$ ($\approx 0.05\%$) across all 1,800 simulated alignments while cleanly identifying "Protein Wobble Anchors" (codons that are strictly invariant at the amino-acid level despite active synonymous turnover, which `phyloP` misclassifies as unconserved).
* **Exact Hypergeometric Scan Statistic with Permutation FWER Calibration [Well Supported]:** Rather than relying on parametric hidden Markov model transition smoothing (`phastCons`), modeling regional Stasis Clusters via the upper tail of the exact finite-population hypergeometric distribution conditioned on the gene length $L$ and total gene ESAs $K$, and calibrating the minimum local $p$-value threshold via 10,000 positional shuffles per gene, provides rigorous non-asymptotic family-wise error control.
* **Prior Adaptation "Self-Masking" When Comparing EBFs Across Globally Conserved vs. Fast-Evolving Genes [Moderately Supported]:** Because $\text{EBF}(S) = \frac{\text{Posterior Odds}(S|D_s)}{\text{Prior Odds}(S|\pi)}$ divides the site posterior odds by the *gene-wide prior odds* estimated from the same gene, `B-STILL` measures *intra-genic statistical surprise* rather than absolute selective constraint. As the authors note (chunk 1, lines 104 and 116), in globally ultra-conserved genes (such as histones or `CALM1`, or simulated genes with high ESA density in moderate-depth trees, Fig. 2b), the high prior probability of stasis $P(S|\pi)$ inflates the denominator and depresses site-level `EBF` values, whereas an isolated invariant site in a rapidly evolving gene (`OR4N5`, mucins) receives $\text{EBF} > 10^8$. While this is a deliberate feature for finding anomalous anchors within fast-evolving proteins, pooling raw `EBF` values across heterogeneous genes (as done in the multi-gene `ClinVar` AUROC, the 85-gene `REVEL` rank correlation, and the uniform $\text{EBF} \ge 10$ exome threshold) penalizes genuinely immutable functional residues located inside globally conserved housekeeping proteins. Reporting both the posterior probability $P(E[S] \le X | D_s)$ (or a global exome-wide prior EBF) alongside the gene-relative `EBF` would prevent users from misinterpreting low EBFs in ultra-conserved proteins as weak constraint.
* **Minor Gene-Count Inconsistencies Across Text Sections and Truncated Table 1 Reporting [Moderately Supported]:**
  1. In the mammalian exome analysis, the total number of analyzed genes is reported as `19,117` on lines 108 and 110 (chunk 1), but as `19,152` on lines 11, 13, and 29 (chunk 2).
  2. In the `FRESCO` viral overlapping reading frame benchmark, the dataset size is stated as `111 viral protein-coding alignments` on line 75 (chunk 1), but as `110` (`110-gene FRESCO viral dataset` / `Of the 110 viral genes analyzed`) on lines 5 and 9 (chunk 2).
  3. For the `gnomAD v4.1.1` validation across $N = 68$ mammalian genes (lines 71 and 118, chunk 1), Table 1 lists only the top 20 genes by Spearman $|\rho|$ without reporting the summary distribution (median/IQR or proportion significant after multiple-testing adjustment) across all 68 tested genes.

## 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **High Sensitivity to Pathogenic Synonymous Variants and Viral Overlapping ORFs [Well Supported]:** A major strength of `B-STILL` is its ability to isolate nucleotide-level regulatory constraint inside coding sequences via $\text{EBF}_{\text{syn}}$ ($\alpha \approx 0$). In the 18-gene `ClinVar` benchmark, $\text{EBF}_{\text{syn}}$ achieves an aggregate $\text{AUROC} = 0.88$ (mean per-gene $\text{AUROC} = 0.89$) for distinguishing confirmed pathogenic synonymous variants (splicing enhancers, mRNA stability, cotranslational folding in `TP53`, `CFTR`, `BRCA1`, `F9`) from benign synonymous variants—a regime where protein-level predictors like `REVEL` are blind. Importantly, the authors explicitly caveat (chunk 1, line 148) that `ClinVar` ACMG classifications partially incorporate sequence conservation, avoiding circular overclaiming.
