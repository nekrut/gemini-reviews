# Blinded Review: SP_ANON_001

**Bias Category:** Single-Cell Dropout/Expression Ascertainment Bias & Unadjusted Multi-Alignment Testing
**Verdict:** Minor Revision

**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript generates single-cell transcriptomes (SCTs) from 43 cells across 33 ciliate species spanning 10 classes—with an emphasis on uncultivable Karyorelictea (which possess paradiploid, non-dividing macronuclei) and giant polyploid Heterotrichea—and combines them with 13 public transcriptomes to investigate the impact of nuclear dimorphism on gene family expansion, sequence evolution (`RELAX` and `aBSREL`), and stop-codon reassignment across 509 to 943 conserved gene families (GFs). While the phylogenetic reconstruction (confirming Intramacronucleata vs. Postciliodesmatophora and nested *Stephanopogon*) and genetic code reassignment survey are well-executed, the comparative evolutionary claims equating single-cell transcript counts with genomic paralog repertoires and interpreting unadjusted `RELAX` $p$-values across 503 gene families suffer from technical confounding between expression breadth/dropout and true gene copy number.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Confounding of Single-Cell Transcriptomic Dropout and Cell Volume with Genomic Paralog Diversity:** The study's primary comparative metric for paralog diversity counts partial sequences ($\ge 1/3$ of reference length) assembled from 1 to 4 pooled Smart-seq2 single-cell libraries per species, normalized by the number of recovered conserved GFs (lines 67, 134–138). In single-cell RNA-seq of protists spanning orders-of-magnitude differences in cell volume and total RNA content (e.g., giant heterotrichs *Stentor* and *Spirostomum* vs. smaller karyorelictid cells), transcript capture efficiency and expression breadth directly govern how many paralogous transcripts cross the assembly threshold. Furthermore, pooling an uneven number of cells per species (1 to 4 cells) without rarefaction or per-cell downsampling biases transcript isoform/paralog discovery toward multi-cell pools and giant transcriptionally hyperactive cells.
* **Multiplicity in Gene-Family Selection Scans (`RELAX` and `aBSREL`):** In `RELAX` "group mode," 503 single-gene alignments were tested comparing Postciliodesmatophora (test) against Intramacronucleata (reference) using a nominal threshold of $p \le 0.05$ (`LRT`), yielding 267 families (53%) with $k < 1$ and 49 (10%) with $k > 1$ (lines 71, 146). No false discovery rate (FDR) correction (e.g., Benjamini–Hochberg) is applied across the 503 parallel hypothesis tests. Similarly, in the exploratory 5-partition `RELAX` model (`Het`, `Kar`, `Oli`, `Col`, `Other`), fitting separate $\omega$ distributions to small clade subsets in short single-gene alignments introduces substantial estimation variance on $\omega_3$ (often hitting boundary values where $\log_{10} k$ saturates).
* **Ortholog vs. Paralog `aBSREL` Comparison Confounded by Branch Length and Tree Size:** The manuscript compares the fraction of branches with $\text{LRT } p \le 0.05$ under `aBSREL` between 85 paralogous alignments (2.4% of branches, 0.2% of tip branches) and 72 orthologous alignments (0.2% of branches, 0.0% of tip branches) (lines 71, 148). Because `aBSREL` statistical power scales strongly with branch length and tree size—and multi-copy paralog trees contain substantially more branches and deeper duplication divergences than single-copy ortholog trees—raw proportions of nominally significant branches cannot be directly compared without controlling for branch length distributions and family-wise error adjustments.
* **Genetic Code Assignment Thresholds:** The stop-codon reassignment heuristic (`10%` of in-frame positions in single-copy core regions across 943 GFs) is sensible and recovers known dual-code behaviour in *Condylostoma magnum*, though frame-shift or sequencing errors in low-coverage SCT contigs could inflate apparent UGA readthrough near transcript termini if 3' UTR boundaries are misannotated.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *Ciliate phylogenomics splits ciliates into Postciliodesmatophora (Karyorelictea + Heterotrichea) and Intramacronucleata, with Stephanopogon nesting within Karyorelictea.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Strongly supported by concatenated supermatrices (43,899 amino acid positions) under both maximum likelihood (`IQ-TREE LG+C60+F+G4`, 100% bootstrap) and Bayesian `PhyloBayes CAT-GTR`, as well as coalescent `ASTRAL-III` supertrees.
* **Claim 2:** *Karyorelictea harbor fewer paralogs per gene family and experience relaxed purifying selection compared to Heterotrichea and Intramacronucleata due to their non-dividing paradiploid macronuclei.* **`[Fragile]`** — Without whole-genome sequencing of Karyorelictea macronuclei and micronuclei, lower paralog recovery in SCT data cannot be disentangled from lower transcriptional diversity or higher allelic/paralog dropout in smaller cells. Moreover, `RELAX` $k < 1$ simultaneously shrinks both purifying selection ($\omega_{1,2} \to 1$) and positive selection ($\omega_3 \to 1$), which can also be driven by differences in effective population size ($N_e$) or synonymous saturation across deep class-level divergences rather than amitotic chromosomal assortment alone.
* **Claim 3:** *Stop-codon reassignment (UAA/UAG to Q) evolved independently at least 8 times across Ciliophora, with Karyorelictea exhibiting unambiguous stop-to-sense reassignment alongside context-dependent stops in Parduczia.* **`[Moderately Supported]`** — The comparative mapping across 943 conserved domains provides convincing evidence for widespread UAA/UAG translation in Karyorelictea, though confirming context-dependent stop/sense duality in *Parduczia* requires ribo-seq, proteomics, or tRNA/eRF1 characterization rather than transcriptome alignments alone.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Raw sequencing reads and assemblies are deposited under NCBI BioProject `PRJNA813589` (`SRR18306620`–`SRR18306662`, `GJWJ00000000`–`GJXu00000000`), and intermediate alignments, trees, `HyPhy` JSON outputs, and custom Python scripts are archived on Figshare (`10.6084/m9.figshare.19330673`).
* **Deficiencies:** The exact `HyPhy` software version used for `RELAX` and `aBSREL` is omitted in the Methods (line 146), and no FDR-adjusted $q$-value tables are provided for the 503 `RELAX` and 157 `aBSREL` runs.
