### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates why standard Bayesian molecular clock analyses (using nucleotide substitution models such as GTR$+\Gamma_4$ on tip-dated contemporary sequences) infer implausibly young times to the most recent common ancestor (tMRCAs) for anciently diverged RNA viruses. Analyzing three negative-sense RNA virus datasets—measles/rinderpest/peste-des-petits-ruminants virus (MeV/RPV/PPRV) nucleoprotein ($n = 145$), Ebola virus (EBOV) glycoprotein ($n = 34$), and avian influenza virus (AIV) neuraminidase ($n = 270$)—alongside 10,000-replicate single-branch codon simulations parameterized by empirical internal-branch IFEL selection profiles, the authors demonstrate that strong purifying selection preserves amino acid sequence identity while synonymous sites saturate completely. Standard nucleotide models (GTR$+\Gamma_4$, SRD06) misinterpret conserved nonsynonymous sites as evidence of low overall divergence, reaching an asymptotic branch-length ceiling (e.g., $0.36$ subs/site under $100\%$ amino acid conservation, or $7.1$ subs/site under $10\%$ conservation; Figure 1A). Applying a hierarchy of Muse–Gaut codon models in HyPhy culminating in the `Lineage+Dual` model (which combines 3-bin GDD site-to-site variation in both $\alpha$ and $\beta$ with separate $\mathbb{E}[\beta]/\mathbb{E}[\alpha]$ ratios on deep inter-species/subtype/serotype internal branches), the authors show that deep internal branch lengths expand by $1.93\times$ (95% CI $1.76\text{--}2.16$) in MeV/RPV/PPRV (shifting the MeV–RPV split from 1483 CE to $\sim 899\text{ CE}$, consistent with Rhazes' 9th-century clinical description), and reach synonymous saturation ($32.1\times$ and $107.3\times$ tree-length expansion) along inter-subtype EBOV and inter-serotype AIV stems.

This is a foundational, mechanistically insightful paper that clearly identifies and simulates the interaction between purifying selection and synonymous saturation in viral molecular dating. Its main statistical limitation—which the authors candidly discuss—is that when synonymous sites on deep branches are completely saturated, maximum-likelihood branch lengths under `Lineage+Dual` diverge to numerical infinity, making linear age extrapolations ($70,000$ years for EBOV and $200,000$ years for AIV) qualitative lower-bound heuristics rather than identifiable parameter estimates.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Mechanistic Demonstration of the Purifying Selection Asymptote in Nucleotide Models (Pillars 3 & 5):** The single-branch simulation design (Figure 1A–D, Figure 2) is clean and decisive: simulating codon evolution along branches of length $0.01$ to $100$ expected substitutions/site using empirical site-specific internal-branch rates ($\alpha_s, \beta_s^I$) inferred via IFEL demonstrates that GTR$+\Gamma_4$ is unbiased under neutrality ($\alpha_s = \beta_s = 1$) but severely underestimates branch lengths once $t > 0.25\text{--}0.5$ subs/site under realistic viral purifying selection ($45\%\text{--}89\%$ negatively selected sites), whereas the `Dual` codon model remains nearly unbiased up to $5\text{--}10$ subs/site.
* **Non-Identifiability and Numerical Infinity of Saturated Branches in `Lineage+Dual` (Pillars 3 & 4):** In `Lineage+Dual`, deep internal branches are assigned their own mean selective regime $\omega_{\text{deep}} = \mathbb{E}[\beta]_{\text{deep}}/\mathbb{E}[\alpha]_{\text{deep}}$ (two-rate for MeV and AIV; eight-rate for EBOV; Table 2). On a single branch (or small set of deep branches) where synonymous substitutions are completely saturated ($d_S \to \infty$) while nonsynonymous divergence $d_N$ is finite and small, the branch length $t$ (dominated by $d_S$) and the branch-specific ratio $\omega_{\text{deep}} \approx d_N / d_S$ become inversely collinear: as $\hat{d}_S \to \infty$ (the numerical optimizer upper bound), $\hat{\omega}_{\text{deep}} \to 0$ ($0.0004$ in AIV and $0.0005$ in EBOV; Table 2) while yielding essentially the same likelihood. Because these branches hit the numerical upper bound ("infinite branch lengths" in Figure 3, line 82 and line 123), the Latin Hypercube Sampling Importance Resampling (LHS-IR) 95% confidence intervals reported for EBOV ($32.14$, 95% CI $12.14\text{--}32.22$) and AIV ($107.34$, 95% CI $101.06\text{--}117.36$; line 125) reflect the arbitrary numerical cap of the optimizer and the 1D profile-likelihood bounding box rather than a finite-variance asymptotic normal distribution.
* **Two-Step Decoupled Clock Extrapolation vs. Joint Bayesian Codon Dating (Pillar 3):** Because `Lineage+Dual` was not implemented inside BEAST, the authors fixed the tree topology to the BEAST GTR$+\Gamma_4$ MCC tree, re-optimized branch lengths by maximum likelihood in HyPhy under `Lineage+Dual`, and then divided the expanded root height by the tip-calibrated GTR$+\Gamma_4$ clock rate to obtain the revised dates ($899\text{ CE}$ for MeV/RPV; $\ge 70,000\text{ yr}$ for EBOV; $\ge 200,000\text{ yr}$ for AIV). While the authors explicitly call this *"a rather crude approximation"* and *"minimum bounds"* (lines 127, 131), this decoupled multiplication does not propagate coalescent prior or relaxed-clock rate variance jointly with codon branch expansion.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Critique of Prior Measles Dating (Furuse et al. 2010) and Reconciliation with 9th-Century Historical Records [Well Supported]:** The authors identify two concrete flaws in Furuse et al.'s MeV/RPV dating (inclusion of hypermutated/attenuated vaccine strains and imposition of an exponential growth coalescent prior on viruses undergoing vaccine-driven eradication) and show that once vaccine strains are removed and `Lineage+Dual` branch expansion ($2.04\times$ at the MeV–RPV split) is accounted for, the estimated split ($899\text{ CE}$, range $597\text{--}1144\text{ CE}$) aligns with Rhazes' 9th-century treatise without hitting synonymous saturation.
* **Diagnosis of Complete Synonymous Saturation in Deep EBOV and AIV Splits [Well Supported]:** Rather than claiming exact ancient dates for EBOV and AIV, the authors conclude (lines 131, 136, 142) that when deep viral branches hit synonymous saturation under `Lineage+Dual`, molecular clock dating from modern sequences alone has reached its "evolutionary horizon" and can only provide minimum bounds.
