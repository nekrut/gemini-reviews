### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This study presents genome-wide association studies (GWAS) across 36 quantitative traits in 153,950 Korean individuals from the Korean Cancer Prevention Study-II (KCPS2) Biobank (genotyped on either the Illumina GSA v2.0 array [$n = 64,812$] or the Korean Chip v1 [$n = 89,138$] and imputed to 1000 Genomes Project Phase 3). Using linear mixed models (`SAIGE`), the authors identify 2,962 independent genome-wide significant trait–locus associations (2,631 unique loci), of which 301 loci were not previously reported for the corresponding Experimental Factor Ontology (EFO) trait in Open Targets Genetics, the GWAS Catalog, KoGES, or TWB—including an East Asian-enriched missense variant in `CD36` (`p.Pro90Ser`, `rs75326924-T`, $\text{AF} = 0.068$) associated with thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH). The authors further conduct cross-trait genetic correlation analyses (`LDSC`), fixed-effect meta-analyses (`METAL`) across 21 shared traits spanning KCPS2, KoGES ($n = 72\text{K}$), Biobank Japan ($n = 179\text{K}$), Taiwan Biobank ($n = 102\text{K}$), and UK Biobank ($n = 420\text{K}$), comparative genetic architecture modeling (`SBayesS` and `Popcorn`), and `SuSiE` / `coloc.susie` fine-mapping at the pleiotropic `ALDH2` (`rs671`) locus across 26 traits. Full GWAS summary statistics and analysis scripts are publicly archived on Zenodo.

This is a large, well-organized, and reproducible biobank resource that substantially expands East Asian quantitative trait genetics. A few methodological and interpretive limitations temper specific downstream analyses: (1) merging two distinct genotyping arrays imputed against 1000 Genomes Phase 3 (which lacks Korean reference haplotypes) and feeding `SAIGE` summary statistics with a 50K-subset LD matrix into `SuSiE` at an extreme-effect locus (`rs671` for alcohol intake, $P = 1.9 \times 10^{-2658}$) produces six likely artifactual secondary single-variant credible sets with $\text{PIP} = 1.00$ that vanish when $L = 1$; (2) the `SBayesS` cross-biobank genetic architecture comparison mixes linear regression summary statistics (KCPS2, TWB, BBJ, UKB) with linear mixed model summary statistics (KoGES) and applies a Korean LD matrix to Taiwanese summary statistics (yielding an out-of-bounds SNP-heritability of $h^2_g = 1.12$ for bilirubin on chromosome 2); and (3) the 5-biobank fixed-effect meta-analysis does not account for potential sample overlap between the two Korean cohorts (KCPS2 and KoGES) or cross-ancestry allelic effect heterogeneity. These issues can be addressed through targeted sensitivity analyses and clarifications in the text.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Discovery of 2,962 genome-wide significant associations (including 301 previously unreported trait loci such as `CD36` `rs75326924` for TSH) across 36 quantitative traits in KCPS2: [Well Supported].**  
  Association testing via `SAIGE` LMM with leave-one-chromosome-out (LOCO) adjustment and array covariate control shows well-calibrated test statistics across all 36 traits (median $\lambda_{\text{GC}} = 1.23$, median S-LDSC intercept $= 1.04$, Supplementary Data 1). The multi-step novelty filter against Open Targets Genetics, the GWAS Catalog (at both specific EFO term and broader EFO category levels), recent large height/TSH GWAS, KoGES, and TWB is carefully documented, and the enrichment of higher minor allele frequencies in East Asians versus Europeans among the 301 novel loci ($0.207$ vs. $0.118$, $P = 2.2 \times 10^{-16}$) is biologically and statistically sound.

* **Five-biobank meta-analysis (KCPS2, KoGES, BBJ, TWB, UKB) identifies 12,224 significant loci across 21 traits, including 4,588 novel loci: [Moderately Supported].**  
  While the large combined sample size ($\sim 927\text{K}$) provides substantial discovery power, three methodological details affect the `4,588` count: first, the Abstract states that the 4,588 loci *"were not significant in any contributing GWAS"*, whereas the Results, Discussion, and Methods state that they were not significant in *"any of the other four contributing GWAS"* (i.e., KoGES, BBJ, TWB, or UKB, which includes loci that *were* already genome-wide significant in KCPS2 alone). Second, the clumping window in `FUMA` was changed from `500 kb` in the KCPS2 analysis to `5 Mb` in the meta-analysis without explanation. Third, fixed-effect inverse-variance-weighted meta-analysis in `METAL` neither adjusts for potential participant overlap between the two nationwide Korean health-checkup cohorts (KCPS2 and KoGES) nor models ancestry-specific effect heterogeneity between the four EAS cohorts and UKB.

* **Comparative genetic architecture (`SBayesS` heritability $h^2_g$, selection parameter $S$, and polygenicity $\pi$) across East Asian and European biobanks: [Moderately Supported].**  
  The negative selection parameter estimates ($S \approx -0.51$) and high within-EAS genetic correlations (`LDSC` $r_g \approx 0.89\text{–}1.00$, compared to `Popcorn` EAS–EUR $r_g = 0.815$) are consistent across cohorts. However, the `SBayesS` comparisons of $h^2_g$ and $\pi$ are sensitive to technical mismatches: applying the KCPS2 Korean LD matrix to TWB summary statistics inflated $h^2_g$ (including an impossible $h^2_g = 1.12$ on chromosome 2 for total bilirubin at `UGT1A1`), KoGES used `SAIGE` LMM summary statistics rather than `PLINK2` linear regression summary statistics, and the estimated polygenicity $\pi$ ranks monotonically with cohort sample size (`UKB [0.02] > BBJ [0.007] > KCPS2 [0.005] > KoGES [0.002] > TWB [0.001]`), reflecting differential statistical power to detect small-effect variants rather than intrinsic population differences in polygenicity.

* **`SuSiE` fine-mapping and `coloc.susie` colocalization identify `rs671` (`ALDH2`) as a shared causal variant across 8 cardiometabolic, hepatic, and dietary traits, alongside 6 additional `PIP = 1` credible sets for alcohol intake: [Fragile].**  
  That `rs671` (`c.1510G>A`, `p.Glu504Lys`) is the primary functional driver at `ALDH2` ($\text{PIP} > 0.90$ and $\text{PP4} = 100\%$ across alcohol intake, `GOT`, `GPT`, `GGT`, `SBP`, `DBP`, triglycerides, and coffee intake) is well established and convincingly replicated here. However, the claim of six additional single-variant credible sets all with $\text{PIP} = 1.00$ (`rs555501971`, `rs141043717`, `rs61055528`, `rs149178839`, `rs11066008`, `rs550463060`) at the same locus for alcohol intake is a classic summary-statistic fine-mapping artifact: at an extreme lead association ($P = 1.9 \times 10^{-2658}$, $Z^2 \approx 12,240$), minor LD mismatches between the two merged genotyping arrays (`GSA` vs. `Korean-chip` differential imputation quality) or between full-sample `SAIGE` LMM statistics and the 50K unrelated LD matrix induce spurious conditional signals with $\text{PIP} = 1.00$.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Validate Secondary `ALDH2` Fine-Mapping Signals Against Array-Specific LD Mismatch (`DENTIST` / `SLALOM` / Individual-Level Conditioning)
* **WHY:** In `SuSiE-RSS`, when the primary causal variant has $Z^2 > 10^4$ (`rs671` $P = 1.9 \times 10^{-2658}$), even a $0.5\%$ discrepancy in pairwise $r$ between the summary statistics (computed across $n = 153,950$ samples merged from two different SNP arrays) and the reference LD matrix (computed from a 50K subset) produces huge residual $Z$-scores that `SuSiE` assigns $\text{PIP} = 1.00$.
* **WHAT:** Test whether the six secondary `PIP = 1.00` variants for alcohol intake remain genome-wide significant when conditioning directly on individual-level `rs671` dosage in `SAIGE` (or separately within the GSA-chip and Korean-chip subcohorts), rather than relying on summary-statistic LD conditioning (`SuSiE-RSS` / `GCTA-COJO`).
* **HOW:** Since the authors have individual-level KCPS2 genotypes and phenotypes, run a single region-wide `SAIGE` step 2 scan across `chr12:111.7M–112.7M` including individual-level `rs671` dosage (and `rs671 × sex`, given the strong sex-dimorphic drinking rate in Korea) directly as a covariate in the model, or run `SuSiE` Diagnostic (`susieR::estimate_s_rss`) to check LD consistency before claiming seven independent causal variants at `ALDH2`.

#### 2. Check Sample Overlap Between KCPS2 and KoGES and Clarify the Meta-Analysis Novelty Count
* **WHY:** Both KCPS2 ($n = 153,950$) and KoGES ($n = 72,000$) recruited middle-aged South Korean adults through national health examination centers, creating a realistic possibility of participant overlap. Uncorrected overlap inflates inverse-variance-weighted `METAL` $Z$-scores. Additionally, the Abstract and Main Text give conflicting definitions of the `4,588` meta-analysis loci.
* **WHAT:** Report the bivariate `LDSC` intercept between KCPS2 and KoGES across the 21 shared traits to quantify any sample overlap, adjust the meta-analysis if the intercept exceeds zero, and reconcile the definition of the 4,588 loci.
* **HOW:**
  1. Extract the cross-biobank `LDSC` intercept ($I_{\text{LDSC}} \approx \rho_{\text{pheno}} \frac{N_{\text{overlap}}}{\sqrt{N_1 N_2}}$) already computed during the KCPS2–KoGES genetic correlation analysis (Supplementary Data 9) and report its median and range in the Methods. If $I_{\text{LDSC}}$ is significantly above 0, apply overlap-aware meta-analysis (`METAL` with `OVERLAP ON` or `MTAG`/`METASOFT`).
  2. Reconcile the Abstract (*"4588 loci that were not significant in any contributing GWAS"*) with the Results/Methods (*"not genome-wide significant in any of the other four contributing GWAS"*), reporting both numbers explicitly: (a) loci not significant in KoGES, BBJ, TWB, or UKB (`4,588`), and (b) loci not significant in *any* of the five individual cohorts including KCPS2.

#### 3. Account for Sample Size When Interpreting `SBayesS` Polygenicity ($\pi$) and Standardize Summary Statistics
* **WHY:** In `SBayesS`, the posterior estimate of $\pi$ (proportion of common SNPs with non-zero effects) shrinks toward the prior or underestimates very small effects when GWAS sample size is smaller, which explains why $\pi$ follows the exact order of biobank sample sizes (`UKB 420K > BBJ 179K > KCPS2 153K > KoGES 72K` / `TWB 102K`).
* **WHAT:** Explicitly note in the Results and Discussion that cross-biobank differences in $\pi$ are driven primarily by differences in GWAS discovery power (sample size) rather than biological differences in trait polygenicity across ancestries, and use external 1000G Phase 3 Southern Han Chinese (`CHS`) LD alongside KCPS2 LD as a sensitivity check for TWB.
* **HOW:** Add a statement in *Genetic architecture compared between KCPS2, KoGES, BBJ, TWB, and UKB* noting the strong rank correlation between cohort $N$ and median $\pi$ ($\rho = 0.90$), or down-sample/scale the effective sample size $N_{\text{eff}}$ in the summary statistics to a matched sample size ($N = 72\text{K}$) across cohorts to test whether $\pi$ differences persist at equal power.

#### 4. Technical and Editorial Cleanup
* **WHY:** Several minor numerical and typographical inconsistencies appear in the Methods section.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  1. **Pre-QC vs. Post-QC Sample Counts (*Study population* vs. *Results*):** In *Results* (Paragraph 1), the text reports `64,812` participants on the GSA-chip and `89,138` on the Korean-chip (summing to `153,950`), whereas *Methods* (*Study population*) states *"78,260 samples"* on GSA v2.0 and *"90,245 samples"* on Korean Chip v1 (summing to `168,505`). Clarify in *Methods* that `168,505` is the pre-QC genotyped count and `153,950` (`64,812 + 89,138`) is the post-QC sample size.
  2. **Biobank Abbreviation Typo (*Methods — Taiwan Biobank*):** Change *"We extracted 23 quantitative traits from BBJ that matched the quantitative traits we analyzed in KCPS2"* to *"from TWB"*.
  3. **Trait Category Count and Spelling (*Methods — Genome-wide association analysis in KCPS2*):** Correct `arthrometry` to `anthropometry`, and standardize the number of trait categories across sections (currently stated as `9` categories in *Results* Paragraph 1, `7` categories in *Results — Genetic architecture*, `8` categories [while listing 9] in *Methods — Genome-wide association analysis in KCPS2*, and `6` categories in *Methods — Genetic architecture*).
  4. **Clumping Window Size (*Methods*):** Clarify why `FUMA` locus definition used a `500 kb` window for the KCPS2 single-cohort GWAS but a `5 Mb` window (`5,000 kb`) for the 5-biobank meta-analysis.
