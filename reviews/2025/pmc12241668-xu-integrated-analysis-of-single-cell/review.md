## 1. Executive Summary & Synthesis
The manuscript attempts to characterize lactylation-related gene (LRG) signatures across glioblastoma (GBM) single-cell RNA-sequencing (scRNA-seq), spatial transcriptomics, and bulk transcriptomic cohorts (`GSE131928`, `GSE162631`, `GSE273274`, `GSE197543`, `GSE278456`, TCGA, and CGGA), followed by machine-learning selection of three genes (`SSBP1`, `RPA3`, and `TUBB2A`) and in vitro knockdown experiments in U87 and U251 cells. However, the study fails basic scientific, methodological, and editorial integrity checks. Throughout the text, machine-translation or synonym-swapping artifacts replace "lactylation" with "emulsification" (Abstract lines 16, 21: "experimentally validated the emulsification efficiency", "prognostic gene model associated with emulsification in GBM") or "agglutination activity" (Results line 129: "Cancer cell, monocytes and stromal cell exhibited the highest agglutination activity"). Worse, the text, methods, and figure captions contain pervasive internal contradictions—including sample counts, cluster counts, module names, and cell-type identities borrowed from unrelated vascular/connective-tissue analyses ("fibroblast network" at line 151; "expression level of key genes in VSMC" in Figure 9H–I; "immune-related adverse events" and "immune checkpoint blockade therapy" in the Conclusion at line 225)—alongside an inverted scRNA-seq quality-control filter that retains dying cells with $>20\%$ mitochondrial reads and a circular feature-selection pipeline.

## 2. Direct Appraisal of Core Claims
* **Claim 1: Single-cell clustering of 40 GBM samples identifies 8 (or 11) cell types with cancer cells and monocytes showing the highest lactylation activity** — `[Methodologically Flawed]`
  The scRNA-seq preprocessing and clustering pipeline is internally inconsistent and methodologically inverted. In Materials and Methods (line 56), the authors state they retained "cells with more than 20 percent mitochondrial genes," which selects exclusively for lysed, apoptotic, or low-quality droplets rather than filtering them out ($<20\%$). The pipeline also cites non-existent Seurat functions (`"Discover Variant Signatures"`, `"FindAIIMarkers"`, and `"lever lactylation cell cycle markers incorporated in the Seurat package"`, lines 56–59). Furthermore, sample and cluster numbers contradict each other across sections: line 51 describes 40 samples (`ndGBM = 22, rGBM = 18`), whereas Results line 121 states "extracting three samples from the GEO database (Figure 2A)" while the Figure 2A caption states "UMAP profiles of 40 samples"; Results line 121 reports "27 clusters were generated (Figure 2B)" and "8 distinct cell types (Figures 2C, D)", whereas the Figure 2B caption reports "18 cell clusters" and Figure 2F reports "11 major cell types." Similarly, Results line 129 claims Figure 3A compares recurrent GBM (`rGBM`) against newly diagnosed GBM (`ndGBM`), whereas the Figure 3A caption states it compares "normal individuals and patients with GBM."
* **Claim 2: hdWGCNA and spatial transcriptomics define a lactylation-associated co-expression network in GBM** — `[Methodologically Flawed]`
  In Results line 151, the authors state that hdWGCNA constructed "A scale-free fibroblast network... corresponding to an optimal connectivity degree of $R^2 = 0.85$," despite analyzing brain tumors (GBM) where fibroblasts are not even among the annotated cell types in Figure 2. In the same paragraph (line 151), the text states that the "red, turquoise, blue, and magenta module exhibited the highest level of expression in the LHS group," but the very next sentence selects the top 25 KME genes from the "black, turquoise, blue, and brown modules," while the Figure 5B caption refers to "eight modules" and Figure 5C refers to "15 gene modules." Subsequently, Disease Ontology enrichment of these genes (line 162) highlights "connective tissue cancer, autosomal dominant disease and ovary epithelial cancer," indicating uncurated or mismatched pipeline outputs.
* **Claim 3: Machine-learning algorithms identify `SSBP1`, `RPA3`, and `TUBB2A` as diagnostic/prognostic lactylation hub genes and risk-score predictors** — `[Methodologically Flawed]`
  The feature selection pipeline suffers from circularity (data leakage) and undefined target variables. First, candidate genes are derived by intersecting hdWGCNA module genes with differentially expressed genes (DEGs) between the high-lactylation score (`LHS`) and low-lactylation score (`LLS`) cell groups (line 168). The authors then apply machine-learning classifiers (`LASSO`, `RF`, `Boruta`, `XGBoost`, plus univariate Cox regression—described inconsistently as "four machine algorithms" in line 81 and Figure 7F, but five algorithms in line 173) without specifying whether the response variable for the classifiers is `LHS` vs. `LLS` status, tumor vs. normal, or survival. They then validate `SSBP1`, `RPA3`, and `TUBB2A` in Figure 8B–D by testing whether their expression differs between `LHS` and `LLS` ($p < 0.0001$), which is circular because the input pool was already pre-filtered for DEGs between `LHS` and `LLS`. Moreover, Figure 9H–I captions (line 201) describe pseudotime expression of these genes in "VSMC" (vascular smooth muscle cells), and the Discussion conclusion (line 225) claims the signature predicts "immune-related adverse events" and "response to immune checkpoint blockade therapy," neither of which was evaluated anywhere in the study.
* **Claim 4: Knockdown of `TUBB2A` and immunoprecipitation (IP) validate lactylation-driven proliferation, migration, and invasion in GBM cells** — `[Fragile]`
  While functional assays (CCK-8, EdU, wound healing, and Transwell invasion in U87 and U251 cells, Figure 10) are presented, the experimental validation of protein lactylation is not supported by the described protocol. Results line 206 claims that "immunoprecipitation (IP) was conducted to validate the lactylation of TUBB2A (Figure 10A)," and Abstract line 16 calls this "emulsification efficiency." However, the IP/Western blot protocol in Materials and Methods (lines 106–108) lists only antibodies against `SSBP1` (misspelled `SSBPP1`), `RPA3`, `TUBB2A`, and `GAPDH`, with no mention of an anti-pan-lactyllysine (`pan-Kla`) antibody or L-lactate treatment required to detect or assay protein lactylation.

## 3. Essential Revisions & Methodological Critiques
1. **Resolve pervasive text-figure contradictions and template/splice artifacts:**
   - Explain why terms from unrelated biological contexts appear in the manuscript: "fibroblast network" (line 151), "VSMC" in Figure 9H–I (line 201), and "immune-related adverse events" / "immune checkpoint blockade therapy" in the concluding paragraph (line 225).
   - Correct all machine-translation/synonym errors replacing "lactylation" with "emulsification" (lines 16, 21), "lactoacylation" (line 63), and "agglutination activity" (line 129).
   - Reconcile the contradictory sample counts (3 vs. 40 in line 121 vs. Figure 2A), cluster counts (27 vs. 18 in line 121 vs. Figure 2B), cell-type counts (8 vs. 11 in line 121 vs. Figure 2F), group comparisons (`rGBM` vs. `ndGBM` in line 129 vs. normal vs. GBM in Figure 3A), and hdWGCNA module lists (`red, turquoise, blue, magenta` vs. `black, turquoise, blue, brown` in line 151; 8 vs. 15 modules in Figure 5B–C).
2. **Fix scRNA-seq quality control and eliminate circular feature selection:**
   - In Materials and Methods (line 56), clarify whether cells with $>20\%$ mitochondrial reads were retained (an error that selects damaged cells) or excluded ($<20\%$), and provide the exact R/Seurat code used (`FindAllMarkers`, `FindVariableFeatures`).
   - Do not use DEGs defined between `LHS` and `LLS` cells as input to classifiers trained on the same cohort and then report `LHS` vs. `LLS` differential expression (Figure 8B–D) as independent validation. Define the exact binary or survival outcome used for `LASSO`, `RF`, `Boruta`, `XGBoost`, and `Uncox` in the TCGA training cohort, and report external validation metrics (concordance index, time-dependent ROC AUC with 95% confidence intervals, and calibration curves) on the independent CGGA cohort.
3. **Provide authentic biochemical evidence for `TUBB2A` lactylation:**
   - Specify the exact anti-lactyllysine antibody (catalog number, dilution, and IP pull-down vs. immunoblot configuration) used in Figure 10A, as the current Methods section (lines 106–108) only lists total protein antibodies (`TUBB2A`, `SSBP1`, `RPA3`, `GAPDH`), which cannot measure post-translational lactylation.
