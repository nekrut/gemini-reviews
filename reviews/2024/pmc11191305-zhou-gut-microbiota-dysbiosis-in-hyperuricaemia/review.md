## 1. Executive Summary & Synthesis
The manuscript investigates the role of the gut-kidney axis in hyperuricaemia (HUA)-induced renal injury using a uricase-knockout (`UOX-/-`) Sprague-Dawley rat model, 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing, targeted kidney LC-MS/MS metabolomics (`MT1000` MRM panel), and inter-species fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) into antibiotic-depleted wild-type (WT) and `NLRP3-/-` mice subjected to unilateral renal ischemia/reperfusion (I/R) injury. While the multi-modal experimental design—progressing from spontaneous HUA rat phenotyping to microbial/metabolic profiling and causal testing in `NLRP3-/-` recipient mice—is biologically well motivated, the statistical and bioinformatic execution has several substantive weaknesses: (1) pooling fecal samples from donor rats into a single composite slurry prior to FMT pseudoreplicates the donor microbiota treatment ($N_{\text{pool}} = 1$ per group); (2) multi-group $2 \times 2$ factorial experiments (`FMT Donor` $\times$ `Sham vs. I/R` in Figures 6 and 7) are analyzed with unpaired two-group Student's $t$-tests or unadjusted post-hoc Fisher's LSD tests rather than two-way ANOVA with family-wise error control; (3) targeted metabolomics (266 differential metabolites) and microbe-metabolite correlation networks lack false discovery rate (FDR) correction; and (4) there are internal inconsistencies in the 16S bioinformatic pipeline description, Western blot figure legends (`NLRP3` protein detection in `NLRP3-/-` mice), and data availability statements.

## 2. Direct Appraisal of Core Claims
* **Claim 1: `UOX-/-` rats develop sustained hyperuricaemia (`~400 μmol/L`), renal tubular injury, interstitial fibrosis, and renal NLRP3 inflammasome activation** — `[Well Supported]`
  The longitudinal characterization of `UOX-/-` rats against WT littermates across 4–24 weeks (Figures 1–3) is supported by converging lines of evidence: elevated serum uric acid, creatinine (`SCR`), BUN, and 24-h urine protein (Figure 1D–H); birefringent urate crystals under polarized light, PAS tubular injury scoring, TUNEL staining, transmission electron microscopy, and five urinary tubular damage biomarkers (`KIM-1`, `NGAL`, `RBP`, `β2-MG`, `CysC`, Figure 2B–M); and Masson trichrome/collagen III fibrosis staining alongside cleaved caspase-1 `p20`, `IL-1β`, and `NLRP3` immunoblots (Figure 3).
* **Claim 2: HUA induces gut microbiota dysbiosis (enriched `Proteobacteria` and `Escherichia-Shigella`) and impairs intestinal barrier integrity in `UOX-/-` rats** — `[Moderately Supported]`
  The in vivo FITC-dextran (`FD-4`) portal-vein permeability assay, colonic Alcian blue/H&E histology, and tight-junction Western blots (`ZO-1`, `occludin`, `claudin-1`, Figure 4P–T) provide direct physiological evidence of intestinal barrier dysfunction. However, the 16S rRNA bioinformatic analysis ($n = 6$/group, Figure 4A–O) has three methodological flaws:
  1. Beta-diversity separation on Bray–Curtis PCoA plots (Figure 4A and Figure S8B–C) is asserted visually ("showed a relative separation") without reporting a permutational multivariate ANOVA (`PERMANOVA` / `adonis2` $R^2$ and $p$-value).
  2. The 16S pipeline description in Methods (chunk 2 line 27) is internally contradictory: it states sequences were denoised with the `DADA2` plugin in QIIME2 (which resolves exact Amplicon Sequence Variants, ASVs), then states the SILVA v138 classifier was applied "for the classification of ASVs with a threshold of 97% sequence similarity," and finally states "`PICRUSt` based on OTUs was employed."
  3. Differential genus testing (`Wilcoxon rank-sum tests`, Figure 4G–N) and `PICRUSt` KEGG pathway predictions (Figure 4O) across dozens of features are reported at nominal $p < 0.05$ without Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction.
* **Claim 3: Targeted metabolomics identifies 266 altered kidney metabolites, including gut-derived uremic toxins (`IS`, `p-Cresol`, `HA`, `PAGln`, `PAGly`, `KA`, `KYN`, `QA`) that correlate with `Escherichia-Shigella` and renal dysfunction** — `[Moderately Supported]`
  Targeted MRM profiling (`MT1000` kit) in $n = 6$ kidneys per group shows accumulation of tryptophan- and aromatic-amino-acid-derived uremic toxins (Figure 5D). However, calling 266 significant metabolites out of ~1,000 targeted transitions using `OPLS-DA VIP > 1` and unadjusted two-tailed Student's $t$-test $p < 0.05$ (chunk 2 line 20), and constructing microbe-metabolite-kidney Pearson correlation heatmaps/networks at unadjusted $p < 0.05$ on $n = 6$ per group (Figure 5E, Figures S5–S6), does not control for multiple hypothesis testing or between-group Simpson's paradox (where pooling $n = 6$ WT and $n = 6$ `UOX-/-` animals induces artificial correlations between any two variables that differ between genotypes).
* **Claim 4: FMT of HUA rat microbiota into mice exacerbates I/R-induced renal injury and barrier loss in an `NLRP3`-dependent manner** — `[Fragile]`
  While the `WT` and `NLRP3-/-` recipient mouse experiments (Figures 6 and 7) address causality, three experimental and statistical issues weaken this claim:
  1. **Donor pooling pseudoreplication:** In Methods (chunk 2 line 37), fresh feces from donor rats were "combined and resuspended" into a single composite suspension per genotype prior to gavaging recipient mice. Pooling donor feces collapses biological variance across donor rats into a single technical pool ($N_{\text{donor}} = 1$), so the $n = 4$–$6$ recipient mice measure recipient-level variance rather than independent biological replication of the HUA donor microbiota.
  2. **Inappropriate statistical testing for $2 \times 2$ designs:** Figures 6 and 7 involve four experimental groups (`FMT_WT + Sham`, `FMT_UOX + Sham`, `FMT_WT + I/R`, `FMT_UOX + I/R`), yet the Figure 7 caption (chunk 1 line 143) states comparisons were performed using "two-tailed unpaired Student's $t$-tests," and the Methods section (chunk 2 line 47) specifies one-way ANOVA followed by the unadjusted "post hoc LSD test," which fails to control family-wise Type I error. Furthermore, claiming that the effect is eliminated in `NLRP3-/-` mice requires testing the `Genotype (WT vs. NLRP3-/-) × Donor Microbiota` interaction term rather than relying on $p > 0.05$ within $n = 4$–$6$ `NLRP3-/-` mice.
  3. **Western blot anomalies in Figure 7I–J:** Results line 137 states that "the expression of NLRP3, cleaved caspase-1 p20, and IL-1β in the kidneys of `NLRP3-/-` mice after I/R surgery remained similar (Fig. 7 I and J)." If the recipient mice are germline `NLRP3-/-` knockouts, `NLRP3` protein should be absent rather than expressed at similar levels across groups; moreover, both Figure 6H–I and Figure 7I–J captions erroneously describe these samples as "in the kidneys of **rats** ($n = 4$)" instead of recipient mice.

## 3. Essential Revisions & Methodological Critiques
1. **Apply formal multivariate and multiple-testing corrections to 16S and metabolomics data:**
   - Report `PERMANOVA` (`adonis2`, 999 permutations) $F$-statistics, $R^2$, and $p$-values for all Bray–Curtis PCoA plots (Figure 4A, Figure S8B–C).
   - Apply Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction ($q < 0.05$ or $q < 0.10$) to the `MT1000` targeted metabolomics $t$-tests, `PICRUSt` KEGG pathway comparisons, and microbe–metabolite–clinical correlation matrices (or compute correlations within the `UOX-/-` group separately from the `WT` group to avoid two-group clustering artifacts).
   - Clarify in Methods (chunk 2 line 27) whether `DADA2` ASVs or 97% OTUs were used, and whether `PICRUSt2` (which accepts ASVs) or `PICRUSt1` was executed.
2. **Re-analyze FMT experiments with two-way ANOVA and resolve the `NLRP3-/-` Western blot discrepancy:**
   - Replace unpaired $t$-tests and post-hoc Fisher's LSD tests in Figures 6 and 7 with two-way ANOVA (`Donor Microbiota × I/R Surgery` and `Recipient Genotype × Donor Microbiota`) followed by Tukey's HSD or Holm–Šidák post-hoc correction. Explicitly acknowledge the limitation of using a pooled donor fecal inoculum in the Discussion.
   - Explain why a band for `NLRP3` is quantified in `NLRP3-/-` mice in Figure 7I–J (line 137), correct "kidneys of rats" to "kidneys of recipient mice" in the Figure 6H–I and Figure 7I–J captions, and remove unused antibodies (`anti-E-cadherin`, `anti-N-cadherin`, `anti-collagen IV`, `anti-α-SMA`) from the Western blot Methods (chunk 2 line 13) if those blots are not included in the manuscript.
3. **Fix the contradictory Data Availability statements:**
   - In Methods (chunk 2 line 25), the authors provide NCBI SRA Bioproject `PRJNA1026121` for the 16S data, yet the *Availability of data and materials* sections (chunk 2 lines 97 and 142) state "No datasets were generated or analysed during the current study." Update the Data Availability statement to list `PRJNA1026121` and `Additional file 2`.
