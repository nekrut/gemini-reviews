### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

Amendola et al. retrospectively screen 435 archival clinical specimens (148 oropharyngeal swabs, 141 urine samples, and 146 sera) collected from 156 measles/rubella-negative ("discarded") morbilliform rash patients in Lombardy, Italy across three epochs: a pre-pandemic control window (August 2018 – July 2019, $N=100$), a late pre-pandemic window (August 2019 – February 2020, $N=44$), and a pandemic window (March 2020 – March 2021, $N=12$). Using four hemi-nested RT-PCR assays targeting short fragments of `NsP3` (263 bp, spanning C3037T), `RdRp` (335 bp, spanning C14408T), `Spike A` (421 bp, spanning A23403G / D614G), and `Spike B` (470 bp, receptor-binding domain), alongside CDC real-time RT-qPCR, Euroimmun IgG/IgM/IgA ELISA, and plaque-reduction neutralization tests ($\text{PRNT}_{80}$), the authors report SARS-CoV-2 RNA in 11/44 (25%) pre-pandemic cases (earliest September 12, 2019) and 2/12 (16.7%) pandemic cases. Mapping Sanger amplicons (GenBank `MZ223385`–`MZ223398`) and re-analyzed Brazilian sewage reads (`PRJNA679821`) onto the Mutation Order Approach (MOA) backbone of Kumar et al. (2021), the authors find that every pre-pandemic Italian amplicon covering positions 3037, 14408, or 23403 carries the derived B.1 ($\alpha\beta$) alleles, and extrapolate a `proCoV2` emergence date of late June to late July 2019.

While leveraging subnational measles/rubella surveillance biobanks to probe early cryptic viral circulation is an imaginative epidemiological concept—and the authors commendably deposit their 14 Sanger sequences (`MZ223385`–`MZ223398`) and test 183 pre-August 2019 negative controls—the manuscript's central conclusions are severely undermined by molecular, evolutionary, and epidemiological contradictions. Specifically: (1) **0 of 13 cases tested positive by standard CDC real-time RT-qPCR**, and **11 of 13 cases amplified in only 1 of 4 hemi-nested assays**, yet every amplified fragment in October 2019 carries the full derived B.1 haplotype (`C3037T`, `C14408T`, `A23403G`) that served as the laboratory's positive control / dominant European strain in 2020–2021; (2) claiming that the highly fit B.1 (D614G) lineage circulated at a 25% positivity rate in Lombardy rash patients from October 2019 to February 2020 without triggering exponential hospitalization surges for 4.5 months is biophysically and epidemiologically implausible; and (3) backward linear extrapolation of $k=6$ mutations from short single-locus amplicons without whole genomes or a phylogenetic clock model cannot support a June–July 2019 origin.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Pre-Pandemic Circulation of Derived B.1 ($\alpha\beta$) Variants (`C3037T`, `C14408T`, `A23403G`) in Italy in October 2019: Poorly Supported / High Risk of Amplicon Carryover Artifact.**  
  Every pre-pandemic specimen that yielded an `NsP3` (Patients #5, #7), `RdRp` (Patients #3, #4, #6, #9), or `Spike A` (Patient #9) sequence in October–December 2019 carried the derived B.1 alleles (`3037T`, `14408T`, `23403G`), with zero ancestral `Wuhan-Hu-1` (lineage B) or lineage A alleles ever detected at those loci. In global genomic surveillance, the B.1 haplotype ($\alpha_1\text{--}\alpha_3 + \beta_1\text{--}\beta_3$) arose on the lineage B background in early 2020. Detecting derived B.1 alleles in October 2019—exclusively after 2 rounds of 35+ cycle hemi-nested PCR, in samples 100% negative by CDC real-time RT-qPCR, where 9/11 pre-pandemic patients failed to amplify 3 of the 4 target genes, and where a SARS-CoV-2 positive control was co-amplified in every round—is the classic diagnostic signature of trace laboratory amplicon/positive-control carryover rather than authentic pre-Wuhan circulation of B.1.

* **June–July 2019 Emergence of the SARS-CoV-2 Progenitor (`proCoV2`): Conceptually Invalid / Uncalibrated Extrapolation.**  
  The authors assume that because individual 263–421 bp fragments in October 2019 carry one or two $\beta$ mutations, the unsequenced remainder of those viral genomes must also carry all three $\beta$ and all three $\alpha$ mutations ($k=6$ mutations from `proCoV2`), and then subtract $11.6\text{--}16.2$ weeks from October 17, 2019. Even setting aside the contamination risk, extrapolating a multi-locus root age from single-amplicon Sanger reads (where no single patient was ever shown to carry all three $\beta$ mutations, let alone $\alpha_1\text{--}\alpha_3$) violates basic molecular clock requirements.

* **4.5 Months of Cryptic B.1 (Spike D614G) Community Transmission in Lombardy Without Epidemic Expansion: Biophysically & Epidemiologically Implausible.**  
  In Discussion Section 4.2, the authors favor the hypothesis that "a strain capable of efficient human-to-human transmission [with an $\alpha\beta$ mutational signature] was circulating undetected in northern Italy by September 2019." However, the $\alpha\beta$ (B.1 / D614G) strain is the exact high-$R_0$ virus that caused Lombardy's catastrophic exponential mortality surge within weeks of February 2020. A virus with $R_0 \approx 2.5\text{--}3.5$ and a doubling time of 3–5 days infecting 25% (11/44) of non-measles rash cases across Milan and Brescia in September–October 2019 could not remain epidemiologically silent for 130+ days without overwhelming ICUs by November 2019.

* **Serological Corroboration of Pre-Pandemic Cases (ELISA & $\text{PRNT}_{80}$): Weakly Supported / Internally Contradictory.**  
  Among the 11 pre-pandemic RNA-positive cases, **0 of 9 tested sera achieved neutralizing capability at the standard $\text{PRNT}_{80}$ threshold** (only Patient #5 showed partial 62% plaque reduction at 1:10 dilution), whereas two pandemic cases (#12, #14) neutralized at 1:60 and 1:160. Moreover, the authors found 12 ELISA-positive sera (including 4 with 50–59% plaque reduction at 1:10) in the *August 2018 – September 11, 2019 control cohort* and dismissed them as false-positive/inconclusive background reactivity—yet treated identical non-neutralizing ELISA reactivity in pre-pandemic cases (#1, #4, #6) as confirmatory evidence of SARS-CoV-2 infection.

* **Etiological Attribution of the 2019 Measles/Rubella Discard Rate Increase to SARS-CoV-2: Overstated.**  
  Figure 1 shows that the proportion of discarded measles/rubella cases rose in late 2019. However, measles epidemics naturally cycle in multi-year waves (Italy experienced a massive measles outbreak in 2017–2018 followed by a post-epidemic trough in 2019). When true measles incidence drops while baseline rash reporting continues (due to HHV-6, parvovirus B19, enteroviruses, EBV, or drug reactions), the *percentage* of measles-negative ("discarded") cases mathematically rises without requiring a novel coronavirus.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Disclose the Positive Control Genotype and Audit Against Patient Sequences
* **WHY:** In nested PCR assays operating at the limit of detection (where CDC RT-qPCR is 100% negative), trace aerosol or reagent carryover from the positive control or pandemic clinical isolates processed in the same facility is the primary competing hypothesis whenever archival 2019 samples yield 2020-derived B.1 haplotypes.
* **WHAT:** Allow readers to evaluate whether the positive control could be the template source for `C3037T`, `C14408T`, and `A23403G`.
* **HOW:** Explicitly report the exact Pango lineage and complete nucleotide sequence of the "RNA isolated from a SARS-CoV-2-positive patient" used as the positive control in each PCR round. If the positive control was a B.1-derived Italian isolate carrying `C3037T`, `C14408T`, and `A23403G`, acknowledge that nested amplicon identity at those positions cannot rule out positive-control carryover without independent whole-genome sequencing in a SARS-CoV-2-naive ancient-DNA/cleanroom facility.

#### 2. Explain or Resolve the Coordinate Shift in `NsP3` Mutations (`T2987C` / `T3012C`) and Verify Chromatogram Quality
* **WHY:** The authors argue against contamination by pointing to two unique non-synonymous mutations in Patient #5 (`MZ223388`): `T2987C (F908L)` and `T3012C (L916S)`. However, in `Wuhan-Hu-1` (`NC_045512.2`), nucleotide position 2987 is in codon 908 of **ORF1a** (`CTT`, Leucine, not Phenylalanine; Phenylalanine is at codon 907, `TTT`, nt 2984–2986, or codon 89, nt 2985–2987 of NsP3), and Table 2 defines the `NsP3` amplicon (`2949–3212`) as amino acids `76–164` of NsP3 (where `C3037T` is labeled `F106F`), whereas the text switches to full `ORF1a` polyprotein numbering (`F908L`, `L916S`) with a 1-codon frame shift. Furthermore, non-proofreading `DreamTaq` polymerase across 70+ total cycles of two-round hemi-nested PCR starting from 1–2 template copies routinely introduces $1\text{--}2\times 10^{-4}$ $\text{T}\to\text{C}$ transition errors per cycle.
* **WHAT:** Harmonize coordinate numbering and distinguish Taq polymerase incorporation error from genuine in-vivo viral mutations.
* **HOW:** Re-verify `MZ223388` against `NC_045512.2` to correct the codon numbering discrepancy (`F908L` vs `F106F`), and repeat the `NsP3` amplification from the original Patient #5 RNA extract in duplicate using a high-fidelity proofreading polymerase to confirm whether `T2987C` and `T3012C` are reproducible template variants or single-molecule `DreamTaq` errors.

#### 3. Apply Uniform Serological Criteria Across Control (2018–2019) and Pre-Pandemic (Late 2019) Cohorts
* **WHY:** In the pre-September 2019 control cohort ($N=98$ sera), 12 samples (12.2%) were ELISA-positive (IgM/IgA/IgG) and 4 showed 50–59% PRNT plaque reduction at 1:10; these were dismissed as non-specific background. In the pre-pandemic cohort, 4 of 9 RNA-positive cases with available serum were ELISA-positive and 1 showed 62% plaque reduction at 1:10 ($\text{PRNT}_{80}$ negative).
* **WHAT:** Test statistically whether serological reactivity in the pre-pandemic cases exceeds the false-positive baseline established by the 2018–2019 control cohort.
* **HOW:** Perform a Fisher's exact test comparing ELISA positivity and $\text{PRNT}_{80}$ positivity rates between the pre-September 2019 control cohort (12/98 ELISA+, 0/98 $\text{PRNT}_{80}$+) and the pre-pandemic cohort, and refrain from citing non-neutralizing ($\text{PRNT}_{80}$-negative) ELISA hits as independent confirmation of pre-pandemic SARS-CoV-2 infection.

#### 4. Screen Discarded Measles/Rubella Samples for Common Differential Viral Etiologies
* **WHY:** Concluding that SARS-CoV-2 drove the late-2019 rise in measles/rubella-negative rash cases without testing for standard viral causes of pediatric/adult morbilliform exanthema leaves a major epidemiological confounder unaddressed.
* **WHAT:** Contextualize the discarded rash trend against routine differential pathogens and the natural post-2017/2018 measles trough.
* **HOW:** Report multiplex differential PCR results (or clinical history) for HHV-6, HHV-7, Parvovirus B19, Enterovirus, and EBV in these 44 pre-pandemic cases, and plot absolute measles-positive vs. measles-negative counts in Figure 1 so readers can see that the percentage increase in discarded cases coincides with the collapse of true measles incidence after the 2017–2018 epidemic.

#### 5. Remove or Heavily Qualify the June–July 2019 Molecular Clock Extrapolation
* **WHY:** Inferring that a 335-bp `RdRp` amplicon carrying `C14408T` on October 17, 2019 must belong to a full genome carrying 6 derived $\alpha\beta$ mutations—and linearly subtracting $11.6\text{--}16.2$ weeks—is circular: it assumes the October 2019 sequence is part of the same linear evolutionary trajectory as the 2020 pandemic clade while contradicting the global phylogenetic tree of thousands of whole genomes from Dec 2019 – Feb 2020.
* **WHAT:** Align evolutionary claims with what short Sanger amplicons can actually support.
* **HOW:** Remove the claim of a "late June – late July 2019" progenitor origin from the Abstract, Results, and Conclusions, and state explicitly that short single-gene amplicons cannot date the tMRCA without complete, independently replicated whole-genome sequences.
