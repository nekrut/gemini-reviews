### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript reanalyzes a publicly available RNA sequencing dataset (NCBI GEO accession GSE85107) consisting of two human acute myeloid leukemia (AML) cell lines, OCI-AML2 (wild-type *NPM1*) and OCI-AML3 (*NPM1*-mutant), treated with either DMSO or 10 µM of the DOT1L histone methyltransferase inhibitor EPZ004777 in triplicate ($n = 12$ total samples). The authors report 3,578 differentially expressed genes (DEGs; 1,725 upregulated and 1,853 downregulated), perform KEGG pathway and protein-protein interaction (PPI) network analyses, and conduct blind AutoDock Vina molecular docking of EPZ004777 against DOT1L and six proteins encoded by selected DEGs (CT45A3, HOXA4, SNX19, TPBG, ZNF185, and BEX3). Based on Vina docking scores ranging from $-5.2$ to $-8.2$ kcal/mol, they conclude that EPZ004777 directly binds and modulates SNX19, TPBG, and ZNF185 as alternative pharmacological targets in AML. Although the raw sequencing reads are publicly hosted in GEO, the authors' custom preprocessing scripts, docking configurations, and analysis code are not publicly deposited ("available from the authors on reasonable request").

The manuscript's core bioinformatic and pharmacological conclusions are invalidated by three fundamental methodological errors. First, as explicitly revealed in the captions for Figures 6 and 8 and the header of Table 4, the differential expression contrast ("DEG2") tests the baseline difference between the two cell lines ("gives the change in the AML3 line compared to the AML2 reference category" / "DEG2 Analysis: AML2 vs. AML3 Pathways") rather than the pharmacological treatment effect of EPZ004777 versus DMSO; this is further corroborated by the PCA plot (Figure 3A), where PC1 (68.8% variance) separates OCI-AML2 from OCI-AML3 while DMSO and EPZ004777 replicates cluster together. Second, the preprocessing pipeline introduces directional selection bias by explicitly excluding "genes with minimal downregulation" prior to statistical testing and misapplying PCA to filter 35,237 genes down to 16,050. Third, the study commits a basic molecular biology fallacy by assuming that because a small molecule downregulates the mRNA transcript of a gene (*SNX19*, *TPBG*, *ZNF185*), the small molecule must physically bind the protein encoded by that transcript, and attempts to validate this via uncalibrated, whole-protein blind docking on full-length unvalidated AlphaFold structures using enormous grid boxes (up to $100 \text{ \AA} \times 100 \text{ \AA} \times 100 \text{ \AA}$). Addressing these flaws requires a complete re-analysis of the raw counts using a properly specified generalized linear model testing the treatment and cell-line-by-treatment interaction effects, removal of the circular gene filtering, and removal of the unsupported direct-binding claims.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **EPZ004777 treatment induces 3,578 DEGs (1,725 upregulated and 1,853 downregulated), including downregulation of *CT45A3*, *TPBG*, *HOXA4*, *ZNF185*, and *SNX19* and upregulation of *BEX3*: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The captions for Figure 6 and Figure 8 ("The experimental design is the cell line and gives the change in the AML3 line compared to the AML2 reference category") and the column header of Table 4 ("DEG2 Analysis: AML2 vs. AML3 Pathways") show that the statistical contrast actually compares the OCI-AML3 cell line against the OCI-AML2 cell line, confounding baseline genetic and lineage differences between two distinct patient-derived cell lines with the drug effect of EPZ004777. Furthermore, Section 2.1 states that OCI-AML2 and OCI-AML3 are "both NPM1-mutant AML cell lines," whereas in reality OCI-AML2 is *NPM1*-wild-type (carrying an *MLL-AF6* fusion) and OCI-AML3 is *NPM1*-mutant. In addition, Section 2.2 states that "Genes with minimal downregulation were excluded" during preprocessing and that logarithmic transformation "had a significant impact on the downregulated genes," introducing severe pre-test filtering bias.

* **EPZ004777 suppresses the Rap1 signaling and cell adhesion pathways while activating autoimmune thyroid disease, graft-versus-host disease, and allograft rejection pathways: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Because the underlying DEG list reflects the cell-line comparison (OCI-AML3 vs. OCI-AML2) rather than the drug effect within each cell line, the enriched KEGG pathways reflect baseline transcriptional differences (such as HLA/MHC class II expression differences driving the "Allograft rejection," "Graft-versus-host disease," and "Autoimmune thyroid disease" terms) between two distinct leukemic cell lines. Additionally, Table 4 lists "Proteoglycans in cancer" as simultaneously significantly downregulated (38 genes, $-\log_{10}(\text{FDR}) = 3.18$) and significantly upregulated (28 genes, $-\log_{10}(\text{FDR}) = 17.5$), while Section 3.4 reports the upregulated $-\log_{10}(\text{FDR})$ for the same pathway as $1.75$, demonstrating internal numerical contradiction and a failure to perform directional pathway analysis (e.g., GSEA).

* **EPZ004777 is a potent direct modulator/inhibitor of SNX19 ($-8.2$ kcal/mol), TPBG ($-7.7$ kcal/mol), and ZNF185 ($-7.2$ kcal/mol) proteins: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Transcriptional downregulation of a gene following 7 days of epigenetic inhibitor (DOT1L H3K79 methyltransferase) treatment is a downstream consequence of chromatin remodeling and transcriptional repression, not evidence that the small molecule physically binds the encoded protein product. Moreover, the docking protocol (Section 2.4, Table 2) uses blind docking across massive grid volumes ($60 \times 60 \times 60 \text{ \AA}^3$ to $100 \times 100 \times 100 \text{ \AA}^3$) on full-length AlphaFold models (CT45A3, BEX3, HOXA4, SNX19, ZNF185) without filtering out low-confidence/disordered regions (pLDDT < 50). A flexible, high-molecular-weight adenosine-derivative ligand like EPZ004777 (~540 Da) will yields nonspecific AutoDock Vina scores of $-7$ to $-8$ kcal/mol against virtually any hydrophobic cavity on any protein surface; no negative control ligands or decoy proteins were docked to establish a baseline false-positive binding energy.

* **PCA reduces the gene set from 35,237 genes to 16,050 genes and demonstrates reproducible replicate correlations ($R = 0.82\text{--}0.89$): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In Section 2.1, the authors state that "Dimensionality reduction via Principal Component Analysis (PCA) narrowed this to 16,050 genes for downstream analysis." PCA projects samples onto orthogonal linear combinations of genes (principal components); it does not filter a gene count matrix from 35,237 to 16,050 genes unless an unstated loading threshold was applied. Furthermore, in Section 3.1 (Figure 5), the authors compute Pearson correlations between OCI-AML2 and OCI-AML3 ($R = 0.82\text{--}0.89$) and misinterpret cross-cell-line correlation as biological replicate reproducibility.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Correct the Differential Expression Design Matrix to Test Treatment Rather Than Cell Line Identity
* **WHY:** Testing OCI-AML3 against OCI-AML2 ("DEG2") attributes thousands of baseline cell-line differences (and *NPM1*-mutant vs. *NPM1*-WT differences) to EPZ004777 treatment. Moreover, excluding genes based on directional fold change ("Genes with minimal downregulation were excluded") invalidates false discovery rate control.
* **WHAT:** Re-run the differential expression analysis from the raw GSE85107 count matrix using a proper factorial design without directional pre-filtering.
* **HOW:** In `DESeq2` or `edgeR`, filter out only lowly expressed genes across all samples (e.g., `rowSums(counts >= 10) >= 3`). Specify the design formula `~ CellLine + Treatment + CellLine:Treatment`. Extract three distinct contrasts: (1) `Treatment_EPZ004777_vs_DMSO` within OCI-AML3 (*NPM1*-mutant), (2) `Treatment_EPZ004777_vs_DMSO` within OCI-AML2 (*NPM1*-WT), and (3) the interaction term `CellLineAML3.TreatmentEPZ004777` to identify genes uniquely sensitive to DOT1L inhibition in *NPM1*-mutant cells. Also normalize using the ERCC spike-in controls included in GSE85107 (`estimateSizeFactors` with ERCC control genes).

#### 2. Remove or Rigorously Control the Off-Target Molecular Docking Analysis
* **WHY:** Transcriptional downregulation of a gene by a chromatin-modifying enzyme inhibitor does not imply physical binding of the inhibitor to the encoded protein. Furthermore, blind docking in $60\text{--}100\text{ \AA}$ boxes on unvalidated AlphaFold structures with default Vina scoring produces nonspecific scores driven by molecular weight.
* **WHAT:** Either remove the molecular docking section entirely and frame the study around transcriptional network remodeling downstream of DOT1L inhibition, or implement rigorous biophysical controls if off-target protein binding is still hypothesized.
* **HOW:** If retaining docking, restrict target structures to experimentally resolved domains or high-confidence AlphaFold regions (pLDDT > 70), define specific binding pockets (grid dimensions $\le 25 \times 25 \times 25 \text{ \AA}^3$) using pocket-detection tools (e.g., fpocket), and dock EPZ004777 against a background distribution of 100 random human proteins and 50 property-matched decoy ligands (DUD-E) to compute an empirical $Z$-score and $p$-value for binding specificity relative to DOT1L ($-9.7$ kcal/mol, where the co-crystallized pose RMSD in PDB `4ER3` should also be reported).

#### 3. Replace Over-Representation Analysis with Ranked Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA)
* **WHY:** Running separate hypergeometric enrichment tests on up- and downregulated gene lists from a misspecified contrast produced contradictory results where the exact same pathway ("Proteoglycans in cancer") was reported as both significantly upregulated and downregulated.
* **WHAT:** Perform directional, threshold-free GSEA on the treatment-specific $\log_2$ fold changes.
* **HOW:** Rank all expressed genes by their signed Wald statistic from the `DESeq2` `EPZ004777 vs. DMSO` contrast in OCI-AML3 and OCI-AML2 separately, and run `fgsea` or `clusterProfiler::GSEA` against KEGG and Hallmark gene sets (including known *HOXA*/*MEIS1* target signatures). Report the Normalized Enrichment Score (NES) and Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted $p$-values.

#### 4. Align Biological Claims and Cell Line Descriptions with Experimental Reality
* **WHY:** The manuscript incorrectly describes both OCI-AML2 and OCI-AML3 as *NPM1*-mutant cell lines (Section 1, p. 2) and claims in the Abstract and Conclusions that "EPZ004777 has been identified as a potent modulator of SNX19, TPBG, and ZNF185."
* **WHAT:** Correct the genotypes of the cell lines and reframe all conclusions to reflect secondary transcriptional changes rather than direct protein modulation.
* **HOW:** Explicitly state in the Abstract, Introduction, and Methods that OCI-AML3 carries an *NPM1* mutation (Type A) whereas OCI-AML2 is *NPM1*-wild-type (carrying an *MLL-AF6* translocation), as documented in the original GSE85107 publication. Revise the Abstract and Conclusion to state that *SNX19*, *TPBG*, and *ZNF185* are transcriptionally downregulated downstream of EPZ004777 treatment in AML cells.

* **WHAT & HOW:**

  * **Section 3.6 & Section 4.4 (Lines 278–304, p. 2, Line 27):** Correct the repeated misspelling of the compound "EPZ004777" as "EPZ004447" across Figures 10–13 captions, main text, and Section 4.4.
