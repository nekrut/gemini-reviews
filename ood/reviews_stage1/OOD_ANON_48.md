### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This study proposes an explainable machine learning framework to classify obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD) patients vs. healthy controls (HC) from 19-channel eyes-closed resting-state EEG across two cohorts: Dataset 1 ($35\text{ OCD}, 37\text{ HC}$, $N = 72$, from the public TDBRAIN archive) and Dataset 2 ($21\text{ OCD}, 21\text{ HC}$, $N = 42$, recruited at Xinxiang Medical University). From 30 non-overlapping 2-second EEG epochs (60 s total) per participant, the authors extract eight feature families (coherence, phase-locking value [PLV], power spectral density, Kolmogorov complexity, approximate entropy, fuzzy entropy, sample entropy, and Lempel-Ziv complexity), apply Random Forest Recursive Feature Elimination with Cross-Validation (RFECV) and 50-trial Optuna hyperparameter optimization across six classifiers (RF, KNN, SVM, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost) on Dataset 1, and evaluate the retrained models on Dataset 2 alongside SHAP feature attribution and Wilcoxon rank-sum tests. They report that LightGBM using the top 16 PLV features achieves 86.6% accuracy ($\text{AUC} = 0.809$) on Dataset 1 and 83.3% accuracy ($\text{AUC} = 0.839$) on Dataset 2, highlighting frontal–parietal (`F4-P3`) alpha PLV and frontal/parietal–occipital (`P3-O1`, `F3-O1`) delta PLV as primary OCD biomarkers. Neither analysis code nor Dataset 2 is publicly available.

While testing an EEG classifier on an external cohort (Dataset 2) and comparing multiple feature families are well-motivated goals, a forensic audit of the reported tables, confusion matrices, test statistics, and directional interpretations reveals pervasive mathematical impossibilities and internal contradictions that invalidate the study's core results:
1. **Impossible confusion matrices and performance metrics (Tables 4 and 5):** In Table 4 (Dataset 1, $N = 72$: 35 OCD, 37 HC), the confusion matrices for KNN, RF, and CatBoost sum to $26+10+4+32 = 72$ with row sums of $36$ and $36$ (impossible for 35 OCD and 37 HC), and for SVM, LightGBM, and XGBoost (`[[31, 5], [5, 31]]`), $31/36 = 0.8611$ is reported as Precision and Recall while Accuracy is reported as `0.866`, Sensitivity as `0.856` or `0.857` ($30/35$), and Specificity as `0.875` ($28/32$ or $35/40$—impossible for $N=37$ or $36$). In Table 5 (Dataset 2, $N = 42$: 21 OCD, 21 HC), KNN has a confusion matrix `[[10, 1], [8, 23]]` whose rows sum to **11 and 31** (instead of 21 and 21!), SVM has `[[16, 1], [7, 18]]` (rows summing to **17 and 25**), and XGBoost has `[[15, 2], [7, 18]]` (rows summing to **17 and 25**), with Sensitivity/Specificity numbers that contradict both the true class counts ($21/21$) and the printed confusion matrices (e.g., KNN reports $\text{SEN} = 0.952$ [$20/21$] and $\text{SPE} = 0.776$ while the matrix has $10$ and $23$ correct predictions).
2. **Impossible Wilcoxon $Z$-statistics and $p$-values (Table 6):** For a sample of $N = 72$ subjects ($n_1 = 35, n_2 = 37$), the maximum possible Wilcoxon rank-sum $|Z|$-statistic when the two distributions are 100% non-overlapping is $Z_{\max} = \sqrt{\frac{35 \times 37 \times 73}{12 \times (35\times 37/\dots)}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{3 \times 35 \times 37}{73}} = 7.29$. Yet Table 6 reports $Z = 25.508$ ($p = 0.0001$), $Z = 18.702$ ($p = 0.0004$), and $Z = 13.340$ ($p = 0.0027$)—which are mathematically impossible $Z$-scores (and instead match 1-way ANOVA $F$-statistics or $\chi^2 = Z^2$ statistics where $\sqrt{25.508} = 5.05$ and $\sqrt{13.340} = 3.65$, or else indicate that epoch-level pseudoreplication across $72 \times 30 = 2,160$ segments was used, violating subject-level independence).
3. **Direct contradiction between Table 6, SHAP (Fig. 6), and Discussion on the direction of OCD connectivity changes:** Table 6 (lines 154–158) shows that for the #1 SHAP feature `PLV-alpha(F4-P3)`, OCD is **lower** than HC ($0.41 \pm 0.05$ vs. $0.44 \pm 0.05$), for `PLV-delta(P3-O1)` OCD is slightly **lower** ($0.68$ vs. $0.69$), for `PLV-delta(F3-O1)` OCD is **identical** ($0.70$ vs. $0.70$, $p = 0.783$), and for `PLV-theta(C3-T4)` OCD is **higher** ($0.51$ vs. $0.47$, $p < 0.001$). Yet Section "SHAP analysis results" (line 239) claims the exact opposite for alpha and delta (*"higher values (in red) of PLV-alpha (F4–P3) and PLV-delta (P3–O1) are predominantly associated with positive SHAP values, indicating a stronger likelihood of OCD classification"*), and the Discussion (line 260) contradicts both Table 6 and line 250 by claiming *"increased delta-band synchronization and reduced theta-band and alpha-band synchronization observed in OCD patients"*.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **LightGBM trained on 16 RFECV-selected PLV features achieves 86.6% accuracy on Dataset 1 and 83.3% accuracy on external Dataset 2 (Tables 4 and 5): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  First, on Dataset 1, performing RFECV feature selection and 50-iteration Optuna hyperparameter tuning across the full Dataset 1 before reporting 10-fold CV metrics on Dataset 1 (Section "Machine learning classification", line 110) leaks test-fold information into feature selection and hyperparameter tuning. Second, the confusion matrices and metric columns in Tables 4 and 5 are mathematically inconsistent with the sample sizes and with one another: in Table 4 ($35\text{ OCD}, 37\text{ HC}$), every confusion matrix has 36 cases per row (`[[31,5],[5,31]]` or `[[26,10],[4,32]]`), and `[[31,5],[5,31]]` yields $\text{ACC} = 62/72 = 86.1\%$, not $86.6\%$, while $\text{SEN} = 0.856$ and $\text{SPE} = 0.875$ cannot be obtained from integers with denominator 35, 36, or 37. In Table 5 ($21\text{ OCD}, 21\text{ HC}$), the true class sizes are 21 and 21, yet the confusion matrices for SVM (`[[16,1],[7,18]]`), KNN (`[[10,1],[8,23]]`), and XGBoost (`[[15,2],[7,18]]`) have row totals of $17$ and $25$ (or $11$ and $31$), indicating that predicted labels and true labels were transposed or fabricated/miscalculated. Moreover, in Dataset 1, all 35 OCD patients were pharmacologically medicated while all 37 controls were medication-naïve (Limitations, line 300), confounding the classification target with psychotropic drug effects.

* **Alpha-band PLV (`F4-P3`), delta-band PLV (`P3-O1`, `F3-O1`), and theta-band PLV (`C3-T4`) are key discriminative biomarkers of OCD identified by SHAP and statistical testing (Table 6, Figs. 5–6): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Of the 16 PLV features selected by the model and ranked by SHAP (Table 6), **13 out of 16 do not differ significantly between OCD and HC after FDR correction** (and 10 out of 16 have uncorrected $p > 0.05$). Specifically, the top three SHAP features—`PLV-alpha(F4-P3)` ($P_{\text{fdr}} = 0.0711$), `PLV-delta(P3-O1)` ($0.69 \pm 0.02$ vs. $0.68 \pm 0.02$, $P = 0.164, P_{\text{fdr}} = 0.2518$), and `PLV-delta(F3-O1)` ($0.70 \pm 0.03$ vs. $0.70 \pm 0.02$, $P = 0.783, P_{\text{fdr}} = 0.7829$)—are statistically indistinguishable between groups (`F3-O1` has identical group means of $0.70$ and $Z = 0.077$). Furthermore, the test statistics reported in the column labeled `Z` in Table 6 ($25.508, 18.702, 13.340$) are actually Kruskal–Wallis $\chi^2$ statistics ($\chi^2 = Z^2$, since $1 - \text{cdf}_{\chi^2_1}(5.470) = 0.0193 \approx 0.022$, $1 - \text{cdf}_{\chi^2_1}(1.975) = 0.160 \approx 0.164$, $1 - \text{cdf}_{\chi^2_1}(0.077) = 0.781 \approx 0.783$, $1 - \text{cdf}_{\chi^2_1}(13.340) = 0.00026$) mislabeled as Wilcoxon $Z$-values, with $P$-values and $P_{\text{fdr}}$ values that do not even match standard Benjamini–Hochberg adjustments of the unrounded $p$-values (e.g., for $Z^2 = 13.340$, two-tailed $p = 0.00026$, which for rank 3 out of 16 gives $P_{\text{fdr}} = 0.00026 \times \frac{16}{3} = 0.0014$, not $0.0027$).

* **OCD is characterized by increased delta-band synchronization and reduced theta-band and alpha-band synchronization (Discussion, line 260) / higher `PLV-alpha(F4-P3)` and `PLV-delta(P3-O1)` increase OCD likelihood (SHAP, line 239): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The manuscript contradicts itself across three consecutive sections on the sign of every frequency band:
  1. **Table 6 & Section "Statistical comparison of PLV connectivity" (lines 154–170, 250):** Theta PLV (`C3-T4`, `F8-O2`) is **higher** in OCD ($0.51$ vs. $0.47$, and $0.67$ vs. $0.62$, $p < 0.001$), alpha PLV (`F4-P3`, `F4-PZ`) is **lower** in OCD ($0.41$ vs. $0.44$, and $0.43$ vs. $0.48$), and delta PLV (`P3-O1`, `F3-O1`) is **unchanged or slightly lower** in OCD ($0.68$ vs. $0.69$, and $0.70$ vs. $0.70$).
  2. **Section "SHAP analysis results" (line 239):** Claims that *higher* values of `PLV-alpha(F4-P3)` and `PLV-delta(P3-O1)` yield positive SHAP values (higher probability of OCD), which is the opposite of Table 6 (where OCD has *lower* `PLV-alpha(F4-P3)` and *lower* `PLV-delta(P3-O1)`, meaning either the class labels `0=OCD, 1=HC` were inverted in SHAP or the text description is reversed).
  3. **Discussion (line 260):** Claims *"with increased delta-band synchronization and reduced theta-band and alpha-band synchronization observed in OCD patients"*, directly contradicting Table 6 and line 250/line 269 where theta synchronization is *increased* and delta synchronization is *unchanged*.

* **Zero-lag sensor-level PLV between 19 scalp electrodes reflects true long-range cortical synchronization between frontal, parietal, and occipital lobes: [Fragile].**  
  Phase-locking value (PLV) is highly susceptible to volume conduction and common-average reference leakage in 19-channel scalp EEG, where zero-phase-lag field spread from a single cortical dipole or ocular/muscular source inflates phase locking across distant scalp sensors (`F3-O1`, `F8-O2`, `P3-O1`), especially in the delta (1–4 Hz) band where residual eye-movement/blink potentials dominate.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Audit and Recompute All Confusion Matrices and Classification Metrics in Tables 4 and 5
* **WHY:** In Table 4 ($35\text{ OCD}, 37\text{ HC}$), the confusion matrices have equal row sums of 36 and 36, and the reported Accuracy, Sensitivity, and Specificity do not equal the ratios of the confusion matrix entries. In Table 5 ($21\text{ OCD}, 21\text{ HC}$), the confusion matrices for SVM, KNN, and XGBoost have row totals of $17/25$ and $11/31$ instead of $21/21$, indicating that predicted classes were placed on the rows and true classes on the columns (or epoch-level predictions were mis-aggregated), while the Sensitivity and Specificity columns were computed from a different matrix.
* **WHAT:** Verify that classification is performed strictly at the **subject level** ($N = 72$ in Dataset 1; $N = 42$ in Dataset 2) rather than leaking 30 epochs per subject across train/test splits, orient all confusion matrices consistently as `[[TN, FP], [FN, TP]]` (with row sums equal to the exact number of HC and OCD subjects: 37 and 35 in Table 4; 21 and 21 in Table 5), and recompute ACC, SEN, SPE, Precision, Recall, F1, and AUC directly from those subject-level predictions.
* **HOW:** Nest RFECV and Optuna inside the outer 10-fold subject-wise cross-validation loop on Dataset 1 so that feature selection is never performed on the validation folds, and output the exact subject-level out-of-fold confusion matrices for Table 4 and external test confusion matrices for Table 5.

#### 2. Correct the Statistical Test Column in Table 6 and Reconcile the Inverted SHAP/Discussion Directions
* **WHY:** Table 6 labels Kruskal–Wallis $\chi^2$ (or one-way ANOVA $F$) statistics as Wilcoxon `Z` values ($25.508, 18.702, 13.340$), and the SHAP interpretation (line 239) and Discussion (line 260) reverse the sign of the OCD vs. HC group differences for alpha, delta, and theta PLV.
* **WHAT:** Replace the `Z` column in Table 6 with the true standardized Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon $Z$-statistic (where $|Z| = \sqrt{\chi^2}$, i.e., $5.05, 4.32, 3.65$), recompute exact Benjamini–Hochberg $P_{\text{fdr}}$ values, verify whether `0` or `1` encoded the OCD class in `shap.summary_plot`, and correct lines 239 and 260 so that the direction of delta, theta, and alpha changes is consistent across the entire manuscript.
* **HOW:** Check `model.classes_` in the Python `scikit-learn` / `LightGBM` script to confirm the target label encoding (`0` vs. `1`), regenerate Fig. 6 with `OCD = 1`, and remove the claim that `PLV-delta(F3-O1)` ($p = 0.783$) and `PLV-delta(P3-O1)` ($p = 0.164$) show significant delta-band synchronization increases in OCD.

#### 3. Benchmark Against Volume-Conduction-Robust Connectivity Metrics (wPLI / Imaginary Coherence)
* **WHY:** Standard PLV includes zero-lag phase locking, which in 19-channel common-average-referenced scalp EEG is heavily contaminated by volume conduction and reference spread.
* **WHAT:** Compare PLV classification performance against the weighted Phase Lag Index (wPLI) or Imaginary Part of Coherency (iCoh), which discard zero-phase-lag volume conduction artifacts.
* **HOW:** Compute 19-channel wPLI matrices across delta, theta, alpha, and beta bands on Datasets 1 and 2 using the same 30 segments per subject and run the nested LightGBM pipeline to verify whether the discriminative signal reflects true non-zero-lag phase synchronization or volume-conducted power/reference differences.
