## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study examines the signaling hierarchy between Hedgehog (HH) and TGF-beta pathways during zebrafish larval tail regeneration and investigates downstream transcriptional targets of TGF-beta at 18 hours post excision (hpe). Using HCR in situ hybridization and pharmacological inhibition, the authors show that *tgfb1a* is induced in the extruded notochord bead after tail excision in a cyclopamine-sensitive manner, while SB431542 inhibition of TGF-beta increases notochordal *ihha*/*ihhb* expression and reduces paracrine *tgfb1a* spread into surrounding tissue. Bulk 3' QuantSeq RNA-seq of 4 conditions ($\pm\text{injury} \times \pm\text{SB431542}$) identifies keratin (*krt*) genes as major TGF-beta targets that partition into an injury-upregulated/TGF-beta-dependent set (`krt_up`, represented by *krt97*) and an injury-downregulated/TGF-beta-repressed set (`krt_down`, represented by *krt5*). Mapping *krt97* (finfold epithelium) and *krt5* (trunk/periderm epithelium) across regeneration and embryonic development via HCR in situ, public scRNA-seq (Daniocell), and nuclear tracking (`h2b-dendra` photoconversion and light-sheet time-lapse), the authors propose that TGF-beta promotes collective epithelial cell migration by upregulating `krt_up` and repressing `krt_down`.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision / Mixed & Salvageable (5.7 / 10.0).** The epistasis experiments between Hedgehog and *tgfb1a* in the notochord bead and the spatial mapping of *krt97* versus *krt5* by HCR in situ and scRNA-seq are clean and visually convincing. However, the manuscript suffers from several methodological, statistical, and causal-inference flaws:
1. **Anatomical Tissue Removal Confounding in the Bulk RNA-seq & Unvalidated Causal Link to Cell Migration:** As the authors themselves observe in Figure 5a (Line 65), *krt97* is normally expressed in a distal crescent of the finfold that is **physically amputated** by tail excision, whereas *krt5* is expressed on the anterior trunk stump! In the bulk RNA-seq design (Figure 3a), "unoperated" control pieces are taken from the posterior 200 µm tip of the intact tail (which is enriched for distal finfold *krt97*$^+$ tissue and has less trunk *krt5*$^+$ tissue), whereas "operated" pieces at 18 hpe are taken from a second 200 µm cut of the regenerating stump (which contains a higher proportion of anterior trunk tissue and newly forming wound epithelium). Furthermore, no functional perturbation of *krt97* or *krt5* is performed, nor did the authors even test whether SB431542 treatment blocks finfold epithelial migration in their Dendra photoconversion or light-sheet time-lapse assays (Figure 9 only compares untreated finfold vs. untreated trunk!).
2. **Loose & Biased Synexpression Classification, Uncalibrated Phylogenetics, and Conditional Regression Plots:** Keratin genes were assigned to `krt_up` or `krt_down` if they achieved $P < 0.05$ in *at least one* of four pairwise comparisons (Line 53), and even non-significant genes ($P > 0.05$, miswritten as $P > 0.5$ in Line 58) were manually grouped into the synexpression branches on a "one-click" `phylogeny.fr` tree lacking bootstrap support to claim a "single evolutionary event." Moreover, in Figure 3c–e, linear regression slopes ($0.62$ and $0.90$) are fitted *after* conditioning/filtering on genes with $P_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ on the X-axis variable, which mathematically induces regression dilution (winner's curse / attenuation bias toward $< 1.0$).
3. **Internal Gene Nomenclature Contradictions (`ihha` vs. `ihhb`) and Statistical Test Contradictions:** The text repeatedly contradicts itself on whether SB431542 regulates `ihha` (Lines 23, 31) or `ihhb` (Fig. 2 legend Lines 31, 34, and Discussion Line 95), and whether Figure 7b was analyzed by an unpaired $t$-test (Fig. 7 legend, Lines 77 & 80) or a 2-way ANOVA with Sidak's test (Methods, ContentOffset 46080 Line 14).

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Hedgehog signaling acts upstream of *tgfb1a* induction in the notochord bead after tail excision, while TGF-beta signaling promotes paracrine *tgfb1a* expression and negatively feeds back on notochordal *ihh* expression: [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by HCR in situ showing injury-specific activation of *tgfb1a* in the notochord bead ($13/13$ vs. $0/10$ uninjured, Fig. 2e) and pharmacological epistasis with cyclopamine and SB431542 (Fig. 2b–d), though the authors must resolve whether the probe in Fig. 2c targeted *ihha* or *ihhb*.
* **Keratin genes partition into two TGF-beta-regulated synexpression groups (`krt_up` and `krt_down`) that separated in a single evolutionary event within Type I keratins: [Overstated].**  
  While the inverse regulation of core members like *krt97* and *krt5* by injury and SB431542 is confirmed by HCR in situ (Fig. 5), the broader classification of all zebrafish *krt* genes relies on passing $P < 0.05$ in at least one of 4 comparisons, and the evolutionary claim rests on an unbootstrapped "one-click" `phylogeny.fr` dendrogram where non-significant genes were visually assigned to match the branches (Fig. 4b).
* ***krt97* (`krt_up`) and *krt5* (`krt_down`) occupy distinct spatial and cellular compartments (finfold epithelium vs. trunk/periderm) during both regeneration and embryonic finfold development, and finfold epithelial cells migrate faster than trunk epithelial cells: [Well Supported].**  
  Demonstrated by HCR in situ across regeneration (24–48 hpe, Fig. 5) and development (20–48 hpf, Fig. 6–7), scRNA-seq co-expression analysis in Daniocell (Fig. 6b, Fig. 8), and live tracking via `h2b-dendra` photoconversion and light-sheet microscopy (Fig. 9).
* **TGF-beta initiates collective epithelial migration by upregulating `krt_up` (*krt97*) and downregulating `krt_down` (*krt5*): [Fragile].**  
  No functional genetic perturbation of *krt97* or *krt5* is performed, and the cell-migration tracking experiments (Fig. 9) did not even include an SB431542-treated group to show that TGF-beta inhibition slows finfold collective migration at the tracked stages. Comparing migration rates of two anatomically distinct tissues (finfold vs. trunk) only establishes a spatial correlation with their marker keratins.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Regression Dilution Bias in Filtered DEG Scatterplots & Missing Interaction Term in $2 \times 2$ Bulk RNA-seq (Pillars 3 & 4 — Major)
* **Location:** Results ("Transcriptional analysis of TGF-beta signalling during regeneration", Lines 39–48, Fig. 3b–e).
* **Statistical Flaw:** The bulk RNA-seq experiment is a classic $2 \times 2$ factorial design ($\text{Injury} \in \{\text{Unoperated}, \text{Operated}\} \times \text{Drug} \in \{\text{DMSO}, \text{SB431542}\}$, $n=3$ per cell). Instead of fitting the proper generalized linear model in `DESeq2` with an interaction term (`~ Injury + Drug + Injury:Drug`) to directly test which genes have an injury-specific response to SB431542, the authors compute four separate pairwise contrasts and plot $\log_2\text{FC}$ regressions in Figure 3c–e *conditioned on genes having $P_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ in one of the two pairwise contrasts*. In Figure 3e, filtering for genes that are significant in one contrast and then regressing $Y$ on $X$ suffers from classic **regression dilution (attenuation bias)** due to estimation error in the predictor $X$ and selection bias (winner's curse), which mathematically forces the ordinary least squares slope below $1.0$ (reported as $0.9$) even under the null hypothesis that the true effects are identical!
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Fit the full $2 \times 2$ factorial interaction model (`design = ~ Injury + Drug + Injury:Drug`) in `DESeq2` on `GSE296469` and report the genes with a statistically significant interaction term ($P_{\text{adj}}(\text{Injury:Drug}) < 0.05$) as the rigorous set of regeneration-specific TGF-beta targets, replacing the ad-hoc slope comparisons in Figure 3c–e.

### Defect 2: Absence of SB431542 Perturbation in Migration Assays & Anatomical Confounding Between Finfold and Trunk (Pillars 1 & 5 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 65, 87–90, Fig. 9) and Discussion (Line 95).
* **Methodological Flaw:** The paper's central mechanistic model (Fig. 1i, Abstract, Discussion) is that TGF-beta drives collective epithelial migration by switching cells from `krt_down` (*krt5*) to `krt_up` (*krt97*). However, the migration experiments in Figure 9 (`ubi:h2b-dendra` photoconversion during regeneration, $n=6$ operated vs. $n=3$ unoperated; `actb2:h2b-mCherry` light-sheet time-lapse during development, $n=3$ embryos) only compare the migration speed of **finfold epithelium vs. trunk epithelium** in untreated/control animals. Showing that finfold cells move faster than trunk cells does not demonstrate that *krt97* causes movement or that *krt5* inhibits it—finfold and trunk epithelia differ in hundreds of genes, basement membrane composition, and mechanical geometry. Crucially, the authors never tested whether SB431542 treatment (which reduces *krt97* and increases *krt5* in Fig. 5 and Fig. 7) actually reduces epithelial migration velocity in the Dendra or time-lapse assays!
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Either perform the `ubi:h2b-dendra` photoconversion migration assay in DMSO vs. SB431542-treated operated larvae to directly test whether TGF-beta inhibition blocks collective finfold epithelial migration, or temper the Abstract and Discussion to frame the role of `krt_up`/`krt_down` in epithelial mobility as a correlative hypothesis rather than a demonstrated mechanism.

### Defect 3: Uncalibrated "One-Click" Phylogeny & Uncorrected Multi-Group Statistics (Pillars 4 & 5 — Moderate)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 53, 58, Fig. 4b, Fig. 5b, Fig. 7b) and Methods (ContentOffset 46080, Lines 8–14).
* **Statistical & Phylogenetic Flaws:**
  1. **Unbootstrapped "one-click" tree (Fig. 4b, Line 53 & 58):** The keratin phylogeny was generated using "one-click" default settings on `www.phylogeny.fr` without reporting alignment trimming of the hypervariable head/tail domains (vs. conserved $\alpha$-helical rod domain), substitution model selection, or branch support values (bootstraps/aLRT), yet is used to claim that `krt_up` and `krt_down` Type I keratins diverged at a single evolutionary branch point.
  2. **Uncorrected Fisher's LSD test in Fig. 5b (ContentOffset 46080, Line 12):** Uses a 2-way ANOVA followed by *"Fisher's multiple comparison"* (Fisher's Least Significant Difference), which provides **no family-wise error rate control** across multiple comparisons.
  3. **Direct contradiction on the statistical test used for Fig. 7b:** The Figure 7 legend (Lines 77 & 80) states that significance asterisks result from an **"unpaired t test"**, whereas the Methods section (ContentOffset 46080, Line 14) states that Figure 7b was analyzed using **"2 way ANOVA with Sidak's multiple comparison test"**.
  4. **Naive cell-level Pearson correlation in scRNA-seq (Fig. 8, Line 82):** Computing parametric Pearson correlation across thousands of sparse droplet scRNA-seq cells pooled across all developmental stages (3–120 hpf) is heavily confounded by zero-inflation (dropout) and temporal stage composition.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Reconcile the unpaired $t$-test vs. 2-way ANOVA + Sidak contradiction between the Fig. 7 legend and Methods, replace Fisher's LSD in Fig. 5b with Sidak's or Tukey's multiple-comparison test, and add bootstrap support values (restricting alignment to the conserved intermediate filament rod domain) to the keratin phylogeny in Fig. 4b.

### Defect 4: Inconsistent Gene Nomenclature (`ihha` vs. `ihhb`) and Unit/P-Value Typos (Pillar 7 — Minor)
* **Location:** Introduction (Line 23), Results (Lines 31, 34, 53, 56, 58, 65, 68), and Discussion (Line 95).
* **Technical & Editorial Typos:**
  1. **`ihha` vs. `ihhb` contradiction:** Introduction Line 23 and Results Line 31 state that SB431542 regulates **`ihha`**, whereas the Figure 2c legend (Lines 31 & 34) and Discussion Line 95 state that Figure 2c measures **`ihhb`**.
  2. **Unit typo in Fig. 5a legend (Lines 65 & 68):** Written as `50 M SB431542` (50 Molar!) instead of `50 µM SB431542`.
  3. **P-value threshold typos (Lines 53, 56, 58):** Fig. 4b legend (Lines 53 & 56) states *"grey arrows indicate a trend that was not significant (P < 0.05)"* (should be $P > 0.05$ or $P \ge 0.05$), and Line 58 states *"have a P value that is greater than 0.5"* (should be $0.05$).
  4. **Missing chemical character in Fig. 7 legend (Lines 77 & 80):** Written as `SB43154` instead of `SB431542`.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Clarify whether *ihha* or *ihhb* was probed in Figure 2c and standardize throughout the text; fix `50 M` to `50 µM`, `P < 0.05` (non-significant) to `P > 0.05`, `0.5` to `0.05`, and `SB43154` to `SB431542`.
