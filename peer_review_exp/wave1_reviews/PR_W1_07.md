## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This comparative immunogenomics study investigates the conservation, inducibility, and lineage-specific evolution of innate immune genes across three non-model drosophilid species diverged >45 Mya from *Drosophila melanogaster*: *Hirtodrosophila cameraria*, *Hirtodrosophila confusa*, and *Scaptodrosophila deflexa*. Using first-generation ($F_1$) laboratory-reared offspring of wild-caught females, the authors compare total rRNA-depleted RNA-seq transcriptomes of unchallenged control flies ($n=5$ per species) and flies septic-wounded 16 h earlier with the Gram-negative pathogen *Providencia rettgeri* ($n=5$ per species). They evaluate three questions: (1) whether pathogen-challenged RNA-seq improves `BRAKER3` annotation of 638 curated *D. melanogaster* immune orthologs (568 Hierarchical Orthologous Groups [HOGs]); (2) how transcriptional responses (`DESeq2`) and metatranscriptomic microbiome profiles (`rnaSPAdes` + `DIAMOND blastp` on unmapped reads) vary across species; and (3) whether strongly upregulated orphan short secreted peptides ($\le 200\text{ aa}$) represent novel antimicrobial peptides (AMPs), screened via `SignalP 6.0`, `AMP Scanner v2`, `amPEPpy`, and `AlphaFold 3`. They find that pathogen challenge does not meaningfully improve `BRAKER3` immune gene recovery, that *H. cameraria* and *H. confusa* mount strong canonical Imd/Toll transcriptional responses (363 and 149 upregulated genes) whereas *S. deflexa* mounts almost none (33 upregulated genes, lacking *DptA*, *DptC*, *AttC*, and *AttD* orthologs while harboring constitutive *Spiroplasma ixodetis*-group and *Wolbachia* endosymbionts), and they identify 20 secreted AMP-like candidates across the three species. Raw RNA-seq reads are publicly available under NCBI BioProject `PRJNA1270041`.

The manuscript is thoughtfully framed and remarkably candid about its experimental constraints: in the Discussion (Second part, line 7), the authors explicitly acknowledge the absence of sterile-pin wounding (sham) controls, the sibling/single-brood structure of the $F_1$ flies (all 10 *S. deflexa* individuals came from a single wild-caught female, explaining 100% *Spiroplasma* carriage), and the sex mismatch in *S. deflexa* (where 1 male and 4 females were used instead of 5 females). However, several technical and statistical issues require remediation: (1) comparing annotations across species with unequal extrinsic protein hint distances (`BRAKER3` was fed *only* *D. melanogaster* RefSeq proteins, which is >50 Myr diverged from *S. deflexa*); (2) extreme random-effect variance inflation in the `MCMCglmm` binomial mixed model where group-level posterior means are pushed to $99.58\%\text{–}99.97\%$ despite empirical HOG recovery rates of $91.9\%\text{–}94.9\%$ ($522/568$ to $539/568$); and (3) filtering de novo metatranscriptomic ORFs at $\ge 200$ amino acids (`getorf`, Second part, line 42), which systematically discards short bacterial/viral transcripts and ribosomal RNAs.

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1: Pathogen-challenged RNA-seq does not substantially improve `BRAKER3` genome annotation of immune-related genes compared to unchallenged RNA-seq.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Across all three species, `BRAKER3` annotations trained on unchallenged vs. challenged RNA-seq recover nearly identical numbers of total genes and immune HOGs (e.g., 515 vs. 520 in *H. cameraria*; 490 vs. 500 in *H. confusa*; 490 vs. 485 in *S. deflexa*, Table 1), with combined reads recovering 522–539 of 568 HOGs.
* **Claim 2: Core immune signaling genes are conserved across all three lineages, whereas AMP effector families (*Diptericin* and *Attacin*) undergo rapid lineage-specific expansions in *Hirtodrosophila* and losses (*DptA*, *DptC*, *AttC*, *AttD*) in *S. deflexa*.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Synteny and `IQ-TREE2` phylogenies across 51 drosophilids (Fig. 3A–C) confirm tandem *DptC* and *AttC* duplications in *H. cameraria* and *H. confusa* and the absence of *DptA/C* and *AttC/D* in *S. deflexa* (supported by comparison to *S. lebanonensis*).
* **Claim 3: *H. cameraria* and *H. confusa* mount a robust canonical Imd/Toll transcriptional response to *P. rettgeri* challenge, whereas *S. deflexa* shows a muted response associated with low *Providencia* load and constitutive *Spiroplasma* infection.** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  The transcriptional contrast between *Hirtodrosophila* (strong induction of *Dpt*, *Att*, *Cec*, *Bom*, *PGRPs*, *Relish*, *pirk*) and *S. deflexa* (only 3 *PGRPs* induced, Fig. 4) and the assembly of a *Spiroplasma ixodetis*-group symbiont in *S. deflexa* (Figs. 5–6) are clear. However, because all 10 *S. deflexa* flies were full/half-siblings from $n=1$ wild female ($N=1$ biological family/isofemale line) and lacked a sterile-wounding control, species-level vs. maternal-brood/symbiont effects are completely confounded.
* **Claim 4: Combined expression, physicochemical, and `AlphaFold 3` structural screening identifies 20 novel secreted AMP-like candidates (including apidaecin-like proline-rich and holotricin-like glycine-rich peptides).** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Combining differential expression ($\log_2\text{FC} \ge 1$), absence of *D. melanogaster* orthology, length $\le 200\text{ aa}$, `SignalP 6.0` cleavage sites, cationic charge, furin cleavage motifs (`RXRR` in `Hconf/g6966`), and `AlphaFold 3` structure predictions provides a well-triangulated computational prioritization, though antimicrobial activity versus wounding/stress functions remains unvalidated experimentally.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

1. **Severe Logit-Scale Random-Effect Distortion in the `MCMCglmm` Binomial Mixed Model:**  
   In the Results (First part, line 107), the authors fit a Bayesian binomial GLMM (`MCMCglmm`) to test whether species and immune functional categories differ in the probability of HOG recovery. Looking at Table 1 (lines 84–93), the actual empirical proportion of the 568 *D. melanogaster* immune HOGs recovered in the combined annotations is **$539/568 = 94.89\%$** for *H. cameraria*, **$534/568 = 94.01\%$** for *H. confusa*, and **$522/568 = 91.90\%$** for *S. deflexa*. Yet line 107 reports `MCMCglmm` posterior means of **$99.88\%$** (95% CI: 99.61–99.98) for *H. cameraria*, **$99.79\%$** (CI: 99.35–99.97) for *H. confusa*, and **$99.58\%$** (CI: 98.80–99.93) for *S. deflexa*, and **$99.97\%$** for signaling vs. **$99.46\%$** for effectors! Why are the model-estimated probabilities $>99.5\%$ when the true empirical rates are $91.9\%\text{–}94.9\%$? In `MCMCglmm`, when a binary 0/1 outcome (with many HOGs present across all 3 species, i.e., $497/568$ invariant 1s) is fitted with an uninformative or poorly constrained HOG-level random intercept without marginalizing over the random-effect variance $\sigma^2_{\text{HOG}} + \sigma^2_{\text{units}}$, the conditional logit intercept explodes ($\beta_0 \approx +6\text{ to }+8$), pushing back-transformed conditional probabilities $1/(1 + e^{-\beta})$ artificially close to $100\%$. Furthermore, the model specifications (priors, random effects structure, burn-in, thinning, effective sample size) for `MCMCglmm` are completely omitted from the Methods section!
2. **Pseudoreplication in *S. deflexa* ($N=1$ Maternal Family) and Unmodeled Sex Covariate in `DESeq2`:**  
   In the Methods (Second part, line 20), the authors state that all *S. deflexa* flies came from a single wild-caught female ($n=1$), and that *"Five females per species (four females and one male for S. deflexa) were challenged in this way."* Yet in the `DESeq2` analysis (Second part, line 35), the design formula was specified simply as `~ treatment`, without controlling for `sex` in *S. deflexa*. Including a single male in one treatment arm of a 5-vs-5 comparison introduces strong sex-biased gene expression variance (testis/accessory gland transcripts vs. ovarian transcripts) into the residual dispersion estimate of *S. deflexa*, reducing statistical power to detect differentially expressed genes in that species.
3. **Biased Extrinsic Protein Hints (`D. melanogaster` Only) in `BRAKER3` and Missing `tblastn` Validation of Putative Gene Losses:**  
   In the Methods (Second part, line 30), `BRAKER3` was run in ETP mode *"with extrinsic protein hints provided from D. melanogaster RefSeq proteins."* Providing extrinsic protein hints from a single species (*D. melanogaster*, subgenus *Sophophora*) rather than a multi-species Diptera/OrthoDB protein database creates an alignment bias against rapidly evolving short peptides in deep outgroups like *S. deflexa* (>50 Mya divergence). Moreover, before claiming that *DptA*, *DptC*, *AttC*, and *AttD* are genuinely lost from the *S. deflexa* genome (rather than missed by `BRAKER3` or `OrthoFinder`), the authors do not report running direct six-frame `tblastn` or `miniprot` searches of the raw *S. deflexa* genome assembly across the syntenic regions shown in Fig. 3B–C.
4. **Length Truncation Bias ($\ge 200\text{ aa}$) in Metatranscriptomic Profiling:**  
   In the Metagenomic Methods (Second part, line 42), unmapped RNA-seq reads were assembled with `rnaSPAdes` and ORFs were predicted with `EMBOSS getorf`, **"retaining protein length $\ge 200$ amino acids"** ($\ge 600\text{ bp}$) before `DIAMOND blastp` taxonomic assignment. Retaining only ORFs $\ge 200$ amino acids discards all bacterial small proteins, fragmented/low-coverage microbial transcripts (such as low-titer *Providencia* transcripts in *H. confusa* and *S. deflexa* that assemble into contigs <600 bp), and all non-coding microbial RNAs. Consequently, relative microbial read counts depend heavily on whether a taxon's transcripts assembled into contigs encoding $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ ORFs in that specific sample.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

* **Absence of Wounding (Mock Injection) Control:** As noted by the authors (Second part, line 7 and line 20), control flies were CO$_2$-anesthetized but unpricked, whereas challenged flies received a thoracic pin prick dipped in *P. rettgeri*. Because thoracic cuticle wounding alone activates Toll/melanization/stress pathways in *Drosophila*, the 33 upregulated genes in *S. deflexa* and several novel peptides in *Hirtodrosophila* cannot be definitively separated between septic infection vs. sterile wound healing.
* **Missing Code and Annotation Repository:** While raw RNA-seq reads are in SRA (`PRJNA1270041`), the `BRAKER3` GFF3/protein annotations for the three species, the *S. deflexa* genome assembly (cited as personal communication from Kim & Petrov, Second part, line 25), and the analysis scripts are not linked in a public repository (e.g., Zenodo/GitHub).

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Document the `MCMCglmm` Model in Methods and Report Marginalized Empirical Probabilities:**
   * **WHY:** The `MCMCglmm` model is missing from the Methods section, and reporting conditional logit back-transformed probabilities of $99.58\%\text{–}99.88\%$ when empirical recovery is $91.9\%\text{–}94.9\%$ is a random-effect scaling artifact.
   * **WHAT:** Add the `MCMCglmm` model specification to the Methods and marginalize over the random-effect variance when back-transforming logit posteriors to the probability scale.
   * **HOW:** Use the standard marginalization formula $p_{\text{marginal}} \approx \text{logit}^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\hat{\beta}}{\sqrt{1 + c^2 \sigma^2_{\text{random}}}}\right)$ where $c = \frac{16\sqrt{3}}{15\pi}$ (or report simple binomial/Fisher's exact/logistic GEE proportions alongside the empirical percentages from Table 1).
2. **Re-run *S. deflexa* `DESeq2` Excluding the Single Male Sample (or Including `~ sex + treatment`) and Verify Gene Losses by Syntenic `tblastn`:**
   * **WHY:** Including 1 male with 4 females in the challenged *S. deflexa* group without a `sex` covariate inflates within-group dispersion across thousands of sex-biased genes.
   * **WHAT:** Re-run `DESeq2` on the female-only *S. deflexa* samples ($4\text{ challenged vs. }5\text{ unchallenged}$) or with `~ sex + treatment`, and confirm the absence of *DptA/C* and *AttC/D* via direct nucleotide-level synteny search.
   * **HOW:** Check whether removing the male sample increases the number of detected DEGs in *S. deflexa*, and run `tblastn -evalue 1e-3` and `miniprot` using all drosophilid Diptericin and Attacin proteins against the intergenic regions flanking `S. deflexa DptB` and `AttA/B` (Fig. 3B–C) to rule out unannotated pseudogenes or short ORFs missed by `BRAKER3`.
3. **Validate Unmapped Read Microbiome Profiles Using Direct Read Classification (`Kraken2` / `Sylph` / `MetaPhlAn`) Without the $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ Assembly Filter:**
   * **WHY:** Requiring de novo `rnaSPAdes` contigs to encode ORFs $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ penalizes lower-coverage *Providencia* transcripts that fail to assemble into >600 bp contigs in *H. confusa* and *S. deflexa*.
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Map all unmapped reads directly against the *Providencia rettgeri* Dmel reference genome and the assembled *Spiroplasma* contigs (or classify unmapped reads directly with `Kraken2`), and verify that the inter-species differences in *Providencia* load and alpha diversity (Fig. 5) hold without the $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ `getorf` threshold.
4. **Deposit the `BRAKER3` Annotations and Candidate AMP Sequences in Zenodo/Figshare:**
   * **WHY:** The `BRAKER3` GFF3/FASTA files and the 41 novel AMP candidate sequences are critical outputs for the comparative immunology community.
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Upload the combined `BRAKER3` GFF3 and protein FASTA files for *H. cameraria*, *H. confusa*, and *S. deflexa*, along with the IQ-TREE alignments/treefiles and R scripts, to Zenodo and cite the DOI in Data Availability.
