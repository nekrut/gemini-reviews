## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This concise structural and synthetic plant biology manuscript investigates how the integrated Heavy Metal-Associated (HMA) domains of two cereal Tandem Kinase Proteins (TKPs)—barley Rmo2 and wheat Rwt7—recognize their cognate *Magnaporthe oryzae* (blast fungus) effectors, PBY2 and PWT7. Using isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC), analytical size-exclusion chromatography (aSEC), high-resolution X-ray crystallography (1.6–2.2 Å), *Nicotiana benthamiana* split-GAL4 RUBY protein-protein interaction assays, cereal spray infections, and wheat protoplast luciferase cell-death assays, the authors map two spatially distinct binding interfaces and engineer dual-specificity HMA domains (`Rmo2_HMA+` and `Rwt7_HMA+`), demonstrating that full-length `Rmo2+` gains functional immune recognition of PWT7 in cereal protoplasts.

### Primary Claims Audit
1. **Claim 1:** The isolated N-terminal integrated HMA domains of Rmo2 and Rwt7 directly and specifically bind their cognate *M. oryzae* effectors (PBY2 and PWT7, respectively) in vitro with nanomolar affinity ($K_D = 53.5\text{ nM}$ for $\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}}/\text{PBY2}$; $K_D = 2.92\text{ nM}$ for $\text{Rwt7}_{\text{HMA}}/\text{PWT7}$), with no detectable binding to non-cognate effectors. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Orthogonal in vitro validation via aSEC peak shifts and quantitative ITC isotherms.
2. **Claim 2:** $\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}}/\text{PBY2}$ (2.2 Å) and $\text{Rwt7}_{\text{HMA}}/\text{PWT7}$ (1.6 Å) bind via spatially non-overlapping interfaces on the HMA scaffold, and single structure-guided charge-reversal/steric substitutions ($\text{PBY2}^{\text{E42R}}$ and $\text{PWT7}^{\text{N39R}}$) completely abolish binding in vitro, in planta (split-GAL4 RUBY), and during live *M. oryzae* infection of barley and wheat cultivars. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* High-resolution X-ray crystal structures corroborated by aSEC, ITC, split-GAL4 RUBY betalain assays, and spray inoculation of isogenic *M. oryzae* `Br48 ΔPBY2` transformants on susceptible/resistant barley (Nigrate/Russia 81) and wheat (Transfed/Chinese Spring) cultivars.
3. **Claim 3:** Structure-informed resurfacing of $\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}}$ along the PWT7 interface ($\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}+}$) and of $\text{Rwt7}_{\text{HMA}}$ along the PBY2 interface ($\text{Rwt7}_{\text{HMA}+}$) confers nanomolar dual-effector binding in vitro, permits simultaneous tripartite complex formation ($\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}+}/\text{PBY2}/\text{PWT7}$ crystal structure), and enables full-length `Rmo2+` to trigger immune cell death in response to both PBY2 and PWT7 in wheat protoplasts. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* ITC, co-crystal structures of resurfaced complexes and the tripartite assembly, split-GAL4 RUBY assays, and Kronos wheat protoplast luciferase cell-death assays. Importantly, the authors transparently report that while `Rwt7_HMA+` binds PBY2 in vitro and in the chimeric `Rmo2^(Rwt7-HMA+)` RUBY assay, full-length `Rwt7+` fails to trigger cell death in response to PBY2 in protoplasts.
4. **Claim 4:** Integrated HMA domains in TKPs are programmable modules for designing new disease-resistance specificities against blast fungus in cereal crops. `[Moderately Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* While dual recognition by `Rmo2+` is convincingly shown in transient wheat protoplast cell-death assays, the engineered `Rmo2+` receptor was **not** tested in stable transgenic barley or wheat plants challenged with *M. oryzae* spores, and exhibits lower baseline luciferase activity (autoactivity/fitness cost) in protoplasts (line 53, fig. S11).

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Unquantified Autoactivity / Baseline Cell Death of Engineered `Rmo2+` and `Rwt7+` in Cereal Protoplasts
In line 53, the authors note briefly: *"The engineered Rmo2+ and Rwt7+ receptors displayed generally lower luciferase activity compared to wild-type (fig. S11)."* In protoplast luciferase viability assays, reduced relative luciferase activity in the presence of non-interacting mutant effectors (`PBY2^E42R` or `PWT7^N39R`) or empty vector directly indicates **constitutive receptor autoactivity (ligand-independent cell death)**. In NLR and TKP engineering, resurfacing integrated decoy domains frequently destabilizes intramolecular autoinhibitory interactions between the integrated domain and the kinase/pseudokinase core, causing constitutive immune signaling and necrosis in planta. Because Figure 4E normalizes or compares `Rmo2+` with wild-type effectors against `Rmo2+` with mutant effectors on an already depressed baseline, the dynamic range (fold-change between uninduced and effector-induced states) is substantially compressed compared to wild-type `Rmo2`. Without a vector-only (no effector) control in Figure 4E and immunoblot verification of full-length `Rmo2+` and `Rwt7+` protein accumulation in cereal protoplasts, it is difficult to separate genuine ligand-triggered activation from heightened basal autoactivity.

### 2.2 Omission of Materials & Methods, Statistical Test Specifications, and PDB Accession Codes in the Main Text
Because the manuscript is formatted as a short-format report, the main text completely omits the Materials & Methods section, statistical test specifications (e.g., ANOVA/post-hoc test type, exact $n$ biological vs. technical replicates, error bar definitions in Figures 2A–B and 4A–E), exact amino acid sequences/substitutions comprising the resurfaced `Rmo2_HMA+` and `Rwt7_HMA+` constructs, ITC stoichiometry ($N$) and thermodynamic parameters ($\Delta H$, $T\Delta S$, confidence intervals on $K_D$), and Protein Data Bank (PDB) accession codes for the five reported X-ray crystal structures (`Rmo2_HMA/PBY2`, `Rwt7_HMA/PWT7`, `Rwt7_HMA+/PBY2`, `Rwt7_HMA+/PWT7`, and `Rmo2_HMA+/PBY2/PWT7`). Furthermore, in line 25, the authors note that for `Rwt7_HMA`, *"the first residue of Rwt7_HMA (M1) formed extensive interactions with the effector"*; if `M1` is an artificial N-terminal methionine cloning scar or expression tag remnant on the excised domain construct, its contribution to the 2.92 nM in vitro affinity should be explicitly evaluated against the native N-terminal context.

### 2.3 Failure of Full-Length `Rwt7` in Split-GAL4 RUBY Assay Requires Chimeric Workaround
Full-length `Rwt7-GAL4` failed to produce betalain in the *N. benthamiana* split-GAL4 RUBY assay (line 32, fig. S6), forcing the authors to test `Rwt7_HMA` and `Rwt7_HMA+` inside a chimeric `Rmo2^(Rwt7-HMA)` backbone (Figures 2B and 4C–D). Why did `Rwt7-GAL4` fail in *N. benthamiana*—was it due to protein instability, nuclear exclusion (since GAL4-VP16 requires nuclear import to drive the RUBY reporter), or rapid HR cell death in *N. benthamiana* preventing betalain synthesis? Clarifying this is essential because full-length `Rwt7+` subsequently failed to respond to PBY2 in wheat protoplasts (Figure 4E), indicating that the `Rmo2` kinase scaffold and `Rwt7` kinase scaffold have non-equivalent activation or conformational coupling requirements.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Biophysical Confirmation of Effector Mutant Folding:**
   - When single point mutations ($\text{PBY2}^{\text{E42R}}$ and $\text{PWT7}^{\text{N39R}}$) abolish binding in ITC and aSEC (Figure 1E–F), standard biophysical rigor requires showing that the mutant proteins remain properly folded (e.g., identical monomeric elution volume and sharp peak profile on aSEC, or CD/DSF melting temperature) rather than aggregating or unfolding.
2. **Tripartite Complex Competition vs. Cooperativity:**
   - The 3D crystal structure of the tripartite complex ($\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}+}/\text{PBY2}/\text{PWT7}$, Figure 3G) is a remarkable result. However, no competitive ITC experiment (titrating PWT7 into pre-formed $\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}+}/\text{PBY2}$ complex, and vice versa) was performed to quantify whether binding of one effector allosterically enhances or diminishes the $K_D$ for the second effector in solution.
3. **Typographical Inconsistencies:**
   - Line 48: `Rmo HMA` (missing `2`) and `Rmo2 Rwt7-HM +` (missing `A`).

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Quantify Basal Autoactivity and Protein Expression in Cereal Protoplasts:**
   - Include an empty-vector (no effector) control alongside `PBY2`, `PWT7`, `PBY2^E42R`, and `PWT7^N39R` in Figure 4E, report the exact fold-reduction in luciferase activity relative to empty vector for WT vs. `Rmo2+`/`Rwt7+`, and provide immunoblots confirming equal expression of WT and engineered full-length TKP constructs in wheat protoplasts.
2. **Provide Complete Biophysical, Crystallographic, and Statistical Reporting:**
   - State the exact residue substitutions introduced in `Rmo2_HMA+` and `Rwt7_HMA+` in the main text.
   - Report mean $\pm$ SD from $n \ge 3$ independent ITC titrations (including $K_D$, $\Delta H$, $-T\Delta S$, and stoichiometry $N$) and test cooperative/allosteric binding in the tripartite $\text{Rmo2}_{\text{HMA}+}/\text{PBY2}/\text{PWT7}$ complex by sequential ITC titration.
   - Include PDB accession codes for all five crystal structures and specify the exact statistical tests, replicate numbers ($n$), and multiple-comparison corrections used in Figures 2 and 4.
3. **Temper Agricultural Deployment Claims or Validate in Transgenic Cereals:**
   - Unless stable transgenic barley or wheat lines expressing `Rmo2+` are generated and shown to resist both `PBY2`- and `PWT7`-expressing *M. oryzae* pathotypes without yield/lesion-mimic autoactivity penalties, temper the Abstract and Discussion to state that `Rmo2+` expands effector recognition *in cereal protoplasts*, while noting that overcoming basal autoactivity and scaffold-specific signaling constraints (as seen in `Rwt7+`) remains necessary for crop deployment.
