## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study investigates the evolutionary mechanisms underlying master sex-determining (MSD) gene emergence and rapid sex chromosome turnover across 53 anuran species (>200 Mya divergence). Examining four "usual suspect" sex-determining genes (*Dmrt1*, *Foxl2*, *Sox3*, and *Bod1l*), the authors report that chromosome-level macro-synteny and local micro-synteny are highly conserved without translocations of these four genes, that *Dmrt1* rarely duplicates (3 near-complete and 5 partial exon duplications among 50 diploid species) while *Foxl2*, *Sox3*, and *Bod1l* show no duplications, and that intact *Dmrt1*, *Foxl2*, and *Sox3* evolve under strong purifying selection ($\omega \ll 1$) regardless of whether *Dmrt1* is autosomal or sex-linked, whereas duplicated copies (*DmW* in *Xenopus laevis* and a *Dmrt1* duplicate in *Pelodytes ibericus*) show elevated $\omega$. From these findings, the authors conclude that in anurans, MSD genes rarely evolve via gene duplication and instead likely evolve via allelic diversification, and that sex chromosome turnover is not driven by gene translocation.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision / Mixed & Salvageable (5.6 / 10.0).** While the comparative synteny and *Dmrt1* exon-duplication curation across 53 amphibian genomes is a useful resource with open GitHub scripts, the study's primary evolutionary conclusions suffer from a **foundational ascertainment flaw** and **misinterpretation of molecular evolution statistics ($d_N/d_S$)**:
1. **Heterogametic (Y/W) Ascertainment Blindness Invalidates the Exclusion of Duplication and Translocation:** Whenever a master sex-determining gene arises via gene duplication or inter-chromosomal translocation (such as *DmW* on the W chromosome of *X. laevis*, *dmrt1bY*/*DmY* on the Y chromosome of medaka, or *sdY* in salmonids), the duplicated/translocated MSD locus resides **exclusively on the Y or W chromosome**. However, as the authors briefly acknowledge in a single sentence late in the Discussion (Line 33), most published chromosome-level anuran reference assemblies were sequenced from the **homogametic sex (XX females or ZZ males)** or collapsed into a single haploid primary assembly that drops hemizygous Y/W-specific insertions! Inspecting an XX or ZZ genome—which physically lacks the Y or W chromosome—cannot detect a Y-specific or W-specific gene duplication or translocation. Concluding from the absence of Y/W duplicates in XX/ZZ assemblies that anuran MSD genes evolve by allelic diversification rather than duplication/translocation treats an experimental ascertainment gap as biological proof.
2. **Conflation of Relaxed Purifying Selection / Pseudogenization ($\omega < 1$) with Positive Selection in PAML Analyses:** In Section 3.4, the authors repeatedly interpret branch $\omega = 0.47$ (*DmW*) and $\omega = 0.92$ (*Pelodytes ibericus* duplicate, for which no RNA-seq data exist to even confirm transcription!) as "tendency towards positive selection" or "strong pressure towards positive selection," and mark non-significant branch-site Bayes Empirical Bayes sites (ContentOffset 46080, Line 24 & Figure 6) with red stars as "sites under positive selection." In codon models, $\omega = 0.92$ on an unexpressed gene duplicate is the classic signature of **neutral drift / pseudogenization ($\omega \to 1.0$)** or relaxed purifying selection, not positive Darwinian selection ($\omega > 1$).
3. **Uncorrected Multiple Testing Across 54 Foreground Branches & Non-Phylogenetic GLM:** Running 54 separate PAML branch and branch-site likelihood ratio tests without multiple-testing correction, and fitting a standard non-phylogenetic Gamma GLM (`ω ~ chromosome + duplication`) on tip branch values with $n=2$ duplicates, violates phylogenetic independence and inflates significance.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Chromosome-level macro-synteny and ancestral micro-synteny of *Dmrt1*, *Foxl2*, *Sox3*, and *Bod1l* are strongly conserved across >200 Mya of anuran evolution: [Well Supported].**  
  Demonstrated across 12 annotated chromosome-level assemblies with `MCScanX` (Figure 1) and 25 chromosome-level assemblies via flanking-gene micro-synteny (Figures 3–4), confirming that the *ancestral* X/Z/autosomal copies of these four genes have remained in their syntenic blocks.
* **Ancestral *Dmrt1* exhibits strongly testis-biased expression and evolves under strong purifying selection ($\omega \ll 1$) across both autosomal and sex-linked lineages: [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by RNA-seq reanalysis across 14 species (8 with gonadal data, Figure 2) and low $d_N/d_S$ ratios across 53 anuran branches (Figure 5).
* **Master sex-determining genes in anurans rarely arise via gene duplication and sex chromosome turnover is not associated with gene translocation: [Overstated].**  
  First, the survey only examined 4 candidate genes (*Dmrt1, Foxl2, Sox3, Bod1l*), so translocations or duplications of any other sex-pathway gene (e.g., *Amh, Amhr2, Gsdf, Sox9, Cyp19a1, Sf1*) were not tested. Second, and more critically, because most assemblies are from the homogametic sex (XX or ZZ) or unphased collapsed assemblies (and 28 fragmented assemblies were excluded from translocation analysis), any Y-linked or W-linked MSD duplication or hemizygous translocation is invisible in the analyzed genomes.
* ***Dmrt1* duplicates (*DmW* and the *Pelodytes ibericus* copy) and specific DM-domain sites evolve under positive selection: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  A branch $\omega = 0.47$ (*DmW*) or $\omega = 0.92$ (*P. ibericus* duplicate) is $< 1.0$ and indicates relaxed purifying selection (or neutral pseudogenization at $\omega \approx 1$), not positive selection. Furthermore, the authors themselves state in ContentOffset 46080 Line 24 that the branch-site tests for the DM-domain sites in *DmW* and *P. ibericus* *"are not statistically significant (Table S12D),"* yet plot them with red stars in Figure 6 and claim in the Abstract and Conclusions that *"duplications are linked to positive selection"* and *"The positively selected sites identified in the duplicated Dmrt1 copy of Pelodytes ibericus point to potential functional innovations."*

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Homogametic Sex (XX/ZZ) Assembly Ascertainment Bias When Testing for Y/W-Specific Duplications and Translocations (Pillars 1, 2 & 5 — Critical)
* **Location:** Abstract (Line 5), Results (Sections 3.2 & 3.3), Discussion (Lines 33, 37), and Conclusions (Line 46).
* **Methodological Flaw:** When a new master sex-determining gene evolves by duplication or translocation onto a proto-Y or proto-W chromosome (the exact mechanism of *DmY* in medaka, *sdY* in salmonids, and *DmW* in *X. laevis*), the ancestral copy remains in its conserved autosomal/X/Z syntenic position, while the duplicated/translocated copy exists **only on the Y or W chromosome**. In an XX female or ZZ male genome assembly, the Y or W chromosome is absent; consequently, `MCScanX` and `BLASTn` will *only* see the stationary ancestral copy and will report 100% conserved synteny and zero duplications/translocations, even if the species actually possesses a Y- or W-specific duplicated/translocated MSD gene! Moreover, in the 8 true frog (*Ranidae*) species where Chr01, Chr02, Chr03, Chr05, or Chr08 is the sex chromosome, finding that the ancestral copies of *Dmrt1, Foxl2, Sox3*, and *Bod1l* did not move out of their ancestral syntenic blocks does not test whether a duplicate of *another* gene translocated *into* those sex chromosomes.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** (1) Explicitly stratify the 53 species in Figure 2 and Table S1 by the **sex and heterogametic status (XY male, ZW female, XX female, ZZ male, or unknown)** of the sequenced individual. (2) Restrict firm negative conclusions about Y/W-linked gene duplications and translocations strictly to those species where the **heterogametic sex (XY or ZW)** was sequenced with long reads or verified via male-vs-female raw genomic read coverage (`Table S2`). (3) Temper the Abstract, Results, and Conclusions to reflect that conserved ancestral synteny rules out *whole-gene relocation of the ancestral copy*, but cannot rule out hemizygous Y/W-specific insertions in homogametic assemblies.

### Defect 2: Misinterpreting Relaxed Purifying Selection ($\omega < 1$) and Non-Significant Branch-Site Tests as Positive Selection (Pillars 3 & 4 — Critical)
* **Location:** Abstract (Line 5), Section 2.6 (Lines 69–71), Section 3.4 (ContentOffset 46080, Lines 1, 6, 8, 10, 20, 24), Discussion (Line 39), and Conclusions (Line 46).
* **Statistical & Evolutionary Flaw:**
  1. **Branch models ($\omega < 1$):** In PAML branch models, comparing a two-ratio model ($\omega_{\text{fg}} \ne \omega_{\text{bg}}$) against $M_0$ ($\omega_{\text{fg}} = \omega_{\text{bg}} \approx 0.05$) tests whether $\omega_{\text{fg}}$ differs from the background—NOT whether $\omega_{\text{fg}} > 1$. Finding $\omega_{\text{fg}} = 0.47$ (*DmW*) or $\omega_{\text{fg}} = 0.92$ (*P. ibericus* duplicate) with $p < 0.05$ against $M_0$ demonstrates **relaxation of purifying selection** (or neutral drift $\omega = 1$, which must be tested against a fixed $\omega_{\text{fg}} = 1.0$ null model!). For *Pelodytes ibericus*, no transcriptome data exist to show the duplicate is even expressed, making neutral drift of a non-functional duplicate ($\omega \approx 1.0$) the parsimonious null hypothesis.
  2. **Non-significant branch-site tests reported as positive selection:** In ContentOffset 46080 Line 24, the authors admit that the branch-site tests for positions 43, 53, and 72 in *DmW* and *P. ibericus* *"are not statistically significant (Table S12D)"*, yet highlight them with red stars in Figure 6 and state in the Conclusions (Line 46): *"The positively selected sites identified in the duplicated Dmrt1 copy of Pelodytes ibericus point to potential functional innovations."*
  3. **Extreme small-sample $d_S \approx 0$ artifacts included in Spearman and GLM tests:** In ContentOffset 46080 Line 1, *Oophaga pumilio* ($d_N = 0.003556, d_S = 0.000004 \implies \omega = 889$) and *Rana septentrionalis* ($d_N = 0.007135, d_S = 0.000007 \implies \omega > 1000$) have boundary $d_S \to 0$ artifacts where $0$ synonymous substitutions occurred along a short terminal branch. Including boundary $d_S \approx 0$ estimates in a Gamma GLM (`ω ~ chromosome + duplication`) and non-phylogenetic Spearman correlation across 53 tips distorts the parametric fit.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** (1) Replace all claims of "positive selection" or "tendency towards positive selection" for $\omega = 0.47$ and $\omega = 0.92$ with **"relaxed purifying selection (or neutral evolution)"**, and report the likelihood ratio test comparing the branch-free model ($\omega_{\text{fg}} = 0.92$) against the branch-neutral model ($\omega_{\text{fg}} = 1.0$). (2) Remove red stars and claims of "positively selected sites" in Figure 6, the Abstract, and the Conclusions for any site where the branch-site LRT did not pass Benjamini–Hochberg FDR $< 0.05$ across the tested branches. (3) Use Phylogenetic Generalized Least Squares (PGLS) or a phylogenetic mixed model rather than an i.i.d. Gamma GLM in Section 3.4, excluding terminal branches with $d_S < 0.001$.

### Defect 3: Substitution Model Mismatch in Section 2.2 & Position Numbering Inconsistency in Section 3.5 (Pillar 7 — Minor)
* **Location:** Section 2.2 (Line 41) and Section 3.5 (ContentOffset 46080, Lines 20 & 24).
* **Technical Flaws:**
  1. **Model selection vs. tree inference mismatch (Line 41):** Section 2.2 states: *"The best model `--model HKY+I+G4` was selected based on the Bayesian Information Criterion score. Third, we generated maximum likelihood genome-wide trees using raxml-ng with 1000 bootstraps, employing the `GTR+I+G4` model."* If `ModelTest-NG` selected `HKY+I+G4`, why was `GTR+I+G4` used in `raxml-ng` without explanation?
  2. **Residue position mismatch between Figure 6 legend and text (ContentOffset 46080, Lines 20 vs. 24):** The Figure 6 legend (Line 20) states `"position 64 in H. sarda"`, whereas the main text four lines later (Line 24) states `"at position 60, with an amino acid change from lysine (K) to arginine (R)"`.
  3. **Taxonomic spelling typos:** `Bufo varilis` (Line 22) vs. `Bufo viridis` (Line 35) vs. `Bufotes viridis` (ContentOffset 46080 Line 10); `Ranidea` (Line 124 and ContentOffset 46080 Line 29) instead of `Ranidae`; `Pelodvtes ibericus` (Line 33); `Bol1l` / `Bodl1l` (Lines 114, 122, 124) instead of `Bod1l`; `ovaryies` (Line 103).
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Reconcile the `HKY+I+G4` vs. `GTR+I+G4` model description in Section 2.2, fix the residue position discrepancy (`60` vs. `64`) in *H. sarda*, and correct the taxonomic and gene-name typos throughout the text.
