## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study investigates motor, anxiety-like, social, and object-recognition behaviors alongside whole-brain bulk transcriptomics and label-free proteomics in 1-year-old $sacs^{-/-}$ mutant zebrafish (an autosomal recessive spastic ataxia of Charlevoix-Saguenay [ARSACS] model) and evaluates the effects of chronic dietary supplementation with 0.1% tauroursodeoxycholic acid (TUDCA) from 5 dpf to 12 months. The authors report that TUDCA rescues early larval mortality, partially improves adult swimming speed, anxiety-like behavior, shoaling, social preference, and novel object recognition (NOR), and alters brain transcriptomic and proteomic pathways associated with circadian rhythms, neuroinflammation, lipid metabolism, and mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation.

**Editorial Verdict: Reject / Flawed & Overstated (4.5 / 10.0).** While testing a neuroprotective bile acid in an adult vertebrate ARSACS model is biologically interesting, the manuscript suffers from severe experimental design, statistical, and methodological flaws that invalidate several primary conclusions:
1. **Severe Locomotor & Color Confounding in Behavioral Cognitive/Social Assays:** The authors establish in Figure 2B that 1-year-old $sacs^{-/-}$ fish have severely impaired swimming speed and motor control and spend most of their time immobilized at the bottom/walls of the tank. Yet in the Novel Object Recognition (NOR) and Social Preference assays, cognitive memory and sociability are scored purely by spatial zone occupancy (proximity within an $8 \times 8\text{ cm}$ box around a cube or time spent in the conspecific sector) without normalizing for total distance traveled or baseline object exploration time. Furthermore, in the NOR task (Methods, Line 6), Phase 1 uses two *blue* cubes and Phase 2 replaces one with a *red* cube without counterbalancing colors—confounding innate color preference in zebrafish (which have strong innate preferences/aversions between red and blue) with recognition memory!
2. **Survivorship Selection Bias From Larval Lethality Rescue:** Untreated $sacs^{-/-}$ zebrafish experience high mortality during the first 30 days of life, whereas TUDCA rescues this early lethality (Figure 1B). Consequently, the 12-month-old untreated $sacs^{-/-}$ cohort represents a highly selected survivor subpopulation, whereas the TUDCA-treated cohort includes fish that would otherwise have died—confounding chronic drug treatment effects at 12 months with differential survivorship selection.
3. **Pseudoreplication in Shoaling & Unspecified Sample Sizes / Upstream RNA-seq Pipeline:** In the shoaling test, inter-fish distance is a group-level property of a shoal, yet the Methods do not specify how many independent shoals (tanks) versus individual fish were used as the unit of replication $N$. In the RNA-seq Methods (Line 71), the read aligner, reference genome assembly version, and differential expression tool (e.g., DESeq2/edgeR) are completely omitted, and RNA integrity numbers (RIN 5.6–8.1) span degraded to intact samples without batch/RIN covariate adjustment in an $n=4$ design.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Dietary 0.1% TUDCA supplementation improves 30-day larval survival in $sacs^{-/-}$ zebrafish: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Figure 1B shows improved survival over the first month, though the exact sample sizes ($N$ larvae and $N$ independent rearing tanks per condition) and survival log-rank test statistics are not reported in the text or Methods.
* **Aged $sacs^{-/-}$ zebrafish exhibit specific deficits in recognition memory (NOR) and social preference that are rescued by TUDCA: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In the NOR test, the "novel" object is always a red cube and the "familiar" object is always a blue cube (no color counterbalancing), and exploration is defined simply as presence within an $8 \times 8\text{ cm}$ zone in a $30 \times 30\text{ cm}$ tank despite severe motor ataxia and thigmotaxis in $sacs^{-/-}$ mutants. A fish that hugs the perimeter wall or moves slowly fails to enter the object zone due to motor/thigmotactic impairment, not amnesia, and WT/TUDCA preference for the red cube over the blue cube is completely confounded with innate zebrafish chromatic preference.
* **Transcriptomic and proteomic analyses demonstrate dysregulation of circadian rhythm and neuroinflammatory pathways in $sacs^{-/-}$ brains and reversal by TUDCA: [Fragile].**  
  With only $n=4$ brains per group, RIN values ranging from degraded (5.6) to intact (8.1), no documented aligner or DE statistical model in Methods, and acknowledged absence of core clock proteins (CLOCK, BMAL1, PER, CRY) in the proteomics dataset (Discussion, Line 58), the circadian claim rests on unvalidated RNA-seq GO enrichment that could easily arise from slight differences in sacrifice time-of-day across groups.
* **Control WT and $sacs^{-/-}$ fish share an identical genetic background and environmental history: [Fragile].**  
  Methods (Line 66) contain a direct internal contradiction regarding control diets and breeding: it states both that "The homozygous $sacs^{-/-}$ zebrafish mutant line was crossed, and the F2 progeny were used" and that "The control animals used in the study were from the same batch of offspring as the $sacs^{-/-}$ mutants" (which is only possible from a heterozygous $sacs^{+/-} \times sacs^{+/-}$ cross, not a homozygous $sacs^{-/-}$ incross), and states first that "1 control diet and 1 experimental diet" were prepared, but six sentences later states "Control fish were maintained under the same conditions as the treated fish but without exposure to any treatment."

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Uncounterbalanced Color Confounding & Motor/Thigmotaxis Confounding in the Novel Object Recognition (NOR) Assay (Pillars 1, 3 & 5 — Critical)
* **Location:** Results (Line 33) and Methods ("Novel object recognition task", Line 6).
* **Methodological Flaw:** In Phase 1 (training), fish are presented with two identical *blue* cubes. In Phase 2 (testing), one blue cube is replaced with a *red* cube, without counterbalancing (i.e., half the fish trained with red cubes and tested with a novel blue cube). Adult zebrafish exhibit well-documented innate color preferences (typically strong preference for or avoidance of red vs. blue depending on illumination). Thus, a higher percentage of time near the red cube in Phase 2 cannot be distinguished from an innate color preference that was absent in Phase 1 (where both cubes were blue). Furthermore, "exploration" is defined purely as occupancy inside an $8 \times 8\text{ cm}$ square around the cube (Line 6: *"This measurement reflects proximity rather than active exploration based on head orientation"*). Because untreated $sacs^{-/-}$ fish have severe thigmotaxis (wall-hugging, Figure 2F) and reduced swimming velocity (Figure 2B), their lack of preference is confounded by failure to leave the tank walls to visit the interior cubes.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Report the total time spent in *both* object zones combined ($T_{\text{blue}} + T_{\text{red}}$) and total distance traveled during Phase 1 and Phase 2 for each group, compute the normalized discrimination index $DI = (T_{\text{novel}} - T_{\text{familiar}}) / (T_{\text{novel}} + T_{\text{familiar}})$ excluding animals that failed to visit both objects for a minimum threshold (e.g., $\ge 5\text{ s}$), and explicitly acknowledge the lack of color counterbalancing (blue vs. red) as a major limitation of the NOR paradigm.

### Defect 2: Uncontrolled Time-of-Day Sacrifice Confounding for Circadian Transcriptomics & Omitted Bioinformatics Pipeline (Pillars 2, 3 & 7 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 38–40), Discussion (Line 58), and Methods ("RNA-sequencing", Line 71).
* **Methodological Flaw:** Behavioral testing was conducted between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. (a 6-hour window across the 14h/10h light/dark cycle), and Methods (Line 66) state: *"After completing the behavioral experiments, the fish were sacrificed, and organ dissection was performed on the same day."* Core circadian clock genes (*per*, *cry*, *clock*, *bmal1*) undergo massive multi-fold transcriptional oscillations between 10 a.m. (ZT2–4) and 4 p.m. (ZT8–10). If the 4 WT brains were dissected in the morning and the 4 $sacs^{-/-}$ brains were dissected in the afternoon after behavioral testing, strong differential expression of *per* and *cry* genes is guaranteed purely from Zeitgeber time (ZT) drift! Moreover, the RNA-seq Methods section (Line 71) jumps directly from NovaSeq6000 sequencing ("48.3 million fragments per sample") to `ClusterProfiler (v3.18.1)`, completely omitting the read trimming tool, alignment software (e.g., STAR/HISAT2), *Danio rerio* genome build (e.g., GRCz11), read counting tool, and differential expression package (e.g., DESeq2/edgeR) or RIN covariate adjustment (RIN 5.6 to 8.1).
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Document the exact Zeitgeber time (ZT) of sacrifice for each of the 12 RNA-seq and 12 proteomics brain samples. Add the complete RNA-seq bioinformatic pipeline (aligner, reference assembly, counting tool, DE statistical model, and RIN covariate handling) to the Methods section.

### Defect 3: Internal Contradictions in Breeding Design, Diet Controls, and Copy-Pasted Methods Text (Pillar 7 — Major)
* **Location:** Methods, Lines 66, 71, 79, 84, and Page 2 Lines 1, 11.
* **Technical & Editorial Flaws:**
  1. **Breeding contradiction (Line 66):** States both that *"The homozygous $sacs^{-/-}$ zebrafish mutant line was crossed, and the F2 progeny were used"* AND *"The control animals used in the study were from the same batch of offspring as the $sacs^{-/-}$ mutants"*. A homozygous $sacs^{-/-} \times sacs^{-/-}$ cross produces 100% $sacs^{-/-}$ offspring; WT controls can only come from the same batch of offspring if generated via a heterozygous $sacs^{+/-} \times sacs^{+/-}$ cross.
  2. **Copy-pasted RNA text in Proteomics section (Line 79):** Under `## Protein isolation`, the second sentence reads: *"RNA was extracted from individual samples to maintain biological variability according to standard procedures"*—verbatim copy-pasted from the `## RNA-sequencing` section.
  3. **Duplicated MS chromatographic description & unit typos (Line 84):** The Ultimate 3000 / Q Exactive Plus setup is described twice in the same paragraph, with column dimensions misstated as `75 mm, 50 cm, 2 mm particle size` (instead of $75\text{ }\mu\text{m} \times 50\text{ cm}, 2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ particle size) and protease inhibitors in Line 79 written as `20 g/ml leupeptin, 25 g/ml aprotinin, 10 g/ml pepstatin` (which would be $20\text{ g/mL}$, a million-fold error for $\mu\text{g/mL}$).
  4. **Identical formulas for Time Ratio and Distance Ratio (ContentOffset 46080, Line 1):** Both `Time ratio %` and `Distance ratio %` are defined identically as `(Time spent in conspecific sector / total time observed) × 100`.
  5. **Missing sample sizes ($N$) across all behavioral assays:** Nowhere in the Methods or main text are the exact per-group animal numbers ($N$) or tank replicate numbers reported for survival, novel tank, open-field, shoaling, social preference, or NOR tests, nor is a `wt + TUDCA` control group included to test whether TUDCA non-specifically increases locomotion.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Resolve the breeding and diet control contradictions, report exact $N$ (fish and independent tanks/shoals) and ANOVA $F$-statistics/degrees of freedom for every behavioral panel, and fix the copy-paste and unit errors in Lines 79, 84, and Page 2 Line 1.
