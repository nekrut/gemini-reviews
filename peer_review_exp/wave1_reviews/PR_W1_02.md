## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript establishes a mouse model of unilateral stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS)-induced sensorineural hearing loss using a clinical Leksell Gamma Knife ICON platform with cone-beam CT (CBCT) co-registered to MRI and CT mouse atlases. Thirty-one C57BL/6J mice received single-fraction doses of 8 Gy ($n=3$), 16 Gy ($n=5$), 24 Gy ($n=8$), or 32 Gy ($n=15$, of which $n=8$ were sacrificed at 4 weeks and $n=7$ followed to 16 weeks) prescribed to the 80% isodose line passing through the medial edge of the right cochlea. Using the contralateral ear (receiving $<15\%$ of the prescribed dose, i.e., $1.2\text{–}4.83\text{ Gy}$) as a paired internal control, the authors evaluate auditory brainstem response (ABR) thresholds and click-evoked wave I amplitudes alongside cochlear histopathology (Myo7a outer/inner hair cell cytocochleograms, CtBP2 presynaptic ribbon counts, H&E spiral ganglion neuron [SGN] density segmented via Cellpose, and stria vascularis thickness). They report dose-dependent, high-frequency-predominant, progressive ABR threshold shifts and wave I amplitude reductions at 24 and 32 Gy accompanied by basal/high-frequency OHC loss, ribbon synapse loss, and SGN loss, without systemic toxicity or weight loss.

The experimental stereotactic dosimetry and longitudinal pairing are well executed and represent a clear technical refinement over broad-field head irradiation models. However, the study's statistical precision is limited by splitting the sacrificed cochleae within each group into two mutually exclusive histological pipelines (paraffin H&E vs. whole-mount immunofluorescence), reducing histological sample sizes to $n=3\text{–}5$ mice per condition and producing severely inflated paired effect size estimates (e.g., $d_z = 7.05$ on $n=3$). There are also numerical discrepancies between the Abstract and Results text regarding OHC loss percentages, and raw data are only available "upon request."

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1: Image-guided Gamma Knife SRS achieves unilateral cochlear targeting with $<15\%$ contralateral scatter dose and zero systemic morbidity.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Dosimetric segmentation shows contralateral mean doses of $1.2 \pm 0.3\text{ Gy}$ (8 Gy group) to $4.83 \pm 0.44\text{ Gy}$ (32 Gy group), sparing the brainstem and pharyngeal mucosa and eliminating the weight loss and mortality seen with cabinet irradiators.
* **Claim 2: Unilateral SRS at 24 and 32 Gy induces dose-dependent, progressive, high-frequency ABR threshold shifts and suprathreshold wave I amplitude decline.** `[Well Supported]`  
  In paired interaural comparisons ($n=8$ at 4 weeks; $n=7$ at 16 weeks), 32 Gy irradiation produces ~25 dB shifts at 22.6–32 kHz by week 4 ($p < 0.0001$) and progressive pan-frequency shifts through week 16 alongside a 36–37% drop in wave I amplitude, outpacing the C57BL/6J age-related hearing loss in the contralateral ear.
* **Claim 3: Functional hearing loss is mirrored by dose- and time-dependent basal OHC loss, CtBP2 ribbon synapse reduction, and SGN loss.** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Whole-mount cytocochleograms ($n=4\text{–}5$) and mid-modiolar sections ($n=3$) show concordant high-frequency OHC and ribbon loss and basal/middle turn SGN reduction at 32 Gy. However, the sample sizes after splitting tissues ($n=3$ for SGN and stria vascularis) are too small to verify normality or support parametric repeated-measures ANOVA.
* **Claim 4: Stria vascularis thickness is significantly reduced at the middle turn at 16 weeks post-32 Gy SRS.** `[Fragile]`  
  At 4 weeks, the 4.3 $\mu\text{m}$ (21%) reduction in middle-turn stria thickness is non-significant ($p=0.20, n=3$), whereas at 16 weeks a smaller absolute difference ($3.7\ \mu\text{m}, n=3$) reaches $p=0.02$ solely due to lower sample variance across 3 animals.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

1. **Severely Underpowered Histological Subgroups ($n=3$) Analyzed via Parametric Repeated-Measures ANOVA:**  
   In Section 2.4 (line 121), the authors state that after decalcification, "both cochleae from each mouse were randomly assigned to one of two downstream applications: (1) paraffin embedding for mid-modiolar sectioning or (2) whole-mount dissection." Consequently, out of $n=8$ mice in the 24 Gy and 32 Gy 4-week groups, only $n=5$ were used for hair cell/ribbon counts and $n=3$ for SGN density and stria vascularis thickness; in the 16-week 32 Gy group ($n=7$), only $n=4$ were used for whole-mounts and $n=3$ for H&E sections (Figures 4 and 5 legends). In the 8 Gy group (total $n=3$), splitting between two histological pipelines leaves at most $n=1\text{ or }2$ animals for one of the modalities, yet Section 3.4 (line 197) reports a parametric $p=0.01$ (95% CI: 1.2 to 7.3) for 8 Gy OHC loss. Inspecting residuals for normality (line 156) with $n=3$ pairs ($2\text{ df}$) is statistically uninformative, and computing paired Cohen's $d_z = t / \sqrt{n}$ on $n=3$ animals yields extreme small-sample volatility (e.g., $d_z = 7.05$ in line 223 and $d_z = 4.75$ in line 225).
2. **Numerical Contradictions Between the Abstract and Main Text Results:**  
   In the Abstract (line 59), the authors state: *"At 32 Gy, outer hair cells were reduced by 14 and 44% at 32 and 45.2 kHz, respectively, at 4 weeks, and by 38 and 80% at 16 weeks."* However, in the Results text, Section 3.4 reports entirely different numbers for the exact same comparisons:
   * At 4 weeks (line 197): OHC reduction is reported as **13.3 percentage points** at 32 kHz and **40.1 percentage points** at 45.2 kHz (95% CI: 25 to 55.1).
   * At 16 weeks (line 205): OHC reduction is reported as **33 percentage points** at 32 kHz (95% CI: 10.9 to 55.1) and **48.5 percentage points** at 45.2 kHz (95% CI: 26.4 to 70.6).  
   Whether this discrepancy arises from conflating relative percentage reduction ($[C - I]/C$) in the Abstract with absolute percentage-point interaural differences ($C\% - I\%$) in the Results—especially as C57BL/6J contralateral ears also lose basal OHCs by 16 weeks (22 weeks of age)—must be explicitly reconciled.
3. **Uncontrolled Confounding from Scattered Dose ($4.83\text{ Gy}$) plus Cadherin-23 Age-Related Hearing Loss in the Contralateral Ear:**  
   C57BL/6J mice carry the hypomorphic $Cdh23^{ahl}$ allele and develop accelerated high-frequency hearing loss between 6 and 22 weeks of age (the 16-week follow-up window). In addition, the contralateral cochlea in the 32 Gy cohort received $4.83 \pm 0.44\text{ Gy}$. Without an unirradiated sham-anesthetized age-matched control cohort, the study cannot test whether a 4.83 Gy scatter dose interacts synergistically with the $Cdh23^{ahl}$ allele to accelerate contralateral basal OHC or ribbon degeneration, which would attenuate the estimated interaural differences at 16 weeks.
4. **Two-Way Repeated-Measures ANOVA Applied Without Greenhouse-Geisser Correction or Omnibus Interaction Reporting:**  
   Section 2.6 (line 156) notes that "Because repeated-measures ANOVA assumes sphericity for factors with more than two levels, results were interpreted primarily on the basis of the planned post hoc paired comparisons." Bypassing the omnibus `Ear × Frequency` or `Ear × Time` interaction and sphericity (Greenhouse-Geisser) corrections to rely directly on family-wise post hoc tests across 9 frequencies or 6 cytocochleogram bins does not formally test whether radiation damage is frequency-selective (interaction effect) versus uniform across turns.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

* **Missing Section 2.1 in Materials and Methods:** The Methods section jumps directly from `## 2. Materials and methods` (line 80) to `### 2.2. Stereotactic radiosurgery setup, imaging, and targeting` (line 83), while Section 2.3 (line 106) refers back to "animals under general anesthesia, as described in Section 2.1." Section 2.1 (animals, sex distribution, age, ethics approval, and anesthesia doses) was inadvertently deleted during manuscript preparation.
* **Mismatch in Tested vs. Histologically Mapped Frequencies:** ABR tone bursts were only tested up to 32 kHz (line 110), whereas the main histological OHC and ribbon synapse effects at lower doses (8–24 Gy) were only statistically significant at the 45.2 kHz place-frequency region (line 197), where no functional ABR measurement was recorded.
* **Lack of Postsynaptic Marker for Ribbon Synapses:** As acknowledged in Section 4.6, quantifying CtBP2 puncta alone via maximum-intensity Z-projections ($10\text{–}15\ \mu\text{m}$ depth collapsed into a 2D plane, line 147) without GluA2/PSD95 co-staining or 3D spot segmentation can undercount vertically overlapping ribbons and cannot distinguish orphan presynaptic ribbons from intact functional synapses.
* **Data Availability:** Raw electrophysiological and morphometric tables are not deposited in a public repository ("will be made available by the authors, without undue reservation," line 26).

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Reconcile the OHC Loss Figures Between the Abstract and Section 3.4:**
   * **WHY:** Reporting 44% and 80% OHC loss at 45.2 kHz in the Abstract while reporting 40.1 and 48.5 percentage points in Section 3.4 creates a major numerical contradiction (particularly the 80% vs. 48.5% figure at 16 weeks).
   * **WHAT:** Standardize the definition of OHC loss across the Abstract, Results text, and Figures 4–5.
   * **HOW:** Explicitly state both the absolute interaural difference in percentage points ($\text{Survival}_{\text{contra}} - \text{Survival}_{\text{irrad}}$) and the relative percentage reduction ($1 - \text{Survival}_{\text{irrad}}/\text{Survival}_{\text{contra}}$), and report the baseline OHC loss in the 22-week-old contralateral C57BL/6J ears at 32 and 45.2 kHz.
2. **Restore Missing Section 2.1 and Report Exact Sample Sizes for All Dose Groups:**
   * **WHY:** Section 2.1 is missing from the text (referenced in line 106), and the exact whole-mount vs. paraffin sample split for the 8 Gy ($n=3$) and 16 Gy ($n=5$) groups is not stated.
   * **WHAT:** Re-insert Section 2.1 detailing animal sex, exact age at irradiation, housing, ethics protocol number, and ketamine/xylazine dosage, and add a summary table showing exact $n$ allocated to ABR, whole-mount immunostaining, and H&E sectioning for all four dose groups.
   * **HOW:** Verify how $n=3$ mice in the 8 Gy group were allocated between whole-mount (Supplementary Fig. S2I) and paraffin embedding; if $n \le 4$ in any histological subgroup, supplement or replace parametric $t$/ANOVA $p$-values and inflated Cohen's $d_z$ values with exact paired permutation or Wilcoxon signed-rank bounds and show all individual animal data points on Figures 2–5.
3. **Report Greenhouse-Geisser Corrected Omnibus `Ear × Frequency` and `Ear × Time` Interactions:**
   * **WHY:** Claiming frequency-specific and progressive toxicity requires demonstrating a statistically significant `Ear × Frequency` and `Ear × Time` interaction in the repeated-measures model.
   * **WHAT:** Report the $F$-statistics, Greenhouse-Geisser $\epsilon$-adjusted degrees of freedom, and $p$-values for the main effects and interaction terms in Sections 3.2–3.6.
   * **HOW:** Export the full two-way repeated-measures ANOVA tables from GraphPad Prism 10.4.2 (with Geisser-Greenhouse sphericity correction enabled) and include them in the main text or as a supplementary statistical table alongside a public Zenodo/Figshare spreadsheet of the per-animal ABR and histological counts.
