## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study develops a qualitative and quantitative in vitro agar confrontation assay using confluent lawns of *Zymoseptoria tritici* blastospores to screen root-associated *Pseudomonas* isolates for antifungal activity. Screening 534 *Pseudomonas* isolates from the Rothamsted collection against the reference strain *Z. tritici* `IPO323` identified 52 antagonistic isolates. A subset of 5 antagonistic (`BFR20`, `BFB20`, `Roth82`, `Roth107`, `Roth112`) and 6 non-antagonistic *Pseudomonas* isolates were then tested quantitatively across 12 genetically diverse European *Z. tritici* isolates ($n=4$ replicate plates per fungal genotype, with 3 radial inhibition-zone measurements per colony). Whole-genome sequencing of the 11 *Pseudomonas* isolates followed by `autoMLST` phylogenetics, `antiSMASH 7.0.0`, and `BiG-SCAPE` networking identified 131 biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs) grouped into 46 gene cluster families (GCFs). Three *P. corrugata*-subgroup isolates (`Roth82`, `Roth107`, `Roth112`) harbored the 2,4-diacetylphloroglucinol (`2,4-DAPG`) BGC (`GCF_02551`), while two *P. mandelii*-subgroup antagonists (`BFR20`, `BFB20`) lacked known antifungal BGCs. Disrupting `phlD` in `Roth82` (`Roth82ΔphlD`) abolished both *Z. tritici* growth inhibition and the `2,4-DAPG` peak ($m/z\ 209.0455$) in UPLC-QTOF-MS agar extracts.

**Editorial Verdict: Minor-to-Major Revision (Solid bioassay-guided genome mining study requiring statistical refinement and resolution of an omitted genetic complementation control).**  
The integration of a standardized blastospore-lawn bioassay across 12 pathogen genotypes with BGC networking (`antiSMASH`/`BiG-SCAPE`), targeted mutagenesis (`phlD`), and UPLC-QTOF-MS chemical verification against an authentic standard is well-conceived and practical. However, four specific technical issues should be resolved prior to publication:
1. **Omitted Complementation Control Promised in Methods Heading (Line 130):** The Methods section is titled `## Construction of phlD mutant and complement`, yet the text only describes constructing a single-crossover `pKNOCK-Km` insertional disruption (`Roth82ΔphlD`) and contains no complementation construct or assay. Because `pKNOCK-Km` plasmid insertion can cause polar effects on downstream genes in the `phl` operon, either the complementation data should be included or the heading and claims should be adjusted to reflect an uncomplemented insertional mutant.
2. **Separate One-Way ANOVAs Instead of a Unified Two-Way Mixed-Effects Model (Lines 41, 72, 107, 147):** Although the 5 antagonistic *Pseudomonas* isolates were spotted together on the same replicate plates across 12 *Z. tritici* genotypes in a factorial design, the authors ran five separate one-way ANOVAs (log-transforming `BFR20` but not the other four strains) rather than a two-way linear mixed-effects model (`Zone ~ Z_tritici * Pseudomonas + (1 | Plate_ID)`), leaving their Discussion claims of strain-by-strain rank reversals formally untested.
3. **Impossible Pixel-to-Millimeter Calibration Factor (`2253 pixels = 1 mm`, Line 107):** A Nikon D80 camera ($3872 \times 2592$ pixels total) with a 70 mm lens at a height of 78.5 cm cannot have a spatial scale of `2253 pixels = 1 mm` (which would mean the entire camera frame is only $1.72\text{ mm}$ wide; the true scale is $\sim 22.53\text{ pixels} = 1\text{ mm}$).
4. **Incomplete LC-MS Reporting (Lines 59, 137):** Line 137 states that agar extracts were prepared from `Roth82` and `Roth82ΔphlD` *"grown with and without Z. tritici confrontation"*, but the "without confrontation" LC-MS data and the number of LC-MS replicates are omitted from the Results.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1: The blastospore-lawn confrontation assay reproducibly identifies and quantifies *Pseudomonas* antagonism across genetically diverse *Z. tritici* isolates, revealing significant variation in pathogen sensitivity ($p < 0.001$ for all 5 antagonistic strains, Figs 1–2).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Each of the 5 antagonistic *Pseudomonas* strains produced measurable clearing zones across all 12 *Z. tritici* isolates ($F(11,36) = 4.46\text{ to }11.48, p < 0.001$), while all 6 non-antagonistic strains and broth controls produced zero clearing across all 12 fungal genotypes.
* **Claim 2: No single *Z. tritici* isolate is consistently the most sensitive or resistant across all *Pseudomonas* antagonists, indicating diverse modes of action (`Pseudomonas` $\times$ `Z. tritici` interaction, Discussion Line 72).** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Visual inspection of Figure 2d–h and the distinct BGC repertoires (`P. corrugata` 2,4-DAPG producers vs. `P. mandelii` `BFR20`/`BFB20`) support this interpretation, but a formal two-way interaction test ($\text{Fungal Genotype} \times \text{Bacterial Strain}$) with a random effect for shared `Plate_ID` is needed to statistically prove rank-order interaction, especially since `BFR20` was analyzed on a log scale while the other four strains were analyzed on a linear scale.
* **Claim 3: Antagonism by *P. corrugata*-subgroup isolate `Roth82` is mediated by 2,4-DAPG biosynthesis encoded by `GCF_02551` (`phlD`), whereas *P. mandelii* isolates `BFR20` and `BFB20` antagonize *Z. tritici* via uncharacterized BGCs (Figs 3–5).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Supported by `clinker` synteny alignment with `MIBiG` `BGC0000280`/`BGC0000281`, loss of both the inhibition zone and the $m/z\ 209.0455$ UPLC-QTOF-MS peak matching authentic `2,4-DAPG` in `Roth82ΔphlD`, and absence of known antifungal BGCs in `BFR20`/`BFB20`. (Adding the genetic complementation referenced in Line 130 would make the `phlD` genetic proof definitive).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Missing Genetic Complementation Despite Section Heading `## Construction of phlD mutant and complement` (Line 130)
* **Location:** Experimental procedures line 130–133; Results line 59.
* **Methodological & Editorial Discrepancy:**
  1. Line 130 is titled `## Construction of phlD mutant and complement`, yet Line 132 describes only the construction of a single-crossover insertional mutant (`Roth82ΔphlD`) created by electroporating the suicide plasmid `pKNOCK-Km` carrying a 333-bp internal fragment of `phlD`. No complementation plasmid, strain, or bioassay is described anywhere in the manuscript.
  2. Terminologically, single-crossover integration of `pKNOCK-Km` into an internal gene fragment creates an insertional disruption (`phlD::pKNOCK-Km`), not a clean deletion (`ΔphlD`). If a complemented strain was generated, those data should be added to Fig 5; if not, the section header in Line 130 must be corrected and the text should note the possibility of polar effects within the `phl` gene cluster.

### Defect 2: Five Separate One-Way ANOVAs (With Inconsistent Log Transformation) Instead of a Two-Way Mixed-Effects Model Accounting for Shared Plates (Lines 41, 72, 107, 147)
* **Location:** Results line 41; Discussion line 72; Experimental procedures lines 107 and 147.
* **Statistical Design Flaw:**
  1. In the quantitative assay (Line 107), all 11 *Pseudomonas* isolates (5 antagonistic, 6 non-antagonistic) and 1 LB control were spotted onto the **same** $120 \times 120\text{ mm}$ PDA plate inoculated with a given *Z. tritici* isolate ($n=4$ replicate plates per *Z. tritici* isolate; 48 plates total).
  2. Because the 5 antagonistic *Pseudomonas* colonies on a single plate share the same fungal lawn preparation, incubation environment, and potential volatile/diffusible metabolite cross-talk inside a Parafilm-sealed dish, measurements from the same plate are clustered by `Plate_ID`.
  3. Furthermore, to test whether *Z. tritici* genotypes differ in their relative sensitivity rankings across different *Pseudomonas* antagonists (Discussion Line 72), the data should be analyzed in a unified two-way linear mixed-effects model (`Zone ~ Z_tritici_Isolate * Pseudomonas_Isolate + (1 | Plate_ID)`) on a consistent scale, rather than running five separate one-way ANOVAs where `BFR20` is log-transformed and the other four strains are untransformed (Line 147).

### Defect 3: Optical Calibration Error (`2253 pixels = 1 mm`, Line 107)
* **Location:** Experimental procedures line 107.
* **Quantitative Typo:** Line 107 states that plates were photographed from a height of $78.5\text{ cm}$ using a Nikon D80 digital camera with a Sigma 70 mm macro lens, and quantified in FIJI/ImageJ using the scale **`2253 pixels = 1 mm`**. The Nikon D80 sensor has a total resolution of $3872 \times 2592\text{ pixels}$; at $2253\text{ pixels/mm}$, the entire image width would be $1.72\text{ mm}$. At a working distance of $78.5\text{ cm}$ with a $70\text{ mm}$ lens, a $100\text{ mm}$ ($10\text{ cm}$) ruler measures $\sim 2253\text{ pixels}$, meaning the true calibration is **`22.53 pixels = 1 mm`** (or `2253 pixels = 100 mm`).

### Defect 4: Missing "Without Confrontation" LC-MS Data, Unreported LC-MS Replicates, Misinterpreted `BUSCO` Metric, and Template Placeholders
* **Omitted Unconfronted LC-MS Control and Replicate Count (Lines 59, 137):** Line 137 states that agar sections were extracted from `Roth82` and `Roth82phlD` *"grown with and without Z. tritici confrontation on PDA plates"*, yet Figure 5c and Line 59 only report the confronted extracts and do not state how many biological replicates were analyzed by UPLC-QTOF-MS.
* **Misstatement of `BUSCO` Purpose (Lines 46, 117):** Lines 46 and 117 state that `BUSCO` with `bacteria_odb10` was run *"to check for bacterial contamination within the genome assembly"* and returned `>99%`. Total `BUSCO` completeness (`>99%`) measures assembly completeness; contamination in `BUSCO` is reflected specifically by the percentage of *duplicated* single-copy orthologs (or assessed via `Kraken`/`CheckM`), which should be stated precision-wise.
* **Unedited Template Text and Typos:**
  * Line 152 (Data Availability) contains unedited bracketed template text: `[are/will be available following publication]` and duplicated wording (`will be made publicly available, with Datasets associated with this project will be made available`).
  * Line 26 writes `blastopore-based assay` and Line 52 writes `Z. tritici blastopores` instead of `blastospore(s)`.
  * Line 48 writes `BGC000281` (missing a zero; corrected to `BGC0000281` in Line 127).
  * Line 76 has an unclosed parenthesis `(predicted from BFB20,` and writes `consistent with s with previous studies`.
