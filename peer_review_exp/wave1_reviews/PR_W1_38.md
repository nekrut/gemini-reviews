## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study characterizes the population pharmacokinetics of oral pretomanid (200 mg daily for 24 weeks) in 94 patients with rifampicin-resistant tuberculosis (RR-TB) enrolled in the multi-country TB-PRACTECAL randomized controlled trial (BPaL, BPaLM, and BPaLC regimens), and evaluates probability of target attainment (PTA) against baseline MGIT MIC distributions ($N=478$, median $\text{MIC} = 0.125\text{ mg/L}$, IQR $0.125$–$0.25\text{ mg/L}$). Using `nlmixr2` (FOCE-I), the authors fit a one-compartment model with first-order absorption and elimination and *a priori* fat-free mass (FFM) allometric scaling ($\text{CL}/F = 3.10\text{ L/h}$, median $\text{AUC}_{0-24} = 63,733\text{ }\mu\text{g}\cdot\text{h/L}$). In 2,000-patient Monte Carlo simulations assuming 85% protein binding, they find that 200 mg daily pretomanid achieves high PTA for $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ targets (48% and 77%) up to $\text{MIC} = 0.250\text{ mg/L}$, whereas PTA for the $f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC} \ge 167$ target is achieved only at $\text{MIC} \le 0.032\text{ mg/L}$ and drops to near 0% at the clinical median MIC of $0.125\text{ mg/L}$.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision / Mixed & Salvageable (6.1 / 10.0).** While the clinical PK and MGIT MIC datasets from the landmark TB-PRACTECAL trial are valuable and confirm that moxifloxacin, clofazimine, and integrase-inhibitor ART do not meaningfully alter pretomanid clearance, the pharmacometric modeling and reporting exhibit several important methodological and internal consistency defects:
1. **Unexplained Handling of 24.6% Below-Limit-of-Quantification (BLQ) Samples & Pre-Dose Baseline Accounting Discrepancy:** Out of 952 total plasma samples, 234 (24.6%) were BLQ ($<7\text{ ng/mL}$). The Methods section never states how BLQ observations were handled in `nlmixr2` FOCE-I (e.g., M1 discarding vs. M3 likelihood censoring vs. $\text{LLOQ}/2$ imputation). Furthermore, the sample accounting in Results Line 21 is internally inconsistent: 86 samples were collected *before the first dose* (Day 1, 0 h, when pretomanid concentration must be zero/BLQ) and 151 BLQ samples were collected after treatment completion (Weeks 32 and 72), leaving $234 - 151 = 83$ BLQ samples before/during treatment—which implies either that practically all 83 "on-treatment" BLQ samples were actually the 86 pre-first-dose baseline samples (and $83/866 = 9.58\%$ erroneously divided pre-dose BLQs by post-dose samples!), or that pre-dose samples were excluded from the BLQ count without explanation.
2. **Selective Reporting in the Abstract & Unreported 95% Protein-Binding Simulations:** The Abstract (Line 5) states that *"PK-Pharmacodynamic (PD) simulations indicated that nearly all participants achieved drug exposures exceeding %fT>MIC, supporting the regimen's efficacy across the study population"*, completely omitting the stark finding in Results Line 33 that for the alternative PK-PD index ($f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC} \ge 167$), **PTA dropped to near 0%** at the median baseline MIC of $0.125\text{ mg/L}$! Similarly, Methods Line 69 states that simulations accounted for protein binding of **85%–95%** and doses of **50, 100, 150, and 200 mg**, yet the Results text only reports the 200 mg dose at 85% protein binding.
3. **Inconsistent Trough Concentrations, Inverted Kill Targets, and Missing In-Text Parameter Tables:** The Abstract (Line 5) reports a median trough concentration of **$1,965\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$**, whereas Results (Line 21) reports a median trough concentration of **$1,789\text{ ng/mL}$** ($= 1,789\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$); Results Line 31 inverts the kill magnitudes for the $77\%$ and $48\%$ $fT>\text{MIC}$ targets compared to Methods Line 69; and key population PK parameters ($V/F$, $k_a$, inter-individual variability $\omega_{\text{CL}}, \omega_V$, residual error $\sigma$, and $\eta$-shrinkage) are not stated in the main text because Tables 1–3 and Appendices 1–5 are omitted from the manuscript file.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Pretomanid PK in RR-TB patients is described by a one-compartment model with Typical $\text{CL}/F = 3.10\text{ L/h}$ scaled allometrically by fat-free mass, with no significant clearance differences between BPaL, BPaLM, and BPaLC arms or HIV co-infection on integrase-inhibitor ART: [Well Supported].**  
  The estimated apparent clearance ($3.10\text{ L/h}$, corresponding to steady-state $\text{AUC}_{0-24} = 200\text{ mg} / 3.10\text{ L/h} = 64.5\text{ mg}\cdot\text{h/L}$, matching the reported median $63,733\text{ }\mu\text{g}\cdot\text{h/L}$) is internally consistent and aligns with prior pretomanid PopPK studies, and the lack of drug-drug interaction from moxifloxacin, clofazimine, or dolutegravir-based ART is clinically sound.
* **100% of baseline *M. tuberculosis* isolates ($N=478$) in TB-PRACTECAL had MGIT MICs at or below the provisional critical concentration of $1.0\text{ mg/L}$ (median $0.125\text{ mg/L}$, IQR $0.125$–$0.25\text{ mg/L}$): [Well Supported].**  
  Based on prospective MGIT susceptibility testing of 478 trial isolates across South Africa and Belarus (Results Line 29).
* **At 200 mg daily dosing and 85% protein binding, nearly all patients achieve $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ targets (48% and 77%) at the median MIC ($0.125\text{ mg/L}$), whereas PTA for $f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC} \ge 167$ is achieved only at $\text{MIC} \le 0.032\text{ mg/L}$ and falls to ~0% at the median MIC: [Well Supported].**  
  Mathematically verified: with median $\text{AUC}_{0-24} = 63.73\text{ mg}\cdot\text{h/L}$ and free fraction $f_u = 0.15$, median $f\text{AUC}_{0-24} = 9.56\text{ mg}\cdot\text{h/L}$. Thus median $f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC}$ is $299$ at $\text{MIC} = 0.032\text{ mg/L}$ ($>167$), but falls to $152$ at $0.063\text{ mg/L}$ and $76.5$ at $0.125\text{ mg/L}$ ($\ll 167$). Similarly, for $\text{MIC} = 0.125\text{ mg/L}$, total concentration needed to exceed free MIC is $0.125 / 0.15 = 0.833\text{ mg/L}$ ($833\text{ ng/mL}$), which is well below the 25th percentile trough ($1,126\text{ ng/mL}$), ensuring $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ is near 100%. However, the Abstract's selective presentation of only $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ while omitting the ~0% PTA for $f\text{AUC}/\text{MIC}$ is misleading.
* **Absorption and disposition kinetics are fully resolved by the sparse clinical sampling schedule: [Fragile].**  
  Post-dose sampling during the absorption/peak window was limited to a single sample at 2 h on Day 1 and a single sample at 6.5 h at Week 8 (plus 23 h troughs; Line 57), with no samples collected near pretomanid's $T_{\max}$ of 4.5 h. Estimating $k_a$, comparing transit-compartment absorption models, and estimating inter-individual variability on $V/F$ from such sparse post-dose time points is subject to high parameter uncertainty and $\eta$-shrinkage.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Unspecified BLQ Handling in `nlmixr2` & Contradictory Pre-Dose / On-Treatment BLQ Accounting (Pillars 2, 3 & 4 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Line 21) and Methods (Lines 57–61).
* **Methodological & Arithmetic Flaw:**
  1. **Arithmetic contradiction in BLQ breakdown:** Line 21 states: *"The total number of timed pretomanid plasma samples included in the population PK analysis was 952, 86 samples were collected before the first dose and 866 samples after the first dose. A total of 234 samples were below the limitation of quantification (BLQ), of which 151 samples were collected after treatment completion, rendering 9.5% of the pretomanid concentration BLQ during treatment."*
     Notice that $234 - 151 = 83$, and $83 / 866 = 0.0958$ ($9.6\%$). However, 86 of the 952 samples were collected **before the first dose** (Day 1 at 0 hours), when patients had not yet received pretomanid and concentrations are necessarily BLQ! If those 86 pre-first-dose samples are part of the 234 total BLQ samples, then $234 - 151 - 86 = -3$, meaning virtually **zero** samples during active treatment were BLQ (and 3 pre-first-dose samples had quantifiable pretomanid!), and dividing $83$ by $866$ conflated pre-first-dose 0 h samples with on-treatment BLQ samples. Conversely, if the 86 pre-first-dose samples were not counted in the 234 BLQs, why were pre-first-dose samples included in the 952 PK analysis dataset at all?
  2. **Omitted BLQ statistical method:** When 24.6% (234/952) of observations are BLQ—including 151 washout samples at Weeks 32 and 72 (8 and 48 weeks after the 24-week treatment ended)—the choice of BLQ method in `nlmixr2` (e.g., M1 discarding vs. M3 censored likelihood) strongly affects terminal elimination and two-compartment peripheral washout estimation. The Methods section does not state how BLQ data were treated.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Explicitly state in the Methods how the 86 pre-first-dose (Day 1, 0 h) and 234 BLQ observations were handled in `nlmixr2` (e.g., whether pre-dose samples were excluded, whether washout Week 32/72 BLQs were modeled via the M3 likelihood method or discarded via M1), and correct the on-treatment BLQ percentage in Line 21 after separating pre-first-dose baseline samples from post-dose on-treatment samples.

### Defect 2: Unreported Core PopPK Parameters in Main Text & Sparse Sampling Around $T_{\max}$ (Pillars 3, 4 & 7 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 23–27) and Methods (Lines 57, 61–65).
* **Methodological & Reporting Flaw:** First, while the text references `Table 1`, `Table 2`, `Table 3`, `Figure 1`, `Figure 2`, `Figure 3`, `Figure 4`, and `Appendices 1–5`, none of these tables or appendices are included in the manuscript text, leaving critical PopPK parameters—apparent volume of distribution ($V/F$), absorption rate constant ($k_a$), inter-individual variability ($\text{IIV } \omega_{\text{CL}}, \omega_{V}$), residual error ($\sigma_{\text{prop}}, \sigma_{\text{add}}$), relative standard errors (RSE%), and $\eta$-shrinkage—completely unreported in the narrative text. Second, blood sampling (Line 57) collected only 2 h and 23 h on Day 1, predose, 6.5 h, and 23 h at Week 8, and single samples at Weeks 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, and 72. Because no samples were collected near $T_{\max}$ (~4.5 h), estimating $k_a$ and IIV on $V/F$ is poorly informed by the data and likely exhibits substantial $\eta_V$-shrinkage, which also undermines the empirical Bayes estimate ($\eta$) covariate correlation pre-screen described in Methods Line 63.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Report the point estimates, RSE%, IIV (CV%), and $\eta$-shrinkage for all model parameters ($\text{CL}/F$, $V/F$, $k_a$, and residual error parameters) directly in the Results text (Lines 23–27 & 40) and include Tables 1–3 in the manuscript file. Explicitly acknowledge the sparse sampling around $T_{\max}$ (only 2 h on Day 1 and 6.5 h at Week 8) as a limitation for characterizing absorption ($k_a$) and peak concentration ($C_{\max}$).

### Defect 3: Selective Omission of $f\text{AUC}/\text{MIC}$ Failure in the Abstract & Unreported 50–150 mg and 95% Protein-Binding Simulations (Pillars 1 & 5 — Major)
* **Location:** Abstract (Line 5), Results (Lines 31–33), Discussion (Line 46), and Methods (Line 69).
* **Framing & Completeness Flaw:**
  1. **Abstract omission:** The Abstract states: *"PK-Pharmacodynamic (PD) simulations indicated that nearly all participants achieved drug exposures exceeding %fT>MIC, supporting the regimen's efficacy across the study population."* It omits the co-primary PK-PD finding (Results Line 33) that for the $f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC} \ge 167$ target, PTA at the trial's 200 mg dose and median MIC ($0.125\text{ mg/L}$) **dropped to near 0%** (only achieving $\ge 80\%$ PTA at $\text{MIC} \le 0.032\text{ mg/L}$).
  2. **Unreported simulations promised in Methods:** Methods Line 69 states: *"PTA of pretomanid PK-PD thresholds at doses of 50mg, 100mg, 150mg and the TB-PRACTECAL dose of 200mg daily were evaluated using 2,000 Monte Carlo simulations... accounted for protein binding at 85%-95%."* However, the Results section (Lines 31–33) only reports the 200 mg dose at 85% protein binding and never mentions the PTA outcomes for 50 mg, 100 mg, 150 mg, or 95% protein binding! At 95% protein binding ($f_u = 0.05$, a 3-fold lower free fraction than $f_u = 0.15$), a total plasma concentration of $2,500\text{ ng/mL}$ is required just to reach a free concentration of $0.125\text{ mg/L}$, which exceeds the median trough ($1,789$ or $1,965\text{ ng/mL}$) and would substantially reduce $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ PTA at $\text{MIC} = 0.125$ and $0.250\text{ mg/L}$.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Revise the Abstract to report both the $\%fT > \text{MIC}$ ($\sim 100\%$ PTA at $\text{MIC} \le 0.25\text{ mg/L}$) and $f\text{AUC}_{0-24}/\text{MIC}$ ($\sim 0\%$ PTA at median $\text{MIC} = 0.125\text{ mg/L}$) simulation results, and add the quantitative PTA numbers for the 50–150 mg doses and 95% protein-binding sensitivity analysis to the Results section.

### Defect 4: Trough Concentration Mismatch ($1,965\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$ vs. $1,789\text{ ng/mL}$), Inverted Kill Targets, and Editorial Typos (Pillar 7 — Minor)
* **Location:** Abstract (Line 5), Introduction (Line 10), Results (Lines 21, 31), Discussion (Line 46), and Methods (Lines 59, 69).
* **Technical & Editorial Flaws:**
  1. **Trough concentration mismatch:** Abstract Line 5 reports *"median trough concentration was 1,965 $\mu\text{g/L}$"*, whereas Results Line 21 reports *"the median trough concentration was 1,789 ng/ml"* (note that $1\text{ }\mu\text{g/L} \equiv 1\text{ ng/mL}$). Clarify that $1,789\text{ ng/mL}$ is the empirical observed median across all trough samples whereas $1,965\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$ is the model-predicted steady-state median $C_{\text{min,ss}}$, and standardize units ($\text{mg/L}$ or $\mu\text{g/L}$) across the manuscript.
  2. **Inverted $\log_{10}$ kill targets in Results Line 31:** Line 31 states *"both target of 77% and 48%, associated with 1 and 1.59 log10 kill"*, whereas Methods Line 69 correctly states that **48% is associated with 1-$\log_{10}$ kill and 77% is associated with 1.59-$\log_{10}$ kill**. Similarly, Discussion Line 46 conflates them as *"the maximal efficacy %fT>MIC targets for a 1-log10 kill"*.
  3. **Typographical errors:** `TB-PRACTEAL` (Line 10, missing `C`); `below the limitation of quantification` (Line 21, should be *limit* of quantification); `within with MIC` (Line 46); `an open-source packages` and `R-pakages` and `Intir-individual variability` (Line 59).
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Clarify observed ($1,789\text{ ng/mL}$) vs. model-predicted ($1,965\text{ }\mu\text{g/L}$) median trough concentration, swap `77% and 48%` to `48% and 77%` in Line 31 to match `1 and 1.59 log10 kill`, and fix the spelling typos in Lines 10, 21, 46, and 59. Also deposit the `nlmixr2` model script in a public repository or include it in the supplementary text.
