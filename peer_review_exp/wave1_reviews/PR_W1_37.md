## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript applies the authors' CHROMAS computer-vision pipeline to quantify anisotropic radial deformations of cephalopod skin chromatophores (divided into 36 polar sectors) during spontaneous resting activity in *Euprymna berryi* and *Sepia officinalis*, aiming to infer the single-chromatophore and multi-chromatophore organization of motor units (MUs). Using PCA + FastICA on individual chromatophores and Affinity Propagation clustering across 6,795 independent components (ICs) from 1,829 chromatophores in *S. officinalis*, the authors report an average of ~3.7 ICs per chromatophore forming contiguous "petal-shaped" radial domains, and 754 putative MUs (90% spanning $<14$ chromatophores) that coordinate opposing sectors of adjacent chromatophores into "virtual chromatophores." They validate the existence of anisotropic, petal-like multi-chromatophore expansion via intracellular patch-clamp stimulation of 16 single motor neurons in reduced *E. berryi* preparations.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision / Mixed & Salvageable (6.0 / 10.0).** The combination of sub-chromatophore polar sector tracking and intracellular motor neuron patch-clamp validation in *E. berryi* is creative and biologically insightful, and code/data availability is exemplary. However, a forensic audit of the quantitative text and methods reveals **severe mathematical, physical-scaling, and statistical defects** that require correction before the quantitative results in *S. officinalis* can be trusted:
1. **Mathematically Impossible Summary Statistics & 10-Fold Spatial Scale Contradictions:** In Line 61, the authors report that across 754 clusters, convex hull areas had a **median (50th percentile) of $136,199.10\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$**, yet in the very next sentence claim that **90% of clusters had a convex hull area smaller than $51,513.25\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$**—which is mathematically impossible (the 90th percentile cannot be smaller than the 50th percentile!). Furthermore, the spatial scale of the *S. officinalis* ROI is contradicted across four places in the paper: Figure 2—figure supplement 1 states `0.5 × 0.5 cm` ($5,000 \times 5,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}$); Line 36 states `0.5 by 0.5 mm` ($500 \times 500\text{ }\mu\text{m}$); Methods Line 47 states `400 × 400 µm` ($160,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$ total area); and Line 61 reports cluster convex hull areas up to $915,087.66\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$ (nearly $6\times$ larger than a $400 \times 400\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ field of view!) and a nearest-neighbor distance between chromatophore epicenters of $6.13\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (impossible for cuttlefish chromatophores, which are $30$–$100\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ wide, indicating a 10-fold pixel-to-micron conversion error).
2. **Non-Linear Half-Wave Rectification Before PCA/FastICA & Unpenalized Affinity Propagation:** Setting negative detrended area changes to zero before PCA and FastICA violates the linear mixing assumption $X = AS$ of ICA and guarantees positive split-petal components even under passive isotropic wobble or centroid shift. Moreover, Affinity Propagation automatically determines cluster count ($K=754$ from $6,795$ ICs) based on an unstated `preference` hyperparameter without synthetic ground-truth calibration.
3. **Spatially Naive Null Permutation Model for Second-Order Co-Innervation & Incorrect $t$/$Z$ Calculations:** The null model for pairwise co-occurrence (Line 63 & Methods Line 131) permutes chromatophore IDs uniformly across the entire field of view without preserving spatial proximity, guaranteeing a massive $Z$-score simply because MUs are spatially localized. In addition, the reported Welch's $t$-statistic in Line 47 ($t=0.21$) and $Z$-score in Line 63 ($Z=49.36$) do not match the reported means, SDs, and sample sizes ($t=0.75$ and $Z=47.77$).

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Individual motor neurons can evoke anisotropic, petal-like deformations across a small group of adjacent chromatophores ($4.38 \pm 0.55$ in *E. berryi*): [Well Supported].**  
  Directly demonstrated via intracellular current injection into 16 posterior chromatophore lobe motor neurons across 12 *E. berryi* preparations paired with synchronized video microscopy (Figure 6, Figure 6—video 1).
* **Each *S. officinalis* chromatophore is innervated by an average of $3.71 \pm 1.21$ independent motor neurons with contiguous petal-like domains: [Fragile].**  
  Derived from a single *S. officinalis* individual ($N=1$ animal) by applying PCA `KneeLocator` elbow detection and FastICA to half-wave rectified (positive-only) 36-slice time series. Because `KneeLocator` on any smooth monotonic cumulative variance curve of 36 spatially smoothed circular sectors will robustly find an elbow around 3–5 harmonics, and non-negative clipping + FastICA forces localized unimodal bumps ("petals"), no null simulation (e.g., isotropic expansion with small center-of-mass jitter or passive mechanical coupling) is provided to prove that 3.7 ICs reflect discrete motor axons rather than spatial Fourier harmonics of contour deformation.
* **90% of *S. officinalis* motor units occupy a convex hull area $< 51,513\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$ (~227 µm square), matching the grain size of Mediterranean very fine sand (125–250 µm): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  First, the reported distribution statistics are internally contradictory (median = $136,199\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$, mean = $209,793\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$, yet 90th percentile = $51,513\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$). Second, the pixel-to-micron conversion ($10\text{ px} = 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$, giving NND = $6.13\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ and 1,829 chromatophores inside $0.16$–$0.25\text{ mm}^2$) appears off by a factor of 10 in linear dimension (100 in area), which invalidates the ecological sand-grain size comparison in the Discussion (Line 114).
* **Chromatophore pairs are co-innervated by multiple motor units far more often than expected by chance ($1,073$ vs. $263.7 \pm 16.9, Z = 49.36$): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The permutation null reassigns chromatophore IDs globally across the entire 1,829-chromatophore array without constraining cluster spatial extent or inter-chromatophore distance. Any spatially compact clustering on a 2D lattice will produce orders of magnitude more shared neighbor pairs than a spatially uniform random hypergraph.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Mathematically Impossible Percentiles & 10-Fold Spatial Scale / Unit Conversion Errors (Pillars 4, 6 & 7 — Critical)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 36, 42, 57, 59, 61), Discussion (Line 114), and Methods (ContentOffset 46080, Line 47).
* **Mathematical & Physical Contradictions:**
  1. **Impossible Median vs. 90th Percentile (Line 61):** *"Across 754 clusters, convex hull areas ranged from 25.18 µm² to 915,087.66 µm², with a median of 136,199.10 µm² and a mean of 209,793.70 µm². 90% of clusters had a convex hull area smaller than 51,513.25 µm²."* A distribution cannot have 50% of its values above $136,199\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$ (the median) while simultaneously having 90% of its values below $51,513\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$! Either "smaller than" is inverted, or a unit conversion factor ($0.01\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2/\text{px}^2$) was applied inconsistently to the 90th percentile vs. the median/mean.
  2. **Contradictory ROI Dimensions & Biologically Impossible Chromatophore Density:**
     - Figure 2—figure supplement 1 legend (Line 42) states the ROI is **$0.5 \times 0.5\text{ cm}$** ($5,000 \times 5,000\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 25,000,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$).
     - Results Line 36 states the ROI is **$0.5 \times 0.5\text{ mm}$** ($500 \times 500\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 250,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$).
     - Methods Line 47 (ContentOffset 46080) states the $3968 \times 3968\text{ px}$ ROI is **$400 \times 400\text{ }\mu\text{m}$** ($160,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$, i.e., $10\text{ px} = 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$).
     - Yet in Line 61, cluster convex hull areas reach **$915,087.66\text{ }\mu\text{m}^2$** (which is nearly $6\times$ larger than the entire $400 \times 400\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ camera frame!), and in Line 59 the global nearest-neighbor distance (NND) between chromatophore epicenters is reported as **$6.130 \pm 1.589\text{ }\mu\text{m}$**! In *S. officinalis*, a single contracted chromatophore pigment sacculus is $30$–$80\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ in diameter, and center-to-center NND is $\sim 60$–$100\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ (consistent with 1,829 chromatophores residing in a $4 \times 4\text{ mm}$ or $5 \times 5\text{ mm}$ field of view, where $1\text{ px} \approx 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$, NOT $10\text{ px} = 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$!).
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Audit the physical calibration of the Qioptiq Apo-Rodagon-D $2\times 4/75$ macro lens on the GMAX3265 sensor ($3.2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ physical pixel pitch $\div 2\times$ magnification $= 1.6\text{ }\mu\text{m/px}$ in object space, meaning $3968\text{ px} \approx 6.35\text{ mm}$ or $\sim 0.5\text{ cm}$, exactly matching Figure 2—figure supplement 1!). Correct all linear measurements ($\mu\text{m}$, off by $\sim 10$–$16\times$) and area measurements ($\mu\text{m}^2$, off by $\sim 100$–$250\times$), fix the median vs. 90th percentile contradiction in Line 61, and revise or remove the Mediterranean fine-sand grain size comparison in Line 114.

### Defect 2: Spatially Unconstrained Null Model for Second-Order Co-Occurrence (Pillar 5 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Line 63, Figure 3g) and Methods ("Second-order innervation", ContentOffset 46080, Line 131).
* **Statistical Flaw:** To test whether pairs of chromatophores are co-innervated by $\ge 2$ motor units more often than chance, the authors randomly permute chromatophore IDs across all 754 clusters globally across the entire 1,829-chromatophore population without preserving the spatial locality (nearest-neighbor structure) of clusters. Because real MUs are spatially localized groups of nearby chromatophores (whereas the null model assigns chromatophores uniformly across the entire field of view), the null expectation ($263.72 \pm 16.94$) merely reflects the birthday-paradox collision rate of uniform random subsets of size $\sim 9$ drawn from $N=1,829$. Rejecting a spatially uniform random graph ($Z=47.77$, misreported as $49.36$) does not prove non-random synaptic co-innervation beyond spatial proximity.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Replace the uniform random ID permutation with a **spatially constrained null model** (e.g., randomizing cluster assignments among nearby chromatophores matching the empirical pairwise distance distribution of clusters, or randomly rotating/translating cluster templates on the torus) to test whether second-order pair sharing exceeds what is expected from spatial locality alone.

### Defect 3: Uncalibrated `KneeLocator` PCA + Rectified FastICA + Affinity Propagation Pipeline & Pseudoreplication ($N=1$ Animal) (Pillars 3, 4 & 5 — Major)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 36, 47–54) and Methods (ContentOffset 46080, Lines 71, 112–119).
* **Methodological Flaw:**
  1. **Rectification and `KneeLocator` bias:** Applying `kneed.KneeLocator` to the cumulative explained variance of 36 adjacent polar slices will *always* select an elbow of 3–5 PCs for any smooth contour deformation (because low angular frequencies $k=1, 2$ dominate smooth closed curves), even if a chromatophore is driven by 1 neuron with slight center-of-mass translation or 10 neurons. Furthermore, zeroing out negative detrended values before PCA/FastICA (Line 112) introduces a hard non-linearity that breaks ICA's linear mixing model.
  2. **Unstated Affinity Propagation hyperparameters:** `sklearn.cluster.AffinityPropagation` determines the number of clusters ($754$ MUs from $6,795$ ICs, giving almost universally $\sim 9$ ICs per cluster) entirely as a function of the `preference` and `damping` parameters, which are not reported or validated against synthetic ground-truth simulations.
  3. **Single-animal pseudoreplication ($N=1$):** Although 12 *S. officinalis* and 14 *E. berryi* individuals were filmed during method development, all quantitative *S. officinalis* results (1,829 chromatophores in HF+S and 1,456 in HF) come from a single head-fixed animal, and all *E. berryi* video clustering results come from a single hatchling.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** (1) Validate the PCA-KneeLocator + FastICA + Affinity Propagation pipeline on synthetic surrogate chromatophores driven by a known ground-truth number of motor units ($M_{\text{true}} \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$) plus realistic centroid jitter and passive elastic coupling. (2) Report the exact Affinity Propagation `preference` and `damping` parameters. (3) Replicate the *S. officinalis* summary metrics across at least $N \ge 3$ animals or explicitly state in the Abstract and Results that the *S. officinalis* dataset is from $N=1$ individual.

### Defect 4: Arithmetic Discrepancies in Reported Test Statistics & Formula Typo (Pillar 7 — Minor)
* **Location:** Results (Lines 47, 63) and Methods (ContentOffset 46080, Line 105).
* **Technical Flaws:**
  1. **Line 47:** For $3.715 \pm 1.211$ ($n=1829$) vs. $3.683 \pm 1.224$ ($n=1456$), Welch's $t = 0.032 / \sqrt{1.211^2/1829 + 1.224^2/1456} = 0.75$ ($p = 0.45$, Cohen's $d = +0.026$), whereas the text reports `t=0.21, p=0.83; Cohen's d = -0.03`.
  2. **Line 63:** For observed $= 1073$ vs. null $= 263.72 \pm 16.94$, $Z = (1073 - 263.72)/16.94 = 47.77$, whereas the text reports `Z=49.36`.
  3. **Methods Line 105 (ContentOffset 46080):** Expansion amplitude is written `A exp = r p r start` (missing the minus sign: $A_{\text{exp}} = r_p - r_{\text{start}}$).
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Re-verify and correct the Welch's $t$-statistic in Line 47, the $Z$-score in Line 63, and the minus sign in $A_{\text{exp}} = r_p - r_{\text{start}}$ in Line 105.
