## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript evaluates nickase-based (PE2) versus nuclease-based (PEn) prime editing in zebrafish embryos across substitutions (2 bp in *crbn*, 1 bp in *ror2*) and insertions ranging from 3 bp to 46 bp (*ror2* 3 bp stop codon, *adgrf3b* 3 bp and 12 bp, *smyhc1:gfp* 30 bp c-myc NLS, and *wls* 46 bp *attP* site), alongside comparisons of delivery mode (mRNA vs. RNP), guide architecture (homology-arm pegRNA vs. NHEJ-based springRNA ± abasic spacer), and ssODN HDR controls. The authors demonstrate that while PE2 yields higher precision ratios (precise edits / total edits) due to low indel backgrounds, PEn achieves substantially higher absolute frequencies of precise insertions (up to 27.3% for 3 bp in *adgrf3b*, 8.5% for 30 bp NLS in *smyhc1:gfp*, and 1.4% for 46 bp *attP* in *wls*). They further establish a stable germline-transmitted *ror2* W722X Robinow syndrome model (6/10 F0 founders) and characterize maternal-zygotic larval axial defects and adult craniofacial/barbel phenotypes via micro-CT.

**Editorial Verdict: Strong / Sound (7.4 / 10.0).** The experimental design is thorough, controlled with ssODN HDR benchmarks and off-target amplicon sequencing, and validated through F2 homozygous/maternal-zygotic phenotyping and in vivo protein relocalization. The primary limitations are: (1) an unaddressed copy-number confounding in the *Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104* NLS insertion assay, where the transgene array contains $\ge 4$ tandem copies—meaning that a single in-frame NLS-edited copy drives dominant nuclear GFP accumulation even when the other 3 copies harbor frameshift indels, and amplicon sequencing across a multi-copy tandem array is vulnerable to PCR template-switching chimeras; (2) missing public repository accession numbers (SRA/ENA) for the NextSeq amplicon datasets; and (3) pooling of 10 embryos in initial PCR/cloning assays prior to individual-embryo NGS.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **PE2 provides higher edit-to-indel precision ratios for substitutions and short insertions, whereas PEn achieves higher absolute insertion efficiency (3–46 bp) in zebrafish embryos: [Well Supported].**  
  Demonstrated consistently across *crbn* (PE2 8.4% vs. PEn 4.4% precise substitution; precision score 40.8% vs. 11.4%), *ror2* (PEn/pegRNA 20.5% vs. PE2/pegRNA 11.5% RNP), and *adgrf3b* (3 bp and 12 bp insertions) using deep amplicon sequencing and CRISPResso2 quantification.
* **Incorporating an abasic spacer between the RT template and gRNA scaffold in springRNAs suppresses unintended scaffold incorporation without increasing precise prime editing frequency: [Well Supported].**  
  Directly tested at *ror2* (Figure 3f–h) and replicated at *adgrf3b* (Supplementary Figure 3j–k) across both mRNA and RNP deliveries, showing that blocking RT read-through reduces scaffold by-products but does not redirect repair flux into error-free insertion.
* **PEn-generated *ror2* W722X alleles transmit efficiently through the germline and phenocopy Robinow syndrome craniofacial and axial defects: [Well Supported].**  
  6/10 F0 adults transmitted the precise allele (6.3%–31.3% F1 germline transmission), and F2 MZ mutants and 1-year-old adults exhibit the expected axial shortening, loss of nasal/maxillary barbels, and micro-CT-quantified mandibular aspect ratio changes matching known *ror2* null alleles.
* **Confocal screening of *Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104* embryos distinguishes precise prime edits (nuclear GFP) from imprecise edits/indels (loss of GFP) at the single-cell level: [Overstated].**  
  As the authors later note in Line 60, the *smyhc1:gfp* $i104$ line carries at least 4 transgene copies at the locus. Because nuclear GFP is genetically dominant over null/frameshifted copies (and masks residual unedited copies if the other copies are knocked out by PEn DSBs), a muscle fiber with 1 precise NLS edit and 3 frameshift indels will display purely nuclear GFP and be scored phenotypically as "precise," overestimating cellular editing fidelity.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Multi-Copy Tandem Array Confounding in *Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104* Phenotypic & Amplicon Assays (Pillars 1 & 3 — Major)
* **Location:** Results ("Insertion of nuclear localisation signal into transgenic reporter gene", Lines 58–60).
* **Methodological Flaw:** The cellular phenotyping assay (Figure 5a) is premised on a three-state mapping: unedited = cytosolic GFP, imprecise indel = no GFP (frameshift), and precise 30 bp NLS insertion = nuclear GFP. However, the target locus *Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104* contains $\ge 4$ tandem copies per allele (Line 60). Under nuclease-active PEn, which generates a high rate of frameshift indels (~70–80%) alongside ~8.5% precise edits, a mononuclear slow-twitch muscle fiber that receives 1 precise NLS insertion and 3 frameshifting indels across its 4 tandem copies will express *only* nuclear GFP—making a compound imprecise/precise allele phenotypically indistinguishable from a purely precise edit. Furthermore, in F1 founder embryos (Figure 5e), tandem repeats undergoing simultaneous DSBs are prone to intra-array deletion of intervening copies, and bulk PCR amplification of nearly identical tandem copies over 30+10 cycles produces in vitro recombinant chimeras.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Explicitly qualify the single-cell phenotypic readout in the Results and Discussion to state that nuclear GFP in a multi-copy concatemer reports the presence of *at least one* functional in-frame NLS-tagged copy combined with inactivation/editing of remaining copies, rather than single-copy allele purity. Quantify copy-number loss (intra-array deletion between Cas9 cut sites) in the F1 *smyhc1:gfp* founders via qPCR or droplet digital PCR relative to a single-copy reference gene.

### Defect 2: Asymmetric Guide Comparison at the 46 bp *wls* Locus (Pillar 5 — Moderate)
* **Location:** Results, Line 62; Discussion, Line 81.
* **Methodological Flaw:** After demonstrating at *ror2* and *adgrf3b* that homology-arm `pegRNA` substantially outperforms `springRNA` in both efficiency (e.g., 26.1% vs. 2.9% at *adgrf3b* 3 bp; 8.3% vs. 0.9% at 12 bp) and precision score, the 46 bp *attP* insertion at the *wls* locus was tested *only* with `PEn + springRNA` (yielding 1.4% precise insertion) and compared against ssODN HDR, without testing `PEn + pegRNA` or `PE2 + pegRNA`. Similarly, the F0 germline transmission experiment at *ror2* (Line 53) used `PEn + springRNA` rather than the superior `PEn + pegRNA` condition, without explaining why the lower-precision guide was chosen to generate the stable line.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Clarify in the text why `springRNA` rather than `pegRNA` was used for the *ror2* germline founder screen and the *wls* 46 bp *attP* insertion (e.g., chemical synthesis length limits for pegRNAs containing both a 46 nt RT template and a homology arm), so readers understand the experimental constraint.

### Defect 3: Missing Public Repository Accession for Raw Amplicon Sequencing Data (Pillar 7 — Moderate)
* **Location:** Methods ("Amplicon sequencing and bioinformatics", Line 113).
* **Reproducibility Flaw:** All quantitative editing efficiencies and precision scores in Figures 1–3, 5, and Supplementary Figures 1, 3, and 6 derive from Illumina NextSeq amplicon sequencing processed with CRISPResso2, but no Data Availability section or NCBI SRA / ENA BioProject accession number is provided for the raw `.fastq` files.
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Deposit all demultiplexed Illumina NextSeq amplicon `.fastq` files in NCBI SRA or ENA and add a `Data Availability` section listing the BioProject accession number.

### Defect 4: Minor Textual & Typographical Errors (Pillar 7 — Minor)
* **Location:** Results, Line 36 ("whereas the there is no significant difference"); Line 48 ("was and 1.9% and 0.7%, respectively").
* **Concrete Fix (HOW):** Correct the grammatical typos in Lines 36 and 48.
