## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study introduces a Repeated Asymptomatic Murine Pneumococcal Colonization (`RAMPC^3`) model in which C57BL/6J mice are sequentially colonized at ~28-day intervals with three genetically distinct *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (`Spn`) strains—WU2 (serotype 3), D39 (serotype 2), and TIGR4 (serotype 4)—in two opposing orders: Cohort A (`WU2 -> D39 -> TIGR4`) and Cohort B (`TIGR4 -> D39 -> WU2`). The authors measure nasopharyngeal bacterial burden by nasal wash CFU, profile serum and NALT IgG/IgA responses against planktonic vs. 24-hour polystyrene-grown biofilm whole-cell lysates (WCL) and a 254-antigen TIGR4 protein microarray, test isogenic `TIGR4ΔcbpA` and `TIGR4ΔspxB` mutants, examine sera from $N=17$ naturally colonized human adults alongside rough (unencapsulated) and capsule-switch mutants, and perform heterologous intratracheal challenge with serotype 6A (`6A-10`).

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision / Mixed (Salvageable with substantial statistical and interpretive restructuring).**  
While the longitudinal `RAMPC^3` animal work ($N=40\text{–}45$ mice per group across three experiments in Fig 1) and the capsule-independence controls using unencapsulated and capsule-switch derivatives (S10–S11 Figs) provide useful empirical observations, the manuscript's primary analytical claims rest on four severe methodological flaws: (1) linear regression models fit across only $K=3$ bacterial strains by artificially pairing independent in vitro crystal violet wells with individual mouse or human sera (Figs 4D, 5F, 5G); (2) a claim of "antigenic imprinting / original antigenic sin" derived from 1D SDS-PAGE band-subtraction tautologies (Fig 2B) that is directly contradicted by the 254-protein microarray showing identical immunodominant antigen rankings (`PhtD`, `LysM`, `PsaA`, `LtgG`, `Subtilase`, `PspA`) across both exposure orders and timepoints (Fig 3B); (3) confounding of the biofilm mutant control (`TIGR4ΔcbpA` deletes a major immunodominant surface antigen and adhesin, while `TIGR4ΔspxB` abolishes biofilms without reducing biofilm-WCL IgG); and (4) non-existent hybrid statistical tests reported in the Methods ("Mann-Whitney log-rank t-test" and "nonparametric one-way or two-way ANOVA with Tukey's pairwise test").

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1: Sequential heterologous `Spn` colonization reduces bacterial burden and accelerates clearance of subsequent colonizing strains (`RAMPC^3` model, Fig 1).** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  CFU trajectories show lower nasopharyngeal carriage during the second (D39) and third (TIGR4 or WU2) exposures compared to primary colonization ($N=40\text{–}45$ mice). However, in Fig 1C, primary colonization at Day 3 (9-week-old mice) is compared directly to third colonization at Day 59 (~17-week-old mice after two prior infections and repeated isoflurane nasal washes) without an age-matched naïve cohort inoculated in parallel at Day 56 to separate adaptive immunity from age-related mucosal maturation and repeated mechanical washing.
* **Claim 2: The first colonizing `Spn` strain imprints the humoral response ("original antigenic sin"), preventing diversification after subsequent heterologous exposures (Figs 2–3).** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  This claim relies on counting "newly detected reactive bands" on 1D Western blots after subtracting molecular-weight bands seen after the first exposure (Fig 2B, lines 34 and 130–133). One-dimensional SDS-PAGE cannot distinguish whether a band of similar molecular weight across WU2, D39, and TIGR4 represents a strain-specific imprinted epitope or a conserved housekeeping/surface protein. Moreover, running a one-way ANOVA comparing the baseline total band count at Event 1 against marginal new band increments ($\Delta$) at Events 2 and 3 (Fig 2B) tests a mathematical tautology. Crucially, the 254-protein microarray (Fig 3B, S4B–C) refutes strain-order imprinting: both Cohort A (`WU2` first) and Cohort B (`TIGR4` first) recognize the exact same top six conserved proteins (`PhtD`, `LysM`, `PsaA`, `LtgG`, `SP_0641a`, `PspA`) at comparable intensities after the first and third colonizations.
* **Claim 3: Serum IgG and IgA reactivity in mice and humans correlates linearly with a strain's biofilm-forming capacity (Figs 4D, 4F, 5F, 5G).** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  The linear regressions in Figs 4D, 5F, and 5G contain only $K=3$ bacterial strains on the predictor axis (WU2, D39, TIGR4, or three clinical isolates). Plotting $N=10\text{–}17$ points per strain by arbitrarily pairing independent in vitro crystal violet wells with individual mouse or human sera commits severe pseudoreplication (true strain-level degrees of freedom $df = K - 2 = 1$). Furthermore, comparing 24-hour stationary polystyrene plate cultures ("biofilm WCL") against early-exponential cultures ($\text{OD}_{621} = 0.3\text{–}0.5$, "planktonic WCL") confounds stationary-phase/autolytic antigen accumulation with biofilm-specific gene expression, and the `TIGR4ΔspxB` biofilm-null mutant retains wild-type IgG reactivity (Fig 4F).
* **Claim 4: Adult human serum reactivity to `Spn` biofilm lysates is independent of capsular polysaccharide (Fig 5, S10–S11 Figs).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Testing the $N=17$ adult human sera against unencapsulated rough derivatives of WU2, D39, and TIGR4 (S10 Fig) as well as TIGR4 isogenic capsule-switch mutants expressing serotypes 6D and 19A (S11 Fig) demonstrates that elevated IgG binding to 24-hour biofilm WCL persists in the absence of homologous capsule.
* **Claim 5: Prior repeated colonization or wild-type biofilm formation confers partial systemic protection against heterologous serotype 6A (`6A-10`) pneumonia and bacteremia (Fig 6, S13 Fig).** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Triple-colonized Cohort A and B mice show improved survival over naïve controls after intratracheal `6A-10` challenge ($N=12\text{–}15$, Fig 6B). However, attributing the lower bacteremia in WT-primed vs. `TIGR4ΔcbpA`- and `TIGR4ΔspxB`-primed mice (S13 Fig) specifically to "the biofilm-associated host response" is confounded because CbpA (PspC) is itself a major protective cross-reactive protein antigen and SpxB deletion pleiotropically alters bacterial physiology and innate immune sensing.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Pseudoreplication and Artificial Variable Pairing in Cross-Strain Linear Regressions (Figs 4D, 5F, 5G)
* **Location:** Results lines 57–60 (Fig 4D) and lines 67–70 (Figs 5F, 5G).
* **Mathematical/Statistical Flaw:** In Fig 4D, the $x$-axis is in vitro biofilm biomass measured by crystal violet $\text{OD}_{595}$ across $K=3$ laboratory strains (WU2, D39, TIGR4 from Fig 4A, where $N=8\text{–}10$ technical/biological culture wells were grown in polystyrene plates), and the $y$-axis is the ratio of mouse serum antibody recognition ($N=10\text{–}12$ mice). In Figs 5F and 5G, in vitro crystal violet $\text{OD}_{595}$ ($N=8\text{–}10$ culture wells across $K=3$ strains) is regressed against ELISA reactivity from $N=17$ human sera. Because an in vitro crystal violet well has no natural 1-to-1 pairing with a specific mouse or human patient, pairing them to plot dozens of points across $K=3$ strains fabricates degrees of freedom and violates observation independence. Worse still, the same mouse or human serum sample is tested across all three strain lysates (repeated measures within subjects). A linear correlation across $K=3$ strains has only $K - 2 = 1$ degree of freedom and cannot support the reported regression lines or $p$-values.

### Defect 2: Tautological Western Blot Band-Subtraction Metric and Contradiction with Microarray Data (Fig 2B vs. Fig 3B)
* **Location:** Results lines 32–40; Methods lines 130–133 ("Detection of immunoglobulin protein band patterns via immunoblot").
* **Methodological Flaw:** To quantify "antigenic imprinting," the authors count reactive bands on 1D SDS-PAGE Western blots after the 1st colonization, and for the 2nd and 3rd colonizations they *exclude* all bands at molecular weights already detected after the 1st colonization, plotting only the marginal count of "newly detected proteins" (Fig 2B).
  1. **Analytical tautology:** Comparing total cumulative bands at Event 1 ($\text{mean} \approx 6\text{–}9$) against marginal newly added bands at Event 2 ($\Delta_2 \approx 0\text{–}2$) and Event 3 ($\Delta_3 \approx 0\text{–}1$) via one-way ANOVA (`**** p <= 0.0001`) tests a baseline stock against an increment, which would be highly significant even if antibodies were strongly boosted against every shared protein during Events 2 and 3.
  2. **1D SDS-PAGE resolution failure:** *S. pneumoniae* core surface proteins and housekeeping enzymes share molecular weights across WU2, D39, and TIGR4; a 1D gel cannot distinguish strain-imprinted epitopes from conserved cross-reactive antigens.
  3. **Direct contradiction with protein microarray:** On the 254-protein TIGR4 microarray (Fig 3B, S4B), both Cohort A (`WU2` first) and Cohort B (`TIGR4` first) exhibit the exact same hierarchy of top targets (`PhtD > LysM > PsaA / LtgG / Subtilase / PspA`). Because WU2 forms almost no biofilm (Fig 4A) yet imprints the exact same top microarray antigens as TIGR4 when given first, the response reflects immunodominance of conserved core proteins rather than strain-specific antigenic sin.

### Defect 3: Non-Existent Hybrid Statistical Tests and Unjustified Outlier Removal
* **Location:** Methods line 175 ("Statistical analysis") and Figure legends 1, 2, 4, 5, 6.
* **Statistical Flaw:**
  1. Line 175 states: *"For comparisons of two groups, the nonparametric two-tailed Mann-Whitney log-rank t -test was used... For all multiple comparisons, a nonparametric one-way or two-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) with Tukey’s pairwise test was used."* These test names are mutually contradictory hybrids of distinct parametric, nonparametric, and survival tests (Mann-Whitney $U$ test vs. log-rank Mantel-Cox test vs. Student's $t$-test; Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-hoc vs. parametric ANOVA with Tukey's HSD).
  2. In Fig 4B–C and Fig 5A–D, each mouse or human serum sample is assayed against all three bacterial strain lysates (`WU2`, `D39`, `TIGR4`), which is a **repeated-measures (within-subject)** design, yet the authors apply independent-sample tests without pairing by donor serum.
  3. In Fig 4F, the legend notes *"Outliers were removed using the ROUT method"* without stating the false discovery rate parameter $Q$, the number of excluded animals per group, or whether the comparison (`TIGR4` vs. `TIGR4ΔcbpA`, marked `* p <= 0.0332`) remains significant when all colonized mice are retained.

### Defect 4: Confounded Biofilm-Deficient Genetic Controls (`TIGR4ΔcbpA` and `TIGR4ΔspxB`) and Growth-Phase Confounding
* **Location:** Results lines 62–63 (Fig 4E–F) and Methods lines 112–117.
* **Biological/Experimental Confounding:**
  1. **CbpA is a major immunodominant antigen:** `cbpA` (`pspC`) encodes Choline-binding protein A, one of the most abundant immunodominant surface proteins in TIGR4. Colonizing mice with `TIGR4ΔcbpA` removes CbpA antibodies from the resulting sera; when those sera are tested by ELISA against wild-type TIGR4 biofilm WCL (where CbpA is abundant), lower ELISA reactivity is expected purely from the absence of anti-CbpA antibodies, independent of biofilm architecture.
  2. **SpxB refutes the simple biofilm-dependence hypothesis:** `TIGR4ΔspxB` shows an equally severe biofilm defect in vitro (Fig 4E) and in vivo (S8 Fig), yet sera from `TIGR4ΔspxB`-colonized mice react to biofilm WCL just as strongly as WT-colonized sera (Fig 4F).
  3. **Exponential vs. 24-hour stationary batch culture:** "Planktonic" lysates are harvested at early exponential phase ($\text{OD}_{621} = 0.3\text{–}0.5$), whereas "biofilm" lysates are harvested after 24 hours of static incubation in polystyrene plates without media exchange (line 112). In *S. pneumoniae*, 24-hour static batch culture triggers stationary-phase metabolic shifts and LytA-mediated autolysis, releasing cell-wall hydrolases (`LysM`, `LtgG`) and stationary-phase stress proteins. No stationary-phase planktonic or dispersed non-adherent control from the 24-hour culture supernatant was tested to separate biofilm adhesion from culture age.

### Defect 5: Minor Textual and Methodological Discrepancies
* **Protein Array Incubations Truncated / Unclear:** Line 159–160 reads *"incubating the plasmids for 1 6 hours"* (formatting split of "16 hours").
* **BioRender Citation Year:** Fig 1 legend cites `Orihuela, C. (2026) https://BioRender.com/hfo4l9o`, while line 183 cites `Olivella-Gomez et al. 2025` without a full bibliographic entry in a References list.
* **Intratracheal Inoculum Discrepancy:** Fig 6 legend states mice were intratracheally challenged with $10^4\text{ CFU}$ of `6A-10`, whereas Methods line 154 states *"100 μL containing $10^{3\text{–}4}\text{ CFU}$."*
* **Table 1 Annotation Error:** Table 1 lists `Lysozyme M (LysM); host (17 kDa) (SP_0107)`. `SP_0107` is a pneumococcal gene encoding a putative LysM-domain protein in TIGR4, not mammalian "host" lysozyme M.
