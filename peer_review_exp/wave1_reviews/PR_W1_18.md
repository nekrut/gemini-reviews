## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This developmental and quantitative biology manuscript investigates how deterministic cell-cycle state and stochastic cell-to-cell gene expression variation interact to drive symmetry breaking and probabilistic cell fate proportioning (stalk vs. spore) in the social amoeba *Dictyostelium discoideum*. Combining analytical probit/error-function modeling, high-depth Fluidigm C1 scRNA-seq ($n=81$ cells), high-throughput ICELL8 scRNA-seq ($n=620$ cells across WT and $\text{set1}^-$), a genome-wide REMI-seq forward genetic screen for DIF-1 insensitivity, ChIP-seq re-analysis, and live-cell lineage tracking, the authors demonstrate that Set1/COMPASS-dependent H3K4 methylation tunes the transcriptional burst frequency and cell-to-cell variability of lineage-priming genes, acting additively with cell-cycle cues to buffer developmental proportioning against environmental perturbations.

### Primary Claims Audit
1. **Claim 1:** Probabilistic stalk differentiation across the cell cycle cannot be explained by a purely deterministic threshold model of a decaying cell-cycle-associated factor (CCAF) or by cell-cycle-independent noise (CCIF) alone, but is quantitatively captured ($R^2_{\text{adj}} = 0.92$, $\text{AIC} = -56.37$) by a 2-parameter additive stochastic-deterministic probit threshold model: $P_t = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \text{erf}\left[\frac{C_0^* - \beta t}{\sqrt{2}}\right]\right)$. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Mathematical derivation from the telegraph/Bernoulli bursting model and formal AIC/Akaike-weight model selection comparing linear, exponential, quadratic, and cubic CCAF decay as well as Gaussian vs. Gamma noise against time-lapse single-cell tracking data (Figure 1, Appendix 1).
2. **Claim 2:** Growing vegetative *D. discoideum* cells exhibit widespread cell-cycle-independent stochastic expression variation ($1,057$ genes out of $2,073$ hyper-variable genes at $\text{FDR} < 0.05$), and these stochastically expressed genes are significantly enriched for developmental induction, prestalk/prespore specificity, and functional necessity for stalk differentiation in an unbiased genome-wide REMI-seq screen ($244$ DIF-signaling mutants). `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* `Scran::modelGeneCV2` mean-variance trend fitting, conservative AUROC ($>0.65$) filtering of cell-cycle markers, two-tier counter-screened REMI-seq selection (controlling for growth advantage and 8-Br-cAMP competence) with $8/9$ independent single-mutant validations (Figure 3E).
3. **Claim 3:** Variably expressed genes are enriched for H3K4me1 and depleted for H3K4me3 around the TSS, and abolishing H3K4 methylation via $\text{set1}^-$ knockout preferentially upregulates variable genes while **reducing** their single-cell expression variability ($\text{CV}^2$), consistent with Set1 repressing transcriptional burst frequency $p_i$. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Expression-matched stratified permutation analysis of ChIP-seq profiles (10 weighted random draws of $2,024$ non-variable genes across 30 expression bins), bulk RNA-seq, ICELL8 scRNA-seq ($310$ WT vs. $310$ $\text{set1}^-$ cells), and dual-color FACS reporter validation (`gtaU-RFP` and `hspF-2-RFP` normalized to internal `actin15-GFP`).
4. **Claim 4:** Loss of H3K4 di-/tri-methylation ($\text{set1}^-$, $\text{ash2}^-$, and hypomorphic `Set1-FLAG`) or downstream H3K9/14 acetylation ($\text{gcn5}^-$) lowers the threshold of DIF-1 responsiveness, shifts cells toward prestalk (`pstO`/`pstB`) fate throughout the cell cycle independently of cell-cycle progression, and causes catastrophic developmental arrest when combined with cold-shock cell-cycle perturbation ($11.5^\circ\text{C}$). `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Chimeric and clonal slug FACS-RNA-seq, `ecmA`/`ecmB` knock-in GFP dose-response curves, single-cell live tracking in $\text{set1}^-$, genetic epistasis with COMPASS ($\text{ash2}^-$) and HAT ($\text{gcn5}^-$) mutants, and $2 \times 2$ environmental-genetic interaction assays (Figure 8D).

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Confounding of Mean-Induced Poisson/Binomial Scaling with "Increased Burst Frequency" in $\text{CV}^2$ Analysis
In the Results ("H3K4 methylation is required for normal expression of stochastic genes", second part, lines 20–22), the authors derive from their Bernoulli-Gaussian burst model that the mean expression is $\mu_i = p_i b_i$ and the squared coefficient of variation is:
$$\text{CV}^2 = \frac{1 - p_i}{p_i} + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{p_i b_i^2}$$
They then argue that because upregulated Set1-dependent variable genes in $\text{set1}^-$ cells show both an **increase in mean expression** (Figure 5E) and a **decrease in raw $\text{CV}^2$** (Figure 5F), Set1 disruption must act specifically by increasing burst frequency ($p_i$) rather than burst size ($b_i$).
Look closely at the second term of their own equation, $\frac{\varepsilon^2}{p_i b_i^2}$, as well as the universal Poisson measurement noise term $\frac{1}{\mu_i} = \frac{1}{p_i b_i}$ inherent to UMI-based scRNA-seq (ICELL8) and flow cytometry:
1. Even if burst frequency $p_i$ remained completely constant and **only burst size $b_i$ increased**, both $\frac{\varepsilon^2}{p_i b_i^2}$ and the Poisson UMI counting noise $\frac{1}{p_i b_i}$ **decrease monotonically as $b_i$ (and therefore mean expression $\mu_i$) increases**!
2. Furthermore, as the authors themselves showed in Figure 2A and Figure 2—figure supplement 1, raw $\text{CV}^2$ decreases with mean expression across *all* genes in the genome. In Figure 2, they properly corrected for this mean-variance relationship using `Scran::modelGeneCV2` (or the Distance to Median, DM). However, in Figure 5F, they compare **raw unnormalized $\text{CV}^2$** between WT (where these genes have low mean expression) and $\text{set1}^-$ (where these genes were specifically selected because they have $>2$-fold higher mean expression in bulk RNA-seq!).
3. Selection bias + regression to the mean: By defining the gene set as "genes that are hyper-variable in WT (`FDR < 0.05`) AND upregulated $>2$-fold in $\text{set1}^-$", raw $\text{CV}^2$ is guaranteed to drop in $\text{set1}^-$ both because of mean-dependent Poisson/technical noise scaling and because of regression to the mean of genes selected at the upper tail of WT $\text{CV}^2$. To prove that noise decreased *beyond* what is expected from the increase in mean expression, Figure 5F must compare **mean-adjusted residual $\text{CV}^2$ (Distance to Median / `modelGeneCV2` residual)** in WT vs. $\text{set1}^-$.

### 2.2 Algebraic/Typographical Errors in Appendix 1 Equations (S1, S2, S4, and Line 153)
While the conceptual modeling in the main text is sound, Appendix 1 contains several mathematical typesetting errors that do not match the code/curves plotted in the figures:
1. **Equation (S1) (Third part, line 74):** Written as $P_t(\text{deterministic}) = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{(C_0 - R - \beta t)^2}}{C_0 - R - \beta t}\right)$. This is correct for the sign function, though undefined at $t = (C_0 - R)/\beta$.
2. **Equation (S2) (Third part, line 108):** Written as:
   $$P_t = \frac{\theta^{-k}}{\Gamma(x)} \int_R^\infty e^{-x\theta} x^{k-1} dx = \frac{\Gamma\left(k, \frac{C_0 + \beta t}{\theta}\right)}{\Gamma(k)}$$
   First, $\Gamma(x)$ outside the integral uses the dummy integration variable $x$ instead of $k$. Second, for a scale parameter $\theta$, the gamma density has $e^{-x/\theta}$, not $e^{-x\theta}$. Third, the upper incomplete gamma argument has $+ \beta t$ instead of $- \beta t$ (or $R - C_0 + \beta t$), which flips the direction of decay vs. threshold.
3. **Equation (S4) (Third part, line 133):** Written as:
   $$P_t = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{e^{\delta t}}{\sqrt{e^{-2\delta t}}}\right) \text{erf}\left[\frac{\sqrt{e^{-2\delta t}}(A^* - C_0^* e^{\delta t})}{\sqrt{2}}\right]$$
   Since $\sqrt{e^{-2\delta t}} = e^{-\delta t}$, the prefactor $\left(1 - \frac{e^{\delta t}}{e^{-\delta t}}\right) = 1 - e^{2\delta t}$ is negative and diverges exponentially to $-\infty$, and the $+1$ constant term is inside the product with $\text{erf}[\cdot]$ instead of $\frac{1}{2}(1 + \text{erf}[\cdot])$! Clearly, Mathematica simplified $\sqrt{e^{-2\delta t}}$ symbolically during an algebraic manipulation of $(C_0^* - A^* e^{-\delta t})/\sqrt{2}$ and the manual transcription into Equation (S4) scrambled the parentheses.
4. **Missing Equation on Line 153 (Third part):** The text reads: *"We measured the impact of non-random sampling as the relative change in stalk proportioning: ."*—the formula $(P_d - P_u)/P_u$ was omitted before the period. Also on line 153, the text writes $(2 - Q_t)/4$ instead of $(2 - \delta)/4$.

### 2.3 Mean-Centering Normalization of Four Heterogeneous Datasets Prior to 2-Parameter Curve Fitting
In the Methods ("Model fitting to measurements of stalk cell induction", third part, line 21), the authors fit the 2-parameter model ($C_0^*, \beta$) to $N = 23$ data points obtained by pooling 4 experimental groups (`WT G+`, `WT G-`, `gefE- G+`, `gefE- G-` across 6 timepoints, minus 1 outlier) after **adjusting the values in each set so that their mean propensity matched the global mean propensity**. Because both glucose deprivation (`G-`) and `gefE-` knockout shift the baseline threshold $C_0^*$ inside a nonlinear sigmoid/error function $\frac{1}{2}(1 + \text{erf}[(C_0^* - \beta t)/\sqrt{2}])$, shifting probabilities additively by their mean (rather than shifting the latent threshold $C_{0,\text{group}}^*$ on the probit scale) distorts the curvature near the bounds $[0, 1]$ and absorbs degrees of freedom ($3$ group-mean parameters) that are not penalized in the reported $\text{AIC} = -56.37$.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Duplicate / Corrupted Typos in Main Text and Appendix:**
   - Main text line 82: `β β is its rate of decay` (duplicate $\beta$).
   - Second part line 20: `in interval t t.` (duplicate $t$).
   - Third part line 139: `function of t, t^2 and t^4` (should be $t^3$ for a cubic model).
   - Third part line 6: `SAMN07833758 - SAMN0783383` (missing final digit in BioSample accession range).
2. **Cold Shock ($11.5^\circ\text{C}$) Pleiotropy:**
   - In Figures 7A–B and 8D, incubating *D. discoideum* at $11.5^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 hours is used as a specific "cell cycle perturbation." While cold shock arrests cells around mitosis, it also alters membrane fluidity, metabolic flux, and protein folding. Using a pharmacological or genetic cell-cycle perturbation at $22^\circ\text{C}$ (or acknowledging the pleiotropy of $11.5^\circ\text{C}$ cold shock) would strengthen the interpretation of Figure 8D.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Compare Mean-Adjusted Residual Variability (`modelGeneCV2` / Distance to Median) in Figure 5F:**
   - Re-analyze the ICELL8 scRNA-seq dataset ($310$ WT vs. $310$ $\text{set1}^-$ cells) using `Scran::modelGeneCV2` to compute the **mean-corrected residual $\text{CV}^2$ (or Distance to Median, DM)** for the Set1-upregulated variable genes in both genotypes, or fit a Poisson-Beta hierarchical bursting model (`SCALE` / `scModels`) to directly estimate burst frequency $k_{\text{on}}$ and burst size $s$ per gene. This will rigorously separate genuine increases in burst frequency from expected mean-dependent reductions in raw $\text{CV}^2$.
2. **Correct the Equations in Appendix 1:**
   - Fix Equations (S2) and (S4), restore the missing formula $(P_d - P_u)/P_u$ and $(2 - \delta)/4$ on line 153 of Appendix 1, and fix the $t^3$ typo on line 139.
3. **Latent-Scale (Probit) Group Offset in Model Fitting:**
   - In Appendix 1, verify that fitting the stochastic-deterministic model with a group-specific threshold offset $C_{0,g}^*$ on the latent probit scale (rather than additive mean-centering of raw proportions) and penalizing the group parameters in AIC still favors the linear-decay Gaussian stochastic-deterministic model over the exponential decay model.
