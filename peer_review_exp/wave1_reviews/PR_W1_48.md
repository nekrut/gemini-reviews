## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study profiles cellular and transcriptional maturation in the embryonic chicken (*Gallus gallus*) spinal cord from embryonic day 5 to day 10 (HH26–HH36) using 10x Chromium 3' scRNA-seq (34,340 cells across 8 libraries: brachial E5 [`B05`], brachial E7 [`B07`], duplicate hemisected brachial E10 control sides [`B10_1`, `B10_2`], duplicate lumbar E10 [`L10_1`, `L10_2`], and duplicate hemisected brachial E10 sides innervating a retinoic-acid-induced `432234` polydactyl wing [`Poly10_1`, `Poly10_2`]). Using single-cell weighted gene co-expression network analysis (`scWGCNA`), the authors resolve maturation trajectories of motor neurons (`lightgreen` $\to$ `darkred`) and oligodendrocytes (`darkgreen` OPCs $\to$ `tan` myelinating MFOLs), identify novel marker genes (`CACNA1G`, `SFRP5`, `CRTAC1`) for rare cerebrospinal fluid-contacting neurons (`CSF-cNS`, `PKD2L1+`), and characterize the avian-specific lumbar glycogen body (`GBE1+`, `MSX2+`, and a posterior `HOXD3/4/8/9/10` + `HOXA11` code), validating both rare populations in vivo by in situ hybridization (ISH). Finally, while scRNA-seq reveals minimal transcriptional divergence between control and polydactyl brachial segments, confocal quantification of $>191,000$ `FOXP1+` lateral motor column (LMC) motor neurons across E6, E8, and E10 ($n=4\text{–}5$ embryos per stage) demonstrates a spatially restricted loss of rostral LMC motor neurons on the polydactyl side.

**Editorial Verdict: Minor-to-Major Revision (Biologically rich and self-critical developmental study, but missing the final Methods subsections and scRNA-seq repository accession numbers).**  
The biological execution and self-critical interpretation are strong: the authors transparently note that E10 enzymatic dissociation depletes large fragile motor neurons and that manual midline bisection of `B10`/`Poly10` creates floor/roof-plate abundance imbalances, and they overcome the scRNA-seq motor neuron dropout by counting $>191,000$ `FOXP1+` nuclei in situ across three developmental stages. However, the manuscript file terminates abruptly at the end of `## Confocal Imaging` (Line 182), omitting (1) the image segmentation and counting methodology for the $>191,000$ `FOXP1+` nuclei and the statistical tests used in Figs 5J–L, and (2) the Data Availability Statement / GEO or SRA accession numbers for the 8 scRNA-seq libraries. In addition, several bioinformatic caveats (confounding of Cell Ranger v5.0.1 vs. v3.1.0 with developmental time, $N=1$ at E5/E7, `miloR` on $N=2$, and a misstated QC inequality in Line 122) require correction.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1: `scWGCNA` resolves developmental state transitions in motor neurons and oligodendrocytes and identifies novel validated markers (`CACNA1G`, `SFRP5`, `CRTAC1`) for rare `PKD2L1+` CSF-contacting neurons (Figs 1–3).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Supported by consistent co-expression module preservation across `B10int`, `L10int`, and `Poly10int` (63-cell cluster) and direct anatomical verification adjacent to the central canal via fluorescent ISH (Fig 3I, S3E).
* **Claim 2: Wing-innervating (brachial) and leg-innervating (lumbar) spinal cord segments share a conserved neuronal and glial cell-type repertoire, except for the lumbar-specific avian glycogen body characterized by `GBE1`, `MSX2`, and a `HOXD` transcriptional code (Fig 4).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Confirmed by integrated clustering, `miloR` neighborhood enrichment in clusters 21–22, `scWGCNA` modules `magenta` and `brown4`, and chromogenic/fluorescent ISH for `GBE1` and `MSX2` in the lumbar glycogen body (Fig 4D).
* **Claim 3: Replacing anterior forelimb musculature via RA-induced mirror-image polydactyly (`432234`) induces minimal transcriptional reprogramming in spinal cord neurons at E10 (Fig 5A–C, S5D).** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Within non-midline neuronal clusters (`B/Poly10int-5`, `11`, `25`), differential expression is minimal. However, as the authors rightly acknowledge (Line 98), the severe depletion of mature motor neurons during papain dissociation at E10 limits statistical power to detect pool-specific transcriptional shifts within surviving motor neurons.
* **Claim 4: Peripheral muscle duplication/anterior-loss selectively reduces rostral (proximal-innervating) `FOXP1+` LMC motor neuron numbers between E6 and E10 without significantly increasing caudal (autopod-innervating) motor neuron numbers (Fig 5D–L).** `[Well Supported]`  
  Demonstrated by internal paired comparisons (left control vs. right polydactyl side of the same spinal cord) across $n=4\text{–}5$ embryos per stage at E6, E8, and E10 ($>191,000$ counted nuclei), showing divergence specifically in the rostral LMC half at E8 and E10. However, the exact statistical test and image-counting pipeline were truncated from the end of the Methods section.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### Defect 1: Truncated Methods Section Omitting `FOXP1+` Image Quantification, Statistical Testing for Fig 5J–L, and scRNA-Seq Data Availability Accessions (Line 182)
* **Location:** End of Material & Methods (Line 182, `## Confocal Imaging`).
* **Reproducibility & Completeness Defect:**
  1. The manuscript cuts off immediately after *"For FOXP1-positive cell counting, Z-stacks were acquired with 3 μm steps between virtual sections"* (Line 182). It omits how 3D Z-stacks across serial 50-$\mu\text{m}$ cryosections were segmented to count $>191,000$ `FOXP1+` nuclei without Z-slice overcounting, how rostral-caudal positions were normalized across embryos, and what statistical test (e.g., paired $t$-test, Wilcoxon signed-rank test, or linear mixed model) generated the significance calls in Figs 5J–L.
  2. No Data Availability section or public repository accession (GEO / SRA / ArrayExpress) is provided for the 8 newly generated scRNA-seq libraries.

### Defect 2: Mathematically Impossible QC Threshold Formula in Methods (`< 2/3 of maximum detected genes`, Line 122)
* **Location:** Material & Methods line 122 (`## QC and filtering`) vs. Results line 24.
* **Methodological Contradiction:** Line 122 states: *"Cells with a gene to UMI ratio smaller than 15% and **less expressed genes than 2/3 of the maximum number of detected genes** were also not considered for further analysis."* In Line 24, the maximum number of detected genes is reported as $6,894$, the mean is $3,293$, and the minimum is $346$. Two-thirds of $6,894$ is $4,596$ genes; if cells with *fewer* than $4,596$ detected genes had been excluded, the dataset could not contain cells with $346\text{–}4,595$ genes or have a mean of $3,293$. The authors should state the actual upper/lower gene-count thresholds applied in the R script.

### Defect 3: Confounding of Cell Ranger Versions (`v3.1.0` vs. `v5.0.1`) with Developmental Stage and Single-Replicate Timepoints (`B05`, `B07`)
* **Location:** Material & Methods lines 118 and 122.
* **Bioinformatic Design Caveat:**
  1. The E5 (`B05`) and E7 (`B07`) timepoints consist of a single biological library each ($N=1$) and were processed with **Cell Ranger v5.0.1**, whereas all six E10 libraries (`B10`, `L10`, `Poly10`, $N=2$ each) were processed with **Cell Ranger v3.1.0** (Line 122). Cell Ranger v5 altered empty-droplet calling and default alignment/counting behavior compared to v3.1; processing all 8 FASTQ sets with a single unified Cell Ranger version is necessary to avoid technical batch confounding along the developmental time course.
  2. Furthermore, `B10_1` and `B10_2` are contralateral *hemisected* brachial cords from RA-implanted embryos (with needle-cut roof/floor plates), whereas `B05`, `B07`, and `L10_1`/`L10_2` are *intact bilateral* neural tubes from untreated embryos (Line 118).

### Defect 4: Underpowered `miloR` ($N=2$ vs. $N=2$), Pseudoreplicated Cell-Level `MAST` DE Testing, and Unadjusted GO Enrichment ($p < 0.01$)
* **Location:** Results lines 61, 70; Material & Methods lines 127, 152, 160, 165.
* **Statistical Caveats:**
  1. `miloR` uses `edgeR`'s negative-binomial GLM to test neighborhood count differences across biological replicates. Running `miloR` with only $N=2$ vs. $N=2$ replicates (Figs 4B and 5B) provides minimal degrees of freedom for dispersion estimation, and in `B10 vs. Poly10` (Fig 5B–C), technical variance in midline needle bisection created large false-positive neighborhood shifts in roof/floor plate and NPC clusters, which in turn inflated cell-level `MAST` differential expression counts (Fig 5C) because `MAST` treated thousands of individual cells as independent observations without donor random effects.
  2. In `scWGCNA` functional annotation (Lines 152, 160), GO and KEGG enrichment was performed with `limma::goana()` and `kegga()` and filtered at an unadjusted `p.val < 0.01` for `REVIGO` without Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control.
