## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This bioinformatics manuscript presents `SNooPy`, a multi-locus statistical single-nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) caller designed for long-read (Oxford Nanopore) metagenomic datasets. Building upon the author's previously published `HairSplitter` haplotype assembly algorithm, `SNooPy` groups candidate variant columns within sliding windows via pairwise $\chi^2$ correlation tests and complete-linkage hierarchical clustering, validates submatrices of $a$ reads $\times$ $b$ loci using a union-bound binomial/power inequality, and applies three post-hoc rescue heuristics (isolated SNPs, high-noise correlated variants, and iterative multi-allelic masking). `SNooPy` is benchmarked against five diploid/genomic callers (`bcftools`, `Longshot`, `NanoCaller`, `Clair3`, and `DeepVariant`) on three ONT R10.4.1 metagenomic datasets (ZymoBIOMICS mock community *E. coli* contigs, human gut, and soil) and simulated *E. coli* strain mixtures.

### Primary Claims Audit
1. **Claim 1:** `SNooPy` implements a rigorous, parameter-free statistical framework whose only assumption is that sequencing errors occur independently across distinct reads, bounding the probability of observing a false-positive variant pattern of $a$ reads $\times$ $b$ loci in an $n \times m$ pileup matrix by $p \le s^{ab} \binom{n}{a} \binom{m}{b}$. `[Methodologically Flawed]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* As demonstrated mathematically in Section 2.1 below, both the independence assumption (violated by strand-specific systematic ONT basecalling errors and local indel alignment artifacts) and the probability bound itself (which omits the post-selection conditional filtering of the $m$ columns to $>5\%$ allele frequency and uses an uncalibrated heuristic $s = 3\hat{e}$ that can exceed $1.0$ or fail when $b=1$) are mathematically and statistically invalid.
2. **Claim 2:** `SNooPy` outperforms existing statistical and deep-learning SNP callers (`bcftools`, `Longshot`, `NanoCaller`, `Clair3`, and `DeepVariant`) on real and simulated long-read metagenomic datasets. `[Overstated]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* All competing tools were executed with **default diploid parameters** rather than ploidy-agnostic, pooled, or somatic/low-allele-frequency modes (e.g., `bcftools` was not run with `-P` prior adjustments or `lofreq`/`clairS`/`freebayes --pooled-continuous`), and contigs where `DeepVariant` crashed on multi-allelic sites were selectively excluded. Furthermore, no numerical values of precision, recall, or F1-score are reported in any table in the main text.
3. **Claim 3:** Grouping reads that share identical start and end mapping coordinates ($\ge 5$ reads) within half-read-length windows effectively segregates strains carrying structural variations prior to variant calling. `[Methodologically Flawed]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* In shotgun long-read sequencing with heterogeneous read lengths, unclipped reads spanning a window of length $L_{\text{med}}/2$ all share the window boundaries, whereas reads terminating inside the window only share **exact** mapping start/end coordinates if they are clipped at the exact same structural variant breakpoint or are PCR/optical duplicates. Requiring $\ge 5$ reads to share *exact* mapping coordinates discards or fragments ordinary shotgun reads that happen to start or end within the window.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Mathematical Invalidity of the Union-Bound Probability Formula and the $s = 3\hat{e}$ Heuristic
In Materials and Methods (lines 36–42), the authors define $n$ as the number of reads, $m$ as the number of candidate loci (pre-filtered to those where alternative alleles appear in $>5\%$ of reads), $a$ as the number of reads in the pattern, $b$ as the number of loci in the pattern, and $s = 3 \hat{e}$ as three times the measured average divergence between reads and the reference. They then state that under the null hypothesis of independent sequencing errors, the probability $p$ of observing at least one $a \times b$ submatrix of all-0s (non-reference bases) is bounded by:
$$p \le s^{ab} \binom{n}{a} \binom{m}{b}$$
This formulation contains four severe mathematical and statistical flaws:
1. **Violation of Conditioning on Pre-Filtered Loci ($>5\%$ Alt Frequency):** The $m$ columns were not chosen a priori; they were selected *post hoc* from a window of $M_{\text{total}}$ genomic positions (e.g., $M_{\text{total}} = 5,000$ bp in the authors' example on line 42) specifically because each of the $m$ columns **already contained at least $0.05n$ non-reference bases!** Conditioning on the event that each column $j \in \{1,\dots,m\}$ has $\ge 0.05n$ errors drastically inflates the conditional probability that a subset of $a$ reads shares errors across $b$ of those pre-selected columns. Using $m$ instead of $M_{\text{total}}$ while ignoring the conditional truncation $\text{Binomial}(n, \hat{e}) \ge 0.05n$ invalidates the bound.
2. **Conflation of True Strain Divergence with Sequencing Error Rate in $s = 3\hat{e}$:** On line 40, the authors state: *"The error rate is estimated as the divergence between the reads and the reference... we empirically set $s$ as three time the measured error rate."* In a metagenomic sample where abundant strains diverge from the consensus assembly by 2–5% true SNPs plus 2–5% ONT sequencing/indel errors, the measured read-to-reference divergence $\hat{e}$ is the **sum of true biological polymorphism and sequencing error**! If $\hat{e} = 0.08$, then $s = 3 \times 0.08 = 0.24$, and in the "Detection of isolated SNPs" rescue step (line 57)—which models single positions as $\text{Binomial}(c, s)$—**no strain with $<24\%$ abundance can ever be called as an isolated SNP** because its allele frequency is below the null mean $s$! Worse, if a highly divergent strain or bad alignment region has $\hat{e} \ge 0.34$, $s = 3\hat{e} > 1.0$, rendering $s^{ab}$ and $\text{Binomial}(c, s)$ mathematically nonsensical.
3. **Breakdown of Cross-Read Error Independence in ONT Sequencing:** The sole assumption of the model—that sequencing errors occur independently across distinct reads—is famously false in Oxford Nanopore and PacBio sequencing. Systematic context-dependent basecalling errors (e.g., methylation motifs, homopolymers, and $k$-mer transitions) and reference alignment artifacts around small indels occur at the **exact same genomic coordinates across multiple reads** (especially reads on the same strand). Because two systematic error loci within a window will be correlated across reads that share strand or local indel phase, `SNooPy` has no strand-bias test (unlike `Clair3`, `DeepVariant`, or `LoFreq`) and no base-quality weighting (collapsing everything into a binary $0/1$ matrix where all non-reference bases, including deletions/insertions or A/C/G/T mismatches, are treated identically as $0$).
4. **Uncontrolled Multiple Testing in the "Recovery of High-Noise Correlated Variants" Step:** In lines 60–64, after calling initial SNPs, *all* remaining loci in the window are tested for $\chi^2$ correlation with *every* confirmed SNP at a fixed threshold of $p < 10^{-6}$ (and initial clustering uses an unadjusted $\chi^2$ cutoff of $p < 0.05$ across $\binom{m}{2}$ pairs). For a standard $2 \times 2$ contingency table with low counts ($a \approx 5$), Pearson's $\chi^2$ approximation without Yates' continuity correction or Fisher's exact test produces severely inflated Type I error rates (extreme anti-conservatism in sparse cells).

### 2.2 Biased Benchmark Design: Running Diploid Callers Against Pooled/Metagenomic Data Without Pooled Callers
The authors compare `SNooPy` exclusively against five callers run with default **diploid** assumptions (`bcftools`, `Longshot`, `NanoCaller`, `Clair3`, `DeepVariant`), noting that diploid callers naturally fail when strain frequency is $<25\%$ or when $>2$ strains are present. However, the benchmark omits **every single ploidy-agnostic / pooled / somatic / viral / metagenomic variant caller** used by the field for mixed-strain or low-frequency variant calling on long reads—such as `LoFreq`, `FreeBayes` (with `--pooled-continuous` / `--pooled-discrete`), `ClairS` / `Clair3-Trio/Pooled`, `iVar`, or `Medaka` variant tools. Benchmarking a multi-strain caller only against diploid germline callers run with default diploid flags is a straw-man comparison.

### 2.3 Circular Ground-Truth Definition in the Human Gut and Soil Benchmarks
In the Results ("Evaluation metrics", line 101), for the human gut and soil datasets, the authors define the ground truth of ONT variants by checking whether the variant allele is observed in a PacBio HiFi read alignment to the same assembly, and define recall as:
$$\text{Recall} = \frac{\text{HiFi-confirmed SNPs called by tool } X}{\text{Total pool of HiFi-confirmed SNPs called by ANY tested software}}$$
Because `SNooPy` is the only non-diploid caller in the tested pool and calls vastly more low-frequency variants than the five diploid callers, the denominator ("union of HiFi-confirmed SNPs called by any software") is dominated by `SNooPy`'s own calls! Meanwhile, any true variant present in the sample at 5–20% frequency that was missed by `SNooPy` and missed by the diploid callers is **excluded from the denominator** ("intentionally excludes variants observed exclusively in HiFi data"). This union-of-callers pseudo-recall systematically favors the most permissive caller in the pool (`SNooPy`) over conservative callers. Why not call variants directly on the high-accuracy PacBio HiFi alignments (e.g., requiring $\ge 2$ HiFi reads supporting the alternative allele) to establish an independent, caller-agnostic ground truth?

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Collapsing All Alternative Alleles to `0` in Binary Matrix $M_{ij}$:**
   - In line 32, $M_{ij} = 1$ if read $i$ matches the reference at locus $j$, and $0$ if it contains *any* alternative base. In high-error reads, three reads having `A`, `C`, and `G` (or a 1-bp deletion) at a reference `T` site are all coded as $0$ and treated as supporting the *same* variant pattern during correlation clustering! Each alternative nucleotide (`A`, `C`, `G`, `T`) must be tracked distinctly before correlation testing.
2. **Complete Absence of Quantitative Tables in the Main Manuscript:**
   - The main text contains zero tables of precision, recall, F1-score, or runtime/memory numbers, referring only qualitatively to Figures 2 and 3 (e.g., "achieving more than 90% recall", "called 13% fewer variants"). Exact numerical values for Precision, Recall, and F1 across all datasets and coverage tiers must be tabulated in the main text.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Reformulate the Statistical Model and Fix $s = 3\hat{e}$:**
   - Estimate the sequencing error rate $\hat{e}$ robustly (e.g., from the median or truncated mean of low-frequency $<2\%$ singleton mismatches, or directly from base Phred quality scores) so that true high-frequency strain SNPs do not inflate $\hat{e}$ and break the binomial rescue model.
   - Replace the uncorrected $2 \times 2$ $\chi^2$ test on sparse binary counts with **Fisher's exact test** (or a one-sided hypergeometric test on allele-specific matches rather than collapsed $0/1$ mismatches), and add a strand-bias filter (e.g., Fisher's exact test on forward vs. reverse strand support) to eliminate systematic ONT basecalling artifacts.
   - Correct the union-bound multiplicity factor $\binom{M_{\text{total}}}{b}$ to account for the total window size prior to the $>5\%$ frequency filter.
2. **Benchmark Against Ploidy-Agnostic / Pooled Variant Callers:**
   - Include `FreeBayes (--pooled-continuous)`, `LoFreq`, and `ClairS` (or `bcftools` tuned for low-frequency variants) in the real and simulated benchmarks so that `SNooPy` is evaluated against methods actually designed for non-diploid mixtures.
3. **Establish an Independent PacBio HiFi Ground Truth:**
   - Define the ground-truth polymorphic sites in the gut and soil datasets directly from the PacBio HiFi read alignments (e.g., all sites with $\ge 2$ or $\ge 3$ high-quality HiFi reads supporting a non-reference base and frequency $\ge 5\%$), independent of the ONT callers being evaluated, and report complete Precision, Recall, and F1 tables in the main text.
