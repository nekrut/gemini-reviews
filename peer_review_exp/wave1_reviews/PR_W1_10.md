## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript investigates the intercellular transfer and pro-apoptotic function of small intron-derived non-coding RNAs (sinRNAs)—specifically `sinR-D1/D2/D3` (16–18 nt fragments mapping to an intronic enhancer of *Dnaaf1*) and `sinR-T` (24 nt fragment mapping to an intron of *Ttll11*)—in pancreatic $\beta$-cells during early autoimmune type 1 diabetes (T1D). Re-analyzing 5'-ethynyl uridine (EU)-tagged small RNA sequencing data from FACS-sorted $\beta$-cells of `NOD.SCID` mice two days after adoptive transfer of EU-labeled `NOD BDC2.5` $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells, the authors identify `sinR-D1/2/3` and `sinR-T` among transferred small RNAs (`DESeq2`, adjusted $p \le 0.01$). They show by RT-qPCR that `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` increase in whole islets of 8-week-old (insulitic) vs. 4-week-old prediabetic NOD mice (but not in immune-deficient `NOD.SCID` mice or cytokine-treated C57BL/6N islets), are present in activated $\text{CD4}^+\text{CD25}^-$ T cells and their ultracentrifuged extracellular vesicles (EVs), and increase in dispersed mouse and human islet cells incubated with T-cell EVs. Transfecting synthetic `sinR-D3` mimics into dispersed mouse islet and MIN6B1 cells increases cleaved caspase-3+ apoptotic $\beta$-cells without altering insulin content or glucose-stimulated insulin secretion, pulls down Ago2 via 5'-biotinylated RNA affinity capture in MIN6B1 lysates, and alters the abundance of several apoptosis- and cell-cycle-linked proteins (`RBL2`, `OIT3`, `ITGB1BP1`, `SUPT3`, `CA4`, `LZTR1`, `ENDOV`, `CTDSPL2`, `ST3GAL5`) measured by DIA-PASEF proteomics (`PXD065257`).

While the proteomic mass spectrometry dataset (`PXD065257`) and in vivo EU-labeling paradigm are valuable resources, the manuscript suffers from several methodological and statistical flaws:
1. Using a two-tailed one-sample $t$-test against a fixed control value of $1.0$ (`Section Statistical analysis`, line 81) on fold-change normalized qPCR and apoptosis datasets violates normality and homoscedasticity assumptions and underestimates variance in the control condition; moreover, exact sample sizes ($n$), degrees of freedom, and $p$-values are omitted across the Results narrative (lines 85–100).
2. In the whole-islet comparison between 4-week-old and 8-week-old NOD mice (line 88, Fig. 2A), 8-week-old NOD islets are heavily infiltrated by $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{CD8}^+$ T lymphocytes (`insulitis`). Because T lymphocytes express high endogenous levels of `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` (Fig. 3A–B), extracting RNA from un-sorted whole islets at 8 weeks confounds intracellular transfer into $\beta$-cells with the physical presence of infiltrating T cells inside the islet preparation!
3. All functional apoptosis and DIA-PASEF proteomics claims rely solely on supraphysiological Lipofectamine 2000 transfection of synthetic mimics (`sinR-D2/D3`) without any loss-of-function (antisense inhibitor/tough decoy) experiment during T-cell EV treatment to demonstrate that endogenous EV-transferred `sinR-D3` is necessary for EV-induced $\beta$-cell death.

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1: `sinR-D1/2/3` and `sinR-T` are transferred from adoptively transferred EU-labeled BDC2.5 $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells to FACS-sorted $\beta$-cells in vivo and via T-cell EVs in vitro.** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  Detection of EU-biotin-captured `sinR-D1/2/3` and `sinR-T` in sorted $\beta$-cells by small RNA-seq (`padj <= 0.01`) and RT-qPCR (Fig. 1, Fig. S2), combined with increased `sinR-D3` after incubating dispersed mouse and human islets with T-cell EVs (Fig. 3C–D), supports intercellular transfer. However, in the adoptive transfer experiment, it is critical to rule out co-sorting of T-cell doublets or phagocytosed apoptotic T-cell bodies by $\beta$-cells, and in vitro EV incubations lack an RNase/proteinase wash or EV-depleted supernatant control.
* **Claim 2: Upregulation of `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` in whole islets of 8-week-old vs. 4-week-old NOD mice reflects EV transfer into $\beta$-cells rather than cytokine induction or aging.** `[Fragile]`  
  In Fig. 2A (line 88), RNA was extracted from **whole islets** of 8-week-old NOD mice (which have active insulitis, i.e., infiltrating T cells) vs. 4-week-old NOD mice (devoid of insulitis) and 8-week-old `NOD.SCID` mice (which lack T cells). Because activated $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells express high levels of `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` (Fig. 3A–B), any RNA extracted from whole insulitic 8-week NOD islets contains RNA directly from the infiltrating T lymphocytes themselves; without FACS-sorting $\beta$-cells away from $\text{CD45}^+$ leukocytes at 4 and 8 weeks, whole-islet qPCR cannot distinguish T-cell infiltration from $\beta$-cell uptake.
* **Claim 3: `sinR-D3` physically associates with Ago2 in MIN6B1 cells and regulates target proteins (`RBL2`, `OIT3`, `ITGB1BP1`, `SUPT3`, etc.) in a miRNA-like manner.** `[Fragile]`  
  Binding to Ago2 was tested solely by incubating $3\ \mu\text{g}$ of synthetic 5'-biotinylated oligonucleotides with $1\text{ mg}$ of detergent-lysed MIN6B1 cell extract in vitro (lines 64, 96; Fig. 4D). Adding a vast molar excess of naked single-stranded 16–18 nt RNA oligo to a crude cell lysate drives in vitro loading onto free Ago2 (`riscome` artifact) and does not prove that endogenous `sinR-D3` is loaded into Ago2 in intact cells; moreover, a 5'-biotin group blocks the 5'-phosphate binding pocket of the Ago2 MID domain required for canonical miRNA seed positioning. In the DIA-PASEF proteomics experiment (line 98, Fig. 4E), the statistical threshold (raw $p$-value vs. Benjamini-Hochberg FDR) and seed-match enrichment in the 3' UTRs of downregulated proteins are not reported.
* **Claim 4: Transfer of `sinR-D` via T-cell EVs contributes to $\beta$-cell apoptosis and the onset of T1D.** `[Overstated]`  
  While Lipofectamine transfection of synthetic `sinR-D3` mimics increases cleaved caspase-3 staining in dispersed islet cells and MIN6B1 cells (Fig. 4A, Fig. S4), Lipofectamine transfection delivers $10^4\text{–}10^6$-fold higher intracellular oligo copy numbers than physiological EV uptake. No antisense inhibition (antagomir/LNA inhibitor) of `sinR-D` in $\beta$-cells exposed to T-cell EVs or in NOD mice was performed to show that blocking `sinR-D` rescues EV-induced apoptosis.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

1. **Whole-Islet Cellular Composition Confounding in 4-Week vs. 8-Week NOD Mice (Fig. 2A):**  
   In the Results (line 88), the authors measure `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` in whole pancreatic islets isolated from 4-week-old (insulitis-free) and 8-week-old (insulitic) NOD mice, using 4- and 8-week-old `NOD.SCID` islets (which lack T and B cells) as an age control. Because 8-week-old NOD islets contain a substantial fraction of infiltrating $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{CD8}^+$ T cells, and because the authors show in Fig. 3A–B that activated $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells express high endogenous levels of `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T`, an increase in bulk islet `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T` at 8 weeks is the expected compositional consequence of leukocyte infiltration into the islet preparation, not proof of EV transfer into $\beta$-cells.
2. **Invalid One-Sample $t$-Tests Against Fixed Control $= 1$ and Complete Omission of $n$ and $p$-Values in Results:**  
   In Section "Statistical analysis" (line 81), the authors state: *"A two-tailed one-sample t-test was conducted to compare the datasets against a control value set at 1."* Normalizing each replicate treatment value to its paired control ($T_i / C_i$) so that the control group has zero variance ($\text{Control} \equiv 1.0$) and running a one-sample $t$-test against $1.0$ is a classic biostatistical error: ratios $T_i / C_i$ are right-skewed (non-normal), and collapsing the control variance violates the two-sample variance structure when reference gene $\Delta Ct$ or baseline apoptosis varies across batches. Instead, paired two-sample $t$-tests (or mixed-effects models with batch as a random block) must be run on the log-transformed quantities ($\Delta Ct$ or $\log_2$ intensities) prior to setting controls to 1. Furthermore, throughout the entire Results section (lines 85–100), not a single biological replicate count ($n$), mean $\pm$ SD value, $t$/$F$ statistic, or exact $p$-value is reported in the text.
3. **In Vitro Lysate Biotin Pull-Down and 5'-Biotin Interference with the Ago2 MID Domain:**  
   To test Ago2 binding (lines 64, 96), the authors incubated $3\ \mu\text{g}$ (~$500\text{ pmol}$) of 5'-biotinylated synthetic `sinR-D3` or `sinR-T` RNA with $1\text{ mg}$ of MIN6B1 cell lysate for 1 h at room temperature. First, structural studies of mammalian Ago2 show that the 5'-monophosphate of a guide RNA docks into a tight positively charged pocket in the Ago2 MID domain; attaching a bulky 5'-biotin moiety sterically perturbs canonical 5'-end anchoring (which is why standard miRNA pull-downs use 3'-biotinylated duplexes or endogenous Ago2 RIP/CLIP). Second, incubating hundreds of picomoles of single-stranded RNA in a cell lysate tests in vitro affinity for unloaded Ago2, not whether `sinR-D3` is genuinely loaded into RISC in living $\beta$-cells. Given that the authors mention `TANT-seq` datasets in Hepa 1-6 and 293T cells [18] (line 96), performing an endogenous anti-Ago2 RIP followed by RT-qPCR for `sinR-D3` in EV-treated or `sinR-D3`-expressing MIN6B1 cells would be far more physiologically valid.
4. **Unspecified Statistical Thresholds and Missing Multiple-Testing Correction in DIA-PASEF Proteomics:**  
   In the Proteomics Methods (lines 72–77) and Statistical Analysis (line 81), the authors detail the `Spectronaut 19.9` MaxLFQ quantification, log2 transformation, and normal down-shifted imputation (`width = 0.3 SD`, `downshift = 1.8 SD`) using `taram-backend`, but **never state the statistical test or multiple-testing correction (FDR/$q$-value threshold)** used to call differentially expressed proteins across the mouse proteome in Fig. 4E and Supplementary Table 3! If an unadjusted $p < 0.05$ cutoff (from line 81) was applied across >5,000 quantified proteins, hundreds of false positives are expected by chance, and any subset of 9 proteins (`RBL2`, `OIT3`, `ITGB1BP1`, `SUPT3`, `CA4`, `LZTR1`, `ENDOV`, `CTDSPL2`, `ST3GAL5`) can be post-hoc rationalized as "linked to apoptosis or stress."

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

* **Discrepancy in Mouse Strain Nomenclature and Source:** Line 24 lists "Male C57BL/6NRj mice (aged 12–14 weeks) and female NOD.Cg-Prkdc scid/Rj (aged 4 weeks and 8 weeks)," while line 39 refers to "NOD/ShiLtJ (aged 4 and 8 weeks, Janvier)" and line 86 refers to "NOD BDC2.5 mice." The Methods should clearly list all four mouse strains used (C57BL/6NRj, NOD/ShiLtJ, NOD.SCID, and NOD.BDC2.5) in the Animals section (line 24).
* **Gene Name Typo in Discussion:** Line 111 states that "`sinR-T` is derived from `Ttll1`," whereas line 86 states `Ttll11` (Tubulin tyrosine ligase-like family, member 11).
* **Western Blot Secondary Antibody vs. Detection Method Contradiction:** Line 64 states that nitrocellulose membranes were incubated *"for 1h at RT with the secondary antibody **AlexaFluor 555 anti-rabbit** (1:400, Invitrogen, USA). The blots were developed using **ECL substrate** (cat# 34075, Thermo Fisher, USA)."* AlexaFluor 555 is a fluorophore, not horseradish peroxidase (HRP), and does not react with chemiluminescent ECL substrate! Either an HRP-conjugated anti-rabbit secondary antibody was used for ECL, or fluorescence imaging was used for AlexaFluor 555.
* **Small RNA-Seq Accession Missing:** While the proteomics data are deposited in PRIDE (`PXD065257`, line 74), no GEO/SRA accession is provided for the small RNA-seq dataset re-analyzed in line 69 (other than citing ref [17]).

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Replace One-Sample $t$-Tests on Ratios with Paired/Mixed Linear Models and Report Exact $n$ and $p$-Values:**
   * **WHY:** One-sample $t$-tests against a fixed control of $1.0$ ignore control variance and distort Type I error rates, and omitting $n$ and $p$-values prevents quantitative audit.
   * **WHAT:** Re-analyze all RT-qPCR ($\Delta Ct$ values) and immunofluorescence apoptosis counts using paired two-sample $t$-tests or randomized-block mixed models (with islet isolation batch as a random intercept), and report exact $n$, mean $\pm$ SD, and exact $p$-values in the text and figure legends for Figs. 1–4.
   * **HOW:** For multi-group comparisons (`Scrambled` vs. `sinR-D3` vs. `sinR-T`), apply one-way repeated-measures/blocked ANOVA with Dunnett's post-hoc test on the raw percentage of cleaved-caspase-3+ $\text{insulin}^+$ cells rather than fold-change ratios.
2. **State the Exact FDR / Multiple-Testing Threshold for the DIA-PASEF Proteomics Dataset and Test for Seed Enrichment:**
   * **WHY:** With thousands of quantified proteins in `PXD065257`, calling hits at an unadjusted $p < 0.05$ produces hundreds of false positives.
   * **WHAT:** Report the exact `limma`/Moderated $t$-test Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted $q$-values for `RBL2`, `OIT3`, `ITGB1BP1`, `SUPT3`, `CA4`, `LZTR1`, `ENDOV`, `CTDSPL2`, and `ST3GAL5` in Results (line 98) and Methods (line 76).
   * **HOW:** If `sinR-D2/D3` acts via Ago2 in a miRNA-like manner (line 98), test whether the 3' UTRs (or coding sequences) of the downregulated proteins in `PXD065257` are statistically enriched for complementary 6–8 nt seed matches to the conserved 16-nt core sequence (`CTCGGTAGAACCTCCA`, line 86) using Sylamer or Fisher's exact test.
3. **Qualify the Whole-Islet NOD qPCR Interpretation (Fig. 2A) and Overexpression Claims:**
   * **WHY:** Whole islets from 8-week-old NOD mice contain infiltrating T lymphocytes that endogenously express `sinR-D3` and `sinR-T`, and synthetic oligo transfection does not prove necessity of EV-transferred `sinR-D3`.
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Explicitly acknowledge in Results (line 88) and Discussion (line 111) that whole-islet RNA at 8 weeks reflects both $\beta$-cells and infiltrating insulitic lymphocytes (whereas the in vivo EU-tagging experiment in FACS-sorted $\beta$-cells specifically isolates transferred RNA). If an antisense LNA inhibitor experiment blocking `sinR-D3` during T-cell EV co-culture cannot be performed, temper the Abstract (line 7) and Discussion (line 113) to state that exogenous `sinR-D3` overexpression is *sufficient* to induce $\beta$-cell apoptosis in vitro, while its quantitative necessity during EV-mediated $\beta$-cell death in vivo remains to be tested.
4. **Correct Technical Descriptions in Methods:**
   * **WHY:** Listing `AlexaFluor 555` with `ECL substrate` (line 64), `Ttll1` vs. `Ttll11` (line 111), and omitting NOD/ShiLtJ and NOD.BDC2.5 from the Animals section (line 24) hinders reproducibility.
   * **WHAT & HOW:** Specify the exact HRP-conjugated secondary antibody used for ECL western blotting (line 64), harmonize `Ttll11` throughout the text, and list all four mouse strains in the Animals section.
