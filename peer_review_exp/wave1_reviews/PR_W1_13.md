## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript introduces `MitoPerturb-Seq`, a single-cell multiomic platform combining pooled CROP-seq CRISPR/Cas9 screening, TAP-seq guide enrichment, whole-cell 10X Genomics Multiome (scRNA-seq + scATAC-seq), NUMT masking, and custom hybridization-capture mtDNA enrichment to simultaneously quantify nuclear gene perturbations, single-cell transcriptomes, relative mtDNA copy number (CN), and mtDNA heteroplasmy in mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs) carrying the pathogenic `m.5024C>T` ($mt\text{-}Ta$) mutation.

### Primary Claims Audit
1. **Claim 1:** Combining whole-cell 10X Multiome with TAP-seq gRNA enrichment, NUMT blacklisting, and xGen hybridization capture increases single-cell gRNA assignment from 10.2% to 60.1% and elevates mean single-cell mtDNA coverage to $80\times \pm 61\times$ (integrated dataset: $60.6\times$ across 6,551 gRNA-assigned cells), enabling simultaneous readout of relative mtDNA CN and haplotype-phased heteroplasmy across 7 linked SNVs. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Rigorous step-by-step empirical benchmarking (Figure 1, Figures S1–S4), long-read Nanopore haplotype phasing of the 7 linked variants in cis/trans, and in silico binomial sampling simulations establishing read-depth accuracy bounds ($\ge 20$ reads).
2. **Claim 2:** Perturbation of *Tfam*, *Opa1*, and *Polg* significantly depletes single-cell mtDNA CN without altering mean `m.5024C>T` heteroplasmy, while increasing cell-to-cell heteroplasmy variance in a manner largely explained by sampling noise and genetic drift through a reduced mtDNA bottleneck. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Concordant results across individual gRNAs (excluding the non-functional `Polg-6` gRNA), bulk ddPCR validation, and a well-designed 5,000-iteration binomial resampling simulation controlling for reduced sequencing depth in *Tfam* KD cells ($p = 0.070$).
3. **Claim 3:** While *Tfam* and *Polg* knockouts trigger a shared mitochondrial integrated stress response (mtISR) primarily proportional to mtDNA CN depletion, *Opa1* perturbation induces a more severe nuclear transcriptomic response and disproportionate mtRNA reduction even when controlling for single-cell mtDNA CN. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Single-cell covariate-adjusted PCA (regressing out ATAC-derived mtDNA coverage), `Mixscape` Gaussian mixture classification of KO vs. non-perturbed cells, and independent bulk RNA-seq / ddPCR time-course validation.
4. **Claim 4:** *Atf4* directly binds the promoter/TSS of only ~38.8% (126/325) of differentially expressed genes in *Tfam*/*Opa1*/*Polg* KO cells, acting alongside a broader network of 34 shared regulons (including *Ddit3*, *Cebpg*, *Jund*, *Maff*, and *Mafg*). `[Moderately Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* SCENIC regulon inference paired with `DamID-seq` chromatin profiling of Atf4 in heteroplasmic MEFs (IDR < 0.01). The support is moderated by the fact that `DamID-seq` was performed using transient overexpression of `pCAG-mCherry-P2A-i4Dam-ATF4` in unperturbed basal heteroplasmic MEFs rather than in *Tfam*/*Opa1*/*Polg*-depleted cells undergoing active mtISR.
5. **Claim 5:** Severe mtDNA depletion (*Tfam*/*Polg* KO) delays cell-cycle progression uniformly across all phases (preserving phase proportions), whereas high mtDNA heteroplasmy/OXPHOS dysfunction under glucose restriction selectively arrests cells at the G1/S transition; furthermore, mtDNA undergoes continuous "relaxed" replication across all cell-cycle phases. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Concordant `Seurat` and `tricycle` continuous pseudotime analyses in MEFs, validated orthogonally by live-cell `PIP-FUCCI` time-lapse microscopy in `TFAM-KO` HeLa and `DeltaH2.1` cybrid clones, plus FACS-sorted single-cell ddPCR across 6 sub-stages of the cell cycle.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Confounding of ATAC-Seq "Relative mtDNA Copy Number" by Tn5 Accessibility and Tfam Nucleoid Compaction
The manuscript defines relative single-cell mtDNA CN as the average per-cell mtDNA sequencing depth obtained from scATAC-seq reads ( normalized implicitly against cell barcode inclusion). However, scATAC-seq measures **Tn5 transposase accessibility**, not naked DNA copy number. Tfam is the core mitochondrial nucleoid packaging protein that coats and compacts mtDNA into tight nucleoids. When *Tfam* is depleted, residual mtDNA molecules become unpacked and hyper-accessible to Tn5 transposition before being degraded, whereas in *Opa1* or *Polg* perturbations, Tfam-to-mtDNA stoichiometry increases, potentially hyper-compacting the remaining mtDNA. Furthermore, raw per-cell mtDNA coverage in scATAC-seq is confounded by total per-cell library complexity/transposition efficiency unless explicitly normalized by nuclear ATAC read count (e.g., $\text{mtDNA reads} / \text{nuclear reads}$ or percentage of ATAC reads mapping to mtDNA). While the authors validate *Tfam* and *Opa1* CN reduction at the bulk level by ddPCR (Figure S7A), the single-cell regression analyses (e.g., Figure 3C and Figure S6H–K comparing mtRNA vs. ATAC-derived mtDNA CN between *Opa1* and *Polg*) treat ATAC mtDNA coverage as an unbiased linear measure of absolute mtDNA molecules without accounting for differential nucleoid compaction or normalizing to nuclear ATAC fragments.

### 2.2 QC Filtering Threshold (`percent.mt < 10%`) Induces Selection Bias Against High-mtRNA Cells
In the Bioinformatics Methods (second part, line 43), the authors state that scRNA-seq quality control removed cells with *"mitochondrial RNA percentage greater than 10"*. In metabolically active fibroblasts—and especially when assaying perturbations targeting mitochondrial biogenesis (*Ppargc1a*), mitophagy (*Pink1*, *Prkn*, *Atg5*), or fusion/fission (*Opa1*, *Dnm1l*)—a strict `percent.mt < 10%` cutoff systematically censors cells with high baseline mitochondrial content or compensatory mitochondrial transcription, while preferentially retaining mtDNA-depleted (*Tfam*/*Polg*/*Opa1* KO) cells whose `percent.mt` drops toward zero. This asymmetric truncation of the upper tail of the mitochondrial expression distribution can bias differential expression and mask phenotypes for perturbations that increase mitochondrial mass or transcript abundance.

### 2.3 DamID-Seq Overexpression Under Basal Conditions vs. Endogenous Stress State
To test what fraction of *Tfam*/*Opa1*/*Polg* KO DEGs are direct Atf4 targets, the authors transiently transfected unperturbed `m.5024C>T` MEFs with a strong `pCAG` promoter driving `mCherry-P2A-i4Dam-ATF4` (Methods line 108: *"resulting in overexpression of Dam and Atf4 (i.e. not 'targeted'), to override post-transcriptional/translational regulation of Atf4"*). First, GATC adenine methylation by Dam accumulates over 48 hours and has a spatial resolution limited by GATC site density (~300–1000 bp). Second, Atf4 is a bZIP transcription factor whose genomic binding specificity depends critically on heterodimerization partners (e.g., Ddit3/CHOP, C/EBP$\gamma$, JunD) that are only induced during active mtISR (as shown in their own SCENIC analysis in Figure 4A). Overexpressing Atf4 alone in unstressed cells without its stress-induced heterodimer partners may fail to capture cofactor-dependent binding sites active in *Tfam*/*Opa1* KO cells, causing an underestimation of the true fraction of Atf4-regulated DEGs.

### 2.4 Pseudoreplication in Single-Cell Differential Expression (`FindMarkers` Default Wilcoxon Test)
In the Differential Gene Expression methods (second part, line 84), DEGs for perturbation groups and `Mixscape` KO classes were identified using Seurat's `FindMarkers` comparing single cells in each KO group against control cells. Standard cell-level tests in `FindMarkers` treat each cell as an independent sample ($N = \text{cells}$) rather than aggregating counts by guide RNA (`3 gRNAs/gene`) and biological replicate (`2 experiments`) via pseudobulk `DESeq2`/`edgeR` or mixed models (`MAST` with random effect for replicate/gRNA). This inflates statistical power and can yield false-positive DEGs driven by batch or guide-specific off-target effects.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Inconsistent Glucose Concentration in "Low-Glucose" Media Description:**
   - In Materials and Methods (first part, line 95), the authors write: *"For low-glucose medium, DMEM 1 g/L glucose (Gibco, 31885-023) was supplemented with 4.5 g/L glucose, 10% fetal bovine serum and 50 $\mu$g/mL uridine."* Supplementing 1 g/L DMEM with 4.5 g/L glucose yields 5.5 g/L (high glucose)! This is almost certainly a copy-paste error from the standard medium recipe.
2. **Haplotype Summation Assumption Across 7 Linked Variants:**
   - Combining read counts across 7 SNVs (`m.5024C>T`, `m.13715C>T` in cis vs. 5 variants in trans) boosts effective read depth ~7-fold, which is clever. However, if a single ~50–100 bp paired-end ATAC fragment spans two nearby variants (e.g., `m.13614T>C` and `m.13715C>T`, which are only 101 bp apart, or `m.1781T>C` and `m.1866G>A`, which are 85 bp apart), treating their base calls as independent binomial trials double-counts the same physical DNA molecule and underestimates binomial sampling variance.
3. **Missing Data and Code Availability Section:**
   - Although the bioinformatic pipelines are described in exceptional detail with exact package versions, the manuscript lacks a dedicated Data and Code Availability statement providing the GEO/SRA/ArrayExpress accession numbers for the Multiome, bulk RNA-seq, DamID-seq, and Nanopore amplicon datasets, as well as a GitHub/Zenodo link for the custom `UMI-tools`/`bwa` gRNA assignment and binomial simulation scripts.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Normalize Single-Cell ATAC mtDNA Coverage to Nuclear ATAC Depth:**
   - Re-verify all single-cell mtDNA CN analyses (Figures 2J, 3C, 3F, 5E–F, S6H–K) using mtDNA ATAC deduplicated fragments normalized to nuclear ATAC deduplicated fragments per cell ($\text{mtDNA} / \text{nuclear ratio}$) to remove technical variation in per-cell transposition and sequencing depth. Explicitly discuss the potential impact of Tfam nucleoid packaging density on Tn5 accessibility.
2. **Pseudobulk Validation of Single-Cell DEGs:**
   - Perform pseudobulk differential expression aggregating cells by `(experiment_replicate, gRNA_id)` using `edgeR` or `DESeq2` to confirm that the core shared and Opa1-specific DEGs remain significant when accounting for biological replicate and guide-level variance.
3. **Avoid Double-Counting Linked SNVs on Single Paired-End Reads:**
   - For the variant pairs separated by <150 bp (`1781`/`1866` and `13614`/`13715`), collapse read pairs spanning both sites into a single molecule observation before computing combined per-cell heteroplasmy and running the binomial variance simulation.
4. **Clarify Media Recipe and Deposit Data/Code:**
   - Correct the low-glucose media composition description on line 95, acknowledge the limitation of basal-state Atf4 overexpression in `DamID-seq`, and add a complete Data and Code Availability section with public accession numbers and repository links.
