## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript demonstrates that the NCoR corepressor subunit TBLR1 (encoded by *TBL1XR1*) acts as a bivalent scaffold that bridges ANKRD11 (via TBLR1's N-terminal LisH/F-box region binding a 292-aa C-terminal globular domain of ANKRD11) and SETD5 (via TBLR1's C-terminal WDR domain binding an MeCP2-like NCoR interaction domain [NID] motif, `PLKKWKS`, at residues 830–836 of SETD5), forming a mammalian counterpart of the yeast Set3 histone deacetylase complex (SET3C). Using quantitative co-immunoprecipitation mass spectrometry (DDA/DIA), Western blotting, and NanoLuc fragment complementation across 23 single-copy FRT-integrated TBLR1 missense mutants in *TBLR1*-null Flp-In T-REx 293 cells, the authors show that pathogenic N-terminal TBLR1 mutations selectively abolish ANKRD11 binding without disrupting NCoR incorporation or MeCP2 binding, whereas WDR mutations concordantly impair both SETD5 and MeCP2 binding. Reciprocally, five KBG syndrome missense mutations in the C-terminus of ANKRD11 (S2475P, R2512Q, E2522K, R2523W, L2605R) and two patient mutations adjacent to the SETD5 NID (R837C, Y838C)—as well as an engineered R306C-equivalent mutation ($\text{SETD5}^{\text{W834C}}$), but not three gnomAD neutral variants (D826, L827, K835)—disrupt SET3C assembly. In vivo, $\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/+}}$ mice phenocopy *Setd5*-null heterozygotes (reduced body/kidney weight and length, increased brain-to-body weight ratio), while homozygosity ($\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/W834C}}$) abolishes SETD5 binding to NCOR1 in brain without reducing SETD5 protein abundance and causes high-penetrance prenatal lethality (3.4% homozygotes at weaning on a mixed background, $p = 1.24 \times 10^{-8}$; 0/31 on an inbred C57BL/6J background). The authors also test whether halving functional SETD5 ($\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/+}}$) rescues $\text{Mecp2}^{-/y}$ Rett-like lethality and progression in vivo, transparently reporting a negative result. Finally, RNA-seq in $\text{Setd5}$ and $\text{Ankrd11}^{\text{yod/yod}}$ mutant mESCs and reanalysis of K562 genome-wide Perturb-seq data show highly correlated transcriptional changes across SET3C subunit perturbations. Raw proteomics (PXD063846, PXD077916), transcriptomics (E-MTAB-15318, E-MTAB-16803), code (GitHub/Zenodo), and source data are publicly deposited.

This is a thorough, well-controlled mechanistic study that integrates human genetics, quantitative interactomics, negative-control neutral variants from gnomAD, and an informative separation-of-function mouse knock-in allele ($\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C}}$). The main methodological issues are (1) conditioning the Pearson correlation calculations in Fig. 6c ($r=0.85$) and Fig. 6e ($r=0.95$) strictly on the subset of genes reaching $p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ in *both* perturbations, which inflates correlation magnitude; (2) potential nested technical pseudoreplication (`3 clones × 3 outgrowths per clone`) and pooling of two $\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/W834C}}$ clones with one $\text{Setd5}^{\Delta\text{KWK}/\Delta\text{KWK}}$ clone in the primary mESC DESeq2 analysis; (3) adding *Drosophila* S2 cell and ERCC spike-ins to the mESC and cortical RNA-seq samples (Methods Part 2, line 84) without reporting whether spike-in normalization was applied to test the global transcriptional repression hypothesis; and (4) listing only the *Mus musculus* proteome in the MaxQuant/DIA-NN Methods despite running most IP-MS experiments in human 293 cells.

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1: TBLR1 physically bridges ANKRD11 (via its N-terminal domain) and SETD5 (via its C-terminal WDR domain binding the `PLKKWKS` NID motif) to the NCoR/HDAC3 complex, and patient missense mutations in any of the three proteins disrupt this assembly.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Demonstrated across orthogonal assays (endogenous/isogenic FRT-integrated co-IP Westerns, label-free DDA/DIA mass spectrometry, NanoLuc complementation, biotinylated peptide pull-downs, and gnomAD neutral variant controls in Figs. 1–3).
* **Claim 2: Specific disruption of SETD5 incorporation into SET3C via the $\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C}}$ NID mutation leaves SETD5 protein levels intact but causes growth defects in heterozygotes and prenatal lethality in homozygotes.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Brain DIA-MS confirms unchanged total SETD5 abundance ($p = 0.2761$, Fig. 4e) alongside selective loss of SETD5 from NCOR1 complexes ($p_{\text{adj}} = 0.00466$, Fig. 4d), and backcrossing $>6$ generations onto C57BL/6J confirms 100% homozygous prenatal lethality (0/31 at weaning, Supplementary Fig. 4g).
* **Claim 3: Reducing functional SETD5 dose by half ($\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/+}}$) does not modify the phenotypic progression or survival of $\text{Mecp2}^{-/y}$ mice.** `[Well Supported]`  
  Blinded weekly phenotypic scoring and Kaplan-Meier survival analysis ($n = 12\text{–}15$ males per genotype, log-rank $p = 0.488$, Fig. 5b–d) rigorously refute the hypothesis that heterozygous SET3C reduction rescues *Mecp2*-null pathology.
* **Claim 4: Disruption of SETD5 and ANKRD11 causes highly correlated transcriptional changes and preferentially upregulates already highly transcribed genes.** `[Moderately Supported]`  
  The functional convergence of *SETD5*, *ANKRD11*, *NCOR1*, *GPS2*, and *HDAC3* across ~11,000 K562 CRISPRi perturbations (Fig. 6f–g) and mESC mutants is clear. However, reporting Pearson $r = 0.85$ (Fig. 6c) and $r = 0.95$ (Fig. 6e) strictly on double-significant DEG intersections overstates the transcriptome-wide correlation, and the claim that SET3C preferentially restrains highly expressed genes is based on Z-scored K562 scRNA-seq rather than the authors' own *Drosophila*/ERCC spike-in bulk RNA-seq datasets.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 1. Selection-Induced Inflation of Transcriptome Correlations by Conditioning on Double-Significant DEGs (Pillar 4)
* **Defect:** In Fig. 6c ($r = 0.85$) and Fig. 6e ($r = 0.95$), the Pearson correlation between $\log_2$ fold changes (or Z-scores) of *Ankrd11* and *Setd5* perturbations is computed exclusively on *"genes which are differentially expressed in both mutants ($p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$)"* (Part 2, lines 1–4). Conditioning on simultaneous statistical significance in both variables excises the central cloud of genes where one perturbation has a significant effect and the other has an effect near zero, mechanically inflating $|r|$.
* **Actionable Remediation:**
  * **WHY:** Filtering both axes by $p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ creates a post-selection truncation bias in the correlation coefficient.
  * **WHAT & HOW:** Report the Pearson and Spearman correlation coefficients across (i) all expressed genes transcriptome-wide, (ii) the *union* of genes differentially expressed in either mutant ($p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ in *Setd5* OR *Ankrd11*), and (iii) the intersection, both in the main text and in the Fig. 6c/e legends.

### 2. Nested Technical Outgrowth Design (`3 clones × 3 outgrowths`) and Unreported Spike-In Normalization in RNA-seq (Pillar 3 & Pillar 4)
* **Defect:**
  1. *Nested outgrowths:* Methods Part 2 line 84 and the Fig. 6a legend state that mESC RNA-seq was performed on 3 clones per genotype (for *Setd5* mutant: 2 clones of $\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/W834C}}$ and 1 clone of $\text{Setd5}^{\Delta\text{KWK}/\Delta\text{KWK}}$) with *"n = 3 separate outgrowths per clone."* Standard `DESeq2` treats all input columns as independent biological replicates; if the 9 libraries per genotype were not collapsed by clone (`collapseReplicates`) prior to the Wald test, within-clone outgrowth covariance inflates the effective sample size from $N=3$ to $N=9$ and exaggerates DEG counts (11,063 DEGs in $\text{Ankrd11}^{\text{yod/yod}}$).
  2. *Unused spike-ins:* Methods Part 2 line 84 documents that both *Drosophila* S2 cells (5% cell count in ESCs; 6,000 cells/mg in cortex) and ERCC RNA spike-ins were added prior to extraction and library preparation. Yet the manuscript never reports spike-in-normalized differential expression, even when arguing in the Abstract and Results (Part 2, line 6) that SET3C restrains highly transcribed genes (where global transcriptional amplification in mutant cells would be masked by standard DESeq2 median-of-ratios normalization).
* **Actionable Remediation:**
  * **WHY:** Treating 3 outgrowths of the same CRISPR clone as independent biological replicates causes pseudoreplication, and exogenous cell/RNA spike-ins directly test whether SET3C loss causes global upregulation of abundant transcripts.
  * **WHAT & HOW:** Clarify in the Methods whether `collapseReplicates` (or a linear mixed model via `dream`/`limma-voom` with `clone` as a random effect) was used for Fig. 6a–c; if not, report the clone-collapsed DEG counts alongside the outgrowth-level analysis. Additionally, compute `DESeq2` size factors from the *Drosophila* S2 and ERCC spike-in reads to test directly whether $\text{Setd5}$ and $\text{Ankrd11}$ mutant mESCs (and $\text{Setd5}^{\text{W834C/+}}$ cortex) exhibit global upregulation of highly expressed genes.

### 3. Species Database Mismatch in Mass Spectrometry Methods and Minor Editorial Errors (Pillar 7)
* **Defect:**
  1. *MS database species:* Methods Part 2 lines 70–72 state that both MaxQuant and DIA-NN searched raw files exclusively against the *Mus musculus* reference proteome (UniProt July 2017). However, all IP-MS experiments in Figs. 1b, 2c, 2e, and 3c were performed in **human** Flp-In T-REx 293 or HEK293T cells.
  2. *Broken figure reference and typo:* Discussion line 11 (Part 2) cites `(Fig. 5h)` instead of `(Fig. 6h)`, and Discussion line 13 (Part 2) contains the wording typo *"reducing the reduction in SETD5 by ~2-fold"*.
* **Actionable Remediation:**
  * **WHY:** Searching human cell line proteomes against a mouse database (or omitting the human database from the Methods description) compromises proteomics documentation.
  * **WHAT & HOW:** Specify the exact *Homo sapiens* UniProt reference database (and custom tagged construct sequences) used for the HEK293 / Flp-In T-REx 293 IP-MS analyses in the Mass Spectrometry Methods section; correct `(Fig. 5h)` to `(Fig. 6h)` in Discussion line 11; and fix *"reducing the reduction in SETD5"* to *"reducing functional SETD5"* in Discussion line 13.
