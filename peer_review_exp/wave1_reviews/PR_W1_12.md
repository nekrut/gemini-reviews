## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript investigates the clearance mechanism of chromatin-trapped PARP1 following Poly(ADP-Ribose) Polymerase inhibitor (PARPi) treatment. Combining LysoIP, live-cell mCherry-PARP1-GFP imaging, HDX-MS, proximity ligation assays (PLA), and clinical transcriptomic survival analysis (SCAN-B), the authors propose that the selective autophagy receptor TEX264 and the AAA+ ATPase p97/VCP orchestrate the selective nucleophagy of trapped PARP1 into lysosomes, preventing cytotoxic PARP1 aggregation and promoting cell survival.

### Primary Claims Audit
1. **Claim 1:** PARPi treatment induces cytoprotective autophagy specifically in response to PARP1 trapping (talazoparib/niraparib) rather than catalytic inhibition alone (veliparib), and inhibiting autophagy (ATG7/STX17/ATG9A depletion or bafilomycin A1) increases chromatin-trapped PARP1 and PARPi cytotoxicity. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Multi-modal convergence across RNA-seq, re-analysis of two published CRISPR screens, detergent-pre-extracted chromatin IF, and pharmacological/genetic epistasis comparing strong trappers (talazoparib) against weak trappers (veliparib) and trapping-defective PARP1 mutants (`p.43delIM;44F>I` and `del.p.119K120S`).
2. **Claim 2:** Chromatin-trapped PARP1 is exported from the nucleus independently of CRM1/nuclear pore transport (leptomycin B-insensitive, ATR-dependent) and delivered to lysosomes via core autophagy machinery (ATG7, STX17, ATG9A, Beclin-1) and p97/VCP (independent of the canonical RNF4–UFD1 proteasomal axis). `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* LysoIP (TMEM192-3xHA) immunoblotting and live/fixed dual-fluorescent `mCherry-PARP1-GFP` lysosomal quenching assays under genetic and chemical perturbations (CB-5083, MLN7243, ML-792, VE-822, leptomycin B).
3. **Claim 3:** TEX264 directly binds PARP1 via its extreme C-terminus (residues 272–313) and bridges trapped PARP1 to p97 (via its SHP motif) and LC3 (via its LIR motif) to prevent cytotoxic insoluble PARP1 aggregate accumulation and replication-associated DNA damage. `[Well Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Recombinant E. coli purified protein pull-downs, HDX-MS peptide exchange mapping, truncation mutagenesis (`TEX264-272`, `TEX264-Δα-Helix`), point-mutant complementation (`TEX264-SHP*`, `TEX264-LIR*`) in `TEX264-/-` cells, and formic-acid solubilization of SDS-insoluble PARP1 aggregates.
4. **Claim 4:** Depletion of TEX264 or ATG7 re-sensitizes acquired olaparib-resistant `RPE1 TP53-/- BRCA1-/-` cells to talazoparib and olaparib. `[Moderately Supported]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Viability assays in chronically adapted RPE1 cells (Figures 6F–G). However, because the specific resistance mechanism acquired by this RPE1 line (e.g., loss of 53BP1/Shieldin vs. PARP1 downregulation vs. drug efflux) was never characterized, it is unclear whether TEX264 depletion reverses an autophagy-driven resistance mechanism or simply lowers the general apoptotic threshold to residual trapped PARP1.
5. **Claim 5:** Low `TEX264` mRNA expression in homologous recombination-deficient (HRD) triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) patients in the SCAN-B cohort correlates with ~28% improved 10-year overall survival, supporting the clinical relevance of TEX264-mediated nucleophagy of trapped PARP1. `[Fragile]`
   - *Epistemic basis:* Retrospective Kaplan-Meier log-rank analysis of $n=712$ TNBC patients from SCAN-B stratified by an NMF-inferred transcriptional HRD signature (`scanb.4 > 0.25`) and `TEX264` expression quartiles. Crucially, the SCAN-B cohort (2010–2018) consists of standard-of-care chemotherapy-treated patients who **did not receive PARPi therapy**. Attributing improved baseline survival in PARPi-naïve HRD patients to the clearance of PARPi-trapped PARP1 is a logical non-sequitur.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Clinical Cohort Disconnect and Unadjusted Confounding in SCAN-B Survival Analysis
In Figures 6H–I and lines 84 / 143–152 (Materials and Methods), the authors use the SCAN-B TNBC cohort ($n=712$) to claim clinical validation of the TEX264–PARPi trapping axis. First, SCAN-B patients were treated primarily with standard anthracycline/taxane/platinum chemotherapy, not PARP inhibitors; endogenous PARP1 trapping does not occur at pharmacologically relevant levels without PARPi (as the authors themselves show in Figure 2B and Figure 1F, where even catalytic inhibition by veliparib fails to trigger the pathway). Second, HRD status was not determined by genomic scar (HRDetect / GIS) or germline/somatic *BRCA1/2* mutation status, but rather by an unvalidated ad hoc threshold (`scanb.4 signature weight > 0.25`) derived from a 4-component Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF) of a 228-gene transcriptional list. Because transcriptional "HRD" signatures are heavily enriched for cell-cycle and proliferation genes, and `TEX264` expression correlates with ER/Golgi/autophagy housekeepers, stratifying by `TEX264` quartiles without a multivariable Cox proportional hazards model adjusting for age, tumor stage, node status, proliferation index (Ki67/MKI67), and chemotherapy regimen leaves the survival curve highly vulnerable to clinical confounding.

### 2.2 Chemical Trapping Protocol Relies on Co-Treatment with the Alkylating Agent MMS
Throughout the mechanistic LysoIP, chromatin IF, live imaging, PLA, and aggregate isolation experiments (Figures 1G–H, 2B–I, 3A–J, 4H–J, 5A–I, 6C–E), "PARP1 trapping conditions" are defined not as PARPi monotherapy, but as simultaneous co-treatment with **200 nM talazoparib + 0.01% methyl methanesulfonate (MMS)** (lines 40, 45, 49, 85 of Methods). MMS is a potent alkylating agent that induces massive base excision repair intermediates (AP sites and single-strand breaks) and global protein alkylation/unfolding stress. By contrast, the RNA-seq (Figure 1A), clonogenic survival assays (Figures 1D–F, 4A–G, 5J, 6A–B, 6F–G), and DDR foci assays (Figures S7–S8) use talazoparib **without** MMS. Demonstrating lysosomal PARP1 accumulation and SDS-insoluble aggregation only under supraphysiological MMS + talazoparib co-treatment leaves open the question of whether clinical PARPi monotherapy in BRCA-deficient cells generates enough trapped PARP1 to saturate the RNF4–UFD1–proteasome pathway and trigger nuclear envelope rupture and aggregate formation.

### 2.3 Subjective, Unblinded Manual Quantification and Underpowered Replication
1. **Manual Puncta Counting:** In the `mCherry-PARP1-GFP` assay (Methods line 100), the authors state: *"Quantification was performed manually by observing the number of red cytosolic puncta per cell, with only puncta in proximity to the nucleus counted."* Unblinded manual counting of subjective "proximity to the nucleus" when automated CellProfiler pipelines were already used for PLA and IF introduces severe observer bias.
2. **Replication Floor of $n=2$:** In Statistical Analysis (Methods line 156), the authors state: *"All experiments were performed at least 2 times... Statistical tests (Student's t-test, one-way ANOVA, two-way ANOVA) used are indicated."* Performing parametric $t$-tests or ANOVAs on $n=2$ biological replicates (or treating technical replicate wells/cells as independent $N$) violates distributional assumptions and inflates Type I error.
3. **Ratio Normalization in LysoIP:** In Methods line 95, LysoIP PARP1 levels were normalized by dividing by HA levels and then *"divided by one condition to display the PARP1 level as a fold-change."* Setting the control condition variance to zero (all replicates equal to 1.0) and applying standard parametric $t$-tests or ANOVAs across conditions violates homoscedasticity.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Unresolved Placeholder and Typos in Manuscript Text:**
   - Main text line 79: Contains an unedited placeholder `(PARP1-KS) (ref) .` where the citation was never inserted.
   - Main text line 84: Typo `TEX624` instead of `TEX264`.
   - Methods line 58: Character encoding artifact `2□μg/mL blasticidin`.
   - Methods line 95: Truncated construct name `cells expressing TMEM192- were harvested`.
   - Methods line 107: Missing word `cells were once with PBS`.
2. **HDX-MS Back-Exchange and Statistical Thresholds:**
   - While the HDX-MS acquisition protocol is well-described and PRIDE credentials (`PXD071389`) are provided, the Methods do not state the average back-exchange percentage, peptide coverage of TEX264/PARP1, or the specific hybrid significance thresholds ($\Delta \text{HX}$ in Da and $p$-value cutoff) required by community guidelines (Masson et al., *Nature Methods* 2019).
3. **Lack of RNA-seq Accession Numbers:**
   - Unlike the proteomics data (`PXD071389`), the newly generated RNA-seq datasets (WT and `TEX264-/-` HeLa and CAL51 cells treated with talazoparib, Tables S1–S2, S5–S6) have no GEO/SRA/ENA accession number reported anywhere in the manuscript.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Demonstrate LysoIP and Aggregate Clearance Under PARPi Monotherapy in HR-Deficient Cells:**
   - Perform key validation experiments (LysoIP for PARP1 and SDS-insoluble aggregate fractionation) in `BRCA1-/-` or `BRCA2-/-` cells treated with **talazoparib alone** (without 0.01% MMS) at clinically relevant concentrations over 24–48 hours to confirm that endogenous replication-associated trapping engages the TEX264–nucleophagy pathway without acute alkylating damage.
2. **Rigorous Re-Analysis of Clinical SCAN-B Data:**
   - Fit a multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression model in SCAN-B adjusting for age, tumor size/stage, nodal status, proliferation score, and treatment type (specifically testing whether the `TEX264` effect is driven by platinum/anthracycline DNA-crosslinking/TOP2-trapping chemotherapy, which would align with the authors' prior TOP1cc/DPC model rather than PARPi). Explicitly state in the Results and Discussion that SCAN-B patients did not receive PARPi.
3. **Automate Puncta Quantification and Fix Statistical Analysis:**
   - Re-quantify the `mCherry-PARP1-GFP` images using an automated, objective CellProfiler pipeline (identifying nuclear boundaries via DAPI and quantifying cytoplasmic red-only vs. red+green puncta within a fixed $\mu\text{m}$ annulus).
   - Ensure all statistical comparisons have $n \ge 3$ independent biological replicates, and analyze fold-change normalized LysoIP data using linear mixed models on log-transformed unnormalized intensity ratios rather than $t$-tests against a zero-variance control column.
4. **Data Deposition and Text Cleanup:**
   - Deposit the HeLa and CAL51 RNA-seq datasets in NCBI GEO/SRA and provide the accession number; fix the `(ref)` placeholder on line 79 and the typographical errors noted above.
