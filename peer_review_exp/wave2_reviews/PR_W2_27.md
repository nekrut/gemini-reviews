## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study introduces two complementary technologies for engineering allosteric protein switches in vivo: (1) `POGO-PANCE` (Protein-switch On/Off Gene Optimization using Phage-Assisted Non-Continuous Evolution), which alternates positive (`pBAD-gIII`) and negative (`pBAD-gIII-Neg` + tunable basal `gIII`) selection steps synchronized with the absence or presence of an external trigger (blue light); and (2) `RAMPhaGE` (Recombitron-Assisted Multiplexed Phage Gene Evolution), which adapts retron-mediated recombineering (`Eco1` retron + `CspRecT` single-stranded annealing protein + dominant-negative `mutL`) to ssDNA M13 phage to install programmable deep mutational scanning (DMS; 2,820 single amino acid substitutions across `LOV2`) and multi-codon insertion/deletion (InDel) libraries at sensor-effector junctions. Applying these platforms to the bacterial transcription factor `AraC` and a weakly functional `AraC-LOV2` chimera yields arabinose-independent optogenetic switches with ~1,000-fold dark/light dynamic range (validated by bulk fluorometry and single-cell flow cytometry). Short- and long-read sequencing across selection rounds tracks epistatic mutation trajectories (`E144K` + `V223M`, `D501`, `E541`, `A543`) and identifies a recurrent left-linker single-residue deletion plus positively charged/polar substitution (`GS` $\rightarrow$ `K`/`R`/`Q`/`N`) predicted by AlphaFold3 to form a continuous sensor-effector $\alpha$-helix. Raw measurements, plasmid sequences, analysis scripts, and NGS datasets are publicly archived on GitHub (`Niopek-Lab/POGO_PANCE`) and Zenodo (`10.5281/zenodo.15650047`).

**Editorial Verdict: Accept with Minor Revisions (Strong / Sound).**  
The experimental engineering is creative, thoroughly controlled (including reversed-input `P3` evolution controls and RT-inactive / non-targeting retron controls), and quantitatively validated by deep sequencing and single-cell flow cytometry. Several technical and presentation items should be addressed:
1. **Omission of the Methods Section from the Main Manuscript File:** Although the main text repeatedly directs readers to `(see Methods)` (lines 39, 58, 60, 85), the Materials and Methods text is completely absent from the manuscript file. Essential experimental parameters (illumination intensity, phage bottleneck dilution factors, culture durations, flow cytometry gating, and long-read sequencing error filtering) should be included or summarized in the manuscript body.
2. **Characterization of Evolutionary Escape ("Cheater") Recombination Events:** Three of the 12 `POGO-PANCE` evolution pools (one `R2-LOV` pool in `P3` and one `R5-LOV` pool each in `P2` and `P3`) were overtaken by recombination events and excluded (line 58). Because evolutionary escape via domain deletion or `gIII` recombination is a primary operational bottleneck in opposing-selection phage evolution, the exact sequence architecture of these cheater phages should be briefly documented.
3. **Biophysical Caveats on the AlphaFold3 Continuous $\alpha$-Helix Model and `mutL`/`mutS` Terminology Error:** The structural rationale for the left-linker `GS` $\rightarrow$ `K` deletion/substitution relies solely on a static AlphaFold3 model (Fig. 5I) without specifying dark vs. light state or measuring LOV2 thermal reversion kinetics. Additionally, line 79 inaccurately describes `mutL` as *"a dominant-negative variant of the E. coli mismatch repair suppression protein mutS"*.

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (Conventional PANCE with `DP6` evolves potent constitutive, arabinose-independent `AraC` activators `R1`–`R6`; Fig. 2A–G): [Well Supported].**  
  Demonstrated across six independent evolution pools under two arabinose-tapering regimes and validated using a weak-RBS plasmid reporter assay against induced WT `AraC`.
* **Claim 2 (`POGO-PANCE` evolves `R2-LOV` and `R5-LOV` hybrids into potent light-repressible transcription factors with up to ~1,000-fold dynamic range, whereas reversed-input controls (`P3`) fail; Figs. 3–4): [Well Supported].**  
  Confirmed across 28 isolated clones by plate-reader assays, single-cell flow cytometry (~3 orders of magnitude median shift), and longitudinal PacBio/nanopore long-read sequencing across mutagenesis, negative, and positive selection steps.
* **Claim 3 (`RAMPhaGE` enables programmable, unbiased DMS (>40% programmed edit reads, Spearman $r = 0.77$) and up to 5-AA InDel mutagenesis in ssDNA M13 phage, outperforming `DP6`; Fig. 5A–F): [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by inactive-RT and non-targeting retron negative controls, direct head-to-head comparison against `pLlacO1-DP6` mutational bias, and two-step layered mutagenesis (DMS followed by linker InDel library).
* **Claim 4 (Combining `RAMPhaGE` linker InDel libraries with `POGO-PANCE` enriches a single-residue deletion and basic/polar substitution (`GS` $\rightarrow$ `K`) in the left `AraC-LOV2` junction that extends a continuous $\alpha$-helix to enhance allosteric coupling; Fig. 5G–J): [Moderately Supported].**  
  The functional improvement (near-digital switching in 5 enriched linker clones; Fig. 5J) and replicate selection convergence (>40% `GS` $\rightarrow$ `K`; Fig. 5G–H) are strongly supported, while the continuous $\alpha$-helix mechanism rests on an unvalidated static AlphaFold3 prediction (Fig. 5I).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Missing Materials and Methods Section Despite Inline `(see Methods)` Cross-References (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Main text lines 39, 58, 60, 85, and end of manuscript (line 121).
* **Defect:** The manuscript text contains four explicit cross-references to `(see Methods)` for phage bottleneck titers, `POGO-PANCE` passage dilutions, deep sequencing variant calling, and custom barcoded retron oligo design software. However, the manuscript ends after the Discussion and Data Availability statement without a Methods section.
* **Remediation:** Append the complete Materials and Methods section to the manuscript (or explicitly change `(see Methods)` to `(see Supplementary Materials and Methods)` while stating key quantitative parameters—light irradiance in $\text{mW/cm}^2$, passage durations, input/output PFU/mL titers, and statistical replicate definitions $n$ in Figs. 2F, 4B, and 5J—directly in the main text and figure legends).

### 3.2 Incomplete Characterization of Recombination "Cheater" Takeover in `POGO-PANCE` Pools (Pillars 3, 6)
* **Location:** Results (line 58) and Discussion (line 111).
* **Defect:** Line 58 states: *"Of note, a recombination event overtook one R2 pool in condition P3 and one R5 pool in conditions P2 and P3, eliminating these three pools from further consideration (see Discussion )."* However, the Discussion only mentions that mismatched regimes (`P3`) were taken over by "cheater" phages, without explaining why one of the two `R5-LOV` replicate pools under the aligned `P2` regime (which started with two positive selection cycles before negative selection) also suffered recombination takeover, or whether the recombination deleted the `LOV2` insert from the phage genome vs. recombining `gIII` from an accessory plasmid.
* **Remediation:** Briefly describe the exact genetic structure of the recombination events observed in `P2` (`R5-LOV`) and `P3` (`R2-LOV`, `R5-LOV`), and note how starting `P2` with two consecutive positive-selection rounds increases the selective advantage for `LOV2`-deletion cheaters prior to the first negative-selection bottleneck.

### 3.3 Molecular Biology Terminology Error Regarding `mutL` and `mutS` (Pillar 6)
* **Location:** Results ("RAMPhaGE: A highly effective, targeted gene diversification strategy for PANCE", line 79).
* **Defect:** Line 79 defines the third component of the Recombitron operon as: *"and mutL , a dominant-negative variant of the E. coli mismatch repair suppression protein mutS ( Fig. 5A )."* In *E. coli*, `MutL` and `MutS` are two distinct proteins of the methyl-directed mismatch repair (MMR) system (not "mismatch repair suppression" proteins—MMR *repairs* mismatches, and its inactivation *suppresses* repair). The Recombitron uses a dominant-negative mutant of `mutL` (`mutL-E32K`), which is a variant of `MutL`, not `MutS`.
* **Remediation:** Correct line 79 to read: *"and mutL (E32K), a dominant-negative variant of the E. coli mismatch repair protein MutL that transiently suppresses methyl-directed mismatch repair ( Fig. 5A )."*

### 3.4 Static AlphaFold3 Interpretation and Pooled Sanger Sequencing Quantification Caveat (Pillars 4, 6)
* **Location:** Results (lines 81, 104; Figs. 5B, 5I).
* **Defect:**
  1. In line 81 (Fig. 5B), the authors claim *"pooled sanger sequencing of an AraC-LOV PCR amplicon derived from ~10,000 phages revealed precise, 100% editing of the target site"*. Pooled Sanger chromatogram peak heights have a detection limit of ~5–10% for minor alleles and cannot distinguish ~92% from 100% editing (as shown in the subsequent long-read sequencing experiment at $10^{10}\text{ pfu/mL}$ in Fig. S8C, which measured >70% editing).
  2. In line 104 (Fig. 5I), AlphaFold3 is used to predict that the `GS` $\rightarrow$ `K` left-linker edit forms a continuous $\alpha$-helix between `AraC` and `LOV2`. Because AlphaFold3 defaults to predicting the ground/dark-state `LOV2` fold and is known to over-predict continuous $\alpha$-helices across short linkers, this structural model should be framed as a structural hypothesis alongside the possibility that mutations in `LOV2` (`D501`, `E541`, `A543`) also alter flavin photocycle dark-recovery kinetics.
* **Remediation:** Qualify "100% editing" in line 81 as "near-complete editing within the detection limit of pooled Sanger sequencing", and note in the Discussion that evolved `LOV2` pocket mutations (`D501`) may modulate photocycle recovery lifetimes in addition to allosteric coupling.
