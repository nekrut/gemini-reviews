## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript introduces the Repeated Asymptomatic Murine Pneumococcal Colonization (`RAMPC^3`) model to investigate how sequential nasopharyngeal carriage with three heterogeneous *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (*Spn*) strains—serotype 2 (D39), serotype 3 (WU2), and serotype 4 (TIGR4)—shapes humoral immunity and cross-protection against pneumonia. Comparing two exposure orders (Cohort A: `WU2 -> D39 -> TIGR4`; Cohort B: `TIGR4 -> D39 -> WU2`), the authors report that primary colonization produces high nasopharyngeal carriage ($10^4\text{--}10^5\text{ CFU/mL}$) whereas secondary and tertiary exposures exhibit 10- to 100-fold lower bacterial burdens and rapid clearance. Immunoblots and a 254-protein TIGR4 microarray show that serum IgG and IgA profiles change minimally between the first and third colonization events, targeting conserved surface adhesins and cell-wall hydrolases (PhtD, LysM, PsaA, PspA). Using whole-cell lysates (WCL) grown under planktonic versus 24-hour polystyrene biofilm conditions—as well as unencapsulated rough derivatives, sera from mice colonized with biofilm-attenuated TIGR4 mutants ($\Delta psrP$, $\Delta cbpA$, $\Delta spxB$), and sera from 17 naturally colonized Colombian adults—the authors conclude that humoral immunity preferentially recognizes capsule-independent biofilm antigens and that initial colonization causes "antigenic imprinting" ("original antigenic sin"). Finally, triple-colonized mice show improved survival following intratracheal challenge with serotype 6A (`6A-10`).

While distinguishing biofilm- versus planktonic-phase immunogens during pneumococcal carriage addresses an important biological question, the manuscript's primary mechanistic and immunological conclusions are undermined by severe experimental design and biostatistical flaws. Specifically: (1) the causal experiment testing biofilm-deficient mutants (`TIGR4ΔpsrP`, `TIGR4ΔcbpA`, `TIGR4ΔspxB`) used only **$n = 2$ mice per group** and failed to measure nasopharyngeal bacterial burden, confounding biofilm formation with colonization dose; (2) the claim of "antigenic imprinting / original antigenic sin" is confounded by the 10- to 100-fold lower bacterial burden during secondary/tertiary challenges, lacks single-colonization longitudinal controls, and relies on a TIGR4-only core protein array incapable of detecting strain-specific antigens; (3) the Statistical Analysis section describes nonexistent hybrid tests (*"nonparametric two-tailed Mann-Whitney log-rank t-test"* and *"nonparametric two-way ANOVA with Tukey's pairwise test"*) and reports no correlation statistics ($r, p$) anywhere in the text; and (4) group sample sizes ($N$), animal welfare (IACUC), human subjects (IRB) approvals, and raw data depositions are omitted. Major experimental controls and complete statistical re-analysis are required.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Sequential pneumococcal colonization (`RAMPC^3`) results in reduced bacterial burden and accelerated clearance during secondary and tertiary heterologous exposures: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Figure 1B–C and S1 Fig show that primary colonization reaches $10^4\text{--}10^5\text{ CFU/mL}$ whereas second (D39) and third (TIGR4 or WU2) exposures have 10- to 100-fold lower initial burdens and clear within 7 days. However, exact mouse sample sizes ($n$) per cohort and exact statistical test outputs are omitted, and age-matched naive mice challenged at Day 28 and Day 56 alongside the longitudinal cohorts are needed to separate adaptive immunity from age-dependent mucosal maturation between 9 and 18 weeks of age.
* **The first colonizing strain causes "antigenic imprinting" ("original antigenic sin") that restricts antibody diversification during subsequent colonizations: [Overstated].**  
  The observation that few new immunoblot bands or protein array signals appear after the 2nd and 3rd colonizations (Figures 2–3) does not prove immunological imprinting. Because the 2nd and 3rd inocula are cleared rapidly with 10- to 100-fold lower CFU burden (Figure 1B–C), they provide minimal antigenic stimulation. Moreover, the 254-protein microarray was built exclusively from conserved TIGR4 genomic DNA (line 127) and showed identical top hits (PhtD, LysM) in both Cohort A and Cohort B (Figure 3B), which reflects cross-reactive recognition of conserved core antigens rather than strain-specific antigenic sin.
* **Murine and human carriage-induced IgG/IgA preferentially recognize capsule-independent antigens expressed in in vitro biofilms rather than planktonic cultures: [Well Supported].**  
  Across both murine `RAMPC^3` sera (Figure 4B–C) and $n = 17$ naturally colonized human adult sera tested against wild-type, clinical isolate, and unencapsulated rough (R) mutant lysates (Figure 5 and S7 Fig), ELISA reactivity is consistently higher against biofilm WCL than planktonic WCL at matched total protein coating (`2 µg/mL`).
* **In vivo biofilm aggregate formation causally drives the humoral immune response to colonization (tested via `TIGR4ΔpsrP`, `TIGR4ΔcbpA`, and `TIGR4ΔspxB` mutants): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  According to the Methods (line 117), this experiment used only **two mice per group ($n = 2$)** for WT TIGR4 and each mutant (`ΔpsrP`, `ΔcbpA`, `ΔspxB`). A sample size of $n = 2$ cannot achieve statistical significance in any nonparametric two-group test. Furthermore, nasopharyngeal CFU burdens were not measured for the mutants; since $\Delta spxB$ and $\Delta cbpA$ have known colonization defects and lack major surface antigens present in WT TIGR4 WCL, reduced ELISA signal is completely confounded by lower bacterial load and missing target antigens.
* **Triple asymptomatic colonization protects mice against lethal heterologous serotype 6A (`6A-10`) intratracheal pneumonia challenge: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Cohort A and B mice show improved survival and modestly reduced day-2 bacteremia compared to naive controls after `6A-10` challenge (Figure 6B–C), though group sizes ($n$), exact log-rank $\chi^2 / p$-values, and discrimination between humoral vs. Th17-mediated protection are absent.

## 3. Forensic Technical Audit & Constructive Remediation Plan

### Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

#### 3.1. Critically Underpowered ($n = 2$) and Uncontrolled Biofilm-Mutant Experiment
In the Materials & Methods ("Biofilm-deficient mutant colonization and ex vivo biofilm quantification", line 117), the authors state:
> *"C57BL/6J female and male mice aged 8-10 weeks were inoculated intranasally as above with Spn strains TIGR4, TIGR4ΔpsrP, TIGR4ΔcbpA, or TIGR4ΔspxB ($10^6$ CFU/mL), with two mice per group."*

1. **Statistical invalidity of $n = 2$:** With $n = 2$ biological replicates per genotype, the minimum possible two-sided $p$-value in a Mann-Whitney $U$ test is $p = 0.333$. In the Results (line 40, Fig 4E–G), the authors refer to these as a *"mixed panel of TIGR4 mutants"*, suggesting that either the three genetically distinct mutants ($3 \times 2 = 6$ mice) were improperly pooled into a single statistical group against $n = 2$ WT mice, or technical ELISA/microscopy counts from the 2 mice were treated as independent biological replicates.
2. **Unmeasured bacterial burden and antigen-deletion confounding:** The authors did not report nasal wash CFU/mL at days 1, 3, 7, 10, or 14 for `TIGR4ΔpsrP`, `TIGR4ΔcbpA`, or `TIGR4ΔspxB` (only counting crystal-violet-stained diplococcal clump sizes on slides at days 7 and 10). Because `spxB` (pyruvate oxidase) and `cbpA` (choline-binding protein A) are required for robust nasopharyngeal colonization, a 10- to 100-fold reduction in total bacterial load—or the absence of immunodominant CbpA and PsrP proteins in the colonizing mutant strains when sera are subsequently tested against wild-type TIGR4 WCL—directly explains lower IgG titers without invoking biofilm aggregate structure.

#### 3.2. Invalid Statistical Test Specifications and Unquantified Correlations
The Statistical Analysis section (line 132) states:
> *"For comparisons of two groups, the nonparametric two-tailed Mann-Whitney log-rank t-test was used, and the median with a 95% confidence interval or the standard deviation (SD) is shown. For all multiple comparisons, a nonparametric one-way or two-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) with Tukey's pairwise test was used, and the SD is shown."*

* **"Mann-Whitney log-rank t-test" does not exist:** This phrase conflates three distinct statistical procedures: the nonparametric Mann-Whitney $U$ test (for two unpaired continuous/ordinal groups), the log-rank Mantel-Cox test (for censored time-to-event survival data in Figs 6B and 7A), and the parametric Student's $t$-test.
* **"Nonparametric one-way or two-way ANOVA with Tukey's pairwise test" is incoherent:** Tukey's Honestly Significant Difference (HSD) test is a parametric post-hoc procedure that compares group means using pooled variance. The nonparametric one-way ANOVA is the Kruskal-Wallis test (followed by Dunn's post-hoc test on ranks), and standard two-way ANOVA in GraphPad Prism is strictly parametric.
* **Correlations claimed without $r$, $R^2$, or $p$-values on $k = 3\text{--}4$ points:** In Figures 4D, 4G, 5F, and 5G, the text claims positive correlations between in vitro crystal violet biofilm biomass and serum IgG reactivity across strains. However, no Pearson $r$, Spearman $\rho$, $R^2$, or $p$-values are reported in the text, and computing a linear correlation across only 3 laboratory strains (WU2, D39, TIGR4; $df = 1$) or 4 clinical isolates ($df = 2$) is statistically underpowered and driven by a single low-biofilm serotype 3 point (WU2).

#### 3.3. Confounded Interpretation of "Antigenic Imprinting" and Protein Array Ascertainment Bias
The claim that primary colonization establishes "antigenic imprinting" / "original antigenic sin" (lines 5, 31, 74) rests on two confounded observations:
1. **Immunoblot band counts (Fig 2A–B):** Cohort A (`WU2 -> D39 -> TIGR4`) recognized up to 20 bands whereas Cohort B (`TIGR4 -> D39 -> WU2`) recognized up to 9 bands, with few new bands added after the 1st colonization. However, without single-strain colonization controls bled at the same timepoints (Days 21, 49, 79), the persistence of bands from Day 21 to Day 79 simply reflects the normal half-life of serum IgG (~21–28 days) and long-lived plasma cells from the primary infection, while the lack of new bands after the 2nd and 3rd challenges is explained by rapid mucosal clearance and 10- to 100-fold lower bacterial burden (Fig 1B–C).
2. **Protein microarray design (Fig 3A–B):** The 254-protein array was cloned exclusively from `TIGR4` genomic DNA and restricted to proteins highly conserved across >600 *Spn* strains (line 127). Because strain-variable antigens unique to WU2 or D39 are absent from the array, the array cannot test whether Cohort A vs. Cohort B imprinted strain-specific epitopes; indeed, both cohorts produced virtually identical array profiles dominated by conserved PhtD and LysM (Fig 3B).

### Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Complete Omission of Animal Welfare (IACUC) and Human Subjects (IRB) Ethics Statements:** The manuscript reports invasive murine procedures (retro-orbital bleeding, isoflurane sedation, intratracheal pneumonia challenge with mortality endpoints) and human clinical sera ("human Colombia patient cohort", $n = 17$ adults), but contains no ethics section, IACUC protocol number, humane endpoint criteria, or IRB approval / informed consent documentation.
2. **Missing Sample Sizes ($n$) Across Murine Experiments:** The number of mice per group ($n$) is never stated in the Results or Methods for Cohort A, Cohort B, the single-strain D39 control (S1 Fig), the `6A-10` pneumonia challenge (Fig 6), the single-colonization heterologous challenges (Fig 7), or the NALT IgA cohorts (S10 Fig).
3. **Inoculum Concentration vs. Dose Ambiguity:** Lines 112 and 117 state that mice were inoculated intranasally with *"~10^6 CFU/mL"*, without specifying the volume instilled into the nares (typically 10–20 µL, which corresponds to $1\text{--}2 \times 10^4\text{ CFU}$ per mouse).
4. **Denaturation Typo in Immunoblot Methods:** Line 97 states *"before denaturing for 10 minutes at 95°C for 10 minutes."*
5. **Absence of Data Availability Statement:** No raw numerical tables (CFU counts, ELISA OD450 values, protein array MFI matrix) or uncropped western blots are deposited or included in a Data Availability section.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Adequately Power the Biofilm-Mutant Experiment and Quantify Mutant Colonization Burden
* **WHY:** A sample size of $n = 2$ mice per group (`WT`, `ΔpsrP`, `ΔcbpA`, `ΔspxB`) is statistically invalid, and reduced serum IgG against biofilm WCL cannot be attributed to lack of biofilm aggregates if the mutants colonized at lower CFU burdens.
* **WHAT:** Expand the mutant colonization cohorts to at least $n = 6\text{--}8$ mice per individual genotype (analyzed separately rather than pooled) and measure longitudinal nasal wash CFU/mL alongside aggregate microscopy.
* **HOW:** Inoculate $n = 8$ C57BL/6J mice per group with WT TIGR4, `TIGR4ΔpsrP`, `TIGR4ΔcbpA`, or `TIGR4ΔspxB`, plate nasal washes at days 1, 3, 7, and 14 for CFU/mL enumeration, and normalize day-21 biofilm WCL ELISA IgG reactivity to each mouse's area-under-the-curve (AUC) log10 CFU nasal carriage burden (or test sera against purified recombinant PhtD/LysM so that deletion of CbpA/PsrP from the colonizing strain does not directly remove target antigens from the ELISA).

#### 2. Reframe "Antigenic Imprinting / Original Antigenic Sin" and Acknowledge Protein Array Scope
* **WHY:** Primary colonization reaches 10–100$\times$ higher CFU burden than secondary/tertiary challenges, and the 254-protein array only contains conserved core TIGR4 proteins.
* **WHAT:** Temper claims of "original antigenic sin" and "antigenic imprinting" in the Title, Abstract, Results, and Discussion to state that primary high-burden colonization establishes a durable baseline IgG/IgA response against conserved core surface proteins that is minimally altered by subsequent low-burden, rapidly cleared exposures.
* **HOW:** Explicitly note in the Results (lines 31–33) and Discussion (lines 68, 74) that (a) the 254-protein array measures conserved core antigens rather than strain-unique polymorphic antigens, and (b) reduced antigenic diversification during the 2nd and 3rd exposures correlates with rapid mucosal clearance and 10- to 100-fold lower bacterial load.

#### 3. Correct the Statistical Analysis Methods and Report Exact Test Statistics, Sample Sizes, and $p$-Values
* **WHY:** Reporting nonexistent statistical tests (*"Mann-Whitney log-rank t-test"*, *"nonparametric two-way ANOVA with Tukey's test"*) and omitting $n$, $r$, and $p$-values undermines statistical credibility.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  * Rewrite the Statistical Analysis section (line 132) to state the exact test used for each figure panel: e.g., Kaplan-Meier survival curves compared via Mantel-Cox log-rank test (Figs 6B, 7A); two-group unpaired comparisons via two-tailed Mann-Whitney $U$ test; multi-group single-factor comparisons via Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's post-hoc test (or parametric one-way/two-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc test after log10 transformation of CFU/ELISA data).
  * For all correlation plots (Figs 4D, 4G, 5F, 5G), report the exact Pearson $r$ (or Spearman $\rho$), 95% confidence interval, degrees of freedom, and exact two-tailed $p$-value, while noting the small number of strains ($k = 3\text{--}4$).
  * State the exact number of mice ($n$) per cohort and experiment in every figure legend and in the Methods.

#### 4. Provide Mandatory Ethics Approvals (IACUC & IRB), Inoculation Volumes, and Raw Data Tables
* **WHY:** Vertebrate animal research and human clinical serum studies require documented ethical oversight, and open raw data are required for verification.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  * Add an **Ethics Statement** section detailing the institutional IACUC protocol approval number and humane endpoint criteria for all murine studies, as well as the IRB approval number and participant informed-consent framework for the Colombian adult cohort ($n = 17$).
  * Specify the exact intranasal instillation volume (in µL) and total CFU delivered per mouse in lines 112 and 117.
  * Deposit a supplementary spreadsheet (or repository archive) containing all raw CFU counts, ELISA OD450 values, crystal violet OD595 values, and the 254-protein microarray MFI dataset, along with uncropped immunoblot images for Figure 2 and S2 Fig.
