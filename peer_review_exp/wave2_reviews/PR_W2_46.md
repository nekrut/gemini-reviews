## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript presents a single-cell transcriptomic atlas (34,340 cells across 8 10x Chromium v3.1 libraries) of the maturing chicken spinal cord across three developmental stages at brachial (wing-innervating) levels (E5/HH26, E7/HH30, and E10/HH36), contrasted at E10 with lumbar (leg-innervating) segments and with the ipsilateral brachial hemicord innervating a retinoic-acid-induced polydactyl wing (`432234` digit formula). Combining `Seurat`/`SCTransform` clustering, `miloR` differential neighborhood abundance testing, and single-cell weighted gene co-expression network analysis (`scWGCNA`) with promoter motif enrichment (`SEA`), RNA in situ hybridization (ISH), Periodic Acid-Schiff (PAS) histology, and confocal quantification of $>191,000$ FOXP1$^+$ motor neuron nuclei across serial $50\ \mu\text{m}$ cryosections (E6, E8, E10), the authors report four main findings: (1) `scWGCNA` resolves maturation trajectories in motor neurons (`lightgreen` $\to$ `darkred`) and oligodendrocytes (`darkgreen` $\to$ `tan`); (2) conserved gene modules isolate rare cerebrospinal fluid-contacting neurons (CSF-cNS; ~63 cells per sample), identifying novel markers (*CACNA1G*, *SFRP5*, *CRTAC1*) validated by ISH around the central canal; (3) brachial and lumbar segments share a conserved neuronal and glial cell-type repertoire, with the prominent exception of the avian-specific lumbar glycogen body, which exhibits a roof-plate-like signature and a specific *HOXD* (*HOXD3/4/8/9/10/11*) and *HOXA11* code validated by ISH and PAS staining; and (4) experimental polydactyly induces minimal transcriptional remodeling in spinal neurons at E10, but causes a spatially restricted reduction of FOXP1$^+$ motor neurons in the rostral half of the brachial lateral motor column (LMC) between E6 and E10. Raw and processed data (`GSE297980`) and analysis code (`GitHub` / `Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.18553805`) are publicly available.

**Editorial Verdict: Accept with Minor Revisions (Strong / Sound).** This is a technically thorough, biologically insightful resource and experimental evo-devo study that pairs droplet scRNA-seq and co-expression network analysis with extensive in vivo histological validation and an admirably transparent `Limitations of the study` section. A few minor statistical and methodological points should be clarified in revision: (1) processing the developmental E5/E7 libraries with `Cell Ranger v5.0.1` ($n=1$ per stage) while processing E10 libraries with `Cell Ranger v3.1.0`, and using hemisected contralateral control cords (`B10`) from RA-treated embryos for comparisons against unbisected whole lumbar cords (`L10`); (2) using an unpaired Wilcoxon rank-sum test rather than a paired test or mixed-effects model when comparing left (control) vs. right (polydactyl) LMC motor neuron counts within the same embryos ($n=4\text{--}5$); and (3) minor text/caption discrepancies (a misstated QC threshold in STAR Methods line 107, a caption typo in Fig. 5C, and `HOXD10` vs. `HOXD11` labeling in Fig. 4F).

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1 (`scWGCNA` co-expression modules capture developmental maturation states in motor neurons and oligodendrocytes and isolate rare CSF-cNS neurons with novel central-canal markers *CACNA1G*, *SFRP5*, and *CRTAC1*):** `[Well Supported]`  
  Supported by strong cross-dataset module preservation ($Z_{\text{summary}} > 10$; Fig. 3E, Fig. S3D), core overlap of 14 CSF-cNS genes (*PKD2L1*, *GATA2/3*, *TAL1/2*, *SST*, *ESPN*, *MYO3B*) across brachial, lumbar, and polydactyl datasets, and direct anatomical verification via ISH on E10 brachial spinal cord sections (Fig. 3I).
* **Claim 2 (Wing-innervating [brachial] and leg-innervating [lumbar] spinal cord segments rely on a largely conserved neuronal and glial cell-type repertoire at E10, whereas the lumbar-specific avian glycogen body expresses a distinct glycolytic and *HOXD*/*HOXA11* transcriptional program):** `[Well Supported]`  
  Demonstrated by integrated clustering and `miloR` differential abundance analysis (`B/L10_int`, Fig. 4A–B) and validated in vivo by PAS glycogen staining and ISH for *GBE1*, *MSX2*, *HOXA11*, *HOXD4*, and *HOXD10/11* (Fig. 4F), alongside promoter motif enrichment for HOX and TALE co-factors (*MEIS*, *PBX*) in modules `magenta` and `brown4` (Fig. S4L).
* **Claim 3 (RA-induced forelimb polydactyly [`432234`] induces minimal transcriptional divergence in E10 brachial neurons, with apparent DE genes in progenitors largely reflecting midline bisection imbalances in roof/floor plate cells):** `[Well Supported]`  
  The authors carefully cross-reference `Seurat::FindMarkers` DE gene counts against `miloR` neighborhood log-fold changes (Fig. 5B–C, Fig. S5D), demonstrating that clusters with balanced representation ($|\log_2\text{FC}| < 1$) show very few DE genes, and explicitly caveat in the `Limitations of the study` that droplet dissociation under-samples mature motor neurons.
* **Claim 4 (Altering the peripheral limb musculature in `432234` polydactyl wings causes a spatially restricted reduction of FOXP1$^+$ motor neurons in the rostral half of the brachial LMC between E6 and E10):** `[Well Supported]`  
  Anchored on an exhaustive stereological quantification of $>191,000$ FOXP1$^+$ nuclei across serial $50\ \mu\text{m}$ sections at E6 ($n=5$), E8 ($n=5$), and E10 ($n=4$) (Fig. 5D–L), showing identical rostral-caudal profiles at E6 followed by progressive, selective loss in the rostral half of the ipsilateral polydactyl LMC by E8 and E10—consistent with neurotrophic culling due to the replacement of proximal-anterior muscles (e.g., *extensor metacarpi radialis*) by posterior duplicates.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Dissection Geometry and `Cell Ranger` Version Differences Across Comparisons (Pillars 2, 3)
* **Hemisected Contralateral `B10` vs. Whole Unbisected `L10`, `B05`, and `B07`:** In STAR Methods (`Sample dissociation and single-cell RNA sequencing preparation`, Part 2 line 102), the authors note that `B10_1` and `B10_2` were harvested from the contralateral (left) side of the polydactyl embryos after cutting the roof plate and floor plate with a needle and dissecting the region corresponding to the caudal LMC, whereas `B05`, `B07`, and `L10_1, L10_2` were dissected from untreated whole spinal cords. While the authors thoughtfully account for midline bisection artifacts when comparing `B10` vs. `Poly10` (noting RP/FP enrichment in `Poly10`), any subtle differences in RP/FP or rostral-vs-caudal LMC representation between `B10` and `L10` could also reflect the hemisection and caudal-LMC-focused dissection of `B10`.
* **`Cell Ranger v5.0.1` vs. `v3.1.0` Across the Developmental Timeline:** STAR Methods line 107 states that `B05` and `B07` ($n=1$ library each) were processed with `Cell Ranger v5.0.1`, whereas all E10 samples (`B10`, `L10`, `Poly10`) were processed with `Cell Ranger v3.1.0`. Because `Cell Ranger v5.0` updated the EmptyDrops cell-calling parameters and alignment handling relative to `v3.1`, this software version difference across the `Devel_int` timeline should be noted alongside the single-library sample size at E5 and E7.

### 3.2 Paired Experimental Design in Motor Neuron Counting and Pseudoreplication in Single-Cell DE (Pillars 3, 4)
* **Unpaired Wilcoxon Rank-Sum Test Applied to Paired Left/Right Hemicords:** In Figure 5G–L (and STAR Methods line 177), control and polydactyl FOXP1$^+$ motor neuron counts were obtained from the left and right halves of the **same embryos** ($n=5$ at E6, $n=5$ at E8, $n=4$ at E10), yet statistical significance was evaluated using an unpaired Wilcoxon rank-sum test. With $n=4$ embryos at E10, an unpaired two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test has a minimum attainable $p$-value of $2/\binom{8}{4} = 0.0286$ and ignores the within-embryo pairing (which removes inter-embryo staging/size variance). Reporting a paired $t$-test (or a linear mixed-effects model with embryo ID as a random intercept) alongside the non-parametric test would strengthen the statistical presentation.
* **Cell-Level `MAST` Testing Without Pseudobulk Replicate Aggregation:** Differential expression between `B10` and `L10` ($n=2$ vs. $n=2$) and between `B10` and `Poly10` ($n=2$ vs. $n=2$ paired hemicords) was performed using `Seurat::FindMarkers(..., test.use = "MAST")` treating individual cells as independent observations (Part 2, lines 112, 122). While standard in exploratory scRNA-seq workflows and appropriately interpreted with caution by the authors, cell-level DE testing across $n=2$ biological replicates inflates nominal $p$-values.

### 3.3 Minor Editorial and Methodological Text Discrepancies (Pillar 7)
* **Ambiguous Phrasing of Gene-Count QC Filter (STAR Methods line 107):** Line 107 states: *"Cells with a gene to UMI ratio smaller than 15% and less expressed genes than 2/3 of the maximum number of detected genes were also not considered for further analysis."* Because the maximum number of detected genes per cell is $6,894$ (Results line 26), $2/3$ of the maximum is $4,596\text{ genes}$—which exceeds the dataset mean ($3,293\text{ genes}$)! Presumably, this filter either removed doublets with *more* than $2/3$ of the maximum gene count, or only excluded cells failing *both* the `<15%` complexity ratio and the gene-count threshold simultaneously; the wording should be clarified.
* **Figure 5C Caption Copy-Paste Error:** In the legend for Figure 5C (line 87), which displays DE genes between control brachial (`B10`) and polydactyl (`Poly10`) samples, the caption erroneously reads: *"(C) Beeswarm plot of differentially expressed genes between **brachial and lumbar** cells by cluster."* This should read **"control and polydactyl"**.
* **`HOXD10` vs. `HOXD11` In Situ Hybridization Label Mismatch:** Results line 72 states that lumbar cryosection ISH was performed *"using probes against HOXD4, **HOXD10**, and HOXA11"*, whereas the Figure 4F legend (line 75) states *"day 10 (HOXA11, HOXD4, and **HOXD11**) lumbar chick spinal cord"*.
