## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript presents a streamlined, low-input ribosome profiling (RP) library preparation protocol that replaces the classical Ingolia workflow's intramolecular circularization (`CircLigase`) and intermediate PAGE purifications with a 3'-biotinylated linker (`L1`), streptavidin magnetic bead capture, sequential on-bead 5' phosphorylation, and T4 RNA Ligase 1-mediated 5' ligation of a 5'-FAM DNA-RNA hybrid adaptor (`L2`). To suppress `L1-L2` empty adaptor dimers in ultra-low-input samples, the authors relocate the 5-nt UMI from `L1` to `L2` and introduce a locked nucleic acid (`LNA`) antisense blocker during ligation and reverse transcription, alongside post-PCR Cas9 (`Ribocutter`) rRNA depletion. Benchmarking against the classical protocol on split *E. coli* footprints ($2.5\ \mu\text{g}$ each), synthetic 28-nt RNA dilutions ($12\text{–}1000\text{ fmol}$), *S. cerevisiae* footprints ($5\text{ pmol}$ vs. $50\text{ fmol}$), mouse brain footprints, and fragmented *E. coli* total RNA demonstrates ~30% RNA-to-cDNA conversion (vs. ~2% for the classical method) and library generation down to $12\text{ fmol}$ input RNA.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision (Mixed / Salvageable).**  
Replacing `CircLigase` and multiple PAGE size-selections with an on-bead two-adaptor workflow and an `LNA` dimer-blocking clamp is a practical and cost-effective biochemical design. However, as a sequencing methods paper, the manuscript suffers from major omissions in both reproducibility and gold-standard ribosome profiling validation metrics:
1. **Complete Omission of Adaptor/Primer/LNA Oligonucleotide Sequences and Public Sequencing Data Accessions:** Neither the Materials and Methods nor the main text provides the nucleotide sequences and chemical modifications of `L1`, `L2`, the redesigned UMI-shifted `L1`/`L2`, the antisense `LNA` oligo, the RT primer, or the PCR primers. Furthermore, there is **no Data Availability statement** and **no public repository accession (GEO, SRA, or ENA)** for any of the Illumina NextSeq 550 datasets generated in the study.
2. **Absence of Essential Ribo-seq Quality Metrics (3-Nucleotide Periodicity, Footprint Length Distributions, and 5'-Ligation Sequence Bias):** The defining feature of ribosome profiling (highlighted in the first sentence of the Abstract) is **codon-level resolution**, which requires sharp footprint length distributions (~28 nt in eukaryotes) and strong 3-nucleotide triplet periodicity without terminal sequence bias. Because intermolecular 5' ssRNA ligation by T4 RNA Ligase 1 is well known to exhibit stronger sequence and secondary-structure bias than intramolecular ssDNA circularization (`CircLigase`), showing only gene-level read count scatter plots (Figs. 2B, 3C) and within-gene profile correlations on highly covered genes (Figs. 2D, 3D) is insufficient; the manuscript must quantify 3-nt reading frame periodicity, metagene start/stop codon profiles, and 5'/3' nucleotide composition biases.
3. **Unreported Statistical Metrics ($r$, $n$, Replicates) and Severe Volume/Unit Typos in Methods:** No numerical correlation coefficients ($r$, $\rho$, or $R^2$), sample replicate numbers ($n$), or error bar definitions are reported anywhere in the text for Figures 2–4, Tables 1 and 2 are cited in the text but omitted from the manuscript file, and the Methods section contains a $1,000\times$ volume typo (`780 ml of isopropanol` added to a $1.5\text{ mL}$ tube on line 130).

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (The on-bead `L1`/`L2` library protocol achieves ~30% conversion efficiency on a 28-nt control oligo and generates translatome libraries down to $12\text{–}50\text{ fmol}$ of RNA footprints; Fig. 3): [Moderately Supported].**  
  The dilution series (Fig. 3A) and $5\text{ pmol}$ vs. $50\text{ fmol}$ *S. cerevisiae* comparison (Fig. 3B–D) support high recovery from low inputs, though the 30% vs. 2% conversion rate was measured on a single homogeneous 28-nt synthetic sequence rather than complex cellular footprints, and numerical correlation coefficients and library complexity/duplication rates (post-UMI deduplication) at $50\text{ fmol}$ are not reported in the text.
* **Claim 2 (The new protocol fully replicates the codon-level data quality of the classical `CircLigase` protocol at ~30% of the cost and time; Abstract, Fig. 2, Table 1): [Fragile].**  
  While gene-level read counts (Fig. 2B) and high-coverage gene profiles (Fig. 2C–D) correlate well in *E. coli*, the manuscript presents no analysis of 3-nucleotide triplet periodicity, metagene P-site framing, footprint length distributions, or 5'-terminal nucleotide ligation bias comparing T4 RNA Ligase 1 (`L2`) against `CircLigase`.
* **Claim 3 (Relocating the UMI to `L2` and adding an antisense `LNA` oligonucleotide reduces empty `L1-L2` adaptor dimers 6-fold, and post-PCR Cas9 treatment nearly doubles coding reads in mouse brain RP; Fig. 4A–D): [Moderately Supported].**  
  Biochemically sound design, but Table 2 is missing from the manuscript body, the exact `LNA` sequence and melting temperature are omitted from Methods, and the number of replicates in Fig. 4C–D is unstated.
* **Claim 4 (The protocol adapts directly to bacterial total RNA-seq; Fig. 4E): [Moderately Supported].**  
  Demonstrated qualitatively on the *E. coli* `rplA–rplJ` operon track (Fig. 4E), though no transcriptome-wide RNA-seq benchmark or replicate correlation is shown.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Missing Oligonucleotide Sequences (`L1`, `L2`, `LNA`, RT/PCR Primers), Missing Tables 1–2, and Missing Raw Sequencing Data Accessions (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Results (lines 12, 24, 31, 45), Materials and methods (lines 95–111, 137–151).
* **Defect:**
  1. **No Public Sequencing Repository Accession:** The manuscript contains no Data Availability statement and no GEO, SRA, or ENA accession numbers for the *E. coli*, *S. cerevisiae*, and *M. musculus* sequencing runs.
  2. **Omitted Oligonucleotide Sequences:** The sequences, modifications (3'-biotin spacer, 5'-pre-adenylation, 5'-FAM, ribo vs. deoxyribo bases in the `L2` DNA-RNA hybrid, UMI length/position, LNA locked base positions), and supplier details for `L1`, `L2`, the antisense `LNA` oligo, the RT primer, and the forward/barcoded reverse PCR primers are completely absent from the Methods. In line 143, `cutadapt` command 2 trims `^CCCCCC -g ^CCCCC -g ^CCCC` after UMI extraction, implying an unexplained poly-C tract at the 3' end of `L2` that is never described in the text.
  3. **Missing Tables 1 and 2:** `Table I` / `Table 1` (line 12, line 31) and `Table 2` (line 45) are cited in the main text but are not present in the manuscript.
* **Remediation:** Deposit all raw FASTQ and processed bedGraph/count files in NCBI GEO/SRA or ENA and add a formal Data Availability section; add a complete Oligonucleotide Sequence Table to Materials and Methods listing all linker, primer, and LNA sequences with exact chemical modification codes; explain the `^CCCC` sequence in `cutadapt` command 2; and include Tables 1 and 2 in the manuscript text.

### 3.2 Absence of Codon-Resolution Periodicity, Metagene Framing, and 5'-Ligation Bias Benchmarks (Pillars 3, 5)
* **Location:** Results ("Protocol validation", lines 29–31; "superior sensitivity", lines 40; Figs. 2–3).
* **Defect:** The Abstract states that ribosome profiling studies *"mRNA translation in cells with codon-level resolution"*, and the Introduction notes that labs retain the classical protocol *"due to its well-established robustness, minimal sequence bias and high accuracy in footprint assignment"*. However, when validating the new T4 RNA Ligase 1 on-bead `L2` ligation against the classical `CircLigase` protocol, the authors only evaluate per-gene read totals (Fig. 2B) and Pearson/Spearman profile correlations on high-coverage genes in *E. coli* (where MNase digestion already has blurry 5' ends). They do not report:
  1. **3-nucleotide triplet periodicity (frame 0 vs. frame 1/2 fraction)** and **start/stop codon metagene profiles** in the RNase I-digested *S. cerevisiae* ($5\text{ pmol}$ and $50\text{ fmol}$) and mouse brain datasets;
  2. **Footprint length histograms** (comparing 20–40 nt size recovery between classical and new protocols);
  3. **Terminal nucleotide bias** at the 5' end of footprints (comparing T4 RNA Ligase 1 `L2` ligation vs. `CircLigase` intramolecular circularization) and **PCR duplicate rates** (using the 5-nt UMI) at $50\text{ fmol}$ vs. $5\text{ pmol}$ input.
* **Remediation:** Add quantitative comparisons of (i) 5' and 3' terminal nucleotide frequencies and amino-acid/codon pause scores in the split *E. coli* classical vs. new dataset, (ii) 3-nucleotide reading frame periodicity and start/stop codon metagene plots for the *S. cerevisiae* ($5\text{ pmol}$ and $50\text{ fmol}$) and mouse brain datasets, and (iii) UMI-deduplicated unique molecule complexity curves at $50\text{ fmol}$ input.

### 3.3 Unreported Correlation Coefficients, Sample Sizes ($n$), and Severe Volume/Unit Typos in Methods (Pillars 4, 7)
* **Location:** Results (lines 31, 40, 45, 47) and Materials and methods (lines 88, 130).
* **Defect:**
  1. Throughout the Results (Figs. 2B, 2D, 3C, 3D, 4C, 4D), no numerical correlation coefficients ($r$ or $\rho$), $p$-values, or biological/technical replicate counts ($n$) are stated in the text.
  2. In Materials and methods ("Preparation of samples for RNA-seq", line 130), the precipitation step states: *"About 700 µl of the aqueous phase were transferred to a fresh tube and 3 µl of GlycoBlue™ (Thermo Scientific, AM9515), 77.7 µl of 3 M NaOAc pH 5.5, and **780 ml of isopropanol** were added."* (`780 ml` instead of `780 µl`).
  3. In the same paragraph (line 130): *"concentrated to 6 with RNA Clean & Concentrator Kit"* (missing unit `µl`), and in line 88: *"1 volume µL isopropanol"* (redundant/conflicting unit `µL` after `1 volume`).
* **Remediation:** Report exact Pearson/Spearman correlation coefficients and replicate numbers ($n$) in the Results text and figure legends for all comparisons in Figures 2–4, and fix the unit typos on lines 88 and 130 (`780 ml` $\rightarrow$ `780 µl`; `to 6` $\rightarrow$ `to 6 µl`; `1 volume µL` $\rightarrow$ `1 volume`).
