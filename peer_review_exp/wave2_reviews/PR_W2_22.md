## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study evaluates Cas9-nickase-based (PE2) and Cas9-nuclease-based (PEn) Prime Editors in zebrafish embryos across four endogenous loci (`crbn`, `ror2`, `adgrf3b`, and `wls`) and one transgenic reporter (`Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104`), comparing mRNA versus ribonucleoprotein (RNP) delivery and standard pegRNAs versus single-primed insertion gRNAs (springRNAs), including abasic spacer modifications to prevent gRNA scaffold read-through. Using targeted amplicon sequencing ($n = 10$ individual embryos per condition), the authors show that PE2 yields higher editing purity (ratio of precise edits to total edits) for substitutions and short insertions, whereas PEn achieves higher absolute insertion frequencies (up to 27.3% for 3 bp in `adgrf3b`, 8.5% for a 30 bp NLS in `smyhc1:gfp`, and 1.4% for a 46 bp `attP` site in `wls`) at the expense of DSB-induced indels. They further demonstrate germline transmission of the `ror2 W722X` Robinow syndrome allele (6/10 F0 founders) and the 30 bp NLS knock-in (4/6 F0 founders), and characterize the craniofacial and axial phenotypes of maternal-zygotic (MZ) `ror2 W722X` mutants.

**Editorial Verdict: Minor Revision (Strong / Sound).**  
The experimental design is systematic, appropriately controlled with HDR ssODN baselines and off-target amplicon sequencing, and candid about the trade-off between PEn insertion efficiency and DSB-driven byproduct indels. Several technical, statistical, and data-archival issues should be addressed to strengthen the manuscript:
1. **Unarchived Raw Amplicon Sequencing Data:** While processed editing proportions are included in Source Data tables and CRISPResso2 parameters are provided, the raw Illumina NextSeq FASTQ files are not deposited in a public sequence archive (e.g., NCBI SRA or ENA).
2. **Instability of the "Precision Score" Metric at Near-Zero Editing Efficiency and Missing Uninjected Baselines:** When PE2 mRNA produces near-zero editing (e.g., 0.4% precise and 0.2% imprecise edit in `ror2`, Fig. 2F; or 1.3% precise edit in Fig. 3B), dividing by total edits yields an inflated precision score (58.9%–71.6%) that is sensitive to background sequencing/PCR noise, especially since uninjected wild-type control error rates are omitted from the main amplicon sequencing figures.
3. **Multi-Copy Transgene Confounding in the `smyhc1:gfp` Reporter Assay:** The `Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104` line carries at least four copies of the transgene per locus (Fig. 5E). Because a single in-frame NLS insertion produces dominant nuclear eGFP localization even if the remaining copies carry frameshift indels, visual F0/F1 phenotype screening does not have a 1:1 correspondence with allele-level editing precision.
4. **Omitted Sample Size in Adult Micro-CT Morphometrics and Competing Interest Contradiction:** Figure 4L–M omits the exact number of 1-year-old WT and MZ `ror2 W722X` fish scanned by micro-CT, and the declarations section contains a contradictory competing interests statement (`No competing interests declared` followed by `employee of AstraZeneca`).

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (PE2 provides higher precision scores for base substitutions at `crbn`, whereas PEn achieves higher absolute insertion efficiency for 3–46 bp fragments at the cost of DSB-induced indels; Figs. 1–3, 5): [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by individual-embryo deep amplicon sequencing ($n = 10$ per group), clone sequencing, and direct comparisons across mRNA/RNP delivery and HDR ssODN controls, though precision scores at very low total edit rates require caution.
* **Claim 2 (Abasic RNA/DNA spacers in springRNAs suppress unintended gRNA scaffold incorporation without increasing precise prime editing frequency; Fig. 3F–H, Fig. 3—supp 1H–K): [Well Supported].**  
  Directly demonstrated at both `ror2` and `adgrf3b` across mRNA and RNP delivery modes.
* **Claim 3 (PEn-mediated edits are germline-transmitted to generate a stable `ror2 W722X` line that genocopies human Robinow syndrome and phenocopies zebrafish `ror2` loss-of-function mutants; Fig. 4): [Well Supported].**  
  Confirmed by F1 genotyping across 10 F0 founders (60% founder rate), Sanger sequencing, larval body-length quantification in zygotic and MZ mutants, barbel absence, and 3D micro-CT mandibular morphometrics.
* **Claim 4 (PEn efficiently inserts a 30 bp NLS into `smyhc1:gfp` and a 46 bp `attP` sequence into `wls`; Fig. 5): [Moderately Supported].**  
  Amplicon sequencing confirms 8.5% (`smyhc1:gfp`) and 1.4% (`wls`) precise insertion in F0 embryos and germline transmission of the NLS tag, but the multi-copy nature of the `smyhc1:gfp` array complicates single-cell phenotypic interpretation, and the 1.4% `attP` knock-in is not significantly different from ssODN HDR (1.4% and 0.5%).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Missing Public Repository Accession for Raw Amplicon Sequencing Reads (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Materials and methods ("Amplicon sequencing and bioinformatics") and Data availability (lines 93–95).
* **Defect:** The study relies extensively on Illumina NextSeq deep amplicon sequencing across five loci (`crbn`, `ror2`, `adgrf3b`, `wls`, `smyhc1:gfp`) and three off-target sites, processed with CRISPResso2. However, the Data Availability statement only refers to the summary tables in the figure source data and Supplementary Files 1–3; raw FASTQ files have not been deposited in NCBI SRA, ENA, or Zenodo.
* **Remediation:** Deposit all demultiplexed raw amplicon sequencing FASTQ files in ENA or NCBI SRA and include the study accession number in the Data Availability section.

### 3.2 Precision Score Ratio Distortion at Low Editing Efficiency and Absence of Uninjected Controls (Pillars 4, 5)
* **Location:** Results (Fig. 2F; Fig. 3B, D; Fig. 3—figure supplement 1B) and Materials and methods.
* **Defect:**
  1. The precision score is calculated as $\text{Precise Edits} / (\text{Precise Edits} + \text{Imprecise Edits} + \text{Indels})$. In `ror2` PE2 mRNA + pegRNA (Fig. 3B and Fig. 3—supp 1B), the mean precise edit frequency is only 1.3% (and 0.4% in the pooled amplicon of Fig. 2F), yet it is assigned the highest precision score (71.6% in Fig. 3—supp 1B; 58.9% in Fig. 2F) because its denominator is near zero. At such low editing rates, background PCR/sequencing errors heavily influence the denominator.
  2. Unlike the off-target analysis (Fig. 3—supp 1G), the on-target amplicon sequencing experiments in Figures 1, 2, 3, and 5 do not include uninjected wild-type sibling embryos to establish the baseline false-positive indel and substitution call rates in CRISPResso2.
* **Remediation:** Report the baseline CRISPResso2 call rates from uninjected control embryos for each target amplicon, and qualify the precision score metric whenever total editing falls below 2%.

### 3.3 Multi-Copy Transgene Array Confounding in `Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104` Phenotypic Readout (Pillars 3, 6)
* **Location:** Results ("Insertion of nuclear localisation signal into transgenic reporter gene", Fig. 5A–F).
* **Defect:** Figure 5A frames the `smyhc1:gfp` assay as a clean three-outcome cellular readout: cytosolic GFP = unedited, loss of GFP = imprecise edit/indel, and nuclear GFP = precise prime edit. However, as the authors show in Fig. 5E, the `smyhc1:gfp i104` line contains at least four tandem transgene copies. Consequently, a muscle fiber with one precisely edited copy, one unedited copy, and two frameshifted copies will still exhibit nuclear GFP (or mixed nuclear/cytoplasmic GFP, which the authors note prevented F0 pre-screening). Thus, cellular fluorescence does not distinguish clean precise editing from compound imprecise/mosaic editing across the tandem array.
* **Remediation:** Explicitly update the schematic interpretation in Fig. 5A and the text to explain how multi-copy concatenation in `Tg(smyhc1:gfp)i104` masks co-occurring frameshift indels within the same cell or F1 embryo.

### 3.4 Missing Sample Size ($n$) in Adult Micro-CT Analysis and Contradictory Competing Interests Declaration (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Figure 4L–M legend; Additional information (lines 69–71).
* **Defect:**
  1. While Figure 4F specifies `n=10 per group` for 5 dpf larval length, the legend for Figure 4L–M (mandibular aspect ratios from 1-year-old micro-CT reconstructions) states *"individual data points on the graph indicate values from single reconstructed image"* without reporting $n$ per group in the text, legend, or Methods.
  2. Under "Additional information" (lines 69–71), the text states `No competing interests declared.` immediately followed on the next line by `employee of AstraZeneca.` (alongside BBSRC Industrial Partnership Award funding with AstraZeneca).
* **Remediation:** State the exact sample size ($n$) for WT and MZ `ror2 W722X` adult CT scans in the Figure 4 legend and Methods, and correct the Competing Interests declaration to formally disclose the AstraZeneca employment and industrial partnership.
