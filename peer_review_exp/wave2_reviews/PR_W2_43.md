## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This study examines the relationships among early maternal sensory unpredictability (conditional Shannon entropy of maternal visual, auditory, and tactile transitions during a 5-minute "No Toy" interaction at 2–6 months; $N = 256$), infant visual orienting behavior (VOB; gaze shifts to and from the mother), toddler inhibitory control at 19–28 months (glitter-wand wait time; $n = 195$), and infant gut microbiome composition via shotgun metagenomics ($n = 93$). The authors report that higher maternal unpredictability correlates with higher age-adjusted VOB ($r = 0.314, p < 0.001$) and lower toddler inhibitory control, with a 3-way interaction ($p < 0.05, \eta_p^2 = 0.022$) suggesting that high VOB among older infants (4.6 months) buffers the negative slope of maternal unpredictability on inhibitory control. In the $n = 93$ metagenomic subsample, VOB explains $3.8\%$ of taxonomic and $2.1\%$ of functional Bray-Curtis variance (PERMANOVA), tracks a *Bifidobacterium breve* ($\beta = +0.109, q < 0.20$) vs. *B. longum* ($\beta = -0.117, q < 0.20$) gradient, and shows gene-set enrichment signals for tryptophan and glutamate synthesis. Raw reads (`PRJNA1128723`), tabular data (`Dryad`), and Julia code (`GitHub`/`Zenodo`) are publicly archived.

**Editorial Verdict: Major Revision Required (Mixed / Salvageable).** The longitudinal cohort in Gugulethu, South Africa is an important empirical resource, and the behavioral observation linking maternal sensory entropy to infant gaze switching is well documented. However, the manuscript's statistical execution and mechanistic microbiome claims suffer from severe flaws: (1) major infant microbiome confounders—specifically **breastfeeding vs. formula feeding**, **delivery mode (vaginal vs. C-section)**, and **perinatal HIV exposure**—are completely omitted from the metagenomic models despite driving the *B. breve* / *B. longum* axis in 2–6 month infants; (2) the Feature Set Enrichment Analysis (FSEA) applies a Wilcoxon rank-sum test across individual `UniRef90` genes without accounting for bacterial genome co-carriage (linkage), inflating $p$-values by orders of magnitude, and claims "significant enrichment" for quinolinic acid degradation at **$q = 0.48$**; (3) the Random Forest model performs worse than predicting the sample mean ($\text{RMSE} = 1.10$ on a z-standardized target, i.e., out-of-fold $R^2 = -0.21$); and (4) the text contains direct contradictions between Results and Methods regarding stool collection windows (`±30 days` vs. `<3 months`), regression equations, and simple-slope arithmetic ($\Delta b = -1.064$ reported vs. $-2.020$ actual), alongside unedited template placeholders (`x-y minutes`, `Table X`, `VEP features`).

### Assessment of Specific Claims

* **Claim 1 (Higher maternal sensory unpredictability at 2–6 months predicts more frequent infant visual orienting shifts [VOB] independent of infant age, and persists 3 months later):** `[Well Supported]`  
  Supported by ANCOVA controlling for infant age ($F_{1,253} = 29.19, p < 0.001, \eta_p^2 = 0.103$; age-residualized $r = 0.314$) and replicated concurrently at the 3-month follow-up ($r = 0.19, p = 0.006, n = 207$). Caveated by an iterative coding reliability procedure that re-coded sessions until $\ge 80\%$ agreement was reached.
* **Claim 2 (High infant VOB buffers against the negative longitudinal effect of high maternal unpredictability on toddler inhibitory control at 19–28 months):** `[Fragile]`  
  Rests on a fragile 3-way interaction (`Age × Maternal Unpredictability × VOB`, $F_{1,185} = 4.092, p < 0.05, \eta_p^2 = 0.022$). In Results line 45, the simple slope at $+1\text{ SD}$ age is $b = -1.431$ ($\text{SE} = 0.437$) for low VOB and $b = +0.589$ ($\text{SE} = 0.547$) for high VOB, yet the reported difference between slopes ($\Delta b = -1.064, \text{SE} = 0.713$) is arithmetically inconsistent with $(-1.431) - (+0.589) = -2.020$ and yields a non-significant $t = -1.49$ ($p = 0.137$) as printed.
* **Claim 3 (Infant VOB is associated with gut microbial community structure along a *Bifidobacterium breve* to *B. longum* axis and can be predicted by Random Forest models):** `[Fragile]`  
  While PERMANOVA ($R^2 = 0.038, p = 0.003$) and single-taxon regressions at a lenient FDR threshold ($q < 0.20$) show modest associations with *B. longum* and *B. breve*, the models omit infant diet (exclusive vs. partial breastfeeding vs. formula) and delivery mode—the primary ecological determinants of *Bifidobacterium* species dominance at 2–6 months. Furthermore, the Random Forest model achieved an validation $\text{RMSE} = 1.10$ on z-normalized VOB ($\text{SD} = 1.0$), indicating negative out-of-sample variance explained ($R^2 = -0.21$), and the combined RF model ($R = 0.407$ vs. $0.367$) was statistically non-significant ($p > 0.05$).
* **Claim 4 (Neuroactive gene-set enrichment links VOB to tryptophan and glutamate synthesis, while high maternal unpredictability enriches acetate synthesis and quinolinic acid degradation to biochemically modulate behavioral plasticity):** `[Methodologically Flawed]`  
  First, quinolinic acid degradation had **$q = 0.48$** (Results line 71), failing even the authors' lenient $q < 0.20$ threshold, yet is highlighted in the Abstract and Discussion as a significant finding. Second, running a Wilcoxon rank-sum test across hundreds of individual `UniRef90` gene families treats physically linked genes on the same bacterial chromosome as independent observations, severely inflating Type I error. Third, the taxonomic and functional claims are internally contradictory: Results line 75 states that *B. longum* is the primary carrier of tryptophan synthesis genes, yet *B. longum* is *negatively* associated with VOB ($\beta = -0.117$) while tryptophan synthesis is claimed to be *positively* enriched with VOB ($ES = +0.291$), and Discussion line 90 subsequently states that *"Infant-type Bifidobacterium sp. cannot fully synthesize tryptophan de novo."*

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Omission of Critical Perinatal and Dietary Confounders in Infant Metagenomics (Pillars 2, 3, 6)
* **Uncontrolled Feeding Mode, Delivery Mode, and Maternal HIV Status:** Between 2 and 6 months of age, infant gut colonization by *Bifidobacterium longum* subsp. *infantis/longum* versus *B. breve*, *Klebsiella pneumoniae*, and *Bacteroides vulgatus* is governed by human milk oligosaccharide (HMO) intake (breastfeeding vs. formula feeding), mode of delivery (vaginal vs. cesarean), and perinatal antibiotic/HIV prophylaxis exposure. None of these covariates were included in the PERMANOVA (line 52), linear models (line 60), Random Forest (line 62), or FSEA (line 69). Because breastfeeding also shapes mother-infant physical proximity and gaze dynamics during floor interactions, dietary confounding must be ruled out before attributing VOB variance to microbial neuroactive synthesis.
* **Contradictory Stool Collection Window (`±30 days` vs. `< 3 months`):** Introduction line 18 and Results line 50 state that stool samples were collected *"concurrently (±30 days)"* with behavioral videos. However, Methods (Part 2, line 45) states: *"Participants were included if the stool sample was collected less than 3 months apart from the video recording."* For an infant cohort aged 2–6 months, a $\pm 90$-day gap spans nearly the entire developmental window under study.

### 3.2 Statistical Contradictions, Invalid FSEA Independence Assumptions, and False Discovery Reporting (Pillars 3, 4)
* **Reporting $q = 0.48$ as "Significantly Enriched":** In Results line 71, the authors write: *"while acetate synthesis and quinolinic acid degradation were significantly enriched (respectively, ES = +0.138, q = 0.183; ES = +0.141, q = 0.48) in children with more unpredictable mothers."* An FDR $q$-value of $0.48$ is non-significant under any standard (including the Methods threshold of $q < 0.20$, Part 2 line 72). Nevertheless, the Abstract (line 5) and Discussion (lines 88–90) build a core mechanistic narrative around quinolinic acid degradation.
* **Pseudoreplication of Co-Inherited Bacterial Genes in FSEA:** In Methods (Part 2, lines 70–72), the authors fit logistic regressions (`presence/absence ~ Age + Sex + MatEnt + VisAtt`) separately to each of ~274,300 `UniRef90` gene families and then run a Wilcoxon rank-sum test on $z$-statistics within curated gene sets. Because `UniRef90` gene families within a pathway (e.g., tryptophan or glutamate biosynthesis enzymes) reside together on the same *Bifidobacterium* or *Escherichia* genomes, their presence/absence across infants is highly collinear. Applying a non-parametric rank-sum test that assumes independent gene-level observations produces spuriously tiny $q$-values ($q = 7.1 \times 10^{-6}$ for tryptophan synthesis in Model 2) from a weak species-level signal ($p = 0.003$).
* **Contradiction Between Results and Methods Linear Model Specifications:**
  * **Results (line 60):** *"We modeled z-normalized VOB as an outcome for each microbial feature ($m = 45$)... while controlling for age at stool collection. Microbial relative abundances were arcsine-transformed."* (`z_VOB ~ arcsin(species) + age`).
  * **Methods (Part 2, line 60):** *"Individual taxonomic features were assessed for associations with VEP features [sic] using multivariable linear models... The equation tested was `species ~ VOB + mbiome_age_months + child_sex`; where species is the arcsin-normalized relative abundance of each taxon when present in the sample, VOB is the unit-range-normalized numerical value..."*
  * Not only do the dependent/independent variables, normalizations (`z-normalized` vs. `unit-range-normalized`), and covariates (`age` vs. `age + sex`) contradict each other, but fitting linear models only *"when present in the sample"* discards zero counts without reporting the effective sample size $n$ per taxon, and arcsine transformation of relative abundances is not a compositional log-ratio transform as claimed in Introduction line 18 (*"compositional-aware transforms"*).
* **Arithmetic Error in Three-Way Interaction Simple Slopes:** In Results line 45, at $+1\text{ SD}$ infant age, the simple slope of maternal unpredictability on inhibitory control is $b = -1.431$ ($\text{SE} = 0.437$) at low VOB and $b = +0.589$ ($\text{SE} = 0.547$) at high VOB. The difference between these two slopes is $(-1.431) - (+0.589) = -2.020$, yet the text reports $\Delta b = -1.064$ ($\text{SE} = 0.713$, which gives an unstated, non-significant $p = 0.137$).

### 3.3 Machine Learning Overfitting and Leakage (Pillars 3, 4)
* **Out-of-Fold $\text{RMSE} > 1.0$ on a Z-Normalized Outcome:** In Results line 62, the Random Forest predicting z-normalized VOB ($\text{mean} = 0, \text{SD} = 1.0$) achieved a 3-fold CV $\text{RMSE} = 1.10$. Because predicting $\hat{y} = 0$ for every infant yields $\text{RMSE} = 1.00$, an $\text{RMSE}$ of $1.10$ means the Random Forest has a negative out-of-sample coefficient of determination ($R^2 \approx 1 - 1.10^2 = -0.21$) and is overfitting noise. Furthermore, selecting the "winning combination of hyperparameters" on the validation sets (Methods Part 2, line 65) and pre-selecting the "30 top-ranking microbial features" prior to cross-validation introduce selection leakage.

### 3.4 Iterative Inter-Rater Reliability Bias and Unedited Manuscript Placeholders (Pillars 2, 7)
* **Iterative Re-Coding Until $\ge 80\%$ Agreement:** Methods (Part 1 line 119 – Part 2 line 1) states that if inter-observer reliability fell below 80%, coders revised their coding and repeated the check until $\ge 0.80$ agreement was achieved for every behavior. While useful for consensus training, this procedure truncates the lower tail of disagreements; pre-revision inter-rater reliability (Cohen's $\kappa$ or ICC) must be reported.
* **Unedited Template Placeholders and Copy-Paste Errors:**
  * Part 1, line 117: `"total time of video-recordings ranged from x-y minutes"` and `"behaviors listed in Table X"`.
  * Part 2, line 60: `"Individual taxonomic features were assessed for associations with VEP features"` (leftover text from a visual evoked potential manuscript).
  * Results line 56 reports VOB cluster means in unexplained scientific notation ($3.15 \times 10^{-5}$ to $6.13 \times 10^{-5}$), without specifying the denominator time unit.

### Actionable Remediation Plan
1. **Control for Infant Feeding Mode, Delivery Mode, and Exact Stool-to-Video Time Lag:** Re-run the PERMANOVA, taxon-level linear models, and pathway analyses including infant feeding status (exclusive breastfeeding, mixed feeding, formula), delivery mode (vaginal vs. C-section), maternal HIV status, and the exact time lag ($\Delta\text{days}$) between video recording and stool collection; also perform a sensitivity analysis restricted strictly to infants whose stool was collected within the claimed $\pm 30\text{ days}$ window.
2. **Replace Gene-Level Wilcoxon FSEA with Stratified Pathway Abundance Models and Remove $q = 0.48$ Claims:** Aggregate `HUMAnN3` gene families into Gut-Brain Module (GBM) or MetaCyc pathway abundances *per sample* first (using a true compositional transform such as centered log-ratio, CLR, or `MaAsLin3` prevalence/abundance modeling across the $n = 93$ subjects) rather than treating co-inherited `UniRef90` genes as independent observations in a Wilcoxon rank-sum test. Remove all claims that quinolinic acid degradation ($q = 0.48$) is significantly enriched.
3. **Reconcile Regression Equations, Simple-Slope Arithmetic, and Editorial Placeholders:**
   * Correct the arithmetic and report the exact two-sided $p$-value for the difference between simple slopes ($\Delta b$) in Results line 45.
   * Reconcile the linear model equation and variable normalizations between Results line 60 and Methods line 60, and report the exact non-zero sample size $n$ for each tested species.
   * Replace `"ranged from x-y minutes"`, `"Table X"`, and `"VEP features"` with exact values, define the physical units of VOB ($10^{-5}$), and report pre-revision inter-rater reliability (ICC / Cohen's $\kappa$).
