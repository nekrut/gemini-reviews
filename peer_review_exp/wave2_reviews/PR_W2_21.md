## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript investigates adult behavioural phenotypes (locomotor, anxiety-like, social, and object recognition memory) and whole-brain transcriptomic (`n = 4` brains/group) and proteomic (`n = 4` brains/group) profiles in 12-month-old `sacs -/-` zebrafish modelling autosomal recessive spastic ataxia of Charlevoix-Saguenay (ARSACS), alongside chronic dietary supplementation with 0.1% tauroursodeoxycholic acid (TUDCA) from 5 days post-fertilization (dpf) to 12 months. The authors report that TUDCA improves early larval survival, partially ameliorates adult locomotor, social, and cognitive deficits, and alters brain expression of circadian rhythm, mitochondrial, and neuroinflammatory pathways. Raw proteomics data are deposited in PRIDE (`PXD058522`) and transcriptomics data in Zenodo (`14809944`, `15123596`).

**Editorial Verdict: Reject (Major Methodological and Experimental Flaws).**  
While characterizing aged `sacs -/-` zebrafish and exploring neuroprotective interventions are biologically worthwhile goals, the study's primary conclusions are compromised by fundamental experimental design and statistical defects:
1. **Absence of a Treated Wild-Type Control Group (`WT + TUDCA`):** All experiments use an asymmetric three-group design (`WT`, `sacs -/-`, and `sacs -/- + TUDCA`) analyzed by one-way ANOVA, making it impossible to test for a Genotype $\times$ Treatment interaction or distinguish disease-specific rescue from general stimulant or metabolic effects of chronic 12-month TUDCA administration.
2. **Severe Differential Survivorship Bias:** Untreated `sacs -/-` zebrafish experience high mortality during the first month of life, whereas TUDCA rescues this early lethality (Fig. 1B). Consequently, all 12-month behavioural and multi-omics comparisons contrast a highly selected survivor subpopulation of untreated mutants against a non-selected survivor cohort of TUDCA-treated mutants.
3. **Pseudoreplication and Statistical Misspecification in Behavioural Assays:** In the shoaling assay (Fig. 3A–B), inter-fish distance is a shoal-level metric, yet individual fish ($N = 10$) are treated as independent statistical replicates in a one-way ANOVA. In the novel object recognition (NOR) task (Fig. 4C), interdependent within-subject exploration percentages (`NO` vs. `FO`) are analyzed via one-way ANOVA across all bars rather than paired/repeated-measures testing or a discrimination index.
4. **Uncontrolled Circadian Harvesting Confound in Brain Transcriptomics:** The headline transcriptomic finding—differential expression of core circadian clock genes (`PER` and `CRY` families) in `sacs -/-` brains—is confounded by sacrificing animals *after* completing behavioural testing conducted between 10:00 and 16:00, without controlling or matching Zeitgeber Time (ZT) of brain dissection across groups.
5. **Internal Contradictions and Copy-Paste Errors in Text, Tables, and Methods:** The manuscript contains contradictory protein direction claims between text and tables (e.g., `OPTN` is listed as downregulated in Table 2 but described as increased in the Discussion), a self-contradictory Figure 7B legend, an incorrect mathematical formula for Distance Ratio in Methods, and verbatim copy-paste errors in the proteomics and mass spectrometry Methods.

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (TUDCA improves early survival and growth in `sacs -/-` zebrafish; Fig. 1B–C): [Fragile].**  
  The Kaplan-Meier survival analysis in Fig. 1B omits sample sizes ($N$ per group), censoring rules, and the number of replicate tanks, precluding assessment of tank-level batch effects.
* **Claim 2 (TUDCA partially rescues locomotor, anxiety-like, social, and cognitive deficits in aged `sacs -/-` zebrafish; Figs. 2–4): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Undermined by (i) differential survivorship selection between untreated and TUDCA-treated `sacs -/-` cohorts at 12 months, (ii) absence of a `WT + TUDCA` group to test Genotype $\times$ Treatment interaction, (iii) pseudoreplication in the shoaling assay, and (iv) inappropriate one-way ANOVA on paired percentage data in the NOR task.
* **Claim 3 (Adult `sacs -/-` brains exhibit transcriptomic dysregulation of circadian rhythm pathways; Fig. 5): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Brains were harvested on the same day *after* behavioural experiments spanning 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Because expression of `per` and `cry` genes oscillates steeply over a 6-hour daytime window, sequential behavioural testing and dissection by group introduces a direct time-of-day confound. Moreover, RNA Integrity Numbers (RIN) were as low as 5.6 ($5.6\text{–}8.1$) with $n = 4$ per group and no adjustment for RIN or batch.
* **Claim 4 (Proteomic profiles demonstrate TUDCA-mediated reversal of neuroinflammation and ER/oxidative stress; Tables 1–4): [Overstated].**  
  Tables 1–4 report qualitative lists of selected proteins without fold changes, raw $p$-values, or FDR-adjusted $q$-values, and the Discussion contradicts Table 2 regarding `OPTN` directionality while citing proteins (`SSUH2`) absent from the results tables.

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Asymmetric Three-Group Design and Missing `WT + TUDCA` Control (Pillars 3, 5)
* **Location:** Results (Figs. 1–8, Tables 1–4) and Methods ("Fish husbandry and feeding").
* **Defect:** The entire study compares three groups (`WT`, `sacs -/-`, `sacs -/- + TUDCA`) using one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-hoc test. No `WT + TUDCA` group was included (even though Methods notes that preliminary 60-day dosage tests were run on both WT and mutant fish). Without `WT + TUDCA`, the study cannot estimate a $2 \times 2$ Genotype $\times$ Diet interaction. Furthermore, it is unclear from Methods ("Control fish were maintained under the same conditions as the treated fish but without exposure to any treatment") whether untreated `WT` and `sacs -/-` fish received the exact re-pelleted trehalose-containing vehicle control diet or standard commercial feed.
* **Remediation:** Clarify whether untreated groups received the trehalose-re-pelleted vehicle diet, and explicitly reframe all TUDCA comparisons to acknowledge that disease-specific interaction effects cannot be separated from baseline pharmacological effects of TUDCA in the absence of a `WT + TUDCA` cohort.

### 3.2 Unreported Sample Sizes and Survivorship Bias in 12-Month Cohorts (Pillars 2, 4)
* **Location:** Results ("TUDCA improves early survival...", Fig. 1B–C).
* **Defect:** Fig. 1B reports a log-rank Mantel-Cox test ($p < 0.001$) for Kaplan-Meier survival curves over 12 months, but never states the starting number of larvae ($N$) per group or how many replicate tanks were tracked. Because untreated `sacs -/-` fish suffer steep mortality during the first 30 days while TUDCA-treated `sacs -/-` fish survive at near-WT rates, the 12-month-old untreated `sacs -/-` fish sampled for behaviour ($N = 10$) and omics ($n = 4$) represent a non-random survivor subset ("healthy survivor bias").
* **Remediation:** Report exact initial $N$, censored counts, and tank replicate numbers for Fig. 1B, and explicitly discuss survivorship selection as a major caveat when interpreting 12-month adult phenotypes.

### 3.3 Pseudoreplication in Shoaling Test and Flawed Statistical Testing in NOR (Pillars 3, 4)
* **Location:** Fig. 3A–B (Shoaling test) and Fig. 4C (Novel Object Recognition test).
* **Defect:**
  1. In the shoaling assay (Fig. 3A–B; Methods), a group of conspecific fish is placed together in a tank and the average inter-fish distance of the shoal is tracked. Because fish within a shoal mutually determine each other's inter-fish distance, the experimental unit is the *shoal* (independent tank trial), not the individual fish. Reporting $N = 5$ males and $5$ females per group and running a one-way ANOVA on $N = 10$ treats non-independent individuals within a shoal as independent biological replicates.
  2. In the NOR test (Fig. 4C), percentage exploration time is plotted separately for Object 1 and Object 2 (training) and for New Object (`NO`) and Familiar Object (`FO`) (test session) across groups, and analyzed via one-way ANOVA with Tukey's test. Because `%NO` and `%FO` are paired measurements from the same animal (and sum to 100% of total object exploration time), comparing them with an omnibus one-way ANOVA violates independence and homoscedasticity assumptions.
* **Remediation:** Re-analyze shoaling data using independent shoal/trial replicates as the statistical unit ($N = \text{number of shoals}$). For NOR (Fig. 4C) and open-field inner/outer zone times (Fig. 2F), compute a per-animal Discrimination Index $\text{DI} = (T_{\text{NO}} - T_{\text{FO}}) / (T_{\text{NO}} + T_{\text{FO}})$ (or use a paired test for within-group preference and a one-way ANOVA across groups on the Discrimination Index).

### 3.4 Uncontrolled Time-of-Day (ZT) Harvesting Confound in Circadian Transcriptomics (Pillars 2, 6)
* **Location:** Results ("Key insights from multiomics analyses...", Fig. 5), Discussion, and Methods ("Fish husbandry and feeding", "Behavioural analysis").
* **Defect:** Behavioural testing was performed between 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., and Methods states: *"After completing the behavioral experiments, the fish were sacrificed, and organ dissection was performed on the same day."* Core circadian clock genes (`per1/2/3`, `cry1/2`, `clock`, `bmal1`) in zebrafish brain undergo multi-fold transcriptional changes between ZT2 and ZT8 (10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.). If the three experimental groups ($n = 4$ brains/group) were tested and sacrificed sequentially rather than in counterbalanced blocks at matched ZT timestamps, the enrichment of circadian rhythm transcripts in Fig. 5A–B is an artifact of dissection time. Notably, as the authors themselves admit in the Discussion, core clock proteins (`CLOCK`, `BMAL1`, `PER`, `CRY`) were *not* altered in the proteomics dataset.
* **Remediation:** Report the exact clock time (ZT) of sacrifice for each of the 12 RNA-seq brain samples. If sacrifice times were not randomized/interleaved across groups, reframe the circadian transcriptomic signature as potentially confounded by sampling time and acute post-behavioural stress.

### 3.5 Omission of Differential Expression Statistics, Contradictions Between Text and Tables, and Methods Errors (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Tables 1–4, Fig. 7B legend, Discussion, and Methods.
* **Defect:**
  1. **Missing RNA-seq & Proteomics Statistical Parameters:** The RNA-seq Methods section does not state the alignment software, reference genome build, differential expression package (e.g., DESeq2, edgeR, limma), or fold-change cutoff used to define the 527 and 817 DEGs. Similarly, Tables 1–4 list selected "upregulated" and "downregulated" proteins without reporting log2 fold changes, $p$-values, or FDR-adjusted $q$-values, and Methods ("Mass spectrometry data analysis") does not state the significance or fold-change thresholds applied in `IMP-apQuant`.
  2. **Direct Contradictions Between Discussion and Proteomics Tables:** Table 2 lists `OPTN` under *downregulated* proteins in `sacs -/-` vs. WT brains, but the Discussion (page 9) states: *"The increased presence of pro-inflammatory proteins like CRP and OPTN could suggest a chronic inflammatory state."* Likewise, the Discussion claims *"downregulation of ubiquitin-proteasome-related proteins such as SSUH2 and MARCHF2"*, but `SSUH2` is absent from Table 4 and the Results text. Additionally, `B2M` is listed as *downregulated* in `sacs -/-` vs. WT (Table 2) and *also* downregulated in TUDCA-treated vs. untreated `sacs -/-` (Table 4), meaning TUDCA pushes `B2M` further away from WT rather than restoring it.
  3. **Contradictory Figure 7B Legend:** The legend for Fig. 7B reads *"GSEA in the brains of TUDCA-treated sacs vs. WT adult sacs −/− zebrafish"*, which is both internally nonsensical ("WT adult sacs -/-") and contradicts the main text (which states Fig. 7 compares TUDCA-treated `sacs -/-` vs. untreated `sacs -/-`).
  4. **Mathematical Formula Error in Methods:** In "Social preference test", the Distance Ratio formula is given as `[(Distance ratio %=Time spent in conspecific sector/ total time observed) ×100]`, duplicating the Time Ratio formula instead of dividing distance travelled in the conspecific sector by total distance travelled.
  5. **Copy-Paste and Unit Errors in Methods:**
     - Under "Proteomic analysis -> Protein isolation": *"For proteomic analysis, 4 brains were isolated from adult animals per experimental group. RNA was extracted from individual samples to maintain biological variability according to standard procedures."*
     - Under "Mass spectrometry": The nano-UHPLC-MS/MS setup is described twice in the same paragraph, and the second description states that tryptic digests were *"resuspended with 1.5 mL of the LC-LOAD component"* and separated on a *"PepMap RSLC C18 EASY spray column (75 mm, 50 cm, 2 mm particle size)"* (physical dimensions off by $10^3$: should be $\mu\text{L}$ and $75\ \mu\text{m} \times 50\ \text{cm}, 2\ \mu\text{m}$ particle size).
     - Under "Fish husbandry and feeding": Duplicate clause *"supplementation was carried out by including the compound in the pelleted feed supplementation was carried out by including the compound in the pelleted feed"*.
* **Remediation:** Add log2FC and FDR-adjusted $p$-values to Tables 1–4; specify the exact RNA-seq bioinformatic pipeline and thresholds; correct the `OPTN`, `SSUH2`, and `B2M` statements in the Discussion; fix the Figure 7B legend; and correct all formula, unit, and copy-paste errors in the Methods.
