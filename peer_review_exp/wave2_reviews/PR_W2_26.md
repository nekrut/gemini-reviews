## 2. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

This manuscript presents the `ALL-GCL` dataset (v2.0), a standardized neurophysiology resource integrating two-photon $\text{Ca}^{2+}$-imaging recordings (bulk-electroporated OGB-1) from $>80,000$ cells in the ganglion cell layer (GCL) of 227 ex vivo C57BL/6J mouse retinae across 155 sessions collected over nine years by eight experimenters. Every cell was recorded under core "fingerprinting" visual stimuli (full-field chirp and 8-direction moving bar), with subsets exposed to natural movies, dense/shifty binary noise, contrast saturation, spot size, and chromatic flicker stimuli. The authors provide raw and detrended traces, receptive field (RF) estimates with space-time separability ($QI_{\text{RF}}$) and extreme-value-corrected SNR metrics, CASCADE-inferred spike rates, probabilistic assignments to 46 functional GCL groups (32 RGC and 14 displaced amacrine cell groups) via an uncertainty-calibrated Random Forest classifier, retinal topographic validations, and systematic classifier-based audits of technical and biological batch effects. Data are archived in Neurodata Without Borders (`.nwb`) and `.parquet` formats on Zenodo (`10.5281/zenodo.21789286`) and Hugging Face (`eulerlab/all-gcl`), with full preprocessing and classifier code on GitHub.

**Editorial Verdict: Accept with Minor Revisions (Strong / Sound).**  
This is a well-engineered, candid, and highly reusable neuroinformatics resource. The mathematical definitions of response quality ($QI_{\text{chirp}}, QI_{\text{MB}}$), extreme-value size-corrected RF signal-to-noise ratio ($\text{SNR} = z_{\text{peak}} / \sqrt{2 \log(TS)}$), topographic validation against spatial transcriptomic atlases, and date-stratified / leave-one-experimenter-out batch-effect audits are rigorous and transparent. A few minor methodological and editorial items should be clarified to maximize utility for downstream users:
1. **Unflagged Inclusion of SR101-Labeled (Potentially Damaged) Somata via Cellpose 3 Augmentation:** Sulforhodamine-101 (SR101) was included in the ACSF specifically to label damaged GCL cells and blood vessels in the red channel (line 54), and earlier studies excluded SR101-filled somata from manual ROI masks. Because the authors retroactively augmented manual ROI masks with automated Cellpose 3 ROIs (`IoU <= 0.1`) to include SR101-filled cells (line 101), the dataset should explicitly document whether each ROI carries a metadata flag (`roi_source: manual vs. cellpose`) and a quantitative SR101 red-channel fluorescence metric so users can filter out damaged cells independently of $QI$.
2. **Chirp Stimulus Diameter (`800 µm` vs. `1,000 µm`) and Temporal Warping Effects on Classifier Calibration:** The core full-field chirp stimulus had either an $800\ \mu\text{m}$ or $1,000\ \mu\text{m}$ diameter across studies (line 70) and differed in timing from the original reference taxonomy (ref. 7), requiring temporal warping prior to sparse PCA projection (line 130). Because surround suppression in RGCs is sensitive to $800\ \mu\text{m}$ vs. $1,000\ \mu\text{m}$ illumination and temporal warping alters transient kinetics, the authors should clarify whether chirp diameter is stored in the metadata and whether it contributes to the session/experimenter predictability in Fig. 5.
3. **Formatting Duplications in the Methods and Data Record Text:** Four passages in the manuscript repeat bulleted lists verbatim immediately after their inline text (lines 31–45, 72–86, 116–124, and 168–178).

### Assessment of Specific Claims
* **Claim 1 (The `ALL-GCL` dataset provides standardized, quality-controlled functional recordings and RF estimates for $>80,000$ mouse GCL somata in NWB and Parquet formats; Figs. 1–2): [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by clear mathematical definitions of preprocessing, Savitzky-Golay detrending, variance-ratio quality indices (~65% passing $QI_{\text{chirp}} \ge 0.35$ or $QI_{\text{MB}} \ge 0.6$), and Gaussian extreme-value-corrected RF SNR.
* **Claim 2 (Probabilistic classification maps `ALL-GCL` cells onto the 46 reference functional GCL groups with population proportions and topographic density gradients matching established molecular and anatomical atlases; Figs. 3–4): [Well Supported].**  
  The uncertainty-calibrated Random Forest achieves 76.4% held-out accuracy across 75 sub-clusters (chance 1.3%) and reproduces known dorsal Off / ventral On supergroup gradients as well as specific RGC topographies (e.g., G24 On-alpha sustained and G28 On contrast-suppressed).
* **Claim 3 (Biological covariates like sex, eye, scan field order, and cross-experimenter age introduce minimal response bias, whereas recording setup, experimenter, and recording session introduce measurable structure of ~55–70%; Fig. 5): [Well Supported].**  
  Demonstrated using date-stratified train/test splits, downsampled balanced classes (50% chance), and leave-one-experimenter-out validation (which cleanly reveals that apparent age predictability in pooled data is a confounding artifact of experimenter sampling).

## 3. Forensic Defect Inventory

### 3.1 Retroactive Addition of SR101-Filled Cells via Cellpose 3 Without Documented Damage/Source Flags (Pillars 2, 6)
* **Location:** Methods ("Animals and preparation", line 54; "Regions of interest", line 101).
* **Defect:** Line 54 notes that SR101 ($0.1\ \mu\text{M}$) was added to ACSF *"to reveal blood vessels and damaged ganglion cell layer (GCL) cells in the red fluorescence channel"*. Line 101 then explains that while original studies excluded cells with strong SR101 filling from manual IGOR Pro ROI masks due to suspected cell damage/death, the authors ran Cellpose 3 on all fields and added any missing somata (`IoU <= 0.1`) to the ROI masks. While non-responsive dead cells will generally fail the $QI_{\text{chirp}} > 0.35$ / $QI_{\text{MB}} > 0.6$ filter, partially damaged or hyperexcitable cells or astrocytes added by Cellpose 3 may pass or affect population counts (e.g., Fig. 3d cells per field). It is not stated whether the `.nwb` and `.parquet` files store (i) a flag distinguishing original manual ROIs from Cellpose-added ROIs, and (ii) the mean SR101 red-channel intensity per ROI.
* **Remediation:** Clarify in the "Regions of interest" and "Data Record" sections what proportion of the $>80,000$ ROIs were added via Cellpose 3, what fraction of Cellpose-added ROIs pass the $QI$ threshold compared to manual ROIs, and whether ROI source (`manual` vs. `cellpose`) and SR101 channel intensity are included as columns in `all_GCL_table.parquet`.

### 3.2 Confounding of Chirp Stimulus Diameter (`800 µm` vs. `1,000 µm`) and Temporal Warping in Batch-Effect and Classifier Analyses (Pillars 3, 4)
* **Location:** Methods ("Light stimulation", line 70; "Classification of functional ganglion cell layer groups", line 130; "Estimating batch effects", lines 146–154).
* **Defect:**
  1. Line 70 states that the core full-field chirp stimulus had a diameter of *"either 800 µm or 1,000 µm"*, followed two sentences later by *"These core stimuli were identical across all experiments"*. Because RGC surround inhibition extends several hundred micrometers, an $800\ \mu\text{m}$ vs. $1,000\ \mu\text{m}$ spot can differentially recruit surround antagonism in peripheral cells of the $100 \times 100\ \mu\text{m}$ scan field and systematically contribute to the experimenter/session classification accuracy observed in Fig. 5d–e.
  2. Line 130 notes that the chirp timing in ref. 7 differed from the present dataset, so the new traces were temporally warped to match ref. 7 before classification. The exact functional form or piecewise linear mapping of this temporal warping is not specified in the text.
* **Remediation:** Specify in Methods which input studies/experimenters used the $800\ \mu\text{m}$ vs. $1,000\ \mu\text{m}$ chirp diameter (and confirm this is recorded in the NWB metadata), briefly state how temporal warping was parameterized (e.g., piecewise linear interpolation between stimulus phase triggers), and note whether restricting the batch-effect classifier to a single chirp diameter reduces inter-experimenter predictability.

### 3.3 Repeated Text Blocks in Methods and Data Record (Pillar 7)
* **Location:** Methods (lines 31–45, 72–86, 116–124) and Data Record (lines 168–178).
* **Defect:** Four sections contain verbatim duplicated text where an inline list is immediately followed by the exact same items formatted as separate paragraphs:
  - Input studies list (line 31 repeated in lines 33–45).
  - Complementary light stimuli list (line 72 repeated in lines 74–86).
  - GCL classifier input features list (line 116 repeated in lines 118–124).
  - NWB file metadata contents list (line 168 repeated in lines 170–178).
  Additionally, in line 70,72,74,76,84, the diameter symbol is duplicated (`∅ ∅`).
* **Remediation:** Remove the four duplicated list blocks and fix the doubled diameter symbols (`∅ ∅` $\rightarrow$ `∅`).
