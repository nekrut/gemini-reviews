## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript describes a preclinical mouse model of stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS)-induced unilateral hearing loss using a clinical Leksell Gamma Knife (LGK) ICON system with cone-beam CT (CBCT) and MRI/CT atlas co-registration to deliver single-fraction doses of $8\text{ Gy}$ ($n=3$), $16\text{ Gy}$ ($n=5$), $24\text{ Gy}$ ($n=8$), or $32\text{ Gy}$ ($n=15$, with $n=8$ sacrificed at 4 weeks and $n=7$ followed to 16 weeks) to the right cochlea of 6-week-old C57BL/6J mice.

* **Claim 1:** CBCT/atlas-guided LGK ICON targeting with a single $4\text{ mm}$ isocenter aligned to the $80\%$ isodose line delivers focal unilateral radiation to the right cochlea while sparing the animal from systemic/pharyngeal toxicity and limiting the contralateral cochlea to $<15\%$ of the prescribed dose ($1.2\text{--}4.83\text{ Gy}$). `[Well Supported]`
* **Claim 2:** Unilateral SRS induces dose-dependent, predominantly high-frequency ($22.6\text{--}32\text{ kHz}$) auditory brainstem response (ABR) threshold shifts and suprathreshold wave I amplitude reductions by 4 weeks at $24\text{--}32\text{ Gy}$, which progress across all frequencies by 16 weeks at $32\text{ Gy}$ without altering wave I latency. `[Well Supported]`
* **Claim 3:** Histological analyses at 4 and 16 weeks demonstrate dose- and time-dependent basal outer hair cell (OHC) loss, inner hair cell (IHC) ribbon synapse loss (`CtBP2`), spiral ganglion neuron (SGN) density reduction, and mild middle-turn stria vascularis thinning relative to the contralateral control ear. `[Fragile]`
* **Claim 4:** The contralateral cochlea remains unaffected by radiation and serves as a valid internal control that overcomes the early-onset age-related hearing loss (`Cdh23^ahl`) of the C57BL/6J background. `[Overstated]`

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1 Severe Histological Sample Size Attrition ($n = 1\text{--}4$ per Modality) Due to Splitting Cochleae Between Paraffin Sectioning and Whole-Mount Dissection
A critical experimental design bottleneck appears in Section 2.3 (*Cochlear dissection and preparation*, line 62):
> *"After decalcification, both cochleae from each mouse were randomly assigned to one of two downstream applications: (1) paraffin embedding for mid-modiolar sectioning, or (2) whole-mount dissection followed by immunofluorescent analysis of the sensory epithelium."*

Paraffin embedding for mid-modiolar H&E sectioning (used to quantify SGN density and stria vascularis thickness in Fig. 4G–L and Fig. 5F–I) and whole-mount surface preparation (used to quantify IHC/OHC cytocochleograms and CtBP2 ribbon synapses in Fig. 4A–F, Fig. 5A–E, and Supplementary Fig. S2E–L) are **mutually exclusive, destructive histological procedures** on a given cochlea.
1. **Arithmetic consequence on per-group sample sizes:** The total number of mice enrolled per group (lines 9 and 38) was:
   * $8\text{ Gy}$: $n = 3$ mice total
   * $16\text{ Gy}$: $n = 5$ mice total
   * $24\text{ Gy}$: $n = 8$ mice total
   * $32\text{ Gy}$ (4-week cohort): $15 - 7 = 8$ mice total
   * $32\text{ Gy}$ (16-week cohort): $n = 7$ mice total.
   Splitting each cohort between (1) whole-mount confocal immunostaining and (2) paraffin H&E sectioning leaves **only $n = 1$ or $2$ mice in the $8\text{ Gy}$ group, $n = 2$ or $3$ in the $16\text{ Gy}$ group, $n = 4$ in the $24\text{ Gy}$ and $32\text{ Gy}$ (4w) groups, and $n = 3$ or $4$ in the $32\text{ Gy}$ (16w) group** for each histological assay!
2. **Invalidity of two-way repeated-measures ANOVA on $n = 1\text{--}3$ animals:** In line 126, the authors report statistically significant OHC loss at $45.2\text{ kHz}$ in the $8\text{ Gy}$ group ($p = 0.01, 95\%\text{ CI}: 1.2\text{ to }7.3$) and $16\text{ Gy}$ group ($p = 0.003$) using two-way repeated-measures ANOVA with Bonferroni post-hoc tests. With only $n = 3$ total mice in the $8\text{ Gy}$ group split across two histological protocols, at most $n = 2$ (or $3$ if no $8\text{ Gy}$ mice were sectioned for H&E) paired ears were analyzed by whole-mount cytocochleogram. Furthermore, the exact number of cochleae ($n$) included in each histological panel of Figs. 4, 5, and S2 is never disclosed in the text or captions.
3. **Ambiguity in randomization of paired ears:** The sentence *"both cochleae from each mouse were randomly assigned to one of two downstream applications"* must be clarified to confirm that the left and right cochleae of the *same mouse* were always assigned together to the *same* downstream application (since pairing irradiated vs. contralateral ears within animals in a repeated-measures ANOVA is impossible if one ear of a mouse was paraffin-embedded and its contralateral ear was whole-mounted).

### 2.2 Direct Numerical Contradiction in OHC Loss Between the Abstract and Results, Driven by `Cdh23^ahl` Confounding in C57BL/6J Mice
Comparing the Abstract (line 11) and the Results (lines 126–128) reveals a major numerical discrepancy in reported OHC loss for the $32\text{ Gy}$ group:
* **Abstract (line 11):** *"At 32 Gy, outer hair cells were reduced by **14% and 44%** at 32 and 45.2 kHz, respectively, at 4 weeks, and by **38% and 80%** at 16 weeks."*
* **Results (lines 126–128):**
  * At 4 weeks: *"OHCs at 45.2 kHz were reduced by **40.1** [percentage points] in the 32 Gy group... A significant OHC loss was also observed at 32 kHz in the 32 Gy group, with a reduction of **13.3 percentage points** compared to the contralateral cochlea."*
  * At 16 weeks: *"We also observed an OHC reduction by **48.5 percentage points** at 45.2 kHz ($p = 0.0003$) and **33 percentage points** at 32 kHz ($p = 0.005$) relative to the contralateral cochlea."*

Why does a $48.5\text{ percentage-point}$ interaural difference at $45.2\text{ kHz}$ at 16 weeks become an **$80\%$ reduction** in the Abstract?
* Because C57BL/6J mice carry the hypomorphic `Cdh23^ahl` allele, which causes severe, progressive high-frequency OHC degeneration and hearing loss starting at 12 weeks of age. By 16 weeks post-SRS (when mice are **22 weeks old**), the **unirradiated contralateral ear has already lost $\sim 40\%$ of its own OHCs at $45.2\text{ kHz}$** (leaving only $\sim 60\%$ OHC survival in the control ear, so $(60\% - 11.5\%)/60\% \approx 80\%$).
* This exposes two serious issues: (1) the manuscript conflates relative percentage reductions with absolute percentage-point differences between the Abstract and Results, and (2) by 22 weeks of age in C57BL/6J mice, severe baseline degeneration in the contralateral ear compresses the dynamic range at high frequencies ($\ge 32\text{ kHz}$) and creates a ceiling/floor effect where radiation injury interacts non-linearly with `Cdh23^ahl` pathology.

### 2.3 Non-Negligible Radiation Dose ($4.83\text{ Gy}$) to the Contralateral "Internal Control" Ear Without Sham-Irradiated Controls
In the $32\text{ Gy}$ group, the contralateral cochlea received a mean dose of **$4.83 \pm 0.44\text{ Gy}$** (line 105).
* A single-fraction dose of $\sim 5\text{ Gy}$ is more than $60\%$ of the $8\text{ Gy}$ target dose, where the authors already detected a statistically significant $4.2\text{ percentage-point}$ OHC loss at $45.2\text{ kHz}$ at 4 weeks (line 126).
* Because no sham-irradiated (0 Gy) age-matched C57BL/6J control group was included at 4 or 16 weeks, the study cannot rule out that $4.83\text{ Gy}$ accelerated subclinical synaptopathy, SGN loss, or `Cdh23^ahl`-mediated high-frequency threshold shifts in the contralateral "control" ear—which would cause interaural comparisons to underestimate bilateral radiotoxicity.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Mismatch between highest ABR frequency ($32\text{ kHz}$) and histological place-frequency map ($45.2\text{ kHz}$):** ABR tone bursts were tested up to $32\text{ kHz}$ (line 51), whereas the primary histological OHC and ribbon synapse effects in all dose groups were quantified at the $45.2\text{ kHz}$ cochlear place (lines 126–135). In the 8, 16, and 24 Gy groups, statistically significant OHC loss was detected *only* at $45.2\text{ kHz}$ (where ABR was not measured), while at $\le 32\text{ kHz}$ neither OHC loss nor ribbon loss was significant at $\le 24\text{ Gy}$.
2. **Unblinded visual ABR threshold determination and manual cell counting:** ABR thresholds were determined as *"the lowest sound intensity at which a consistent and visually detectable wave pattern was observed"* (line 51), and IHC/OHC and CtBP2 puncta were manually counted in Fiji (lines 86–88). The Methods do not state whether the investigators scoring ABR thresholds or counting hair cells/ribbons were blinded to treatment side (irradiated vs. contralateral) and dose group.
3. **Absence of a Data Availability Statement:** The manuscript contains no Data Availability section or repository link for the raw ABR waveforms, threshold tables, dosimetry logs, or histological counts.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

1. **Explicitly report exact sample sizes ($n$) for every histological dataset and justify parametric tests:**
   * State the exact number of animals ($n$) allocated to whole-mount cytocochleograms/synapse counts vs. paraffin H&E SGN/stria vascularis measurements in each dose group ($8, 16, 24, 32\text{ Gy}$ at 4w and 16w) in the Methods and every figure legend.
   * Confirm that paired left and right cochleae from each mouse were processed by the same histological protocol. For any group with $n < 5$ animals in a histological modality, replace parametric two-way ANOVA with non-parametric permutation / exact tests or report descriptive effect sizes without over-interpreting small-$n$ p-values.
2. **Harmonize percentage vs. percentage-point reporting between Abstract and Results:** Revise the Abstract (line 11) so that OHC loss values match the Results section (explicitly distinguishing percentage-point interaural differences from relative percentage reductions), and report the absolute baseline OHC survival and ABR thresholds of the contralateral ear at 4 and 16 weeks to transparently show the magnitude of `Cdh23^ahl` progression.
3. **Compare contralateral $4.83\text{ Gy}$ ears against baseline/sham C57BL/6J reference data:** Compare the 4-week and 16-week contralateral ABR thresholds and hair cell counts across the four dose groups (where contralateral dose scales from $1.2\text{ Gy}$ to $4.83\text{ Gy}$) to test whether contralateral cochlear outcomes correlate with scatter dose, and recommend CBA/CaJ or `Cdh23`-corrected C57BL/6J strains for long-term ($\ge 16\text{ week}$) studies.
4. **Add blinding details and deposit source data:** Clarify whether ABR and histological evaluations were performed blinded to ear side and radiation dose, and provide a supplementary dataset or public repository containing the individual-animal ABR thresholds, wave I amplitudes, dosimetry metrics, and histological counts.
