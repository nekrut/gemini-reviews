## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This comparative immunogenomics manuscript investigates the conservation and inducibility of innate immune pathways in three wild-derived European drosophilid species that diverged from *Drosophila melanogaster* $>45\text{ Mya}$: *Hirtodrosophila cameraria*, *H. confusa*, and *Scaptodrosophila deflexa*. Using first-generation ($F_1$) lab-reared progeny of wild-caught females (derived from $n = 3$ wild *H. cameraria* mothers, $n = 2$ *H. confusa* mothers, and $n = 1$ *S. deflexa* mother), the authors challenge 5 flies per species by thoracic septic pin-prick with the Gram-negative pathogen *Providencia rettgeri* ($\text{OD}_{600} = 0.1$) alongside 5 unmanipulated (non-wounded) control flies, harvesting whole-fly RNA at 16 hours post-infection (~58.35M paired-end reads per fly). Using `BRAKER3` and `OrthoFinder` across challenged, unchallenged, and combined RNA-seq datasets, they show that pathogen challenge does not meaningfully improve genome-wide or immune gene annotation recovery, that core signaling genes are strongly conserved across all three species whereas antimicrobial peptide (AMP) effectors undergo rapid turnover (notably *S. deflexa* lacks detectable *DptA*, *DptC*, *AttC*, and *AttD* orthologs), and that *H. cameraria* (363 upregulated genes) and *H. confusa* (149 upregulated genes) mount strong canonical Imd/AMP transcriptional responses whereas *S. deflexa* mounts almost none (34 upregulated genes, 0 AMPs). Metatranscriptomic assembly of unmapped reads reveals high *Providencia* replication in *H. cameraria*, lower *Providencia* in *H. confusa*, and universal colonization of *S. deflexa* by the defensive endosymbionts *Spiroplasma* and *Wolbachia*. Finally, screening short ($\le 200\text{ aa}$), secreted, infection-induced orphan genes with `AMP Scanner v2`, `amPEPpy`, `SignalP 6.0`, and `AlphaFold 3` identifies 20 novel candidate AMPs (14 strong, 6 likely).

The study is thoughtfully framed, candidly acknowledges several experimental constraints (such as the absence of a sterile-wound control), and integrates comparative genomics, host transcriptomics, metatranscriptomics, and structural AMP prediction effectively. However, the study has four substantive methodological and reporting limitations: (1) all *S. deflexa* individuals came from a single wild-caught mother ($n = 1$ isofemale brood) and included a sex mismatch (4 females and 1 male in the challenged group vs. females in the other species), confounding species-level conclusions with single-genotype / vertical-endosymbiont effects; (2) the control flies were unmanipulated rather than sterile-PBS-pricked, confounding septic infection with thoracic cuticle wounding and melanization; (3) the metatranscriptomic alpha-diversity analysis (`rnaSPAdes` contig assembly + `getorf` $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ + `diamond blastp` on poly(A)-selected or total RNA-seq unmapped reads) suffers from severe coverage-dependent assembly bias; and (4) the manuscript lacks a Data Availability section and accession numbers for the 30 newly generated RNA-seq libraries and the unpublished *S. deflexa* genome assembly.

### Assessment of Specific Conclusions
* **Pathogen-challenged RNA-seq does not substantially improve `BRAKER3` annotation of immune genes compared to unchallenged RNA-seq: [Well Supported].**  
  Systematically tested by running `BRAKER3` (in ETP mode with *D. melanogaster* RefSeq proteins) independently on challenged, unchallenged, and combined libraries across all three species and comparing `OrthoFinder` HOG recovery (Table 1, Fig. 2).
* **Core immune signaling pathways are deeply conserved across Drosophilidae while AMP effector families (*Diptericin*, *Attacin*, *Drosomycin*, *Turandot*) exhibit high lineage-specific turnover and loss in *S. deflexa*: [Well Supported].**  
  Supported by `OrthoFinder` HOG mapping across 638 curated *D. melanogaster* immune genes, `IQ-TREE2` gene trees for *Diptericin* and *Attacin*, and the `MCMCglmm` binomial mixed model confirming higher retention of signaling genes vs. effectors.
* ***H. cameraria* and *H. confusa* mount strong canonical Imd transcriptional responses to *P. rettgeri* challenge, whereas *S. deflexa* shows a muted response potentially linked to *Spiroplasma* colonization and low *Providencia* load: [Moderately Supported].**  
  The transcriptomic contrast is stark and biologically plausible, but because all 10 *S. deflexa* flies were $F_1$ offspring of a **single wild-caught female** ($n = 1$ maternal lineage, which vertically transmits *Spiroplasma* and *Wolbachia* to 100% of her brood!) and the challenged group mixed 4 females and 1 male (line 106), the study cannot separate a species-wide trait of *S. deflexa* from the peculiarities of a single *Spiroplasma*-infected maternal brood.
* **Bacterial challenge with *P. rettgeri* significantly increases gut microbiome alpha diversity in *H. cameraria* (Wilcoxon $p = 0.01$, Fig. 5C): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In lines 69 and 128, microbial abundance was profiled by assembling unmapped host RNA-seq reads per sample with `rnaSPAdes`, predicting ORFs $\ge 200\text{ aa}$, running `diamond blastp`, and computing the Shannon index on genus-level mapped read counts. Because *de novo* assembly of unmapped RNA-seq reads only assembles contigs ($\ge 600\text{ nt}$ for a $200\text{ aa}$ ORF!) when a microbe's transcript coverage exceeds the `rnaSPAdes` $k$-mer threshold, any increase in bacterial proliferation or host-unmapped read fraction (which was 33.45% in *H. cameraria* vs. 16–18% in the other species, line 111!) pushes low-abundance resident gut taxa over the contig-assembly threshold, artificially inflating assembled genus richness and Shannon diversity.
* **Identification of 20 novel secreted AMP-like candidates (14 strong, 6 likely) induced upon septic challenge: [Moderately Supported].**  
  The bioinformatic triage (differential expression + lack of *D. melanogaster* orthology + length $\le 200\text{ aa}$ + `SignalP 6.0` signal peptide + `AMP Scanner v2` / `amPEPpy` + cationic charge + `AlphaFold 3` structure) is well-executed, though because controls were unpricked (unwounded), some induced short secreted peptides (e.g., glycine/proline-rich peptides) may be cuticle-repair or wound-healing peptides rather than bactericidal AMPs.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1. Coverage-Dependent Assembly Threshold Bias in Metatranscriptomic Alpha Diversity (Lines 69, 128, Fig. 5C) [Methodologically Flawed]
To profile the microbiome, the authors extracted unmapped RNA-seq reads from each individual fly, ran *de novo* transcriptome assembly (`rnaSPAdes`) separately on each library ("assembled de novo... for each library", line 69), filtered for predicted proteins $\ge 200\text{ amino acids}$ (`EMBOSS getorf`, line 128), assigned taxonomy via `diamond blastp`, and calculated genus-level Shannon diversity (`vegan`/`phyloseq`).
This pipeline has two severe biases for measuring alpha diversity:
1. **Per-sample *de novo* assembly threshold:** Taxa present at low transcriptional abundance fail to assemble into contigs $\ge 600\text{ bp}$ ($\ge 200\text{ aa}$). In *H. cameraria* challenged flies, where unmapped reads are higher (average 33.45% unmapped in *H. cameraria* vs. 16.02% in *H. confusa*, line 111) and *Providencia* proliferates (including cross-mapping of conserved *Enterobacterales* housekeeping proteins across *Providencia*, *Klebsiella*, *Escherichia*, *Citrobacter*, and *Salmonella*—note that all five of these genera are closely related *Enterobacteriaceae*/*Morganellaceae* whose conserved ribosomal/chaperone proteins $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ frequently split hits in `diamond blastp`!), per-sample assembly recovers more *Enterobacterales* contigs and inflates apparent genus richness.
2. **Exclusion of rRNA and short bacterial transcripts:** Filtering for ORFs $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ discards 16S/23S rRNA (which dominates bacterial total RNA) and all short bacterial transcripts (<600 bp). Direct taxonomic classification of unmapped reads (or co-assembled contigs across all samples) using `Kraken2`/`Bracken` or `MetaPhlAn4`/`Sylph` with rarefaction to equal microbial read depth is required before comparing Shannon diversity.

### 2.2. Single Maternal Brood ($N = 1$ Wild Female) and Sex Admixture in *Scaptodrosophila deflexa* (Line 106) [Fragile]
In Methods (line 106), the authors report that all *S. deflexa* experimental flies were $F_1$ progeny of a **single wild-caught female (`n = 1`)**, and that due to limited $F_1$ emergence, the 5 challenged *S. deflexa* flies consisted of **four females and one male** ("Five females per species (four females and one male for *S. deflexa*) were challenged in this way").
1. Including a single male in one treatment group of $n = 5$ in whole-fly RNA-seq introduces massive sex-specific transcriptional variance (testis/accessory gland vs. ovary transcripts, representing thousands of sex-biased genes in *Drosophila*), which directly explains why the *S. deflexa* PCA (Fig. 4A) shows high inter-individual dispersion and no treatment separation along PC1, and why `DESeq2` (`~ treatment`, line 121) has reduced statistical power due to inflated within-group dispersion!
2. Because *Spiroplasma* and *Wolbachia* are maternally transmitted cytoplasmic endosymbionts, sampling 10 $F_1$ siblings from $n = 1$ wild mother means the 100% *Spiroplasma* prevalence in *S. deflexa* represents a single maternal infection event ($N = 1$ independent biological unit for endosymbiont status), not a species-level population estimate.

### 2.3. Absence of a Sterile-Wound (Mock-Prick) Control Group (Line 106) [Moderately Supported]
As the authors transparently note on line 106 ("Control flies were anesthetized but otherwise left unmanipulated, and thus this experiment did not control for wounding effects"), thoracic pin-pricking physically breaches the cuticle and triggers hemocyte activation, phenoloxidase/melanization cascades, and wound-repair epidermal transcription. Consequently, upregulated genes (and candidate novel AMPs) reflect the combined response to **thoracic wounding + *P. rettgeri* infection**, not bacterial recognition alone.

### 2.4. Missing Data Availability Section and Sequencing Accession Numbers [Methodologically Flawed]
The manuscript contains no Data Availability section and provides no NCBI SRA / ENA BioProject accession numbers for the 30 newly generated RNA-seq libraries (10 flies $\times$ 3 species), nor does it provide a public link, Zenodo archive, or accession number for the unpublished *Scaptodrosophila deflexa* genome assembly (line 111) or the `BRAKER3` GFF3/FASTA annotation files.

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

### 1. Re-Run *S. deflexa* Differential Expression and PCA Excluding the Single Male Sample (or Including `~ sex + treatment`)
* **WHY:** Including 1 male alongside 4 females in the challenged *S. deflexa* group (while unchallenged flies are female) inflates within-group gene expression dispersion across thousands of sex-biased gonad/somatic genes in `DESeq2`, suppressing power to detect immune induction and distorting the PCA (Fig. 4A).
* **WHAT:** Re-run the *S. deflexa* `DESeq2` analysis and `plotPCA()` restricting exclusively to the female samples (4 challenged females vs. 5 unchallenged females).
* **HOW:** Drop the single male library from the *S. deflexa* `featureCounts` matrix, re-estimate `DESeq2` dispersions and log$_2$ fold changes on the female-only dataset, and report in Results (line 54) whether removing male sex-bias variance uncovers additional induced immune genes in *S. deflexa*.

### 2. Control for Per-Sample Assembly Bias and *Enterobacterales* Cross-Mapping in the Microbiome Analysis
* **WHY:** Assembling unmapped reads per library (`rnaSPAdes`) and filtering for ORFs $\ge 200\text{ aa}$ causes samples with high *Providencia* load (and conserved *Enterobacterales* proteins splitting hits across *Escherichia*, *Klebsiella*, *Salmonella*, and *Citrobacter*) to assemble more contigs and exhibit artificially higher genus Shannon diversity (Fig. 5C).
* **WHAT:** Re-compute microbiome alpha diversity after (a) mapping unmapped reads against a single pooled co-assembly (or using `Kraken2`/`Sylph`), and (b) excluding *Providencia* and conserved *Enterobacterales* cross-mapped contigs.
* **HOW:** Co-assemble unmapped reads across all 10 libraries per species (so contig recovery does not depend on single-sample coverage), map each library's reads back to the co-assembled ORFs, and recalculate Shannon diversity both with and without the inoculated pathogen (*Providencia* / *Enterobacterales*) after subsampling microbial reads to equal depth.

### 3. Deposit All 30 RNA-seq Libraries, Annotations, and the *S. deflexa* Genome Assembly
* **WHY:** Reproducibility and reuse of the 20 novel candidate AMPs and comparative annotations require public access to the raw reads, genome assembly, and gene models.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Deposit the 30 raw FASTQ files in NCBI SRA/ENA, archive the *S. deflexa* genome assembly, `BRAKER3` GFF3/protein FASTA files for all three species, and `OrthoFinder` / `IQ-TREE2` outputs on Zenodo, and add a formal Data Availability section with all accession numbers. Fix the typographical errors on lines 30, 32, 45, 58, 91, and 111.
