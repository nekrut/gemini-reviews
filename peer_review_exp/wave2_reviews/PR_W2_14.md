## 1. Summary of Claims & Epistemic Architecture

This manuscript reports a hybrid genome assembly and gene annotation for *Micractinium conductrix* strain 186b, the facultative green algal endosymbiont of the ciliate *Paramecium bursaria* 186b. Combining PacBio HiFi (168× mean coverage), Oxford Nanopore R10.4.1 (59×), and Illumina NovaSeq (60×) reads, alongside Illumina short-read RNA-seq (three light, three dark replicates) and PacBio Revio Iso-Seq (two light replicates), the authors generate a 62.57 Mbp nuclear assembly across 30 contigs (N50 = 3.83 Mbp, L50 = 6, GC = 67.36%). Telomere analysis identifies 9 contigs with telomeric repeats (`CCCTAAA`$_n$) at both ends and 14 contigs with a telomere at one end, from which the authors infer a haploid chromosome count of $n = 9 + 14/2 = 16$. Using `MitoHiFi`, they also assemble a complete 75,292 bp circular mitochondrial genome and a partial 56,167 bp chloroplast genome. Finally, comparing `BRAKER` gene models generated with RNA-seq (12,545 genes) vs. Iso-Seq (11,628 genes) against *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 (99.87% FastANI, 9,217 predicted genes) using `fdfBLAST`, the authors show that estimated gene fusion rates vary from 5.6% (186b RNA-seq) to 9.8% (186b Iso-Seq) to 21.5% (SAG 241.80), arguing that false ORF fusions are a widespread annotation artifact in Chlorellaceae.

While the multi-platform sequencing resource for *M. conductrix* 186b is valuable for experimental evolution studies of *P. bursaria*–*M. conductrix* endosymbiosis, the assembly and polishing pipeline contains several technical flaws: polishing a high-accuracy PacBio HiFi (`hifiasm`) assembly with raw Nanopore reads via `Medaka` introduces ONT systematic homopolymer/indel errors into HiFi consensus sequence; the 10.7 Mbp discrepancy between the $k$-mer genome size estimate (51.9 Mbp) and assembly size (62.6 Mbp) is misattributed to three tiny low-coverage contigs totaling <315 Kbp; the chloroplast genome is <46% complete despite 168× HiFi coverage; and the manuscript lacks any Data Availability Statement or public accession numbers for the assemblies and raw reads.

### Assessment of Specific Conclusions
* **High genetic identity (99.87% FastANI) between *M. conductrix* 186b and *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 contrasted with large disparities in predicted gene counts (12,545 vs. 9,217) driven by ORF fusion artifacts: [Well Supported].**  
  Comparing annotations on nearly identical genome sequences (`FastANI` = 99.87%) alongside `fdfBLAST` all-vs-all split/fusion analysis cleanly demonstrates that differences in gene count and mean gene length (3,696 bp vs. 5,247 bp) are dominated by annotation methodology and isoform merging rather than true evolutionary divergence.
* **Near telomere-to-telomere nuclear assembly of *M. conductrix* 186b with 16 chromosomes: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Nine telomere-to-telomere contigs and 14 single-telomere contigs support an estimated chromosome number of 16, though without Hi-C scaffolding or genetic linkage mapping, pairing the 14 single-telomere arms into 7 chromosomes remains an unverified parsimony assumption, and `OMArk` proteome completeness is only 80.2%.
* **The ~10.7 Mbp discrepancy between the `LVgs` $k$-mer genome size estimate (51.9 Mbp) and assembled genome size (62.57 Mbp) is explained by variant-coverage accessory contigs: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In line 24, the authors state that the eight short contigs (<100 Kbp, three with low coverage) "make up only ~315 Kbp of the final assembly," yet two sentences later claim "The inclusion of these variant contigs may lead to variant bioinformatic completion estimates, as seen in our LVgs analyses compared to the total assembly." First, `LVgs` is run on raw HiFi reads (line 104), not assembled contigs; second, 315 Kbp (0.315 Mbp) cannot mathematically account for a 10.67 Mbp discrepancy ($62.57 - 51.9 = 10.67\text{ Mbp}$).
* **Complete mitochondrial genome (75.3 Kbp) and chloroplast genome assembly: [Fragile].**  
  The mitochondrial assembly (75,292 bp, 60 ORFs) is complete and matches reference `KY629619.1` (74,708 bp), but the chloroplast assembly (56,167 bp, 67 ORFs) is less than half the length of the reference *Micractinium* plastome (123,552 bp, 114 genes), likely because aggressive GC filtering in `BlobTools` (~30% and ~34% GC cutoffs, line 94) or `MitoHiFi` parameter settings discarded the inverted repeat or higher-GC plastid regions.

## 2. Major Methodological & Statistical Flaws

### 2.1. Polishing a PacBio HiFi (`hifiasm`) Assembly with Nanopore Reads via `Medaka` Degrades Consensus Quality [Methodologically Flawed]
In Materials & Methods (line 100), the authors state that the `hifiasm` PacBio HiFi assembly (168× coverage, where HiFi consensus accuracy routinely exceeds QV50 / 99.999%) "was polished using MEDAKA... for one round, incorporating the Oxford Nanopore Technology reads, and one round of polishing using PILON incorporating the Illumina reads." It is a well-established principle of long-read genome assembly that polishing a 168× PacBio HiFi assembly with lower-accuracy ONT reads via `Medaka` overwrites accurate HiFi bases with ONT systematic basecalling/homopolymer errors, and subsequent short-read `Pilon` polishing introduces short-read mapping biases in repetitive regions (21.5% repeats) and high-GC regions (67.36% GC). No `Merqury` $k$-mer QV assessment was performed before and after `Medaka`/`Pilon` polishing to check whether polishing degraded consensus accuracy and caused frame-shift split ORFs.

### 2.2. Unexplained 10.7 Mbp Discrepancy Between $k$-mer Genome Size (51.9 Mbp) and Assembly Size (62.57 Mbp) [Methodologically Flawed]
The nuclear assembly is 62.57 Mbp across 30 contigs, whereas `LVgs` ($k = 27$ on HiFi reads) estimates a haploid genome size of 51.9 Mbp (lines 24, 104)—an excess of **10.67 Mbp (>20% of the genome)**. As noted above, the eight short contigs (<100 Kbp) sum to only ~315 Kbp and cannot explain a 10.7 Mbp excess. Furthermore, the initial unfiltered `hifiasm` assembly was 101.15 Mbp across 1,085 contigs (line 94). When an algal culture is derived from a sonicated *Paramecium bursaria* lysate (which harbors hundreds of algal cells per host cell, line 12, 66) or has unpurged haplotigs/segmental duplications or residual bacterial co-isolates with ~65% GC (many *Alphaproteobacteria* / *Actinomycetota* / *Pseudomonadota* have 65–68% GC!), a 10.7 Mbp excess must be audited using `purge_dups`, a `Merqury` copy-number spectrum plot, and taxonomic assignment in `BlobToolKit`.

### 2.3. Incomplete Chloroplast Genome (56.2 Kbp vs. 123.6 Kbp Reference) [Fragile]
Despite 168× PacBio HiFi, 59× ONT, and 60× Illumina coverage of a photosynthetic green alga, the chloroplast genome recovered by `MitoHiFi` is only 56,167 bp (67 genes) compared to 123,552 bp (114 genes) in *Micractinium simplicissimum* (line 44) and ~129 Kbp in *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80. In line 94, the authors partitioned reads by extracting HiFi reads mapping to `BlobTools` scaffolds filtered by GC cutoffs (~30% and ~34% GC). If parts of the plastome (such as the rRNA operon in the inverted repeat, which typically has >45–50% GC) had higher GC content or failed to bin into the ~34% GC blob, those reads were excluded prior to running `MitoHiFi`.

### 2.4. Complete Absence of Data Availability Statement and Accession Numbers [Methodologically Flawed]
The manuscript contains no Data Availability section, no BioProject/SRA/ENA accession numbers for the PacBio HiFi, ONT, Illumina WGS, RNA-seq, or Iso-Seq datasets, and no GenBank/ENA/Zenodo accessions for the nuclear, mitochondrial, and plastid assemblies or `BRAKER` gene annotations.

## 3. Minor / Technical Issues

1. **Unresolved Placeholder Citation in Main Text:** Line 49 contains the literal placeholder `(REF)` ("compared our RNA-seq and Iso-Seq informed results with the publicly available gene repertoire for *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 (REF)").
2. **Missing Numerical Value in Growth Protocol:** Line 66 reads: "and at 25°C with $\mu\text{E m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$ of light at a 14:10 light: dark cycle for growing biomass"—the numeric light intensity value before $\mu\text{E m}^{-2}\text{ s}^{-1}$ was accidentally deleted.
3. **Inconsistent Mean Gene Length Reported in Adjacent Sections:** Line 37 reports a mean ($\bar{x}$) gene length of **3,688 bp** for the 12,545 RNA-seq predicted genes, whereas line 49 reports a mean gene length of **3,696 bp** for the exact same 12,545 RNA-seq predicted genes. Similarly, assembly size is reported as 62.5 Mbp (line 24), 62,572,437 bp (line 96), and 62.7 Mbp (line 104).
4. **Wide Unexplained Range of BUSCO Scores:** Line 28 states "BUSCO (Tegenfeldt, et al. 2025) analyses of single copy genes demonstrated that the *M. conductrix* 186b assembly ranged from 92.5% to 99.1% completion," without specifying in the text which lineage dataset (`eukaryota_odb10`, `viridiplantae_odb10`, or `chlorophyta_odb10`) corresponds to which percentage, nor why `OMArk` completeness is substantially lower (80.2%).

## 4. Constructive Remediation Plan

### 1. Deposit All Raw Reads, Assemblies, and Annotations and Provide Accession Numbers
* **WHY:** A genome resource paper cannot be evaluated or reused without public access to the sequence data and annotations.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Deposit the PacBio HiFi, ONT, Illumina WGS, RNA-seq, and Iso-Seq reads in NCBI SRA / ENA, deposit the nuclear, mitochondrial, and chloroplast assemblies in GenBank/ENA, and upload the GFF3/FASTA gene prediction sets (`RNA-seq` and `Iso-Seq` BRAKER models) and `fdfBLAST` outputs to Zenodo. Add a formal Data Availability section listing all accessions.

### 2. Evaluate Assembly Consensus Quality (QV) Without `Medaka`/`Pilon` Polishing and Audit the 10.7 Mbp Size Discrepancy
* **WHY:** Polishing 168× PacBio HiFi contigs with ONT `Medaka` degrades base accuracy and can introduce artificial frameshifts that split genes (which would also inflate the apparent number of predicted genes relative to SAG 241.80!). Meanwhile, the 10.7 Mbp difference between `LVgs` (51.9 Mbp) and the assembly (62.6 Mbp) requires explanation.
* **WHAT:** Run `Merqury` using the Illumina and HiFi $k$-mers on the unpolished `hifiasm` assembly vs. the `Medaka`+`Pilon` polished assembly, and inspect the copy-number spectrum and contig alignments to SAG 241.80.
* **HOW:**
  1. Compute `Merqury` QV and frameshift/BUSCO metrics on the direct `hifiasm` output; if QV is higher without `Medaka`, retain the native HiFi consensus.
  2. Align the 30 contigs of *M. conductrix* 186b (62.57 Mbp) against *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 using `minimap2 -ax asm5` / `Mummer4` to determine whether the extra ~10 Mbp relative to `LVgs` represents repetitive regions collapsed in $k$-mer counting, unpurged haplotigs/duplications, or non-algal contigs.

### 3. Re-Assemble the Complete Chloroplast Genome from Unfiltered HiFi Reads
* **WHY:** The current 56.2 Kbp chloroplast contig is missing >54% of the plastome because reads were pre-filtered by GC cutoffs in `BlobTools` before running `MitoHiFi`.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Run `MitoHiFi` (or `GetOrganelle` / `Oatk` with plastid HMMs) directly on the **unfiltered** PacBio HiFi read set using *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 (`NC_037245` / `KY629620`) and *M. simplicissimum* (`NC_071969.1`) plastomes as references to recover the full ~124–129 Kbp circular chloroplast genome.

### 4. Fix Editorial Placeholders and Numerical Inconsistencies
* **WHY:** Placeholders and conflicting summary statistics undermine manuscript quality.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Replace `(REF)` on line 49 with the citation for *M. conductrix* SAG 241.80 (Arriola et al. 2018), fill in the missing light intensity number on line 66, harmonize the mean gene length (3,688 bp vs. 3,696 bp) and assembly size (62.57 Mbp vs. 62.7 Mbp), and explicitly label the BUSCO percentages by database lineage (`eukaryota`, `viridiplantae`, `chlorophyta`) on line 28.
