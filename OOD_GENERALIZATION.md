# Out-of-Domain Generalization Study ($N = 60$ Wild Manuscripts Across 3 Untested Adjacent Fields)

## 1. Study Design & Two-Stage Adaptive Review Protocol
To test whether the high methodological failure rates observed in our original 5-domain wild sample ($N = 100$: Molecular Evolution, Viral Evolution, Statistical Genetics, Cancer & Microbiome, and Transcriptomics/CompBio ML) generalize to adjacent quantitative fields that were **not** part of our calibration benchmark, we drew a stratified random sample of **$N = 60$ Open Access research articles (`2018–2025`, $20$ per domain)** from Europe PMC (`seed = 20261028`) across three untested domains:

1. **Systems & Cognitive Neuroscience (`Neuroscience & Neuroimaging`, $n = 20$):** Resting-state and task fMRI functional connectivity, EEG/fNIRS biomarkers, graph-theoretic brain network topology, and neural decoding.
2. **Quantitative Ecology, Macroevolution & Climate Change Biology (`Ecology & Macroevolution`, $n = 20$):** Species distribution modeling (`MaxEnt`, `Biomod2`), climate-change range shifts, plant–pollinator phenological mismatch, and macroecological population dynamics.
3. **Structural Bioinformatics, Chemical Biology & AI Drug Discovery (`Structural Bioinfo & AI Drug Discovery`, $n = 20$):** Graph neural network (GNN) and quantum-kernel drug–target interaction (`DTI`) / binding affinity prediction, structure-based virtual screening, molecular docking, QSAR, and molecular dynamics (`MD`) simulations.

### Two-Stage Adaptive Blinded Audit (`88` Total Reviews)
Following the protocol validated in [`REPEATABILITY.md`](REPEATABILITY.md) ($\text{ICC}(2,1) = 0.967$, $\text{ICC}(2,3) = 0.989$):
* **Stage 1 ($N = 60$ Blinded Reviews):** All 60 manuscripts were stripped of titles, author names, affiliations, venues, publication years, DOIs, and PMCIDs (`OOD_ANON_01`–`OOD_ANON_60`, shuffled across all 3 domains) and audited by 6 parallel isolated `anon-reviewer` subagents.
* **Stage 2 ($14 \times 2 = 28$ Independent Re-Anonymized Replicate Reviews):** The **14 manuscripts (`23.3%`)** whose Stage 1 scores fell inside the borderline zone (`5.2 <= S <= 6.8`) were independently re-shuffled into two fresh waves (`OOD_REP2_01`–`OOD_REP2_14` and `OOD_REP3_01`–`OOD_REP3_14`) and audited by 4 isolated `anon-reviewer` subagents. Their final adjudicated scores are the **3-run ensemble means**.

## 3. Domain-Specific Forensic Taxonomy in the 3 New Fields

### A. Systems & Cognitive Neuroscience (`Mean = 4.88`, `50% < 5.0`, `75% < 6.0`)
* **Exemplary Benchmarks (`9.10 / 10`):**
  * `D03` (`PMC12240839`, *Nat Methods* 2025, `9.10`): Comprehensive benchmark of 239 pairwise interaction statistics across $N=326$ HCP participants and 6 replication cohorts with open code.
  * `D05` (`PMC11513045`, *Commun Biol* 2024, `9.10`): 100-node Wilson-Cowan neural mass + Balloon-Windkessel hemodynamic benchmark proving that correlational PPI fails to isolate task modulation and deconvolution + FIR regression eliminates co-activation false positives.
* **Recurrent Failure Modes in the Lower Tail:**
  1. **Circular ROI Selection ("Double-Dipping" / Voodoo Correlations):** `D06` (`PMC11231604`, *Neuroimage Clin* 2024, `4.40`), `D09` (`PMC12638409`, *Eur Arch Psychiatry Clin Neurosci* 2025, `5.10`), `D12` (`PMC11347390`, *CNS Neurosci Ther* 2024, `3.40`), `D13` (`PMC12134841`, *Psychophysiology* 2025, `4.50`), and `D04` (`PMC11157927`, *BMC Psychiatry* 2024, `5.83`) select clusters or electrodes using a whole-brain group contrast ($t$-test/MVPA) and then run secondary seed-to-voxel, symptom correlation, or ROC classification tests (`AUC = 1.000` in `D12`) on those exact same clusters in the exact same participants.
  2. **Severe Experimental & Table Fabrication / Copy-Paste Defects:**
     * `D18` (`PMC11589569`, *Sci Rep* 2024, `2.80`): Administers the **1-month retrospective PSQI sleep quality questionnaire before and immediately after an 11-minute awake sitting TENS session** ($p < 0.001$), confounds "resting-state" fNIRS with active 150 Hz electrical stimulation without a sham group, and assigns identical MNI coordinates `(32.2, 64.13, 41.15)` to both BA6 premotor cortex and BA9 DLPFC in Table 1.
     * `D16` (`PMC12714814`, *Brain Topogr* 2025, `2.20`): Contains verbatim copy-pasted EEG microstate transition probability rows (`B to A/C/D/E` identical to `A to B/C/D/E` in Table 3), microstate coverages summing to only 63.9%–75.0%, and Education percentages pasted into the Marriage rows of Table 1.
     * `D10` (`PMC11810936`, *Front Neurosci* 2025, `2.30`): Computes group EEG–fMRI coupling maps by applying PCA across the subject dimension ($Z = 90$) and thresholding unscaled PC1 scores at $|r| > 0.4$.
     * `D07` (`PMC12413946`, *Depress Anxiety* 2025, `4.60`): Claims 41 healthy controls completed 8-week follow-up MRI and cognitive testing when Tables 1 and 3 simply copy the baseline HC numbers into the post-treatment rows.
     * `D15` (`PMC12685844`, *Front Neurosci* 2025, `3.30`): Claims significant group differences in structural synchronization ($s$), assortativity ($r$), and rich-club topology that its own Results text reports as non-significant ($P > 0.05$), while containing unedited text from an acupuncture trial.

### B. Quantitative Ecology & Macroevolution (`Mean = 5.19`, `55% < 5.0`, `75% < 6.0`)
* **Strong Studies (`7.00 – 8.60 / 10`):**
  * `D20` (`PMC7314097`, *PLoS Genet* 2020, `8.60`): Population genomics of 1,294 Australian *E. coli* isolates with 1,000-permutation rarefaction and RefSeq controls demonstrating two-phase gene-repertoire divergence.
  * `D29` (`PMC9873912`, *Nat Commun* 2023, `8.40`): Hierarchical Bayesian synthesis of 1,123 large carnivore population time series modeling OU temporal autocorrelation and imputation uncertainty.
  * `D23` (`PMC9381542`, *Nat Commun* 2022, `7.70`): Whole-genome resequencing ($15\text{--}19\times$), `gradientForest` genomic offset, and ensemble niche models across montane bird populations.
  * `D27` (`PMC10861922`, *PNAS* 2024, `7.30`): 9-year geolocator dataset (274 migrations across 145 shearwaters) partitioning within-individual plasticity from between-individual variation.
* **Recurrent Failure Modes in Species Distribution Modeling (`MaxEnt` / `Biomod2` Mill):**
  1. **Unprojected DEM Bugs, VIF Contradictions, and Pixel-Count Area Confusion:** `D59` (`PMC11317415`, *Front Plant Sci* 2024, `2.50`) explicitly states in Section 2.2 that `Bio17` and `Slope` were eliminated during VIF screening, yet reports `Bio17` and `Slope` as the top model predictors in Tables 4–5, computes physically impossible slope values (`245,018.6` to `500,000`) from an unprojected lat/lon DEM, and reports raw 2.5-arcminute pixel counts (`534,434`) as China's total land area in $\text{km}^2$.
  2. **Present-vs.-Future Covariate Scale Mismatch:** `D26` (`PMC9751338`, *Front Microbiol* 2022, `5.03`) trains its current *Sanghuangporus* `MaxEnt` model on sparse raw GBIF host-tree point counts ($\text{tree/km}^2$) but projects future distributions onto continuous `0–100%` MaxEnt host-suitability rasters, creating an artifactual $+67\%$ to $+118\%$ explosion in future suitable habitat.
  3. **Roadside Ascertainment Bias & Static Topographic Locking:** `D37` (`PMC12414639`, *Ecol Evol* 2025, `3.30`) and `D33` (`PMC11676850`, *Insects* 2024, `4.80`) feed unthinned road-transect or citizen-science records into `MaxEnt` along with `distance to roads`, which absorbs `51.6%–75.4%` of model importance (`mapping road networks rather than species niche`), while `D60` (`PMC10864348`, *Sci Rep* 2024, `4.63`) lets static `Altitude` account for up to `81.2%` of model weight after dropping collinear temperature variables, mechanically preventing upslope thermal tracking under 2050/2070 warming.
  4. **Circular Algebraic Regression ($S_e = P \times M$ regressed onto $M$):** `D25` (`PMC12519188`, *PNAS* 2025, `4.80`) defines "secondary local extinction risk" algebraically as $S_e = P(\text{occurrence}) \times M$ (Eqs. 5–7) and then fits deterministic regressions of $S_e$ onto phenological mismatch $M$ ($Z = 382.4$) while conflating the regression standard error of the mean ($\text{SE}(\hat{\mu})$) with biological flowering duration.

### C. Structural Bioinformatics, Chemical Biology & AI Drug Discovery (`Mean = 4.57`, `60% < 5.0`, `80% < 6.0`)
* **Strong Studies (`7.80 – 8.40 / 10`) — Including a Direct Meta-Validation of Our Forensic Audit:**
  * Remarkably, the highest-scoring paper in this random sample—`D43` (`PMC12552120`, *Nat Mach Intell* 2025, **`8.40 / 10`**)—is a forensic benchmark (*"Resolving data bias improves generalization in binding affinity prediction"*) proving that deep learning GNNs evaluated on `PDBbind` / `CASF-2016` achieve inflated performance purely by memorizing homologous protein pockets and ligand scaffolds leaked between training and test sets!
  * Simultaneously, two other papers drawn in the **exact same blinded random sample**—`D40` (`PMC10243863`, *Bioinformatics* 2023 `GraphscoreDTA`, `5.10`) and `D50` (`PMC11684089`, *J Cheminform* 2024 `DeepTGIN`, `4.90`)—committed that exact `PDBbind` $\to$ `CASF-2016` pocket-homology leakage (with `DeepTGIN` achieving much higher $R = 0.834$ on the leaked `CASF-2016` test set than on its own random validation split $R = 0.736$), and both were independently caught by our blinded reviewer!
* **Recurrent Failure Modes in the Docking / DTI / QSAR Lower Tail:**
  1. **Docking and Simulating Against the Wrong Protein Target (`PDB 6G2O`):** `D44` (`PMC11335128`, *PLoS One* 2024, `2.20`) claims to discover selective **NSD2-PWWP1** inhibitors by running virtual screening, docking, and 100 ns MD simulations against PDB ID **`6G2O`**—which is actually the crystal structure of **human NSD3-PWWP1** bound to the NSD3 probe BI-9321!
  2. **Mis-Centered Grid Boxes (`0.0 kcal/mol` Vina Energies) & Wrong Differential Expression Contrasts:** `D39` (`PMC10987597`, *Sci Rep* 2024, `2.40`) reports physically impossible `0.0 kcal/mol` AutoDock Vina binding affinities on DPP8 and DPP9 due to mis-centered docking grid boxes and a 550-Hartree DFT enthalpy error. `D49` (`PMC11941229`, *Curr Issues Mol Biol* 2025, `2.10`) defines its drug-response signature ("DEG2") by comparing untreated `OCI-AML3` vs. untreated `OCI-AML2` cells rather than `EPZ004777`-treated vs. DMSO control cells, and then blind-docks the small molecule onto the encoded proteins of downregulated mRNAs.
  3. **Impossible Classification Metrics ($F_1 > \max(P, R)$, $\text{RMSE} \ne \sqrt{\text{MSE}}$) & Blank Equations:** `D55` (`PMC12297535`, *Sci Rep* 2025 `QKDTI`, `1.80`) has all 16 mathematical equations blank and violates $\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\text{MSE}}$ and $R^2 = 1 - \text{MSE}/\sigma^2$ in Table 8; `D42` (`PMC11569207`, *Sci Rep* 2024 `VirtuDockDL`, `2.10`) reports `82%` binary classification accuracy for continuous AutoDock Vina energy scores and an impossible $F_1 = 0.962$ for $P = 0.94, R = 0.95$; and `D52` (`PMC12704439`, *Brief Bioinform* 2025 `MCANet`, `4.60`) reports multiple $F_1$ scores in Tables 1–2 that exceed both Precision and Recall (`P = 0.823, R = 0.627, F1 = 0.841`).
