### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript follows a commodity "genome-wide gene family characterization" template to catalog 26 putative Formin (`StFH`) genes in potato (*Solanum tuberosum* L.) using public database searches (Phytozome v13, SpudDB v6.1) and web-server tools (ProtParam, MEGA11/IQ-TREE, GSDS, MEME, WoLF PSORT, PlantCARE, PlantRegMap, psRNATarget, STRING, and TBtools), supplemented by re-alignment of a public drought RNA-seq dataset (`PRJNA541096`).

A forensic audit of the text and tables reveals severe, disqualifying scientific, mathematical, and bioinformatic errors throughout the manuscript: (1) Table 1 and the text misreport protein molecular weights by a factor of $1,000\times$ (reporting Daltons as kilodaltons, e.g., claiming a 1,746-aa protein has a mass of `186,282.23 kDa` [186 MDa]), and Table 1 contains an impossible length-to-mass discrepancy for `StFH21` (`50 A.A.` with a mass of `54,435.60`); (2) several included "formin" genes (`StFH12`, `StFH13`, `StFH14`, `StFH21`) are 50–107 amino acids long—far too short to encode a canonical ~400-aa FH2 domain—representing truncated annotation fragments or pseudogenes; (3) the Methods describe retrieving formins using *"A. thaliana formin DNA-binding domains"* (formins are cytoskeletal actin-nucleating proteins, not transcription factors, and do not possess DNA-binding domains) with an impossible BLASTP E-value threshold of `-1`; (4) internal counts of transcription factors fail basic arithmetic; and (5) the RNA-seq drought analysis lacks any statistical differential expression testing ($p$-values, FDR, or biological replicate variance).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Gross Unit Error ($1,000\times$) and Corrupted Entries in Table 1 (Lines 120, 127–155):**
   - In Section 3.1 (Line 120) and Table 1 (Column 4, `Mass (kDa)`), the molecular weights of all 26 proteins are reported in Daltons (Da) while labeled as kilodaltons (`kDa`). For example, `StFH1` (900 A.A.) is reported as `99423.69 kDa` (~99.4 MDa, larger than an entire eukaryotic ribosome!) instead of `99.42 kDa` (`99,423.69 Da`), and `StFH8` (1,746 A.A.) is reported as `186282.23 kDa` instead of `186.28 kDa`.
   - For `StFH21` (`Soltu.DM.10G001770`, Line 149), the size is reported as `50 A.A.`, yet the mass is reported as `54435.60` (~54.4 kDa, which corresponds to a ~490 A.A. protein, a $10\times$ discrepancy with the reported 50 A.A. length).
   - Four genes included in the 26-member `StFH` family are tiny peptide fragments: `StFH21` (50 A.A., if not a typo), `StFH14` (79 A.A.), `StFH13` (91 A.A.), and `StFH12` (107 A.A.), plus `StFH22` (150 A.A.). A functional Formin Homology 2 (FH2) domain forms a tethered antiparallel dimer of ~400 amino acids. Peptides of 50–107 amino acids cannot contain a complete FH2 domain and represent partial gene fragments, pseudogenes, or misannotations in the tandem clusters on chromosomes 7 and 10 (`Soltu.DM.07G018190–018330` and `Soltu.DM.10G001740–001880`).
2. **Nonsensical Bioinformatic Methods Descriptions (Lines 27, 37, 67, 77):**
   - **Line 27:** *"The A. thaliana formin DNA-binding domains were used to retrieve gene-encoded formin proteins in the potato (S. tuberosum) genome using the BlastP version 2.15.0... The comparison matrix (BLOSUM62), an expected (E) threshold value of -1, and other default parameters were employed."* First, formins are actin-binding cytoskeletal proteins characterized by FH1 and FH2 domains; they do **not** have "DNA-binding domains" (this phrasing appears copy-pasted from a transcription factor family paper). Second, BLAST expectation ($E$) values are strictly non-negative ($E > 0$); an $E$-value threshold of `-1` is mathematically impossible and invalid in BLASTP.
   - **Line 37:** *"The Maximum Likelihood (ML) method was used with default parameters and a significant 1000 bootstrap value... in MEGA version 11.0.10... The FASTA format of the phylogenetic tree was further processed using IQ Tree version 1.6.12."* Phylogenetic trees are stored in Newick/Nexus format, not FASTA format (which stores sequences), and one does not infer an ML tree in MEGA and then "process the FASTA format of the tree" in IQ-TREE.
   - **Line 67 & Line 243:** The authors refer to three separate potato genome assemblies of different cultivars and ploidy levels—autotetraploid *S. tuberosum* cv. Otava, doubled monoploid Group Phureja DM 1-3 516 R44, and heterozygous diploid Group Tuberosum RH89-039-16—as *"the three sub-genomes of S. tuberosum"*. Distinct cultivars/assemblies are not "sub-genomes" (which refer to homeologous genomes A, B, D within an allopolyploid like wheat). Furthermore, the authors state DM 1-3 516 R44 had 23 `StFH` genes on 9 chromosomes in Section 3.9 (Line 243), directly contradicting Section 3.8 (Line 232) and Table 1, which list 26 `StFH` genes (`Soltu.DM...`) on 10 chromosomes in the exact same DM reference genome!
   - **Line 77:** *"The 2000 bp from the 5' untranslated region (5' UTR) of the StFH gene was collected... for CAREs."* Cis-acting promoter analysis examines the 2,000 bp genomic sequence *upstream* of the transcription start site or start codon, not 2,000 bp of 5' UTR.
3. **Arithmetic and Identifier Inconsistencies in Transcription Factor Analysis (Lines 8, 287, 298):**
   - The Abstract (Line 8) states: *"Six major transcription factor families, including bZIP, C2H2, ERF, GATA, LBD, NAC, and HSF, were identified"*—listing **seven** families while stating "Six".
   - Section 3.13 (Line 287) states that *"Thirty-six unique TFs regulating the StFH were identified"* and then breaks them down as: ERF = 26 TFs (but only lists 23 `PGSC0003DMG` IDs in parentheses), bZIP = 4 TFs, LBD = 3 TFs, NAC = 3 TFs, C2H2 = 1 TF, GATA = 1 TF, and omits the count for HSF. Summing the stated numbers gives $26 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 1 + (\ge 1\text{ HSF}) \ge 39 \neq 36$; summing the parenthetical IDs gives $23 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 1 = 35 \neq 36$. Moreover, the TF gene IDs use legacy `PGSC0003DMG` nomenclature whereas the `StFH` genes use `Soltu.DM` v6.1 nomenclature.
4. **Absence of Statistical Rigor in RNA-Seq Drought Analysis (Lines 110–113, 340–349):**
   - In Section 2.18, raw reads from BioProject `PRJNA541096` were aligned with STAR and quantified as FPKM using RSEM (version 1.1.17, a 2012 release, paired with samtools v1.20 from 2024). No differential gene expression software (e.g., DESeq2, edgeR, limma-voom), no biological replicate counts, no dispersion estimates, no log2 fold-changes, and no $p$-values or FDR adjustments are reported. Instead, the authors simply eyeball a raw FPKM heatmap across three flowering stages (25, 50, and 75 days) in two cultivars (Atlantic and Qingshu No. 9) without even comparing drought-stressed vs. well-watered control samples at matched developmental stages. Changes in gene expression between 25, 50, and 75 days after flowering confound plant development/senescence with drought response.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *The potato genome contains 26 functional StFH genes distributed across 10 chromosomes (and 23 in DM in Fig. 9).* `[Methodologically Flawed]` — Internally inconsistent gene counts for the same DM assembly (26 in Table 1 vs. 23 in Section 3.9) and inclusion of 50–107 A.A. truncated fragments that lack a complete FH2 domain.
- **Claim 2:** *60 unique miRNAs target 24 StFH genes to regulate gene expression in response to drought stress (Abstract, Line 8; Section 3.15).* `[Overstated]` — Running `psRNATarget` with default sequence-complementarity parameters yields high false-positive in silico target predictions; no degradome sequencing, sRNA-seq expression correlation, or experimental validation was performed to show that any of these 60 miRNAs cleave `StFH` transcripts under drought.
- **Claim 3:** *Up-regulation of StFH2, StFH10, StFH19, and StFH25 in Qingshu No. 9 alleviates drought stress and can be used in breeding programs.* `[Unsupported]` — Based entirely on uncalibrated FPKM heatmaps across flowering timepoints without untreated controls, statistical testing, or functional validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Sequence files, Newick trees, and supplementary spreadsheets (`S1 Data`–`S14 Data`) are provided in the Supporting Information, and the public SRA accession (`PRJNA541096`) is cited.
- **Pipeline Integrity:** Because the methodological descriptions contain copy-paste artifacts ("formin DNA-binding domains"), impossible parameters ($E$-value $= -1$, "FASTA format of the phylogenetic tree"), $1,000\times$ unit errors in Table 1, and internal arithmetic contradictions, the computational pipeline cannot be reproduced as described.
