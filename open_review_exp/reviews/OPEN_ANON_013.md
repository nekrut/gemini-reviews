# Blinded Review: OPEN_ANON_013
**Composite Score:** 6.8 / 10.0 (Rigor: 2.2/3.5 | Claims: 1.7/2.5 | Biology: 1.5/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.4/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Technical/Assay
**Verdict:** Major Revision
**Study Modality:** Hybrid Computational + Wet-Lab
**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

---

### 1. Executive Summary
This study profiles peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from $n = 9$ people living with HIV-1 (PLWH) during early infection ($<6$ months) using 10x Genomics scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, single-cell Multiome (snRNA-seq + scATAC-seq), and Oxford Nanopore targeted long-read sequencing of HIV-1 transcripts. The authors report $985$ HIV-1 RNA+ CD4+ T cells (and $3,450$ uninfected CD4+ T cells) in the transcriptomic dataset and $39$ HIV-1 DNA+ CD4+ T cells in the scATAC-seq dataset. Through integrative gene regulatory network (SCENIC) and motif accessibility (chromVAR/Signac) analyses, the study identifies `KLF2` as an upregulated transcription factor across HIV-1 RNA+ CD4+ T cell subsets and reports that HIV-1 DNA+ cells are enriched within a Th17 cluster characterized by `RORC`, `STAT3`, and `RORA` motif accessibility. Direct ex vivo multi-omic profiling of unstimulated acute/early HIV-1 patient cells and independent validation in public cohorts (`GSE157829`, `GSE242997`) are major strengths. However, the study requires major revisions to address: (1) an unusually high reported fraction of HIV-1 RNA+ CD4+ T cells ($22.2\%$) potentially inflated by relaxed `STARsolo` alignment parameters ($25\%$ mapping length) and nested PCR barcode chimeras; (2) cell-level pseudoreplication across donors; and (3) cell-type confounding in the HIV-1 DNA+ analysis where comparing a Th17-enriched cluster against all other CD4+ T cells naturally recovers Th17 lineage regulators (`RORC`, `STAT3`, `RORA`).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Unusually High HIV-1 RNA+ Call Rate ($22.2\%$) and Stringency of Viral Read Detection:** The authors classify $985$ out of $4,435$ peripheral blood CD4+ T cells ($22.2\%$) as HIV-1 RNA+. Even during acute HIV-1 infection, the in vivo frequency of infected CD4+ T cells in peripheral blood is typically $\le 1\%$. Two methodological factors described in the Methods may inflate false-positive HIV-1 RNA+ cell calls:
   - In `STARsolo` alignment (line 161), the authors lowered the minimum mapped length parameter from $50\%$ to $25\%$, which risks spurious alignment of short or low-complexity host/endogenous retroviral reads to the HIV-1 reference genome.
   - In Oxford Nanopore targeted sequencing (line 156), biotinylated selection followed by hemi-nested PCR amplification of pooled 10x cDNA libraries is highly susceptible to PCR template switching and barcode swapping, which can attach abundant viral amplicons to uninfected cell barcodes unless a stringent minimum UMI/read count threshold per cell barcode is enforced. The Methods do not state the minimum UMI or read threshold required to call a cell HIV-1 RNA+.
2. **Confounding Between Cell-Type Identity and Proviral Integration in the HIV-1 DNA+ Analysis (Figure 5):** Only $39$ CD4+ T cells had $\ge 2$ scATAC-seq reads mapping to HIV-1 DNA. In line 102 and Figure 5C–E, the authors state that "DARs were identified in the cluster enriched with HIV-1 DNA+ CD4+ T cells compared to the remaining CD4+ T cells" and that motifs were tested in the "HIV-1 DNA+ CD4 T cell cluster compared to the rest of CD4 T cells." Because the cluster containing the $39$ HIV-1 DNA+ cells is the Th17 cluster, comparing this cluster (or the 39 cells within it) against all other CD4+ T cells (predominantly naive and effector memory cells) guarantees the recovery of canonical Th17 lineage-defining transcription factors (`RORC`, `STAT3`, `RORA`) and chemokine receptors (`CCR6`, `CCR5`). To distinguish HIV-1 integration-specific chromatin remodeling from baseline Th17 lineage identity, the $39$ HIV-1 DNA+ cells must be compared specifically against *uninfected Th17 cells* from the same donors.
3. **Single-Cell Pseudoreplication Without Donor-Level Covariate Adjustment:** Differential gene expression (`FindMarkers`) and differential accessibility analyses were conducted at the single-cell level ($985$ vs. $3,450$ cells) without incorporating donor ID as a latent variable/random effect or performing donor-level pseudobulk aggregation. Because cell-type proportions (Figure 1—figure supplement 1B) and viremia vary across the $9$ donors, donor-level batch and clinical differences can drive cell-level false discoveries.
4. **Assay Breakdown and Cell Count Discrepancies:**
   - Line 29 states that "five scRNA-seq and four snRNA-seq data from single-cell multiome datasets" were obtained from $9$ individuals, whereas Figure 2A caption states $n = 8$ patients were used for the combined transcriptome/epigenome UMAP, and the Methods also describe standalone scATAC-seq libraries (line 144). The exact mapping of which of the 9 donors were profiled by standalone scRNA-seq, standalone scATAC-seq, and 10x Multiome should be explicitly tabulated in the main text.
   - Figure 5B caption reports $5,535$ uninfected cells and $39$ HIV-1 DNA+ cells ($5,574$ total CD4+ T cells), whereas Figure 5—figure supplement 1A caption reports $5,608$ CD4+ T cells on the same ATAC UMAP ($34$ cells discrepancy).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: `KLF2` and its regulon target genes (`LTB`, `PIM1`, `NRP2`, `TXNIP`) are upregulated in HIV-1 RNA+ CD4+ T cells during early infection.** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by concordant scRNA-seq expression, SCENIC regulon activity, chromVAR motif accessibility, and Tn5 footprinting in the primary cohort, and corroborated by reanalysis of an independent scRNA-seq dataset (`GSE157829`). However, it remains associative (it is unclear whether `KLF2` upregulation is a viral-induced program or reflects preferential survival of quiescent/less-activated infected cells).
- **Claim 2: Interferon-alpha/gamma responses are upregulated in HIV-1 RNA+ CD4+ T cells while downstream PI3K–AKT–mTOR and T-cell activation pathways are attenuated in Th2 and Th17 subsets.** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by GSEA and DEG analyses, though sensitive to donor-level pseudoreplication and the specificity of HIV-1 RNA+ cell calls.
- **Claim 3: HIV-1 DNA+ cells are predominantly Th17 cells and exhibit specific epigenetic enrichment of `RORC`, `STAT3`, and `RORA` motifs driving susceptibility and reservoir formation.** `[Fragile]` — Based on only $39$ cells total across all donors, and the motif/DAR enrichment is confounded by comparing the Th17 cluster against non-Th17 CD4+ T cells rather than against matched uninfected Th17 cells.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Raw sequencing data are deposited in the Korea BioData Station / Korea National Research Data Archive under accession `KAP230707` under controlled access due to human biospecimen privacy regulations, with clear access request instructions. External validation accessions (`GSE157829`, `GSE242997`) and processed DEG/DAR supplementary tables plus primer sequences (Supplementary file 1C) are provided.
- **Pipeline Documentation:** Software versions (`CellRanger v6.1.2`, `CellRanger ARC v2.0.0`, `STARsolo v2.7.1a`, `Seurat v3.2.1`, `Signac v1.5.0`, `Guppy v6.1.1`, `CellChat v2.1.1`) are well documented, but the exact barcode/UMI filtering thresholds for ONT targeted HIV-1 read calling and custom analysis scripts are not fully detailed.
