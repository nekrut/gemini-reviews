# Blinded Review: OPEN_ANON_030
**Composite Score:** 7.1 / 10.0 (Rigor: 2.3/3.5 | Claims: 1.7/2.5 | Biology: 1.6/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.5/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Over-Interpretation/Clinical
**Verdict:** Major Revision
**Study Modality:** Hybrid Computational + Wet-Lab
**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
This multi-model study integrates a genome-wide siRNA proliferation screen (18,055 genes) in human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes (`hPSC-CMs`), whole-genome sequencing (WGS) of 25 poor-outcome hypoplastic left heart syndrome (HLHS) proband–parent trios plus an extended multiplex CHD family (`75H`), and in vivo loss-of-function and epistasis assays in *Drosophila melanogaster* (`Hand4.2-Gal4`) and zebrafish (*Danio rerio* F0 crispants and morphants) to implicate ribosomal protein (RP) genes as regulators of cardiomyocyte proliferation and candidate contributors to HLHS. The authors show that knockdown of 59/80 RP genes suppresses hPSC-CM proliferation by activating the nucleolar stress / `TP53`–`CDKN1A` axis (which can be rescued by `TP53`/`CDKN1A` co-knockdown in hPSC-CMs and zebrafish, or by `yorkie`/YAP overexpression in *Drosophila*), and demonstrate synergistic genetic interactions between `RPS15A`/`RpS15Aa`/`rps15a` and core cardiac transcription factors (`TBX5`/`Doc`/`tbx5a`, `NKX2-5`/`tin`/`nkx2.7`, `GATA4`/`pnr`) as well as `EWSR1`/`caz`. While the cross-species developmental and cell-biological experiments are extensive and well-executed, the human genetic link to HLHS is substantially overstated: (1) knocking down core ribosomal subunit genes triggers universal p53-dependent cell-cycle arrest in any dividing cell (as the authors' own fibroblast and hPSC controls confirm), making RPs generic essential housekeeping hits in any proliferation screen; and (2) the human WGS enrichment in 25 trios lacks a matched control trio cohort processed through the identical non-coding/promoter variant pipeline, relies heavily on unvalidated upstream promoter variants (`MAF` up to 0.72% or `CADD < 10`), and contains an internal contradiction in Table 1 where `RPL3L` (the only muscle-specific ribosomal paralog) actually *elevates* CM proliferation upon knockdown.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Absence of a Matched Control Cohort and Over-Reliance on Unvalidated Non-Coding Variants in the WGS Enrichment Analysis (25 Trios + Family 75H):**
   - Rather than performing a case–control burden test comparing the 25 HLHS trios against unaffected control trios (e.g., unaffected siblings from SSC or 1000 Genomes/gnomAD trios) processed through the exact same WGS variant calling and regulatory filtering pipeline (Ingenuity Variant Analysis + VISTA enhancers + JASPAR PWM promoter sites within `<1% MAF`), the authors filtered the 25 probands down to 292 genes and then tested those 292 genes against the static background human proteome in `STRING`/`PANTHER` (`5.24-fold, p = 0.0028`).
   - Because the prioritization pipeline specifically required genes to have **upper-quartile expression in embryonic/fetal heart** (line 90), and ribosomal protein genes (80 genes) are among the most ubiquitously and highly expressed transcripts in every tissue (guaranteed to sit in the top expression quartile and possess dense promoter TFBS annotations in JASPAR), testing the filtered list against the whole reference proteome (rather than against the background of upper-quartile heart-expressed genes or matched control trios) severely inflates the enrichment of ribosomal proteins!
   - Furthermore, inspection of Table 1 ("14 variants found in 9 RP genes among 6 HLHS probands") reveals weak human genetic evidence:
     - **Missing Proband IDs and Confounding Multi-Hit Probands:** Three rows in Table 1 (`RPL36A`, `RPS15`, `RPS28`) have blank Proband IDs (presumably carried by the same probands listed above them, such as `145H` and `76H`), meaning 6 probands carry 9 RP genes.
     - **Loose MAF and CADD Thresholds for a Rare Recessive/X-Linked Lethal Disease:** Under a recessive or X-linked recessive model for a rare lethal condition like HLHS, hemizygous X-linked promoter variants with population allele frequencies of `0.653%` (`RPL39 –1359T>C` in `151H`) and `0.583%` (`RPL10 24–218G>A` in `76H`) or compound heterozygous variants at `0.72%` (`RPL13A`) and `0.966%` (`RPL3L`) are present in thousands of healthy hemizygous males/individuals in gnomAD. Similarly, the homozygous missense variant `RPS17 S136N` (`325H`) has a benign `CADD < 10`.
     - **Opposite Phenotype and Null Phenotypes in Table 1:** Knockdown of `RPL13A` (`201H`) and `RPL36A` had **no effect** on hPSC-CM proliferation (`RPL13A` also had no phenotype in *Drosophila*), `rps28` CRISPR mutants had **no heart phenotype** in zebrafish, and knockdown of `RPL3L` (`96H`, the striated-muscle-specific ribosomal paralog) actually **elevated** hPSC-CM proliferation! None of the non-coding promoter variants in Table 1 or Family `75H` (`RPS15A`) were experimentally tested by reporter assay or CRISPR prime/base editing to show that they actually reduce RP transcription.
2. **Confounding of Universal Ribosomal Haploinsufficiency / Nucleolar Stress with Disease-Specific HLHS Pathogenesis:**
   - In the genome-wide siRNA screen of 18,055 genes, the 80 core ribosomal proteins were the top hits decreasing EdU incorporation and cell number. However, as the authors themselves show in `Figure 1—figure supplement 1C, D` (dermal fibroblasts) and `Figure 7A` (undifferentiated hPSCs), acute siRNA depletion of essential structural ribosomal subunit proteins triggers canonical ribosome biogenesis stress (free RPL5/RPL11 binding MDM2 to stabilize p53 and induce p21/`CDKN1A`), halting the cell cycle across virtually *any* proliferating eukaryotic cell type.
   - Similarly, in human genetics, heterozygous loss-of-function of core RPs (including *RPS17*, *RPS15*, *RPL26*, *RPS28*) causes **Diamond-Blackfan Anemia (DBA)**—a well-defined bone marrow failure and craniofacial/thumb malformation syndrome—rather than isolated HLHS. While ~30% of DBA patients have congenital heart defects (mostly ASD/VSD), no clinical evidence of DBA (macrocytic anemia, reticulocytopenia) is mentioned for the 6 HLHS probands in Table 1 or Family `75H`.
3. **Inconsistent Statistical Tests and Uncorrected Multiple Comparisons Across Cellular Assays:**
   - In the Methods ("Statistical analysis", line 124), the authors state that for all hPSC-VCM and iPSC experiments, "we calculated two-sided p values with Student's *t*-test using GraphPad Prism 8.1.2 software." However, in the Figure 7 caption (line 163), the histograms comparing continuous `% CDKN1A+` and `% EdU+` CMs (Fig 7C, F, H, I) are reported as analyzed by **Chi-square test** ("Chi-square test, *p < 0.05..."), which is inappropriate for continuous replicate-level percentage averages across 4 biological wells.
   - When re-testing all 80 RP genes in quadruplicate (`Figure 1—figure supplement 1A, B`) or testing 292 candidate genes (`Figure 2B`), significance is declared at unadjusted $p < 0.05$ via individual *t*-tests without false discovery rate (FDR) or Dunnett's correction.
   - Typo in Figure 8—figure supplement 1B caption (line 176): `****p < 0001` (missing decimal point, should be `p < 0.0001`).
   - Typo in Results (line 45): HLHS valve morphology distribution lists `MA/AA in 10, MS/AS in 9, MA/AA in 4, MA/AS in 1, and unknown in 1` (`MA/AA` is listed twice; one of them is presumably `MS/AA`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Knockdown of core ribosomal proteins strongly inhibits cardiomyocyte proliferation in vitro and disrupts cardiac development in *Drosophila* and zebrafish via the nucleolar stress / `TP53`–`CDKN1A` and Hippo/`yorkie` pathways.** `[Well Supported]` — The multi-organism phenotyping (`hPSC-CMs`, `Hand4.2-Gal4` *Drosophila*, and zebrafish `Tg(myl7:EGFP);Tg(myl7:H2A-mCherry)` crispants and morphants), together with the `TP53`/`CDKN1A` co-knockdown rescue in human CMs and zebrafish (Fig 4) and the `yorkie`/`scalloped` rescue in *Drosophila* (Fig 5), is experimentally thorough and convincing.
- **Claim 2: Reduced RP dosage sensitizes the developing heart to haploinsufficiency/knockdown of core cardiac transcription factors (`TBX5`/`Doc`/`tbx5a`, `NKX2-5`/`tin`/`nkx2.7`, `GATA4`/`pnr`) and regulates downstream effectors like `EWSR1`/`caz`.** `[Well Supported]` — The two-way ANOVA siRNA titration curves in hPSC-CMs (Fig 6B), transheterozygous adult fly heart defects (Fig 6C–D), and double-morphant zebrafish synergy (Fig 6—figure supplement 1D–E) provide strong cross-species evidence for synthetic genetic interactions between ribosomal capacity and cardiogenic transcription factors.
- **Claim 3: Rare variants in RP genes are significantly enriched in HLHS patients and act as genetic drivers of HLHS.** `[Overstated]` — Because the WGS enrichment lacked a matched control trio cohort and was biased toward highly expressed genes in the embryonic heart, and because most reported human variants are unvalidated non-coding promoter SNPs with `MAF` up to 0.7% (or show no/opposite effect in functional assays, like `RPL13A`, `RPL36A`, `RPL3L`), the human genetic evidence is suggestive at best and does not establish causality without allele-specific CRISPR knock-in or promoter reporter validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Code Availability:** Bulk RNA-seq data (`siRPS15A` and `siRPL39` in hPSCs and hPSC-CMs) are publicly available in NCBI GEO (`GSE207658`), and analysis scripts are archived on GitHub/Zenodo (`https://github.com/gvogler/[REDACTED_VENUE]-2025-nielsen-et-al`). Full zebrafish morpholino sequences, IDT Alt-R crRNA sequences, *Drosophila* BDSC/VDRC stock IDs, and PrimerBank IDs are thoroughly documented in the Methods and Key Resources Table.
- **Human Genomic Data Availability Gap:** No dbGaP / EGA accession number is provided for the 25 HLHS trio WGS datasets or the `75H` family WGS data, and the exact genomic coordinates (hg38 `chr:pos:ref:alt`) for the 14 RP variants in Table 1 and the `RPS15A` variant in Family `75H` (whose variant nomenclature and MAF are omitted from the main text and Table 1) should be explicitly tabulated.
