### 1. Executive Summary
This study profiles 10 low-grade small intestine neuroendocrine tumor (SiNET) samples from 8 patients ($29,198$ cells/nuclei after QC), 1 small intestine adenocarcinoma (SiAdeno), and 1 mixed lung large cell neuroendocrine carcinoma (LCNEC) using 10x Genomics scRNA-seq and snRNA-seq, supplemented by reanalysis of $81$ bulk SiNET RNA-seq samples (`GSE98894`). The authors report three primary findings: (1) the $8$ SiNETs containing sufficient neuroendocrine (NE) cells segregate into an "epithelial-like" subtype ($n = 3$) and a "neuronal-like" subtype ($n = 5$); (2) malignant NE cells exhibit an extremely low cycling fraction ($0.246\%$), whereas non-malignant B/plasma cells proliferate briskly in the 3 epithelial-like SiNETs alongside pan-cell-type upregulation of Macrophage Migration Inhibitory Factor (`MIF`); and (3) an $N = 1$ mixed lung LCNEC contains a proliferative malignant subpopulation with intermediate NE/squamous expression and stemness transcription factors (`PBX1`, `PROX1`, `MSI2`, `TCF4`, `HES6`). While profiling rare, slow-growing SiNETs at single-cell resolution is valuable and the observation of near-zero NE proliferation vs. microenvironment proliferation is biologically intriguing, the manuscript suffers from a direct contradiction between Results and Methods regarding which samples are scRNA-seq vs. snRNA-seq, severe confounding between assay modality (fresh 3' scRNA-seq vs. frozen 5' snRNA-seq) and the proposed subtype split, cross-sample reference subtraction in samples lacking stromal cells, and unadjusted $p$-value thresholding on $n = 3$ vs. $n = 5$ tumors.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Direct Textual Contradiction on Fresh scRNA-seq vs. Frozen snRNA-seq Sample Assignments:** Results line 31 states: "We profiled single cells from three fresh surgical samples (SiNET1-3) and single nuclei from the remaining seven frozen samples (SiNET4-10)." However, Methods line 172 states the exact opposite: "Four samples (SiAdeno, SiNETs 1–3) underwent single nuclei sequencing, while the remaining samples (SiNETs 4–10 and the LCNEC sample) were subjected to scRNA-seq." (Captions for Figures 2B and 5A also conflate the terms as "single-nuclei RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq)"). This discrepancy must be corrected.
2. **Assay Modality Confounding Between Fresh 3' scRNA-seq and Frozen 5' snRNA-seq in the Subtype Division ($n = 3$ vs. $n = 5$):** Assuming Results line 31 is correct, both fresh scRNA-seq samples containing NE cells (`SiNET1`, `SiNET2`) cluster into the "epithelial-like" subtype (`SiNET1`, `SiNET2`, `SiNET9`), whereas 5 of the 6 frozen snRNA-seq samples (`SiNET4`, `SiNET5`, `SiNET7`, `SiNET8`, `SiNET10`) form the "neuronal-like" subtype. Fresh whole-cell 3' scRNA-seq preferentially captures mature cytoplasmic mRNAs (such as cytokeratins `KRT8`, `EPCAM`, and short cytoplasmic transcripts like `MIF`), whereas frozen 5' single-nucleus RNA-seq is well known to enrich for long, nuclear-retained pre-mRNAs characteristic of large neuronal ion-channel and receptor genes (`NAV3`, `RYR2`, `CACNA1A/B`, `KCNIP1`). While the authors show that pattern #1 genes stratify bulk RNA-seq samples (`GSE98894`), bulk ileal tumor resections vary in normal intestinal epithelial admixture and lymphoid follicle content, which can similarly drive `EPCAM`/`KRT8` expression in bulk mixtures.
3. **Cross-Tumor Reference Subtraction in `SiNET7` and `SiNET8` (Methods, Line 181):** To identify NE-specific genes without batch integration, the authors subtracted the expression of $50$ reference non-NE cells (macrophages, fibroblasts, endothelial cells) from $50$ NE cells within each tumor. However, for `SiNET7` and `SiNET8`, which lacked sufficient reference cells, the authors "sampled the reference cells from a different sample that exhibited the highest correlation with the given sample." Subtracting reference cells measured in a *different* tumor re-introduces technical batch differences directly into the normalized NE profiles of `SiNET7` and `SiNET8`.
4. **Confounding by Germinal Center (Peyer's Patch) Inclusion, Ambient RNA, and Unadjusted Testing in the `MIF` / B-Cell Proliferation Analysis:**
   - The authors note (line 114; Figure 4—figure supplement 2) that 2 of the 3 epithelial-like tumors (`SiNET1` and `SiNET2`) exhibit high Germinal Center (GC) signature scores. Because terminal ileum SiNETs arise adjacent to mucosal Peyer's patches, inclusion of normal lymphoid germinal centers naturally introduces highly proliferative GC B cells.
   - In comparing the two subtypes across cell types (Methods line 203), the authors used an unadjusted $p < 0.05$ without FDR correction across $n = 3$ vs. $n = 5$ tumors.
   - The observation that `MIF` is simultaneously upregulated across all six distinct cell types in the epithelial-like subtype (`SiNET1`, `SiNET2`, `SiNET9`) is a classic hallmark of ambient extracellular RNA contamination in droplet scRNA-seq (where a highly abundant cytoplasmic transcript like `MIF` leaks into the ambient soup and is detected in all droplets of those libraries) or 3' vs. 5' chemistry differences, rather than coordinated transcriptional upregulation across six unrelated lineages.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Low-grade SiNET malignant NE cells have an exceptionally low cycling fraction ($0.246\%$), whereas non-malignant TME cells (especially B/plasma cells) account for most mitotic activity in these samples.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by multi-gene cell cycle scoring across single-cell/single-nucleus samples and corroborated by bulk RNA-seq (`GSE98894`) showing that cell cycle gene expression correlates with B/T-cell markers rather than NE markers.
- **Claim 2: SiNETs divide into "epithelial-like" and "neuronal-like" subtypes.** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by internal clustering of $8$ tumors and projection onto $81$ bulk SiNETs, though partially confounded by whole-cell 3' scRNA-seq vs. nuclear 5' snRNA-seq gene-length/cytoplasmic capture biases and normal epithelial admixture in bulk data.
- **Claim 3: `MIF` upregulation across all cell types in epithelial-like SiNETs drives B/plasma cell proliferation and represents a therapeutic target.** `[Fragile]` — Based on $n = 3$ vs. $n = 5$ tumors without FDR correction, confounded by germinal center (Peyer's patch) inclusion in `SiNET1`/`SiNET2` and potential ambient RNA bleed-through across cell types, with no functional or spatial validation.
- **Claim 4: A putative bipotent progenitor population gives rise to both neuroendocrine and squamous components in a mixed lung LCNEC.** `[Fragile]` — Appropriately framed by the authors as an exploratory $N = 1$ hypothesis based on static co-expression of lineage programs and stemness regulators (`PBX1`, `PROX1`, `MSI2`, `TCF4`, `HES6`), without lineage tracing or trajectory validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Raw and processed scRNA-seq/snRNA-seq data are publicly deposited in NCBI GEO under accession `GSE292163`, and the external bulk validation dataset (`GSE98894`) is publicly available.
- **Methods & Metadata Completeness:** Supplementary files 1–5 provide sample metadata and gene lists. However, the contradiction in Methods line 172 regarding scRNA-seq vs. snRNA-seq sample IDs must be resolved, and custom analysis scripts used for the 256-pattern binary correlation clustering and cell-cycle thresholding are not deposited in a public code repository.
