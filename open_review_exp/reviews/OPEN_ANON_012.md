### 1. Executive Summary
This study performs a bidirectional two-sample Mendelian Randomization (MR) and multivariable MR (MVMR) analysis using OpenGWAS summary statistics to test for causal relationships between a periodontal disease-related phenotype (PDRP; `ebi-a-GCST003484`, $n = 975$) and knee osteoarthritis (KOA; `ebi-a-GCST007090`, $n = 403,124$), with MVMR adjustment for smoking status, alcohol consumption, and body mass index (BMI). While the authors appropriately include a multivariable MR sensitivity analysis and correctly report that the marginal IVW association from PDRP to KOA ($\text{OR} = 1.014, p = 0.035$) disappears upon adjusting for smoking, alcohol, and BMI ($\beta = -0.003, p = 0.572$), the study exhibits major methodological flaws characteristic of template-driven two-sample MR studies. Specifically, the exposure GWAS has an extremely small sample size ($n = 975$), Table 3 computes $I^2$ using $|Q - \text{df}| / Q$ instead of $\max(0, (Q - \text{df}) / Q)$ (producing a false positive $I^2 = 35.8\%$ when $Q = 2.945 < \text{df} = 4$), Table 4 exponentiates linear regression coefficients on a continuous trait into meaningless odds ratios spanning 56 orders of magnitude, and individual SNP instruments (rsIDs, alleles, effect sizes) are omitted from the manuscript.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Mathematical Error in $I^2$ Heterogeneity Calculation (Table 3):** The standard Higgins $I^2$ statistic is defined as $I^2 = \max(0, (Q - \text{df}) / Q)$. For the 5 PDRP SNPs under IVW ($\text{df} = 5 - 1 = 4$), Cochran's $Q$ is $2.945$ ($p = 0.567$). Because $Q < \text{df}$, true heterogeneity is $I^2 = 0$. However, Table 3 reports $I^2 = 0.358$, which the authors obtained by taking the absolute value $|2.945 - 4| / 2.945 = 1.055 / 2.945 = 0.3582$. The same error occurs for MR-Egger on PDRP ($\text{df} = 3, Q = 2.773 < 3$, reported as $I^2 = |2.773 - 3| / 2.773 = 0.082$ instead of $0$).
2. **Inappropriate Exponentiating of Continuous Trait Estimates and Zero-Power 3-SNP MR-Egger (Table 4):** In the reverse MR (KOA $\to$ PDRP), the outcome `ebi-a-GCST003484` is a continuous microbial/inflammatory trait (mean gingival crevicular fluid IL-1$\beta$ / periodontal complex trait in $n = 975$ Dental ARIC subjects), not a binary case-control outcome. Reporting the causal effect of KOA on a continuous trait as an Odds Ratio ($\text{OR} = 3.846$ for IVW; $\text{OR} = 297.972$ for MR-Egger with 95% CI from $1.80 \times 10^{-26}$ to $4.92 \times 10^{30}$) is statistically invalid. Moreover, fitting MR-Egger regression on only $3$ SNPs leaves $\text{df} = 1$, where the intercept test ($\text{SE} = 2.0, p = 0.917$) has virtually zero statistical power to detect horizontal pleiotropy. Also, in Tables 2 and 4, the `SE` column reports the standard error on the log-odds ($\beta$) scale directly adjacent to the exponentiated `OR` column without clarification.
3. **Internal Contradictions on Instrument Selection and Thresholds:**
   - Lines 21 and 66 state that all 5 PDRP SNPs and 3 KOA SNPs surpassed the genome-wide significance threshold ($p \le 5.0 \times 10^{-8}$), which corresponds to a minimum $F = \chi^2_1 \ge 29.72$. However, line 82 states that the $F$-statistics ranged from $29$ to $40$ (an $F = 29$ corresponds to $p \approx 7.2 \times 10^{-8}$), and line 90 states "with all five SNPs meeting a less stringent statistical threshold."
   - Line 90 states: "In the PhenoScanner database, six instrumental SNPs showed no association with KOA," which contradicts the reported instrument counts of $5$ SNPs for PDRP and $3$ SNPs for KOA.
   - In the KOA GWAS (`ebi-a-GCST007090`, $n = 403,124$), the original study reported $>20$ genome-wide significant loci for knee osteoarthritis, yet only $3$ SNPs were retained here, and in the MVMR tables (Tables 5 and 6), the `SNP` column lists only `1` SNP for PDRP, `1` SNP for KOA, and `1` SNP for alcohol consumption versus `276`/`234` SNPs for BMI. This indicates severe instrument loss during harmonization/clumping and extreme imbalance in instrument counts across exposures in MVMR.
4. **Unaccounted UK Biobank Sample Overlap in MVMR:** The KOA summary statistics (`ebi-a-GCST007090`), BMI (`ukb-b-19953`), and smoking status (`ebi-a-GCST90029014`) are all derived predominantly or entirely from the UK Biobank cohort. Two-sample MVMR requires accounting for phenotypic correlation and sample overlap across cohorts to prevent biased standard errors and type I error inflation, which was not performed.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: PDRP has a detrimental causal effect on KOA in univariable IVW analysis ($\text{OR} = 1.014, p = 0.035$), which is attenuated to null ($\beta = -0.003, p = 0.572$) after adjusting for smoking, alcohol, and BMI.** `[Moderately Supported]` — The attenuation in MVMR is the most credible finding of the paper and appropriately tempers the marginal $p = 0.035$ association (which itself would not survive Bonferroni correction for bidirectional testing and was inconsistent with the MR-Egger point estimate of $\text{OR} = 0.997$).
- **Claim 2: Absence of directional pleiotropy is confirmed by MR-Egger intercept tests ($p = 0.706$ and $p = 0.917$).** `[Overstated]` — With only $5$ SNPs ($\text{df} = 3$) and $3$ SNPs ($\text{df} = 1$), the MR-Egger intercept test has negligible statistical power; non-significant $p$-values reflect wide standard errors ($\text{SE} = 0.05$ and $\text{SE} = 2.0$), not confirmed absence of pleiotropy.
- **Claim 3: Smoking and elevated BMI independently increase KOA risk in MVMR.** `[Well Supported]` — Consistent with established epidemiological and genetic evidence, though the MVMR estimates are primarily driven by the $276$ BMI SNPs and $35$ smoking SNPs within overlapping UK Biobank samples.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** The OpenGWAS accession IDs (`ebi-a-GCST003484`, `ebi-a-GCST007090`, `ebi-a-GCST90029014`, `ieu-a-1283`, `ukb-b-19953`) are clearly documented and publicly accessible.
- **Missing Instrument Tables & Code:** The manuscript fails to provide a table of the actual instrumental variables (SNP rsIDs, chromosomes, positions, effect/non-effect alleles, EAF, $\beta$, SE, $p$-values, and individual $F$-statistics) for either the 5 PDRP SNPs or the 3 KOA SNPs. Furthermore, the LD clumping parameters ($r^2$ threshold and kilobase window size) and the R analysis scripts are not provided.
