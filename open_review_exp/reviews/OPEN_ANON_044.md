### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates nitroreductase I (*NTRI*)-independent mechanisms of benznidazole (Bz) resistance in *Trypanosoma cruzi* (strain `M-RATTUS/CO/91/GAL-61.SUC`, DTU TcI). By exposing a Bz-susceptible epimastigote clone (`61S`, $\text{IC}_{50} = 6.22\ \mu\text{M}$) to a constant high concentration of Bz ($40\ \mu\text{M}$, ~6$\times$ $\text{IC}_{50}$) over 20 passages, the authors isolate a highly resistant clone (`61R_cl4`, 15.8-fold higher Bz $\text{IC}_{50}$ with cross-resistance to nifurtimox) that retains wild-type *NTRI* coding sequence and protein abundance. As a positive control, they use CRISPR-Cas9 to generate single and double *NTRI* knockouts (`SKO_NTRI`, `DKO_NTRI`) in the `61S` background, confirming that *NTRI* loss suffices to cause comparable Bz resistance, and then profile `61S` vs. `61R_cl4` by poly-A RNA-seq alongside a comparative re-analysis of a published Bz-resistant transcriptome (`WTS` vs. `LER`). While the experimental demonstration that constant high-dose Bz selection yields a wild-type *NTRI* resistant clone (paired with an isogenic CRISPR *NTRI* knockout control) is biologically interesting, the transcriptomic analysis suffers from an underpowered sample size ($n=2$ biological replicates for `61R_cl4`), poly-A selection artifacts misidentified as regulated rRNA genes, absence of loading controls in the Western blot, and lack of genomic copy-number or functional validation of the proposed candidate genes.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Underpowered RNA-seq Design and Sample/File Count Confusion (`Line 62`, `Fig 2A`)**: In the Methods (Line 62), the authors state: *"Ten paired-end samples corresponded to 61S and 61R_cl4, with three or two biological replicates, respectively, were used."* Three replicates of `61S` plus two replicates of `61R_cl4` equals **5 biological samples** (which produce 10 paired-end `R1`/`R2` FASTQ files). Conducting differential gene expression with only $n=2$ biological replicates in the focal resistant group (`61R_cl4`) provides inadequate statistical power for within-group dispersion estimation, and the specific differential expression software (`DESeq2`, `edgeR`, or `limma-voom`) and normalization method are omitted from the Methods (Line 62 simply cites "[23]").
2. **Poly-A Enrichment Artifacts Misinterpreted as Downregulated Ribosomal RNAs (`Lines 62, 101`, `Fig 2C`)**: Libraries were constructed using a "poly-A tails enrichment kit" (Line 62). Yet in Line 101, the authors highlight that the most strongly downregulated transcripts in `61R_cl4` are non-coding ribosomal RNA components: `LSU-srRNA2` (`C4B63_511nc6`), `LSU-beta` (`C4B63_22nc11`), `LSU-srRNA1` (`C4B63_22nc10`), and `LSU-alpha` (`C4B63_190nc23`). Because structural rRNAs lack canonical poly-A tails, their capture in oligo(dT)-selected RNA-seq libraries reflects technical variation in rRNA depletion/carry-over rather than regulated transcription of rRNA genes.
3. **Unaccounted Gene Copy Number Variation (CNV) / Aneuploidy in *T. cruzi* (`Lines 101, 158`)**: Trypanosomatids transcribe protein-coding genes polycistronically without individual Pol II promoters, and *T. cruzi* frequently undergoes rapid chromosomal aneuploidy and tandem gene amplification/deletion under drug pressure. Furthermore, calling *NTRI* sequence variants solely from RNA-seq alignments (`samtools`/`bcftools`, Line 67) without whole-genome sequencing (WGS) or qPCR copy-number assays cannot distinguish genuine mRNA stability regulation from underlying chromosomal somy or CNV changes across the 890 differentially regulated genes (many of which belong to highly repetitive multigene families such as mucins, MASPs, and trans-sialidases).
4. **Absence of Internal Loading Control in NTRI Western Blot (`Line 52`, `S3A Fig`)**: To conclude that *NTRI* protein expression is unchanged between `61S` and `61R_cl4`, 100 $\mu$g of total protein was loaded and band intensity was quantified directly on an Odyssey infrared scanner (Line 52) without probing for an endogenous housekeeping protein (e.g., $\alpha$-tubulin or GAPDH) or normalizing to a total protein stain on the nitrocellulose membrane.
5. **Lack of Functional Validation of Candidate Non-NTRI Genes and Restriction to Epimastigotes**: Although the authors possess a working CRISPR-Cas9 system in `61S_Cas9` (used effectively to knock out *NTRI*), none of the candidate resistance genes identified by RNA-seq (e.g., *CDC45*, *PEPCK*, *TRYS*, ABC transporter `C4B63_328g10`, or ribosomal subunits) were validated by RT-qPCR or functionally perturbed via CRISPR knockout/overexpression, and all drug susceptibility assays were restricted to insect-stage epimastigotes rather than clinically relevant mammalian amastigotes.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that *NTRI* knockout in `61S` induces Bz resistance (`Fig 1C`, `S4 Fig`)**: `[Well Supported]` — Single and double allelic replacement of *NTRI* (`SKO_NTRI`, `DKO_NTRI`) verified by locus-specific PCR clearly increases Bz $\text{IC}_{50}$ in a dose-dependent manner.
- **Claim that `61R_cl4` achieves high-level Bz and Nfx resistance independently of *NTRI* coding mutations (`Fig 1A–B`, `S3 Fig`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The absence of coding mutations in *NTRI* RNA-seq reads and the presence of full-length NTRI protein on Western blot support an *NTRI*-independent mechanism, though a normalized Western blot loading control and genomic copy-number check are needed to make this definitive.
- **Claim that Bz resistance in `61R_cl4` is driven by coordinated transcriptional reprogramming of ribosomal subunits, DNA repair (*CDC45*, *RAD51*), and ABC transporters (`Figs 2–6`)**: `[Overstated]` — Because `61R_cl4` divides significantly faster than `61S` (doubling time 23 h vs. 35 h; 20% vs. 12% cells in G2 phase, Line 80), many of the 890 differentially expressed genes (such as upregulation of DNA replication factors *CDC45*, *RAD51*, *TOP3* and cell-cycle genes) are direct secondary correlates of the faster proliferation rate of clone `61R_cl4` rather than causal mechanisms of Bz resistance.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability**: Raw RNA-seq reads are deposited in NCBI SRA under BioProject `PRJNA1182289`, primer sequences for *NTRI* CRISPR knockout are provided in `S1 Table`, and read count/DEG tables are provided in `S1–S4 Files`.
- **Missing Details**: The exact computational pipeline for RNA-seq read mapping, counting, and differential expression (software versions, parameters, and statistical model) must be written out in the Methods rather than cited to an external paper, and Western blot loading controls should be provided.
