### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates the steady-state role of mucosal enteric glia in regulating the intestinal epithelium in vivo. By integrating published human and mouse single-cell RNA-seq datasets with immunohistochemistry across the radial and crypt-villus axes, the authors establish that *Plp1* (and *Sox10*) broadly and specifically marks mucosal glia—including crypt-associated glia that lack GFAP—whereas *GFAP* is largely undetectable in healthy human mucosal glia and is predominantly expressed by non-glial stromal/immune cells in Crohn's disease. Using tamoxifen-inducible diphtheria toxin ablation in adult `Plp1^{CreER} Rosa26^{DTA/+}` (`Cre+`) mice compared against tamoxifen-treated `Rosa26^{DTA/+}` (`Cre-`) littermates at 11 days post-induction (11dpt), the authors profile full-thickness duodenum, ileum, colon, and mechanically isolated ileal epithelium by bulk RNA-seq. Remarkably, acute ablation of ~90% of mucosal glia causes almost no global transcriptional changes (zero genes at $p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ in duodenum, ileum, or isolated ileal epithelium; 5 genes in colon) and preserves epithelial lineage densities, barrier mucus distance, and crypt cholinergic (`VAChT+`/`TUBB3+`) innervation. However, GSEA, lectin staining, high-pressure freezing transmission electron microscopy (TEM), an ex vivo ligated ileal explant lysozyme assay, and longitudinal (0dpt vs. 11dpt) 16S rDNA sequencing reveal a selective functional defect in ileal Paneth cell granule morphology and baseline lysozyme secretion accompanied by shifts in fecal microbiome composition. This is a rigorous, well-controlled, and refreshingly transparent study that resolves long-standing controversies in enteric glial biology.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Rigorous Experimental Controls for Confounders (Tamoxifen, Sex-Specific Dysmotility, and Denervation)**:
   - Every comparison pairs `Plp1^{CreER} Rosa26^{DTA/+}` (`Cre+`) mice with tamoxifen-gavaged `Cre-` (`Rosa26^{DTA/+}`) littermates, controlling for potential off-target effects of tamoxifen on epithelial cells.
   - Because female `Plp1^{CreER} Rosa26^{DTA/+}` mice develop GI transit dysmotility whereas males do not, the authors deliberately performed the primary bulk RNA-seq screen in male littermates ($n=4$ per group) to isolate direct glial-epithelial effects from secondary motility artifacts, and then verified cellular, functional, and microbiome phenotypes across both sexes (explicitly plotting males as triangles and females as circles in all figures).
   - To test whether impaired Paneth cell secretion was secondary to mucosal denervation (as occurs in skin after Schwann cell ablation), the authors quantified crypt-proximal `TUBB3+` and `VAChT+` cholinergic fibers and stimulated explants with carbachol ($10\ \mu\text{M}$), demonstrating intact anatomical innervation and preserved muscarinic receptor (`Chrm3`) responsiveness.
2. **Transparent Reporting of Null `DESeq2` Results Paired with Exploratory Unadjusted $p < 0.05$ (`DiVenn`) Analyses (`Figs 2B–C, 3A`)**:
   - The authors candidly report that zero genes passed $p_{\text{adj}} < 0.05$ in the duodenum, ileum, and isolated ileal epithelium. To explore subtle regional patterns, they relaxed the threshold to unadjusted $p < 0.05$ for `DiVenn` overlap analysis (`Fig 2C`, `Fig 3A`), finding 331, 516, and 916 genes in duodenum, ileum, and colon, respectively, with only 16–29 shared between pairs and 2 shared across all three.
   - From a statistical standpoint, testing ~15,000–20,000 expressed genes under a global null hypothesis at unadjusted $\alpha = 0.05$ is expected to yield ~750–1,000 false positives per tissue by chance alone, with random pairwise overlap of $0.05^2 \times 18,000 \approx 45$ genes and three-way overlap of $0.05^3 \times 18,000 \approx 2.25$ genes. Thus, the numbers of unadjusted $p < 0.05$ genes and their minimal inter-regional overlap in `Fig 2C` are mathematically indistinguishable from independent random noise under the null! While the Paneth cell enrichment in ileal epithelium is independently supported by rank-based GSEA ($\text{FDR} < 0.001$ across two independent gene-set collections in `Fig 3B` and `Fig 3—supp 1D–F`) and orthogonal functional assays, the authors should explicitly note in the text that the lack of overlap at unadjusted $p < 0.05$ in `Fig 2C` may reflect expected false positives under low signal-to-noise rather than true region-specific regulation.
3. **Genotype-Segregated Group Housing and Statistical Test Labeling in 16S Microbiome Analysis (`Fig 5D–E`, `Offset Line 161`)**:
   - In the Methods (Offset Line 161), the authors note that `Cre+` and `Cre-` littermates were "group-housed segregated by sex and genotype from the time of weaning." Housing mice segregated by genotype introduces a potential cage effect on the gut microbiota. Although the paired longitudinal sampling design (0dpt baseline vs. 11dpt post-tamoxifen in `Fig 5D–G` and `Fig 5—supp 2C`, excluding taxa that differed at 0dpt) substantially mitigates pre-existing cage drift, the number of cages per genotype is not stated, and cage ID was not included as a random effect.
   - Additionally, in the caption for `Fig 5E` (Line 104), the $\beta$-diversity ordination statistics ($R^2 = 0.25679, p = 0.003$) are attributed to an "unpaired parametric t-test" in the general legend sentence ("Graphs depict $\alpha$-diversity (D) and $\beta$-diversity (E)... p-values reflect unpaired parametric t-test"), whereas $R^2$ and $p$ on Bray-Curtis ordination derive from PERMANOVA (`adonis`). This caption label should be corrected.
4. **Quantitative Morphometrics for Paneth Cell Granule Abnormality (`Fig 4A–B`)**:
   - While all cell-type density counts (`Fig 3C–D`) and nerve fiber areas (`Fig 5—supp 1E`) were rigorously quantified by blinded investigators across $n=4$–$10$ mice per group, the Paneth cell granule morphological abnormality in `Fig 4A` (UEA-1 lectin IHC, $\ge 3$ mice/group) and `Fig 4B` (high-pressure freezing TEM, $n=2$ mice/group) is presented solely as representative micrographs. Adding a blinded quantitative metric from the UEA-1 images (e.g., percentage of crypts or Paneth cells exhibiting fused/giant/apically displaced granules per mouse across $n \ge 4$ mice per genotype) would strengthen the link between the morphological phenotype and the quantitative explant lysozyme secretion assay (`Fig 5C`, $n=9$ mice/group, $p = 0.0188$).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that *Plp1* is the most sensitive and specific marker of mucosal glia in mice and humans, whereas *GFAP* is absent in crypt-associated glia and enriched in non-glial cells in human IBD (`Fig 1`)**: `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated across four independent human and mouse scRNA-seq cohorts, human small-intestinal DAB IHC, and whole-mount confocal quantification in `Plp1-eGFP` mice.
- **Claim that acute depletion of $Plp1^+$ enteric glia does not disrupt global epithelial transcriptional programs, lineage composition, barrier mucus separation, or crypt cholinergic innervation at steady state (`Figs 2–3`, `Fig 5—supp 1–2`)**: `[Well Supported]` — Backed by blinded quantification of five epithelial cell types, 16S bacterial FISH distance to crypts, `VAChT`/`TUBB3` nerve terminal density, and well-powered RNA-seq.
- **Claim that glial depletion impairs baseline Paneth cell lysozyme secretion while preserving intrinsic responsiveness to cholinergic stimulation (`Fig 4`, `Fig 5B–C`, `Fig 5—supp 1C–D`)**: `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated via an acutely ligated ileal explant enzymatic assay ($n=9$/group) benchmarked against dithizone chemical Paneth cell disruption and rescued by exogenous carbachol stimulation.
- **Claim that glial depletion alters fecal microbiome composition via Paneth cell dysfunction (`Fig 5D–G`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The longitudinal 0dpt-to-11dpt depletion of *Ligilactobacillus murinus* and *L. animalis* and expansion of *Bacteroides acidifaciens* closely phenocopies published `Lyz1^{-/-}` mice, though genotype-segregated caging and potential extra-epithelial effects of `Plp1+` glial ablation leave room for minor indirect contributions.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability**: Newly generated bulk RNA-seq datasets (`GSE280442`) and 16S rRNA sequencing datasets (`PRJNA1234316`) are deposited in NCBI GEO/SRA, and all nine re-analyzed public scRNA-seq and bulk RNA-seq accessions (`GSE132465`, `GSE144363`, `GSE99815`, `GSE108529`, `SCP1422`, `GSE134809`, `GSE142431`, `GSE92332`, `GSE113536`) are fully cited with accession numbers.
- **Reagents & Protocols**: A comprehensive Key Resources Table with RRIDs, catalog numbers, primer/probe sequences, and detailed step-by-step protocols for mechanical epithelial isolation, high-pressure freezing TEM, and the explant lysozyme secretion assay ensures high experimental reproducibility.
