### 1. Executive Summary
This computational genomics study integrates published human brain cell-type-specific proximity ligation-assisted ChIP-seq (H3K4me3-anchored PLAC-seq), ATAC-seq, and H3K27ac/H3K4me3 ChIP-seq datasets from cortical microglia, neurons, and oligodendrocytes with European-ancestry GWAS summary statistics for four neurodegenerative disorders—Alzheimer's disease (AD; Jansen 2019 and Kunkle 2019), Parkinson's disease (PD; Nalls 2019 ex-23andMe), multiple sclerosis (MS; Andlauer 2016), and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS; van Rheenen 2021)—alongside schizophrenia (SCZ; Trubetskoy 2022). Using stratified LD score regression (`sLDSC` controlling for the 97-annotation baseline-LD v2.2 model) and Hi-C coupled multimarker analysis of genomic annotation (`H-MAGMA`), followed by Expression Weighted Celltype Enrichment (`EWCE`) and `gprofiler2` pathway analysis, the authors report that SNP heritability is significantly enriched at microglial chromatin contact sites for AD, PD, and MS (and in neurons/oligodendrocytes for SCZ), while H-MAGMA yields the highest raw count of candidate risk genes in microglia across all four neurodegenerative diseases, with minimal cross-disease gene overlap. While the integrative pipeline is well-executed and reproducible, the study has a notable inconsistency between `sLDSC` and `H-MAGMA` for ALS (where sLDSC shows zero microglial heritability enrichment yet H-MAGMA claims microglial predominance driven by direct promoter/exon SNP assignment rather than distal loops), an under-controlled comparison of raw H-MAGMA gene counts across cell types, and severe linkage disequilibrium (LD) gene-cluster inflation in the extended MHC/HLA and 17q21.31 (`MAPT`/`KANSL1`) inversion regions.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Promoter/Exon SNP Assignment Confounding the Claim of "Microglial Chromatin Interaction" Enrichment in ALS (Fig 1d vs. Fig 2a & S4 Fig):**
   In the `sLDSC` partitioned heritability analysis (Fig 1d and S1 Fig), ALS exhibits **no significant heritability enrichment** in microglial, neuronal, or oligodendrocyte PLAC-seq bins (neither total, promoter, nor enhancer). Yet in Figure 2a–b, the authors emphasize that H-MAGMA identifies more ALS risk genes in microglia than in neurons or oligodendrocytes, concluding in the Abstract and Discussion that genetic risk for ALS is enriched in microglia via 3D chromatin interactions. Looking closely at the H-MAGMA construction in the Methods (line 132) and S4 Fig explains this discrepancy:
   - First, in standard H-MAGMA (Fig 2a), exonic and promoter SNPs ($1.5\text{ kb}$ upstream to $500\text{ bp}$ downstream of TSS for all Gencode v41 genes) are assigned directly to target genes in *all* cell types, and in S4A Fig, ALS microglial risk genes are overwhelmingly driven by `exon and promoters only` (124 genes) rather than `enhancer regions only` (47 genes).
   - Second, why did `exon and promoters only` yield different gene counts across microglia, neurons, and oligodendrocytes in S4A Fig? Because the authors restricted H-MAGMA SNPs to those overlapping cell-type PLAC-seq anchor bins (`H3K4me3`-anchored 5 kb bins), and the microglial PLAC-seq dataset has more total interaction bins ($108,802$ vs. $93,290$ in neurons and $61,895$ in oligodendrocytes) and broader H3K4me3 anchor coverage across the genome!
   - Third, in the downsampling experiment (Fig 2b), downsampling the *distal loop table* to $60,000$ interactions 10 times does not equalize the baseline exonic/promoter SNP assignments or the number of unique promoters covered if exonic/promoter SNPs were included without downsampling, and applying Dunn's non-parametric test across 10 random subsamples of the *same* underlying PLAC-seq dataset ($N = 10$ Monte Carlo draws) measures Monte Carlo resampling variance rather than independent biological replication! Hence, the five-asterisk significance values (`****` / `*****`) in Figure 2b and S4B Fig reflect arbitrary resampling sample size ($n = 10$ iterations) rather than biological variance across donors.
2. **Linkage Disequilibrium (LD) Gene-Cluster Inflation at the MHC (6p21) and 17q21.31 (`KANSL1`/`MAPT`) Loci:**
   H-MAGMA aggregates SNP $p$-values per gene using the `SNP-wise mean` model and accounts for intra-gene SNP-SNP LD via the 1000 Genomes European panel, but it does **not** perform conditional/fine-mapping analysis across neighboring genes that share the same GWAS LD block or shared enhancer loops. Consequently, a single causal signal in an extended LD block flags dozens of adjacent genes simultaneously:
   - In Figure 3a–b and the Discussion (lines 65, 86–88), the shared genes across MS, ALS, AD, and SCZ (`BAG6`, `NEU1`, `PRRC2A`, `PSMB8`, `PSMB8-AS1`, `PSMB9`, `TAP1`, `TAP2`, `TAPBPL`, `MICB`, `HSPA1A`, `HSPA1B`, `HSPA1L`, `TNF`, `HLA-DRB1`, `HLA-DRA`) all reside within the extended **MHC class I/II/III region on chromosome 6p21**!
   - Similarly, the shared genes across PD, ALS, and SCZ (`KANSL1`, `KANSL1-AS1`, `ARHGAP27`, `PLEKHM1`) all reside within the famous **$900\text{ kb}$ 17q21.31 (`MAPT`) inversion polymorphism**!
   Treating 10–15 co-localized genes inside the MHC or 17q21.31 inversion as independent H-MAGMA risk genes in downstream `gprofiler2` Gene Ontology enrichment (Fig 3c) heavily inflates antigen-presentation, proteasome, and autolysosome GO terms unless MHC genes are excluded or collapsed to one representative gene per LD locus (as is standard practice in LDSC and MAGMA pathway analyses).
3. **Unintended Anonymization Scrubbing Artifact in Methods Line 100 and Data Availability Line 48:**
   In line 100, the reference to Nott et al. (2019) [40] was partially scrubbed as `[AUTHOR et al.], 2019 [40]` while line 105 retains `Nott 2019 [40]`. Also, in line 11 of offset 2, `Tsartsalis et al. (2014) [58]` is a typo for `Tsartsalis et al. (2024) [58]` (correctly written as 2024 in the Fig 2D caption).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Partitioned heritability (`sLDSC`) for AD, PD, and MS is significantly enriched in microglial chromatin interaction bins (particularly enhancer-promoter loops in AD), whereas schizophrenia heritability is enriched in neuronal and oligodendrocyte chromatin interactions. `[Well Supported]` — The `sLDSC` regressions properly condition on the 97 baseline-LD v2.2 annotations and restrict to HapMap3 SNPs, providing rigorous genome-wide enrichment estimates for AD, PD, MS, and SCZ.
- **Claim 2:** Genetic risk for ALS is enriched in microglial chromatin interactions. `[Overstated]` — `sLDSC` (the gold-standard genome-wide heritability partitioning method used in Fig 1d) explicitly found **no enrichment** for ALS in microglial PLAC-seq bins (line 41). The higher raw number of ALS genes output by H-MAGMA in microglia (driven primarily by promoter/exon SNPs rather than distal enhancers, S4 Fig) should not be conflated with statistically significant cell-type heritability enrichment.
- **Claim 3:** Microglial H-MAGMA risk genes are predominantly expressed in microglia (`EWCE`) and map to largely disease-specific biological pathways. `[Moderately Supported]` — The `EWCE` cell-type expression enrichment (Fig 2d) and disease-specific pathway divergence (e.g., endocytosis/amyloid/lipoproteins in AD vs. T-cell/MHC signaling in MS) are biologically coherent, though pathway significance in `gprofiler2` is inflated by multi-gene LD clusters in the MHC and 17q21.31 regions.
