### 1. Executive Summary
This study profiles the transcriptome (bulk stranded total RNA-seq at PND8, PND15, and adulthood; $n = 6$ per stage across two batches) and chromatin accessibility landscape (Omni-ATAC-seq at PND15, $n = 6$, and adulthood, $n = 5$) of FACS-enriched mouse spermatogonial cells (SPGs), supplemented by scRNA-seq deconvolution (CIBERSORTx), integration with public histone ChIP-seq datasets, and transposable element (TE) accessibility quantification. While the multi-omic dataset is a valuable resource and the joint analysis of TF motifs and LTR/LINE accessibility is well organized, the study's central comparisons between postnatal (PND8/PND15) and adult stages are systematically confounded by unaddressed protocol divergences across ages: different enzymatic dissociation and Percoll protocols, different FACS surface marker antibodies ($\alpha_v$-integrin in pups vs. $\alpha_6$-integrin in adults), different RNA-seq library inputs (purified RNA from Qiagen columns in pups vs. direct cell lysis of 1,000 cells in adults), and a 5-fold difference in Omni-ATAC cell input (25,000 cells in PND15 vs. 5,000 cells in adults).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Systemic Protocol Confounding Between Postnatal and Adult Stages (Methods, Lines 56–76):** Every step of the experimental workflow differs between the postnatal (PND8/PND15) and adult samples:
   - *Dissociation:* PND8/PND15 testes (pooled from 2 pups) were digested with 0.25% trypsin-EDTA + DNase I and strained at 20 µm; adult testes were digested sequentially with collagenase type IV and trypsin-EDTA, strained at 40 µm, and centrifuged through a 30% Percoll gradient.
   - *FACS Gating Antibodies:* PND8/PND15 SPGs were sorted using anti-$\beta 2$M, anti-THY1 (clone 53-2.1), and anti-$\alpha_v$-integrin (CD51, clone RMV-7; note that Appendix 1 Key Resources Table line 137 erroneously labels CD51 as *"integrin a5"*), whereas adult SPGs were sorted using anti-$\beta 2$M, anti-THY1 (clone 30H-12), and anti-$\alpha_6$-integrin (CD49f, clone GoH3). Because $\alpha_v$-integrin and $\alpha_6$-integrin mark overlapping but non-identical testicular subpopulations, sorting with different surface markers directly alters the captured cell state distribution.
   - *RNA-seq Input Method:* PND8 and PND15 libraries were generated from column-extracted, heat-fragmented (94°C, 4 min) total RNA (Qiagen AllPrep), whereas adult RNA-seq libraries were prepared **directly from 1,000 intact sorted cells** lysed for 6 min at 85°C without RNA extraction. Direct-from-cell vs. purified-RNA library preparation introduces substantial technical bias in transcript length recovery, rRNA depletion efficiency, and dropout of low-abundance transcripts, which is completely collinear with the PND15-vs.-Adult contrast (where 2,483/2,493 DEGs were called).
   - *Omni-ATAC Cell Input:* PND15 Omni-ATAC used 25,000 sorted cells per reaction whereas adult Omni-ATAC used 5,000 cells per reaction with the same Nextera Tn5 protocol. A 5-fold change in nuclei-to-Tn5 ratio alters tagmentation efficiency, fragment length distribution, and background noise.
2. **Cellular Heterogeneity and Somatic/Spermatocyte Contamination Driving "Adult SPG" Signatures (Results, Lines 48, 55, 94):** CIBERSORTx deconvolution (Fig 1—figure supplement 2F–G) shows that estimated SSC purity drops from 99% at PND8 to 85% at PND15 and 82% in adults, with adult libraries containing differentiating SPGs and up to 10% "Other" cells. Crucially, the top up-regulated pathways and TFs in adult SPGs are *"cilium movement"*, *"sperm motility"*, *"male gamete generation"*, and *Rfx2* (a master transcriptional regulator of ciliogenesis/flagellar assembly whose expression peaks in pachytene spermatocytes and round spermatids). Because differential expression in bulk RNA-seq does not adjust for the CIBERSORTx cell-type proportions as covariates, genes highly expressed in contaminating meiotic/post-meiotic germ cells or somatic cells appear as "adult SPG up-regulated DEGs."
3. **Peak Calling Bias on Unbalanced Merged BAMs (Methods, Line 106):** To call nucleosome-free ATAC-seq peaks, the authors merged all aligned BAMs within PND15 ($n = 6$, 25,000 cells each) and adult ($n = 5$, 5,000 cells each) groups prior to MACS2 peak calling without downsampling to equalize total nucleosome-free fragment (NFF) depth across stages. Moreover, 76% of all called DARs (2,452 of 3,212) show increased accessibility in adults, which may reflect differences in library complexity and Tn5-to-chromatin ratio rather than true biological opening.
4. **Omission of Batch Covariate in Differential Expression and Accessibility Models (Methods, Lines 89 & 111):** All heatmaps (Fig 1C, F, G, H) explicitly display two experimental batches (`B1` and `B2`) with visible batch variation, yet the `limma-voom` and `edgeR` `glmQLFit` model specifications do not state whether `Batch` was included in the design matrix (`~ Batch + Stage`).
5. **Internal Arithmetic Discrepancy in DEG Counts (Results, Lines 53 & 57):** Line 53 states: *"2483 DEGs between PND15 and adult stage (914 down-regulated and 1579 up-regulated)."* However, $914 + 1,579 = 2,493$, not $2,483$. Line 57 subsequently uses $2,493$ (`93% (2325/2493)`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *SPGs undergo extensive transcriptional and chromatin accessibility remodeling between PND8, PND15, and adulthood, with 2,493 DEGs and 3,212 DARs between PND15 and adulthood.* `[Fragile]` — While developmental maturation of SPGs is biologically real, the magnitude and direction of the PND15-vs.-Adult shift are confounded by the switch from extracted RNA to 1,000-cell direct lysis (RNA-seq), 25,000 vs. 5,000 cell input (ATAC-seq), and $\alpha_v$- vs. $\alpha_6$-integrin sorting.
- **Claim 2:** *RFX2 and ciliary genes are specifically up-regulated in adult SPGs, suggesting a regulatory relationship between RFX2 and ciliary gene expression in adult SPGs.* `[Overstated]` — *Rfx2* and ciliary/sperm motility genes are canonical markers of spermatocytes and spermatids; given the 85–95% FACS purity and the presence of differentiating germ cells and 5–10% "Other" testicular cells in adult preparations, this signature likely reflects low-level meiotic/post-meiotic germ cell carryover in adult whole-testis dissociations (which contain millions of spermatocytes/spermatids absent at PND8/PND15) rather than intrinsic ciliogenesis programming inside undifferentiated SSCs.
- **Claim 3:** *Decreased chromatin accessibility at LTR retrotransposons (such as RLTR17 overlapping the TSS of pluripotency-associated lncRNAs Lncenc1/Platr18 and Platr14) correlates with repression of pluripotency programs in adult SPGs.* `[Moderately Supported]` — The locus-specific concordance between RLTR17 accessibility decrease and *Lncenc1* downregulation (Fig 5D) is biologically plausible and consistent with stemness downregulation from PND8/15 to adulthood.

- **Reagent & Parameter Reporting:** Software versions, command-line flags (TrimGalore, Salmon v0.9.1, Bowtie2 v2.3.5, alignmentSieve, MACS2 v2.2.7.1, csaw v1.20.0, edgeR v3.28.1), and antibody catalog numbers are thoroughly documented in the Methods and Key Resources Table (aside from the minor CD51 "integrin a5" vs. $\alpha_v$ label discrepancy).
