### 1. Executive Summary
This integrative functional genomics and statistical genetics study investigates the downstream transcriptional network of *MEF2C*, a neurodevelopmental transcription factor implicated in intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder (ASD), cognitive ability (IQ, educational attainment [EA]), and schizophrenia (SCZ). The authors integrate published RNA-seq differential expression profiles from human iPSC-derived neural stem cells (NSCs) and glutamatergic induced neurons (iNs) harboring CRISPR-engineered heterozygous or homozygous coding deletions (`DEL_het`, `DEL_hom`) or proximal TAD boundary CTCF-site deletions (`DEL_het_PB`, `DEL_hom_PB`) of *MEF2C* (Mohajeri et al., 2022; 8 DEG sets) with human fetal brain *MEF2C* ChIP-seq peaks using BETA (rank product $< 0.01$) to define 8 sets of putative direct *MEF2C* target genes. They then evaluate these 8 target sets using stratified LD score regression (`sLDSC`), gene-set polygenic risk score (`PRSice-2`) cross-prediction in an independent Irish cohort ($N = 1,512$), rare *de novo* mutation (DNM) enrichment (`denovolyzeR`), single-nucleus Expression Weighted Cell-type Enrichment (`EWCE`) in prenatal and adult human brains, ClueGO pathway analysis, and GTEx brain *trans*-eQTL mapping. Three large target gene sets (`NSCs_DEL_hom`, `iNs_DEL_het_PB`, `iNs_DEL_hom_PB`) consistently show Bonferroni-significant enrichment for SCZ/IQ/EA common-variant heritability, cross-trait SCZ-PRS prediction of IQ, ID/DD missense/LoF *de novo* mutations, and prenatal/adult excitatory and inhibitory neuronal expression, with NSC targets enriched for neurogenesis and iN targets enriched for mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation. Finally, the authors identify a cerebellar *trans*-eQTL linking the IQ-associated *MEF2C* variant `rs6893807` to the SCZ-prioritized mitophagy gene *BNIP3L*. The analytical framework is well-controlled and thoughtfully executed, with a few methodological limitations regarding ChIP-seq context and gene-set size power that should be clarified.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Unreplicated ChIP-seq from a Mismatched Developmental/Cellular Context (Section 2.3):** To filter NSC and iN differentially expressed genes for direct *MEF2C* binding, the authors used a single-replicate ($n = 1$) *MEF2C* ChIP-seq dataset from primary human fetal brain cultures [18] aligned to `hg19`. While the authors candidly note this limitation in the Discussion, two technical aspects warrant clarification: (i) with a single ChIP-seq replicate against input DNA, Irreproducible Discovery Rate (IDR) filtering could not be performed, leaving peak calls susceptible to single-library noise; and (ii) enhancer binding of *MEF2C* is highly stage- and cell-type-specific between proliferating neural progenitors (NSCs) and post-mitotic glutamatergic neurons (iNs), which may explain why homozygous coding deletion in iNs (`iNs_DEL_hom`) yielded only 145 target genes whereas indirect TAD boundary deletion (`iNs_DEL_hom_PB`) yielded 1,174 target genes. Additionally, in Section 2.3 (line 45), the text cites *"Burrows Wheeler Aligner (BWA; http://bowtie-bio.sourceforge.net/bowtie2 ) [ 19 ]"*—inadvertently pairing BWA with the URL for Bowtie2.
2. **Gene-Set Size Disparity and Competitive Null Calibration in Gene-Set PRS (Table 1, Fig 3):** As noted in the Discussion, the three gene sets that achieved Bonferroni-significant enrichment across sLDSC, PRS, DNM, and EWCE (`NSCs_DEL_hom` = 1,170 genes; `iNs_DEL_het_PB` = 1,034 genes; `iNs_DEL_hom_PB` = 1,174 genes) are $2.5\times$ to $8\times$ larger than the other five gene sets (145 to 412 genes). While `sLDSC` (ratio of proportion of $h^2$ to proportion of SNPs controlling for the 53-annotation baseline model), `denovolyzeR` (Poisson rate ratio controlling for gene-specific mutation rates and background genes), and `EWCE` (100,000 equal-length random gene sets) explicitly account for gene-set size, the `PRSice-2` validation generated empirical p-values by permuting the *phenotype labels* 10,000 times (`self-contained` null) rather than drawing 10,000 size-matched random sets of brain/iN-expressed genes (`competitive` null). Because any random set of ~1,100 neuronally expressed genes in a highly polygenic trait like SCZ or IQ will capture non-zero polygenic variance ($R^2 > 0$) under phenotype permutation, utilizing PRSice-2's competitive gene-set permutation (`--perm-set`) or comparing $R^2$ per SNP against matched brain-expressed gene sets would provide a stricter test of specificity above general neural expression.
3. **Minor Discrepancy in Trio Sample Sizes Between Methods Text and Table 2:** In Materials and Methods ("Analysis of De Novo Mutations", line 70), the trio sample sizes are reported as `SCZ (n = 3447 trios)` and `ID and/or DD (n = 4,851 trios)`, whereas the column headers of Table 2 (line 148) list `SCZ n = 3394 trios` and `ID/DD n = 4485 trios`. The authors should clarify in the Table 2 footnote or Methods text whether the smaller numbers in Table 2 reflect the effective trio counts after excluding DNMs with posterior probability $< 0.00781$.
4. **Multiple-Testing Scope and *cis*-Mediation Check for the *BNIP3L* *trans*-eQTL (Section 3.7):** The authors tested 10 LD-independent *MEF2C* GWAS SNPs against 15 prioritized SCZ target genes (and subsequently 300 mitochondrial target genes) using GTEx data, reporting a significant *trans*-eQTL between `rs6893807` and *BNIP3L* in the cerebellar hemisphere ($P = 1.60 \times 10^{-5}$, adjusted $P = 0.025$). From $P_{\text{raw}} = 1.60 \times 10^{-5}$ to $P_{\text{adj}} = 0.025$, the correction factor is $1,562.5$ (likely corresponding to $10\text{ SNPs} \times 15\text{ genes} \times \sim 10\text{–}13\text{ GTEx brain tissues}$), but the exact number of GTEx tissues queried and the multiple-testing denominator are not stated in the Methods or Results. Furthermore, to support the hypothesis that `rs6893807` regulates *BNIP3L* in *trans* via *MEF2C* transcription factor activity, the authors should report whether `rs6893807` is also associated with *MEF2C* *cis*-expression or splicing in GTEx brain tissue or fetal brain eQTL catalogs.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Putative direct *MEF2C* target gene sets dysregulated in human NSCs and iNs are enriched for common-variant heritability of SCZ, IQ, and EA, as well as rare LoF/missense *de novo* mutations in ID/DD and ASD.** `[Well Supported]` The convergence of sLDSC (with 4 negative control traits showing null enrichment in S10 Table) and `denovolyzeR` (with synonymous mutations and unaffected siblings showing null enrichment in Table 2) provides robust genetic support.
- **Claim 2: *MEF2C* target genes shift from regulating neurogenesis and lineage differentiation in NSCs to regulating mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation and energy production in mature glutamatergic iNs.** `[Well Supported]` ClueGO pathway analyses using cell-type-matched expressed background genes ($\text{TPM} \ge 1$) clearly demonstrate this developmental divergence.
- **Claim 3: Genetic variation at *MEF2C* (`rs6893807`) exerts a *trans*-eQTL effect on the SCZ risk and mitophagy gene *BNIP3L*.** `[Moderately Supported]` The statistical association survives multiple-testing correction ($P_{\text{adj}} = 0.025$) and aligns with the downregulation of *BNIP3L* upon *MEF2C* disruption in iNs, though replication in larger brain eQTL consortia (e.g., PsychENCODE or MetaBrain) and direct demonstration of *cis*-mediation via *MEF2C* levels would solidify this mechanism.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** The underlying RNA-seq data (`GSE204778`), GWAS summary statistics (PGC, CTG, SSGAC), DNM trio tables (S4 Table), prenatal/adult snRNA-seq atlases, and GTEx portal resources are all publicly available and clearly cited, with extensive supplementary tables (S1–S28 Tables) documenting every gene set and enrichment output.
- **Code & Parameters:** Software versions and key command-line flags (MACS2 `-f BAM -g hg -q 0.01`, BETA `RP < 0.01`, ClueGO v2.5.9, PRSice-2 p-value thresholds, denovolyzeR posterior probability cutoff `0.00781`) are thoroughly specified.
