### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript follows the canonical "TCGA/GEO LASSO-Cox X-related gene signature + CIBERSORT + oncoPredict + single-siRNA cell line assay" template, focusing on 543 antibody-dependent cellular phagocytosis (ADCP)-related genes (from Kamber et al., 2021) in metastatic cutaneous melanoma (`TCGA-SKCM`, $n=351$, plus `GSE7553`, `GSE46517`, `GSE19234`, `GSE54467`, `GSE65904`, and `GSE189889`). The authors construct a 6-gene risk score (`NDRG1`, `HRAS`, `KPNA2`, `ICAM1`, `DOCK10`, `CDC20`) and perform functional knockdown of `DOCK10` in A375 melanoma cells. Critically, the manuscript contains a fundamental sign contradiction at the heart of its thesis: in the multivariate Cox risk score formula, `DOCK10` has a **negative** coefficient (`-0.13036`), meaning high `DOCK10` expression lowers the risk score and predicts **better** patient survival (a protective factor), yet the authors claim that `DOCK10` is upregulated in metastatic melanoma (`FC = 2.10`), higher in the high-risk group (Fig 8E), and acts as a pro-metastatic oncogene whose knockdown suppresses proliferation, migration, invasion, and `CDH5`/`ITGB1` expression! Compounded by data leakage during feature selection, torture-phrased / machine-paraphrased text ("antibody-arbitrated", "persuading anti-tumor immune responses", "not been extensively deliberate", "immunotherapy rejoinder", "castoff to concept a prognostic model... as trails", "beneficial possessions", "we industrialized an ARG-grounded prognostic model"), and use of only a single unvalidated siRNA without Western blot confirmation of DOCK10 protein knockdown, the study is fundamentally flawed.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Direct Sign Contradiction Between the Cox Prognostic Model (`-0.13036`) and the Functional/Clinical Claims for `DOCK10`:**
   - On line 144 (and line 137), the multivariate Cox regression risk score is explicitly defined as:
     $$\text{Risk Score} = -0.11826 \times \text{NDRG1} + 0.12952 \times \text{HRAS} + 0.22421 \times \text{KPNA2} - 0.13578 \times \text{ICAM1} \mathbf{- 0.13036 \times \text{DOCK10}} + 0.05932 \times \text{CDC20}$$
   - Because `DOCK10` has a negative regression coefficient ($\beta = -0.13036, p = 0.003$), higher expression of `DOCK10` *decreases* the Risk Score and is associated with **longer overall survival** (favorable prognosis, just like `ICAM1` and `NDRG1`, consistent with `DOCK10` being predominantly expressed in hematopoietic/immune cells infiltrating the tumor).
   - Yet in Fig 8E, the authors claim to compare `DOCK10` expression between high-risk and low-risk groups, and throughout the Abstract, Results (lines 137, 209–217), and Discussion, they treat `DOCK10` as a pro-metastatic driver of poor prognosis and therapeutic target for inhibition! How can a gene with a negative weight in the linear Risk Score formula be elevated in the high-risk group and act as a poor-prognosis oncogene? This fatal internal contradiction invalidates the entire integrative premise of the paper.
2. **Data Leakage Between Training and Validation Splits in TCGA (`n = 351`):**
   - In the Methods ("Identification of ADCP-related genes", line 52), the authors state: "we performed univariate Cox regression analysis on the TCGA cohort and identified 181 genes significantly associated with survival (p < 0.05). By overlapping these survival-associated genes with 543 ADCP-related genes, we identified nine ADCP-related genes..." Then, in the next section (line 57), they state that "a training set and a validation set were randomly assigned to the TCGA cohort in a 6:4 proportion" and on line 61 add: "An additional analysis using the entire TCGA dataset (n = 351) was performed for model development."
   - Screening the candidate survival genes across the *entire* TCGA cohort ($n=351$) prior to evaluating Kaplan-Meier curves on the 40% "TCGA-Validation" split (Fig 2B) constitutes classic test-set data leakage, guaranteeing optimistic survival separation in the internal validation set. Furthermore, no multiple-testing correction (e.g., Benjamini-Hochberg FDR) was applied during the univariate Cox screen of 543 genes.
3. **Inconsistent Description of the 9-Gene Intersection (Fig 1A vs. Methods):**
   - In the Fig 1A caption (line 131), the authors state that the Venn diagram shows the intersection between "887 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) from the GSE46517 and GSE7553 datasets and 543 ADCP-related genes from Kamber et al. (2021). This intersection yielded 9 genes."
   - However, on line 52 and line 133, the text states that the 9 genes were obtained by overlapping the **181 univariate Cox survival-associated genes** with theADCP-related genes (or a 3-way intersection of 887 DEGs, 181 survival genes, and 543 ADCP genes). If $887 \cap 543 = 9$, then all 9 DEGs were somehow also significant in univariate Cox regression; or else Fig 1A's caption misdescribes a 3-way intersection as a 2-way intersection.
   - In addition, the Fig 1B–C caption claims that the heatmaps show expression profiles across "primary tumors, metastatic tumors, and normal tissues in the GSE46517 and GSE7553 datasets," whereas the Methods (line 45) state that `GSE7553` contains 14 primary and 40 metastatic samples and `GSE46517` contains 31 primary and 73 metastatic samples, with no normal tissue cohort described in the DEG contrast.
4. **Inadequate Wet-Lab Validation Controls and Assay Flaws:**
   - **Single Unvalidated siRNA & No DOCK10 Protein Blot:** Knockdown of `DOCK10` in A375 cells was performed using a single siRNA sequence (`5'-GAUCGCAGCUACUACGAAU-3'`, line 104) without a second independent siRNA duplex to rule out off-target seed effects. Remarkably, while the authors performed Western blotting for `Ki67`, `ITGB1`, `CDH5`, and `GAPDH` (Fig 8L), they **never blotted for DOCK10 protein itself** to confirm protein-level knockdown!
   - **Western Blotting for Ki67 (~359 kDa) on Standard SDS-PAGE:** In the Methods (line 110) and Fig 8L, the authors report standard SDS-PAGE and PVDF transfer to blot for `Ki67` alongside `ITGB1` (~130 kDa), `CDH5` (~130 kDa), and `GAPDH` (36 kDa), and use `Ki67` mRNA qPCR (Fig 8H) instead of any direct cell proliferation/viability assay (such as CCK-8, EdU incorporation, or colony formation).
   - **No Sample Sizes or Error Bar Definitions in Fig 8:** Neither the Methods nor the Fig 8 legend states the number of independent biological replicates ($n$) or the statistical test (e.g., *t*-test vs. Mann-Whitney) used for the in vitro panels Fig 8F–L.
5. **Pervasive "Tortured Phrases" / Machine-Paraphrasing Artifacts:** The manuscript contains numerous hallmark synonyms generated by automated paraphrasing tools: "antibody-arbitrated" (antibody-mediated), "persuading anti-tumor immune responses" (inducing), "developing resistance to these handlings" (treatments), "not been extensively deliberate" (studied/discussed), "immunotherapy rejoinder" (response), "castoff to concept a prognostic model... as trails" (used to construct... as follows), "beneficial possessions by additional activating" (effects by further activating), and "we industrialized an ARG-grounded prognostic model" (developed an ARG-based prognostic model).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: The 6-gene ARG risk score is an independent prognostic biomarker for overall survival in metastatic melanoma.** `[Moderately Supported]` — The linear combination of cell-cycle/proliferation markers (`CDC20`, `KPNA2`, `HRAS`, positive weights) and immune/adhesion markers (`ICAM1`, `DOCK10`, `NDRG1`, negative weights) stratifies OS in `GSE65904` ($n=210$) and `GSE19234` ($n=38$), though it fails to reach significance in `GSE54467` ($n=79$, Fig 2D) and essentially recapitulates the well-known prognostic contrast between proliferating/immune-cold vs. immune-infiltrated ("hot") melanomas rather than specifically measuring macrophage ADCP activity.
- **Claim 2: `DOCK10` is a pro-metastatic driver associated with high risk in the prognostic model and represents a therapeutic target for inhibition.** `[Methodologically Flawed]` — Directly contradicted by the model's own negative Cox coefficient (`-0.13036`), under which inhibiting `DOCK10` *increases* the patient's predicted hazard of death.
- **Claim 3: `DOCK10` knockdown inhibits proliferation, endothelial adhesion, migration, and invasion via `CDH5` and `ITGB1` in A375 cells.** `[Fragile]` — Relies on a single siRNA without DOCK10 protein validation, without direct proliferation assays, and with unspecified biological replicate numbers.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Public accessions (`TCGA-SKCM`, `GSE7553`, `GSE46517`, `GSE19234`, `GSE54467`, `GSE65904`, `GSE189889`) and a Figshare archive (`29143184`) are provided.
- **Missing Details:** The manuscript does not provide the R scripts used for model fitting, the exact $\lambda$ penalty parameter selected in `glmnet` LASSO cross-validation, the numeric table of univariate and multivariate Cox hazard ratios and 95% CIs (only shown graphically in Fig 4A), or the molecular weight markers and biological replicate counts for the Western blots in Fig 8L / S4 Fig.
