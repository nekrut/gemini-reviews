### 1. Executive Summary
This study screens 106 epigenetic/chromatin-modifying compounds (spanning 35–36 target classes) across five patient-derived gliomasphere lines (`357, 372, 385, 408, 412`), selects 12 representative compounds for 7-day $\text{IC}_{50}$ profiling and bulk RNA-seq across 12 gliomasphere lines (156 total RNA-seq libraries; $N = 1$ per cell line × treatment condition), and performs ATAC-seq on four gliomasphere lines treated with three BET bromodomain inhibitors (CPI203, JQ1, OTX015). Using gene-set overlap and Weighted Gene Co-expression Network Analysis (WGCNA), the authors identify the BET inhibitor CPI203 as the most consistent down-regulator of DNA replication genes and of an *FBXO5*-centered cell-cycle module, and report that higher baseline *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* expression distinguishes a more drug-sensitive PCA cluster (Cluster 1) from a more resistant cluster (Cluster 2). While the multi-line gliomasphere screening concept and ATAC-seq validation of BET inhibitor chromatin closing are valuable, the study has major methodological weaknesses: (1) a mathematically flawed $\Delta\text{AUC}$ metric in the primary screen that subtracts a single-dose Day 28 viability (500 nM) from a two-dose Day 3 viability (1 µM and 10 µM); (2) an unreplicated RNA-seq design ($N = 1$ per line × drug) that relies on raw $\ge 2$-fold change cutoffs and conflates sampling noise with cell-line-specific drug responses; (3) severe confounding of the *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* sensitivity cluster by *IDH1* mutation status (3 of 4 lines in Cluster 2 are *IDH1*-mutant); and (4) lack of public repository accession numbers for the 156 RNA-seq and ATAC-seq datasets.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Confounded $\Delta\text{AUC}$ Metric and Invalid $t$-Test in the Primary 106-Compound Screen (Figure 1):** In the initial screen on 5 gliomasphere lines (Results line 41; Methods line 109), Day 3 cell viability was measured at two concentrations (**1 µM and 10 µM**), whereas Day 28 viability was measured at a single, lower concentration (**500 nM**), with only two technical/biological replicates ($n = 2$). First, an "Area Under the Curve" ($\text{AUC}_{28\text{d}}$) cannot be defined from a single concentration point (500 nM) without an explicit baseline anchor. Second, computing $\Delta\text{AUC} = \text{AUC}_{3\text{d}}(1\text{ µM}, 10\text{ µM}) - \text{AUC}_{28\text{d}}(500\text{ nM})$ and computing a volcano plot $t$-test between Day 3 (1–10 µM) and Day 28 (500 nM) viabilities (Figure 1F) directly confounds incubation time (3 days vs. 28 days) with a 2-to-20-fold change in drug concentration. Third, ranking 36 drug classes and testing each class against the lowest-ranked class (BRPF, which serves as a post-hoc minimum rather than a vehicle control; Figure 1E) inflates statistical significance due to post-hoc reference selection.
2. **Unreplicated RNA-Seq Design ($N = 1$ per Cell Line × Drug Condition) Drives Apparent Target Heterogeneity (Figures 2–3):** As stated in Methods line 114, only **one** RNA-seq library was sequenced for each of the 156 cell-line × condition pairs (12 lines × [12 drugs + 1 control]). Because $N = 1$ per condition precludes within-line variance estimation or statistical differential expression testing (`DESeq2` p-values/FDR), the authors defined "target genes" using an unadjusted $\ge 2$-fold change threshold on `rlog()`-normalized counts (lines 60, 67, 119). In single-replicate RNA-seq, technical variance and shot noise frequently produce $\ge 2$-fold fluctuations in lowly/moderately expressed transcripts, which naturally fail to overlap across lines. Thus, the central claim in Figure 3A–C that *"most up-regulated and down-regulated genes were unique to one specific cell line/compound combination"* is heavily confounded by unreplicated measurement noise.
3. **Confounding of PCA Clusters and *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* Differential Expression by *IDH1* Mutation Status (Figure 2F–H):** In Figure 2F–H (line 62), PCA of baseline transcriptomes divides the 12 lines into Cluster 1 (8 lines, more drug-sensitive, high *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* expression) and Cluster 2 (4 lines, more drug-resistant, low *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* expression). However, in the Discussion (line 99), the authors disclose that **3 of the 4 cell lines in Cluster 2 carry an *IDH1* mutation**. Under current neuro-oncology classification (WHO CNS5), *IDH1*-mutant astrocytomas/gliomas are a biologically distinct entity from *IDH*-wildtype glioblastoma, with distinct global DNA methylation (G-CIMP), transcriptome architecture, proliferation rates, and *CDKN2A/B* deletion frequencies. Slower proliferation rates in *IDH1*-mutant lines also directly affect 7-day metabolic viability assays (MTT/MTS). Presenting *CDKN2A/CDKN2B* as a novel predictive biomarker of epigenetic drug sensitivity in glioblastoma without stratifying by *IDH1* mutation status and *CDKN2A/B* genomic copy-number deletion status is misleading.
4. **Multiple Internal Text, Gene-Name, and Assay-Name Errors:**
   - **Gene vs. Drug Name Error (`MLL5` vs. `ML228`, Line 80):** In Results line 80, the text states: *"While CPI203 was the most effective compound at reducing FBXO5 gene expression specifically, **MLL5** had a larger effect on the FBXO5 gene module as a whole."* `MLL5` (*KMT2E*) is a histone methyltransferase gene, not one of the 12 tested drugs; as shown in the Abstract (line 21) and Figure 4B caption (line 86), the compound is **ML228** (a HIF activator).
   - **Cell Line ID and Novelty Error in Figure 4C Caption (Line 86):** Line 86 states that JQ1 and OTX015 were tested on *"an additional four cell lines (272, 412, 357, 385)"*, and the next sentence says *"four lines (357, 372, 385 and 412)"*. Not only is `272` a typo for `372`, but lines `357, 372, 385, 412` are four of the original five lines from Figure 1, not "additional" cell lines.
   - **Duplicate Panel Letter in Figure 3 Caption (Line 73):** The Figure 3 caption labels two different panels as `D.` (both the GO enrichment of the 103 CPI203 genes and the WGCNA module bar plot), causing a mismatch with `Fig 3E` and `Fig 3F–G` in the main text.
   - **Assay Contradiction (MTS vs. MTT) and Histone Mutation Typo:** Results line 41 states cell viabilities were measured via an **MTS** assay, whereas Methods lines 109 and 114 state they were measured by **MTT** (which requires DMSO crystal solubilization). Additionally, Introduction line 31 refers to the canonical DIPG histone mutation H3K27M as *"H3K23M"*, and the number of target genes in the 106-drug library is stated as 36 in lines 16, 41, and 109 but 35 in line 44.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *BET bromodomain inhibitors (CPI203, JQ1, OTX015) consistently down-regulate DNA replication genes and an FBXO5-associated cell-cycle transcriptional module across gliomasphere lines, accompanied by global and locus-specific loss of chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq peaks).* `[Well Supported]` — Because the CPI203 signature (103 shared down-regulated genes) and WGCNA *FBXO5* module suppression were replicated across 12 cell lines and validated with two additional BET inhibitors (JQ1, OTX015) and ATAC-seq across 4 lines (Figures 3–4), the multi-line design effectively compensates for the lack of within-line replicates for this specific claim.
- **Claim 2:** *Most chromatin-modifying compounds induce highly cell-line-specific transcriptional targets with minimal overlap across glioblastomas.* `[Fragile]` — Cannot be distinguished from technical noise due to the $N = 1$ RNA-seq design per cell line × drug condition and unadjusted 2-fold cutoff.
- **Claim 3:** *High baseline expression of CDKN2A and CDKN2B distinguishes epigenetic drug-sensitive from drug-resistant glioblastomas.* `[Overstated]` — Heavily confounded by the enrichment of *IDH1*-mutant glioma lines (3/4 lines) in the resistant Cluster 2 ($n = 4$ vs. $n = 8$).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability Deficiency:** Despite generating 156 bulk RNA-seq datasets and 16+ ATAC-seq datasets on human patient-derived glioma lines, no NCBI GEO, SRA, or dbGaP accession numbers are provided; the Data Availability statement (line 140) points only to a lab GitHub repository. Deposition of raw and processed sequencing data in a permanent public archive (e.g., GEO) is essential.
- **Protocol & Metadata Completeness:** The exact concentrations used for each cell line × drug pair in the RNA-seq experiment (500 nM vs. 250 nM) and the molecular characterization of the gliomasphere lines (including *IDH1* status) should be explicitly tabulated in the main text or Methods rather than referenced only in supplementary figures.
