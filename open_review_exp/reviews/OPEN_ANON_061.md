### 1. Executive Summary
This study characterizes the putative mitochondrial iron-sulfur (Fe-S) cluster assembly chaperone HscB (`PBANKA_0821000`) in the rodent malaria model *Plasmodium berghei* and evaluates its utility in a dual-gene deletion strategy for generating a late liver stage-arresting replication-competent (LARC) genetically attenuated parasite (GAP) alongside `PlasMei2` (`PBANKA_1122300`). Using endogenous C-terminal GFP tagging, PlasmoGEM knockout vectors, automated live imaging in HeLa cells, immunofluorescence microscopy (LC3B, MSP1, UIS4, *Tg*Hsp70), IVIS bioluminescence in albino mice, and high-dose intravenous sporozoite breakthrough assays ($5 \times 10^3$ and $1 \times 10^5$ sporozoites) in C57BL/6 mice, the authors show that *Pb*HscB localizes to the mitochondrion, is dispensable for mosquito stage development, and causes a marked growth defect and a 2.6-day prepatency delay during exo-erythrocytic development. When combined with *Pb*Mei2 deletion via 5-FC negative selection, the resulting *Pb*HscB-*Pb*Mei2-dKO line produces normal salivary gland sporozoite numbers, invades mouse livers in vivo, and shows zero breakthrough blood-stage infections over 41 days following challenge with $100,000$ sporozoites ($n = 6$ mice). While the genetic engineering and phenotypic characterization are solid, the manuscript exhibits several statistical unit-of-analysis issues (pseudoreplication of individual parasites/oocysts across experiments, improper application of unpaired $t$-tests to binary count proportions in Fig 2C), omitted colocalization statistics despite describing them in the Methods, and absence of protective immunization/challenge experiments to substantiate the vaccine-efficacy framing.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Pseudoreplication in Oocyst and Liver-Stage Size Measurements (Figs 2A, 3A, 4A, S2A):** In Figures 2A and 4A, the authors measure the size of $n = 500$ individual parasites from "one out of three experiments" and apply a Kruskal-Wallis test across the 500 cells per group ($p < 0.0001$). Similarly, in Figure 3A and S2A, $n = 240$ or $n = 300$ individual oocysts from 10 mosquitoes in a single representative experiment are treated as independent observations in a Kruskal-Wallis test. Individual parasites within the same culture well or oocysts within the same mosquito midgut are technical pseudoreplicates; statistical inference should be performed on biological replicates (independent infection experiments or mosquito cohorts, e.g., $N = 3$ or $N = 4$ independent means/medians) or via a hierarchical/linear mixed-effects model accounting for experiment and host-well clustering.
2. **Inappropriate Statistical Test for Binary Proportion Data (Fig 2C):** In Figure 2C, LC3-staining on the parasitophorous vacuole membrane (PVM) was quantified manually at 24 and 48 hpi ($n = 200$ WT 24h, $107$ KO 24h, $137$ WT 48h, $81$ KO 48h), and significance is reported using an "unpaired t-test; **** represents $p < 0.0001$." If LC3 positivity is scored as a binary classification per parasite across pooled counts, a Fisher's exact test or $\chi^2$ test is required; if it represents replicate percentages across independent experiments, the number of independent biological replicates ($N$) must be stated rather than the pooled parasite counts ($n$), and bounded percentage data with small $N$ should be analyzed with appropriate generalized linear models or paired/stratified tests.
3. **Inconsistent Figure 4A Caption vs. Plotted Groups:** The caption for Figure 4A states: *"HeLa cells were infected with either WT or Pb HscB-KO sporozoites and imaged at indicated time points..."* yet the main text (lines 69) and the p-value annotations in the caption (`p 24h1868-Mei2KO = 0.9528`, `p 48h1868-Mei2KO = 0.076`, `p 56h1868-Mei2KO = 0.0674`) clearly include WT, *Pb*HscB-KO, *Pb*Mei2-KO, and *Pb*HscB-*Pb*Mei2-dKO. This copy-paste error from Figure 2A's caption should be corrected.
4. **Omitted Quantitative Colocalization Values (Fig 1B, S1D Fig):** In the Experimental Procedures (line 28 of offset 2) and Supporting Information caption (S1D Fig), the authors describe calculating the Pearson correlation coefficient (PCC) and Manders' overlap coefficient (MOC) using the FIJI Coloc2 plugin between *Pb*HscB-GFP and *Tg*Hsp70 at 48 hpi. However, the numerical PCC and MOC values (and the number of imaged parasites over which they were quantified) are never reported in the Results text or captions.
5. **Sample Size and Statistical Power in Breakthrough Infection Assays (Fig 4F):** The central safety claim that *Pb*HscB-*Pb*Mei2-dKO provides a complete block of liver-to-blood transition rests on $n = 6$ C57BL/6 mice injected with $100,000$ sporozoites monitored for 41 days (with $n = 4$ WT, $n = 3$ *Pb*HscB-KO, and $n = 4$ *Pb*Mei2-KO controls). While $0/6$ breakthroughs at a $100,000$-sporozoite dose is encouraging (upper 95% binomial Clopper-Pearson confidence bound of $\sim 45.9\%$ per mouse), the sample size of 6 mice from a single experiment is modest for ruling out low-frequency breakthrough events, which the authors themselves note in the Discussion can occur at very low rates in GAPs.
6. **Absence of Genetic Complementation for *Pb*HscB-KO:** While the phenotype of *Pb*HscB-KO (delayed liver growth and 2.6-day prepatency delay) is consistent with the prior barseq screen (19-fold barcode reduction) and only Clone 1 was phenotyped in detail after limiting dilution, no genetic add-back/complementation control was performed, nor was a second independent knockout clone phenotyped in parallel to formally exclude off-target spontaneous mutations acquired during blood-stage selection.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Pb*HscB localizes to the parasite mitochondrion during oocyst and liver stages and is dispensable for mosquito-stage sporogony. `[Well Supported]` — Live MitoView 650 costaining in oocysts and anti-*Tg*Hsp70 immunofluorescence at 48 and 56 hpi in liver stages, combined with unaltered oocyst sizes and salivary gland sporozoite counts across 4 independent experiments, solidly establish mitochondrial localization and mosquito-stage dispensability.
- **Claim 2:** Single deletion of *Pb*HscB severely impairs liver-stage growth and delays MSP1 expression and prepatency by 2.6 days, but permits breakthrough blood-stage infections. `[Well Supported]` — In vitro size tracking, delayed MSP1 expression at 96 hpi, increased LC3 retention at 48 hpi, and in vivo flow-cytometric parasitemia curves at both $5,000$ and $100,000$ sporozoite doses consistently demonstrate a severe growth delay without complete developmental arrest.
- **Claim 3:** Double deletion of *Pb*HscB and *Pb*Mei2 (*Pb*HscB-*Pb*Mei2-dKO) yields a late liver stage-arresting replication-competent (LARC) GAP that robustly blocks liver-to-blood transition. `[Moderately Supported]` — The absence of breakthrough parasitemia in $6/6$ mice over 41 days at $100,000$ sporozoites and positive 44 hpi IVIS liver bioluminescence support the arrest phenotype. However, calling the double mutant a "LARC" (late liver stage-arresting replication-competent) GAP is somewhat in tension with the data showing that *Pb*HscB-*Pb*Mei2-dKO exhibits the early/mid-liver-stage stunted size phenotype of *Pb*HscB-KO (Fig 4A), significantly lower 44 hpi bioluminescence than WT (Fig 4E), and zero MSP1 expression. Moreover, because no immunization and challenge protection experiments were conducted, any claims regarding its superior immunogenicity or vaccine efficacy remain speculative.
- **Claim 4:** Loss of *Pb*HscB impairs de novo heme synthesis via aconitase/IRE-mediated translational repression of ALAS or electron transport chain deficiency. `[Fragile]` — As acknowledged in the Discussion, no biochemical assays of Fe-S cluster assembly, mitochondrial membrane potential, respiratory chain complex activity, or heme levels were performed; these remain plausible but untested hypotheses.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Gene Identifiers & Constructs:** PlasmoGEM vector identifiers (`PbGEM-239805` for `PBANKA_0821000` and `PbGEM-300555` for `PBANKA_1122300`) and all PCR integration/cloning primer sequences (Primers 1–10 and GFP tagging primers) are fully specified in the Experimental Procedures.
- **Reagent & Antibody Metadata:** Catalog numbers and dilutions for commercial antibodies (Roche anti-GFP `11814460001`, MBL anti-LC3 `M152-3`, Invitrogen secondary antibodies), stains (MitoView 650, Hoechst 33342, RediJect D-Luciferin), and imaging software (INCell Developer Toolbox 1.10.0 segmentation thresholds of $10\text{--}1,200\ \mu\text{m}^2$) are clearly documented.
- **Raw Data Availability:** While summary plots are present in the figures, raw numerical tables of the automated microscopy measurements, flow cytometry counts, and colocalization coefficients (PCC/MOC) are not provided as supplementary spreadsheets.
