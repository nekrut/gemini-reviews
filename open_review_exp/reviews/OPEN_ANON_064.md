### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript describes a protocol for reinforcing thermo-responsive (UpCell™) MDA-MB-231 breast cancer cell sheets using sequential layering of $250\ \mu\text{L}$ Geltrex™ (air-dried for 1 hour at room temperature) and $500\ \mu\text{L}$ 7.5% w/v gelatin (10 minutes at room temperature), followed by subcutaneous flank implantation into female NU/J nude mice ($n = 3$) compared against orthotopic mammary fat pad injection of $5 \times 10^6$ MDA-MB-231 cells in suspension ($n = 3$) and an untreated control ($n = 1$). After 8 weeks, the authors report average tumor diameters of $5.7\text{ mm}$ (subcutaneous cell sheet) and $5.8\text{ mm}$ (orthotopic suspension) and present qualitative H&E sections of primary tumors and putative liver/spleen metastases. Despite the practical goal of handling fragile mesenchymal-like cell sheets, the study is compromised by fundamental experimental design flaws: it confounds the delivery format (cell sheet + Geltrex/gelatin scaffold vs. bare cell suspension) with both anatomic implantation site (subcutaneous dorsal flank vs. orthotopic mammary fat pad) and initial cell number ($1.5 \times 10^6$ vs. $5 \times 10^6$ cells), uses crystal violet DNA/protein staining on fixed cells as a proxy for "cell viability," lacks all quantitative metrics of mechanical integrity, tumor growth kinetics, and metastatic incidence, and is severely underpowered ($n = 3$ mice per group).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Triple-Confounded In Vivo Experimental Design (Format vs. Anatomic Site vs. Cell Dose vs. ECM Matrix):**
   The central in vivo comparison (Section 2.5 and 3.2) contrasts Group B ($5 \times 10^6$ MDA-MB-231 cells in suspension injected **orthotopically** into the fourth mammary fat pad **without** Geltrex/gelatin) against Group C ($1.5 \times 10^6$ seeded MDA-MB-231 cells fabricated as a sheet with **Geltrex™ + Gelatin** implanted **subcutaneously** on the dorsal midline flank). Because anatomic microenvironment (subcutaneous dorsum vs. mammary fat pad), extracellular matrix co-implantation (basement membrane extract Geltrex™ is well known to promote tumor take and angiogenesis on its own), and cell dose ($1.5 \times 10^6$ vs. $5 \times 10^6$) all differ simultaneously, it is impossible to attribute differences in necrosis ($<10\%$ vs. $>60\%$) or liver/spleen metastasis to the cell sheet architecture rather than the presence of Geltrex™ or the subcutaneous surgical pocket. Necessary controls—such as MDA-MB-231 cell suspension mixed with Geltrex™/gelatin implanted in the same anatomic site, or orthotopic implantation of the cell sheet into the mammary fat pad—are entirely absent.
2. **Invalid "Cell Viability" Assay Using Crystal Violet on Fixed Cells (Sections 2.4 & 3.1):**
   In Section 2.4 (lines 74–76) and Section 3.1 (line 154), the authors fix re-cultured cell sheets with methanol/acetic acid, stain with crystal violet, count "349" vs. "280" cells in ImageJ, and run a two-sample $t$-test ($p > 0.05$) claiming this measures "cell viability." As the authors themselves partially concede in a contradictory sentence added in line 154 (*"It is important to note that crystal violet primarily stains DNA and does not distinguish between live and dead cells"*), crystal violet staining of methanol/acetic-acid fixed cells is a static cell-attachment/density stain, not a viability or proliferation assay (which requires Trypan blue exclusion, Calcein-AM/PI live-dead staining, MTT/WST-1, or Annexin V/PI flow cytometry). Moreover, no standard deviations, replicate numbers ($n$), or exact $p$-values are reported for the `349` vs. `280` counts, nor is it explained how drying Geltrex™ for 1 hour at room temperature without media (line 72) avoids desiccating the underlying monolayer.
3. **Inadequate Sample Size and Incorrect Sample Size Justification (Section 2.5):**
   Line 83 states: *"This study used three groups, each consisting of 3 mice. Group A (Negative control: n = 1): no treatment; Group B (Positive control)... Group C (Test)... The sample size was calculated using an online sample size calculator by 'Creative Research Systems' [20]."* First, Group A has $n = 1$, contradicting "each consisting of 3 mice." Second, the Creative Research Systems calculator is a survey-polling margin-of-error calculator for finite human populations, which is mathematically meaningless for calculating statistical power in a two-group preclinical mouse xenograft experiment ($n = 3$ per group).
4. **Absence of Variance Estimates, Longitudinal Growth Curves, and Metastasis Quantification:**
   In Section 3.2 and Figure 4, final tumor sizes at 8 weeks are reported solely as point averages (`5.7 mm` vs. `5.8 mm`) without standard deviations, individual mouse measurements ($n = 3$), tumor volumes ($\text{mm}^3$), longitudinal caliper growth curves over the 8-week period, or statistical comparisons. Similarly, in Section 3.3 (line 187), the claim that *"The modified cell sheet method displayed a higher incidence of metastasis, particularly in the liver (Fig 6a) and spleen (Fig 6b), compared to the injection method"* is presented without stating how many of the 3 mice in each group developed metastases ($x/3$ vs. $y/3$), how many histological sections or metastatic foci were counted, or any immunohistochemical confirmation (e.g., human-specific vimentin/HLA/mitochondrial markers) to distinguish true human MDA-MB-231 micrometastases from extramedullary hematopoiesis or inflammatory infiltrates in nude mouse spleen/liver.
5. **Internal Contradiction in Anesthetic Dosing (Sections 2.6, 2.6.1, 2.7):**
   Section 2.6 and 2.7 state that mice were anesthetized with **Xylazine 10 mg/kg** and Ketamine 100 mg/kg, whereas Section 2.6.1 (line 93) states that mice received *"0.2 ml of Xylazin/Ketamine mix at a dose of **20 mg/kg** and 100 mg/kg body weight, respectively."*

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Layering Geltrex™ and 7.5% gelatin onto UpCell™-detached MDA-MB-231 monolayers prevents sheet tearing and enables forceps handling. `[Moderately Supported]` — Qualitative macroscopic and microscopic photographs (Figs 1–3) plausibly show that casting a hydrogel/gelatin backing over confluent MDA-MB-231 cells allows the composite construct to be lifted intact compared to bare monolayers, though no mechanical or rheological measurements were performed.
- **Claim 2:** The Geltrex™-Gelatin cell sheet construct preserves cell viability compared to unmodified MDA-MB-231 cell sheets (349 vs. 280 viable cells, $p > 0.05$). `[Methodologically Flawed]` — Quantifying methanol/acetic-acid fixed crystal-violet-stained cells in a single field of view does not measure cell viability.
- **Claim 3:** Subcutaneous transplantation of the modified MDA-MB-231 cell sheet facilitates a higher incidence of liver and spleen metastasis and lower tumor necrosis than conventional orthotopic mammary fat pad injection, more closely mimicking human breast cancer. `[Overstated]` — With $n = 3$ mice per group, zero quantitative metastasis counts or human-specific IHC markers, and simultaneous confounding by implantation site (subcutaneous vs. orthotopic), initial cell count ($1.5 \times 10^6$ vs. $5 \times 10^6$), and Geltrex™ co-implantation, this comparative biological claim is unsubstantiated.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Protocol Specification:** Basic reagent concentrations ($250\ \mu\text{L}$ Geltrex™, $500\ \mu\text{L}$ 7.5% w/v gelatin) and seeding densities ($1.5 \times 10^6$ MDA-MB-231, $1.0 \times 10^6$ HUVECs per $35\text{ mm}$ UpCell™ dish) are provided, though key technical details (such as the protein concentration/lot of Geltrex™ and the temperature at which 7.5% gelatin solution is pipetted onto Geltrex™ without melting or thermally detaching the UpCell™ polymer prematurely) are missing.
- **Raw Data Availability:** Despite stating *"All relevant data are within the manuscript,"* individual animal tumor dimensions, body weight logs, replicate cell counts, and incidence tables for necrosis and organ metastasis across the $n = 3$ mice per group are omitted.
