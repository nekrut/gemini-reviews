### 1. Executive Summary
This study expands comparative mitogenomics and phylogenetic resolution within the Neotropical characiform fish family Bryconidae by assembling 15 mitochondrial genomes across 11 species: 5 newly sequenced via Illumina MiSeq ($2 \times 300\text{ bp}$ TruSeq Nano shotgun libraries) from northwestern South America (*Brycon meeki*, *B. moorei*, *B. oligolepis*, *B. rubricauda*, and *Salminus affinis*, plus the outgroup *Triportheus magdalenae*) and 10 assembled by mining public genomic and transcriptomic datasets from NCBI SRA (*B. amazonicus*, *B. chagrensis*, *B. falcatus*, *B. orbignyanus*, *Chilobrycon deuterodon*, and *S. brasiliensis*). Combined with 4 previously published GenBank mitogenomes (*B. henni*, *B. nattereri*, *B. orbignyanus*, *S. brasiliensis*), the authors analyze 19 Bryconidae mitogenomes across 13 species using MITOFISH v3.86 annotation, MAUVE synteny comparison, PCA of feature length variation, and partitioned maximum-likelihood phylogenetics in IQ-TREE 2 across 13 protein-coding genes (CDSs) and 2 rRNAs with 5,000 ultrafast bootstraps (UFB), gene concordance factors (gCF), and site concordance factors (sCF). The results confirm conserved vertebrate mitochondrial synteny, reveal an expanded D-loop region ($>2,100\text{ bp}$) driving larger mitogenome sizes in *Salminus* ($>17,700\text{ bp}$ vs. median $16,848\text{ bp}$ in *Brycon*/*Chilobrycon*), detect a mislabeled SRA sample (`SRR10079810`, labeled *B. falcatus* in SRA metadata but library-named and phylogenetically confirmed as *B. amazonicus*), and demonstrate that *Brycon* is polyphyletic across three lineages, with Pacific-slope trans-Andean *Brycon* clustering with *Chilobrycon deuterodon* while Magdalena-Cauca trans-Andean species (*B. moorei*, *S. affinis*) group with their cis-Andean congeners.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Forensic Identification of SRA Metadata Mislabelling and Concordance Factor Reporting:** The authors carefully cross-checked SRA run metadata against library identifiers and phylogenetic placement to catch that `SRR10079810` was mislabeled as *Brycon falcatus* when the library ID (`brycon_amazonicus58483`) and sequence identity belong to *B. amazonicus* (line 26). Additionally, supplementing standard ultrafast bootstraps (which often saturate at 100% in concatenated mitogenome matrices) with gene concordance factors (gCF) and site concordance factors (sCF) in IQ-TREE 2 provides a much more realistic measure of locus- and site-level phylogenetic signal (e.g., showing that while UFB is 100% and gCF is 80–100%, sCF at deep internal nodes is 43.3–49.9%).
2. **Internal Table Omissions and Numerical Inconsistencies Across Tables 2, 3, and 4:**
   - **Missing Row in Table 3 and Merged/Duplicated Rows in Table 4:** The captions for Table 3 (line 116) and Table 4 (line 155) both state *"in 19 mitogenomes of 13 Bryconidae species"*, matching Table 1 and Table 2 (which have 19 rows). However, **Table 3 and Table 4 only contain 18 rows**:
     - In Table 3 (lines 120–140), **`B. chagrensis 29B07` is completely omitted** without explanation (presumably because its RNA-seq assembly lacked `trnF` and the `D-loop`, though its rRNAs and remaining 21 tRNAs were recovered).
     - In Table 4 (lines 159–179), `B. chagrensis 29B07` is also omitted, and line 173 merges the two *B. amazonicus* samples into one label (`B. amazonicus 58483 /LBP-14082`) while line 174 repeats `B. amazonicus LBP-14082` a second time!
   - **Contradiction Between Text and Table 3 for `mt-rnr1` Length Range:** In the Results text (line 150), the length range for `mt-rnr1` is stated as **`946–992 bp`** (Median: 953 bp). However, inspecting the `mt-rnr1` column of Table 3 (lines 122–139), every single value lies between **`946 and 957 bp`** (maximum is 957 bp in *B. rubricauda* and *Ch. deuterodon*; there is no 992 bp entry).
   - **AT Skew Sign Anomaly in `B. chagrensis 29B07` (Table 2, line 95):** In the Results text (line 83 & Discussion line 196), the authors state *"the overall AT skews ranged from 0.015 to 0.071 (Median: 0.057)"*. Yet in Table 2 (line 95), `Brycon chagrensis 29B07` has an AT skew of **`-0.056`**, while its conspecific `Brycon chagrensis 28B05` (line 94) has `+0.055`! A sign flip of identical magnitude ($-0.056$ vs. $+0.055$) indicates that the RNA-seq contig for `29B07` was reverse-complemented (assembled on the N-strand instead of the J-strand) when computing base composition, and was then silently excluded when stating the range $0.015\text{–}0.071$ in the text.
   - **Inconsistent Species/Sample Counts for the Size Comparison Test (line 113):** On line 113, the authors compare mitogenome lengths between northwestern South America (`Mean: 16894.29; Median: 16,884`) and southeastern South America (`Mean: 16823.57; Median: 16,837; p = 0.011`). Those means (`.29` and `.57`, i.e., $2/7$ and $4/7$) correspond to denominators of $n = 7$ and $n = 7$ (excluding *Salminus* and `29B07`), but the exact statistical test (Mann-Whitney vs. $t$-test) and which 14 of the 19 genomes were included are not specified in the Methods, and treating multiple individuals of the same species (e.g., two *B. falcatus*, two *B. amazonicus*, two *B. orbignyanus*) as independent observations in an inter-regional comparison introduces phylogenetic pseudo-replication.
3. **Partitioning by Gene Only vs. Codon Position in Protein-Coding Sequences:** In IQ-TREE 2, the 13 mitochondrial CDSs were partitioned by gene (15 partitions total: 13 CDS + 2 rRNA) rather than by gene and codon position (1st/2nd vs. 3rd codon positions). Because mitochondrial 3rd codon positions in Characiformes experience rapid substitution rates and base-composition saturation across deep divergences, partitioning by codon position (or testing amino-acid / RY-coded 3rd position trees) is standard best practice to confirm that deep nodes (such as the placement of *B. rubricauda*) are not affected by 3rd-position saturation.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: *Brycon* is polyphyletic across three mitochondrial lineages, with Pacific-slope trans-Andean species clustering with *Chilobrycon deuterodon* and Magdalena-Cauca species (*B. moorei*, *S. affinis*) clustering with cis-Andean congeners.** `[Well Supported]` The phylogenomic topology is strongly supported by UFB and gCF values and is fully congruent with prior multi-locus and morphological studies (e.g., Abe et al. 2014).
- **Claim 2: *Salminus* mitogenomes ($>17.7\text{ kb}$) are significantly expanded relative to *Brycon* and *Chilobrycon* ($\sim 16.85\text{ kb}$) due to control region (D-loop) expansion.** `[Well Supported]` Clearly demonstrated across all four *Salminus* assemblies (both de novo MiSeq and SRA genomic assemblies). Note that short-read (Illumina/SPAdes) assemblies of tandemly repeated D-loop regions can occasionally collapse or misassemble repeat copy numbers, which the authors appropriately contextualize.
- **Claim 3: Historical biogeography and taxonomic revision of *Chilobrycon* vs. trans-Andean *Brycon*.** `[Moderately Supported]` The authors appropriately frame their biogeographic dispersal scenario and potential expansion of *Chilobrycon* as hypotheses requiring broader taxon sampling (13 of 52 Bryconidae species are currently represented) and nuclear loci to rule out mitochondrial introgression or incomplete lineage sorting.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** All 15 newly assembled Bryconidae mitogenomes and the newly sequenced outgroup (*Triportheus magdalenae*) have been deposited in NCBI GenBank with verified accessions (`OR168942`–`OR168957`), and all SRA run accessions (`SRR...`) and museum/institutional voucher codes are clearly listed in Table 1.
- **Missing Details:** The newly generated raw Illumina MiSeq reads for the 5 Colombian species and *T. magdalenae* should also be deposited in NCBI SRA (currently marked `NA` in Table 1), and the concatenated alignment matrix and IQ-TREE partition/substitution model file should be included in Supporting Information or a public repository (such as Zenodo/Dryad).
