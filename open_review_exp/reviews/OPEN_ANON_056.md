### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript reports the derivation and multi-modal characterization of swine trophoblast organoids (sTOs) from full-term porcine placentas ($n = 5$ independent derivations with 100% success), addressing a major gap in comparative placental biology for epitheliochorial placentation. The authors validate the epithelial trophoblast identity and reversible apical-basal polarity (Matrigel domes vs. suspension culture) of sTOs via immunofluorescence (`MKI67`, `KRT18`, `ZO-1`), bulk RNA-seq comparison against term porcine placenta, uterus, and the conventional `PTr2` cell line, and 10x Chromium single-cell RNA-seq across $n = 3$ sTO lines (`STO01`, `STO02`, `STO04`). To resolve the in vivo identity of the five scRNA-seq sTO clusters, the authors generate a 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics atlas of $n = 4$ mid-gestation (GD65/66; 2 male, 2 female) porcine maternal-fetal interfaces (MFI), identifying 10 spatially distinct maternal and fetal clusters—including two transcriptionally and structurally distinct areolar trophoblast populations (`Areola-1` and `Areola-2`) and interface trophoblasts (`IntTroph`). Integrating the sTO scRNA-seq and MFI Visium datasets via probabilistic anchor transfer (`Seurat`), `Slingshot`/`tradeSeq` pseudotime trajectory inference, and `CellChat` ligand-receptor modeling demonstrates that sTOs spontaneously differentiate along a proliferative progenitor $\to$ interface trophoblast $\to$ areola-2 trophoblast trajectory and preserve key in vivo functional programs (e.g., endosomal/vesicular transport in areolar trophoblasts vs. focal adhesion/angiogenesis in interface trophoblasts).

This is an exceptionally well-designed, rigorous, and resource-rich study that combines primary organoid engineering with spatially resolved transcriptomics. A few technical and analytical points regarding the use of `ALRA`-imputed counts in differential expression testing, the biological interpretation of mitochondrial-enriched sTO Cluster 4, and minor reporting discrepancies in the Methods should be addressed in a minor revision.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Use of `ALRA` Zero-Imputed Expression Matrices for Differential Expression and `CellChat` (Lines 99, 114, 127, 137):** In both the scRNA-seq and Visium pipelines, the authors perform low-rank matrix approximation imputation using `RunALRA` and then execute `FindAllMarkers` (Line 99, 127) and `CellChat` ligand-receptor overexpression testing (`identifyOverExpressedGenes`, Line 114, 137) on the `ALRA` assay rather than on the `RNA` (log-normalized count) or `SCT` assay. Because `ALRA` replaces technical and biological zeros with smoothed non-zero values across correlated cells/spots, running Wilcoxon rank-sum tests and permutation tests on `ALRA`-imputed data artificially compresses within-cluster variance and inflates test statistics and $p$-values. Note that on Line 132, the authors correctly used the `SCT` assay to generate shared marker lists between sTO and Visium; they should clarify whether the key differential expression results in `FindAllMarkers` and `CellChat` remain robust when evaluated on non-imputed `SCT` or `RNA` counts.
2. **Identity of sTO Cluster 4 ("Areola-1") Driven by Mitochondrial Genes (Line 102, Fig. 2E, Fig. 6F–G, S6 Table):** On Line 50 and Line 102, the authors note that sTO Cluster 4 is defined by elevated expression of "COX enzymes" and shares only 20 markers with the in vivo `Areola-1` Visium cluster, *"largely including COX1, COX3, ND4, and CYTB (Fig 6F and 6G and S6 Table)."* `COX1` (`MT-CO1`), `COX3` (`MT-CO3`), `ND4` (`MT-ND4`), and `CYTB` (`MT-CYB`) are **mitochondrially encoded oxidative phosphorylation transcripts**. In droplet scRNA-seq, a small cluster distinguished primarily by high mitochondrial transcript levels typically reflects either cells experiencing dissociation-induced cytoplasmic mRNA leakage/stress (note that dead-cell removal was performed for `STO01` and `STO04`, but not `STO02`, Line 87) or metabolically hyperactive cells, rather than a specific transcriptional match to `Areola-1` (which in vivo is defined by `CCDC162`, `SPINK4`, `LIF`, and `MMP25`—markers that are largely absent from Cluster 4 in Fig. 4C). The authors should temper the assignment of sTO Cluster 4 as "Areola-1" and discuss the alternative possibility that Cluster 4 represents a high-mitochondrial/stressed state.
3. **Gestational Age Difference Between sTO Derivation (Term, ~GD114) and Visium Reference (Mid-Gestation, GD65/66):** sTOs were derived from full-term placentas (~GD114), whereas the Visium spatial transcriptomics atlas was generated from mid-gestation (GD65/66) tissues. While the authors thoughtfully discuss this limitation in the Discussion (Line 26 of page 2), they should also reconcile the minor discrepancy between the Fig. 3A caption (Line 67: *"gestational day 66 (GD66) maternal-fetal interfaces"*) and the Methods (Line 46: *"On gestation day 65 (relative to first artificial insemination) pregnant gilts were stunned"*).
4. **Minor Internal Discrepancies and Typos in Methods (Lines 41, 62):**
   - **Line 41 (Animal numbers and parity in sTO collection):** The third sentence states: *"At SEU older parity sows (n = 2; parity 5) that didn't farrow by 114 days were induced using 2 mL of Lutalyse... whereas gilts (n = 1) were allowed to farrow naturally"* ($2 + 1 = 3$ animals). Two sentences later, the text states: *"approved by the NCSU IACUC ID# 22-122-B (n = 2, gilts, IM Lutalyse injection) or via normal farrowing activities performed in NCSU SEU (n = 3)"* ($2 + 3 = 5$ animals, and reverses whether sows or gilts received Lutalyse). This should be reconciled for clarity.
   - **Line 62 (Volume unit typo):** *"Organoids were then resuspended in 500 mL of prewarmed TOM and plated into ultra-low binding 24-well plates."* This should be `500 µL` per well, not `500 mL`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *sTOs derived from full-term porcine placentas can be expanded long-term, cryopreserved, inverted in suspension to achieve apical-out polarity, and more faithfully recapitulate in vivo porcine trophoblast gene expression than PTr2 cells.* `[Well Supported]` — Convincingly demonstrated via brightfield/H&E histology, confocal `ZO-1` polarity reversal, fetal sex-chromosome verification, and bulk/single-cell transcriptomic comparisons.
- **Claim 2:** *Visium spatial transcriptomics resolves 10 distinct maternal and fetal compartments at the mid-gestation porcine maternal-fetal interface, uncovering novel pan-trophoblast (`CTSZ`, `CTSB`), interface (`CA4`, `PAG2L4`), and distinct Areola-1 (`CCDC162`, `SPINK4`, `MMP25`) vs. Areola-2 (`MMP7`, `AGR2`, `LRP2`) markers.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly grounded in co-registered H&E histology across 4 biological replicates (2 male, 2 female conceptuses).
- **Claim 3:** *sTOs spontaneously differentiate along a trajectory from proliferative progenitors (`TOP2A+`/`MKI67+`) through interface trophoblasts to Areola-2 trophoblasts, conserving endosomal and cell-cell signaling networks.* `[Well Supported]` — Strong concordance between `Slingshot`/`tradeSeq` pseudotime dynamics in sTOs and spatial localization in MFI sections (`GRN`, `ITM2C`, `NUS1`, `NUPR1`, `SQSTM1`, `ARF4`), as well as conserved `ANGPTL`, `CDH`, and `AGRN` signaling.
- **Claim 4:** *sTO Cluster 4 represents differentiated Areola-1 trophoblasts.* `[Fragile]` — As noted above, the overlap is driven primarily by 4 mitochondrial genes (`COX1`, `COX3`, `ND4`, `CYTB`) rather than canonical `Areola-1` markers (`SPINK4`, `CCDC162`, `LIF`).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Exemplary Data & Code Availability:** All raw and processed bulk RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, and Visium spatial transcriptomics datasets are publicly deposited in GEO (`GSE296975`, `GSE301436`, with individual sample accessions `GSM8980980`–`GSM8980994` and `GSM9083242`–`GSM9083243` explicitly mapped to every figure panel) and SRA (`PRJNA1252046`). Analysis code is archived on GitHub and Zenodo (`10.5281/zenodo.15747602`), accompanied by an interactive Shiny web portal.
- **Protocol Completeness:** Media formulations (`tTOM` growth factor concentrations and catalog numbers), dissociation timings, sequencing depths, and Seurat/Slingshot/CellChat parameters are documented in full detail.
