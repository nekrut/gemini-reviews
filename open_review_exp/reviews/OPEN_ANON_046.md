### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript re-analyzes a publicly available mouse retinal single-cell RNA-seq dataset (`GSE178121`, 3 diabetic retinopathy [DR] and 3 control mouse samples, 5,818 cells) using a standard assembly-line bioinformatics workflow (`Seurat`, `AUCell` on 363 MSigDB glucocorticoid genes, `limma`, `Monocle 2`, `pySCENIC`, `CellChat`, `STRING`/`CytoHubba`, `StarBase`/`miRTarBase`/`hTFtarget`, and `DGIdb`), supplemented by a 3-gene qRT-PCR assay in high-glucose-treated human retinal microvascular endothelial cells (HRMECs). The study exhibits severe methodological and biological flaws characteristic of automated template pipelines: (1) an unedited copy-paste artifact in the Methods referring to "IFN-I scores" instead of glucocorticoid scores; (2) cross-species database mismatches, including running `CellChatDB.human` directly on mouse scRNA-seq data and confusing human and mouse databases in `hTFtarget`; (3) photoreceptor ambient RNA / doublet contamination in vascular endothelial cells (VECs) mischaracterized in `Monocle 2` and `pySCENIC` as an endothelial differentiation branch for "visual perception / response to light stimulus" driven by the photoreceptor homeobox factor `Rax`; (4) cell-level pseudoreplication on only 130 active cells without multiple-testing correction; and (5) biologically implausible drug predictions (proposing cytotoxic chemotherapy agents etoposide and hydroxyurea or magnesium sulfate for diabetic retinopathy).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Unedited Template Copy-Paste Artifact ("IFN-I scores" in `Line 69`)**:
   - In the Methods section describing `pySCENIC` transcription factor analysis (Line 69), the authors state: *"Next, the correlation between rules and IFN-I scores was analyzed using the Pearson correlation coefficient."* Type I interferon (`IFN-I`) scores have nothing to do with this manuscript (which scores glucocorticoid-related genes) and are never mentioned anywhere else in the text or figures, demonstrating that the Methods text was copied from a prior template paper on IFN-I signaling without adaptation.
2. **Cross-Species Database Mismatches (`Mouse` scRNA-seq Analyzed with `Human` Databases)**:
   - Dataset `GSE178121` was generated from **mouse** (*Mus musculus*) retinal tissue (`GPL24247`, Line 47).
   - However, in Line 79, the authors state: *"The 'CellChatDB.human' ligand-receptor interaction database was used as a guide for the investigation of cell-cell communication."* Using `CellChatDB.human` instead of `CellChatDB.mouse` on mouse single-cell matrices causes systematic ligand-receptor matching failures or requires undocumented cross-species symbol coercion.
   - Similarly, in Line 94, the Methods describe `hTFtarget` as a database of **human** TF ChIP-Seq data ("encompassing 7,190 experimental samples across 659 TFs... human TF ChIP-Seq data"), yet in Results Line 230 the authors state *"we used the hTFtarget mouse database to find TFs"* immediately after stating they converted the 25 mouse hub genes into human gene symbols!
3. **Photoreceptor Ambient RNA / Doublet Contamination Misinterpreted as Endothelial "Visual Perception" Differentiation (`Lines 164, 169, 254`, `Fig 4F`, `Fig 5`)**:
   - In retinal dissociations, rod and cone photoreceptors comprise the vast majority of cells (`Fig 2B`) and release abundant ambient transcripts (e.g., *Rho*, *Gnat1*, *Pde6b*) that contaminate non-photoreceptor droplets or form endothelial-photoreceptor doublets.
   - In `Monocle 2` trajectory analysis of VECs (Line 164, `Fig 4F`), "cell branch 2 (state 3) are mainly enriched in 'visual perception', 'sensor perception of light stimulus', 'response to light stimulus'". Likewise, `pySCENIC` (Line 169, `Fig 5`) identifies `Rax` (Retina and anterior neural fold homeobox, a photoreceptor/retinal progenitor-specific transcription factor) inside the VEC population.
   - Rather than recognizing that "State 3" VECs are photoreceptor-contaminated droplets/doublets that failed quality control (note that no ambient RNA removal tool like `SoupX`/`CellBender` was used, and although Line 134 mentions "double cell removal", no doublet detection tool such as `DoubletFinder` or `Scrublet` is described in the Methods), the authors devote a lengthy paragraph in the Discussion (Line 254) to arguing that vascular endothelial cells express visual perception pathways to respond to light stimuli!
4. **Tiny Active Cell Sample Size (`130 cells`) and Single-Cell Pseudoreplication (`Lines 57, 145`)**:
   - Out of 5,818 cells across 6 mice, only **130 cells** exceeded the `AUCell` threshold ($>0.13$) for glucocorticoid activity (Line 145), and VECs are an even smaller fraction of the retina (`Fig 2B, D`).
   - Differential expression between DR and Control VECs was performed at the single-cell level using `limma` with an unadjusted $P < 0.05$ and $|\log\text{FC}| > 0.25$ (Line 57). Treating individual cells from $n=3$ biological replicates per group as independent observations (pseudoreplication) without pseudobulk aggregation or Benjamini-Hochberg FDR adjustment yields heavily inflated false-positive DEGs.
5. **Uncritical `DGIdb` Drug Screening (`Table 1`, `Lines 180–195, 256`)**:
   - Uploading 11 transcription factors to `DGIdb` produced four "predicted drugs" (`Table 1`): `HYDROXYUREA` for `Klf4`, `MAGNESIUM SULFATE ANHYDROUS` for `Fos`, `ETOPOSIDE` for `Tbp` (the universal basal transcription factor TATA-binding protein!), and `MECAMYLAMINE` for `Atf3`. `DGIdb` aggregates literature co-mentions and toxicogenomic perturbations without distinguishing agonists, antagonists, or nonspecific cytotoxic agents (such as etoposide and hydroxyurea). Proposing systemic antineoplastic agents based on an uncurated `DGIdb` lookup table lacks pharmacological validity.
6. **Basic Endocrine Error in the Discussion (`Offset Line 3`)**:
   - To explain why the KEGG "estrogen signaling pathway" was enriched among the 25 hub genes (which actually occurred simply because general heat-shock chaperones `HSP90AB1`, `HSPA8`, and `HSPB1` are annotated in both glucocorticoid and estrogen receptor KEGG maps), the authors assert (Offset Line 3): *"The first possible reason is that both [estrogen and glucocorticoids] are steroid hormones secreted by the adrenal cortex..."* Estrogens are primarily ovarian/gonadal sex steroids (with only trace adrenal androgen aromatization), not primary adrenal cortical hormones.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that retinal VECs differentiate into a "visual perception / light stimulus" state in DR (`Fig 4F`, `Line 254`)**: `[Methodologically Flawed]` — A classic bioinformatics artifact caused by rod/cone photoreceptor ambient RNA or unremoved doublets within the VEC cluster.
- **Claim that hydroxyurea is a candidate therapy for DR targeting KLF4 (`Table 1`, `Line 256`)**: `[Overstated]` — Based solely on an uncurated `DGIdb` database lookup score without any experimental binding, directionality, or functional testing.
- **Claim that *DUSP6*, *AP1S2*, and *PTPRB* are differentially expressed under high glucose in HRMECs (`Fig 9`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The in vitro qRT-PCR in HRMECs ($5.5\text{ mM}$ vs. $40\text{ mM}$ glucose for 48 h, $n=3$) shows directional mRNA changes, though no osmotic control (e.g., $5.5\text{ mM}$ glucose + $34.5\text{ mM}$ mannitol), protein-level validation, or glucocorticoid perturbation was included to connect these genes to glucocorticoid signaling.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Code Availability**: The re-analyzed GEO accession (`GSE178121`) and qRT-PCR primer sequences are provided in the text. However, no analysis scripts, exact software package versions, random seeds, or per-sample cell counts (how many VECs came from each of the 3 DR and 3 Control mice) are reported.
