### 1. Executive Summary
This hybrid experimental and evolutionary genetics study investigates the functional role and evolutionary history of a host missense polymorphism (*SYNGR2* p.Arg63Cys) previously associated with porcine circovirus type 2b (PCV2b) viral load in swine. Using CRISPR/Cas9 homology-directed repair in PK15 cells, the authors generate homozygous *p.63Cys* knock-in clones (`emSYNGR2+p.63Cys` and a second fully isogenic clone `2emSYNGR2+p.63Cys`) alongside a knockout clone (`emSYNGR2-del`) and wild-type PK15 (`wtSYNGR2+p.63Arg`), demonstrating reduced viral replication across both PCV2b and PCV2d subtypes in vitro. They complement this with a haplotype analysis of 751 *Suidae* sequences from the European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) and `PAML` (`CODEML` M7 vs. M8) positive selection analyses across 40 mammalian species. The wet-lab CRISPR validation—particularly the generation of a second isogenic clone after discovering three non-coding SNPs and baseline expression differences in the first clone—is thorough and convincing. However, the population genetic test for positive selection in domestic swine is methodologically flawed due to conditioning on homozygosity for the focal SNP within a short non-recombining coding region across pooled European and Asian breeds (Wahlund effect), and the longitudinal qPCR and `PAML` analyses omit several statistical details.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Confounded Test for Positive Selection in Domestic Swine (`Tables 2–3`, `Lines 101–150, 243–246`)**: To test for recent positive selection on *SYNGR2* p.63Cys, the authors partition 529 homozygous domestic pigs into *Arg/Arg* ($n=356$) and *Cys/Cys* ($n=173$) groups regardless of breed or geographic origin, and compare heterozygosity ($H_o, H_s$) and minor allele frequencies across 10 other coding SNPs within the 675 bp *SYNGR2* CDS. This test is fundamentally confounded by two factors:
   - First, conditioning on homozygosity for a recently derived mutation (*Cys/Cys*) that arose on a single haplotype (`Hap1`) inside the exact same 675 bp locus mathematically guarantees near-zero internal haplotype diversity ($H_o = 0.018$, $H_s = 0.018$) even under purely neutral genetic drift or a domestication/breed bottleneck, because there has been negligible time for new mutations or intragenic recombination within a 675 bp exon block.
   - Second, pooling European and Asian breeds into the ancestral *Arg/Arg* group mixes European `Hap2` with highly divergent Asian haplotypes (`Hap3`–`Hap10`), inflating expected heterozygosity ($H_s = 0.316$) and creating a massive Wahlund effect ($G_{is} = 0.439$ in Table 2). Formal evidence of a selective sweep requires extended haplotype homozygosity tests (e.g., iHS, XP-EHH, or $nS_L$) across flanking megabase-scale chromosomal regions within a single breed (such as Duroc), benchmarked against the empirical genome-wide distribution to control for breed demography.
2. **Manual BLAST Alignment and Visual Genotype Calling for 751 ENA Samples (`Lines 233–236`)**: Sequencing reads from 751 public ENA samples were aligned to the 675 bp *SYNGR2* reference CDS using NCBI Standard Nucleotide BLAST, "visualized using the multi-sequence alignment viewer, and deviations from the reference SYNGR2 sequence were recorded for each sample." Calling diploid genotypes (especially heterozygotes used to compute $H_o$ in Table 2) via manual visual inspection of BLAST alignments without reporting per-sample read depth, base quality thresholds, strand bias filters, or a probabilistic variant caller (`bcftools`/`GATK`) is non-standard and susceptible to coverage-dependent genotyping errors (e.g., allelic dropout at low read depth inflating $G_{is}$).
3. **Longitudinal Viral Replication Statistical Analysis (`Figs 1–2`, `Line 230`)**: Log10-transformed viral genome copies across multiple post-infection timepoints (0, 12, 24, 48, 72, 96, 120 hpi) from $n=3$ biological replicates are analyzed using separate t-tests or Tukey's HSD tests at each timepoint without a repeated-measures / linear mixed-effects model or multiple-testing correction across timepoints and fractions. Furthermore, with $n=3$ biological replicates per group, parametric tests have limited power and should be accompanied by exact p-values and individual data points on the plots rather than bar/line means $\pm$ SEM alone.
4. **Incomplete Reporting of `PAML` `CODEML` Site Model Parameters (`Table 4`, `Lines 154–170, 250–252`)**: While Table 4 reports overall gene-wide $d_N/d_S$ and the M7 vs. M8 likelihood ratio test (LRT) statistics for Mammals ($n=22$), Primates ($n=16$), and Even-Toed Ungulates ($n=10$), it omits the M8 positive-selection class parameters ($p_1$ proportion of sites and $\omega_s > 1$ estimate) and does not report the Bayes Empirical Bayes (BEB) posterior probabilities for the five highlighted intraluminal loop residues (sites 53, 55, 57, 135, 139). Additionally, the taxonomic composition of the "Mammals ($n=22$)" group relative to "Primates ($n=16$)" and "Even-Toed Ungulates ($n=10$)" (which sum to $22+16+10 = 48 > 40$ total species) should be clarified in the main text.
5. **Exemplary Experimental Rigor in Isogenic Clone Validation (`Lines 59–60`)**: The authors' decision to sequence the non-coding intronic and 3'UTR regions of their initial `emSYNGR2+p.63Cys` clone, identify three unintended background SNPs and reduced baseline *SYNGR2* mRNA expression, and subsequently engineer and validate a second, fully isogenic clone (`2emSYNGR2+p.63Cys`) that had identical baseline expression to wild-type PK15 is a major methodological strength that establishes causal specificity of the p.Arg63Cys substitution in vitro.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that the *SYNGR2* p.63Cys substitution directly reduces PCV2b and PCV2d replication in PK15 cells (`Figs 1–2`)**: `[Well Supported]` — Confirmed across two genetically distinct viral subtypes (PCV2b and PCV2d), two MOIs, and two independent homozygous CRISPR knock-in clones (including the fully isogenic `2emSYNGR2+p.63Cys` clone) alongside a complete knockout control.
- **Claim that *SYNGR2* p.63Cys arose post-domestication on a European haplotype (`Hap2` $\to$ `Hap1`) and was introduced into Asian breeds via introgression (`Table 1`, `Fig 3`, `Fig 5`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The phylogenetic network showing `Hap1` differing by a single nucleotide from `Hap2` (which is at 97.7% frequency in European wild boar and 0% in Asian wild boar) provides parsimonious support for a European origin, though absence in 55 wild boar samples cannot completely rule out a low-frequency standing variant in European wild boar prior to domestication.
- **Claim that reduced intragenic heterozygosity in *Cys/Cys* vs. *Arg/Arg* homozygotes proves positive selection in domestic swine (`Tables 2–3`)**: `[Methodologically Flawed]` — As detailed in Critique 1, comparing intragenic coding diversity within a 675 bp region conditioned on homozygosity for a single derived allele against a multi-breed/multi-continent pool of ancestral homozygotes cannot distinguish positive selection from neutral allele age and population structure.
- **Claim of adaptive evolution in mammalian *SYNGR2* intraluminal loops (`Table 4`, `Fig 4`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The M7 vs. M8 LRTs are statistically significant across all three mammalian alignments and localize candidate sites to the intraluminal loops near residue 63, though BEB posterior probabilities and site-specific $\omega$ values must be reported.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Accession Numbers & Sequences**: The PCV2b isolate accession (`KP016747.1`) and porcine *SYNGR2* reference transcript (`XM_021066554.1`) are provided, and public ENA accessions and mammalian transcript IDs are referenced in Supplementary Information (`S1 Table`, `Table A in S1 Text`). However, the GenBank accession number for the newly sequenced complete PCV2d isolate genome (Line 215) is missing and should be deposited and reported.
- **Experimental Protocols**: Cell culture, TCID50 titration, RNP CRISPR-Cas9 transfection, qPCR normalization (`RPL32`), and `CODEML` settings are clearly described, though the exact primer/probe and sgRNA/ssODN oligonucleotide sequences should be explicitly tabulated in the main Methods or Supplementary Tables.
