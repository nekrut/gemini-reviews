### 1. Executive Summary
This study dissects how Oct4 (`Pou5f1`) and Sox2 establish the pluripotency chromatin landscape and transcriptional network during mouse preimplantation development across early/late compacted morula (E2.75, E3.25) and early/late inner cell mass (ICM; E3.75, E4.5) stages. To overcome three major limitations that confounded prior studies—cellular heterogeneity between epiblast (EPI) and primitive endoderm (PE) within the ICM, partial functional retention in exon-1-only `Pou5f1` alleles, and maternal protein rescue—the authors engineered fluorescently tagged maternal-zygotic knockout (MZ-KO) mouse lines (`Pou5f1^mKO2` deleting exons 2–5 and `Sox2^EGFP`, crossed to `Zp3-Cre` conditional females) and combined immunosurgery with MEK inhibition (`PD0325901`) to direct the entire ICM toward the EPI lineage. Using pooled low-input ATAC-seq ($20\text{--}40$ embryos per replicate) and single-embryo UMI-tagged 3' RNA-seq across developmental stages, alongside cross-validation against non-MEKi datasets (`GSE203194`, `GSE159030`), the authors demonstrate that Oct4 and Sox2 have minimal impact at the morula stage but drive a massive reorganization of chromatin accessibility ($33,520$ differential peaks out of $152,393$) and gene expression in the early and late ICM primarily via cooperative activation of distal enhancers bearing composite `OCT-SOX` motifs ($8,993$ peaks). The experimental genetics, embryological controls, and multi-omic integration are of very high quality; only minor methodological clarifications are recommended.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Clarification of `Sox2^EGFP` Fluorescence Timing and Absence of `Sox2`-KO Morula ATAC-seq (Results Lines 33 & 52):** Line 33 notes that `PGK-EGFP` reporter expression in `Sox2^EGFP` embryos remains weak until the blastocyst stage, and consequently Figure 2A and 2B include ATAC-seq for `Pou5f1`-KO at E2.75 and E3.25 morula stages but only include `Sox2`-KO at E3.75 and E4.5 ICM stages (whereas Figure 2D includes `Sox2`-KO E3.25 morula RNA-seq because single embryos were genotyped bioinformatically by mapping RNA-seq reads to the `PGK-EGFP-hGHpA` cassette). Explicitly stating in Results line 52 that pooled ATAC-seq was not performed on `Sox2`-KO morulae due to weak pre-blastocyst EGFP fluorescence will improve clarity.
2. **Role of ERCC Spike-Ins and `DESeq2` Normalization Under Asymmetric Peak/Gene Loss (Methods, Lines 10 & 19):** The RNA-seq Methods state that $2,493$ copies of ERCC RNA Spike-In were added to each single-embryo sample, but do not specify whether ERCC spike-in counts were used for size-factor normalization in `DESeq2` or solely for technical quality control. Similarly, in the ATAC-seq analysis, decreased peaks outnumber increased peaks across all KO conditions (e.g., $14,016$ decreased vs. $11,884$ increased in `Pou5f1`-KO late ICM; $6,637$ decreased vs. $3,660$ increased in `Sox2`-KO late ICM). Standard `DESeq2` median-of-ratios normalization across merged peaks tends to partially absorb global directional shifts in chromatin accessibility; clarifying how size factors were computed (and confirming that `DESeq2` negative binomial testing was performed on raw integer transposon insertion and UMI counts while `rlog` was used for PCA/visualization) would strengthen the statistical documentation.
3. **Exact Replicate Counts per Stage and Genotype:** While PCA plots (Figure 2A and Figure 2—figure supplement 2A) display individual biological replicates, explicitly listing the exact number of biological replicate pools (for ATAC-seq) and individual embryos (for RNA-seq) per stage and genotype in the main figure legends or Materials and Methods will aid rapid assessment of statistical power across time points.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Maternal-zygotic loss of `Pou5f1` or `Sox2` has minimal impact on global chromatin accessibility and transcription at the morula stage, but causes widespread loss of enhancer accessibility and pluripotency gene expression in the early and late ICM.** `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated across stages (Figure 2A–D) and biologically explained by the negligible expression of `Sox2` prior to the ICM stage.
- **Claim 2: Oct4 and Sox2 cooperatively activate EPI-specific pluripotency targets (`Utf1`, `Il6st`, `Klf2`, `Etv5`, `Prdm14`, `Pecam1`, `Sap30`, `Uhrf1`, `Ezh2`) primarily via distal enhancers containing composite `OCT-SOX` motifs while repressing trophectoderm TFs (`Gata3`, `Cdx2`, `Eomes`).** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by concordant ATAC-seq accessibility, motif enrichment (`OCT-SOX` vs. `GATA`/`TEAD`/`EOMES`), single-embryo UMI RNA-seq, `Pecam1` immunofluorescence, and published Sox2 CUT&RUN and ESC ChIP-seq profiles.
- **Claim 3: Short-term MEK inhibition (`PD0325901`) suppresses PE differentiation without distorting the downstream regulatory targets of Oct4 and Sox2 in the ICM.** `[Well Supported]` — Rigorously validated by GSEA and peak profile comparisons against independent non-MEKi `Pou5f1`-KO and `Sox2`-KO datasets (`GSE203194` and `GSE159030`; Figure 2—figure supplements 1 and 2).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Exemplary data sharing. Raw and processed ATAC-seq and single-embryo UMI RNA-seq datasets are publicly deposited in NCBI GEO (`GSE264614` and `GSE264615`), all external datasets (`GSE203194`, `GSE159030`, `GSE11724`, `GSE27844`) are cited with accessions, and full source imaging zip archives for immunofluorescence figures are provided.
- **Experimental & Bioinformatics Details:** Transgenic targeting constructs, antibody catalog numbers and dilutions, oligo/index sequences (Supplementary files 2 and 3), differential peak/gene tables (Supplementary files 4 and 5), and software parameters (`Bowtie2`, `MACS2`, `BEDTools`, `FIMO`, `PscanChIP`, `GREAT`, `TRUmiCount`, `DESeq2`) are thoroughly documented.
