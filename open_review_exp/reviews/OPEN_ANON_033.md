### 1. Executive Summary
This study conducts a customized membrane-focused CRISPR activation (CRISPRa) screen ($\sim 6,213$ human membrane proteins targeted by $\sim 24,000$ sgRNAs) in HEK293FT cells with and without exogenous ACE2 overexpression (WT vs. ACE2-OE) at low ($0.01$) and high ($0.1$) MOI using SARS-CoV-2 D614G-Spike-pseudotyped lentivirus alongside VSV-G specificity controls. From the screen, the authors prioritize and validate two novel host entry co-factors—the endo-lysosomal asparaginyl endopeptidase legumain (*LGMN*) and the voltage-gated potassium channel *KCNA6*—across three orthogonal viral infection systems: Spike-pseudotyped lentiviruses (D614G, Beta, Delta, Omicron), replicating Spike-pseudotyped VSV (with RABV-G controls), and replication-competent nanoluciferase SARS-CoV-2 (`icSARS-CoV-2-nLuc`). The authors further demonstrate that genetic disruption (arrayed 3-sgRNA CRISPR-Cas9 KO in endogenous *LGMN*- and *KCNA6*-expressing lines NCC-Stc-K140, SW156, and Loucy) and pharmacological inhibition (RR-11a for LGMN; 3,4-diaminopyridine for KCNA6) significantly reduce Spike-mediated entry, that LGMN's pro-viral activity requires its catalytic dyad (H148A, C189A) and secretory/surface/endolysosomal trafficking (signal peptide mutagenesis), and that both factors require basal ACE2 presence (ablated in monoclonal ACE2-KO 293T) without altering *ACE2*, *TMPRSS2*, *FURIN*, *CTSL*, or *NRP1* expression by RNA-seq or qPCR. They complement these wet-lab experiments with single-cell RNA-seq re-analyses of COVID-19 BALF and human olfactory neuroepithelium (resolving a GRCh37/GRCh38 overlapping annotation artifact for *KCNA6* using Salmon-Alevin) and an exploratory Medicare Advantage claims analysis ($N = 7.8\text{ million}$).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Rigorous Orthogonal Viral Assays and Specificity Controls:** A standout strength of the experimental design is the systematic inclusion of envelope specificity controls (VSV-G for lentivirus, RABV-G for replicating VSV) at both the pooled screen level and across arrayed cDNA, CRISPR KO, and small-molecule inhibitor assays. This cleanly separates Spike-specific entry enhancement from pan-lentiviral or general cell viability artifacts.
2. **Mechanistic Dissection of LGMN and ACE2 Epistasis:** The authors transparently troubleshooted expression vector silencing in pLX304 by re-cloning into a bicistronic EF1a-2A-BSD vector with an optimized Kozak sequence (S8 Fig), validated catalytic dead mutants (H148A, C189A) and signal peptide swaps (no-SP, NLS, and Vk surface-targeting), and proved using monoclonal ACE2-KO 293T cells plus bulk RNA-seq/qPCR/Western blot that LGMN and KCNA6 require basal endogenous ACE2 (even when below Western blot detection in WT 293FT) without upregulating canonical entry factors.
3. **Bioinformatic Resolution of *KCNA6* Annotation Masking in scRNA-seq:** The discovery that standard CellRanger pipelines discard *KCNA6* reads due to overlapping annotations (*RP11-234B24.4* and *GALNT8* in GRCh37/GRCh38) and its resolution via custom reference masking and multi-mapping-aware Salmon-Alevin (S13 Fig) is a valuable technical contribution explaining why *KCNA6* expression in olfactory tissues was previously under-detected.
4. **Statistical Pooling of Arrayed sgRNAs in Knockout Experiments:** In Figs 3C–D and 4D, three independent sgRNAs per gene were transduced and infected separately, and then pooled for the main figure bar plots (though individual sgRNA results are transparently provided in S9A–C Fig). When pooling multiple sgRNAs across two biological replicate experiments into a single one-way ANOVA, treating technical/guide replicates as independent observations can inflate degrees of freedom; mixed-effects modeling or reporting guide-level means ($n = 3$ guides) is statistically preferable, although S9 Fig confirms consistent directionality across individual guides.
5. **Directional Contradiction and Confounding by Indication in the Medicare Claims Analysis:** In the final Results subsection (line 136), the authors state they *"evaluated whether common drugs targeting these ion channels were associated with decreased risk of hospitalization and found a significant risk-associated effect for loop diuretics, SSRI antidepressants, and broad-spectrum anticonvulsants that persisted after PSM, with hazard ratios >1.2 consistently and significantly"*. First, an $\text{HR} > 1.2$ means *increased* risk of COVID-19 hospitalization, which is the exact *opposite* direction of the in vitro potassium channel blocker experiment (where 3,4-DAP *inhibited* viral entry) and contradicts the sentence's premise of testing for *decreased* risk. Second, loop diuretics, SSRIs, and anticonvulsants have severe residual confounding by indication (heart failure, fluid overload, neuropsychiatric frailty) that 3-digit ICD-10 propensity score matching cannot fully eliminate, and loop diuretics do not primarily target KCNA6. This claims database section is speculative and weakly connected to the mechanistic biology.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Exogenous and endogenous LGMN and KCNA6 promote SARS-CoV-2 Spike-mediated entry in a manner dependent on basal ACE2 expression.** `[Well Supported]` Demonstrated across pseudotyped lentivirus, replicating VSV-Spike, and live `icSARS-CoV-2-nLuc` across multiple cell lines (293FT, NCC-Stc-K140, SW156, Loucy) and ablated in monoclonal ACE2-KO 293T cells.
- **Claim 2: LGMN's pro-viral activity requires its protease catalytic residues (H148, C189) and cell-surface/endolysosomal localization.** `[Well Supported]` Cleanly demonstrated via site-directed mutagenesis, signal peptide replacement, and pharmacological inhibition with RR-11a. Direct biochemical cleavage of Spike by recombinant LGMN is not shown, which the authors appropriately note as an open question.
- **Claim 3: Real-world clinical claims data support potassium-channel-targeting drugs as COVID-19 therapeutics.** `[Overstated]` The claims analysis actually showed *increased* hospitalization risk ($\text{HR} > 1.2$) for users of loop diuretics, SSRIs, and anticonvulsants, which neither validates KCNA6 inhibition as protective nor isolates KCNA6-specific pharmacology.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagents, Sequences, and Protocols:** Plasmid Addgene IDs, sgRNA library design parameters, signal peptide amino acid sequences, antibody catalog numbers/dilutions, and viral MOI/titering protocols are documented with exceptional granularity.
- **Data & Code Availability:** Raw and processed CRISPRa screen data, cDNA vector lists, and DESeq2 tables are included in S1–S3 Data and S4 Table. Public GEO/SRA accessions (`GSE139522`) are cited. Depositing the newly generated bulk RNA-seq FASTQ files from the cDNA-overexpressing 293FT lines into NCBI GEO/SRA would further strengthen complete computational reproducibility.
