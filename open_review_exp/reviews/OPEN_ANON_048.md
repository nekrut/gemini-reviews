### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript describes the isolation and polyphasic taxonomic characterization of a fluorescent *Pseudomonas* strain (`JDE115`) recovered from surface-sterilized soybean (*Glycine max*) root nodules in Virginia, USA, proposing the novel species `'Candidatus Pseudomonas auctus' sp. nov.`. The authors report morphological microscopy (TEM, SEM, phase-contrast), Biolog GEN III physiological profiling, MIDI Sherlock fatty acid methyl ester (FAME) analysis, 16S rRNA phylogeny, and whole-genome sequencing (`PRJNA1196930`), calculating an Average Nucleotide Identity (ANI) of 94.59% and digital DNA-DNA hybridization (dDDH, GGDC Formula 2) of 57.10% against its closest relative, *Pseudomonas glycinae* $\text{MS586}^\text{T}$. While the genomic distance metrics (ANI $< 95\%$ and dDDH $< 70\%$) plausibly indicate that strain `JDE115` represents a distinct species Sister to *P. glycinae*, the manuscript contains pervasive internal contradictions across nearly every phenotypic parameter, an impossible genome sequencing/assembly methods description (claiming Illumina 150 bp paired-end reads were basecalled with Oxford Nanopore's `Guppy v4.4.1` at $Q \ge 7$ and assembled with the long-read assembler `Flye v2.9`), chemotaxonomic claims contradicted by the paper's own tables, and an invalid use of the taxonomic status *Candidatus*.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Incompatible Sequencing and Assembly Pipeline in Methods (`Line 94`)**:
   - In the whole-genome sequencing section (Line 94), the authors state: *"Sequencing was performed on the **Illumina® NovaSeq PE150 platform** at the NGS core facility, generating paired-end reads of 150 bp. **FASTQ files were generated using Guppy (v4.4.1)**, and sequences with a **Phred score below 7** were excluded. **De novo assembly was performed using Flye (v2.9)**, with default parameters and a genome size setting of 6.2 Mbp."*
   - This pipeline is technically impossible: `Guppy (v4.4.1)` is Oxford Nanopore Technologies' basecaller for raw nanopore ionic current signals (where $Q < 7$ is the standard Nanopore fail filter), and `Flye (v2.9)` is a long-read *de novo* assembler that operates exclusively on PacBio or Oxford Nanopore long reads and **cannot** assemble 150 bp Illumina short reads. Either the genome was sequenced on an Oxford Nanopore platform (and the Covaris S220 / NEBNext Ultra II / Illumina NovaSeq PE150 description is erroneous) or it was sequenced on Illumina (and the Guppy / Q7 / Flye description is erroneous).
2. **Direct Self-Contradictions in Morphology and Growth Physiology Across the Manuscript**:
   - **Flagellation**: The Abstract (Line 8) describes `JDE115` as *"motile with **peritrichous** flagella"*, whereas Results (Line 111), the `Fig 1` TEM caption (Line 117), and `Table 1` (Line 126) state it has a **single polar (monotrichous) flagellum**.
   - **Salinity (NaCl) tolerance**: The Abstract (Line 8) states growth occurs at **0–5.0% NaCl**; the Results (Line 111) and Species Description (Lines 293, 295) state growth occurs at **0–4% NaCl** and is **inhibited at 8% NaCl**; and the Supporting Information (Line 308) states *"Its tolerance to **1%, 4%, and 8% NaCl** suggests that it can withstand osmotic fluctuations"*.
   - **Temperature and pH range**: The Abstract (Line 8) reports growth at **pH 6.0–10.0** and **10–40°C**; the Results (Line 111) report **pH 4.0–10.0** and **10–40°C**; and the Species Description (Line 293) reports **pH 4.0–10.0** (optimum 6.0–7.0) and **4°C to 40°C**.
   - **Cell dimensions**: Results (Line 111) report cell size as **0.5 $\mu$m**, whereas the Species Description (Line 293) reports **0.7–0.9 [written as `.09`] $\times$ 2.2–3.0 $\mu$m**.
3. **Chemotaxonomic Claims Contradicted by `Table 5` and Unsubstantiated Quinone Data**:
   - In Line 283, the authors state: *"The chemotaxonomic differentiation is further highlighted by unique percentages in summed feature 8 and the **presence of minor fatty acids like C19:0 $\omega$8c in JDE115**, which were not detected in many other Pseudomonas strains."* Yet in `Table 5` (Line 270), `C19:0 ω8c` in `P. auctus JDE115` is explicitly listed as **`ND` (Not Detected)** (whereas it is present at 1.2% in *P. glycinae* $\text{MS586}^\text{T}$)!
   - In the Abstract (Line 8), the authors state: *"The primary isoprenoid quinone detected in JDE115 was ubiquinone-9 (Q-9)"*. However, no respiratory quinone extraction, TLC/HPLC methodology, or experimental data for Q-9 appear anywhere in the Materials and Methods or Results.
4. **Improper Use of the Provisional Taxonomic Rank *'Candidatus'* (`Line 288`)**:
   - Under the International Code of Nomenclature of Prokaryotes (ICNP), the provisional status *Candidatus* is strictly reserved for uncultivated organisms that cannot be maintained in pure culture. Strain `JDE115` is an easily culturable bacterium grown on standard LB and King's B media. The authors state (Line 288): *"It remains candidatus because the type strain cannot be released to the public because of its potential development as a biopesticide."* Withholding a culturable type strain from public culture collections for commercial reasons invalidates valid publication under the ICNP and is an improper use of *Candidatus*.
5. **Formatting Defects and Misspelled Taxon Names in `Tables 1, 3, and 4`**:
   - In `Table 1` (Lines 124–165), all phenotypic reaction values (`+`, `-`, `w`) are missing from the table cells because they were encoded purely as cell background colors (Line 170: *"dark green is for positive reaction; red is for negative reaction"*), rendering the table unreadable in text/print. Furthermore, `l-Arabinose` (Lines 135 and 143) and `Glucuronamide` (Lines 151 and 157) are duplicated as separate rows in `Table 1`.
   - Reference *Pseudomonas* species are repeatedly misspelled across text and tables: e.g., `P. gozinkensis` (Table 3) vs. `P. guzikowskii` (Line 209); `P. allokribbensis` (Table 3) vs. `P. alokibbiensis` (Line 211); `P. fitomaticsae` (Table 3) vs. `P. fitomatiaceae` (Line 209).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that strain `JDE115` is genomically distinct from *Pseudomonas glycinae* $\text{MS586}^\text{T}$ below the species threshold (`ANI = 94.59%`, `dDDH = 57.10%`, `Table 3`, `Fig 3`)**: `[Moderately Supported]` — The `FastANI` and `TYGS`/`GGDC` calculations fall just below the 95% ANI and well below the 70% dDDH cutoffs, though the underlying genome assembly method (Illumina vs. Nanopore) must be clarified to ensure assembly base accuracy did not artificially depress ANI (e.g., unpolished Nanopore homopolymer errors can reduce ANI by 0.5%–1.5%, which is critical when ANI is 94.59% vs. the 95.0% species boundary!).
- **Claim of consistent polyphasic phenotypic and chemotaxonomic differentiation (`Tables 1, 5`)**: `[Methodologically Flawed]` — Undermined by direct contradictions between the Abstract, Results, Species Description, and Table 5.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strain and Genome Availability**: While the genome project is listed under GenBank BioProject `PRJNA1196930` (BioSample `SAMN45708326`), the biological strain `JDE115` is explicitly withheld from public repositories/culture collections (Line 288), preventing independent phenotypic or chemotaxonomic verification.
