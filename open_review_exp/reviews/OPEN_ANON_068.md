### 1. Executive Summary
This study presents a chromosome-scale reference genome assembly for the model diploid root-knot nematode *Meloidogyne hapla* strain VW9 ($59.2\text{ Mb}$ across 16 chromosome-length scaffolds, $\text{N50} = 3.8\text{ Mb}$) integrating PacBio HiFi ($56\times$), Oxford Nanopore R10.4.1 ($143\times$), Illumina NovaSeq ($138\times$), and Proximo Hi-C ($263\times$) sequencing, coupled with PacBio Iso-Seq ($4.1\text{M}$ reads across eggs, J2, and adult females) and time-series in planta RNA-seq ($5, 7, 10, 12, 14\text{ dpi}$) to annotate $11,229$ protein-coding genes. The authors validate the physical assembly against two independent genetic linkage maps from RIL-like $F_2$ crosses ($\text{VW9} \times \text{LM}$, $n = 84\text{--}93$ lines; $\text{VW8} \times \text{VW9}$, $n = 183$ lines), discovering that structural karyotypic polymorphisms (chromosome fusions/fissions on S1, S2, S4, S6, S9, S12, S13 and a putative toxin-antidote segregation distorter on S13) exist among geographic isolates, and verifying the central palindromic fusion junction on Scaffold 1 with $2,902$ ultralong ONT reads $>60\text{ kb}$. Furthermore, the authors identify a non-canonical 16-nt tandem repeat (`CCCAAGGTTTAAAAGG`) enriched at 17 scaffold termini (and internally at the S1 fusion junction), confirm its terminal chromosomal localization via DNA FISH, and demonstrate through binary-segmentation change-point analysis and hypergeometric testing that sharply demarcated high-recombination zones ($\text{HRZs}$, $11\text{--}139\text{ cM/Mb}$) are significantly enriched for genes encoding predicted secreted proteins ($\text{PSPs}$, $p = 2.5 \times 10^{-8}$) while evolutionarily conserved *C. elegans* orthologs are enriched in low-recombination zones ($\text{LRZs}$, $p = 1.9 \times 10^{-5}$). This is an exceptionally thorough, multi-modal genomic and genetic study with only minor methodological and statistical points to address.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Gene-to-Domain Boundary Assignment and Tandem Paralog Clustering in the Hypergeometric Test (Lines 117–131 of offset 2):**
   In the hypergeometric enrichment analysis testing whether the $1,258$ predicted secreted protein (PSP) genes are overrepresented in HRZs ($p = 2.5 \times 10^{-8}$), two subtle statistical issues arise:
   - First, the Methods state (line 117 of offset 2): *"Genes overlapping multiple domains were assigned to each matching domain, and genes not directly overlapping any domain were assigned to the nearest domain based on the minimum distance between gene boundaries and domain boundaries."* Duplicating boundary-spanning genes across domains slightly violates the mutually exclusive partition assumption of the hypergeometric distribution (`N_total = N_HRZ + N_LRZ`).
   - Second, effector and CAZyme families in plant-parasitic nematodes (such as pectate lyases and glycoside hydrolases, lines 130) frequently occur in local tandem duplication arrays. If a single tandem array of 5–10 paralogous PSPs lands within an HRZ (such as the $340\text{ kb}$ HRZ on Scaffold 3 containing 23 PSPs, Fig 9D), treating tandem paralogs as independent Bernoulli draws in a standard hypergeometric test inflates significance relative to a circular chromosome permutation test or block-bootstrap test that preserves local gene clustering. Reporting a supplementary block/permutation $p$-value or counting collapsed tandem gene clusters would confirm that the HRZ enrichment is not driven solely by local tandem duplications.
2. **eSNP Ascertainment Density in HRZ vs. LRZ Change-Point Detection (Lines 65–74 of offset 2):**
   Because the high-density Marey maps (Fig 5) were constructed from expressed sequence SNPs (`eSNPs`, $8,455$ filtered variants pruned to $789$ crossover-flanking markers across $84$ $F_2$ lines) derived from *Medicago truncatula* root-gall RNA-seq, marker density depends on local gene expression and polymorphism density between VW9 and LM. While total gene density is nearly uniform along scaffolds (S11 Fig), verifying that physical window sizes of HRZs vs. LRZs are not biased by eSNP marker gaps near unexpressed repeat regions is worthwhile.
3. **Small Secreted Peptide (SSP) Recovery Threshold in Structural Annotation (Lines 132–134):**
   The authors transparently note that default `BRAKER3` / `TSEBRA` filtering excluded proteins shorter than 66 amino acids, and they partially compensated by re-mapping 19 small PSPs from the 2008 ab initio annotation and running targeted BLAST searches for CEPs, IDAs, and RALFs. Clarifying whether these 19 small PSPs and 17 plant peptide mimics (12 CEPs on S13, 2 IDAs, 3 RALFs) were included in the $1,258$ PSP set tested for HRZ enrichment would improve completeness.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** The 16 scaffolds of *M. hapla* VW9 represent a complete, contiguous chromosome-scale assembly, and discrepancies with $\text{VW9} \times \text{LM}$ and $\text{VW8} \times \text{VW9}$ linkage groups reflect genuine inter-strain structural karyotypic variation rather than misassembly. `[Well Supported]` — The convergence of PacBio HiFi, ONT, Hi-C contact maps, Merqury k-mer completeness, two independent genetic crosses sharing VW9 as a common parent, and $2,902$ $>60\text{ kb}$ raw ONT reads spanning the internal palindrome of S1 provides compelling, orthogonal proof of both assembly accuracy and inter-isolate chromosomal fluidity.
- **Claim 2:** A novel 16-bp tandem repeat (`CCCAAGGTTTAAAAGG`) localizes to chromosome ends (and an internal fusion site on S1) in the absence of canonical `(TTAGGC)n` nematode telomeres. `[Well Supported]` — Confirmed independently across the assembly (TRF/MEME), raw ONT reads (`TeloSearchLR`), and cytogenetic prometaphase spreads (`DNA FISH`, Fig 2).
- **Claim 3:** Meiotic recombination in *M. hapla* is concentrated in sharply bounded high-recombination zones ($\text{HRZs}$) that are significantly enriched in predicted secreted proteins (PSPs), whereas conserved *C. elegans* orthologs are enriched in low-recombination zones ($\text{LRZs}$). `[Well Supported]` — The Marey maps, change-point segmentation, and reciprocal enrichment profiles of PSPs vs. conserved housekeeping orthologs provide strong evidence for a "two-speed" recombinational architecture in a sexually competent root-knot nematode.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Accession Completeness:** All raw and processed PacBio HiFi, ONT, Illumina, Hi-C, and Iso-Seq datasets, along with the genome assembly and annotation, are deposited under NCBI BioProject `PRJNA1265270`, supplementary source tables are archived on Zenodo (`10.5281/zenodo.15484846`), and full Snakemake/R/Python pipelines are available on GitHub/GitLab.
- **Methodological Detail:** Wet-lab protocols (HMW DNA extraction, Hi-C crosslinking, Iso-Seq, DNA FISH) and bioinformatic software versions/parameters are documented to a gold-standard level.
