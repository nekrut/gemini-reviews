### 1. Executive Summary
This study develops and validates a 16-locus Multi-Locus Variable-number tandem-repeat Analysis (`MLVA-16`) genotyping scheme for *Xanthomonas citri* pv. *mangiferaeindicae* (`Xcm`), the causal agent of mango and cashew bacterial canker, and evaluates it across a worldwide collection of 152 *Xcm* strains and 3 reference strains of the related Anacardiaceae pathogen *X. citri* pv. *anacardii* (`Xca`). By mining the reference genome `CFBP1716` (`CP156913`) and three additional high-quality *Xcm* assemblies (`CP156909`, `CP156905`, `CP073209`), the authors first show that the *Xcm* CRISPR locus lacks *cas* genes and is monomorphic (5 repeats / 5 spacers), making it unsuitable for subtyping. They then screen 23 candidate Tandem Repeat (TR) loci, retain 14 polymorphic minisatellites (plus 2 diagnostic ICE-associated loci `Xcm2532` and `Xcm2555` deleted in West African strains) supplemented with 2 low-polymorphism microsatellites (`XL6`, `XL7`), verify rare alleles and an `ISXcd1` insertion (`JF955`) by Sanger sequencing, and benchmark `MLVA-16` against historical AFLP data across 98 shared strains. The scheme achieves a Hunter-Gaston discriminatory index (HGDI) of 0.911 (vs. 0.948 for AFLP), strong distance-matrix congruence (Kendall's $W = 0.882, p = 0.0001$), and resolves 46 haplotypes into 5 DAPC clusters (`BIC` $k=5$, assignment probability $>0.99$) with clear epidemiological insights—including ruling out a South African origin for the West African cashew/mango outbreak clone. The study is well-executed and fit for purpose, with minor arithmetic and table-reporting inconsistencies that should be cleaned up.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
1. **Locus Count and Mean Nei's Diversity ($H_T$) Arithmetic in Table 1 vs. Main Text:**
   - **18 Loci in Table 1 vs. "MLVA-16":** Table 1 ("TR markers used in the MLVA-16 scheme") actually lists **18** loci: 16 `Xcm` minisatellite loci (`Xcm0323`, `Xcm0497`, `Xcm0794`, `Xcm0909`, `Xcm0943`, `Xcm1496`, `Xcm2532`, `Xcm2555`, `Xcm3117`, `Xcm3141`, `Xcm3755`, `Xcm3948`, `Xcm4232`, `Xcm4334`, `Xcm4431`, `Xcm4578`) plus the 2 microsatellites (`XL6` and `XL7`). As explained on line 117, `Xcm2532` and `Xcm2555` (located on 23 kb and 8 kb deletions within an integrative and conjugative element [ICE] absent in West African strains) have `ND` (not determined) for $H_T$ and were excluded from the genetic diversity analysis, leaving 14 minisatellites + 2 microsatellites = 16 loci (`MLVA-16`). Table 1's caption and footnotes should explicitly flag `Xcm2532` and `Xcm2555` as ancillary diagnostic markers excluded from the 16-locus numerical profile.
   - **Mean $H_T$ Calculation Discrepancy:** In the bottom row of Table 1 (and on line 126), the mean Nei's diversity index ($H_T$) across the 16 loci is reported as `0.300`. However, summing the 16 reported $H_T$ values in Table 1:
     $$0.389 + 0.795 + 0.333 + 0.051 + 0.493 + 0.496 + 0.013 + 0.039 + 0.013 + 0.494 + 0.494 + 0.209 + 0.389 + 0.299 + 0.482 + 0.397 = 5.386$$
     Dividing by 16 gives $5.386 / 16 = \mathbf{0.3366}$ (or for the 14 minisatellites alone, $4.507 / 14 = \mathbf{0.3219}$). Even if the 2 excluded loci (`Xcm2532`, `Xcm2555`) were treated as $0$, $5.386 / 18 = 0.2992 \approx 0.300$! Thus, `0.300` was inadvertently calculated by dividing the sum of 16 loci by 18 (treating the two `ND` cells as zeros).
2. **Candidate Marker Accounting (23 Candidate Loci vs. Retained/Excluded Loci):**
   - On line 67, the authors state that "TRF identified **23** TR loci that were evaluated using the *Xcm* strain collection and three reference *Xca* strains."
   - On line 117, they state that "**Six** candidate TRs (`Xcm2977`, `Xcm3763`, `Xcm3942`, `Xcm4486`, `Xcm4970` and `Xcm5175`) were found monomorphic for all *Xcm* strains assayed and were not considered further."
   - Subtracting 6 monomorphic loci from 23 evaluated TRF loci leaves **17** polymorphic loci, whereas Table 1 contains **16** TRF-derived `Xcm` loci (14 retained + `Xcm2532` + `Xcm2555`). One evaluated TRF locus is unaccounted for between 23 and $16 + 6 = 22$.
   - Additionally, on line 67, the authors state that 10 TR loci gave no amplification in *Xca* (including `Xcm4970`) and 3 markers (`Xcm4486`, `Xcm2555`, `Xcm3141`) yielded different-sized amplicons in *Xca*, concluding: "**Fourteen** TR markers, therefore, have a diagnostic value at the pathovar level." However, $10 + 3 = \mathbf{13}$, not 14 (unless one of the other loci in Table 1 with "No" amplification in *Xca*—such as `Xcm3948` or `XL6`/`XL7`—is the 14th, as Table 1 lists 11 loci with "No" for *Xca*: `Xcm0497`, `Xcm0794`, `Xcm0909`, `Xcm0943`, `Xcm2532`, `Xcm3117`, `Xcm3948`, `Xcm4232`, `Xcm4578`, `XL6`, `XL7`).
   - Minor typo on line 126: `Xcm37117` should be `Xcm3117`.
3. **DAPC Parameter Reporting (`adegenet`):** When running Discriminant Analysis of Principal Components (DAPC) on the 152 strains (or 46 unique haplotypes), the number of principal components ($n.\text{pca}$) and discriminant functions ($n.\text{da}$) retained—and whether cross-validation (`xvalDapc`) or $a$-score optimization (`optim.a.score`) was used to prevent overfitting—are not stated in the Methods. Retaining too many PCs relative to 16 variables can artificially inflate cluster assignment probabilities to $>0.99$.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: CRISPR arrays in *Xcm* lack *cas* genes and are monomorphic across available genomes, precluding CRISPR typing.** `[Well Supported]` — Directly established by `CRISPRCasFinder` across reference genomes (`S3 Table`).
- **Claim 2: The `MLVA-16` scheme provides high discriminatory power (HGDI = 0.911) and strong congruence with AFLP ($W = 0.882, p = 0.0001$), while distinguishing *Xcm* from *Xca*.** `[Well Supported]` — Validated across 152 *Xcm* strains, 98 AFLP-matched strains, 4 complete genomes, and Sanger sequencing of rare/IS-disrupted alleles (`JF955` harboring a 1,203-bp `ISXcd1` insertion).
- **Claim 3: West African *Xcm* strains emerging on mango and cashew form an exclusive, low-diversity genetic cluster (Cluster 1) carrying two ICE deletions (`Xcm2532`/`Xcm2555`) that did not originate from South African mango budwood.** `[Well Supported]` — Both the `MLVA-16` minimum spanning tree / DAPC clustering and the structural ICE deletions distinguish the West African outbreak clone from South African strains.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Database Availability:** GenBank accessions for the reference genomes (`CP156913`, `CP156909`, `CP156905`, `CP073209`), complete primer sequences and fluorescent labels (Table 1 and `S2 Table`), strain metadata (`S1 Table`), and public deposition of the full allele profiles in `MLVAbank` (`https://webphim.ird.fr/MLVA_Bank/Genotyping/index.php`) are provided.
- **Minor Reporting Additions:** State the exact number of PCs retained in `adegenet` DAPC and correct the denominator in the mean $H_T$ calculation in Table 1 (`0.337` across 16 loci vs. `0.300`).
