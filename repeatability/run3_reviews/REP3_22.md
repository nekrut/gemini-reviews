### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript investigates gut microbiome composition and peripheral blood mRNA expression of fatty acid metabolism enzymes (`ACC1`, `FASN`, and `CPT1A`, normalized to `GAPDH`) in 62 patients with drug-resistant tuberculosis—11 with pre-extensively drug-resistant or extensively drug-resistant TB (pre-XDR/XDR-TB), 23 with rifampicin-resistant TB (RR-TB), and 28 with multi-drug resistant TB (MDR-TB)—compared against 5 healthy controls (CK group) at Wenzhou Central Hospital. Stool DNA was extracted using a QIAamp Fecal DNA Mini Kit, pooled by group ("300 mg of faeces from patients from the same group was mixed"), and sequenced, while blood mRNA expression was assayed via qRT-PCR. The authors report alterations in microbial diversity and taxon abundances across resistance groups alongside elevated `ACC1/GAPDH` and `FASN/GAPDH` ratios in TB patients, concluding that antimicrobial-induced gut dysbiosis enhances *de novo* fatty acid synthesis and drives progression to advanced TB via gut–lung crosstalk.

This study is fundamentally invalid due to fatal experimental design, statistical, and reporting flaws. First, pooling 300 mg of stool across individuals within each clinical group destroys biological variance at the subject level, making valid statistical inference on inter-individual microbiome differences impossible; the caption claim that "Data are represented as means ± SD of three independent experiments" (Table 1, Table 2, Figure 2, Figure 6) confuses technical replicates of pooled samples (or mislabels human demographic data as "three independent experiments") with biological replication. Second, the control group consists of only $n = 5$ individuals compared against $n = 62$ patients, and the study cannot separate the effect of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* disease state from the massive confounding effect of multi-drug antibiotic regimens (despite listing antibiotic use in the past 6 months as an exclusion criterion on line 112 while simultaneously stating on line 107 that all patients were receiving anti-TB treatment). Third, the text contains pervasive internal contradictions regarding patient demographics, sampling schedules, disease labels, and taxon abundances, and no raw sequencing data or accession numbers are provided.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Gut microbiome $\alpha$-diversity and taxonomic composition differ significantly across RR-TB, pre-XDR-TB, MDR-TB, and healthy controls: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In the Abstract (line 21) and Methods (line 117), the authors state that "300 mg of faeces collected from the same group of individuals was mixed and analyzed via DNA extraction, high-throughput sequencing, and bioinformatics." Physical pooling of stool across patients within a group eliminates subject-level variance ($n=1$ composite pool per group). Computing one-way ANOVA $p$-values ($p < 0.05$ in Figure 2) across technical aliquots of a single group pool is pseudoreplication and statistically invalid. Furthermore, the sequencing methodology (line 122) does not even specify whether 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing (or which variable region) or shotgun metagenomics was performed.

* **Antimicrobial-induced gut microbiome dysbiosis enhances host *de novo* fatty acid synthesis (`ACC1`, `FASN`, `CPT1A`) and drives TB progression: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The authors measure mRNA transcripts of `ACC1`, `FASN`, and `CPT1A` in whole blood via qRT-PCR (line 129), yet repeatedly refer to "serum nascent fatty acid synthesis levels" (line 46) and "profiles and compositions of FASN in serum" (line 226), conflating whole-blood leukocyte mRNA expression with serum protein or metabolite levels. No correlation analysis between individual-level microbial taxa and gene expression was (or could be) performed because the stool samples were pooled by group; the claim that *Bacteroidetes*, *Bacteroidales*, and *Bacteroidaceae* "display the most important correlations" with *de novo* fatty acid synthesis (lines 26, 217, 228) is asserted without a single correlation coefficient ($r$) or $p$-value anywhere in the manuscript.

* **The study design isolates the effects of drug-resistant TB and anti-TB treatment on the microbiome: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The study design (lines 107–117) is internally contradictory. Line 112 states that exclusion criteria included "current (in the past six months) use of metabolic agents, probiotics, or antibiotics," yet line 107 states that all patients received anti-TB treatment and were sampled "at the start of anti-TB treatment and three months after," while line 117 states caregivers collected stool "at two and eight weeks," and line 161 states "All patients were monitored for three consecutive months." Moreover, none of the figures (Figures 1–6) present longitudinal time points (baseline vs. follow-up); each figure shows only a single bar or box per group (`Con`, `RR-TB`, `Pre-XDR`, `MDR-TB`).

* **Taxonomic distributions in healthy controls and TB subgroups follow consistent biological patterns: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The manuscript contradicts itself across sections regarding which taxa were present in which groups. Line 188 states that *Bacteroidales* accounted for 65% of bacteria in healthy individuals, 51% in RR-TB, 26% in pre-XDR-TB, and 37% in MDR-TB, whereas two sentences later (line 188) it states "the disappearance of *Bacteroidales* was seen in the patients with RR-TB, pre-XDR-TB, and MDR-TB," and line 224 claims "*Bacteroidetes*... were only found in the healthy participants," while line 228 claims their abundance in patient faeces was "1–3%, 1–2%, and 0–2%." Similarly, line 217 claims that "CPT1A is necessary for reduced immunity" and augments *de novo* fatty acid synthesis, whereas `CPT1A` is the rate-limiting enzyme for mitochondrial $\beta$-oxidation (fatty acid degradation), not *de novo* synthesis.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Sequence Individual Patient Stool Samples Rather Than Group Pools
* **WHY:** Pooling stool across patients within each clinical group erases inter-individual biological variation, prevents adjustment for host covariates (age, sex, BMI, nutritional status), and makes individual-level correlation with blood gene expression impossible.
* **WHAT:** Extract and sequence faecal DNA separately for each of the 62 patients and an appropriately powered, age-, sex-, and BMI-matched healthy control cohort.
* **HOW:** If archived unpooled stool samples stored at $-80^\circ\text{C}$ remain available for the 62 patients and controls, perform individual 16S rRNA (e.g., V3–V4) or shotgun metagenomic library preparation on each subject's sample at each time point, and analyze subject-level $\alpha$- and $\beta$-diversity using linear mixed-effects models or PERMANOVA accounting for repeated measures.

#### 2. Resolve Contradictions in Demographic Data and Statistical Reporting
* **WHY:** The reported demographic numbers in the text and tables are mutually contradictory and contain impossible statistical notes. Table 1 lists the mean BMI of the 62 active TB patients as $22.42 \pm 3.99\text{ kg/m}^2$ and Table 2 lists the mean BMI of the 5 healthy individuals as $24.12 \pm 3.14\text{ kg/m}^2$, but the Results text (line 161) reports that the 62 TB patients had a mean BMI of $24.12\text{ kg/m}^2$ (copying the healthy control number) and an age range of 36–59 years (which is the healthy control age range in Table 2). Furthermore, both Table 1 and Table 2 carry the footnote *"Note: Data are represented as means ± SD of three independent experiments"* for human patient counts, age, BMI, haemoglobin, and albumin.
* **WHAT:** Audit the clinical spreadsheets for the 62 patients and 5 controls and report actual subject-level means and standard deviations.
* **HOW:** Recompute the summary statistics directly from the 62 patient records and 5 control records, remove the erroneous "three independent experiments" footnote from clinical demographic tables, and correct the text on line 161 to match Table 1.

#### 3. Clarify Patient Classification, Sampling Timepoints, and Antibiotic Exposure
* **WHY:** The manuscript switches inconsistently between "pre-XDR-TB" (Abstract, Figures 1–6), "XDR-TB" (line 59), and "former XDR-TB" (line 112), and between sampling at "0 and 3 months" (line 107) versus "2 and 8 weeks" (line 117), without specifying which timepoint is plotted in Figures 1–6.
* **WHAT:** Standardize the resistance definitions according to WHO guidelines and explicitly state the exact timepoint relative to anti-TB drug initiation shown in every figure.
* **HOW:** Add a study flowchart detailing the exact drug regimens received by each subgroup, clarify whether the exclusion criterion for "antibiotics in the past 6 months" applied only to non-TB antibiotics prior to baseline, and separate baseline (pre-treatment) samples from on-treatment (2-week, 8-week, or 3-month) samples in all analyses.

#### 4. Perform Formal Statistical Correlation Between Taxa and Host Gene Expression
* **WHY:** The manuscript claims strong correlations between *Bacteroidetes/Bacteroidales/Bacteroidaceae* and *de novo* fatty acid synthesis enzymes without computing any statistical correlation test.
* **WHAT:** Compute Spearman rank correlations (with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction) between subject-level taxon relative abundances and blood `ACC1`, `FASN`, and `CPT1A` $2^{-\Delta\Delta\text{Ct}}$ expression values.
* **HOW:** Once individual-level microbiome profiles are generated, compute pairwise Spearman $\rho$ and FDR-adjusted $q$-values across the $n = 62$ patients and report the exact statistics in a table or heatmap.

#### 5. Deposit Raw Sequencing Data and Complete Methodological Details
* **WHY:** Reproducibility is currently zero: no sequencing platform (Illumina MiSeq/HiSeq, 454, or Ion Torrent), target amplicon primers, read depth, quality filtering pipeline, or public repository accession number (e.g., NCBI SRA / ENA / GSA) is provided.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Deposit all raw FASTQ files in a public sequence archive, state the exact sequencing instrument and 16S rRNA primers (or shotgun library kit) in the Methods, and fix the typo `GADPH` to `GAPDH` in Table 3 (line 146).
