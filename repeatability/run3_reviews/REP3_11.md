### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This manuscript re-analyzes a publicly available microarray dataset (GEO accession GSE20291) comprising 15 Parkinson's disease (PD) putamen samples and 15 pathologically normal control putamen samples assayed on the Affymetrix Human Genome U133A platform (12,688 genes). Using an unadjusted two-sample Student's $t$-test at $p < 0.05$, the authors report 715 differentially expressed genes (DEGs; 268 up-regulated and 447 down-regulated). They subject these 715 genes to Gene Ontology (GO) and KEGG pathway enrichment via DAVID, select 40 DEGs from 9 neuron-related GO terms, map them onto the Human Protein Reference Database (HPRD) protein-protein interaction (PPI) network, and apply the Clique Percolation Method (CFinder, $k = 3$) to highlight a subnetwork module containing *PRKCA*, *BCL2*, and *CDC42*. While the underlying dataset is publicly available in GEO, no analysis scripts, gene lists, or fold-change tables are provided.

The study's conclusions are undermined by a foundational statistical failure: performing 12,688 independent Student's $t$-tests at an unadjusted threshold of $p < 0.05$ without multiple-testing correction or fold-change filtering. Under a complete null hypothesis of no differential expression, $12,688 \times 0.05 = 634.4$ false positives are expected by chance alone, meaning that roughly 89% of the 715 reported DEGs are expected to be random statistical noise. Building downstream GO/KEGG enrichments, HPRD degree rankings, and speculative mechanistic narratives regarding intrinsic striatal tyrosine hydroxylase (TH) interneuron growth on an uncalibrated gene set invalidates the core claims. Salvaging this analysis requires recomputing differential expression with moderated $t$-statistics (`limma`) and Benjamini-Hochberg false discovery rate (FDR) control, accounting for bulk tissue cell-type composition, and restricting conclusions to supported transcriptional associations.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Identification of 715 differentially expressed genes (268 up-regulated, 447 down-regulated) in the PD putamen: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In the Methods ("Identification of differentially expressed genes (DEGs)", lines 50–52), the authors state that Student's $t$-test was applied across all 12,688 genes and all genes with unadjusted $p < 0.05$ were selected. With 12,688 tests, the expected number of false positives under the global null is $634.4$, yielding an expected false discovery proportion of $634.4 / 715 \approx 88.7\%$. Neither Benjamini-Hochberg FDR adjustment nor minimum $\log_2$ fold-change thresholds were applied, and no table of gene-level $t$-statistics, $p$-values, or effect sizes is presented.

* **Enrichment of neuron morphogenesis GO terms and Gap junction / Calcium signaling KEGG pathways among PD putamen DEGs: [Fragile].**  
  While Table 1 reports Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted $p$-values $< 0.015$ for top GO morphogenesis terms, Table 2 shows that 4 of the 6 highlighted KEGG pathways (*Phosphatidylinositol signaling system*, *Long-term potentiation*, *Alzheimer's disease*, and *GnRH signaling pathway*) fail the standard $\text{FDR} < 0.05$ threshold (adjusted $p$-values between $0.0595$ and $0.0639$). Moreover, feeding an uncalibrated input list of 715 genes (~89% expected null noise) into DAVID without specifying whether the background population was restricted to the 12,688 genes present on the U133A array inflates enrichment significance for brain-expressed genes.

* **Hub gene status of *PRKCA*, *BCL2*, and *CDC42* in the PPI network reflects a specific regulatory module in PD striatal pathology: [Overstated].**  
  In the Results ("PPI network", lines 135–145), the authors map 40 neuron-related DEGs onto the static HPRD literature-curated interactome and rank them by total node degree in HPRD (*PRKCA* degree 166, *RXRA* 81, *CDC42* 70, *BCL2* 69). In literature-curated databases like HPRD, heavily studied cancer and signaling proteins (*PRKCA*, *BCL2*, *CDC42*) inherently possess high global connectivity due to ascertainment bias in the literature, not because of disease-specific co-regulation in the PD putamen. No permutation test against random sets of 40 neuron-annotated genes was performed.

* **Altered expression of *OPHN1*, *PAX6*, connexin 43, *CaMKII*, *PRKCA*, *CDC42*, and *BCL2* promotes intrinsic striatal TH interneuron growth and reduces functional excitatory synapses: [Overstated].**  
  The Abstract (line 26), Discussion (lines 156–166), and Conclusion (line 171) assert a specific cellular mechanism in which these genes promote intrinsic striatal TH interneuron growth and inhibit apoptosis. Bulk U133A microarray profiles of postmortem putamen homogenates cannot resolve rare intrinsic striatal TH interneurons from medium spiny neurons, astrocytes, oligodendrocytes, or microglia, nor can observational postmortem mRNA levels establish causal directionality (compensation vs. degeneration). Furthermore, individual $p$-values, FDR values, and fold changes for *OPHN1*, *PAX6*, connexin 43 (*GJA1*), *CaMKII*, *PRKCA*, *CDC42*, and *BCL2* are never reported anywhere in the manuscript.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Replace Unadjusted Student's $t$-Tests with Empirical Bayes Moderated $t$-Statistics and FDR Control
* **WHY:** Testing 12,688 genes with unadjusted Student's $t$-tests at $p < 0.05$ guarantees ~634 false positives under the null hypothesis and suffers from unstable variance estimates at $n = 15$ per group.
* **WHAT:** Re-run differential expression using empirical Bayes variance moderation (`limma`) with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR adjustment, and report a complete summary table of top DEGs with $\log_2\text{FC}$, raw $p$-values, and BH-adjusted $q$-values.
* **HOW:** Using the RMA-normalized $12,688 \times 30$ expression matrix from GSE20291, fit a linear model via `lmFit()` and `eBayes()` in the R `limma` package. Define DEGs at BH-adjusted $q < 0.05$ (or if power is limited at $n=15$, report the exact FDR at a specified $|\log_2\text{FC}| > 0.5$ and $p < 0.005$ threshold alongside the expected false discovery count).

#### 2. Use the Exact Affymetrix U133A Chip Background and Threshold-Free GSEA for Pathway Analysis
* **WHY:** Default DAVID enrichment uses the whole human genome as background, which artificially inflates neuron/brain GO terms when testing genes expressed in brain tissue on the U133A array, and hard thresholding at unadjusted $p < 0.05$ injects ~634 noise genes.
* **WHAT:** Perform threshold-free Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA) ranked by the moderated $t$-statistic across all 12,688 genes, and explicitly set the 12,688 U133A Entrez genes as the background universe for any over-representation test.
* **HOW:** Rank all 12,688 genes by their `limma` moderated $t$-statistic and run `fgsea` or `clusterProfiler::GSEA` against GO Biological Process and KEGG gene sets with 10,000 permutations, reporting normalized enrichment scores (NES) and BH-adjusted $p$-values.

#### 3. Benchmark HPRD PPI Connectivity Against Degree-Preserving Null Permutations and Co-Expression
* **WHY:** Ranking proteins by their static degree in HPRD simply recovers well-studied signaling hubs (*PRKCA*, *BCL2*, *CDC42*) regardless of the disease phenotype.
* **WHAT:** Test whether the observed PPI connectivity and CFinder module density among the PD-associated genes exceed random expectations for neuron-annotated genes on the U133A platform, and weight edges by empirical co-expression in GSE20291.
* **HOW:** Sample 1,000 random gene sets of size 40 matched for GO neuron annotation from the 12,688 U133A genes, compute the empirical $p$-value of the observed internal edge count and hub degrees in HPRD, and filter HPRD edges to pairs showing significant Pearson correlation ($|r| > 0.5$, $\text{FDR} < 0.05$) across the 30 putamen samples.

#### 4. Reframe Mechanistic Claims Around Bulk Tissue Associations and Account for Cell-Type Proportions
* **WHY:** Postmortem putamen microarray data measure bulk tissue mRNA affected by neuronal loss and gliosis and cannot demonstrate intrinsic TH interneuron proliferation or synaptic remodeling.
* **WHAT:** Estimate cell-type marker shifts across the 30 samples and revise the Abstract, Discussion, and Conclusion to remove unsupported causal claims about TH interneuron growth.
* **HOW:** Check the expression of canonical cell-type markers (e.g., *TH*, *DRD1*, *DRD2*, *RBFOX3* for neurons; *GFAP*, *AQP4* for astrocytes; *AIF1* for microglia) in GSE20291, report their exact $\log_2\text{FC}$ and adjusted $p$-values alongside *OPHN1*, *PAX6*, *GJA1*, *CAMK2A*, *PRKCA*, *CDC42*, and *BCL2*, and rewrite the Conclusion to state that these transcripts represent candidate bulk striatal correlates requiring cell-type-specific experimental validation.
