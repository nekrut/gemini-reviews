### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This study constructs a 13-lncRNA prognostic signature ("necroptosis-related lncRNA signature," NRLS) for glioma by combining lower-grade glioma (LGG) and glioblastoma (GBM) RNA-seq cohorts from TCGA ($n = 663$), normal brain tissue from GTEx, and validation cohorts from CGGA ($n = 1,018$ across mRNAseq_693 and mRNAseq_325). Candidate lncRNAs are selected by differential expression of 67 literature-curated necroptosis genes between merged TCGA-Glioma and GTEx-Brain (`DESeq2` after `limma::removeBatchEffect`), Pearson correlation ($|r| > 0.5, P < 0.001$) in both TCGA and CGGA, univariate Cox regression ($P < 0.001$) in both TCGA and CGGA, and LASSO Cox regression in TCGA. Downstream analyses apply GSEA, immune deconvolution (CIBERSORT, ESTIMATE, ssGSEA, TIDE), drug IC50 prediction (`pRRophetic`), `ConsensusClusterPlus` ($k = 2$), and qRT-PCR of 5 lncRNAs in 5 epilepsy control brain tissues and 11 glioma tissues.

While the study integrates two large public glioma cohorts and includes a small qRT-PCR check, its methodology exhibits major statistical, bioinformatic, and internal consistency flaws characteristic of template-driven biomarker pipelines: (1) direct data leakage from the CGGA "validation" cohort during feature selection (requiring $|r| > 0.5$ and univariate Cox $P < 0.001$ in *both* TCGA and CGGA to select the 43 candidate lncRNAs prior to LASSO, lines 54 and 105); (2) pooling biologically distinct entities (WHO Grade 2/3 IDH-mutant LGG and IDH-wildtype GBM) into a single survival model, where any transcriptional signature correlated with tumor grade/IDH status automatically yields high combined ROC-AUCs (0.86–0.92); (3) direct self-contradiction regarding which patient group benefits from immunotherapy (Section 3.6 line 160 concludes "the low-risk group is more susceptible to immunotherapy," whereas Section 3.8 line 182 concludes "Cluster 1 [which is the high-risk group, lines 182 and 214] was more susceptible to immunotherapy"); and (4) mathematical and methodological errors including reporting negative $r^2$ values ($r^2 = -0.41$, line 155), feeding continuous batch-corrected values into `DESeq2` (which requires raw integer counts, lines 49–54), and selecting GSEA pathways using `adjusted P value > 0.01` (line 144). Re-analysis without validation-set leakage, stratified by 2021 WHO molecular subtypes, and correction of contradictory claims are required.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **The 13-lncRNA NRLS is an accurately validated independent prognostic signature in CGGA (lines 16–21, 54, 105, 116–133): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In Section 2.2 (line 54) and Section 3.1 (line 105), the authors explicitly use the CGGA validation cohort during feature selection: both the Pearson correlation screen ($|r| > 0.5, P < 0.001$) and the univariate Cox survival screen ($P < 0.001$) were performed in *both* TCGA and CGGA, and only the 43 intersecting lncRNAs already known to be highly prognostic in CGGA ($P < 0.001$) were passed to LASSO in TCGA. Evaluating a 13-gene subset of those 43 CGGA-pre-selected genes back on CGGA (Figures 2e, 4b, 4e) constitutes severe test-set leakage and invalidates CGGA as an independent validation cohort. Furthermore, pooling LGG (median survival 5–15 years) and GBM (median survival ~15 months) inflates the overall AUCs (0.88–0.92 in TCGA; 0.77–0.84 in CGGA) due to confounding by tumor grade and IDH mutation status; the authors do not report time-dependent AUCs for the NRLS *within* IDH-wildtype GBM alone or compare the nomogram AUC against clinical covariates (Age + Grade + IDH + 1p/19q) alone.

* **Differential expression of necroptosis-related genes between TCGA-Glioma and GTEx-Brain using `limma::removeBatchEffect` followed by `DESeq2` (lines 49–54, 105): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Lines 49–54 state that TCGA-Glioma and GTEx-Brain count matrices were merged using `removeBatchEffect()` in the `limma` package and then analyzed with `DESeq2` (`|Log2FC| > 2`, `adj. P < 0.05`). `limma::removeBatchEffect()` operates on continuous log-expression values and outputs continuous real numbers (including negative values), whereas `DESeq2` models raw negative-binomial integer counts and cannot validly take `removeBatchEffect`-transformed matrices as input. Moreover, since all tumor samples are in TCGA and all normal cortex samples are in GTEx, cohort/study batch and biological condition (tumor vs. normal) are 100% collinear, meaning `removeBatchEffect()` without a shared reference either removes the biological signal or leaves arbitrary platform artifacts.

* **Predictions of immunotherapy susceptibility across NRLS risk groups and consensus clusters (lines 21, 160, 182, 210): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  The manuscript directly contradicts itself between Sections 3.6 and 3.8. In Section 3.6 (line 160) and Discussion (line 210), based on higher TIDE dysfunction scores in the high-risk group (Figure 6e–g), the authors conclude: *"These results suggested that the low-risk group is more susceptible to immunotherapy."* Two paragraphs later, in Section 3.8 (line 182), after showing that Cluster 1 has higher risk scores and worse survival than Cluster 2 (and stating in line 214 that "all the patients in Cluster 2 were in the low-risk group" and "the consensus clustering method and the risk scoring system were basically similar"), the authors conclude the exact opposite: *"We observed that Cluster 1 has stronger immune infiltration than Cluster 2... Therefore, we could consider Cluster 1 was more susceptible to immunotherapy."* In addition, TIDE was trained on melanoma and non-small cell lung cancer bulk transcriptomes, not brain tumors where microglia and blood-brain barrier dynamics dominate.

* **qRT-PCR validation of 5 NRLs across glioma grades vs. non-tumor brain tissue (lines 97, 193, Figure 9): [Fragile].**  
  First, the sample description in Section 2.9 (line 97) contains a duplication typo (*"five non-tumor brain tissues, three WHO grade 2, three WHO grade 2, and four GBM"*, which sums to $5 + 3 + 3 + 4 = 15$ samples, whereas the Figure 9 caption states *"5 non-tumor brain tissues and 11 glioma tissues"* = 16 samples, likely meaning three Grade 2, four Grade 3, and four GBM, or similar). Second, with only $n = 3$–$4$ samples per grade, no statistical test, $p$-value, or multiple-testing correction is reported in Section 3.9 or Figure 9.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Eliminate Validation Cohort Leakage by Restricting All Feature Discovery to TCGA
* **WHY:** Screening lncRNAs for univariate Cox significance ($P < 0.001$) and correlation ($|r| > 0.5$) in CGGA before fitting the LASSO model in TCGA leaks survival and co-expression information from the validation set into the predictor.
* **WHAT:** Perform all correlation filtering, univariate Cox screening, LASSO variable selection, and risk-score cutoff determination exclusively within the TCGA training cohort, holding CGGA completely blinded until the final model formula and threshold are locked.
* **HOW:** Re-run the pipeline starting from the 14,086 lncRNAs using only TCGA ($n = 663$) to derive the LASSO Cox coefficients and median/survminer threshold, then apply the exact locked linear predictor $\sum \beta_i x_i$ to CGGA (`mRNAseq_693` and `mRNAseq_325` analyzed separately as well as combined) and report the resulting C-indices and time-dependent AUCs.

#### 2. Quantify Incremental Prognostic Value Beyond the 2021 WHO Molecular Classification
* **WHY:** Although the Introduction (line 31) and Discussion (line 206) emphasize the 2021 WHO CNS classification (which separates IDH-wildtype GBM from IDH-mutant astrocytoma and oligodendroglioma), the survival models pool LGG and GBM together. Any gene set differentially expressed between IDH-wildtype GBM and IDH-mutant LGG will appear strongly prognostic in a pooled cohort without necessarily adding clinical value beyond standard molecular pathology.
* **WHAT:** Compare the C-index and 1-, 3-, and 5-year ROC-AUC of a baseline clinical-molecular model (`Age + WHO Grade + IDH status + 1p/19q codeletion + MGMT promoter methylation`) against `Baseline + NRLS`, and report C-indices stratified by 2021 WHO subtype (IDH-wildtype GBM, IDH-mutant 1p/19q-intact astrocytoma, and IDH-mutant 1p/19q-codeleted oligodendroglioma).
* **HOW:** Use `timeROC` or `pec` in R to compute ΔAUC and likelihood-ratio test $p$-values for adding NRLS to the clinical-molecular model in both TCGA and CGGA.

#### 3. Resolve Contradictory Immunotherapy Claims and Correct Statistical/Mathematical Errors
* **WHY:** Claiming that the low-risk group is more responsive to immunotherapy in Section 3.6 while claiming that the high-risk cluster (Cluster 1) is more responsive in Section 3.8 leaves the clinical interpretation incoherent, and impossible mathematical values ($r^2 = -0.41$) indicate confusion between $r$ and $r^2$.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  * **Immunotherapy contradiction (lines 160 vs. 182):** Reconcile Sections 3.6 and 3.8 by noting that higher immune infiltration in high-risk glioma (Cluster 1) is accompanied by high M2 macrophage/Treg infiltration and checkpoint expression (high TIDE dysfunction score), and refrain from claiming clinical immunotherapy benefit without actual checkpoint-inhibitor treated glioma cohort data.
  * **Negative $r^2$ (line 155):** Line 155 reports *"resting T cell CD4+ memory was negatively correlated with B cell plasma ($r^2 = -0.41$)"*. A squared correlation coefficient ($r^2$) cannot be negative; change $r^2$ to Pearson/Spearman $r$ across line 155 ($r = 0.59, r = 0.60, r = -0.41$).
  * **GSEA threshold typo (line 144):** Line 144 states *"We selected terms with adjusted P value > 0.01 and |NES| > 1.5"*. Change `> 0.01` to `< 0.01` (or `< 0.05`).
  * **qRT-PCR sample counts and gene name typo (lines 97, 199):** Correct *"three WHO grade 2, three WHO grade 2, and four GBM"* in line 97 to reflect the actual grade breakdown summing to 11 glioma tissues, and fix `FAM18A-AS1` in the Figure 9 caption (line 199) to `FAM181A-AS1`.
  * **Terminology and citation formatting typos:** Line 31: change *"isocratic dehydrogenase (IDH)"* to *"isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH)"*; line 37: change *"length of up to 200 nucleotides"* to *"length of more than 200 nucleotides"* (the standard definition of lncRNAs); lines 39 and 208: remove stray `(p1)` and `(p2)` artifacts; line 128: fix `1p9ql` / `1p19ql` to `1p/19q`.
