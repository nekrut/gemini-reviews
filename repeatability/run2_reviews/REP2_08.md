### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

This study integrates bladder cancer (BC) single-cell RNA-seq data (`GSE135337`, $n = 7$ patients, subsetted to 1,517 non-epithelial tumor microenvironment [TME] cells) with bulk RNA-seq and overall survival (OS) data from an immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI)-treated cohort (`GSE176307`, $n = 89$) using the `Scissor` algorithm (Cox regression penalty $\alpha = 0.1$) to identify TME subpopulations associated with survival. Finding 115 `Scissor-` cells (enriched for M1/M2 macrophages and plasma B cells) associated with favorable OS and 15 upregulated genes (`FindMarkers` with DESeq2, $\text{FDR} < 0.05$, $|\log_2\text{FC}| > 1.5$), the authors select 10 genes (`SSR4`, `RGS1`, `HLA-DRB5`, `APOE`, `C1QB`, `C1QA`, `APOC1`, `JCHAIN`, `C1QC`, `DERL3`) whose mean expression $z$-score defines the Bladder Cancer Gene Signature (`BC-GS`). They evaluate `BC-GS` (median-split) alone and combined with tumor mutation burden (`TMB`) in `GSE176307` ($n = 89$), `IMvigor210` ($n = 195$ bladder-origin subset), and `UC-GENOME` ($n = 101$), alongside Reactome pathway scoring and `CIBERSORT` immune deconvolution. Custom R scripts are publicly available on GitHub (`https://github.com/mykamki/finding-immunotherapeutic-marker`).

While the replication of the 10-gene macrophage/plasma-cell signature's univariate OS association in `IMvigor210` ($\text{HR} = 0.70, p = 0.043$) and `UC-GENOME` ($\text{HR} = 0.36, p = 0.00081$) indicates a genuine prognostic signal linked to lipid-associated tumor-associated macrophages (`APOE`, `APOC1`, `C1QA/B/C`) and plasma B cells (`JCHAIN`, `SSR4`, `DERL3`), several methodological flaws undermine the strength of the claims: (1) **resubstitution leakage in `GSE176307` and cross-dataset gene filtering**, where `GSE176307` is used to supervise `Scissor` and select the 10 genes "stably overexpressed across the datasets" (Line 63), yet is repeatedly presented as one of "three independent datasets" ($\text{HR} = 0.49, p = 0.008$); (2) **absence of multivariable Cox adjustment** for standard clinical prognostic factors (ECOG performance status, visceral/liver metastasis, age, sex, prior platinum therapy, or PD-L1 immunohistochemistry); (3) **conflation of prognostic vs. predictive utility**, since no non-ICI-treated control cohort (e.g., TCGA-BLCA or chemotherapy arm) is tested for a treatment-by-biomarker interaction; and (4) **contradictory description of single-cell vs. pseudobulk differential expression** across only $n = 7$ scRNA-seq patients. All of these limitations can be resolved through additional computational analyses using existing public datasets.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Integration of `GSE135337` scRNA-seq and `GSE176307` bulk RNA-seq via `Scissor` identifies 115 macrophage/plasma-B cells (`Scissor-`) associated with favorable OS and 15 upregulated DEGs: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Applying `Scissor` to map bulk survival phenotypes onto non-epithelial TME cells is an appropriate exploratory strategy, and the resulting cell clusters clearly express complement macrophage (`C1QA`, `C1QB`, `C1QC`, `APOE`, `APOC1`) and plasma cell (`JCHAIN`, `SSR4`, `DERL3`) markers. However, the Methods section ("Analysis of DEGs", Line 53) contains an internal contradiction: it states that Seurat's `FindMarkers` function with the `DESeq` test argument was used, and immediately claims "To mitigate bias toward highly expressed genes, pseudobulk methods were used instead of single-cell differential expression approaches [19]." Running `Seurat::FindMarkers(..., test.use = "DESeq2")` on 115 `Scissor-` cells vs. 1,229 background cells treats individual cells (drawn from only $n = 7$ patients) as independent replicates rather than aggregating counts by patient sample (true pseudobulk), inflating false-positive significance due to within-patient cell-to-cell correlation.

* **High `BC-GS` significantly predicts longer OS across "three independent datasets" (`GSE176307`, `IMvigor210`, and `UC-GENOME`): [Overstated].**  
  `GSE176307` ($n = 89$) is **not** an independent validation dataset: its bulk RNA-seq profiles and OS times were directly input into `Scissor` to select the `Scissor-` cells from which the signature was derived. Furthermore, in Methods ("BC-GS score", Line 63), the authors state that out of the 15 upregulated DEGs, "10 stably overexpressed genes ... **across the datasets** were carefully selected for further analysis," indicating that expression properties across the validation datasets influenced which 10 of the 15 genes were retained. In the largest external cohort (`IMvigor210`, $n = 195$), the unadjusted univariate association is borderline ($\text{HR} = 0.70$, 95% CI $0.49$–$0.99$, $p = 0.043$), and no multivariable Cox regression adjusting for ECOG performance status, metastatic site, or TMB is reported to confirm independent prognostic value.

* **Combining `BC-GS` and `TMB` (`HH`, `HL`, `LH`, `LL`) refines patient stratification and predicts response to ICI therapy: [Fragile].**  
  While the four-group Kaplan–Meier curves (`HH`, `HL`, `LH`, `LL`; Figure 4b–c) separate survival trajectories in `IMvigor210` and `UC-GENOME`, two issues weaken this claim. First, the authors never test a formal multivariable Cox model containing both continuous (or binary) `BC-GS` and `TMB` (`Surv(OS) ~ BC_GS + TMB + clinical_covariates`) with a likelihood ratio test for added prognostic value ($\Delta \text{C-index}$ or $\Delta \chi^2$). Second, because all three cohorts received ICI without a non-ICI control group, a higher OS in `HH` patients only demonstrates that baseline immune infiltration (`BC-GS`) and high `TMB` are favorable **prognostic** markers, not that `BC-GS` specifically predicts therapeutic benefit from PD-1/PD-L1 blockade over chemotherapy.

* **High `BC-GS` tumors exhibit higher `CD8+ T effector`, antigen presentation (`APM`), and immune checkpoint pathway expression and elevated `CIBERSORT` M1 macrophages and activated memory CD4+ T cells: [Moderately Supported].**  
  Because `BC-GS` is defined by the expression of professional antigen-presenting macrophage genes (`HLA-DRB5`, `C1QA`, `C1QB`, `C1QC`, `APOE`, `APOC1`) and lymphoid/plasma cell genes (`RGS1`, `JCHAIN`), its correlation with bulk RNA-seq `APM`/`CD8+ T effector` signatures and `CIBERSORT` macrophage/CD4+ T-cell fractions (Figures 3 and 5) is biologically consistent, though partly tautological because myeloid and lymphoid transcripts co-vary with overall tumor immune infiltration ("hot" vs. "cold" tumors) in bulk RNA-seq.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Separate Discovery (`GSE176307`) from External Validation (`IMvigor210`, `UC-GENOME`) and Eliminate Gene-Selection Leakage
* **WHY:** Presenting `GSE176307` as an "independent dataset" in Figures 2 and 4 when it was used to train `Scissor` inflates apparent performance, and manually picking 10 of 15 DEGs based on behavior "across the datasets" introduces selection leakage.
* **WHAT:** Label `GSE176307` explicitly as the **Discovery / Training Cohort** and `IMvigor210` and `UC-GENOME` as the **Two External Validation Cohorts** throughout the Abstract, Results, and Figure 2/4 captions, and evaluate the full unweighted 15-gene `Scissor-` signature alongside the 10-gene subset.
* **HOW:** Compute the average $z$-score of all 15 upregulated `Scissor-` genes (`log2FC > 1.5, FDR < 0.05`, determined strictly within `GSE135337`/`GSE176307`) and report its HR, 95% CI, and log-rank $p$-value in `IMvigor210` and `UC-GENOME` to prove that performance does not depend on manual post-hoc filtering of 10 genes.

#### 2. Fit Multivariable Cox Proportional Hazards Models Adjusting for Clinical Covariates and TMB
* **WHY:** Unadjusted univariate Kaplan–Meier median splits ($p = 0.043$ in `IMvigor210`) can be confounded by baseline clinical variables (e.g., ECOG performance status, visceral/liver metastases, age, sex, and PD-L1 immune cell IHC score, all of which are included in `IMvigor210CoreBiologies` and `UC-GENOME`).
* **WHAT:** Add a multivariable Cox regression table for `IMvigor210` and `UC-GENOME` testing continuous and median-split `BC-GS` adjusted for `TMB`, `PD-L1 IHC`, `ECOG`, `Age`, `Sex`, and `Metastasis status`.
* **HOW:** In R (`survival::coxph`), fit `coxph(Surv(os, censOS) ~ BC_GS + log10(TMB) + IC_Level + ECOG + Sex)` in `IMvigor210` and `UC-GENOME`, and report the adjusted HRs, 95% CIs, and Harrell's C-index improvement ($\Delta \text{C-index}$) when adding `BC-GS` to `TMB` + clinical covariates.

#### 3. Test Prognostic vs. Predictive Specificity Using Non-ICI Bladder Cancer Data (`TCGA-BLCA`)
* **WHY:** Because all analyzed patients received ICI, it is unknown whether `BC-GS` is a general prognostic marker of immune-infiltrated bladder cancer or a specific predictive biomarker of ICI response.
* **WHAT:** Evaluate `BC-GS` in non-ICI-treated muscle-invasive bladder cancer (`TCGA-BLCA`, $n \approx 400$) and adjust terminology accordingly.
* **HOW:** Compute the 10-gene `BC-GS` score in `TCGA-BLCA` RNA-seq data (`TCGAbiolinks`) and test its association with OS. If `BC-GS` also predicts longer survival in `TCGA-BLCA` without immunotherapy, reframe `BC-GS` throughout the text as a **prognostic TME biomarker in ICI-treated BC** rather than an ICI-specific predictive biomarker.

#### 4. Clarify the Differential Expression Method (`FindMarkers` vs. Pseudobulk) and Fix Broken Figure References
* **WHY:** Line 53 simultaneously claims to use `Seurat::FindMarkers(test.use = "DESeq")` on single cells and "pseudobulk methods instead of single-cell differential expression approaches," and the Discussion cites a non-existent figure panel.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  1. **Methods ("Analysis of DEGs", Line 53):** Clarify whether `DESeq2` was run on single-cell count columns via `Seurat::FindMarkers` ($n = 115$ vs. $1,229$ cells) or on patient-aggregated pseudobulk count matrices (`AggregateExpression` by patient $\times$ `Scissor` status across the $n = 7$ patients in `GSE135337`). If run at the single-cell level, remove the inaccurate claim that pseudobulk methods were used and include `Patient_ID` as a latent variable (`latent.vars = "Patient_ID"` in `MAST`/`LR`) to control for patient-level clustering.
  2. **Discussion (Line 139):** Change "as depicted in Fig. 1 a, b, f, and Supplementary Fig. 7" to "Fig. 1 a, b, d, e, and Supplementary Fig. 7" (Figure 1 only has panels `a` through `e`).
