### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

Lucaci et al. investigate the molecular evolution of the small Ras-like GTPase gene *Rem2* across Mammalia. Retrieving 186 mammalian RefSeq *Rem2* orthologs from NCBI and filtering out 9 low-quality sequences (yielding a final alignment of 175 species across 627 codon sites), the authors build a codon-aware MAFFT alignment (`codon-msa` with `find-outliers` masking), infer a maximum-likelihood gene tree in IQ-TREE 2, and apply a suite of HyPhy v2.5.40 models (`BUSTED`, `BUSTED+S`, `BUSTED+MH`, `BUSTED+S+MH`, `FEL`, `MEME`, `SLAC`, and `BGM`). They find no statistically significant evidence of gene-wide episodic diversifying selection after accounting for synonymous rate variation and multiple-nucleotide hits (`BUSTED+S+MH` LRT $p = 0.50$, model-averaged $p = 0.2272$; `Table 2`), pervasive purifying selection across 70% of non-invariant codon sites (`FEL`, FDR $\le 0.1$), 251/627 invariant codon sites, and 50 pairs of putatively coevolving codon sites (`BGM` posterior probability $\ge 0.5$). They also cross-reference three coevolving human residues (codons 48, 64, and 98) with gnomAD v4.1.0 variants (`Table 3`) and run ColabFold/AlphaFold2 on 175 extant and 159 `SLAC`-reconstructed ancestral sequences (`SPA` pipeline) to compare pairwise `TM-align` scores (mean $0.62 \pm 0.11$) and backbone RMSD (mean $3.61 \pm 0.54\,\text{Å}$) against Tamura-Nei (`TN93`) genetic distances. Raw alignments, HyPhy JSON outputs, and scripts are publicly archived on GitHub and Zenodo (`10.5281/zenodo.11550125`).

The application of `BUSTED+S+MH` model averaging to demonstrate how apparent episodic positive selection under standard `BUSTED` ($p = 0.0000$, $\omega_3 = 23.16$) and `BUSTED+S` ($p = 0.0000$, $\omega_3 = 50.75$) vanishes when multi-nucleotide substitutions (`+MH`) are modeled ($p = 0.5000$, $\omega_3 = 1.00$) is a solid, instructive empirical demonstration, and the open availability of JSON results and code is commendable. However, several downstream analyses and biological claims suffer from methodological artifacts: (1) feeding `SLAC` ancestral sequences (which reconstruct gappy, unaligned N- and C-terminal intrinsically disordered regions [IDRs]) into AlphaFold2 and computing whole-protein `TM-align`/RMSD metrics conflates low-confidence IDR disorder (`pLDDT < 50`) and lineage-specific indel length differences (627 alignment columns vs. 340 residues in human Rem2) with genuine structural divergence; (2) the Robinson-Foulds distance comparison (`RF = 122`, normalized `0.535`) compares a binary ML gene tree against a multifurcating NCBI Taxonomy tree without collapsing weakly supported gene-tree branches; (3) `GARD` recombination screening is described in Methods as a critical pre-processing step, yet no `GARD` results are reported anywhere in Results; and (4) `Table 3` contains a 469 kb genomic coordinate error on human chromosome 14 for codon 98 (`22,884,863` vs. `23,353,xxx`), and the disease links to Huntington's disease, Long QT syndrome, and Timothy syndrome are overstated relative to the cited evidence. These issues can be fixed via targeted re-analysis and editorial revision.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **Mammalian *Rem2* is dominated by pervasive purifying selection (70% of variable sites via `FEL`), and apparent gene-wide episodic positive selection disappears once synonymous rate variation and multi-nucleotide substitutions are accounted for (`BUSTED+S+MH`): [Well Supported].**  
  `Table 2` clearly shows that adding synonymous rate variation (`+S`) reduces $\text{cAIC}$ by $>820$ points ($47,174.7 \to 46,351.6$), and adding multi-nucleotide hits (`BUSTED+S+MH`, $\text{cAIC} = 46,352.0$) absorbs the tiny tail of high-$\omega$ branch-sites ($p_3 = 0.0012, \omega_3 = 50.75$ under `BUSTED+S`) into the triple-hit rate ($\text{TH Rate} = 0.2599$), collapsing $\omega_3$ to $1.0000$ ($LRT\ p = 0.5000$; model-averaged $p = 0.2272$). Likewise, site-level `MEME` detects zero positively selected sites after FDR correction, while `FEL` identifies widespread purifying selection ($\omega < 1$ at FDR $\le 0.1$) across the core GTPase domain and flanking regions. One unresolved gap is that all `FEL`, `MEME`, `BGM`, and `BUSTED` tests were run on a single IQ-TREE phylogeny even though Methods ("Recombination detection") states that `GARD` was run to screen for recombination breakpoints that would otherwise bias selection inference; the manuscript never reports whether `GARD` found zero breakpoints or whether partitioned trees were used.

* **Ancestral sequence reconstruction coupled with AlphaFold2 (`SPA`) reveals structural divergence and dynamics across mammalian *Rem2* (`TM-score` $0.62 \pm 0.11$, RMSD $3.61 \pm 0.54\,\text{Å}$): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Human Rem2 is 340 amino acids long, with a conserved ~170-residue Ras-like GTPase (RGK) core (`PDB: 3CBQ`, 169 modeled residues) flanked by long N- and C-terminal intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs), whereas the 175-species gappy alignment spans 627 codons. Computing whole-protein `TM-align` scores and global backbone RMSDs across full-length AlphaFold2 predictions of extant and `SLAC`-reconstructed ancestral sequences is biophysically invalid for two reasons: (1) AlphaFold2 does not predict true conformational ensembles for IDRs—it outputs arbitrary, low-confidence (`pLDDT < 50`) extended "spaghetti" ribbons whose random spatial orientations artificially inflate backbone RMSD ($3.61\,\text{Å}$) and depress `TM-score` ($0.62$, producing the large scatter at genetic distance $< 0.05$ noted in `Figure 5`); and (2) `SLAC` performs marginal/joint maximum-likelihood character reconstruction at gappy columns without a probabilistic indel model, generating chimeric ancestral lengths. Structural comparisons must be restricted to the ordered RGK GTPase domain (`pLDDT > 70` or aligned to `PDB: 3CBQ`).

* **Bayesian Graphical Models (`BGM`) identify 50 coevolving codon pairs, including a clinically relevant human network at codons 48, 64, and 98 (`Table 3`): [Fragile].**  
  First, `BGM` infers correlated substitutions mapped onto the gene tree using a lenient posterior probability cutoff of $0.5$, and in gappy regions outside the RGK core (`codon-msa` + `find-outliers` masking), correlated presence/absence or isoform-boundary shifts across RefSeq gene predictions can mimic co-substitutions. Second, in the text (line 217), the authors state that alignment sites `86, 604, 72, 68, 120` "all... fall within the RGK region, and correspond to 64, 319 (in RGK), 52, 48, 98, respectively" in human Rem2; however, human Rem2 residues 48, 52, and 64 lie in the N-terminal intrinsically disordered regulatory region *upstream* of the RGK GTPase core (which spans roughly residues 100–268 in `PDB: 3CBQ`), contradicting the statement that all five sites lie within the RGK domain. Third, in `Table 3`, the genomic coordinate for human Rem2 codon 98 (`Ser98Phe`) is listed as `chr14: 22,884,863`, which is ~469,000 bp away from codons 48 and 64 (`chr14: 23,353,918` and `23,353,969`)—outside the ~5 kb human *REM2* gene locus altogether—and the presence of ultra-rare singleton loss-of-function or missense alleles in gnomAD v4.1.0 is normal background standing variation, not evidence of epistatic clinical disease.

* **The *Rem2* gene tree deviates significantly from the mammalian species tree (normalized Robinson-Foulds distance $= 0.535$) due to incomplete lineage sorting or lineage-specific rate variation: [Overstated].**  
  In a highly conserved 340-codon gene where 40% of sites are invariant and 70% of variable sites are under strong purifying selection, short internal branches within mammalian orders lack informative substitutions and have low bootstrap support (`Figure 1`). Furthermore, the NCBI Taxonomy common tree is heavily multifurcating (polytomous) rather than a fully resolved bifurcating phylogeny. Computing an uncollapsed Robinson-Foulds distance (`RF = 122 / 228 = 0.535`) between a binary ML tree with near-zero-length, low-bootstrap branches and a polytomous taxonomy tree measures phylogenetic estimation noise and resolution mismatch, not biological incomplete lineage sorting (ILS).

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Restrict AlphaFold2 Structural Comparisons (`SPA`) to the Ordered RGK Core (`PDB: 3CBQ`) and Use Indel-Aware Ancestral Reconstruction
* **WHY:** Superimposing full-length AlphaFold2 models that include ~170 residues of low-confidence (`pLDDT < 50`) N- and C-terminal intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs) and `SLAC` gap artifacts dominates both RMSD ($3.61\,\text{Å}$) and `TM-score` ($0.62$) with non-physical IDR ribbon orientations.
* **WHAT:** Recompute pairwise `TM-align` scores and RMSDs strictly across the ordered RGK GTPase domain residues (corresponding to `PDB: 3CBQ` / `pLDDT` $\ge 70$) and verify that ancestral sequences are reconstructed with an indel-aware tool (e.g., `FastML`, `GRASP`, or `IQ-TREE -asr` with indel tracking).
* **HOW:** Slice the extant and ancestral PDB coordinate files in the `SPA-REM2` repository to the conserved RGK domain coordinates (human Rem2 residues ~100–268), re-run `tmalign` across all pairs, and plot the core-domain `TM-score` and RMSD alongside the full-length curves in `Figure 5` and `Supplementary Table S5`.

#### 2. Collapse Weakly Supported Gene-Tree Nodes and Use a Resolved Mammalian Species Tree for Topological Comparison
* **WHY:** Comparing an uncollapsed binary ML gene tree of a short, conserved gene against the multifurcating NCBI Taxonomy tree inflates the Robinson-Foulds distance (`0.535`) due to stochastic rearrangements across short branches with low bootstrap support.
* **WHAT:** Collapse gene-tree branches with bootstrap support $<70\%$ (or conduct an Approximately Unbiased [AU] topology test in IQ-TREE 2 comparing the unconstrained ML gene tree against a resolved mammalian species tree from TimeTree).
* **HOW:** Run `iqtree2 -s alignment.fas -z species_vs_gene.trees -zb 10000 -au` to test whether the *Rem2* alignment statistically rejects the canonical mammalian species phylogeny, and report the generalized/polytomy-aware RF distance after collapsing nodes with $<70\%$ bootstrap support.

#### 3. Report `GARD` Recombination Results and Clarify Sequence Counts (186 $\to$ 175)
* **WHY:** The Methods section includes a full subsection on `GARD` ("Recombination detection") stating that ignoring recombination biases selection inference, yet `GARD` is never mentioned in Results. Additionally, Methods states that 186 sequences were downloaded and 9 low-quality sequences were removed ($186 - 9 = 177$), whereas Results and `Table 2` analyze 175 sequences.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  1. Add the `GARD` output summary to the Results section (stating the number of significant breakpoints detected by $\Delta\text{cAIC}$, or confirming that 0 breakpoints were found).
  2. Reconcile the sequence filtering arithmetic in "Data retrieval and quality control" ($186$ retrieved minus $11$ removed $= 175$ analyzed, or specify why 2 additional sequences were dropped).

#### 4. Fix Genomic Coordinates in `Table 3`, Domain Boundaries, and Editorial Typos
* **WHY:** Numerical and coordinate errors between the text, tables, and human genome assembly undermine technical reliability.
* **WHAT & HOW:**
  1. **Fix `chr14` coordinate in `Table 3` (line 234):** Codon 98 (`Ser98Phe`) is listed at `chr14: 22,884,863` (hg38), which is ~469 kb away from the *REM2* gene (`chr14: 23,351,000–23,356,000`). Replace `22,884,863` with the correct hg38 *REM2* exon coordinate (around `chr14: 23,353,xxx` or `23,352,xxx`).
  2. **Correct RGK domain boundary description (line 217):** Revise the statement that human Rem2 sites `48, 52, 64, 98, 319` "all... fall within the RGK region" to accurately state that sites 48, 52, and 64 reside in the N-terminal regulatory region, site 98 is at the N-terminal boundary of the RGK GTPase core, and site 319 lies in the C-terminal tail/calmodulin-binding region.
  3. **Fix typos in main text:**
     - Line 156: "The $\omega_3$ rate ... was $-50$" $\rightarrow$ change minus sign to tilde ($\sim 50$, matching `50.7482` in `Table 2`).
     - Line 33: "spanning about $-200$ million years" $\rightarrow$ "$\sim 200$ million years".
     - Line 209: "Additionally, t coevolving sites" $\rightarrow$ "Additionally, coevolving sites".
     - Line 43: "he author(s) declare" $\rightarrow$ "The author(s) declare".
     - Lines 129, 348, 375–379: Reconcile the inconsistent GitHub repository names (`SPA-REM2` vs. `AMES-REM2`).
