# Review of PMC7267315

**Manuscript Title:** SARS‐CoV‐2 viral spike G614 mutation exhibits higher case fatality rate  
**Authors:** Manuel Becerra‐Flores, Timothy Cardozo  
**Journal / Preprint:** International Journal of Clinical Practice (2020) | DOI: 10.1111/ijcp.13525  
**Overall Score:** **2.1 / 10.0** (Fundamentally Invalid)  
* **Score Breakdown:** Rigor: 0.4/3.5 | Claims: 0.4/2.5 | Biology: 0.6/2.0 | Reproducibility: 0.7/2.0

---

### 1. SUMMARY AND OVERALL IMPACT STATEMENT

Becerra-Flores and Cardozo investigate whether the SARS-CoV-2 Spike D614G substitution increases clinical lethality by performing an ordinary least squares linear regression of country-level case fatality rates (CFRs)—computed over an 8-day window (March 30 to April 6, 2020) assuming a fixed 11-day lag between case confirmation and death—against the country-level proportion of G614 sequences in GISAID as of April 6, 2020, alongside a descriptive table of 8 US states. They complement this epidemiological correlation with an *in silico* single-protomer free-energy perturbation ($\Delta\Delta G$) calculation in ICM-Pro using cryo-EM Spike structures in the closed/"down" (PDB 6VXX) and open/"up" (PDB 6VYB, 6VSB) states. Reporting a positive country-level correlation between G614 prevalence and CFR ($p < 0.02$ after excluding the United Kingdom) and a positive $\Delta\Delta G$ exclusively in the "up" conformation, the authors conclude that G614 destabilizes the receptor-binding conformation, infects cells less readily, and causes higher human mortality via an immunologic/antibody-dependent enhancement mechanism.

The manuscript's core epidemiological and biophysical conclusions are fundamentally invalid due to classic ecological fallacy, severe unmodeled spatio-temporal and testing-denominator confounding, post-hoc outlier exclusion, and flawed structural modeling. Comparing crude country-level CFRs during a single 8-day snapshot in early April 2020 across countries at vastly different epidemic stages and testing regimes merely rediscovers the geographic founder divide between East Asia (where early D614 outbreaks were met with broad community PCR testing and lower crude CFRs) and Western Europe (where G614 founder waves overwhelmed hospitals and testing was restricted to severe inpatient cases, inflating crude CFRs). Moreover, achieving statistical significance requires the post-hoc removal of the United Kingdom (`Figure S1`), Table 1 contains basic arithmetic errors in calculating G614 percentages, and the structural modeling mutates only one of the three Spike monomers—breaking homo-trimer symmetry and arriving at the biophysically inverted conclusion that G614 impairs receptor accessibility and reduces infectivity. Addressing these fatal flaws cannot be accomplished by text edits; testing whether G614 alters lethality requires patient-level clinical outcome data linked to viral genotypes within the same healthcare system and time period, adjusted for age, sex, comorbidities, and hospital load.

### 2. SPECIFIC CONCLUSIONS AND ASSESSMENT OF SUPPORT

* **The Spike G614 mutation causes a higher case fatality rate and represents a more pathogenic strain of SARS-CoV-2: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Inferring individual-level viral virulence from an unadjusted country-level linear regression across a handful of nations during an 8-day window (March 30–April 6, 2020) commits the ecological fallacy. Because the G614 lineage founded the European epidemic while D614 predominated in China and South Korea, country-level G614 percentage is collinear with healthcare system surge, population age structure, and—most critically—PCR testing denominator policies (broad asymptomatic/mild testing in South Korea vs. restricted hospital-only testing in European countries). The authors' dismissal of testing bias on the grounds that *"the same reporting or incidence bias would have to occur in the same direction and for the same reason across diverse countries"* ignores the fact that Western European countries shared both the G614 founder event and restrictive hospital-triaged testing policies in late March 2020.

* **Country-level CFR correlates robustly with G614 prevalence ($p < 0.02$): [Fragile].**  
  As acknowledged in the Methods and Discussion (lines 59 and 110; Figure S1), the country-level correlation is substantially weakened when the United Kingdom is included. Excluding the UK on the post-hoc rationale that it had "an unusually low level of testing/diagnosis and an unusually high level of death reporting" is arbitrary and asymmetric, since other high-CFR European countries retained in the regression (e.g., Italy, Spain, France, Belgium) experienced similar inpatient-biased testing during March 2020. Furthermore, several countries in the regression have tiny GISAID sequence denominators as of April 6, 2020, introducing large binomial sampling variance on the x-axis that is ignored by ordinary least squares.

* **US state-level data corroborate higher fatality from G614 in eastern versus western states (Table 1): [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  Table 1 lists 8 selected US states without calculating any CFR at all—claiming that daily confirmed case counts were unavailable for US states in April 2020—and instead compares raw cumulative death counts (which scale with total population size and epidemic onset date, e.g., New York vs. Utah) against small, unrepresentative GISAID sequence counts (e.g., Oregon $n = 4$ sequences, Utah $n = 22$, Wisconsin $n = 29$). In addition, Table 1 contains a clear arithmetic error: for Washington state, `Total seq = 379`, `D mutants = 324`, and `G mutants = 55`, which equals $55 / 379 = 14.51\%$, not `16.3%` as printed in Table 1.

* **G614 destabilizes the "up" (RBD-exposed) Spike conformation, reducing infectivity and increasing mortality through an immunologic shielding / antibody-enhancement mechanism: [Methodologically Flawed].**  
  In the structural analysis (Section 2, line 61), the authors mutated only a single monomer (chain A) to G614 inside the trimeric Spike structure and minimized sidechains within a $10.0\text{ \AA}$ radius over $10^6$ Monte Carlo steps. SARS-CoV-2 Spike is a homo-trimer in which D614 on each protomer forms an inter-protomer contact (with T859/K854 on the neighboring protomer's S2 stalk); mutating only 1 of 3 chains creates an artificial chimeric asymmetric trimer, and local $10.0\text{ \AA}$ sidechain minimization with a fixed backbone cannot sample the global domain hinge between the "down" and "up" RBD states. Worse, because loss of the D614–T859 hydrogen bond actually destabilizes the closed ("down") latch relative to the open ("up") state, the authors' local static calculation reverses the true conformational thermodynamics and leads to the unsupported speculation that G614 viruses *"infect less readily"* and kill patients via antibody-dependent enhancement.

### 3. CONSTRUCTIVE AND DOABLE SUGGESTIONS FOR IMPROVEMENT

#### 1. Replace Unadjusted Country-Level Ecological Regression with Patient-Level or Multivariable Adjusted Models
* **WHY:** Unadjusted country-level regression conflates viral genotype with national testing rates (tests per 1,000 population), median population age, epidemic wave timing, and hospital ICU saturation.
* **WHAT:** Control for national testing denominators, demographic structure, and geographic region, or restrict inference to within-country/within-hospital patient-level cohorts where D614 and G614 co-circulated.
* **HOW:** At minimum, compile each country's cumulative tests perconfirmed case, percentage of population aged $\ge 65$, and days since the 100th confirmed case as of March 30, 2020; fit a multivariable binomial/beta-binomial regression of CFR on `%G614` retaining all countries (including the UK) and weighting each country by its GISAID sequence sample size $N_{\text{seq}}$; and report whether `%G614` retains any statistical significance once testing rate per case and region (East Asia vs. Europe) are included as covariates.

#### 2. Correct Table 1 Arithmetic and Compute Actual Age-Adjusted CFRs for US States
* **WHY:** Table 1 compares raw cumulative death counts (e.g., 6,268 in New York vs. 13 in Utah) without dividing by confirmed cases, and miscalculates the G614 percentage for Washington state ($55/379 = 14.5\%$, reported as $16.3\%$).
* **WHAT & HOW:** Correct the Washington `%G` entry in Table 1 from `16.3` to `14.5` (and verify Connecticut $40/84 = 47.6\%$ and Wisconsin $20/29 = 69.0\%$); retrieve publicly available state-level confirmed case counts from March 29, 2020 (11 days prior to April 9, 2020) to compute state-level lagged CFRs; exclude or flag states with $<25$ sequences (Oregon $n=4$, Utah $n=22$); and test whether state-level lagged CFR correlates with `%G614` across all available US states.

#### 3. Perform Symmetric Homo-Trimeric Structural Modeling with Backbone Flexibility
* **WHY:** Mutating only chain A in a homo-trimer while freezing the backbone beyond $10\text{ \AA}$ introduces artificial steric/electrostatic asymmetry and cannot evaluate how D614G alters the inter-protomer S1–S2 interface across all three subunits.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Introduce the D614G substitution simultaneously across all three chains (A, B, and C) in PDB 6VXX (closed), 6VYB (one RBD up), and 6VSB; allow full backbone and sidechain relaxation across the S1/S2 interface (residues 590–700 and 820–870 on all three chains); and decompose $\Delta\Delta G$ per interface (chain A–B, B–C, C–A) to properly compare the relative stability of the closed versus open states before speculating on receptor accessibility or immunologic enhancement.

#### 4. Retract Clinical Prognostic Recommendations and Align Claims with Observational Scope
* **WHY:** The Abstract and Discussion advise that *"Physicians should be aware of this characteristic of the virus to anticipate the clinical course of infection"* and that *"The presence of G614 in a virus detected in a patient may also have prognostic significance."* Making clinical prognostic claims from an unadjusted 8-day ecological correlation is scientifically unwarranted.
* **WHAT & HOW:** Remove all claims of clinical prognostic utility, vaccine redesign, and virus-autonomous lethality from the Title, Abstract, and Discussion, and reframe the manuscript strictly as an exploratory hypothesis-generating observation highlighting the need for patient-level genotype-phenotype cohorts.
