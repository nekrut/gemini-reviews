### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This virology study reports the complete `15,413-nt` consensus genome sequence (GenBank `AY545985`) and construction of a stable full-length infectious cDNA clone (`pFL12`) of a highly virulent North American Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome Virus (`PRRSV`) isolate (`NVSL #97-7895`, associated with severe "atypical" sow abortion storms). By assembling five overlapping RT-PCR amplicons with 12- to 24-fold bidirectional sequencing redundancy and RLM-RACE 5'-end mapping in the low-copy vector `pBR322`, the authors rescue infectious recombinant virus (`vFL12`) directly in permissive `MARC-145` cells alongside a polymerase-defective negative control (`pFL12Pol-`, `SDD -> AAA`) and an engineered `BsrGI` restriction marker (`nt 1170`). Using primer extension analysis, they demonstrate that the PRRSV replicase removes two nonviral 5'-terminal guanosine (`GG`) residues added downstream of the T7 promoter during viral replication. Finally, in a controlled BSL-2 swine challenge (`n = 4` weaned pigs per group: sham, parental `NVSL #97-7895`, and `vFL12`, plus `n = 2` contact sentinels per infected group), they demonstrate that `vFL12` induces classic hyperthermia, persistent viremia, robust seroconversion, and rapid horizontal contact transmission while stably retaining the `BsrGI` genetic marker in vivo.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
- **Rigorous Molecular Controls in Clone Construction & Rescue:**
  - Every nucleotide of the `15,413-nt` genome was determined from `6–12` independent clones sequenced bidirectionally (`12–24x` coverage), overlap junctions were re-amplified and sequenced to purge primer-templated mutations, the authentic 5' cap/terminus was mapped via tobacco acid pyrophosphatase `RLM-RACE`, and three independent full-length `pFL` clones were sequenced in their entirety.
  - Inclusion of the active-site polymerase mutant `pFL12Pol-` (`SDD -> AAA`) rules out carryover DNA/RNA artifact in immunofluorescence and passage assays (`Figure 2`), and `MfeI` restriction profiling across 6 serial bacterial passages confirms plasmid stability in *E. coli* `DH5\alpha` (`Figure 1C`).
  - Direct `^{32}\text{P}` primer extension against a `pFL12` sequencing ladder (`Figure 4`) cleanly proves 5'-terminal trimming/correction of the two nonviral T7 `G` residues by the viral RdRp.
- **Minor Dose & Passage Asymmetry and Sample Size in the Swine Challenge (`Figure 5`):**
  - In the swine experiment (`n = 4` pigs/group), the parental virus (`P5` on MARC-145) was administered intranasally at `10^{5.2} TCID50`, whereas `vFL12` (`P2` on MARC-145) was administered at `10^{4.8} TCID50` (`Figure 5` legend). Despite the slightly lower inoculum titer, `vFL12` caused a more pronounced febrile peak (`P < 0.05` vs sham on days `3–6`), one mortality from secondary bacterial complications at `23 dpi`, higher IDEXX ELISA `S/P` antibody ratios (`P < 0.05`), and faster sentinel transmission (`3–8 days` vs `3 weeks` for parental virus). While the authors reasonably attribute this to `vFL12` representing an unattenuated consensus sequence relative to the `P5` parental quasispecies swarm, introducing only `n = 2` sentinel pigs per group at `19 dpi`—when viremia had already decayed near the `10^{1.5} TCID50/ml` detection limit (`Figure 5B`)—means that the observed difference in contact transmission latency (`3 days` vs `3 weeks`) is subject to stochastic shedding variation in a very small sample (`n = 2` sentinels).
- **Missing Statistical Methods Subsection:** In the Results (`lines 106` and `111`), the authors report `P < 0.05` for rectal hyperthermia (`days 3–6`) and ELISA serology curves (`Figure 5A, C`), but the Materials and Methods section does not state which statistical test (e.g., two-way repeated-measures ANOVA vs time-point *t*-tests) was used.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Capped in vitro transcripts from `pFL12` rescue infectious PRRSV (`vFL12`) in `MARC-145` cells with growth kinetics in `MARC-145` and porcine alveolar macrophages (`PAMs`) matching the parental isolate** — `[Well Supported]`. Validated by immunofluorescence, plaque assays reaching `4 \times 10^7 PFU/ml`, multi-step growth curves in both cell types (`Figure 3B`), and `BsrGI` marker digestion (`Figure 3A`).
- **Claim 2: The PRRSV RNA-dependent RNA polymerase corrects nonviral 5'-terminal guanosine residues during replication** — `[Well Supported]`. Directly demonstrated by high-resolution denaturing PAGE primer extension comparing input capped T7 transcripts against intracellular `vFL12` and parental viral RNA (`Figure 4`).
- **Claim 3: Cloned `vFL12` retains full in vivo virulence, immunogenicity, genetic stability, and horizontal contact transmissibility in swine** — `[Well Supported]`. Demonstrated in seronegative weaned pigs via febrile response, viremia through `22 dpi`, ELISA seroconversion, contact transmission to naïve sentinels, and `BsrGI` restriction verification of virus recovered from both inoculated and sentinel pig sera (`Figure 5`).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Sequence & Reagent Transparency:** The complete `15,413-nt` consensus genome sequence of PRRSV `NVSL #97-7895` is deposited in GenBank under accession `AY545985`, and all cloning, T7 promoter, poly(A)-tailing, and genetic-tag RT-PCR primers are provided with exact genomic coordinates in `Table 1`.
