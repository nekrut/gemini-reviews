### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This study describes four new fungal species in the genus *Phyllosticta* (*P. decaspermi* sp. nov., *P. morellae* sp. nov., *P. clematidea* sp. nov., and *P. pittosporicola* sp. nov.) isolated from plant leaves in Hainan and Yunnan provinces, China, using a five-locus Sanger phylogeny (ITS, LSU, *tef1*, *act*, *gpdh* across 159 isolates / 103 species; 2,911 characters) combined with morphological characterization. In addition, the authors sequence the whole genomes of 10 *Phyllosticta* strains (deposited under NCBI BioProject `PRJNA1302140`), construct a phylogenomic tree from orthologous protein clusters alongside 7 public *Phyllosticta* genomes, and compare eggNOG-mapper KEGG pathway annotations across the 10 newly sequenced genomes. While the five-locus taxonomic placement and culture/herbarium depositions (`HSAUP`, `CGMCC`, `SAUCC`, MycoBank) follow standard mycological conventions, the manuscript contains severe internal inconsistencies, copy-paste errors in the formal taxonomic diagnosis of *P. pittosporicola*, a mismatch between the four newly described species and the genomes actually sequenced, and an overinterpretation of static genomic gene counts as "metabolic pathway activity."

### 2. STRENGTHS
- **Standardized Five-Locus Taxonomic Framework & Living Culture Deposition:** Uses the established five-locus *Phyllosticta* backbone (ITS, LSU, *tef1*, *act*, *gpdh*) with both Maximum Likelihood (`RAxML`) and Bayesian Inference (`MrBayes`), supported by two independent isolates per novel species, 30 micromorphological measurements per structure, MycoBank registration UUIDs, and deposition of holotypes (`HSAUP`) and ex-type living cultures (`CGMCC`).
- **Public Whole-Genome Data Deposition:** Raw sequencing reads and assemblies for 10 *Phyllosticta* isolates are deposited in NCBI under BioProject `PRJNA1302140` with individual `SAMN` BioSample and `SRR` SRA accessions listed in Table 1.

### 3. WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT (Methodological & Statistical Audit)
- **Copy-Paste & Arithmetic Errors in the Taxonomic Notes of *Phyllosticta pittosporicola* (Line 287):** The diagnostic notes for *P. pittosporicola* state: *"However, P. pittosporicola differs from P. elliptica by 55 nucleotides (17/571 in ITS, 0/0 in LSU, 18/406 in tef1, 18/220 in act, and 7/615 in gpdh). In morphology, they are distinguished by different hosts (Clematis vitalba vs. Hedera sp. vs. Kerria japonica) and shorter conidia in Phyllosticta clematidea than P. concentrica and P. kerriae..."* First, $17 + 0 + 18 + 18 + 7 = 60$ nucleotides, not 55 (and the values `55 nucleotides`, `18/220 in act`, and `7/615 in gpdh` were duplicated from the *P. clematidea* description in Line 247). Second, the entire morphological comparison sentence—including the wrong species name (*Phyllosticta clematidea*), wrong comparator species (*P. concentrica* and *P. kerriae* instead of *P. elliptica*), and wrong hosts (*Clematis vitalba* vs. *Hedera* vs. *Kerria japonica* instead of *Pittosporum illicioides*)—was copy-pasted verbatim from the *P. clematidea* section above it.
- **Discrepancy Between the Four New Species and the Sequenced Genomes (Table 1 vs. Introduction):** The Introduction states that the study aims at *"describing four new species using molecular phylogenetics and morphology, and conducting genome/transcriptome sequencing of these new taxa."* However, Table 1, Figure 2, and Figure 3 include only three of the four new species (*P. clematidea*, *P. decaspermi*, and *P. morellae*), omitting *P. pittosporicola* (`CGMCC3.28666`) entirely while sequencing seven other previously known species (*P. capitalensis*, *P. elliptica*, *P. fujianensis*, *P. rhododendri*, *P. saprophytica*, *P. turpiniae*, *P. wuzhishanensis*) without explanation. Additionally, Line 85 states *"The present study identified three novel species"* and immediately lists four.
- **Contradictory Ortholog Counts & Inappropriate Substitution Model in Phylogenomics (Lines 7, 70, 93):** The Methods (Line 70) state that `OrthoFinder v2.5.5` identified `6,364` clusters of orthologous proteins (COPs) that were concatenated into a supergene, whereas the Abstract (Line 7) and Results (Line 93, Figure 2) state that the tree was built from `5,399` clusters of orthologous proteins. Furthermore, Line 70 reports running `RAxML-NG` under the nucleotide model `GTR+G+I` on what is described as clusters of orthologous proteins without clarifying whether `ParaAT` back-translated proteins to coding DNA or whether an amino acid model was actually used.
- **Conflating Static Genomic Gene Counts with Physiological "Pathway Activity" Without Statistical Testing (Lines 107–115, Figure 3):** The KEGG heatmap (Figure 3) is derived solely from `eggNOG-mapper` annotation of predicted genomic ORFs (`TransDecoder` longest protein per gene; Lines 75–77), yet the Results and Discussion repeatedly interpret color differences as "vigorous protein folding," "lower pathway activity," and "active biosynthesis" in *P. fujianensis*, *P. saprophytica*, and *P. rhododendri* (none of which are the four newly described species). No scale bar units (e.g., Z-score of gene counts vs. raw gene counts), normalization procedures, or statistical enrichment tests (Fisher's exact test / hypergeometric FDR) are reported.

### 4. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **[Well Supported] *P. decaspermi*, *P. morellae*, *P. clematidea*, and *P. pittosporicola* represent distinct phylogenetic lineages within the *P. capitalensis* and *P. concentrica* species complexes:** Supported by five-locus ML/BI phylogenetics (`1.00 PP / 100% BS`) and two independent isolates per species, though the diagnostic text for *P. pittosporicola* requires correction.
- **[Overstated] KEGG pathway heatmap demonstrates lifestyle-specific metabolic "activity" differences (e.g., enhanced ER protein processing in saprophytic species):** Inferred from unnormalized genomic gene-family counts in single isolates ($N = 1$ per species) without transcriptomic expression quantification or statistical enrichment testing.

### 5. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
SRA/BioSample accessions (`PRJNA1302140`), herbarium/culture collection numbers, and five-locus alignment statistics are documented, supporting data availability (`1.6 / 2.0`). However, the manuscript lacks genome assembly quality metrics (N50, genome size, BUSCO completeness, coverage depth, or gene counts per genome) and contains multiple numerical and copy-paste inconsistencies between sections.
