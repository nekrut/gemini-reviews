### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This article is a comprehensive minireview synthesizing early-1990s molecular oncology research on the *TP53* (*p53*) tumor suppressor gene on chromosome 17p13.1 (11 exons, 393 amino acids, five evolutionarily conserved domains I–V across human, monkey, mouse, rat, chicken, *Xenopus*, and rainbow trout). It reviews the historical correction from a putative dominant oncogene to a tumor suppressor (G1–S DNA-damage checkpoint "molecular policeman" and apoptosis inducer), somatic missense mutation spectra across exons 5–8 (hotspots at codons 175, 248, 273, 282; CpG transitions; codon 249 $\text{G}\to\text{T}$ transversions in aflatoxin/HBV-associated HCC; UV-induced dipyrimidine $\text{C}\to\text{T}$ and $\text{CC}\to\text{TT}$ mutations in skin cancer; tobacco-associated $\text{G}\to\text{T}$ transversions in lung/aerodigestive cancers), germline mutations in Li-Fraumeni syndrome, *Trp53*-null and mutant-transgenic mouse models (Lavigueur et al. and Donehower et al., where 26/35 [74%] of $p53^{-/-}$ mice develop tumors by 6 months), viral oncoprotein sequestration/degradation (SV40 large T, Adenovirus E1B-55kD, high-risk HPV-16/18 E6), and cellular *MDM2* amplification in soft-tissue sarcomas.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Balanced, critical synthesis of technical caveats in the primary literature:** The authors explicitly highlight two major methodological pitfalls in *p53* cancer studies: (1) mutation frequencies in cultured cancer cell lines are substantially higher than in primary uncultured solid tumors (citing Mazars et al. finding only ~20% mutations in >90 primary breast carcinomas vs much higher cell-line estimates), and (2) positive p53 immunohistochemistry (IHC) cannot be equated with *TP53* gene mutation because wild-type p53 protein is also stabilized by DNA damage (UV, $\gamma$-irradiation), viral proteins (SV40 T, Ad E1B), or cellular factor alterations (Wynford-Thomas; Barnes et al.).
- **Clear integrative schematics:** Figures 1–3 accurately map the 11-exon structure, cross-species sequence alignments of conserved domains II–V with hotspots 175/248/273/282, binding domains on SV40 Large T, Ad5 E1A/E1B, and HPV-16/18 E6/E7, and the multi-pathway inactivation model.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Narrative review without systematic meta-analytic tabulation:** As a narrative minireview, mutation rates across tumor types are cited qualitatively in prose (e.g., "~50% of adult cancers", "up to 70% in lung cancers", "20% in breast carcinomas") without a quantitative summary table detailing sample sizes, assay modalities (IHC vs SSCP vs sequencing), or confidence intervals across studies.
