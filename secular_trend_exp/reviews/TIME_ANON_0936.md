### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This comprehensive evolutionary genomics and molecular ecology study investigates bacterial speciation, incomplete lineage sorting (ILS) versus reticulation, average nucleotide identity (ANI) discontinuity, and barriers to homologous recombination in the nitrogen-fixing cyanobacterial genus *Nostoc*. First, the authors generate 124 high-quality metagenome-assembled genomes (`MAGs`, median `175x` coverage, `98%` BUSCO completeness, curated via assembly-graph refinement in `GraphBin2`/`Bandage`/`Anvi'o`) from global cyanolichens and combine them with 27 public genomes (`N = 151` total) to infer a 1,519-locus coalescent species tree (`weighted-ASTRAL`), a concatenated ML tree (`IQ-Tree`), Bayesian divergence time estimates (`MCMCTree`), quartet concordance tests (`MSCquartets`), and a phylogenetic split network (`NANUQ`). They demonstrate that *Nostoc* evolution was shaped by eight interspecific rapid radiations (`anomaly zone` clusters 1–8) synchronous with land plant diversification, and that `73.8%` of quartets fit a tree-like ILS model versus `26.2%` fitting non-tree-like reticulations. Second, comparing `FastANI` pairwise genomic identities with `PopCOGenT` recent gene-flow clusters across 16 delimited *Nostoc* sections (`2.1–2.4` and `3.1–3.12`), they show that the distribution of ANIs is a heterogeneous mosaic—forming sharp gaps only across long subtending branches while forming a continuous `88–95%` ANI gradient inside rapid radiations (such as section `3.1`). Third, by sequencing PacBio circular consensus amplicons (`rbcLX` for *Nostoc* and `nrITS-partial LSU` for fungal mycobionts) across 2,316 cyanolichen specimens systematically sampled from 366 1-ha grid sites in Alberta, Canada, they demonstrate that cohesive *Nostoc* phylogroups (`PopCOGenT` gene-flow units) maintain strict genetic boundaries even when co-occurring at the same local site and sharing the exact same *Peltigera* fungal partner species.

### STRENGTHS
- **Exemplary Metagenomic Curation and Adversarial Contamination/Heterogeneity Controls:** Because cyanolichen thalli could potentially harbor secondary minor *Nostoc* strains that might distort single-copy ortholog alignments, the authors perform an explicit SNV audit across all 124 MAGs in `Anvi'o` (showing median SNV density `< 0.02%` in BUSCO genes and non-consensus allele fraction `< 0.1`), and then **re-run the entire 1,519-locus phylogenomic, conflict, and anomaly-zone pipeline after removing every gene with an SNV ($\ge 0.1$ minor allele frequency) and removing 6 outlier MAGs** (`Figure S9`), proving that the topology, anomaly zones, and internode-conflict regressions are completely invariant to trace strain heterogeneity.
- **Rigorous Integration of Multispecies Coalescent Theory (`Anomaly Zone` and `NANUQ`) into Bacterial Phylogenomics:** Rather than attributing all bacterial gene-tree discordance to horizontal gene transfer, the authors test mechanistic predictions of the multispecies coalescent: demonstrating log-linear increases in both weak and strong gene-tree conflict and site discordance (`sCF`) as internode duration (`Myr`) decreases (`Fig. 1b–c`), identifying 9 internode clusters satisfying Degnan & Rosenberg's theoretical anomaly-zone inequality $y < a(x)$, showing that all topological disagreements between `weighted-ASTRAL` and concatenated ML reside inside these anomaly zones (`Fig. S2`), and quantifying tree-like (`73.8%`) vs. reticulate (`26.2%`) quartets across multiple $\alpha$ thresholds (`1e-2` to `1e-6`, `Table S1`).
- **Multi-Scale Ecological Validation Across 2,316 Systematically Sampled Symbiotic Pairs:** Combining global genome-scale `PopCOGenT` gene-flow clustering with a systematic 366-site regional grid (`2,316` dual-barcoded cyanolichen specimens) directly tests and refutes the hypotheses that *Nostoc* phylogroups are mere geographic sampling artifacts along a continuum or are isolated solely by host-fungus specialization.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Non-Independence of Pairwise Specimen Comparisons in Table 1 (`Fungal Partner Sharing`):**
  - In `Table 1` (and `Supplementary Data S1f–h`), the authors compare the percentage of co-occurring specimen pairs that share a fungal partner (e.g., `24.29%` for `V and XLII`, `29.58%` for `3.6a and V`, `58.06%` for `3.6a and 3.6a`) against non-co-occurring specimen pairs (`8.68%`, `24.56%`, `33.96%`). Because all $\binom{N}{2}$ or $N_1 \times N_2$ specimen pairs (`18,078`, `17,292`, `34,191` pairs) are generated from a much smaller number of underlying specimens ($n = 426$ specimens in section `3.6` across 152 sites), pairwise observations share specimens and sites and are not statistically independent. Providing a site-permutation test (or Mantel/generalized linear mixed model controlling for specimen and site random effects) would formally quantify the $p$-value of the co-occurrence enrichment.
- **Ordinary Least Squares Regression Across Phylogenetic Internodes (`Fig. 1b–c`):**
  - Fitting `lm(percent_conflict ~ log(branch_length))` across the 148 internal branches of the `weighted-ASTRAL` tree treats nested/adjacent internal branches as independent data points; while gene-tree quartet support on adjacent short internodes has minor covariance, the negative relationship is so strong that non-parametric rank correlation or generalized estimating equations would yield identical conclusions.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`Nostoc` evolutionary history features eight interspecific rapid radiations (`anomaly zone` clusters) contemporaneous with major land plant diversification events, and ILS outweighs reticulation across its core genome.* `[Well Supported]` — Supported by 1,519 gene trees, `weighted-ASTRAL` vs. concatenation comparison, `Degnan & Rosenberg` anomaly-zone calculations, `MSCquartets` (`73.8%` tree-like), and `MCMCTree` dating.
- **Claim 2:** *Pairwise ANI distributions in `Nostoc` do not exhibit a universal 83–95% ANI gap, but instead form a mosaic shaped by branch lengths and diversification rates.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by the phylogenetically ordered `FastANI` matrix (`Fig. 3` and `Fig. S4`).
- **Claim 3:** *Symbiotic `Nostoc` phylogroups (`PopCOGenT` gene-flow clusters) remain genetically cohesive and isolated even when co-occurring at the same local site and sharing the same `Peltigera` mycobiont species.* `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated across 2,316 genotyped cyanolichen specimens from 366 sites in Alberta (`Fig. 4`, `Table 1`, `Fig. S6`).
