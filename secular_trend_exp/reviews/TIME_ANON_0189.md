### 1. Executive Summary
This structural and biophysical study determines the three-dimensional cryo-electron microscopy (`cryo-EM`) reconstruction of both full and empty capsids of the *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* double-stranded RNA (`dsRNA`) virus `L-A` at $16\text{ \AA}$ resolution, combined with computational capsid-shell subtraction to measure internal RNA packing diffraction, dark-field scanning transmission electron microscopy (`STEM`) mass-per-unit-length measurements of purified and capsid-extruded dsRNA chromosomes, and `RNase III` protection assays. The $16\text{ \AA}$ density map resolves the 120-subunit ($T = 1$ icosahedron of 60 asymmetric `Gag` homodimers, $430\text{ \AA}$ outer diameter, $46\text{ \AA}$ average shell thickness) into three outer boomerang-shaped domains (`A1–A3`, `B1–B3`) and inner Y-shaped motifs, perforated by five classes of $10\text{–}15\text{ \AA}$ pores (`107–244` $\text{\AA}^2$) that act as a molecular sieve—retaining the $23\text{ \AA}$-wide A-form dsRNA duplex and excluding nucleases while permitting nucleotide influx and nascent $(+)$ssRNA transcript exit. Crucially, both powder diffraction of shell-subtracted full virions (across a series of defocus values) and geometric volume calculations demonstrate that the 4.6-kbp `L-A` dsRNA genome is completely internalized (`RNase III`-resistant) yet packed loosely with a $40\text{–}45\text{ \AA}$ center-to-center interduplex spacing (compared to $25\text{–}30\text{ \AA}$ in dsDNA phages and reovirus cores), which the authors relate to reducing frictional drag during sequential template "churning" past the capsid-anchored `Gag-Pol` polymerase. This is a remarkably rigorous, internally calibrated biophysical study.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Internal Magnification and Mass Standards:** To eliminate systematic scaling errors in interstrand spacing and linear mass density, the authors co-mixed bacteriophage `T4` with `L-A` virions in cryo-EM preparations to use the $40.5\text{ \AA}$ axial spacing of the `T4` tail sheath as an internal magnification standard, and co-mixed tobacco mosaic virus (`TMV`) in all Brookhaven dark-field `STEM` grids as an internal mass-per-unit-length standard.
- **Direct Matched Comparison of Full and Empty Capsids Across Defocus Regimes:** Full and empty capsids were reconstructed independently from the *exact same micrographs* (ratio ~3:2, combining $>80$ particles across three well-stigmated micrographs with first CTF zeros at $\sim(15\text{ \AA})^{-1}$), ensuring identical ice thickness, electron dose ($\sim 8\ e^-/\text{\AA}^2$), and CTF characteristics. Resolution was objectively verified at $16\text{ \AA}$ via Fourier shell correlation (`FSC`) and eigenvalue spectra.
- **Controlled Computational Shell Erasure and Multi-Defocus Powder Diffraction (Figures 4–5):** Because phase-contrast CTF modulates spatial frequencies as a function of defocus ($\Delta = 1/\lambda k^2$), the authors performed computational capsid-shell reprojection subtraction across micrographs recorded at multiple defocus values (`15–27` $\text{\AA}$ first zero) and ran the exact same shell-subtraction and azimuthal powder-averaging pipeline on *empty* capsids as a negative control for CTF-amplified solvent noise (Fig. 5, dashed curve). Furthermore, they cross-checked the diffraction-derived center-to-center spacing ($41\text{–}46\text{ \AA}$ from the $36\text{–}40\text{ \AA}$ Bragg peak) against an independent first-principles hexagonal close-packing calculation from internal cavity volume ($\text{diameter} = 348\text{ \AA}$) and A-form genome contour length ($4,579\text{ bp} \times 2.81\text{ \AA/bp} = 1.287\ \mu\text{m}$), obtaining $\sim 40\text{ \AA}$ in close agreement.
- **Biochemical Verification of Complete Genome Encapsidation (`RNase III` Protection, Figure 6):** To rule out the alternative hypothesis that the loose $40\text{–}45\text{ \AA}$ packing spacing was an artifact of partial RNA extrusion from damaged virions, the authors demonstrated that freshly purified full virions are 100% resistant to $\text{Mg}^{2+}$-activated dsRNA-specific `RNase III` (whereas naked `L-A` dsRNA and low-ionic-strength-destabilized capsids in $25\text{ mM NaCl}$ are digested).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *The $16\text{ \AA}$ `L-A` capsid comprises 60 asymmetric `Gag` dimers (nonequivalent `A` and `B` subunits oriented in parallel within each penton decamer) perforated by five classes of $10\text{–}15\text{ \AA}$ pores that function as a molecular sieve.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly established by the $16\text{ \AA}$ cryo-EM density map, radial density sections (Figs. 2–3), and `RNase III` exclusion (Fig. 6).
- **Claim 2:** *Purified `L-A` dsRNA has a mass-per-unit-length of $2.8 \pm 0.6\text{ kD/nm}$ ($\pm 0.08\text{ SEM}$), consistent with an A-form RNA duplex ($2.43\text{ kD/nm}$) rather than B-form ($2.00\text{ kD/nm}$), while filaments freshly extruding from disrupted capsids frequently form laterally associated multi-duplex bundles.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by calibrated dark-field `STEM` histograms of purified vs capsid-associated filaments (Fig. 7).
- **Claim 3:** *Encapsidated `L-A` dsRNA is packed at a center-to-center spacing of $40\text{–}45\text{ \AA}$, and this low packing density facilitates long-range template translocation ("churning") past the immobilized inner-wall `Gag-Pol` replicase/transcriptase.* `[Well Supported]` — The $40\text{–}45\text{ \AA}$ spacing is rigorously proven by both diffraction and geometric volume calculations, and the functional "churning" model is clearly framed as a biophysical hypothesis with an explicit experimental test proposed (testing genome insertion length limits).
