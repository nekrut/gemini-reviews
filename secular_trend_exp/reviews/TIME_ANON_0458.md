### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This forward genetics and virology study screens 6,000 EMS-mutagenized haploid genomes in a *Caenorhabditis elegans* strain (`WUM29`: `virIs1; jyIs8; rde-1(ne219)`) carrying chromosomally integrated heat-shock-inducible Orsay virus RNA1 and RNA2 segments (`hsp-16` promoter) and a virus-inducible `pals-5::GFP` reporter, thereby bypassing viral entry to specifically isolate host factors required for intracellular viral replication. The screen isolated three independent mutants (`Viro-9`, `Viro-10`, `Viro-11`) that fail to activate `pals-5::GFP` upon Orsay virus heat-shock induction or exogenous infection (whilst retaining wild-type `pals-5::GFP` induction upon microsporidian *Nematocida parisii* infection) and show $420\text{--}760\times$ lower viral RNA after heat shock and $15,000\text{--}39,000\times$ lower viral RNA after exogenous infection. Whole-genome sequencing and F2 bulk segregant mapping (`MiModD`) pinpointed three independent missense mutations (`G80E`, `G91R`, `E113K`) in the previously uncharacterized SRR1-domain gene `Y55F3BL.4` (renamed *viro-9*). Causal responsibility was confirmed by: (1) transgenic rescue with wild-type `Psur-5::viro-9` in two independent lines; (2) phenocopying in a CGC nonsense allele `viro-9(sy1174)` ($\sim 8,200\times$ viral load reduction); (3) generation of a $4\times$-backcrossed full-locus CRISPR/Cas9 deletion null `viro-9(vir19)` ($\sim 9,600\times$ viral load reduction, plus modest reductions in median lifespan from 12 to 10 days and brood size by 36%); and (4) cross-species trans-complementation with the *C. briggsae* ortholog `CBG23913` (diverged 80–110 Ma). This is a textbook genetic discovery paper with multiple independent allelic, knockout, and cross-species rescue validations, limited only by the use of a low-powered non-parametric test (`Kruskal-Wallis` + Dunn's test) on $N=3$ independent experiments and lack of direct biochemical mechanism for VIRO-9.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
#### Strengths
* **Elegant Entry-Bypass Genetic Screen with Pathogen Specificity Control:** Initiating Orsay virus replication from an integrated heat-shock transgene (`virIs1`) cleanly separates intracellular replication defects from viral entry defects, while challenging the same mutants with *N. parisii* (which activates the same `pals-5::GFP` reporter via the Intracellular Pathogen Response) rules out reporter-silencing or general transcriptional defects (Figure 1A).
* **Five-Fold Independent Genetic Validation of *viro-9*:** Causal proof is exceptionally strong, comprising: (i) three independent EMS missense alleles (`vir16` G80E, `vir17` G91R, `vir18` E113K); (ii) F2 bulk segregant linkage mapping on Chr IV; (iii) an independent nonsense allele `sy1174` (stop at codon 13); (iv) a $4\times$-backcrossed CRISPR/Cas9 full coding deletion `vir19`; and (v) transgenic rescue by both *C. elegans* `viro-9` and *C. briggsae* `CBG23913` across two independent lines each.
* **Quantitative Standard-Curve qRT-PCR With Residual Inoculum Baseline Control:** Viral RNA2 and host *rps20* copy numbers were interpolated from *in vitro* transcript standard curves, and exogenous infection experiments included a no-worm bacterial-lawn viral decay control combined with uninfected worms prior to extraction to measure true input background.

#### Weaknesses & Methodological Audit
* **Application of Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's Test to $N=3$ Independent Experiments:** In Figures 1B, 2C, 3A, and 4B, the legends state: "Each bar represents the mean of three independent experiments, and error bars represent the SD. Three independent experiments were performed. Kruskal-Wallis test was performed, followed by Dunn's multiple comparison test. Adjusted $P$-values are reported as follows: $*, P < 0.05; **, P < 0.01; ***, P < 0.001; ****, P < 0.0001$." Statistically, a rank-based Kruskal-Wallis test with Dunn's multiple comparison adjustment across 4–6 groups with $n=3$ data points per group **cannot mathematically achieve $P < 0.0001$** (the minimum possible two-group rank permutation $p$-value for $n=3$ vs. $n=3$ is $1/\binom{6}{3} = 0.05$, and with Dunn's asymptotic normal approximation across $k=5$ groups, $N=15$, the maximum rank-sum difference between the highest-rank group [mean rank 14] and lowest-rank group [mean rank 2] has $Z = 12 / \sqrt{15(16)/12 \times (1/3 + 1/3)} = 12 / 3.651 = 3.286$, giving unadjusted $p = 0.0010$ and Dunn-adjusted $p > 0.004$). Thus, to obtain $P < 0.0001$ (`****`), the authors either pooled the technical/within-experiment replicates ($3\text{ experiments} \times 3\text{ replicates} = 9$) as independent observations in GraphPad Prism or ran a parametric ANOVA on log-transformed values while labeling it Kruskal-Wallis. Given that viral loads drop by $3\text{--}4.5\text{ orders of magnitude}$ ($420\times$ to $39,000\times$) with non-overlapping distributions, the biological effect is indisputable, but the statistical test description vs. $P < 0.0001$ reporting should be reconciled (parametric one-way ANOVA on $\log_{10}$ viral load across $n=3$ biological means is the appropriate test).
* **Lifespan and Brood Size Sample Size Reporting:** In the Materials and Methods (line 133), the lifespan and brood size assays state "10 young adults were transferred into a well... At least three independent experiments were performed", but Figure 3B–C does not state the total number of censored and uncensored animals ($n$) per curve or whether the log-rank ($P = 0.0002$) and Mann-Whitney ($P = 0.03$, erroneously labeled `(**)` instead of `(*)`) tests were stratified by experimental batch.
* **Minor Textual Typo:** Line 118 writes `viro-16(vir16)` instead of `viro-9(vir16)`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1: *viro-9* (`Y55F3BL.4`) is an essential pro-viral host factor required for intracellular Orsay virus replication in *C. elegans* `[Well Supported]`.** Five independent alleles (3 EMS missense, 1 nonsense, 1 CRISPR null) and two transgenic rescue lines demonstrate $>400\times$ defect upon intracellular heat-shock induction and $>8,000\times$ defect upon exogenous infection.
* **Claim 2: The pro-viral function of the SRR1 domain is evolutionarily conserved between *C. elegans* and *C. briggsae* (`CBG23913`), and residues G80 and E113 are deeply conserved across eukaryotic kingdoms `[Well Supported]`.** Cross-species transgenic complementation fully restores viral replication in `viro-9(vir16)` animals (Figure 4), and multi-kingdom alignment confirms strict conservation of G80 and negatively charged E113.
* **Claim 3: *viro-9* deletion modestly reduces worm lifespan and brood size in the absence of infection `[Well Supported]`.** Demonstrated in the $4\times$-backcrossed CRISPR null allele `viro-9(vir19)` (Figure 3B–C).
