### 1. Executive Summary
This study examines qualitative splicing outcomes (via 10 overlapping RT-PCR amplicons across the 65-exon `FBN1` transcript followed by agarose gel electrophoresis and Sanger sequencing) and quantitative total `FBN1` mRNA expression (via `TaqMan` qRT-PCR normalized to `GAPDH`) in cultured dermal fibroblasts from 16 Marfan syndrome (MFS) patients carrying known `FBN1` variants (8 missense, 3 premature termination codon [PTC] frameshift/nonsense, 4 canonical intronic splice-site, and 1 exonic cryptic splice-site variant), 6 healthy controls, and 3 first-degree relatives of one missense patient (`c.G2447T`). The authors demonstrate that `c.4817-2delA` causes an in-frame skip of exon 39 (`126 bp` deletion) and that `c.A4925G`—originally predicted *in silico* to be a missense substitution (`p.Asp1642Gly`)—activates an exonic cryptic splice site that deletes the terminal `18 bp` of exon 39 in frame (`p.Thr1643_Asp1648del`). Quantitatively, they report that total `FBN1` mRNA levels in PTC and splice-site patients are lower than in missense patients, and that elevated `FBN1` mRNA in the `c.G2447T` proband (`212%`) does not co-segregate with the mutation in his family (his unaffected mutation-negative sister has the highest expression). While the identification of the cryptic splice site for `c.A4925G` and the familial non-segregation observation are valuable, the study is weakened by internal contradictions in reported statistical significance, small subgroup sizes ($n = 3$ to $8$), lack of nonsense-mediated decay (NMD) translation-inhibition controls, and reliance on bulk rather than allele-specific qRT-PCR.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Internal Contradiction Between Abstract/Results and Conclusions on Statistical Significance:**
  - In the Abstract (line 11) and Results (line 104), the authors state: *"Furthermore, there was no significant difference in the mean FBN1 transcript level in the group of eight patients with missense mutations, the group of three patients with PTC, or in the group of five patients with splice site mutations compared to controls (Fig. 2a). However, the mean FBN1 transcript level in the two groups of patients with PTC and splice site mutations were both significantly lower than in the group of patients with missense mutations."*
  - Yet in the Conclusions (line 139), the authors write: *"...the mean expression levels associated with PTC and splice site mutations were significantly lower than the levels in controls and patients with missense mutations."* Claiming a statistically significant reduction relative to controls in the Conclusions when the Results explicitly state the comparison against controls was non-significant is a direct contradiction.
- **Small Subgroup Sizes, Unadjusted Multiple $t$-Tests, and Normalization Single-Reference Gene:**
  - Subgroup comparisons (`controls` $n = 6$, `missense` $n = 8$, `PTC` $n = 3$, `splice site` $n = 5$) and individual patient-vs-control comparisons (16 separate tests in `Additional file 1`) were conducted using unadjusted two-tailed unpaired Student's $t$-tests. With $n = 3$ in the PTC group and high inter-individual variance among controls (`Figure 2a`, spanning ~50% to >150%), statistical power is low and no multiple-comparison adjustment (e.g., ANOVA with Tukey/Dunnett post-hoc test) was applied.
  - Quantitative RT-PCR relied on a single housekeeping gene (`GAPDH`) rather than the geometric mean of $\ge 2\text{–}3$ validated fibroblast reference genes (per MIQE guidelines), which can contribute to the large inter-individual expression swings (`51%` to `219%`).
- **Absence of NMD Inhibition Controls and Allele-Specific Expression Quantification:**
  - For the three PTC mutations (`c.4269_4270delAC`, `c.5559delT`, `c.T6339A`) and three canonical splice-site mutations (`c.3083-2A>G`, `c.4211-1G>A`, `c.4942+2T>C`), no aberrant bands or mutant sequence peaks were detected by standard RT-PCR and Sanger sequencing, which the authors attribute to complete degradation of the mutant transcript by NMD. However, the fibroblasts were not treated with a translation/NMD inhibitor (such as cycloheximide or puromycin) prior to RNA harvesting to stabilize and directly sequence the predicted out-of-frame spliced transcripts (`skip of exon 25`, `skip of exon 34`, `skip of exon 39`). Without NMD blockade or allele-specific qRT-PCR, complete NMD of the mutant allele cannot be separated from wild-type allele expression variability (especially in patients `c.4269_4270delAC` and `c.4817-2delA`, whose total `FBN1` mRNA remained at $88\%$ and $80\%$ of controls).
- **Typographical/Labeling Errors in Figures and Text:**
  - In the `Figure 1c` legend (line 99), the patient is mislabeled as having `"4817 + 2 T > C"` (a nonexistent hybrid of `c.4817-2delA` and `c.4942+2T>C`).
  - In Results line 104, `c.G2447T` is misspelled as `"c.G244T"`, and in Methods line 78 `GAPDH` is misspelled as `"GADPH"`.
  - In `Table 1`, row 9 is an orphaned blank row containing `"Probably damaging"` split from row 8 (`c.G7094A`), and `c.T6339A` (exon 51 nonsense mutation `p.Tyr2113X`) has `"PTC skip of exon 25"` in the *In silico* prediction column (an obvious copy-paste error from `c.3083-2A>G` immediately below it).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **`[Well Supported]` Exonic variant `c.A4925G` (`p.Asp1642Gly`) acts as a cryptic donor splice site causing an in-frame 18-nt deletion in exon 39, and `c.4817-2delA` causes in-frame skipping of exon 39:** Directly proven by RT-PCR fragment sizing (`937 bp` and `829 bp` vs. `955 bp` wild-type in `Figure 1b`) and cDNA Sanger sequencing (`Figure 1c,d`).
- **`[Well Supported]` High total `FBN1` mRNA in the `c.G2447T` index patient is driven by trans/cis background variation in the wild-type allele rather than the missense mutation itself:** Demonstrated by profiling three first-degree relatives (`Figure 2b`), where the unaffected `c.G2447T`-negative sister exhibits equally elevated (~220%) `FBN1` mRNA.
- **`[Moderately Supported]` Out-of-frame PTC and splice-site transcripts (`c.3083-2A>G`, `c.4211-1G>A`, `c.4942+2T>C`) undergo efficient NMD:** Consistent with the absence of mutant peaks on cDNA Sanger traces and lower mean expression compared to missense carriers, but lacks cycloheximide rescue to observe the predicted spliced intermediates directly.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Reference sequence (`NM_000138.4`), exact cDNA/protein variant nomenclature, `TaqMan` assay IDs (`Hs009731199_m1`, `Hs99999905_m1`), cell culture passage numbers (`P3–P5`), and patient-level expression means $\pm\text{ SD}$ and clinical Ghent organ-system codes are tabulated in `Table 1`.
- **Minor Corrections Needed:** The copy-paste errors in `Table 1` (`c.T6339A` annotated as `"skip of exon 25"`) and `Figure 1c` legend (`"4817 + 2 T > C"`), along with the contradictory significance statement in the Conclusions, must be corrected.
