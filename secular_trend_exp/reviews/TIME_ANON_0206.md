### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript is a scientific meeting report summarizing highlights from the 3rd Plant Genomics European Meeting (Plant-GEMS, Lyon, France), attended by ~600 scientists from $>30$ countries. The report synthesizes keynote and plenary presentations on comparative and functional plant genomics, covering the release of the ~500 Mbp *Populus* (poplar) genome across 19 chromosomes, the Solanaceae Genome Initiative, maize BAC-end sequencing (~475,000 clones covering 307 Mb, 60% repetitive, 7.5% genic) and post-tetraploidization fractionation, cereal synteny and rice-assisted fine-mapping of a 7.6 cM wheat leaf-rust resistance QTL on chromosome 7DS to a 300-kb colinear region on rice chromosome 6, the minimal 11.5 Mb genome of the unicellular green alga *Ostreococcus tauri*, MADS-box protein-protein interaction networks, asymmetric expression divergence of duplicated *Arabidopsis* genes, the *openSputnik* EST comparative analysis pipeline ($>5$ million plant ESTs across $>60$ species, quantifying raw vs. unigene-clustered error rates of 2.2% vs. 1.6%), and the *AtGenExpress* *Arabidopsis* developmental microarray atlas (~80 samples showing ~93% of genes expressed during development).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
