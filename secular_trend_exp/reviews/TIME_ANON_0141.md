### 1. Executive Summary
This developmental genetics study characterizes the spatiotemporal expression and embryonic loss-of-function phenotype of *slc7a6os* in zebrafish (*Danio rerio*), the vertebrate ortholog of the *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* RNA polymerase II nuclear import factor *IWR1*. Using TaqMan qRT-PCR, adult tissue RT-PCR, and whole-mount in situ hybridization (WISH), the authors show that *slc7a6os* is maternally deposited, broadly expressed during early cleavage/somitogenesis, and progressively enriched in the developing central nervous system (midbrain, hindbrain, cerebellum, telencephalon, and rhombomeres) by 22–48 hpf. Knockdown with a splice-blocking morpholino targeting the exon 1–intron 1 junction (`slc7a6os-MOspl1`, 6 ng/embryo) causes disorganized somites, impaired motility, pericardial/yolk-sac edema, loss of definition at the midbrain–hindbrain boundary (MHB), and reduced expression of CNS and lateral line patterning markers (*pax2a*, *wnt1*, *neurod*, and *lef1*). Importantly, co-injection of *p53* morpholino does not alter the phenotype, and co-injection of 400 pg synthetic *slc7a6os* mRNA rescues both gross morphology (79% rescue at 28 hpf) and *pax2a*/*neurod* expression patterns. While the mRNA rescue and *p53*-MO controls substantially strengthen the study, minor methodological inconsistencies in the control comparisons, splice-blocking RT-PCR validation, and in vitro transcription protocol temper the technical score.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Inconsistency Between Control Morpholino Design and Uninjected Control Comparisons:** In the Materials and Methods ("Injections", line 61), the authors state that *"A standard control morpholino oligonucleotide (ctrl-MO) was used as negative control (S3 Table)."* However, in the Results ("slc7a6os loss of function leads to CNS disorganization...", line 100), they state: *"In all experiments, slc7a6os-MOspl1 embryos were compared with non-injected embryos at the same developmental stage."* Comparing morpholino-injected embryos against non-injected embryos rather than `ctrl-MO`-injected siblings fails to control for mechanical microinjection trauma and buffer/oligonucleotide toxicity (although the synthetic mRNA co-injection rescue in Figs. 3–7 mitigates this concern).
- **Incomplete Splice-Blocking RT-PCR Characterization and Single-Morpholino Reliance:** Only a single morpholino (`slc7a6os-MOspl1`) was used, without a second non-overlapping translation- or splice-blocking MO or a germline CRISPR/TALEN mutant. In the RT-PCR validation (S4 Fig., line 164), `slc7a6os-MOspl1` abolishes the wild-type 681 bp exon 1–3 amplicon, but no aberrant splice product (predicted 3,368 bp intron-retention product or cryptic splice band) was detected, which the authors attribute to large amplicon size and nonsense-mediated decay (NMD). Quantitative qRT-PCR of the knockdown efficiency or Western blotting (if an antibody were available) was not performed.
- **Protocol Contradiction in Synthetic mRNA Transcription:** In line 66, the *slc7a6os* coding region was cloned into `EcoRI`/`XhoI`-digested `pCS2+`, and the authors write: *"The plasmid construct was linearized and transcribed with T7 RNA polymerase using the mMESSAGE mMACHINE SP6 in vitro transcription kit (Ambion)."* In standard `pCS2+` vectors, sense transcription from the `EcoRI`–`XhoI` polylinker is driven by the **SP6** promoter (whereas T7 is in the antisense orientation downstream of `XhoI`), making "transcribed with T7 RNA polymerase using the mMESSAGE mMACHINE SP6 kit" internally contradictory.
- **Statistical Reporting for Phenotypic Penetrance:** Penetrance percentages and sample sizes ($n = 33$ to $139$ across 2–3 sessions) are clearly reported for morphants and mRNA-rescued embryos, though formal contingency-table tests (e.g., Fisher's exact or $\chi^2$ tests comparing morphant vs. rescued penetrance) are not reported alongside the percentages.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *"Zebrafish slc7a6os is a maternal gene that is expressed throughout development, with a prevalent localization in the developing central nervous system (CNS)."* — `[Well Supported]`. Demonstrated consistently by stage-series TaqMan qRT-PCR (normalized to *ef1$\alpha$* with standard curves), Ensembl RNA-seq tracks, and antisense vs. sense control WISH from 1–2 cell stage (0.2 hpf) through 48 hpf.
- **Claim 2:** *"Zebrafish slc7a6os is required for the correct morphogenesis and patterning of the mesencephalic-metencephalic regions of the developing vertebrate brain and for a proper organization of somites."* — `[Moderately Supported]`. Supported by high-penetrance morphological and marker (*pax2a*, *wnt1*, *neurod*, *lef1*) defects that are insensitive to *p53*-MO and rescued by synthetic *slc7a6os* mRNA, though germline null mutation would be needed to distinguish maternal vs. zygotic requirements and rule out residual morpholino artifacts.
- **Claim 3:** *"Our data suggest that slc7a6os might play a critical role in defined areas of the developing CNS in vertebrates, probably by regulating the expression of key genes [via RNA Pol II]." * — `[Moderately Supported]`. Framed appropriately as a hypothesis based on sequence homology (46% to human SLC7A6OS, 14% to yeast Iwr1 with conserved NLS/NES motifs), as no direct biochemical assay of Pol II binding or nuclear import was performed in zebrafish.
