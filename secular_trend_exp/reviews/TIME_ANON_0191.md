### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular neurobiology study reports the isolation, full-length cDNA cloning, cross-species sequence conservation (rat and cat), developmental Northern blot quantification, and high-resolution $^{35}\text{S}$-cRNA in situ hybridization mapping of `BEHAB` (*Brain Enriched HyAluronan Binding protein*), a novel central nervous system (`CNS`)-restricted member of the proteoglycan tandem repeat (`PTR`) family of hyaluronan (`HA`)-binding proteins. Screening a postnatal day 12 (`P12`) rat brain $\lambda\text{gt10}$ cDNA library with the HA-binding region of rat aggrecan (`pRCP 4`) followed by a `P39` cat visual cortex $\lambda\text{Zap II}$ library, the authors assemble overlapping clones encoding a 371-aa rat protein (1,113 bp ORF, GenBank `Z28366`) and a 378-aa cat ortholog (1,134 bp ORF, GenBank `Z28367`) that share 84% overall amino acid identity (95% in `PTR 1`, with 100% identity across 44 consecutive residues). Northern blots across 7 adult tissues and whole-embryo (`E17`) parasagittal in situ hybridizations demonstrate that the 3.9-kb `BEHAB` transcript is strictly confined to the CNS (absent in peripheral nervous system dorsal root ganglia, cartilage, liver, kidney, spleen, lung, and muscle), is expressed in both neurons (e.g., hippocampal `CA1`, cerebellar Purkinje/Golgi II cells, brainstem nuclei, ventral horn motor neurons) and white-matter glial cells (astrocytes in corpus callosum and spinal tracts), and is developmentally upregulated from `E17` (where it localizes to mitotically active ventricular zones) through the first two postnatal weeks (`N = 5–6` per age normalized to `cyclophilin`). The experimental execution, controls, and anatomical characterization are of high quality; minor limitations are the absence of direct biochemical verification of HA binding and a unit typo (`kD` instead of `D`) for the predicted molecular weight.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Rigorous Cross-Species Sequencing and Hybridization Controls:**
  1. **cDNA Cloning and Compression Resolution:** Full-length sequences were assembled from 8 overlapping rat clones and 5 overlapping cat clones sequenced on both strands, using `dITP` and terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase to resolve GC compressions, and cross-verifying the C-terminal stop codon difference (rat `TAG` at position 372 vs cat `TGG` Trp-373 adding 6 C-terminal residues) across three independent rat clones (`B1`, `A5`, `A8`) and both strands of cat clone `B1`.
  2. **In Situ and Northern Specificity Controls:** In situ hybridizations (Figs. 7, 8, 10) include adjacent-section positive controls (`neurofilament-middle` [`NF`] antisense probe, which displays a reciprocal `CA2`/`CA3`/dentate gyrus pattern in the hippocampus and is absent from white-matter glia) and negative controls (`clone B1` sense cRNA probe). Whole-embryo sections (`E17`, Fig. 10B–C) provide an internal cross-hybridization control by showing zero signal in embryonic vertebral cartilage (which expresses high levels of aggrecan sharing 60% identity in `PTR 1` with `BEHAB`).
  3. **Developmental Northern Quantification (Figure 9):** Developmental expression across 6 stages (`E17`, `P0`, `P5`, `P14`, `P21`, `Adult`) in rat cortex ($N = 5$ per postnatal stage, $N = 1$ at `E17`) and spinal cord ($N = 6$ per postnatal stage, $N = 2$ at `E17`) is standardized by simultaneous hybridization and NIH Image densitometric ratioing against `cyclophilin` (a developmentally invariant CNS reference transcript, unlike actin or tubulin).
- **Minor Limitations and Unit Error:**
  - **Three-Orders-of-Magnitude Unit Error in Predicted Molecular Mass (Results, p. 497):** The text states *"The resulting mature protein has a predicted molecular mass of **38,447 kD**"* for rat `BEHAB` and *"resulting in a predicted molecular mass of **38,955 kD**"* for cat `BEHAB`. A 349–356-amino-acid mature polypeptide has a molecular mass of **$38,447\text{ Da}$ ($38.4\text{ kDa}$)** and **$38,955\text{ Da}$ ($39.0\text{ kDa}$)**, not thirty-eight thousand kilodaltons!
  - **No Error Bars or Statistical Tests on Developmental Densitometry Curve (Figure 9C):** Despite having $N = 5$ (cortex) and $N = 6$ (spinal cord) biological replicates at each postnatal age (`P0`–`Adult`), Figure 9C plots only the point means of the `BEHAB`/`cyclophilin` ratio without error bars (SD/SEM) or one-way ANOVA across developmental stages.
  - **Absence of Direct Biochemical Hyaluronan-Binding Assay:** While sequence conservation of the ten disulfide-forming cysteines, the Ig-fold `YxCxVxH` motif, both `CDAGWL(A/S)D(Q/G)(T/S)VRYPI` (`HAL`) tandem repeats, and `Arg-166` (equivalent to essential `Arg-41` in `CD44`) strongly predicts HA binding, no recombinant protein expression or HA-Sepharose affinity chromatography was performed.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`BEHAB` encodes a novel, highly conserved member of the `PTR` family (84% rat–cat identity; 95% in `PTR 1`) that is distinct from cartilage link protein, aggrecan, versican/GHAP/hyaluronectin, and neurocan.* `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively proven by full-length rat and cat cDNA sequences (Figs. 2–5), domain identity comparisons (Table I), and isolation of a separate cat brain PCR clone matching authentic cartilage link protein (Discussion, p. 505).
- **Claim 2:** *`BEHAB` mRNA expression is strictly restricted to the central nervous system (expressed by both distinct neuronal populations and white-matter glia) and is developmentally regulated from `E17` proliferative ventricular zones to peak during postnatal extracellular matrix maturation.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by multi-tissue and developmental Northern blots (Figs. 6, 9) and cellular-resolution emulsion autoradiography in situ hybridizations (Figs. 7, 8, 10).
- **Claim 3:** *`BEHAB` functions as an extracellular link-like protein stabilizing HA–proteoglycan aggregates in the brain.* `[Moderately Supported]` — Appropriately presented as a structural hypothesis based on its link-protein-like domain architecture (one Ig domain + two PTR domains without a transmembrane segment or chondroitin sulfate attachment domain, even though the 3.9-kb mRNA is much longer than the 1.1-kb coding sequence).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Positive:** Complete nucleotide and deduced amino acid sequences for both rat and cat `BEHAB` are printed in full in Figures 2 and 3 and deposited in EMBL/GenBank/DDBJ under accession numbers `Z28366` and `Z28367`, and all library screening, Northern hybridization, and in situ hybridization protocols are described in full detail.
- **Missing:** Replicate variance measures (SD/SEM) for the $N = 5\text{–}6$ developmental Northern blot series in Figure 9C are omitted.
