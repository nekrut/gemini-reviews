### 1. Executive Summary
This primary virological and genomic study provides a side-by-side characterization of SARS-CoV-2 (isolate BetaCoV/Australia/VIC01) and SARS-CoV (isolate Frankfurt-1) replication in Vero E6 cells. The authors combine (1) plaque-purification and deep Illumina NovaSeq 6000 sequencing across serial passages, discovering rapid selection for point mutations ($\text{R682Q}$, $\text{R682W}$, $\text{R683L}$) and deletions ($\Delta 679\text{--}688$, $\Delta 675\text{--}679$) that abrogate the S1/S2 furin-like polybasic cleavage site ($\text{PRRAR}\downarrow\text{SV}$) and convert the virus from a small-plaque to a large-plaque phenotype (alongside identifying a stock-specific 10-nt deletion in the 3'-UTR *s2m* motif); (2) one-step high-MOI ($\text{MOI}=3$, $n=4$ replicates) viral growth kinetics paired with direct $^{32}\text{P}$-oligonucleotide in-gel hybridization of genomic and subgenomic mRNAs (1–9), revealing that SARS-CoV-2 synthesizes intracellular RNA earlier and to higher levels (particularly mRNAs 7 and 8, elevated ~4-fold and ~2-fold) yet releases ~50-fold less infectious progeny into the supernatant than SARS-CoV; (3) immunofluorescence cross-reactivity validation of 13 SARS-CoV antisera against 10 viral proteins (nsp3, nsp4, nsp5, nsp8, nsp9, nsp13, nsp15, M, N); (4) transmission electron microscopy (TEM) time-courses (6, 8, 10 h p.i.) of replication organelles (DMVs, zippered ER vs. convoluted membranes, vesicle packets) and budding virions; and (5) quadruplicate CPE-reduction antiviral dose-response assays ($n=3$ independent experiments) comparing remdesivir, chloroquine, alisporivir, and pegylated interferon-$\alpha$2a.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Direct Linkage of Genotype to Plaque Phenotype via Clonal Plaque Picking & Deep Sequencing:** Rather than merely reporting bulk consensus mutations, the authors picked individual small (S5) and large (L8) plaques from the p1 stock, passaged them three times (p1 $\rightarrow$ p3), and deep-sequenced the S1/S2 boundary (1,924–4,049 reads spanning nt 23,576–23,665; 95,173 reads at position 23,607 in the p2 stock). This cleanly demonstrates that L8p1 is >99% pure for $\text{G23607A}$ ($\text{R682Q}$) with zero other genomic changes, while small-plaque clone S5 starts >99% wild-type at p1 and independently evolves multiple convergent furin-site mutations ($\text{R682Q}$ at 12.2%, $\Delta 679\text{--}688$ at 22.0%, plus $\text{R682W}$, $\text{R683L}$, and $\Delta 675\text{--}679$) by p3.
- **Direct In-Gel $^{32}\text{P}$ Hybridization Without RT-PCR Amplification Bias:** Quantifying the nested set of 9 viral RNA species via direct in-gel hybridization of formaldehyde-agarose gels with a 19-nt 3'-conserved $^{32}\text{P}$ probe normalized to 18S rRNA (Fig. 3a–b) avoids RT-PCR primer-efficiency artifacts and provides direct molar stoichiometry of genomic and subgenomic transcripts at 12, 14, and 24 h p.i.
- **Antiviral Screening Design & Controls:** The 96-well MTS cell-viability assay includes parallel mock-infected cytotoxicity curves ($\text{CC}_{50}$) on every plate, solvent and background controls, quadruplicate wells across 3 independent biological experiments, and non-linear regression reporting $\text{EC}_{50} \pm \text{SD}$ and selectivity indices ($\text{SI}$).
- **Methodological Caveat — Mixed Working Stock (p2) in Comparative Assays:** Because all comparative growth kinetics, Northern hybridizations, EM, and antiviral assays were performed with the Leiden p2 working stock—which NGS showed already contained 71% $\text{R682Q}$ large-plaque mutant, 29% wild-type S, and the 10-nt 3'-UTR *s2m* deletion—some phenotypic features in Vero E6 cells reflect this cell-culture-adapted population. The authors transparently highlight both the 71% $\text{R682Q}$ frequency and the 10-nt *s2m* deletion in the Results and Discussion, explicitly cautioning that isolates lacking the 3'-UTR deletion should be preferred for in vivo pathogenesis studies.
- **Qualitative EM Spike Density Observation:** In the Results (line 125), the authors note that in TEM thin sections, "whereas spikes were clearly present on SARS-CoV progeny virions, a relatively large proportion of SARS-CoV-2 particles seemed to carry few or no visible spike projections on their surface." However, conventional osmium/uranyl-acetate plastic-embedded 100-nm thin-section TEM is not ideal for quantifying glycoprotein spike density compared to cryo-EM or negative-stain EM, and no morphometric spike counts per virion perimeter across a random sample of virions are presented.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** "Compared to SARS-CoV, SARS-CoV-2 generated higher levels of intracellular viral RNA, but strikingly about 50-fold less infectious viral progeny was recovered from the culture medium." `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by $n=4$ plaque-assay growth curves (Fig. 2a) and phosphorimager-quantified $^{32}\text{P}$ RNA gels (Fig. 3a–b).
- **Claim 2:** "Upon passaging in Vero E6 cells – SARS-CoV-2 apparently is under strong selection pressure to acquire adaptive mutations in its spike protein gene. These mutations change or delete a putative furin-like cleavage site in the region connecting the S1 and S2 domains and result in a very prominent phenotypic change in plaque assays." `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively proven by clonal plaque purification, serial passage monitoring, and deep sequencing of S5p1–p3 and L8p1 (Fig. 1a–b).
- **Claim 3:** "Sensitivity of the two viruses to three established inhibitors of coronavirus replication (remdesivir, alisporivir and chloroquine) is very similar, but SARS-CoV-2 infection was substantially more sensitive to pre-treatment of cells with pegylated interferon alpha." `[Well Supported]` — Supported by 3 independent quadruplicate dose-response experiments (Fig. 6a–d).
- **Claim 4:** "A relatively large proportion of SARS-CoV-2 particles seemed to carry few or no visible spike projections on their surface, perhaps suggesting a relatively inefficient incorporation of spike proteins into SARS-CoV-2 virions." `[Fragile]` — Appropriately hedged with "seemed" and "perhaps suggesting," as thin-section plastic-embedded TEM without quantitative cryo-EM or virion Western blot (S-to-N ratio in purified virions) cannot distinguish reduced spike incorporation from S1 shedding (especially in a stock with 29% intact furin site vs. 71% $\text{R682Q}$) or staining contrast differences.
