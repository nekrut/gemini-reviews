### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This study presents a high-quality, chromosome-scale reference genome assembly for trifoliate orange (*Poncirus trifoliata* accession `DPI 50-7`, $264.9\text{ Mb}$, 9 chromosomes, contig $N_{50} = 4.3\text{ Mb}$, scaffold $N_{50} = 28.6\text{ Mb}$, $97.2\%$ BUSCO completeness, $25,538$ annotated protein-coding genes) constructed using $\sim 140\times$ PacBio continuous long reads, $\sim 80\times$ Illumina short reads, and Dovetail HiRise Hi-C scaffolding, alongside whole-genome resequencing ($32\text{--}46\times$) of four *P. trifoliata* cultivars (`DPI 50-7`, `Flying Dragon`, `Rubidoux`, `Little Leaf`), six *Poncirus*$\times$*Citrus* rootstock hybrids, and the endangered congener *Poncirus polyandra*. Through comparative phylogenomics (`5,747` single-copy orthogroups via both concatenation/`RAxML` and coalescent/`ASTRAL`), gene family evolution (`CAFE`), branch-site positive selection (`PAML`), $K_s$-based whole-genome duplication/divergence dating (`GenoDup`), and population genomic admixture/relatedness analyses (`Eigensoft`, `ADMIXTURE`), the authors: (1) rigorously control for annotation-pipeline artifacts across 10 *Citrus*-related genomes by systematically re-annotating all candidate regions with a uniform `tBlastn/Exonerate` pipeline; (2) identify lineage-specific adaptation in cold tolerance (`CBF`-dependent/independent pathways, positive selection on `COR413-PM1`, `LTI65`, and `PRP`) and disease resistance (`305` NBS-LRR genes, `WRKY70` positive selection, and candidate gene mapping within Huanglongbing [`HLB`], `Ctv`, and `Tyr1` QTLs); and (3) uncover an unexpectedly narrow genetic base among US *P. trifoliata* rootstock germplasm, proving that `Flying Dragon` and `Rubidoux` share $100\%$ of both haplotypes ($r=1$) and represent clonal derivatives with somatic mutations rather than independent sexual accessions.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
- **Exemplary Control for Cross-Species Annotation Bias (`Blast/Exonerate` Re-Annotation):** A pervasive flaw in comparative plant genomics is attributing differences in gene annotation stringency across separately published genomes to biological gene family expansion/contraction (`CAFE`) or species-specific gene gain. Here, the authors explicitly recognized and corrected this artifact (`Results`, lines 183–191; `Methods`, line 80): initial analysis of existing annotations suggested 115 expanded and 765 contracted families in *P. trifoliata*, plus 319 *P. trifoliata*-specific families; after uniform re-annotation of all 10 genomes with `Blast/Exonerate`, these numbers dropped to 24 expanded families, 5 contracted families, and 51 specific families, and `5,747` vs. `4,032` single-copy orthologs were recovered. This self-correcting methodological rigor is outstanding.
- **Lack of Direct Experimental Validation for Candidate HLB/Cold-Tolerance Genes:** While the bioinformatic integration of *P. trifoliata* gene models with previously published cold-stress ESTs (`Wang et al.`) and *Candidatus* Liberibacter asiaticus (`CLas`)-responsive DEGs from *C. sinensis* / *C. jambhiri* / *C. hystrix* identifies compelling candidates (`WRKY70`, `NBS-LRR` clusters in `FS-2016-t9a`, `Ctv`, and `Tyr1`), no new wet-lab expression profiling (e.g., RT-qPCR or RNA-seq under cold or `CLas` challenge in *P. trifoliata*) or functional transgenic/transposon validation is performed in this study.
- **Reference-Guided Assembly Limitations for *Poncirus polyandra*:** Because *P. polyandra* was sequenced only with short reads ($32\text{--}46\times$) and assembled via reference-guided mapping (`RGAAT` + `Genewise`) against *P. trifoliata*, any *P. polyandra*-specific structural insertions, copy-number expansions, or highly diverged genes are inherently invisible, though its use here is appropriately restricted to $K_s$ divergence dating ($K_s = 0.015$, $T \approx 3.8\text{--}7.5\text{ MYA}$) and phylogenetic placement.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality (STRENGTHS)
- **Discovery of Clonal Identity (`r = 1`) and Narrow Genetic Base in US *Poncirus* Germplasm `[Well Supported]`:** Whole-genome haplotype-sharing analysis across $100\text{ kb}$ windows (`Fig. 5`, `Fig. S6`) reveals that `Flying Dragon` and `Rubidoux` share both haplotypes across virtually the entire genome ($r=1$, likely due to nucellar embryony and somatic mutation for the dwarf/curved-spine phenotype) and that `DPI 50-7` and `Little Leaf` are first-degree relatives ($r=0.5$) of `Flying Dragon`/`Rubidoux`. This has immediate, practical implications for citrus rootstock breeding and germplasm conservation.
- **Lineage-Specific Positive Selection and Expansion in Cold-Tolerance Pathways `[Well Supported]`:** After stringent re-annotation and FDR correction ($\text{FDR} < 0.05$, BEB posterior probability $>95\%$ in `PAML` branch-site Model A vs. A1), the identification of positive selection on `LTI65` (`Pt1g002530`), `COR413-PM1` (`Pt8g009260`), and proline-rich `PRP` (`Pt7g017980`), alongside expansion of a compact tandem 4-gene cluster of `At5g06760` (`LEA4-5`-like late embryogenesis abundant proteins on Chr 5), provides well-grounded evolutionary candidates for *P. trifoliata*'s extreme freezing tolerance.
- **Physical Resolution of `Ctv`, `Tyr1`, and HLB-Tolerance QTL Intervals `[Well Supported]`:** Mapping previously unresolved genetic markers onto the contiguous *P. trifoliata* v1.3 pseudomolecules localizes the `Ctv` and `Tyr1` resistance loci to concrete physical intervals on Chromosome 7 containing specific `NBS-LRR` gene clusters.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Raw PacBio and Illumina sequencing reads are deposited in NCBI GenBank/SRA (`BioProject: PRJNA648176`, `SRR12323696–SRR12323706`), and the *P. trifoliata* v1.3 genome assembly and annotations are publicly hosted at `Phytozome`. Exact software versions and full command-line parameter strings are provided for `Falcon` (`v2018.03.12`), `BWA-MEM` (`v0.7.17`), `minimap2`, `Juicer`, `JuiceBox`, `pbalign`/`Arrow`, `FreeBayes` (`v1.3.1`), `ILEC`, `Exonerate` (`v2.2.0`), `RAxML`, `ASTRAL` (`v5.7.3`), `CAFE`, `PAML`, `GATK` (`v3.7`), `Eigensoft`, and `ADMIXTURE`.
- **Weaknesses:** None of consequence; the computational pipeline documentation is exceptionally detailed.
