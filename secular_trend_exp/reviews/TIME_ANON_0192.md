### 1. Executive Summary
This study tests a biophysical model for how skeletal muscle regulates full-length dystrophin (`dp427m`) expression across the 2.3-Mb `Dmd` locus—which requires ~16 hours to transcribe a single 14-kb mRNA—by tracking nascent and mature `dp427m` transcripts, short isoform `dp71` transcripts, and sarcolemmal dystrophin protein across a 30-day time course of $\text{BaCl}_2$-induced tibialis anterior (`TA`) muscle injury and regeneration in mice (`2, 4, 7, 14, and 30 DPI`; $N = 4$ at 2 DPI, $N = 5$ at all other time points, paired with contralateral uninjured muscles). Combining three-region multiplex single-molecule RNAscope FISH (`5'` exons 2–10, `middle` exons 45–51, `3'` exons 64–75, co-labeled with `Pax7` or `Ki67`), region-specific RT-qPCR and digital droplet PCR (`ddPCR` of `5'` exons 1–3, `middle` exons 44–45, `3'` exons 62–64, and `dp71`), and multiplex immunofluorescence (`dp427`, `perlecan`, `IgG`, embryonic myosin `MYH3`), the authors demonstrate three key phenomena: (1) nascent `dp427m` transcription (`5'` and `middle` nuclear foci without `3'` signal) initiates early at `4 DPI` in mononuclear myoblasts/myocytes prior to myotube fusion ("paying the 16-h cost in advance"); (2) during peak sarcolemmal assembly and myofiber hypertrophy (`7–14 DPI`), myonuclear initiation rate increases by only ~50% (from `20–40` to `30–60` nascent transcripts per nucleus by calibrated smFISH intensity), whereas the `3':5'` mature-to-total transcript ratio doubles (from `~0.20–0.25` to `~0.35–0.45` by ddPCR/qPCR), indicating an ~2-fold increase in mature mRNA half-life/lifetime (from ~4 h to ~7–8 h); and (3) by `30 DPI`, once sarcolemmal dystrophin protein is restored, rapid turnover of mature `dp427m` transcripts resumes, returning the muscle to its basal state of constitutive oversupply and post-transcriptional degradation. This is a thoughtfully designed, quantitatively rigorous experimental study with clear mechanistic and therapeutic implications for Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Orthogonal Spatial (`smFISH`) and Bulk Absolute (`ddPCR`/`qPCR`) Quantification:** A major strength of the study is that it addresses the confounding geometric effects of regenerating myotubes (smaller sarcoplasmic volume and higher nuclear density at `7–14 DPI`) by cross-validating spatial smFISH puncta against absolute copy-number `ddPCR` (`5'`, `middle`, and `3'` amplicons calibrated with $R^2 > 0.99$ standard curves and normalized to the geometric mean of three validated injury-invariant reference genes: `ACTB`, `CSNK2A2`, and `AP3D1`).
- **Biophysical Modeling of `5':3'` Transcript Ratios and Non-Equilibrium Caveats:** The authors use the Tennyson steady-state equation:
  $$\frac{5'}{3'} = \frac{\text{nascent} + \text{mature}}{\text{mature}} = \frac{T_{\text{transcrip}} + T_{\text{lifetime}}}{T_{\text{lifetime}}}$$
  with $T_{\text{transcrip}} = 13\text{ h}$ between exon 1–3 (`~2 h`) and exon 62–64 (`~15 h`) at $40\text{ nt/s}$ to infer that a `3':5'` ratio of `0.20–0.25` in uninjured muscle corresponds to a mean mature lifetime $T_{\text{lifetime}} \approx 3.3\text{–}4.3\text{ h}$ ($t_{1/2} = T_{\text{lifetime}} \ln 2 \approx 2.3\text{–}3.0\text{ h}$), rising to $T_{\text{lifetime}} \approx 7\text{–}8\text{ h}$ at `14 DPI`. Importantly, the authors explicitly recognize and illustrate (Fig. 5D boxes) that at `2 DPI` (transcriptional cessation with residual surviving mature mRNA) and `4 DPI` (newly initiated `5'` transcription before the first 16-h wave reaches exon 62–64), the system is out of steady state, so the equilibrium formula applies only during established regeneration (`7–14 DPI`) and basal homeostasis (`uninjured` and `30 DPI`).
- **Minor Potential Confounder of `dp71` and Shorter Isoforms on the `3'` Exon 62–64 Amplicon:** Because the `3'` qPCR/ddPCR primer pair targets exons 62–64, which are shared by `dp427m` and shorter internal-promoter isoforms such as `dp71` (transcribed from intron 62), high `dp71` expression could theoretically inflate the measured `3':5'` ratio. However, the authors directly measure `dp71` using its unique first exon (Fig. 5E–G) and show that at `14 DPI` (when the `3':5'` ratio peaks), `dp71` accounts for only ~10% of `5'` `dp427m` and ~20–25% of `3'` signal—showing that `dp71` alone cannot account for the elevated `3':5'` ratio at `14 DPI` (which is also visually confirmed inside myofibers by three-probe colocalized smFISH puncta in Fig. 4C–D).
- **Statistical Design in Single-Nucleus smFISH Comparison (Figure 4F):** In Figure 4F, the authors measure 100–300 nuclei per muscle across $N = 4$ injured (`14 DPI`) and $N = 4$ uninjured muscles, and properly perform Welch's $t$-test on the *per-muscle means* ($N = 4$ biological replicates) rather than pseudoreplicating across the ~1,000 pooled nuclei.
- **Typo in Figure 1B Caption (Lines 22 & 25):** States *"dp427 protein (~ **1300 amino acids**, 427 kDa) and mRNA (14 kb)"*—full-length `dp427` is **3,685 amino acids** (~427 kDa), whereas 1,300 amino acids would be ~150 kDa.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Myogenic precursor cells initiate nascent `dp427m` transcription early (`4 DPI`) prior to myotube fusion and occasionally in `Ki67+` (likely G2/M) peripheral nuclei, minimizing the 16-hour transcriptional lag prior to sarcolemmal assembly.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by `5'`-only nuclear smFISH foci and the sharp drop in bulk `3':5'` ratio at `4 DPI` (Figs. 4B, 5D, 6B, 7D).
- **Claim 2:** *During active myofiber regeneration and hypertrophy (`7–14 DPI`), increased mature `dp427m` abundance is driven primarily by a ~2-fold increase in mature transcript stability (`3':5'` ratio doubling) alongside a modest (~50%) increase in nuclear initiation.* `[Well Supported]` — Convergently supported by three-probe smFISH (Fig. 4C–F) and absolute multi-region ddPCR/qPCR (Fig. 5A–D).
- **Claim 3:** *Centrally nucleated regenerated mouse myofibers remain transcriptionally active at `14` and `30 DPI` and successfully export mature `dp427m` transcripts to the peripheral sarcolemma.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly visualized by three-probe smFISH in centrally nucleated fibers (Fig. 4D–E).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Positive:** Animal sample sizes, sex randomization (`1–2M / 3F` per group), $\text{BaCl}_2$ injury protocol, antibody clones/dilutions, RNAscope probe catalog numbers (`452801`, `529881-C2`, `561551-C3`, `314181-C3`, `416771-C3`), all 9 target qPCR/ddPCR primer pairs, and linear mixed model specifications are reported with exceptional clarity.
- **Missing:** Raw numeric values/tables for the ddPCR standard curves and individual mouse RQ values are not tabulated in the main text (residing in supplementary data).
