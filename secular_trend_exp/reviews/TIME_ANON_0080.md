### 1. Executive Summary
This document (`TIME_ANON_0080`) is not a single research manuscript, but rather a compilation of 36 short (~200–300 word) conference abstracts from a specialized international symposium on Juvenile Hormone (JH) and arthropod endocrinology. The abstracts span diverse insect and crustacean taxa (*Spodoptera frugiperda*, *Ropalidia marginata*, *Ips pini*, *Helicoverpa armigera*, *Acyrthosiphon pisum*, *Blattella germanica*, *Drosophila melanogaster*, *D. virilis*, *Manduca sexta*, *Bombyx mori*, *Aedes aegypti*, *Procambarus clarkii*, *Gryllus firmus*, etc.) and cover neuropeptide regulators (allatotropins, allatostatins), biosynthetic and catabolic enzymes (FPPS, GPPS, FAMeT, JHAMT, JHE, JHDK), nuclear receptor signaling (`USP`, `EcR`, `Broad`, `E75`, `RXR`), insulin pathway crosstalk (`InR`, `chico`), and analytical chemistry (LC-MS). While many individual abstracts describe biologically interesting and plausible experimental findings, the compilation lacks full experimental protocols, primary data figures, tables, statistical distributions, and sequence accession numbers required for a peer-reviewed scientific article.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Absence of Primary Data Figures, Tables, and Controls:** Because each entry is a conference abstract, virtually all results are presented as narrative assertions without primary data plots, gel images, chromatograms, or summary tables.
- **Incomplete Sample Sizes and Statistical Reporting:**
  - Across the 36 abstracts, only one abstract (Laufer et al., lines 303–309, on methyl farnesoate in *Procambarus clarkii* morph transitions) reports explicit group sample sizes ($n=4$ to $8$ animals per group) and a statistical test ($p < 0.01$ by $\chi^2$ test, though Fisher's exact test is required when cell counts are as small as $n=4$ to $8$!).
  - Quantitative parameters are occasionally stated as point values without variance or sample size—for example, `EC50 of ca. 10^-7 M` for `Spofr-AT 2` (line 15), $K_m = 19.70\ \mu\text{M}$ and $V_{\max} = 228.83\ \mu\text{M/min/}\mu\text{l}$ for *Plutella xylostella* JHE (line 282), $K_D = 2.56, 1.77, 4.32$ (units omitted!) for *Bombyx mori* follicle cell JHBP (line 480), LC-MS limits of detection of 6–8 pg (line 581), and a 2.8 Å crystal structure of *M. sexta* JHE covalently bound to OTFP (line 594, without crystallographic $R_{\text{work}}/R_{\text{free}}$ or PDB accession).
  - Several abstracts explicitly describe work yet to be performed (e.g., Dayton et al., line 109: *"Germ-line transformations of this construct will be carried out to provide localization data"*; Pszczolkowski et al., line 434: *"We will present data on these experiments"*).
