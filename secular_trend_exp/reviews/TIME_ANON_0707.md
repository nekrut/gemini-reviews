### SUMMARY
### 1. Executive Summary
This classical molecular virology study characterizes the synthesis, glycosylation maturation, intracellular trafficking, antigenicity, and immunogenicity of the Spike (S) glycoprotein from a virulent British field isolate of porcine transmissible gastroenteritis virus (TGEV strain `FS772/70`) expressed via recombinant vaccinia virus (rVV) at the thymidine kinase (*tk*) locus under the early/late `P7.5K` promoter. The authors demonstrate that full-length recombinant S protein is synthesized as an Endo H-sensitive 190-kDa high-mannose precursor (`gp190`) that matures into an Endo H-resistant 210-kDa complex-glycosylated form (`gp210`), localizes predominantly to the Golgi apparatus with partial transport to the plasma membrane, binds a panel of native S-specific monoclonal antibodies, and elicits TGEV-neutralizing antibodies in rVV-immunized mice. In contrast, a C-terminally truncated S mutant (`SΔ`, lacking the C-terminal 292 amino acids including the transmembrane anchor and cytoplasmic tail, `gp170`) retains native antigenic epitopes by immunoprecipitation and immunofluorescence but is arrested in the endoplasmic reticulum and fails to reach the cell surface.

### STRENGTHS
- **Biochemical Coupled with Subcellular Trafficking Characterization:** Combining Endo H endoglycosidase sensitivity (`gp190` precursor vs. `gp210` mature complex glycoform) with surface vs. intracellular immunofluorescence cleanly tracks ER-to-Golgi-to-plasma membrane transport.
- **Comparison of Full-Length (`gp190/210`) vs. C-Terminally Truncated (`SΔ`, `gp170`) Spike:** Deleting the C-terminal 292 residues demonstrates that while N-terminal antigenic determinants (sites A–D of TGEV S) fold into mAb-reactive conformations in the ER, the C-terminal domain is essential for ER export and surface display.
- **Functional Neutralization Readout in Vivo:** Testing sera from rVV-immunized mice in TGEV neutralization assays verifies that the recombinant S protein adopts an immunogenically authentic conformation.

### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Archival Text Truncation (Abstract + Bibliography Only):** In the provided file (`TIME_ANON_0707.md`), only the detailed Abstract and 34-reference bibliography were extracted from the historical print archive, omitting the main text, figures, and neutralization titer tables. Consequently, sample sizes ($n$ of immunized mice), plaque-reduction neutralization endpoints, and pulse-chase time points cannot be directly audited from the markdown file.
- **Confounding of Transmembrane Anchor Deletion with Luminal Coiled-Coil Stalk Truncation in `SΔ`:** Deleting 292 amino acids from the C-terminus of TGEV S (~1,447 aa total) removes not only the C-terminal transmembrane domain and cytoplasmic tail (~70 aa), but also >220 amino acids of the conserved S2 heptad-repeat/stalk region required for spike trimerization. Because coronavirus S oligomerization in the ER is a prerequisite for ER export (as shown in the cited Vennema et al. reference), ER retention of `SΔ` (`gp170`) likely reflects failure to trimerize rather than simply absence of the membrane anchor.
