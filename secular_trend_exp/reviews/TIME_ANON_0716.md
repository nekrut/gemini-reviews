### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This short communication tests whether acquired multidrug resistance (MDR) and chromosome 7 short-arm alterations (`7p13->p22` putative homogeneously staining region, HSR, overlapping the `EGFR` locus) are associated with loss of in vivo tumorigenicity or epidermal growth factor receptor (`EGFR`) gene amplification/overexpression in human doxorubicin (DOX)-resistant tumour sublines. Studying the 222-fold DOX-resistant fibrosarcoma subline `HT1080/DR4` (which is resistant via a non-P-glycoprotein mechanism) and the 285-fold DOX-resistant colon adenocarcinoma subline `LoVo/DR5` (which overexpresses P-glycoprotein without `mdr1` gene amplification), the authors demonstrate: (1) by Western blotting with `[125I]`-labelled mAb `C494` (recognizing a distinct epitope from `C219`), `HT1080/DR4` lacks P-glycoprotein overexpression whereas `LoVo/DR4`, `LoVo/DR5`, and positive control `CEM/VBL100` strongly overexpress it; (2) `HT1080/DR4` retains tumorigenicity in female BALB/c athymic nude mice ($5/7$ mice formed tumours vs. $8/8$ for parental `HT1080`, albeit with longer latency of $23 \pm 8$ vs. $14 \pm 3$ days and smaller size of $70 \pm 16$ vs. $211 \pm 74\text{ mm}^2$, $P < 0.01$); and (3) neither `HT1080/DR4` nor `LoVo/DR5` exhibits `EGFR` gene amplification (by Southern blot with `pE7`), `EGFR` mRNA elevation (by actin-normalized slot-blot densitometry, $n = 3$), or increased surface `[125I]-EGF` receptor binding ($1.72 \pm 0.11\%$ vs. $1.93 \pm 0.25\%$ per $10^6$ cells for `HT1080/DR4` vs. parental; $0.63 \pm 0.08\%$ vs. $0.53 \pm 0.04\%$ for `LoVo/DR5` vs. parental, $n = 3$).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
**Strengths:**
- **Multi-Level Verification Across DNA, RNA, and Protein/Ligand Binding:** To test the hypothesis that the chromosome `7p13->p22` HSR in `HT1080/DR4` amplifies or upregulates `EGFR`, the authors measure `EGFR` at all three biological tiers—genomic DNA copy number (`EcoRI` Southern blot, Figure 2a), mRNA abundance (serial-dilution slot blots normalized to actin across 3 independent extractions scanned in the linear film range, Figures 2b and 3), and functional cell-surface receptor density (`[125I]-EGF` binding in triplicate, page 298)—alongside positive (`A431`) and negative (`CEM`, `CEM/VBL100`) controls.
- **Orthogonal Epitope Confirmation of Non-P-gp MDR:** Confirming the absence of P-glycoprotein in `HT1080/DR4` with mAb `C494` (Figure 1) rules out false-negative immunodetection from epitope masking or splice/point mutation at the `C219` site.
- **Replicated In Vivo Xenograft Bioassay:** Tumorigenicity was tested in two independent experiments ($4\text{ mice/group} \times 2 = 8$ mice inoculated with $1 \times 10^7$ cells) and analysed with a non-parametric Mann-Whitney U test ($P < 0.01$, Table I).

**Weaknesses & Methodological Audit:**
- **Censoring of Non-Tumour-Bearing Mice in Table I Summary Statistics:** In Table I, `HT1080/DR4` formed tumours in $5/7$ surviving mice (two mice failed to develop tumours by day 68, and one mouse died early without tumour). The reported mean latency ($23 \pm 8$ days) and mean tumour area 10 days post-detection ($70 \pm 16\text{ mm}^2$) are calculated exclusively among the 5 mice that formed tumours, omitting the 2 non-tumour-forming mice ($0\text{ mm}^2$ at day 68). While this actually makes the statistical difference from parental `HT1080` ($8/8$ tumours, $14 \pm 3$ days, $211 \pm 74\text{ mm}^2$) conservative, reporting conditional tumour size without a survival/time-to-event (Kaplan-Meier) latency curve slightly understates the reduction in take rate ($71\%$ vs. $100\%$).
- **Lack of Error Bars Reported in Figure 3 Text:** Figure 3 plots the mean actin-normalized `EGFR` mRNA ratios of three independent RNA extractions standardized to `CEM = 1`, but standard deviation bars are not visible/reported numerically in the text for the bar graph (unlike the `[125I]-EGF` binding data, where exact $\text{mean} \pm \text{s.d.}$ values are printed).
- **Unresolved Identity of the 7p HSR:** While the study definitively rules out `EGFR` as the amplified target in the `HT1080/DR4` `7p13->p22` HSR, the actual genetic content of that cytogenetic abnormality remains unidentified.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** `HT1080/DR4` is a 222-fold doxorubicin-resistant human fibrosarcoma line that does not overexpress P-glycoprotein (`C494`-negative) yet retains tumorigenicity in athymic nude mice. `[Well Supported]` by Western blot (Figure 1) and xenograft assays (Table I), with the slower in vivo growth ($23$ vs. $14$ days latency) appropriately linked to its longer in vitro doubling time ($30\text{ h}$ vs. $19\text{ h}$).
- **Claim 2:** Neither `HT1080/DR4` (despite a `7p13->p22` putative HSR) nor `LoVo/DR5` (with chromosome 7 alterations) has `EGFR` gene amplification, mRNA overexpression, or increased EGF binding. `[Well Supported]` by Southern, slot-blot, and radioligand binding assays.
- **Claim 3:** Acquired multidrug resistance and loss of tumorigenicity or `EGFR` upregulation are independent phenomena rather than obligate consequences of MDR selection. `[Well Supported]` and appropriately contextualized against prior neuroblastoma and rodent MDR studies.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagents & Controls:** Cell lines (`HT1080`, `HT1080/DR4`, `LoVo`, `LoVo/DR4`, `LoVo/DR4-DF`, `LoVo/DR5`, `CEM`, `CEM/VBL100`, `A431`), antibody (`C494`), cDNA probes (`pE7`, human actin), inoculum size ($1 \times 10^7$ cells in $0.125\text{ ml}$ PBS), and exact numerical binding percentages are fully documented.
