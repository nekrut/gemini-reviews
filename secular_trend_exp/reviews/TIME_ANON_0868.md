### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This landmark hybrid computational and experimental immunology study investigates the structural basis of T-cell molecular mimicry in multiple sclerosis (MS). Moving beyond naive linear sequence alignment, the authors define a degenerate structural motif encapsulating the side-chain requirements for both MHC class II (HLA-DR2) binding and T-cell receptor (TCR) recognition of an immunodominant myelin basic protein (MBP) epitope. Using this motif to search protein databases, they synthesize and functionally screen a panel of 129 matching pathogen-derived peptides against seven MBP-specific T-cell clones isolated from MS patients.
