### SUMMARY
### 1. Executive Summary
This primary molecular immunology study dissects the relative contributions of intra-epitope versus flanking amino acid residues to the endogenous cytoplasmic processing and $H\text{-}2K^d$-restricted class I MHC presentation of the immunodominant influenza A/Japan/305/57 (H2N2) hemagglutinin determinant `HA 202–221` (`RTLYQNVGTYVSVGTSTLNK`). Because `HA 202–221` contains two overlapping 10-mer subsites—the N-terminal `HA 204–213` (`LYQNVGTYVS`, recognized by $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL clone `11-1`) and the C-terminal `HA 210–219` (`TYVSVGTSTL`, recognized by $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL clone `40-2`)—the authors perform doped-oligonucleotide saturation mutagenesis across a 42–46 amino acid minigene expressed via a double-subgenomic Sindbis virus (`dsSIN`) vector in $H\text{-}2^d$ `P815` target cells. Testing 60 sequenced single and multiple substitution mutants in quadruplicate $^{51}\text{Cr}$-release assays, they show that mutations within `HA 204–213` (especially $K^d$ anchor residues `Tyr-205` and `Val-212`) or `HA 210–219` (`Tyr-211` and `Leu-219`) abolish or impair lysis by CTL clones `11-1` and `40-2`, respectively, whereas almost all substitutions in flanking residues outside each minimal epitope have no effect—with the sole exception of `Val214Gly` (construct `42`), which creates a C-terminal `Gly-214/Gly-215` doublet immediately adjacent to `HA 204–213` and selectively abolishes recognition by clone `11-1`.

### STRENGTHS
- **Reciprocal Internal Control Architecture of Overlapping CTL Subsites:** Because residues `204–209` lie inside the N-terminal `11-1` epitope (`204–213`) but form the N-terminal flank of the `40-2` epitope (`210–219`), while residues `214–219` lie inside the `40-2` epitope (`210–219`) but form the C-terminal flank of the `11-1` epitope (`204–213`), each mutant construct simultaneously serves as a positive control for viral infection, minigene translation, and antigen presentation for the reciprocal CTL clone.
- **High-Throughput Cytoplasmic Mutagenesis and Discovery of the Flanking `Val214Gly` Processing/Trimming Defect:** Screening 60 defined mutants in a uniform 42–46-residue polypeptide context maps both the $H\text{-}2K^d$ anchor motif (`Tyr` at P2, `Val/Leu/Ile` at P9/P10) and TCR contact constraints while pinpointing a rare C-terminal flanking glycine doublet (`Val214Gly`) that blocks clone `11-1` recognition without abolishing recognition by clone `40-2` or a second `HA 204–213`-specific clone (`14-1`).
- **Thoughtful Mechanistic Discussion of Assay Sensitivity Limits:** The authors candidly discuss how high-level cytoplasmic expression from the Sindbis subgenomic promoter ($\text{MOI} = 50\text{ PFU/cell}$) and a single $E:T$ ratio ($5:1$) could mask subtle kinetic differences in proteasomal cleavage or TAP transport.

### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Saturating Sindbis Vector Expression ($\text{MOI} = 50$) and Single $E:T$ Ratio ($5:1$) Limit Detection of Partial Processing Defects:** As noted in the Discussion, expressing a 42–46 aa minigene driven by a strong alphavirus subgenomic promoter at $50\text{ PFU/cell}$ produces abundant cytosolic polypeptide substrate, and testing CTL lysis at a single $E:T$ ratio ($5:1$, yielding 80% parental lysis for clone `40-2` and 50% for clone `11-1`) without $E:T$ titration or Brefeldin A kinetic decay curves can mask $2\text{–}10$-fold reductions in proteolytic generation efficiency by flanking mutations.
- **Graphical Bar Values Omitted in OCR Text of Figures 2 and 3:** While the mutant amino acid alignments for all 60 constructs (`01`–`60`) are shown in Figures 2 and 3, the graphical horizontal bars depicting the exact relative $^{51}\text{Cr}$-release percentages are not captured in the digitized text, and a few single-letter amino acid codes in the OCR alignment (`01`–`60`) suffer minor character corruption (`g`, `7,`, `&`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *"CD8+ T cell recognition of the endogenously processed HA 202–221 minigene product is regulated primarily by residues within the minimal 10-mer epitopes (`204–213` and `210–219`) that govern $H\text{-}2K^d$ binding and TCR contact."* — **[Well Supported]**. Demonstrated across 60 saturation mutants with reciprocal clone specificity.
- **Claim 2:** *"Substitution of Gly for Val-214 immediately flanking HA 204–213 abolishes lysis by clone 11-1 while permitting recognition by clone 40-2 and partial recognition by clone 14-1, suggesting altered C-terminal trimming or an extended 11-mer epitope."* — **[Well Supported]**. Carefully interpreted with multiple testable mechanistic hypotheses.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
