### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript investigates the role of the feline infectious peritonitis virus (FIPV) membrane (M) and nucleocapsid (N) structural proteins in subunit vaccination and antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE) of disease in kittens, following up on prior work showing that recombinant vaccinia virus expressing the FIPV spike (S) protein induced early death upon challenge. The authors cloned and sequenced the FIPV M and N genes (reporting 84.7% and 77% amino acid identity with porcine transmissible gastroenteritis virus [TGEV]), expressed them in recombinant vaccinia vectors, and immunized kittens ($n = 8$ per group) prior to virulent FIPV challenge. Neither M- nor N-vaccinia recombinants induced early death (supporting the conclusion that ADE is specific to anti-S antibodies), N-immunization had no effect on survival, and $3/8$ kittens immunized with the M-protein recombinant survived challenge compared to $1/8$ in the control group.

Crucially, the manuscript text provided for review (`TIME_ANON_0114.md`) consists solely of the Abstract and the Reference list—the entire body of the paper (Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Figures, and Tables) is absent from the record. Even evaluating the study charitable on the quantitative experimental design and results reported in the Abstract, the sample size ($n = 8$ kittens per group) is severely underpowered for binary survival endpoints ($3/8$ vs. $1/8$ survival is statistically indistinguishable from chance, two-sided Fisher's exact test $p = 0.569$).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Missing Main Text, Methods, and Primary Data Tables:** In the manuscript file, the `## Main Text` heading is followed immediately by `References`, omitting all experimental protocols (viral strain and challenge dose, immunization schedule, antibody titers by ELISA/neutralization, time-to-death survival curves, and sequence alignments). No methodological audit of assay controls, blinding, or randomization can be performed when the Methods and Results sections are missing.
- **Underpowered Challenge Trial ($n = 8$ per group):** In the M-protein vaccination arm, $3/8$ ($37.5\%$) kittens survived FIPV challenge compared to $1/8$ ($12.5\%$) in the control group. A two-sided Fisher's exact test yields $p = 0.569$ (odds ratio $= 4.2$, 95% exact CI $[0.25, 263.5]$). At $n = 8$ per group, an increase from $12.5\%$ to $37.5\%$ survival has less than $15\%$ statistical power at $\alpha = 0.05$ and cannot be distinguished from random biological variation.

- **Claim 1:** "Immunization with neither recombinant [M or N] induced early death after challenge with FIPV, strongly suggesting that antibody-dependent enhancement is mediated by antibodies against S only." `[Moderately Supported]` — The absence of accelerated early death in M- and N-vaccinated kittens (in contrast to prior S-vaccinated kittens) is biologically consistent with coronavirus ADE being mediated by surface spike glycoprotein antibodies that facilitate Fc-receptor-mediated macrophage entry, though full survival time-to-death curves and pre-challenge anti-M/anti-N antibody titers are absent due to the missing main text.
