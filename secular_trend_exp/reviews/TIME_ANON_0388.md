### 1. Executive Summary
This experimental insect pathology study profiles transcriptional responses of 1-day-old honey bee (*Apis mellifera*) worker larvae orally challenged with $1 \times 10^5$ spores of the chalkbrood fungus *Ascosphaera apis* ($n=90$ infected vs. $n=90$ control per bioassay, repeated twice; pools of 10 larvae sampled at 24 h and 36 h post-infection). Using cDNA-AFLP across 64 selective *Taq*I/*Mse*I ($+2/+2$) primer combinations at 24 h post-infection, the authors estimate ~7,400 transcript fragments, excise and sequence 98 reproducible polymorphic bands, highlight 14 annotated transcripts in Table 2, and perform follow-up qRT-PCR (normalized to *actin*) at 24 h and 36 h on 3 cDNA-AFLP candidates (*AmelCht* glycosyl hydrolase 18, *cSP6* trypsin-like serine protease, *Hex 70b*) plus 5 candidate immune/storage genes not detected by cDNA-AFLP (*MyD88*, *abaecin*, *defensin-1*, *lysozyme-1*, *vitellogenin*). While the qRT-PCR time-course demonstrates late (36 h) induction of *MyD88*, *cSP6*, *abaecin*, and *defensin-1* alongside a sharp 36 h collapse of storage protein (*Hex 70b*, *Vg*) and chitinase (*AmelCht*) transcripts, the study suffers from severe morbidity/lethality confounding at 36 h (larvae were moribund at 36 h and 100% dead by 48 h), unvalidated qRT-PCR fold-change claims for unprobed cDNA-AFLP bands, and pseudoreplication in the qRT-PCR statistical analysis.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Technical vs. Biological Replicates in qRT-PCR ANOVA:** The Methods state that two independent bioassays were performed and pools of 10 larvae were collected at 24 h and 36 h, and that qRT-PCR "reactions were done in triplicate" and analyzed by one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post-test (reporting Tukey's $q$ statistics and $p$-values in the text). Running one-way ANOVA on technical triplicates ($n=3$ PCR wells from pooled RNA) treats technical pipetting replicates as independent biological replicates, inflating degrees of freedom and underestimating biological variance across colonies/bioassays.
* **Quantitative Fold-Change Claims for Transcripts Never Tested by qRT-PCR:** Table 1 lists qRT-PCR primers for only 8 target genes (*abaecin*, *defensin-1*, *lysozyme-1*, *Hex 70b*, *vitellogenin*, *trypsin-like SP*, *glycosyl hydrolase 18*, *MyD88*) plus *actin*. Yet in the Results ("Genes with putative roles in responses to various stressors", lines 177–187), the text states specific numerical fold changes for genes not in Table 1: *SAM-MT* ("up-regulated more than 5-fold"), *Sec61* ("up-regulated approximately 2-fold"), *ribosomal protein L23* ("up-regulated approximately 2-fold"), *cyb5* ("significantly down-regulated (> 10-fold)"), and *PFKFB* ("significantly up-regulated (> 10-fold)"). Either these fold changes were estimated by eye/densitometry from SybrGold-stained 8% polyacrylamide cDNA-AFLP gels—which is non-quantitative after two rounds of pre-amplification and selective 40-cycle PCR—or qRT-PCR primers used to measure them were omitted from Table 1.
* **Discrepancy Between cDNA-AFLP (24 h) and qRT-PCR (24 h):** As the authors candidly note in line 126, *AmelCht* and *cSP6* were selected as up-regulated at 24 h in cDNA-AFLP, yet qRT-PCR at 24 h showed no significant up-regulation for *AmelCht* (Fig. 2A) or *cSP6* (Fig. 2B). Thus, the unverified 24 h cDNA-AFLP directional arrows in Table 2 carry a substantial false-positive rate.
* **Lethal Dose and Agonal Confounding at 36 h:** Larvae received $1 \times 10^5$ spores, reduced feeding within 24 h, "became visibly sluggish at 36 h, and at 48 h post-infection showed no signs of life." Consequently, transcriptional changes at 36 h (12 hours before 100% death)—such as the collapse of gut chitinase (*AmelCht*), *Hex 70b*, and *vitellogenin*—cannot be cleanly separated into regulated "immune-nutritional trade-offs" versus agonal starvation, gut necrosis, and global tissue death.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Late (36 h) induction of AMPs (*abaecin*, *defensin-1*), *MyD88*, and *cSP6* in *A. apis*-infected larvae:** `[Well Supported]` — The qRT-PCR measurements clearly demonstrate 4- to 24-fold induction of these innate immune transcripts at 36 h post-infection compared to age-matched uninfected controls.
* **Coordinated activation of both Toll and IMD NF-$\kappa$B pathways:** `[Moderately Supported]` — Up-regulation of *MyD88* (8.3-fold at 36 h) alongside *defensin-1* and *abaecin* is consistent with engagement of Toll and IMD pathways, although none of the five Toll receptors showed transcriptional change and IMD pathway signaling intermediates were not directly assayed.
* **Functional roles of unvalidated cDNA-AFLP hits (*Lmtk3*, *26S proteasome subunit 9*, *npc2*, *ABC1*, *Sec61*, *PFKFB*):** `[Overstated]` — Extensive mechanistic narratives (apoptosis, proteasome failure, exocyst suppression, hypoxia) are built around single cDNA-AFLP bands at 24 h that were never validated by qRT-PCR or functional assays.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Primer and Accession Reporting:** Table 1 provides forward/reverse primers, annealing temperatures, and GenBank accessions for the 8 qRT-PCR target genes and *actin*, and Table 2 lists GenBank accessions and conserved domains for the 14 highlighted cDNA-AFLP clones.
* **Missing Data Details:** Selective primer sequences for the 64 *Taq*I/*Mse*I combinations, densitometric or raw standard-curve quantities for Figure 2, and biological replicate variance across the two independent bioassays are not reported.
