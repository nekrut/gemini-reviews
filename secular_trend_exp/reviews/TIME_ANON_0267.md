### 1. Executive Summary
This study profiles the coding and non-coding transcriptome of 6 pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) surgical specimens and 5 normal pancreas control tissues using 3'-end tag sequencing (Massive Analysis of cDNA Ends, MACE, with TrueQuant UMI deduplication) and small RNA sequencing (sRNA-seq). Using DESeq (`FDR < 0.05`, $|\log_2\text{FC}| > 1.6$), the authors report 1,961 differentially expressed mRNAs, 43 lncRNAs (lincRNAs and natural antisense transcripts), and 123 sncRNAs (104 miRNAs, 18 snoRNA-derived sdRNAs, and 1 piRNA, `piR-017061`). They validate 7 mRNAs, 4 miRNAs, and 2 lincRNAs by RT-qPCR in the same cohort, compare top upregulated mRNAs against an external microarray dataset ($N = 36$, $r = 0.61$), perform ZEB1 immunohistochemistry ($n = 10$ PDAC vs. $n = 10$ normal), and transiently overexpress `miR-802` in MiaPaCa-2 cells ($n = 3$), reporting a 33% reduction in nuclear TCF4 protein by Western blot. While the integration of UMI-deduplicated MACE and sRNA-seq with public data deposition (`E-MTAB-3494`) is a strength, the study is heavily confounded by bulk tissue cellular composition differences (acinar vs. desmoplastic stroma), internal numerical inconsistencies regarding `TCF4` fold-change, and a disconnect between the proposed `miR-802` $\to$ `TCF4` $\to$ `ZEB1` epithelial pathway and the IHC finding that ZEB1 is restricted to stromal cells.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Severe Bulk Tissue Cellular Composition Confounding (Acinar Dropout & Desmoplasia):** As the authors themselves document in Results line 33 and Table 2, **70% of all MACE reads in normal pancreas controls map to just 25 acinar-cell secretory enzyme genes** (*PRSS1, CLPS, CTRB1, PNLIP, CPA1*, etc.), compared to only **3% of reads in PDAC samples** ($\sim 100$-fold depletion). Conversely, PDAC bulk tumors are notoriously dominated by desmoplastic stroma (fibroblasts, stellate cells, immune infiltrate) with low neoplastic epithelial cellularity. Because library size normalization (DESeq) is compositional, the massive loss of acinar transcripts (70% $\to$ 3% of the transcriptome pool) and influx of stromal/immune cells mechanically inflates the apparent relative abundance of non-acinar transcripts and makes acinar-restricted RNAs (such as `miR-802`, `miR-216a/b`, and `miR-217`, which are known acinar markers, line 250) appear "downregulated in PDAC cells" ($\log_2\text{FC} = 10.9$), when in reality acinar cells have simply been replaced by ductal tumor and desmoplastic stroma.
- **Internal Contradiction on `TCF4` Fold-Change and FDR:**
  - In Results line 189, the authors state: *"The TCF4 transcript (**3.3-fold upregulated** in PDAC, **FDR = 0.001**)..."*
  - In Discussion line 254, the authors state: *"Bioinformatic in silico prediction points to Wnt signalling related transcription factor TCF4 mRNA (**~12-fold upregulated** in PDAC, **FDR = 5E-7**)..."*
  - A discrepancy between $3.3$-fold ($\log_2\text{FC} \approx 1.72$, $\text{FDR} = 10^{-3}$) and $12$-fold ($\log_2\text{FC} \approx 3.58$, $\text{FDR} = 5 \times 10^{-7}$) for the primary mechanistic target gene of the paper is a major internal inconsistency (likely confusing `log2FC = 3.3` [which is $2^{3.3} \approx 9.8$-fold] with linear fold-change `3.3-fold`, or mixing two different probe/isoform entries).
- **Western Blot Normalization & Statistical Reporting (Figure 4):** For the `TCF4` Western blot (line 316, Figure 4B–C), transfected MiaPaCa cells were fractionated into nuclear and cytoplasmic extracts, and 20 $\mu$g of the *nuclear* fraction was blotted for TCF4 and normalized to $\beta$-Actin. $\beta$-Actin is predominantly a cytoplasmic cytoskeletal protein and a poor loading control for nuclear fractions (where Lamin B1, Histone H3, or TBP are standard). Moreover, no p-value or statistical test is reported for the $n = 3$ Western blot quantification (67% of control).
- **Small Discovery Cohort & Same-Sample qPCR Validation:** With $n = 6$ PDAC and $n = 5$ normal samples, the exact Wilcoxon rank-sum test has a minimum possible two-sided p-value of $2 / \binom{11}{5} = 2 / 462 = 0.00433$. Yet in line 236, the authors report *"p = 0 (Wilcoxon's rank sum test)"* for `miR-802` qPCR, which is mathematically impossible for an exact rank-sum test on $n_1 = 5, n_2 = 6$. Furthermore, performing qPCR on the exact same 11 RNA samples used for MACE/sRNA-seq only validates the technical platform, not biological generalizability.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim: `miR-802` downregulation in PDAC derepresses `TCF4` to induce `ZEB1` and promote epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EMT) of pancreatic cancer** — `[Fragile]`. This central model suffers from a compartment paradox across three cell types: (1) `miR-802` is an acinar-specific miRNA (line 250); (2) MiaPaCa-2 is an epithelial cancer cell line where `miR-802` overexpression modestly reduced TCF4 protein by 33% without a 3'UTR luciferase reporter mutation assay to prove direct binding; and (3) the authors' own ZEB1 immunohistochemistry (Figure 5, line 205) shows that **epithelial tumor cells in PDAC are completely negative for ZEB1**, which is expressed exclusively in desmoplastic stromal fibroblasts! Thus, loss of acinar `miR-802` in tumor cells cannot be what drives `ZEB1` expression in non-neoplastic stromal fibroblasts unless a paracrine mechanism is invoked.
- **Claim: Identification of 43 deregulated lncRNAs (`LINC00261`, `LINC00152`, `AFAP1-AS1`) and 19 non-miRNA sncRNAs (`sno-HBII-296B`, `piR-017061`) between normal pancreas and PDAC bulk tissue** — `[Well Supported]`. As descriptive bulk tissue signatures, the MACE and sRNA-seq measurements are technically solid (using TrueQuant UMI deduplication) and concordant with external datasets (PED and Badea et al. microarray).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Sequencing datasets are deposited in ArrayExpress under accession `E-MTAB-3494` (line 361), which is commendable.
- **Missing Details:** Primer/probe sequences or catalog numbers for the IDT PrimeTime and Exiqon LNA qPCR assays are not listed, and the GEO accession placeholder (`[GEO: will be inserted after publication]`, line 363) was left unpopulated. Notice also that in Table 3, six differentially expressed lncRNAs lack gene symbols or genomic coordinates and are listed merely as generic strings (`lincRNA`, `NAT`, `lncRNA`).
