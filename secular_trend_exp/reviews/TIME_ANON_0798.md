### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This document is not a single research manuscript, but rather a full conference proceedings compilation (2,030 lines; alphabetically ordered by senior author from Abraham/Jacobs-Lorena onwards) from an international symposium on insect molecular biology, physiology, and disease vector genetics. The compiled abstracts span mosquito transgenesis and malaria/arbovirus vector competence (*Anopheles stephensi*, *Anopheles gambiae*, *Aedes aegypti*, *Aedes triseriatus*), double-subgenomic Sindbis-virus-mediated RNAi, *piggyBac* and *Jc*DNV transposon/densovirus vector stability, *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Cry1A/Cry1C) resistance linkage mapping in Lepidoptera (*Plutella xylostella*, *Helicoverpa armigera*), *de novo* juvenile-hormone-regulated isoprenoid pheromone biosynthesis in bark beetles (*Ips pini*, *Dendroctonus jeffreyi*), *Drosophila* neurogenetics (*slab* ceramidase and *rbo* synaptic mutants; *Or* and *Gr* chemoreceptor discovery), and comparative insect evo-devo (*Tribolium* Hox genes, *Schistocerca* appendage patterning). Evaluated as a proceedings collection, many individual abstracts report landmark experimental results with quantitative endpoints, while others represent preliminary work-in-progress summaries lacking statistical detail or archival data depositions.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
**Strengths:**
* **Quantitative Experimental Rigor in Key Vector & Neurogenetics Abstracts:**
  * *Abraham et al. (Jacobs-Lorena lab):* Four independent *piggyBac* transgenic *An. stephensi* lines driving bee venom $\text{PLA}_2$ from the gut-specific *Ag-Aper1* promoter achieve 73–91% inhibition of *Plasmodium berghei* oocyst formation; subtraction libraries across four developmental stages sequenced 2,182 clones (1,657 unique: 1,032 mosquito, 625 *Plasmodium* by AT-content partitioning) with antibody transmission-blocking validation (60–79% oocyst inhibition).
  * *Adelman et al. (James lab):* Southern blot, PCR, and plasmid-rescue demonstration of imprecise integration and concatemeric instability of *piggyBac* line #104 in *Ae. aegypti* across generations, and Sindbis-delivered sense/antisense RNAi against *P. gallinaceum* *TRAP* and *CSP* reducing salivary gland sporozoite loads from 10,000 spz/gland in controls to 1–500 spz/gland in 14–44% of mosquitoes.
  * *Baxter et al. & Brown et al. (Heckel lab):* Backcross AFLP linkage mapping in *Plutella xylostella* and *Helicoverpa armigera* ruling out candidate aminopeptidases and the *Heliothis virescens* YHD2 11-domain cadherin locus as the genetic basis of BX Cry1A resistance.
