### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This cellular and molecular immunology study tests whether the physiological downregulation of the second chain of the interferon-$\gamma$ receptor (IFN-$\gamma$R2 / $\beta$-chain) in differentiating T helper 1 (Th1) cells—which normally renders mature Th1 cells unresponsive to autocrine/paracrine IFN-$\gamma$—is functionally required for Th1 lineage differentiation and effector immunity. The authors generate two independent lines (`TG1` and `TG2`, backcrossed $\ge 4\text{--}5$ generations onto C57BL/6) of transgenic mice expressing murine `IFN-`$\gamma\text{R2}$ cDNA under the T-cell-specific human CD2 promoter/enhancer. Using RNase protection assays, RT-PCR, Stat1 EMSA supershifts, FACS-sorted naive ($\text{CD4}^+\text{Mel-14}^{\text{hi}}$) *in vitro* Th1/Th2 polarization, CFSE division tracking, *Listeria monocytogenes* delayed-type hypersensitivity (DTH) and recall assays, DNP-KLH IgG2a/IgG1 class-switching, and *Leishmania major* promastigote challenge, they demonstrate that forced maintenance of IFN-$\gamma$R2 on Th1 cells restores IFN-$\gamma$-induced (and constitutive) Stat1 activation, severely impairs *in vitro* Th1 polarization (whether driven by IL-12 or IFN-$\gamma$) without inhibiting cell division (CFSE) or defaulting to Th2 (IL-4), abrogates *in vivo* DTH, antigen-specific IgG2a switching, and Th1 recall to *L. monocytogenes* and KLH, and impairs control of *L. major* infection (larger lesions and 10-fold higher parasite burden).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Replication across two independent transgenic founder lines (`TG1` and `TG2`):** Confirming the phenotype across two independent integration lines rules out positional insertional mutagenesis artifacts.
- **Definitive exclusion of cell-extrinsic (APC/B-cell) and proliferative confounders:**
  - By sorting naive $\text{CD4}^+\text{Mel-14}^{\text{hi}}$ T cells and polarizing them in APC-free plate-bound anti-CD3 + cytokine cultures (Fig. 2), and by criss-cross restimulation of TG $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells on wild-type irradiated APCs (Fig. 4C, D) and LPS + IFN-$\gamma$/IL-4 stimulation of purified $\text{IgM}^+$ B cells, the authors prove that the Th1 and IgG2a defects are CD4+ T-cell-intrinsic.
  - Using single-cell CFSE dye-dilution tracking during polarization (Fig. 3) alongside $[\text{3H}]$thymidine incorporation (Figs. 2C, 4B, 5B), they rule out the obvious alternative hypothesis that autocrine IFN-$\gamma$ simply kills or arrests dividing TG Th1 cells.
- **Multi-model *in vivo* validation:** Demonstrates consistent loss of Th1 effector function across three classic immunological models: *L. monocytogenes* DTH/recall, DNP-KLH humoral IgG2a isotype switching, and *L. major* protozoal control on a genetically resistant (C57BL/6) background.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Incomplete backcrossing onto C57BL/6 ($N_4\text{--}N_5$):** Founders were generated in $(\text{C57BL/6} \times \text{CBA/J})\text{F}_1$ oocytes and backcrossed to C57BL/6 for "at least four generations" (fifth backcross for *L. major*, yielding $\sim 93.75\text{--}96.9\%$ B6 background). While using wild-type littermate controls and two independent TG lines mitigates background-locus confounding, $N_4\text{--}N_5$ is short of the congenic $N_{10}$ standard when studying *L. major* susceptibility.
- **Unexplained reactivation of the endogenous *Ifngr2* locus in TG Th1 clones:** In Figure 1C, TG Th1 clones express not only the transgenic `IFN-`$\gamma\text{R2}$ mRNA, but also the *endogenous* `IFN-`$\gamma\text{R2}$ transcript (which is silenced in WT Th1 clones). While the authors note that constitutive Stat1 activation may upregulate endogenous *Ifngr2*, an alternative interpretation is that the "Th1" clones isolated from TG mice represent incompletely polarized/uncommitted Th0-like cells that never underwent normal Th1 chromatin silencing at the *Ifngr2* locus.
- **Reliance on "data not shown" / "unpublished observations" for several controls:** Baseline flow cytometry plots of lymphoid organs, anti-Stat1 supershift gels, WT vs. TG APC criss-cross controls, purified B-cell LPS switch assays, and primary listeriosis survival are reported as "data not shown" or "unpublished observations".

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Forced expression of `IFN-`$\gamma\text{R2}$ under the CD2 promoter maintains IFN-$\gamma$ responsiveness and Stat1 signaling in differentiating and cloned Th1 cells. `[Well Supported]` — Directly proven by RPA, RT-PCR, and EMSA (Fig. 1).
- **Claim 2:** Sustained IFN-$\gamma$R2 expression intrinsically impairs naive $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell differentiation into IFN-$\gamma$-producing Th1 effectors *in vitro* and *in vivo* (DTH, IgG2a switching, *L. major* resistance) without impairing T-cell proliferation or skewing toward Th2. `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated across APC-free *in vitro* cultures, CFSE tracking, and three *in vivo* challenge models across two founder lines.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagents & Primers:** Transgene genotyping and RT-PCR primers distinguishing endogenous vs. transgenic `IFN-`$\gamma\text{R2}$ (lines 26, 63), antibody clones (`XMG1.2`, `11B11`, `Mel-14`), cytokine doses, and immunization schedules are clearly specified. Exact mouse numbers ($n$) per group in Figures 4 and 6 are not explicitly stated in the text (stated as 3–5 mice/genotype for KLH in line 81).
