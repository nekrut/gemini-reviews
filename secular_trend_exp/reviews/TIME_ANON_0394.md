### 1. Executive Summary
This computational comparative genomics study adapts stylometric text-analysis principles (Bissell's length-weighted cumulative sum, scaling oligonucleotide relative abundance differences $\delta(w,s)$ by ORF length standard error) to evaluate gene-by-gene compositional divergence across the two sequenced genomes of *Helicobacter pylori* (strains J99 and 26695). Using dinucleotide (DNS), tetranucleotide (TNS), and hexanucleotide (HNS) signatures computed in $O(N)$ time via hashing and loop flattening, the authors rank all coding ORFs in both strains and inspect pairwise local alignments (`Lalign`) of top outliers. They identify 53 genes in J99 and 60 genes in 26695 exceeding $>2\text{ SD}$ from the genomic mean by DNS, including known pathogenicity island (*cag* PAI), vacuolating cytotoxin (*vacA* paralogs), and restriction-modification (R-M) genes, while showing that HNS captures numerous strain-specific R-M genes missed by DNS (e.g., 11 R-M genes in the top 50 HNS of J99). Strikingly, several conserved "core" housekeeping and DNA metabolism genes (*mutS*, *recN*, *ftsK*, *xerD*, *polA*, *rpoB*) also exhibit divergent signatures and localized inter-strain mosaicism, leading the authors to argue that homologous sequence conservation facilitates inter-strain/inter-species recombinational exchange in core genes.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Truncated/Corrupted Mathematical Equations in the Methods Section:** In the Methods (line 286), the mathematical expressions for Bissell's weighted cusum and the ORF-size standard error scaling are missing their core symbols due to typesetting/encoding loss: `"Bissell's weighted cusum (cumulative sum) [ 34 ], , is modified so that..."` and `"using the standard error σ = √( *# ORF )."` Because the exact variance-weighting formula is absent, a reader cannot reconstruct the exact mathematical normalization applied to short vs. long ORFs from the text alone.
* **Lack of Null Model Calibration for Zero-Order vs. Higher-Order Markov Expectations:** Line 284 defines the oligonucleotide odds ratio $p_i$ as "calculated by dividing the frequency of a length-$L$ oligonucleotide by its expected frequency," without specifying whether the expected frequency of a tetranucleotide ($L=4$) or hexanucleotide ($L=6$) is computed from single-nucleotide frequencies ($f_A f_C f_G f_T$, order-0 Markov model), dinucleotide frequencies, or $L-2$ Markov sub-words (Karlin's $\gamma^*/\tau^*$ zero-bias relative abundance), nor whether reverse-complement strand symmetry is symmetrized or computed only on the coding sense strand. For hexanucleotides ($4^6 = 4,096$ words) inside an average bacterial gene of ~900–1,000 bp, most hexamers have observed counts of 0 or 1; thus, raw $|p_i(w) - p_i(s)|$ is dominated by sparse Poisson sampling noise and amino acid / codon bias unless conditioned on lower-order sub-words and ORF length via exact permutation or parametric null simulations.
* **Confounding of Horizontal Gene Transfer (HGT) with Amino Acid Composition, Repetitive Motifs, and Diversifying Selection:** As the authors rightly acknowledge in line 106 for `JHP0476`/`HP0527` (*cag7*), `JHP1300`/`HP1408`, and `JHP0074`/`HP0080` (and readily visible in Tables 1–6 for histidine/glutamine-rich metal-binding proteins `hpn`/`JHP1321` and poly-E-rich `HP0322`), extreme amino acid composition or internal repeats strongly distort oligonucleotide signatures without HGT. Similarly, localized inter-strain divergence between J99 and 26695 in `ftsK`, `mutS`, or `rpoB` (Figure 1) demonstrates intragenic homologous recombination or indel polymorphism within *H. pylori*, but without phylogenetic comparison to an outgroup donor species (e.g., *H. hepaticus*, *Campylobacter jejuni*), inter-strain sequence divergence does not prove inter-species horizontal transfer vs. intra-species allelic mosaicism.
* **Editorial Artifact Left in Table 3:** In Table 3 (row 20, `JHP0931`, `topA 3`), the column for strain 26695 contains an unedited author draft note: `"NAH – check"`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Complementary sensitivity of hexanucleotide (HNS) vs. dinucleotide (DNS) signatures for R-M systems:** `[Well Supported]` — The empirical demonstration that HNS ranks 11 restriction-modification genes in the top 50 of strain J99 (many absent in 26695, such as `JHP1422` ranked #3 by HNS vs. #319 by DNS, or `JHP1411` ranked #13 by HNS vs. #857 by DNS) is biologically compelling, both because R-M genes are frequently horizontally transferred and because bacteria actively select against self-recognition palindromic hexamers/tetramers (restriction avoidance).
* **Intragenic mosaicism and homologous recombination in core housekeeping/repair genes (*mutS*, *recN*, *ftsK*, *xerD*, *polA*, *rpoB*):** `[Well Supported]` — In naturally transformable, panmictic *H. pylori*, conserved flanking sequences in housekeeping genes provide ideal substrates for homologous recombination, creating mosaic alleles and cautioning against naive single-gene bacterial phylogenies.
* **Directionality of insertion vs. deletion inferred from compositional divergence (`HP0078`/`HP0079` vs. `JHP0073`):** `[Moderately Supported]` — Using normal genomic signature in the fused/flanking segments (`JHP0073` rank #799, `HP0079` rank #751) versus high divergence in the extra segment (`HP0078` rank #68) to polarize the event as an insertion into 26695 is a clever heuristic, though outgroup comparison would provide definitive polarization.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Extensive Tabular Rankings:** Tables 1–6 comprehensively report the top ranked ORFs across DNS, TNS, and HNS for both J99 and 26695 with cross-referenced locus tags (`JHP#` and `HP#`).
* **Missing Equations and Dead FTP/HTTP Links:** The missing mathematical formulas in line 286 and reliance on legacy institutional personal URLs (`ftp://ftp.dcs.warwick.ac.uk/...` and `http://www.molbiol.ox.ac.uk/~saunders/`) without archived source code or exact mathematical definitions limit standalone re-implementation.
