### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This molecular virology and RNA biochemistry study demonstrates that specific stem-loop structures previously identified as RNA recombination hot spots in turnip crinkle virus (TCV) genomic RNA (`motif3-hairpin` and `hairpin4`) and its parasitic satellite RNA C (`motif1-hairpin`) function as *cis*-acting RNA replication enhancers. Combining *in vivo* replication and stability assays in *Arabidopsis thaliana* protoplasts with *in vitro* cell-free transcription assays using partially purified template-dependent TCV RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) and S1 nuclease protection, the authors show that: (1) deleting `motif1-hairpin` from sat-RNA C (`Δmot1`) reduces monomeric plus-strand accumulation to 8.0% of wild-type at 44 h.p.i. ($n=7$ independent experiments) without altering in vivo RNA decay kinetics in the absence of TCV helper virus; (2) `motif1-hairpin` stimulates replication in both forward and reverse orientations (`rev/mot1` reaches 66–80% of wt) and when relocated 73 nt upstream (`Mot1-Nco`, 4-fold above `Δmot1`); (3) inserting `motif1-hairpin`, `motif3-hairpin`, or `hairpin4` into the poorly replicating natural recombinant `sat-RNA CX` stimulates accumulation 5- to 10-fold individually and additively up to 25-fold when all three hairpins are combined (`CXM1+M3+H4`), without changing RNA stability; (4) in cell-free TCV RdRp assays, `motif1-hairpin` and `motif3-hairpin` stimulate S1-resistant *de novo* transcription from a downstream 12-nt minimal plus-strand initiation promoter by 10- to 13-fold, whereas three artificial stable hairpins (`GC+pr`, `AU+pr`, `ministem+pr`) inhibit transcription; and (5) placing single-stranded `CCC`/`CC` initiator sequences 3' of `motif1-hairpin` (`CCA4+Mot1`) is sufficient to reconstitute *de novo* RNA synthesis in the absence of a canonical promoter, supporting a modular bipartite model of viral RNA promoters (hairpin RdRp-recruitment enhancer + 3' single-stranded `CC/CCC` initiator).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Tight integration of *in vivo* protoplast genetics and *in vitro* RdRp biochemistry:** Every major *in vivo* observation in *Arabidopsis* protoplasts (deletion, orientation independence, additive gain-of-function in `sat-RNA CX`, and plus-strand asymmetry) is paired with direct biochemical testing in a cell-free TCV RdRp system using S1 nuclease treatment to distinguish genuine *de novo* initiation from 3'-terminal self-priming extension.
- **Rigorous intracellular RNA stability controls:** To rule out the trivial explanation that stem-loops simply protect viral RNA from cellular ribonucleases, the authors measure input RNA decay time-courses (0–12 h.p.i.) in protoplasts lacking TCV helper virus and include a minus-PEG inoculation control (proving that detected RNA is genuinely intracellular rather than extracellularly adsorbed).
- **Crucial negative structural controls (`GC+pr`, `AU+pr`, `ministem+pr`) and synthetic promoter reconstitution (`CCA4+Mot1`):** Testing three synthetic tetraloop hairpins proves that RdRp stimulation is sequence/structure-specific rather than a generic physical property of any RNA stem-loop, and the `CCA4+Mot1` construct elegantly demonstrates that a recruitment hairpin plus single-stranded 3' `CC/CCC` motifs suffices to direct *de novo* initiation.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Reliance on "data not shown" / "unpublished results" for several auxiliary controls:** S1 nuclease digestion gels for Figure 7, the minus-PEG control for Figure 3A, stability blots for the `sat-RNA CX` derivatives, and full-length product gels for `GC+pr`/`AU+pr` fused to an alternative promoter are described in the text as "data not shown" or "unpublished results".
- **Absence of direct biophysical binding ($K_d$) measurements (EMSA / filter binding / UV crosslinking):** While prior template-competition assays are cited and the *in vitro* transcription stimulation strongly implies RdRp recruitment, direct equilibrium binding affinities ($K_d$) of purified p88/p28 RdRp to `motif1-hairpin` vs. `mutmot1` or `GC` hairpins are not directly measured here.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** The recombination hot-spot hairpins (`motif1-hairpin`, `motif3-hairpin`, `hairpin4`) act as orientation- and position-tolerant *cis*-acting RNA replication enhancers in vivo without altering intrinsic RNA stability. `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated across deletion, inversion, translocation, and additive `sat-RNA CX` gain-of-function series in protoplasts.
- **Claim 2:** `motif1-hairpin` and `motif3-hairpin` directly stimulate *de novo* RNA synthesis by TCV RdRp 10- to 13-fold in vitro, whereas artificial hairpins are inhibitory. `[Well Supported]` — Directly shown in normalized denaturing PAGE densitometry (Fig. 7).
- **Claim 3:** TCV replication promoters have a modular bipartite architecture consisting of an RdRp-recruiting hairpin enhancer and a 3' single-stranded `CC/CCC` initiator element. `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by synthetic reconstitution of *de novo* initiation at three of four `CC/CCC` sites in `CCA4+Mot1` (Fig. 8).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Constructs & Sequences:** Exact nucleotide sequences, secondary structures, coordinates, and stoichiometric normalizations (adjusting densitometry for template molarity and number of incorporated radiolabeled UTP residues) are clearly presented across Figures 1–8.
