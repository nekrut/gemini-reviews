### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
`TIME_ANON_0897` is a published compilation of 15 short conference poster abstracts (`P-086` through `P-100`, categorized under *Replicative cycles*) from an international virology meeting. The abstracts span diverse RNA and DNA viral systems, including:
- `P-086`: Influenza A virus NEP (NS2) dose-dependent inhibition of a Pol I–driven CAT minigenome replicon.
- `P-087`: *Saccharomyces cerevisiae* 20S and 23S narnavirus positive-strand RNA complexes with their respective RdRp proteins (`p91` and `p104`) in a 1:1 stoichiometry, mapped to 5' and 3' terminal cis-signals.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **High breadth of mechanistic molecular virology (`P-086` to `P-100`):** Several abstracts report significant technical milestones of their era, notably the vaccinia-based full-length reverse genetics rescue of HCoV-229E (`P-097`), helper-free lyssavirus rescue (`P-092`), and direct biophysical comparison of Flaviviridae NS5B polymerases (`P-099`).
- **Clear experimental logic within individual summaries:** Most abstracts clearly specify the assay system, viral strain, host cell line, and directional outcome (e.g., `P-094` reports explicit cohort denominators: 99 patients, 20 HGV-RNA+, 23 anti-E2+, 95.2% persistence over 3 years).

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Non-primary conference abstract format:** No experimental protocols, replicate counts, error bars, or statistical tests are included, precluding independent verification of quantitative claims.
- **Internal ambiguity in `P-091` (Influenza C CM2 vs. Influenza A M2):** The abstract states that the `M2-H37A` mutant conducts $\text{Na}^+$ and $\text{K}^+$ instead of only $\text{H}^+$ and then states *"These results are inconsistent with the hypothesis that M2 channel is the counterion channel reducing the membrane potential caused by V-ATPase in endosome, although the function of CM2 channel has yet to be definitively proven"*, without presenting the actual reversal potentials, current-voltage ($I\text{–}V$) curves, or amantadine sensitivity data for CM2.
- **Overinterpretation of ELISA vs. RT-PCR discordance in `P-098`:** Observing 96% seropositivity by 3rd-generation HCV ELISA vs. 64% viremia by nested RT-PCR in 100 clinical sera is a standard biological reflection of spontaneous viral clearance (~25–35% of HCV infections clear RNA while remaining anti-HCV IgG positive) or intermittent low-titer viremia, rather than necessarily indicating false-positive ELISA results as implied.
