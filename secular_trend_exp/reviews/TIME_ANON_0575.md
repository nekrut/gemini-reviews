### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This primary experimental immunology and microbiology study identifies four novel superantigen (SAg) genes from *Streptococcus pyogenes*: `spe-g`, `spe-h`, and the partial ORF `spe-j` mined by TBlastN from the University of Oklahoma *S. pyogenes* M1 shotgun genome database, plus `smez-2` isolated by PCR from strain 2035 (sharing 95% nucleotide / 91.9% amino acid identity with `smez`). The authors clone, express, and purify recombinant SMEZ, SMEZ-2, SPE-G, and SPE-H in *E. coli*, and perform a comprehensive biochemical and functional characterization comprising human PBL and five-strain murine splenocyte mitogenicity (`P50` values, Table I), anchored multiprimer PCR TCR $V\beta$ profiling across 17 human $V\beta$ families (Table II), V$\beta$8+ Jurkat cell stimulation (resolving the identity of the long-sought `SPE-X` contaminant), homology structure modeling (InsightII), $\text{Zn}^{2+}$/EDTA chelation assays, Scatchard radioligand binding kinetics on HLA-DR1+ LG-2 cells (Table III), and a full reciprocal cross-competition binding matrix against reference toxins SEA, SEB, SPE-C, and TSST (Fig. 10). The experimental design is thorough and self-correcting (explicitly eliminating pipette carryover that inflated potency in prior literature). Minor limitations include the absence of donor/replicate dispersion metrics in Tables I–III and reliance on graphical Scatchard linearization for multiphasic binding curves.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
* **Omission of Donor Sample Sizes and Variance Estimates in Proliferation and $V\beta$ Tables (Tables I–II):** In Materials and Methods ("Toxin Proliferation Assay"), human PBLs were purified from "a healthy donor." Neither Table I ($P_{50}$ proliferation potencies in human PBLs and 5 murine strains) nor Table II (percentage of TCR $V\beta$ enrichment) reports standard deviations, standard errors, or the number of independent human blood donors or biological replicates tested. Because human HLA class II polymorphisms and baseline TCR $V\beta$ repertoires vary across donors, reporting single-donor point estimates without dispersion intervals limits precision.
* **Scatchard Linearization of Multiphasic Binding Curves (Figure 8 and Table III):** To estimate high-affinity ($K_{d1} = 15\text{–}65\text{ nM}$) and low-affinity ($K_{d2} = 1\text{–}2\ \mu\text{M}$) dissociation constants on LG-2 cells, the authors use classical Scatchard plots (bound/free vs. bound) of duplicate $^{125}\text{I}$-toxin competition series. Scatchard transformation distorts experimental error structure and is subjective when decomposing curved multiphasic plots into two linear segments, compared to direct nonlinear least-squares fitting of a two-site competitive binding model.
* **Random Loop Generation in Homology Modeling:** In Figure 6, loop conformations between structurally conserved regions (including the $\beta 1\text{–}\beta 2$ loop corresponding to the generic HLA-DR1 $\alpha$-chain binding site) were generated by "random choice" in InsightII/Homology. The authors appropriately note in the text that no conformational conclusions can be drawn from the modeled loops, relying instead on primary sequence hydrophilicity and SEB/TSST non-competition.
* **Minor Typographical Errors in Sequence Numbering and Figure Legends:**
  1. In Results ("MHC Class II Binding", line 198), the $\beta 1\text{–}\beta 2$ loop residues of SMEZ-2 are listed as *"I25, D26, F27, K28, T279 and S30"*, where `T279` is a typo for `T29`.
  2. In the Discussion (line 251), the third zinc-binding ligand is stated as coming from *"the $\beta 9$ strand (in SMEZ, SMEZ-2, and SPE-G) or the $\beta 10$ strand (in SPE-G)"*, where the first `SPE-G` is a typo for `SPE-H` (as correctly stated in Results line 196: D160 in SPE-H and H162 in SMEZ-2 come from $\beta 9$, whereas H167 in SPE-G comes from $\beta 10$).
  3. In the Figure 4 legend, $[^{3}\text{H}]\text{thymidine}$ incorporation is described as being *"counted on a gamma counter"*, whereas the Methods section correctly states it was counted on a liquid scintillation counter ($\beta$-emitter).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality (STRENGTHS)
* **Discovery and Functional Validation of Four Novel Streptococcal Superantigens: `[Well Supported]`**  
  Three independent PCR subclones per toxin were sequenced on both strands to exclude Taq errors, recombinant proteins were purified to homogeneity on SDS-PAGE and IEF (Fig. 3), and picogram-to-femtogram mitogenic potency on primary human T cells (down to $P_{50} = 0.02\text{ pg/ml}$ for rSMEZ-2) was demonstrated with rigorous pipette-tip changes at every 1:5 dilution step.
* **Identification of SMEZ and SMEZ-2 as the Elusive V$\beta$8-Stimulating `SPE-X` Activity: `[Well Supported]`**  
  Both anchored PCR $V\beta$ profiling on primary human PBLs (showing 20.7% and 36.0% V$\beta$8.1 enrichment for rSMEZ and rSMEZ-2 vs. 8.7% resting, Table II) and functional IL-2 bioassays on V$\beta$8+ Jurkat cells ($P_{50} = 0.2$ and $0.02\text{ ng/ml}$, Fig. 5) conclusively explain the potent V$\beta$8-mitogenic activity previously misattributed to contaminated SPE-B/SPE-A preparations.
* **Natural Allelic Divergence Between SMEZ and SMEZ-2 Alters V$\beta$2.1 Specificity: `[Well Supported]`**  
  The 17-residue divergence between SMEZ (strain M1, pI 6.0–6.5) and SMEZ-2 (strain 2035, pI 7.0–8.0)—concentrated in the pentapeptide exchange `96-EEPMS-100` $\rightarrow$ `KTSIL` and $\beta 4\text{–}\beta 5$ loop substitutions—selectively ablates V$\beta$2.1 stimulation (8.4% vs. 1.0%) while preserving V$\beta$8.1 and V$\beta$4.1 targeting.
* **Zinc-Dependent MHC Class II $\beta$-Chain Binding and Hierarchical Peptide-Restricted Competition: `[Well Supported]`**  
  Complete abrogation of $^{125}\text{I}$-toxin binding to HLA-DR1+ LG-2 cells by 1 mM EDTA and full restoration by 2 mM $\text{ZnCl}_2$ (Fig. 7), combined with lack of competition against $\alpha$-chain-binding SEB and asymmetric cross-competition (`(SPE-C, SMEZ) > SMEZ-2 > SPE-H > SPE-G`, Fig. 10), strongly support zinc-coordinated HLA-DR1 $\beta$-chain binding across overlapping peptide-defined class II subsets.
