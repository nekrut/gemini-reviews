### 1. Executive Summary
This candidate-gene case-control association study in a Hungarian cohort (272 COPD cases and 301 smoking controls with $\ge 15$ pack-years) evaluates two coding SNPs in microsomal epoxide hydrolase (`EPHX1`: exon 3 `Tyr113His` [`rs1051740`] and exon 4 `His139Arg` [`rs2234922`]) and three exonic SNPs in peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma (`PPARG`: `Pro12Ala` [`rs1801282`], `Pro113Gln` [`rs1800571`, monomorphic], and synonymous `His447His` [`rs3856806`]), supplemented by immunohistochemistry and TaqMan RT-qPCR of `PPARG` in bronchoalveolar lavage alveolar macrophages (`AM`, $n = 5$ cases and $n = 5$ controls). While all single-SNP allelic and genotypic comparisons for both `EPHX1` and `PPARG` are statistically null in unadjusted analyses, the authors report nominal associations ($P = 0.021\text{–}0.041$, uncorrected for multiple testing) for imputed "slow" `EPHX1` metabolic phenotype ($\text{OR} = 1.64, P = 0.021$), for `PPARG` `His447His` when mutually adjusted for `Pro12Ala` in multivariable logistic regression ($\text{OR} = 1.85, P = 0.021$), and for the rare `PPARG` `GC` (`12Ala`–`447His`) haplotype ($\text{OR} = 0.51, P = 0.035$). Forensic audit of the manuscript tables reveals severe statistical and arithmetic errors—including an erroneous $P = 0.781$ in Table 1 for a highly significant sex imbalance ($\chi^2 = 14.31, P = 0.00015$), a direct violation of the stated `FEV1%` inclusion criteria in Table 1, a 30% percentage typo in Table 2, and collinearity/multiple-testing inflation in the `PPARG` regression models.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Glaring Statistical Error in Table 1 Sex Distribution ($P = 0.781$ Reported for $P = 0.00015$):** In Table 1 (line 82), the proportion of males is `190 / 272 (69.85%)` in Cases versus `164 / 301 (54.48%)` in Controls. A standard $2 \times 2$ Pearson $\chi^2$ test on $\begin{pmatrix} 190 & 82 \\ 164 & 137 \end{pmatrix}$ yields **$\chi^2 = 14.31, P = 0.000155$** (Fisher's exact $P = 0.00016$). Yet Table 1 prints **`P value = 0.781`**, concealing a highly significant 15.4-percentage-point sex imbalance between cases and controls!
- **Contradiction Between Stated Inclusion Criteria and Table 1 Clinical Data:**
  1. **`FEV1% predicted` in Cases:** Methods (line 40) explicitly states: *"Inclusion criteria for COPD subjects were age 40 to 65 years old. Patients must have predicted value of forced expiratory volume at 1 second (FEV1) **50%-80%** and FEV1/FVC% <70% (stage 2 according to GOLD criteria)."* Yet in **Table 1 (line 88)**, the mean $\pm$ SD of `FEV1% predicted` in Cases is **`47.17 (±13.69)`**! If the mean is `47.17%` with an SD of `13.69%`, more than half of the COPD cohort has `FEV1% < 50%` (GOLD Stage 3 and Stage 4), directly violating the stated inclusion criteria (and the age mean $\pm$ SD of `63.87 ± 8.96` also indicates many subjects were $>65$ years old, violating the `40 to 65 years old` criterion).
  2. **`FEV1/FVC%` SD in Controls:** Methods (line 40) requires Control subjects to have `FEV1/FVC% >= 80%`, yet Table 1 (line 90) reports Control `FEV1/FVC%` as `87.10 (±35.24)`—an SD of `35.24%` is impossible if all controls were $\ge 80\%$ unless severe data entry outliers or unit errors were present.
- **Genotype Percentage Typo in Table 2 (Line 105):** For `EPHX1` `rs2234922` (`His139Arg`) in Cases ($N = 169 + 92 + 8 = 269$), the genotype counts and percentages are listed as `AA: 169 (62.8%)`, `AG: 92 (64.2%)`, `GG: 8 (2.9%)`. These percentages sum to **$129.9\%$** because $92 / 269 = 34.2\%$ was misprinted as `64.2%`.
- **Collinearity Artifact in `PPARG` Two-SNP Logistic Regression and Inconsistent Covariates:**
  - In single-locus analysis (Table 4), neither `Pro12Ala` (`C/G`, Case MAF `0.126` vs Control MAF `0.136`, allelic $\text{OR} = 0.92$) nor `His447His` (`C/T`, Case MAF `0.138` vs Control MAF `0.118`, allelic $\text{OR} = 1.18, P = 0.36$) is significantly associated with COPD.
  - Only when both correlated SNPs ($D' = 0.673, r^2 = 0.42$) are entered simultaneously into the same logistic regression model (`adjusted for both SNPs`, lines 11, 131, 172) does `His447His` jump to $\text{OR} = 1.853\ (P = 0.021)$ while `Pro12Ala` shifts in the opposite direction to $\text{OR} = 0.679\ (P = 0.145)$. Because the `G` (`12Ala`) and `T` (`447His`) minor alleles frequently reside on the same `GT` haplotype (`freq = 0.098` in cases vs `0.085` in controls, Table 5), conditioning `His447His` on `Pro12Ala` estimates a collinear contrast rather than a marginal SNP effect. Furthermore, the text contradicts itself on whether sex (`gender`) was included in the model: Abstract (line 11) and Results (line 131) state the model was adjusted for *"both SNPs, age and pack-years"* (omitting gender, despite the massive sex imbalance in Table 1!), whereas Methods (line 69) and Discussion (line 172) state *"both SNPs, age, sex and pack-years."*
- **Absence of Multiple-Testing Correction and Post-Hoc Phenotype Grouping:** The authors explicitly decline to apply any multiple-testing correction across the 4 polymorphic SNPs, 2 haplotype tables, and 4-category imputed `EPHX1` activity phenotype (`Normal`, `Slow`, `Very slow`, `Rapid`, Table 3). Notably, in Table 3, while the imputed `Slow` phenotype is higher in cases (`30.3%` vs `20.2%`, $\text{OR} = 1.64, P = 0.021$), the `Very slow` phenotype (`His113/His113` + `His139/His139`) trends in the *opposite* direction (`3.5%` in cases vs `6.2%` in controls, $\text{OR} = 0.62$), contradicting a monotonic dose-response relationship with `EPHX1` enzymatic activity.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`PPARG` mRNA and protein are highly expressed in human CD68+/DC-SIGN+ alveolar macrophages (comparable to subcutaneous adipose tissue and significantly higher than whole lung or peripheral blood monocytes), with no difference in mRNA expression between COPD patients and controls ($n = 5$ per group).* `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by double immunohistochemistry/immunofluorescence (Fig. 1) and TaqMan RT-qPCR (Fig. 2).
- **Claim 2:** *Imputed "slow" `EPHX1` activity ($\text{OR} = 1.64, P = 0.021$), the `PPARG` `His447His` minor allele ($\text{OR} = 1.85, P = 0.021$), and the `PPARG` `GC` haplotype ($\text{OR} = 0.51, P = 0.035$) significantly modify COPD risk.* `[Fragile]` — None of the individual SNPs are significant in marginal tests, the `Very slow` `EPHX1` phenotype trends in the opposite direction of the `Slow` phenotype, the `His447His` OR is induced by co-adjusting for a linked SNP in a cohort with an unacknowledged sex imbalance, and none of the nominal $P \approx 0.02\text{–}0.04$ values survive multiple-testing correction.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Positive:** TaqMan RT-qPCR primer and probe sequences for `PPARG` and `cyclophilin A` are provided in full, and genotype counts, HWE $p$-values, and haplotype frequencies are tabulated across Tables 2–5.
- **Missing:** The clinical data inconsistencies in Table 1 (`Male P = 0.781`, `FEV1%` mean `47.17%` vs `50–80%` inclusion criterion, `FEV1/FVC%` SD `35.24%`), the percentage typo in Table 2 (`64.2%`), and the exact specification of how genotype combinations were mapped to the four `EPHX1` activity classes in Table 3 require correction.
