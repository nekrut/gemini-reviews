### 1. Executive Summary
This human clinical immunology study examines the in vivo temporal kinetics of mRNA transcription for the CC chemokines *MCP-3* (CCL7) and *RANTES* (CCL5) in relation to leukocyte subset infiltration during the allergen-induced cutaneous late-phase reaction (LPR). Six sensitized atopic human subjects received intradermal forearm injections of Timothy grass pollen (*Phleum pratense*, 30 BU) at three sites (biopsied by 4-mm punch at 6, 24, and 48 h post-challenge) and a diluent control injection at a fourth site (biopsied at 24 h). Serial 6-µm cryostat sections were analyzed by alkaline phosphatase anti-alkaline phosphatase (APAAP) immunocytochemistry (ICC) for total T cells ($\text{CD3}^+$), helper T cells ($\text{CD4}^+$), cytotoxic T cells ($\text{CD8}^+$), total eosinophils ($\text{MBP}^+$ / BMK-13), activated eosinophils ($\text{EG2}^+$), and macrophages ($\text{CD68}^+$), and by radioactive ($^{35}\text{S}$-labeled) in situ hybridization (ISH) using antisense and sense riboprobes for *MCP-3* and *RANTES*. Blinded dual-observer cell counts across 6, 24, and 48 h (analyzed via Friedman's non-parametric repeated-measures test) revealed that *MCP-3* $\text{mRNA}^+$ cells peaked early at 6 h ($P = 0.003$) and progressively declined at 24 and 48 h, paralleling the early 6-h peak of $\text{MBP}^+$ and $\text{EG2}^+$ eosinophils ($P < 0.001$). Conversely, *RANTES* $\text{mRNA}^+$ cells were elevated at 6 h, peaked at 24 h ($P = 0.005$), and declined slightly at 48 h, paralleling $\text{CD3}^+$, $\text{CD4}^+$, and $\text{CD8}^+$ T-cell accumulation ($P = 0.002\text{--}0.014$), while $\text{CD68}^+$ macrophages continued rising through 48 h ($P = 0.004$).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Within-Subject Longitudinal Design, Blinding, and Histological Controls:**
  - Each of the $N = 6$ atopic volunteers served as their own internal control across four punch biopsies (diluent + 6, 24, and 48 h allergen), with strict clinical inclusion criteria (medication-free, RAST-positive $6.3\text{--}100\text{ IU/ml}$, skin-prick wheal $>5\text{ mm}$).
  - All ICC and ISH slides were scored blind in a coded random order by two observers across 4–6 fields ($0.202\text{ mm}^2/\text{field}$) on at least two sections per biopsy, achieving an inter-observer/intra-biopsy mean coefficient of variation $<5\%$.
  - Stringent ISH specificity controls were performed on serial sections: $^{35}\text{S}$-labeled sense riboprobes and RNase A pretreatment prior to antisense hybridization both yielded zero silver grain clusters (Figure 2C–D), and diluent-challenged skin sites showed negligible baseline signal (Figure 2E–F).
- **Appropriate Non-Parametric Statistical Analysis:** Given the small sample size ($N = 6$) and repeated-measures design (4 conditions per subject), using Friedman's non-parametric two-way analysis of variance by ranks across the four biopsy conditions (diluent, 6 h, 24 h, 48 h) is statistically appropriate and avoids normality assumptions.
- **Methodological Limitations:**
  - **Single-Timepoint Diluent Control:** While allergen-injected sites were biopsied at 6, 24, and 48 h, the diluent control was biopsied only at 24 h (understandably, to limit subjects to four punch biopsies rather than six). Consequently, transient mechanical/injection trauma responses at 6 h are not separately controlled by a 6-h diluent biopsy.
  - **Absence of Post-Hoc Pairwise Comparisons & Correlation Power:** Friedman's test provides an omnibus $p$-value across all 4 conditions ($P = 0.003$ for *MCP-3*, $P = 0.005$ for *RANTES*), but no post-hoc pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (e.g., 6 h vs. 24 h for *MCP-3* or *RANTES*) are reported to formally prove that the 6-h *MCP-3* value is significantly higher than the 24-h value. Furthermore, with $N = 6$, rank correlation tests between chemokine $\text{mRNA}^+$ counts and infiltrating leukocyte counts are severely underpowered; the authors commendably report that "No significant correlation between the number of cells expressing MCP-3 or RANTES and the number of infiltrating inflammatory cells was observed."
  - **Lack of Co-Localization / Double-Labeling:** Because ICC and ISH were performed on separate serial 6-µm sections rather than combined ICC+ISH double-labeling on the same section, the cellular identity of the *MCP-3* and *RANTES* $\text{mRNA}^+$ cells (e.g., resident endothelial/fibroblast/epithelial cells vs. infiltrating macrophages, T cells, or eosinophils) could not be determined, which the authors explicitly acknowledge in the Discussion.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** "In contrast to diluent controls, allergen provoked a significant increase in $\text{mRNA}^+$ cells for MCP-3, which peaked at 6 h and progressively declined at 24 and 48 h. This paralleled the kinetics of total ($\text{MBP}^+$) and activated ($\text{EG2}^+$) eosinophil infiltration." `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by Figure 1 and Figure 2 across all 6 subjects ($P = 0.003$ and $P < 0.001$).
- **Claim 2:** "The allergen-induced expression of cells $\text{mRNA}^+$ for RANTES was also clearly demonstrable at 6 h. However, the numbers were maximal at 24 h... paralleling the kinetics of infiltration of $\text{CD3}^+$, $\text{CD4}^+$, and $\text{CD8}^+$ T cells." `[Well Supported]` — Supported by Figure 1 ($P = 0.005$ for *RANTES*, $P = 0.002\text{--}0.014$ for T-cell subsets).
- **Claim 3:** "While these observations give no definite information as to the cellular source of the chemokine mRNA, they are compatible with the hypotheses that MCP-3 and RANTES are important in eosinophil and T cell recruitment, or that these cells are a possible source of these chemokines." `[Well Supported]` — The Discussion is balanced and scrupulously avoids overstating temporal co-occurrence as proof of causal chemotaxis or cellular origin.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Protocol Detail:** Intradermal allergen dosing (30 BU in 0.02 ml), paraformaldehyde/sucrose cryoprotection, proteinase K/iodoacetamide/N-ethylmaleimide/acetic anhydride pre-hybridization blocking, hybridization stringency ($55^\circ\text{C}$ in 50% formamide, RNase A digestion, $0.1\times\text{SSC}$ wash at $65^\circ\text{C}$), and antibody clones (BMK-13, EG2, CD3, CD4, CD8, CD68) are documented with high precision. Providing the subject-level count table (for the $N = 6$ individuals) alongside the summary bar graphs in Figure 1 would allow full re-verification of the non-parametric test statistics.
