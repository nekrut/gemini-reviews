### 1. Executive Summary
This study challenges the prevailing dogma that gammaherpesvirus latency and long-term persistence depend exclusively on resting B lymphocytes by investigating murine gammaherpesvirus 68 (MHV-68) persistence in B-cell-deficient ($\mu\text{MT}$) and immunocompetent (`C57BL/6`) mice. Combining extensive right-ventricular lung perfusion, nested and quantitative competitive PCR (QC-PCR, sensitive to 1 viral genome copy per 100 ng host DNA), *in situ*-lysis Gardella gel electrophoresis coupled with slice-by-slice PCR (`Gardella-PCR`), *in vivo* pharmacological blockade of lytic viral DNA replication with `4'-S-EtdU`, dual *in situ* hybridization (viral latency tRNAs 1–4 vs. lytic `tk + gH` transcripts) and cytokeratin immunohistochemistry, *in vivo* T-cell subset depletion, and bidirectional adoptive transfer of T-depleted splenocytes, the authors demonstrate that: (1) MHV-68 persists long-term (up to 12 months p.i.) in the lungs of both intact and $\mu\text{MT}$ mice; (2) both circular episomal (latent) and linear (lytic) viral genomes reside in persistent lungs, and 28-day treatment with `4'-S-EtdU` eliminates linear genomes while preserving circular episomes; (3) latent viral tRNA transcripts localize specifically to cytokeratin-positive pulmonary epithelial cells in the absence of lytic `tk/gH` expression; (4) persistent lung virus in $\mu\text{MT}$ mice reactivates to high infectious titers upon $\text{CD4}^+/\text{CD8}^+$ T-cell depletion; and (5) virus traffics bidirectionally between pulmonary epithelial reservoirs and splenic B cells.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Rigorous Exclusion of Blood-Borne Lymphocyte Contamination:** To prove that viral DNA detected in the lungs of immunocompetent `C57BL/6` mice at 12 months p.i. was not merely an artifact of circulating latently infected B cells, the authors perfused the pulmonary vasculature with 30 mL PBS via the right ventricle and simultaneously assayed lung, spleen, peripheral blood, bone marrow, liver, and kidney by single-copy nested PCR and LI-COR QC-PCR. Three of five mice had robust lung and spleen viral loads ($1\text{--}1,000$ copies/100 ng DNA) with zero detectable viral DNA in blood, bone marrow, liver, or kidney (Figures 1–2).
- **Orthogonal Physical and Pharmacological Proof of True Episomal Latency (`Gardella-PCR` + `4'-S-EtdU`):** Because Southern blot Gardella gels lack the sensitivity to detect low-abundance persistent genomes *in vivo*, the authors validated a slice-by-slice `Gardella-PCR` assay using latently/lytically infected `S11` B-lymphoma cells and acutely infected `C127` epithelial cells (Figure 3). Applying this assay to disaggregated lung cells alongside a 28-day *in vivo* course of the viral thymidine kinase-dependent chain terminator `4'-S-EtdU` ($1\text{ mg/mouse/day}$) demonstrated that while untreated persistent lungs harbor both circular episomes and low-level linear DNA, `4'-S-EtdU` completely clears linear replicating DNA while leaving circular episomal genomes intact in $4/4$ $\mu\text{MT}$ mice (Figure 4D). This rules out "slow lytic replication" as the sole mechanism of pulmonary persistence.
- **Cellular Localization and Functional Reactivation Controls:** Serial-section *in situ* hybridization with antisense vs. sense riboprobes for latency-associated viral tRNAs 1–4 and lytic `tk + gH` mRNAs, co-stained for epithelial cytokeratin (`MNF116`), cleanly identified small foci of latently infected airway epithelial cells at 54 days p.i. (Figure 5). Moreover, *in vivo* depletion of $\text{CD8}^+$ or $\text{CD4}^+ + \text{CD8}^+$ T cells (`>99%` depletion by FACS) in antibody-deficient $\mu\text{MT}$ mice at 54 days p.i. recovered infectious plaque-forming virus from the lungs, proving that the persistent pulmonary viral genomes are replication-competent rather than defective.
- **Minor Formatting Omission in the Digitized Text:** The Results section refers to `Table 1` for the exact lung plaque titers recovered after CD4/CD8 T-cell depletion at 54 days p.i., but the rendered markdown file omitted the tabular block for Table 1 (though the experimental design, replicate structure, and comparative outcome are described in the text).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Pulmonary Mucosal Epithelial Cells Serve as a Genuine Latent Reservoir for MHV-68 Independently of B Cells:** `[Well Supported]` Supported by five converging lines of evidence: persistence in B-cell-deficient $\mu\text{MT}$ mice, `4'-S-EtdU`-resistant circular episomes on Gardella gels, tRNA-positive / `tk`-negative cytokeratin-positive epithelial cells *in situ*, infectious recovery upon T-cell depletion, and reseeding of naive adoptively transferred B cells.
- **Bidirectional Trafficking Between Lymphoid (Spleen) and Non-Lymphoid (Lung) Compartments:** `[Well Supported]` Directly demonstrated via reciprocal adoptive transfer experiments (`C57BL/6` infected T-depleted splenocytes into uninfected $\mu\text{MT}$ mice seeding the lung, and naive `C57BL/6` T-depleted splenocytes into 80-day infected $\mu\text{MT}$ mice acquiring splenic infection from the lung; Figure 6).
