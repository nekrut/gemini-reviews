### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This classical cell biology study rigorously tests the "frozen Christmas-tree" hypothesis of mitotic rRNA gene regulation—namely, whether RNA polymerase I (Pol I) complexes that remain bound to chromosomal nucleolus organizing regions (NORs) during mitosis retain stalled nascent pre-rRNA transcripts. Using digoxigenin-labeled antisense and sense riboprobes spanning the 5' ETS, 18S, ITS1, and 28S regions of mouse rDNA in mouse 3T3 and rat kangaroo $\text{PtK}_2$ cells (which harbor a single X-linked NOR per chromosome complement), alongside anti-rRNA mAb $\text{tuRF7}$, the authors demonstrate that mitotic NORs across prophase-to-telophase are devoid of detectable nascent pre-rRNA transcripts, even though Pol I, UBF, DNA topoisomerase I, and pp135 remain brightly localized at the NOR. Crucially, to prove that their fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) assay has the sensitivity to detect single active rDNA transcription units, they calibrate their detection limit using DRB-unfolded nucleolar "necklaces" and light-microscopic FISH/immunofluorescence on *Pleurodeles waltl* oocyte Miller chromatin spreads ($n=69$ and $n=36$ repeat units), demonstrating sensitivity down to $\sim 25$ nascent chains on a single gene. Finally, they show that 5' ETS processing factors (U3 snoRNA, trimethyl-guanosine-capped snoRNAs, and fibrillarin) as well as nucleolin (tested across 3 independent antibodies and 3 species) dissociate from mitotic NORs.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Definitive positive-control calibration of single-gene FISH sensitivity:** A negative result in situ ("no signal at mitotic NORs") is only as strong as the lower limit of detection. By performing FISH and anti-Pol I immunofluorescence on DRB-unfolded nucleolar necklaces (Fig. 5) and directly on *Pleurodeles* Miller chromatin spreads (Fig. 6), and quantitatively comparing the repeat period ($3.34 \pm 0.46\ \mu\text{m}, n=69$ for 28S FISH; $3.12 \pm 0.46\ \mu\text{m}, n=36$ for Pol I IF; active unit length $2.02 \pm 0.38\ \mu\text{m}$) against EM Miller spreads ($2.4\ \mu\text{m}$), the authors prove that $\sim 25$ nascent transcripts on a single rRNA gene yield a bright signal—whereas a $\text{PtK}_2$ NOR clusters $\ge 100$ tandem rRNA gene copies at a single locus.
- **Elimination of cytoplasmic background masking via spacer-specific probes:** Because mature 18S/28S probes label cytoplasmic ribosomes, a weak chromosomal signal could be masked by surrounding cytoplasm. The authors specifically use mouse 5' ETSa and ITS1 spacer riboprobes in 3T3 cells (Fig. 3d, e) to reduce cytoplasmic fluorescence to near zero, confirming that mitotic NORs remain completely dark.
- **Orthogonal validation via nascent RNP processing components and multi-antibody resolution of the nucleolin controversy:** Showing that U3 snoRNA, $\text{m}_3\text{G}$-capped snoRNAs, fibrillarin, and nucleolin (using three independent antibodies across mouse, calf, and *Xenopus* antigens) all vacate the mitotic NOR resolves a longstanding literature discrepancy caused by misidentifying early telophase prenucleolar bodies as metaphase NORs.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Resolution limit for promoter-proximal poised transcripts (<100–650 nt):** As the authors themselves carefully note in the Discussion (line 11), nascent chains shorter than $\sim 100\text{ nt}$ (shielded within the Pol I exit channel) or $<650\text{ nt}$ (prior to 5' ETS terminal ball assembly) could escape both FISH and U3/fibrillarin detection; biochemical nuclear run-on or permanganate footprinting on isolated mitotic chromosomes is not performed here to directly map the exact nucleotide position of the paused Pol I complex.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Mitotic NORs retain Pol I, UBF, topoisomerase I, and pp135, but lose nascent pre-rRNA transcripts ("Christmas trees" are stripped rather than frozen at mitosis). `[Well Supported]` — Compellingly demonstrated by multi-region FISH, anti-rRNA IF, and single-gene sensitivity controls.
- **Claim 2:** Pre-rRNA processing complexes (U3 snoRNA, fibrillarin) and nucleolin dissociate from chromosomal NORs during mitosis. `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated across multiple independent probes, antibodies, and cell lines.
- **Claim 3:** A small population of initiated Pol I complexes may remain poised near the transcription initiation site with very short (<100 nt) chains, reconciling the absence of full Christmas trees with prior Sarkosyl/heparin run-on reactivation data. `[Well Supported]` — Thoughtfully and proportionally framed hypothesis.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
