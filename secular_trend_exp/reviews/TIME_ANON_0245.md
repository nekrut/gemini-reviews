### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript is an archival record of a primary experimental study investigating the induction of nitric oxide (NO) and inducible nitric oxide synthase (iNOS) mRNA in HIV-1-infected human primary monocytes, monocyte–astroglial co-cultures, and postmortem brain tissues from pediatric AIDS patients ($n=6$). Based on the quantitative findings reported in the abstract, HIV-1 infection of human monocytes induces significant NO production ($2\text{--}5\ \mu\text{M}$) and iNOS mRNA expression, peaking at day 5 post-infection in parallel with multinucleated giant cell syncytia and ballooning degeneration.
