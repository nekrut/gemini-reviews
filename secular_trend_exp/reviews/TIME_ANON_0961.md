### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This classic *Journal of Experimental Medicine* study investigates how cutaneous dendritic cells (`DCs`, Langerhans/dermal DCs) and resting memory (`CD45RO+`, `Ki-67-`) T lymphocytes emigrating from organ cultures of split-thickness human skin support productive replication of human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (`HIV-1`) in the complete absence of exogenous mitogens, cytokines (`IL-2`), or foreign antigens. First, the authors validate across 50 split-thickness cadaver skin procurements (<36 h post-mortem) that cadaver skin explants yield reproducible numbers of emigrating leukocytes ($52,000 \pm 5,300$ cells/$\text{cm}^2$, totaling $13 \pm 0.9 \times 10^6$ cells per dermatome strip at day 3–4, virtually identical to fresh surgical reduction mammoplasty skin at $57,000 \pm 16,500$ cells/$\text{cm}^2$) comprising `HLA-DR^hi` DCs (`1:3` ratio with T cells), `CD4+`/`CD8+` `CD45RO+` memory T cells, and spontaneous tightly bound DC–T cell conjugates (`5–15%` of total emigrants).

Using FACS cell sorting (`HLA-DR^hi` large DCs vs. `HLA-DR^-` small T cells vs. `HLA-DR^hi CD3+` conjugates) combined with `anti-CD2` (`OKT11`) immunomagnetic bead depletion of residual conjugated T cells from sorted DCs, calibrated semiquantitative duplex PCR (`SK38/SK39` *gag* + `GH26/GH27` *HLA-DQ* against `ACH-2` standards), micro-reverse transcriptase (`RTase`) assays, and `anti-p24` (`183-H12-5C`) immunocytochemistry, the authors establish three foundational findings:
1. **Productive Replication of Primary Patient Isolates (`NSI` and `SI`):** Unstimulated skin emigrants support robust reverse transcription ($>1,000\text{–}10,000$ *gag* DNA copies per $5 \times 10^4$ cells) and virus production of six primary blood HIV-1 isolates (three non-syncytium-inducing `NSI` strains `Pt 1, 2, 3` and three syncytium-inducing `SI` strains `Pt 4, 5, 6`) as well as prototype macrophage-tropic (`Ba-L`, `ADA`, `SF162`, `JR-CSF`, `JR-FL`) and T-tropic (`IIIB`) strains, provided DC–T cell conjugates are present.
2. **Retention and Transmission of Infectious HIV-1 by Purified Skin DCs After 1.5 Days in Culture:** Purified DCs (`anti-CD2` depleted of T cells) pulsed with HIV-1 for 2 h, washed thoroughly, and cultured alone for 24–39 h show only trace proviral DNA ($\le 100$ copies) and no productive `RTase` release on their own, yet when mixed with unpulsed syngeneic `CD4+` (but not `CD8+`) skin T cells at 39 h, they trigger explosive reverse transcription ($>10^4$ copies) and `p24+` multinucleated syncytia formation.
3. **Requirement for Early Reverse Transcription in DCs (`AZT` Sensitivity):** Treating HIV-pulsed purified DCs with `10 uM AZT` during the 39-h pre-incubation—followed by 6 washes to remove all `AZT` prior to adding unpulsed `CD4+` T cells (rigorously verified by a carryover control in which fresh `HIV-1 Ba-L` added after washing replicates normally, `Fig. 6B`)—completely abolishes subsequent viral transmission to `CD4+` T cells.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### Strengths
1. **Stringent Purification of Cutaneous DCs (`FACS` + `anti-CD2` Magnetic Depletion):** Because skin DCs naturally form tight conjugates with memory T cells that can co-sort in the large forward-scatter `HLA-DR^hi` gate, the authors add a second purification step—depleting `CD2+` T cells with `OKT11` + sheep anti-mouse IgG magnetic beads—and functionally verify T-cell removal by showing that `anti-CD2`-depleted DCs can no longer support productive HIV-1 replication unless exogenous sorted `CD4+` T cells are added back (`Fig. 3`, `Fig. 4`, `Fig. 5`).
2. **Definitive `AZT` Carryover Control (`Fig. 6B`):** A major risk in testing whether `AZT` during the 39-h DC incubation blocks subsequent transmission to T cells is that trace residual `AZT` might directly inhibit reverse transcription after T cells are added. In `Figure 6B` (`*HIV`), the authors include an ingenious control: `AZT`-treated, 6×-washed DCs are re-exposed to fresh `HIV-1 Ba-L` at the moment of T-cell addition, and show full restoration of $>10^3\text{–}10^4$ *gag* copies and high `RTase` activity, proving that the 6 washes removed all inhibitory `AZT` and that the block occurred specifically within the DCs during the 39-h window.
3. **Physiological Primary Cell Model Without Mitogen/Cytokine Artifacts:** Unlike classical PBMC PHA-blast assays, neither the skin DCs nor the `CD45RO+ Ki-67-` memory T cells are exposed to lectins (`PHA`), superantigens, `IL-2`, or xenogeneic serum (`5–10% normal human AB+ serum` is used throughout), closely modeling mucosal/cutaneous portal-of-entry biology.

#### Weaknesses & Methodological Caveats
1. **Incomplete Reverse Transcripts Carried in Virions vs. De Novo DC Integration:** While the `AZT` experiment (`Fig. 6`) proves that reverse transcription during the 39-h DC culture is required for retention and transmission of infection at 1.5 days, `SK38/SK39` amplifies a conserved region of *gag* (late reverse transcript, not 2-LTR circles or Alu-gag integrated provirus). Thus, as the authors thoughtfully discuss, this low-level `AZT`-sensitive DNA in DCs ($\le 100$ copies per $5 \times 10^4$ cells) could represent either rare integrated provirus in a small subset of DCs or unintegrated full-length reverse transcripts that only become transcriptionally active when `REL`/`SP1` transcription factors are induced upon DC–T cell syncytium/conjugate formation.
2. **Radiolabeled Band Quantification:** Viral DNA copy numbers in `Figures 2–6` are estimated visually against 10-fold serial dilutions of `ACH-2` T-lymphoblast standards (`10` to `10,000` copies) on autoradiograms rather than by phosphorimager densitometry or real-time PCR (which was not yet standard in the mid-1990s).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Split-thickness cadaver skin (<36 h post-mortem) yields viable emigrating DCs, `CD45RO+` memory T cells, and DC–T cell conjugates equivalent in number, phenotype, and HIV-1 permissiveness to fresh surgical skin. `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 2:** Unstimulated cutaneous DC–memory `CD4+` T cell conjugates support productive replication of both `NSI` and `SI` primary HIV-1 isolates without mitogens or `IL-2`. `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 3:** Purified skin DCs retain infectious HIV-1 for at least 1.5 days via an `AZT`-sensitive reverse transcription intermediate and initiate vigorous cytopathic infection upon conjugating with resting memory `CD4+` T cells. `[Well Supported]`

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Experimental Completeness:** Viral strains (`Ba-L`, `IIIB`, `SF162`, `ADA`, `JR-CSF`, `JR-FL`, primary isolates `Pt 1–6`), `TCID50`/`MOI` doses, antibody clones (`3C10`, `OKT11`, `Leu-3a`, `Leu-2a`, `UCHL-1`, `Ki-67`, `183-H12-5C`), primer pairs (`SK38/SK39`, `GH26/GH27`), and cell numbers per microculture well (`4–5 x 10^4` cells) are documented with exemplary precision.
