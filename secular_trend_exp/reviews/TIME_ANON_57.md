# Blinded Review: TIME_ANON_57
**Composite Score:** 8.0 / 10.0 (Rigor: 2.5/3.5 | Claims: 2.0/2.5 | Biology: 1.7/2.0 | Reproducibility: 1.8/2.0)
**Bias Category:** Informal Visual Tree-Topology Comparison & Percentage Conversion Typo
**Verdict:** Minor Revision
**Study Modality:** Hybrid Computational + Wet-Lab Functional Validation
**Technology Maturity:** Mid-Stage High-Throughput
**Epistemic Stage:** Basic/Mechanistic Discovery

### 1. Executive Summary
This comparative genomics and molecular evolution study reports de novo Illumina draft genome assemblies for 25 *Bacillus cereus sensu lato* strains and integrates them with publicly available genomes ($N = 142$ for phylogenomics; $N = 218$ for toxin screening) to resolve species boundaries and trace the evolutionary history of the chromosomal enterotoxin operons *nheABC*, *hblCDAB* (and its truncated paralog *hblCDA_a*), *cytK*, and the pleiotropic regulator *plcR*. Using whole-genome Average Nucleotide Identity (ANI), core-genome SNPs (kSNP3), and a 7-gene concatenated MLSA master tree, the authors confirm seven major genomospecies clusters ($\ge 94\%$ ANI) and demonstrate striking evolutionary divergence among toxin loci: *hblCDAB*, *cytK*, and *plcR* undergo frequent horizontal gene transfer (HGT) and gene loss across clades, whereas *nheABC* is universally present and strictly vertically inherited. The comparative genomic analysis is broad and well-executed, requiring only formal statistical topology tests to supplement visual tree comparisons and correction of a 100-fold percentage conversion error in the Abstract.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Concordant Multi-Method Species Phylogeny:** The authors validate their 7-gene full-length MLSA species tree against both whole-genome pairwise ANIb ($10,011$ pairs across 142 genomes; Pearson $r^2 = 0.974$ between concatenated housekeeping distance and whole-genome ANI, Fig. 3a) and an alignment-free core-genome SNP maximum-likelihood tree (kSNP3, Fig. 2), establishing a robust reference topology.
* **100-Fold Percentage Conversion Error in the Abstract:** In the Abstract (line 9), the authors state that "duplication of nheABC is extremely rare (0.02 %)". However, in the Results (line 144), the duplicated *nhe_a* operon was identified in 4 out of 142 strains ($4/142 = 0.0282 = 2.82\%$, or $4/218 = 0.0183 = 1.83\%$ of the larger screening set). The authors mistook the decimal proportion ($\approx 0.02$) for a percentage (`0.02 %`).
* **Absence of Formal Statistical Tests for Tree Incongruence:** The central evolutionary claims—that *hblCDAB*, *cytK*, and *plcR* undergo massive HGT whereas *nheABC* and *hblCDA_a* are transmitted vertically—rest on visual comparison of maximum-likelihood gene trees against the MLSA species tree (Figs. 1, 4, 5, 6 and Figures S2, S4). Formal likelihood-based topology tests (such as the Approximately Unbiased [AU] test or Shimodaira-Hasegawa [SH] test constraining the toxin alignment to the MLSA species tree topology) or normalized Robinson-Foulds distance permutation tests would quantify these topological comparisons rigorously.
* **Uniform Nucleotide Substitution Model Applied Across All Loci:** In Methods (page 2, line 1), the authors select `TN93 + G + I` based on the 7 concatenated housekeeping genes and apply this exact nucleotide model to all toxin and regulator trees (including *plcR*, where DNA sequence identity drops to 70%) while retaining all gap sites (`Use all sites`). Locus-specific model selection (or codon/amino-acid models for divergent *plcR* and paralogous *nhe*/*nhe_a* comparisons) and partial deletion of gapped columns would reduce potential long-branch artifacts.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Important Taxonomic and Food-Safety Risk Assessment Insights:** By showing that traditional phenotype-based species labels (*B. cereus*, *B. thuringiensis*, *B. weihenstephanensis*, *B. mycoides*) are polyphyletic across genomospecies clusters II, III, IV, and VI, and that licensed commercial probiotic strains in Cluster V (*B. toyonensis* BCT-7112 and *B. cereus* IP5832) carry intact *nhe* and *hbl* enterotoxin operons, the study provides concrete evidence relevant to regulatory risk assessment.
* **Validation of Duplicated Operons and Transcription:** Because short-read draft assemblies can collapse or misassemble paralogous operons, the authors verify *hbl* and *nhe* duplications in their de novo assemblies via read-depth coverage ratios against background contigs (Tables S5–S6) and experimentally test *nhe* vs. *nhe_a* transcription using RT-PCR (Fig. 7).
* **Clearly Caveated Speculation on NheA Function:** The hypothesis that *nheABC* resists horizontal transfer and deletion because NheA performs an additional, essential cellular function co-evolving with a host partner (line 154) is explicitly framed as a speculative prediction requiring experimental investigation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Complete Genome Deposition and Metadata:** All 25 de novo sequenced draft genomes are deposited in NCBI GenBank with assembly statistics, coverage depths, k-mer sizes, and accession numbers documented in Additional file 1 (Tables S1–S6), alongside complete strain provenance and PCR primer tables.
