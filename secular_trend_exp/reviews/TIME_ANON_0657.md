### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This integrative aquaculture genomics study fine-maps the major quantitative trait loci (QTLs) governing resistance to Pancreas Disease (PD, caused by Salmonid alphavirus subtype 3, `SAV3`) in farmed Atlantic salmon (*Salmo salar*)—a trait previously mapped to a broad ~27-Mb region at the q-arm telomere of chromosome `Ssa03` that shares high homeologous sequence similarity with `Ssa06` due to delayed rediploidization after the salmonid whole-genome duplication (`Ss4R`). Leveraging the long-read Atlantic salmon reference assembly (`Ssal_v3.1`, `GCA_905237065.2`), the authors whole-genome sequenced 293 AquaGen broodstock ancestors (`DeepVariant v1.1.2` / `GL-NEXUS v1.4.1`), filtered SNPs via 5-fold cross-validation in `Beagle 5.4` ($r^2 \ge 0.80$), and imputed 885,287 high-accuracy SNPs (938,582 total SNPs) into 5,628 SAV3-challenged salmon parr across three year-classes (`3,195` intraperitoneally injected [`IP`] and `2,433` cohabitation-infected [`CH`] fish). Using `GCTA-LDMS` and `BOLT-LMM`, they confirm the major `Ssa03` QTL (`ssa03_95086730`, active in both `IP` and `CH` routes) and identify an `IP`-specific QTL on `Ssa07` (`ssa07_49303476`), narrowing the `Ssa03` high-LD block ($r^2 > 0.95$) from 33 Mb down to 45 kb (`Ssa03: 95,076,092–95,121,176`) overlapping 6 genes (`nlrp3`, three tandem `gig1-like` copies, `abcc6b.1`, and `sspd`). By integrating heart RNA-seq from a `SAV3` cohabitation trial ($n=47$), re-analyzed `IP` survival RNA-seq (`PRJNA590166`, $n=12$), Oxford Nanopore long-read isoform curation (`AQUA-FAANG`, $n=12$), poly(I:C) `ATAC-seq` and `H3K27ac`/`H3K27me3` `ChIP-seq` ($n=6$ vs. $6$), `STAR --waspOutputMode` / `ASEReadCounter` allele-specific expression (`ASE`) in $n=10$ QTL heterozygotes, and 14 `PEPPER-Margin-DeepVariant` / `Flye` / `Minigraph-Cactus` long-read haplotype assemblies ($n=7$ fish), the authors resolve misannotations in the three tandem `gig1-like` genes and identify cis-acting coding and promoter `(AC)n` microsatellite variants in `gig1-like` copies 1 and 3. This is a tour-de-force multi-omic dissection of a duplicated teleost QTL.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### Key Strengths
- **Rigorous 5-fold cross-validated WGS imputation (`Beagle 5.4`) and `GCTA-LDMS` / `BOLT-LMM` modeling ($N = 5,628$):** Masking non-array genotypes in 5-fold cross-validation across the 293 WGS broodstock ancestors and retaining only variants with empirical imputation accuracy $r^2 \ge 0.80$ (`891,641` SNPs) prevented imputation artifacts in the duplicated `Ssa03`/`Ssa06` ohnolog region, shrinking the candidate LD block from 27–33 Mb to 45 kb (`10` SNPs in $r^2 > 0.95$ with `ssa03_95086730`).
- **Biological separation of artificial injection (`IP`) vs. natural waterborne cohabitation (`CH`) routes:** Analyzing `3,195` `IP` fish ($h^2 = 0.36 \pm 0.02$) and `2,433` `CH` fish ($h^2 = 0.25 \pm 0.03$) separately revealed that while `Ssa03` confers resistance across both routes, `Ssa07` (`SCAF11` / `slc38a2`) is strictly an `IP`-specific artifact of bypassing mucosal/skin barriers (`Fig. 3`).
- **WASP-corrected Allele-Specific Expression (`ASE`) in QTL heterozygotes (`Fig. 7`):** Because comparing high- vs. low-viral-load fish in bulk RNA-seq is confounded by viral load itself, testing allele-specific expression *within* 10 `CH`-infected fish heterozygous for `ssa03_95086730` using `STAR --waspOutputMode` (to eliminate reference mapping bias) and `--outSAMmapqUnique` (to prevent cross-mapping between the three tandem `gig1-like` paralogs and the `Ssa06` ohnolog) provides clean cis-regulatory proof that the susceptibility-associated reference haplotype drives higher expression of `gig1-like` copies 1 and 3.
- **Resolution of tandem paralog gene structures and structural variants via ONT long reads and `AQUA-FAANG` epigenomics:** Combining ONT full-length cDNA reads (`Flair`), poly(I:C) `ATAC-seq`/`H3K27ac` peaks, and 14 `Flye`/`Minigraph-Cactus` de novo haplotype contigs corrected public Ensembl/NCBI exon misannotations and uncovered polymorphic promoter `(AC)n` and `(ACA/A)` microsatellites overlapping IFN-inducible open chromatin at `gig1-like` copies 1 and 3 (`Fig. 6`).

#### Methodological Weaknesses & Audit Findings
1. **Massive Transcriptome-Wide Inflation in Bulk `DESeq2` Comparisons (`14,712` and `18,760` DEGs) Confounded by Viral Replication / Moribundity (`Line 207`):**
   - In the bulk differential expression analyses (line 207), comparing 12 high-viral-load vs. 12 low-viral-load `CH` fish yielded **14,712 significant DEGs** ($\text{FDR} < 0.05$), and comparing dead/moribund vs. surviving `IP` fish yielded **18,760 significant DEGs** ($\text{FDR} < 0.05$).
   - Because 14,712–18,760 genes represent roughly 35%–45% of all expressed protein-coding genes in the Atlantic salmon genome, nearly half the transcriptome is altered between moribund/high-viremia and healthy/low-viremia hearts. Consequently, the bulk DEG $p$-values in `Fig. 5B–C` mainly reflect global interferon activation secondary to high `SAV3` viral load (`Gig1` is an interferon-stimulated gene) rather than primary QTL causality. Fortunately, the authors supplemented bulk DEG with `ASE` in heterozygotes (`Fig. 7`), which is immune to trans-acting viral load confounding.
2. **Exact Binomial Test (`binom.test`) for Multi-Individual ASE Analysis (`Line 114`):**
   - For the ASE analysis of 10 heterozygous individuals, `Methods` line 114 states that *"SNP-wise significance between gene expression and the alleles of each SNP was estimated using the exact binomial test option 'binom.test' in R."* An exact binomial test assumes independent Bernoulli trials with fixed probability $p=0.5$ and does not account for extra-binomial overdispersion in RNA-seq read counts or inter-individual variance (where a beta-binomial test or paired Wilcoxon test across the 10 biological replicates is more appropriate).
3. **Small Sample Size of Long-Read Sequenced Fish ($n=7$ Broodstock, 14 Haplotypes) Without Direct Phenotypes:**
   - The ONT PromethION pangenome graph (`Minigraph-Cactus`) was constructed from 7 breeding-nucleus individuals (8 reference and 6 alternative haplotypes phased via the 11 LD-block SNPs). As the authors transparently note in the Discussion (line 264), larger-scale targeted genotyping of the polymorphic `(AC)n` promoter microsatellite in phenotyped challenge fish is needed to separate its effect from the linked missense SNPs (`K72M`, `R73K`, `G78D`) in `gig1-like` copy 3.
4. **Minor Figure Reference Typo in Main Text (`Lines 207, 256`):**
   - Lines 207 and 256 cite `(Fig. 4 B and C)` when referring to the differential gene expression plots across the `Ssa03` QTL interval, which are actually located in `Fig. 5 B and C` (`Fig. 4` has no subpanels B or C).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Resistance to `SAV3` in Atlantic salmon is governed by a major route-independent QTL on `Ssa03` (`45 kb` LD block at `95.076–95.121 Mb`) and an `IP`-injection-specific QTL on `Ssa07` (`23.1 kb` LD block at `49.280–49.303 Mb`). `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 2:** Within the 45-kb `Ssa03` QTL block, three tandemly duplicated `gig1-like` genes (which arose via segmental duplication of an ancestral `Ssa03`/`Ssa06` ohnolog) are the primary transcriptional responders to `SAV3`, with copies 1 and 3 exhibiting strong IFN-inducible `ATAC-seq`/`H3K27ac` promoter activation and significant cis-acting allele-specific expression (`ASE`) favoring higher expression on the PD-susceptible reference haplotype. `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 3:** Three conserved-domain missense substitutions (`K72M`, `R73K`, `G78D`) in `gig1-like` copy 3 and polymorphic promoter `(AC)n`/`(ACA/A)` microsatellites overlapping the TSS/open chromatin of copies 1 and 3 represent the primary candidate causal variants on the PD-associated haplotype. `[Well Supported]` — Appropriately framed as strong, prioritized functional candidates warranting CRISPR/in vitro validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Reference genome accessions (`GCA_905237065.2`, `GCF_905237065.1`, `SAMEA8062739`), public RNA-seq (`PRJNA590166`) and `AQUA-FAANG` ImmunoMap datasets, animal welfare permit numbers (`FOTS 15233, 19332, 23006, 29261`), exact physical coordinates and Ensembl/NCBI gene IDs (`Table 2`), mathematical equations for `GCTA` mixed models (`Eq. 1`) and `Shim et al.` PVE (`Eq. 2`), and complete software versions/parameters (`BWA-mem2`, `DeepVariant v1.1.2`, `GL-NEXUS v1.4.1`, `Beagle 5.4`, `BOLT-LMM`, `STAR v2.7.10 --waspOutputMode`, `PEPPER-Margin-DeepVariant r0.8`, `Flye v2.9`, `Minigraph-Cactus v2.6.9`) are documented in exemplary detail.
- **Deficiencies:** Commercial genotype and raw sequencing data from the proprietary AquaGen AS breeding nucleus require owner consent rather than open public repository access, though summary tables (`Supplementary Tables S1–S4`) are provided.
