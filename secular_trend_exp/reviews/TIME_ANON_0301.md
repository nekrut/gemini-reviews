### 1. Executive Summary
This detailed methods protocol chapter describes the "No See'm" reverse-genetics platform for assembling a full-length (~31.5 kb) infectious cDNA clone of murine hepatitis coronavirus strain A59 (`icMHV`) from seven stable plasmid subclones (`MHV-A` through `MHV-G` in `pCR-XL-Topo` or transcription/translation-negative `pSMART` vectors) flanked by asymmetric Type IIs restriction endonuclease sites (`BsmBI`, `BglI`, `SfiI`, plus `MluI` and `AhdI`). Because Type IIs enzymes cleave outside their recognition sequences, orienting the recognition sites outward allows seamless removal of the enzyme sites upon digestion, leaving unique non-palindromic sticky overhangs that drive unidirectional 7-piece in vitro shotgun ligation, T7 RNA polymerase transcription of capped full-length genomic RNA, co-electroporation with SP6-transcribed `N`-gene helper mRNA into `BHK-MHVr` (`CEACAM1a`+) cells plated onto `DBT` indicator cells, plaque purification, and seamless site-directed mutagenesis (illustrated by a `BbsI`-mediated codon substitution at nt 13,354–13,356 in `MHV-E` and a double Lys24Ala/Lys25Ala mutation in `Nsp10`).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Clever Biochemical & Molecular Engineering Design:** Dividing toxic coronavirus replicase sequences across seven low-copy/transcription-free `pSMART` and `pCR-XL-Topo` plasmids propagated at $28.5^\circ\text{C}$ solves *E. coli* instability, while CIP dephosphorylation of the terminal `MluI` (`MHV-A`) and `SfiI` (`MHV-G`) vector junctions prevents 5' or 3' end-to-end concatemerization during the 7-fragment shotgun ligation. Co-electroporating capped SP6 `N`-gene transcripts increases viral rescue ~15-fold (yielding $10^3\text{–}10^4$ infectious centers), and limiting plaque purification to a single passage (`P1` $\rightarrow$ `P2`) while fixing engineered amino acid changes with 2–3 nucleotide substitutions per codon minimizes compensatory second-site reversion.
- **Minor Technical / Stoichiometric Inconsistencies in the Protocol Text:**
  - **Mutated Codon Identity Discrepancy (Line 65 & Figure 5):** On line 65 (second chunk) and in the Figure 5A caption, the authors state: "we selected a glutamate codon at position 13354-56 (CAG) and changed it to an alanine (GTC)." In the standard genetic code ($5'\rightarrow 3'$ coding strand), `CAG` encodes Glutamine (`Gln`, whereas Glutamate is `GAA`/`GAG`) and `GTC` encodes Valine (`Val`, whereas Alanine is `GCN`). Conversely, if written $3'\rightarrow 5'$ on the template strand, $3'\text{-CAG-5'}$ corresponds to $5'\text{-GTC-3'}$ (`Val`) and $3'\text{-GTC-5'}$ corresponds to $5'\text{-CAG-3'}$ (`Gln`)—or if read backward ($3'\rightarrow 5'$ as $5'\text{-GAC-3'}$ = Asp and $5'\text{-CTG-3'}$ = Leu). In `NC_001846`, nt 13,354–13,356 in `ORF1a/1b` (`Nsp10`) should be checked so the codon triplet and amino acid labels (`Glu` vs. `Gln` and `Ala` vs. `Val`) match the $5'\rightarrow 3'$ positive-sense genome.
  - **Primer Stock Concentration Typo (Lines 248, 252–254):** In the `SP6-N-gene` PCR recipe, the sense and antisense primer stocks are listed as `10 mM` (`1 μl of 10 mM` in a $50\text{ }\mu\text{l}$ reaction would be $200\text{ }\mu\text{M}$ final primer concentration, which inhibits PCR; standard working stock is $10\text{ }\mu\text{M}$, giving $0.2\text{ }\mu\text{M}$ final).
  - **Equimolar vs. Equal-Mass Ligation Note (Line 226):** The protocol suggests adding $15\text{–}25\text{ }\mu\text{l}$ of each gel-extracted fragment to achieve "relatively equivalent numbers of molecules of each fragment," but `MHV-G` ($8,711\text{ bp}$) is $6\times$ longer than `MHV-D` ($1,451\text{ bp}$), so equal molarity ($n$ molecules) requires $6\times$ more mass ($\mu\text{g}$) of `MHV-G` than `MHV-D`.
  - **Formatting Duplication:** Several bulleted protocol steps (e.g., lines 162–176, 189–207, 213–217, 234–246, 248–260, 262–274, 283–297, and chunk 2 lines 10–48) appear twice in succession due to XML list-item rendering in the text conversion.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: The 7-fragment Type IIs ("No See'm") assembly and SP6-N co-electroporation protocol reliably rescues wild-type and replicase-mutant recombinant MHV-A59 (`icMHV`).** `[Well Supported]` — Validated by full-length ~31.5 kb cDNA ligation gels, formaldehyde/SDS RNA gels, nested leader–N subgenomic RT-PCR ladders, 7-band Northern blots (Figure 3A–C), plaque assays comparing uniform 5-mm WT `icMHV` plaques against attenuated `Nsp10` (`K24A/K25A`) small/irregular plaques (Figure 4A–B), and >100 engineered mutants generated with the platform.
- **Claim 2: Strengths and limitations of multi-fragment in vitro ligation vs. single full-length BAC/vaccinia clones.** `[Well Supported]` — Note 8 provides a balanced, candid assessment of the advantages (parallel subclone mutagenesis, combinatorics, biosafety, sequence stability) and bottlenecks (technical skill required for 7-way gel extraction/ligation, T7 transcription of 31.5 kb RNA, AT-rich sequence toxicity in other CoVs like NL63).
