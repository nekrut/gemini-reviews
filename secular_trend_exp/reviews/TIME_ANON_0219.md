### 1. Executive Summary
This study establishes and characterizes an *in vivo* murine model of human respiratory coronavirus HCoV-OC43 neuropathogenesis in 21-day postnatal (DPN) C57BL/6 and BALB/c mice. After evaluating intraperitoneal, intraoral, intranasal, and intracerebral (IC) inoculation routes across postnatal ages (1–35 DPN), the authors focus on IC inoculation of 21 DPN mice ($10\text{ TCID}_{50}$ for C57BL/6 and $10^4\text{--}10^5\text{ TCID}_{50}$ for more resistant BALB/c mice) to track viral RNA (RT-PCR), infectious virus titers (immunoperoxidase $\text{TCID}_{50}$ assay on HRT-18 cells), viral N protein (Western blot), neuropathology (H&E, toluidine blue, IHC for viral N, GFAP, and Mac-2, plus transmission electron microscopy), and the effect of T-cell immunosuppression via cyclosporin A (CsA, 10 and 20 mg/kg/day). The authors demonstrate that HCoV-OC43 selectively infects neurons throughout the gray matter, inducing prominent cytoplasmic vacuolation ("spongiform-like" degeneration), ER dilatation, and neuronal necrosis in the hippocampus (CA1/CA3), cortex, thalamus, and spinal cord, and that CsA immunosuppression accelerates mortality (from 80% to 100%) rather than protecting the host, indicating direct viral cytopathology rather than T-cell-mediated immunopathology.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Crucial Exclusion of Enzootic Murine Coronavirus (MHV) Contamination:** Because laboratory mice are natural hosts for murine hepatitis virus (MHV, which is antigenically related to HCoV-OC43), the authors used MHV-seronegative mice and explicitly validated species-specific RT-PCR primers (Fig. 3C) proving that amplicons from infected mouse brain, spinal cord, and spleen originated exclusively from HCoV-OC43 and not from enzootic MHV. They also fulfilled Koch's postulates by passaging brain homogenates through HRT-18 cells and reproducing the neurological disease upon IC re-inoculation.
- **Multi-Modal Time-Course Tracking:** Sacrificing $n = 5$ mice per strain every 2 days for virological assays and $n = 2$ mice every 2 days for perfusion/histology across the 30-day time course, alongside $n = 20$ mice per strain for survival curves, provides a solid longitudinal picture showing that peak infectious titers ($10^8\text{ TCID}_{50}/\text{g}$ in brain at 5–9 dpi) precede Mac-2 microgliosis and GFAP astrogliosis.
- **Statistical Reporting & Dose Inconsistency:**
  - *Absence of Formal Survival and Weight-Loss Statistics:* Survival curves in Fig. 1C ($n = 20$ per strain) and Fig. 8 ($n = 10$ per group for CsA treatment: 80% mortality in oil control vs. 90% in 10 mg/kg CsA and 100% in 20 mg/kg CsA) are presented descriptively without Kaplan-Meier log-rank ($\chi^2$) $p$-values. With $n = 10$ mice per group, the difference between 8/10 (80%) and 10/10 (100%) deaths is modest and requires formal survival-time log-rank testing to establish statistical significance. Similarly, Fig. 2C error bars and weight curves (Fig. 1B) lack SD/SEM definitions.
  - *Minor Dose Discrepancy for BALB/c Mice:* In the Results text (line 28) and Fig. 2B legend, the IC dose administered to 21 DPN BALB/c mice is stated as $10^5\text{ TCID}_{50}$, whereas in the Fig. 1C legend (line 28/40) and Materials and Methods (line 117), it is stated as $10,000\text{ TCID}_{50}$ ($10^4\text{ TCID}_{50}$).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** HCoV-OC43 is neuroinvasive after intranasal inoculation in 21 DPN mice and neurovirulent after intracerebral inoculation, with C57BL/6 mice exhibiting markedly higher susceptibility than BALB/c mice. `[Well Supported]` — Supported by RT-PCR detection in CNS of 4/8 intranasally inoculated mice and a $1,000$- to $10,000$-fold difference in $\text{LD}_{100}$ between C57BL/6 and BALB/c mice (Table 1).
- **Claim 2:** Acute HCoV-OC43 encephalitis targets neurons and induces spongiform-like intraneuronal vacuolation and neuronal death in gray matter (unlike the white-matter oligodendrocyte demyelination typical of MHV). `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by IHC, H&E, toluidine blue, and TEM ultrastructure showing viral particles in the neuronal rough ER/Golgi and cytoplasmic vacuolation with intact plasma membranes.
- **Claim 3:** CNS damage during acute HCoV-OC43 infection is caused by direct viral cytopathology rather than T-cell-mediated immunopathology. `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by the delayed onset of microgliosis relative to viral replication and the exacerbation (rather than amelioration) of mortality under CsA immunosuppression, with the authors appropriately noting that genetic T/B-cell knockout models (e.g., *Rag1*$^{-/-}$ or *Scid*) would provide definitive proof.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Virus strain source (ATCC HCoV-OC43), propagation line (HRT-18), mouse strains and ages, antibody clones (`1-10C.3`, `4-E11.3`, `Mac-2`, `GFAP`), and RT-PCR primer sequences/references are thoroughly documented.
- **Weaknesses:** Clarifying the exact BALB/c IC inoculum ($10^4$ vs. $10^5\text{ TCID}_{50}$) and adding log-rank $p$-values for the survival curves in Figures 1C and 8 would improve quantitative rigor.
