### 1. Executive Summary
This study genotypes a single intronic SNP (*WDR12* rs6725887 C>T) in 101 angiographically confirmed coronary artery disease (CAD) patients and 98 controls (91 genotyped) from Iran, reporting no significant association with CAD or clinical lipid/hemodynamic parameters. Beyond being severely underpowered ($N = 192$ genotyped) to detect a common GWAS variant with a known odds ratio of $1.14\text{--}1.16$, the manuscript contains pervasive mathematical, statistical, and factual errors: an impossible confidence interval in Table 2 where the point estimate lies far outside the bounds (`OR = 6.39 [0.32 to 1.25]`), multiple $p$-values in Table 1 (`<0.0001`) that are arithmetically incompatible with the reported means and standard deviations, direct contradictions between the prose and Table 1 regarding age and LDL directionality, a severe control-selection bias that artificially truncates clinical distributions in controls, and a basic biological misstatement describing human WDR12 as a 40-amino-acid protein.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Mathematically Impossible Confidence Interval in Table 2:** In Table 2 (line 59), the comparison of TT vs. CC genotypes (3/101 CAD vs. 0/91 Control) is reported as `OR = 6.39 (0.32 to 1.25), P-value = 0.22`. A point estimate of `6.39` cannot lie above the upper bound (`1.25`) of its own 95% confidence interval. Applying the Haldane-Anscombe $0.5$ continuity correction to the $2 \times 2$ table $(81, 3\text{ vs. }74, 0)$ yields $\widehat{\text{OR}} = \frac{7 \times 74.5}{0.5 \times 163} = 6.3988$ with $95\%\text{ CI } [0.324, 126.1]$; the authors evidently dropped the decimal place on the upper bound (`125` printed as `1.25`) without noticing that the resulting interval `[0.32 to 1.25]` excludes the point estimate `6.39`.
* **Arithmetically Impossible $t$-Test $P$-Values in Table 1:** Computing standard two-sample $t$-statistics directly from the sample sizes ($n_1 = 98, n_2 = 101$), means, and standard deviations reported in Table 1 reveals that at least four of the `<0.0001` or `0.002` $p$-values are mathematically impossible:
  * **BMI:** $26.01 \pm 4.08$ vs. $27.47 \pm 6.78 \implies t = \frac{1.46}{\sqrt{4.08^2/98 + 6.78^2/101}} = 1.84$, two-tailed $p = 0.067$ (reported in Table 1 as `P = 0.002`).
  * **Total Cholesterol (TC):** $165.42 \pm 27.24$ vs. $173.57 \pm 32.60 \implies t = 1.91$, two-tailed $p = 0.057$ (reported in Table 1 as `P < 0.0001`).
  * **Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS):** $122.20 \pm 39.62$ vs. $139.05 \pm 62.57 \implies t = 2.28$, two-tailed $p = 0.024$ (reported in Table 1 as `P < 0.0001`).
  * **HDL:** $37.01 \pm 5.83$ vs. $39.16 \pm 8.10 \implies t = 2.15$, two-tailed $p = 0.033$ (reported in Table 1 as `P < 0.0001`).
* **Direct Contradictions Between Main Text and Table 1:**
  * **Age:** Results line 33 states "The control group showed significantly higher age (61.14 ± 11.05 years)" and line 78 states "age... were significantly associated with CAD (Table 1)", whereas Table 1 reports `P = 0.18` (and the standard two-sample $t$-test yields $t = 1.75, p = 0.082$).
  * **LDL Directionality:** Results line 33 states "Although LDL level was significantly higher in controls...", whereas Table 1 shows LDL is *lower* in controls ($86.88 \pm 2.74\text{ mg/dl}$, with an implausibly tiny SD of $2.74\text{ mg/dl}$) than in CAD patients ($102.04 \pm 24.53\text{ mg/dl}$), and the Abstract (line 7) claims CAD patients had higher LDL.
  * **Categorical Variables Analyzed by $t$-Test:** Results line 33 states "The presence of hypertension was significantly higher in the CAD group when compared to the controls by T-test", and Table 1 reports `Male` as a continuous mean $\pm$ SD (`0.49 ± 0.57` vs. `0.57 ± 0.49`; note also that a binary $0/1$ variable with mean $0.49$ must have $\text{SD} = \sqrt{0.49 \times 0.51} = 0.50$, so an SD of `0.57` is mathematically impossible for a $0/1$ indicator).
  * **Mislabeled Table Header and Unexplained Sample Loss:** The title of Table 1 (line 36) mislabels the CAD patient group as `angiography negative (CAD) groups` (when Methods line 20 defines CAD cases as angiography *positive* with stenosis $\ge 50\%$). Furthermore, 98 controls were enrolled (Table 1), yet Table 2 includes only $n = 91$ controls without explaining the 7 missing genotypes.
* **Severe Underpowering Conflated with Evidence for the Null:** For a GWAS SNP with minor allele frequency $\approx 0.10$ and true per-allele $\text{OR} \approx 1.15$, a sample of 101 cases and 91 controls has $<7\%$ statistical power at $\alpha = 0.05$. Concluding that "The rs6725887 polymorphism is unlikely to play a key role in CAD risk in our population" (Abstract, line 7) conflates an uninformative, underpowered test with evidence of absence.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Fundamental Factual Error Regarding WDR12 Protein Biology:** In the Introduction (line 13), the authors write that "WD repeat protein 12 (WDR12) is a ribosome biogenesis protein with 40 amino acids". In reality, human WDR12 is a 423-amino-acid protein (~48 kDa) containing seven WD40 repeat domains (where each individual WD40 repeat motif is ~40 amino acids long).
* **Truncated Super-Control Selection Bias:** Methods (line 20) states that angiography-negative individuals were included as controls only "if they also had no history of MI, hypertension, cigarette smoking, diabetes, obesity, and high level of cholesterol." Excluding all individuals with hypertension, diabetes, obesity, smoking, or hypercholesterolemia from the control group guarantees by design that cases and controls will differ on blood pressure, BMI, lipids, and glucose (Table 1), and explains the artificially truncated SD for control LDL ($86.88 \pm 2.74\text{ mg/dl}$), although notice that control mean FBS ($122.20 \pm 39.62\text{ mg/dl}$) and mean BMI ($26.01 \pm 4.08\text{ kg/m}^2$) contradict the stated exclusion of diabetes and overweight/obesity.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Missing Primer/Probe Sequences and Assay Identifiers:** Methods (line 24) states that "Primer-probe sets were designed and manufactured by Applied Biosystem custom service," but neither the Applied Biosystems assay ID nor the custom primer and TaqMan probe nucleotide sequences are provided.
* **Unreported Regression Outputs:** Although Methods (line 26) states that logistic regression and stepwise multivariate regression were performed, no multivariate regression models or adjusted odds ratios are presented anywhere in the Results.
