### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This study investigates the evolutionary history and hybridization dynamics of mitten crabs (*Eriocheir*) across East Asia by generating de novo draft genome assemblies for *E. japonica* (1.24 Gb, N50 = 442.7 kb) and *E. hepuensis* (1.18 Gb, N50 = 122.5 kb) and whole-genome resequencing data (~23x coverage) for 139 individuals across seven geographic populations (Liao, Yellow, Yangtze, and Min Rivers in China; Hepu in southern China; Vladivostok in Russia; and Hokkaido in Japan). By mapping all reads to the *E. sinensis* chromosome-level reference genome, the authors perform PCA, ML phylogenetics, ADMIXTURE, TreeMix, PSMC, and genome scans ($F_{ST}$, $\pi$, XP-CLR), concluding that Hepu (*E. hepuensis*) and Russian (Vladivostok) mitten crabs originated from hybridization between Chinese (*E. sinensis*) and Japanese (*E. japonica*) mitten crabs, and that *E. hepuensis* represents an ancient homoploid hybrid species with established reproductive isolation. While the multi-population whole-genome dataset is an important empirical resource, the analytical interpretation contains a fundamental population-genetic contradiction regarding fixed inter-species heterozygosity in the hybrid populations, relies on single-reference alignment across divergent species, and overstates top-1% outlier scans and thermally plastic life-history traits as proof of homoploid hybrid speciation and reproductive isolation.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
**Strengths:**
* Generates deep whole-genome resequencing (~39 Gb/individual, ~23x depth) across $N = 139$ individuals from 7 geographic populations alongside multi-tissue RNA-seq and morphometric measurements.
* Combines nuclear phylogenomics (684 single-copy orthologs across 12 crustaceans) with complete mitochondrial genome assemblies, revealing mitonuclear discordance in *E. hepuensis* (clustering with *E. japonica* in nuclear trees but with *E. sinensis* in mitochondrial trees; Fig. 2B–C vs. Fig. S3A).
* Full public deposition of raw resequencing reads, RNA-seq reads, and genome assemblies in NGDC GSA/GWH with verified accession numbers.

**Weaknesses & Methodological Audit:**
* **Mathematical and Population-Genetic Contradiction in Reported Fixed-SNP Heterozygosity (Results Line 80 & Fig. S8):** In the section *"Different genome architecture of the two hybrid mitten crabs"*, the authors report that ADMIXTURE estimates parental genomic contributions of **19.59% Chinese / 80.41% Japanese** in Hepu mitten crabs and **37.87% Chinese / 62.13% Japanese** in Russian mitten crabs (Fig. 4A). Yet in the very next paragraph (line 80), they state: *"Of the divergent SNPs (fixed) identified between the Chinese and Japanese mitten crabs, a notable majority (87.65% and 98.84%, respectively) were heterozygous in Hepu and Russian mitten crabs (Figure S8). Additionally, 1.17% and 0.46% of these SNPs matched the Chinese mitten crab genotype, while 11.18% and 0.70% matched the Japanese mitten crab genotype in Hepu and Russian mitten crabs, respectively."*
  This creates two severe contradictions:
  1. **Arithmetic contradiction of ancestry proportions:** At loci fixed between Chinese ($AA$) and Japanese ($BB$) mitten crabs, if 87.65% of genotypes in Hepu crabs are heterozygous ($AB$), 1.17% are homozygous Chinese ($AA$), and 11.18% are homozygous Japanese ($BB$), then the Chinese allele frequency at diagnostic sites is $0.0117 + \frac{1}{2}(0.8765) = 45.0\%$ (and $0.0046 + \frac{1}{2}(0.9884) = 49.9\%$ in Russian crabs), directly contradicting the claimed 19.59% and 37.87% Chinese ancestry proportions!
  2. **Hardy-Weinberg / Mendelian impossibility for an "ancient stabilized homoploid hybrid species":** In any randomly mating, sexually reproducing diploid population, the expected frequency of heterozygotes at a biallelic locus cannot exceed $2pq \le 0.50$ under Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium (and decays toward $0$ over generations in an ancient hybrid species due to genetic drift and selective sweeps). Observing **87.65% and 98.84% heterozygosity** across all fixed parental SNPs is only possible if (a) the sampled individuals are first-generation ($F_1$) hybrids, (b) collapsed paralogs/repetitive regions mapped to the single *E. sinensis* reference genome are being miscalled as fixed heterozygotes across both populations, or (c) Figure S8 counts SNPs that are polymorphic (Carry at least one het individual) rather than individual genotype frequencies. If they are $F_1$ hybrids, *E. hepuensis* cannot be an "ancient homoploid hybrid species"; if it is a paralog/filtering artifact, the genomic architecture analysis is flawed.
* **Single-Reference Mapping Bias Across Species Diverted by ~14.8 MYA:** All 139 individuals (including pure *E. japonica* and *E. hepuensis*, which diverged from *E. sinensis* ~14.84 MYA with coding/non-coding divergence) were aligned exclusively to the *E. sinensis* reference genome (`BWA-MEM`). Divergent *E. japonica* alleles experience lower mapping efficiency in hypervariable regions, artificially depressing apparent nucleotide diversity ($\pi$) in Hokkaido *E. japonica* (reported as lowest at $3.05 \times 10^{-3}$) and biasing allele-frequency-spectrum scans (XP-CLR) and $F_{ST}$.
* **Absence of Demographic Modeling to Test Hybrid Speciation vs. Introgression:** Claims of "ancient homoploid hybrid speciation" versus recent secondary contact or ongoing gene flow are made solely from descriptive PCA, ADMIXTURE, and TreeMix plots without explicit coalescent demographic modeling (e.g., `fastsimcoal2`, `dadi`, or `moments`) to estimate split times, admixture times, or migration rates.
* **Conflation of Environmental Plasticity with Genetic Reproductive Isolation:** The claim that *E. hepuensis* has evolved genetic reproductive isolation (`RI`) is argued from its 1-year sexual maturation time and smaller body size in subtropical Guangxi (>20°C mean annual temperature) compared to 2 years in central China and 3 years in cold Vladivostok. However, as the authors themselves acknowledge in the Discussion (line 117), when *E. sinensis* is cultured at high temperatures in southern China, it also exhibits precocious 1-year maturation and smaller adult size! Without common-garden rearing or reciprocal cross-breeding experiments, attributing latitudinal maturity differences to fixed genetic RI is unsupported.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Divergence of *E. sinensis* and *E. japonica* as distinct species (~14.8 MYA) with mitonuclear discordance and admixture in Russian, Min River, and Hepu populations: [Well Supported].** Supported by 684-ortholog nuclear trees, mitochondrial phylogenies, PCA, ADMIXTURE, and TreeMix.
* **Hepu mitten crab (*E. hepuensis*) is an ancient homoploid hybrid species with established genetic reproductive isolation: [Overstated / Methodologically Flawed].** Directly contradicted by the reported 87.65% heterozygosity at fixed parental SNPs (which is incompatible with a stabilized homoploid hybrid genome) and the lack of common-garden or experimental reproductive isolation tests.
* **Functional adaptive roles of top-1% $F_{ST}$/XP-CLR outlier genes (*Btk29A*, *Lig*, *Pyx*, *Hsf1*, *Birc6*, *Poxn*) in courtship and thermal adaptation: [Fragile].** Identifying top 1% empirical tails of $F_{ST}$ and XP-CLR always yields 1% of the genome by definition; without demographic null simulations to calibrate false positive rates under admixture and bottlenecks, or functional validation, these remain candidate associations.
