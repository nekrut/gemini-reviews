### 1. Executive Summary
This study (`TIME_ANON_0125`) sequences and assembles 57 new complete mitochondrial genomes across all seven extant species of porpoises (family Phocoenidae), combining them with 6 published porpoise mitogenomes ($N=63$ ingroup sequences) and 8 odontocete outgroup sequences ($N=71$ total, $16,302\text{ bp}$ alignment) to resolve porpoise macro-evolutionary phylogenetics, divergence times, selection regimes, and intraspecific demographic history. Using dual mitogenome assembly (`Geneious` reference mapping + `MITObim` baiting/iterative de novo assembly), maximum-likelihood (`PhyML`), neighbor-joining, and Bayesian (`MrBayes` + `BEAST v2.4.3`) phylogenetics, rarefied nucleotide diversity ($\pi$ subsampled to $n=3$), branch-specific $d_N/d_S$ (`BPPML`/`MAPNH`), McDonald–Kreitman (`MK`) tests, and coalescent demographic inference (`Arlequin` mismatch distributions + `BEAST` Bayesian Skyline Plots validated by Path Sampling Bayes Factors against constant size), the authors show that: (1) basal divergence of tropical finless porpoises occurred $\sim 5.42\text{ Ma}$, followed by antitropical divergence of northern (`harbor` + `Dall's`, splitting $\sim 3.12\text{ Ma}$) and southern (`vaquita` + [`spectacled` + `Burmeister's`], splitting $\sim 2.39\text{ Ma}$ and $\sim 2.14\text{ Ma}$) clades; (2) contrary to D-loop studies, `spectacled` and `Burmeister's` porpoises are sister taxa to the exclusion of `vaquita`; (3) all intraspecific ecotypes/subspecies coalesced within the last $<1\text{ Myr}$ during Quaternary glaciations, including newly identified cryptic splits in North Pacific harbor porpoises (`NP1`/`NP2`), Dall's (`DP1`/`DP2`), and spectacled porpoises (`SP1`/`SP2`); and (4) critically endangered `vaquita` ($n=12$, $\pi = 0.02\%$, 16 segregating sites with 13 singletons) experienced a post-bottleneck expansion $\sim 3,079\text{ years BP}$ with a low effective female population size ($N_e \approx 1,665$). The dataset is comprehensive and the population-genetic analyses are thorough, though a few parameterization inconsistencies in the molecular clock description, alignment handling of the *ND5*/*ND6* overlap, and verbal framing of the McDonald–Kreitman Neutrality Index warrant correction.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Mitogenome Sequencing & Dual Assembly Cross-Validation:**
  - Every one of the 57 newly sequenced individuals was assembled independently via reference mapping (`Geneious v8.1.8` against `AJ554063`) and hybrid de novo baiting (`MITObim`), achieving $15\times\text{–}4,371\times$ depth (**Table 1**). `35/57` assemblies were 100% base-for-base identical between methods, and the `22` remaining assemblies had only `1–4` discrepancies that were manually resolved, while a poorly assembled 226-bp repetitive D-loop segment (`pos 15,508–15,734`) was prudently masked.
- **Technical & Parameterization Issues in Phylogenetics and Selection Tests:**
  1. **Reverse-Complemented *ND6* and 17-bp Overlap Duplication in the Alignment (line 77):** The authors note that because *ND6* is encoded on the heavy (opposite) strand, `"we wrote a custom script to reverse complement ND6 in the inputs of all the analyses that separates coding and non-coding regions of the mitogenomes. This led to a 17 bps longer alignment due to the overlapping position of ND5 and ND6."` While reverse-complementing *ND6* and duplicating its 17-bp overlap with *ND5* is necessary when concatenating the 13 CDSs for codon-based $d_N/d_S$ and MK analyses, if this 17-bp duplication was also retained in the whole-mitogenome alignment used for `PhyML`/`MrBayes`/`BEAST`, then any nucleotide substitutions within those 17 overlapping bases were double-counted (and treated as independent sites under a single strand-symmetric `HKY+G` model despite opposite-strand mutational biases in *ND6*).
  2. **BEAST Molecular Clock Prior Specification Inconsistency (lines 89–91):** In `Divergence time estimate`, the authors state that they calibrated Node 1 (Monodontidae–Phocoenidae split) `"with a lognormal mean age at 2.74 Myr (sd = 0.15) as a prior"`. However, in the introduction (line 18) and literature (`Ref. 17`), the Monodontidae–Phocoenidae split is **$\sim 15\text{ Myr}$**, and notably $\exp(2.74) = 15.49\text{ Myr}$! Thus, `2.74` is the **log-scale parameter $\mu$** of the lognormal distribution ($\ln(15.5) = 2.74$), not `"a lognormal mean age at 2.74 Myr"` (in `BEAST`, leaving the `Mean in Real Space` checkbox unchecked sets $\mu = 2.74 \Rightarrow \text{median } 15.49\text{ Myr}$). Furthermore, the authors simultaneously state that they set `ucldMean` to an exponential prior with mean `1` *and* `"assumed a substitution rate of 5 x 10^-8 substitutions per-site and per-year"`. In `BEAST`, one either fixes/priors `ucldMean` to the substitution rate ($5 \times 10^{-8}\text{ yr}^{-1} = 0.05\text{ Myr}^{-1}$) OR estimates the clock rate from node-age calibrations; specifying an exponential(1.0) prior on `ucldMean` alongside a fixed rate of $5 \times 10^{-8}$ is self-contradictory in the Methods text.
  3. **Verbal Framing of the McDonald–Kreitman Neutrality Index ($NI > 1$) (lines 25, 80, 105):** In `Material and methods` (line 105) and `Results` (line 25), the authors define the Neutrality Index via the ratio of divergence to polymorphism and state that `"NI is 1 when evolution is neutral, greater than 1 under purifying selection, and less than 1 in the case of positive selection."` Mathematically, the standard McDonald–Kreitman Neutrality Index is:
     $$NI = \frac{P_N / P_S}{D_N / D_S}$$
     When $NI > 1$, there is an **excess of nonsynonymous polymorphism** relative to nonsynonymous divergence ($P_N/P_S > D_N/D_S$). Under the Ohta nearly neutral model, $NI > 1$ occurs because slightly deleterious nonsynonymous mutations segregate as polymorphisms ($P_N$) at low frequencies before being purged prior to fixation ($D_N$). However, in small/endangered populations (`vaquita`, `YFP`, `BS`), relaxed purifying selection allows more slightly deleterious mutations to rise in polymorphic frequency ($\pi_N / \pi_S$ increases), inflating $NI > 1$. The authors correctly explain this exact population-genetic mechanism in the Discussion (line 80, citing Parsch et al.), but in line 80 they write a typographical inequality `"(pi_N >> d_N)"` (which mixes per-site nucleotide diversity $\pi_N$ with per-site divergence $d_N$, where $d_N \gg \pi_N$ between species; the intended inequality is $\pi_N/\pi_S > d_N/d_S$) and their earlier labels in `Results` and **Figure 4** calling $NI > 1$ in endangered lineages `"stronger purifying selection"` are confusing until the reader reaches the Discussion.
- **Demographic Inference Rigor:** Restricting site-frequency-spectrum (`Tajima's D`, `Fu & Li's D*` and `F*`), mismatch distribution, and `BEAST` Bayesian Skyline Plot (`BSP`) analyses to the two single-ecotype lineages with $n \ge 10$ (`vaquita` $n=12$ and `NP1` $n=10$), running 10 independent $10^8$-step MCMC chains ($\text{ESS} > 200$), and formally testing `BSP` against `CONST` via 100-step Path Sampling marginal likelihood Bayes Factors ($2\Delta \ln L > 6.4$ and $> 8.7$) is statistically rigorous.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Whole-mitogenome phylogenetics resolves the Phocoenidae tree with maximal support, placing `spectacled` and `Burmeister's` porpoises as sister species ($\sim 2.14\text{ Ma}$) and `vaquita` as their basal sister lineage ($\sim 2.39\text{ Ma}$).* **`[Well Supported]`** — Supported by `100%` ML bootstrap and `1.0` Bayesian posterior probability across $16,302\text{ bp}$, overturning earlier noisy D-loop topologies and explaining `vaquita` as a Northern Hemisphere relict of a Pliocene/early-Pleistocene trans-equatorial dispersal from the southern clade.
- **Claim 2:** *Parallel coastal vs oceanic ecological divergence occurred in the Northern (`harbor` vs `Dall's`, $\sim 3.12\text{ Ma}$) and Southern (`Burmeister's` vs `spectacled`, $\sim 2.14\text{ Ma}$) hemispheres during the late Pliocene/early Pleistocene, whereas all intraspecific subdivisions coalesced within the last $<1\text{ Myr}$.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Strongly grounded in the time-calibrated mitogenome tree (**Figures 1 and 2**) and paleoceanographic history.
- **Claim 3:** *Cryptic deep mitochondrial lineages exist within North Pacific harbor porpoises (`NP1`/`NP2`), Dall's porpoises (`DP1`/`DP2`), and spectacled porpoises (`SP1`/`SP2`).* **`[Well Supported]`** — Branch lengths and node ages between `NP1` and `NP2` exceed those between recognized Iberian and Mauritanian harbor porpoise ecotypes or between Yangtze and East Asian finless porpoises, though the authors appropriately note in `Conclusions` (line 87) that single-locus maternal mtDNA trees must be complemented by nuclear genome-wide data to assess incomplete lineage sorting or male-mediated gene flow.
- **Claim 4:** *Vaquita exhibits the lowest mitogenome diversity of any porpoise ($\pi = 0.02\%$, 13/16 segregating sites being singletons), reflecting a historically small effective population size ($N_e \approx 613 \to 1,665$) over the past $\sim 3,000\text{–}5,000\text{ years}$.* **`[Well Supported]`** — Supported by concordant `Fu & Li's D*/F*` ($p < 0.05$), mismatch distribution ($\tau = 4$), and Path-Sampling-validated `BSP` (**Figure 5**).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Table Completeness:** Detailed sequencing metrics, assembly sizes, and coverage depths per lineage are provided in **Table 1**, diversity and neutrality statistics in **Tables 2 and 3**, and individual sample metadata, accession numbers, and assembly comparisons are referenced in **Tables S1–S5** and **Texts S1–S2**.
- **Software & Parameter Reporting:** Software versions (`FastQC v0.11.5`, `Trimmomatic v0.36`, `Geneious v8.1.8`, `MITObim`, `MUSCLE`, `jModelTest2 v2.1.10`, `PhyML v3.0`, `MrBayes v3.2.6`, `BEAST v2.4.3`, `DnaSP v5.10.01`, `BPPML`, `TESTNH/MAPNH v1.3.0`, `Arlequin v3.5.2.2`, `RVAideMemoire v0.9-77`) and MCMC chain lengths/burn-in/ESS thresholds are thoroughly documented (aside from the log-space vs real-space calibration typo noted above).
