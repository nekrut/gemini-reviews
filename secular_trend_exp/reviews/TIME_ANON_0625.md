### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This short communication examines `p53` protein overexpression (via immunohistochemistry with monoclonal antibody PAb 1801) and chromosome 17p allelic loss (loss of heterozygosity, LOH, via Southern blotting with RFLP probes `pYNZ22.1` at 17p13.3 and `pBHP53`) in 30 consecutive primary human gastric carcinomas. Across the cohort, 4/30 cases (13.3%) showed nuclear p53 immunoreactivity and 9/24 informative cases (37.5%) showed LOH at `pYNZ22.1` (only 1/7 tested cases was informative for `pBHP53`, which showed LOH). Combining both criteria yielded 10 cases with LOH, IHC positivity, or both; 9 of these 10 cases had DNA available and were screened across the four conserved `p53` hotspot regions (exons 5–8) by constant denaturant gel electrophoresis (CDGE), perpendicular denaturing gradient gel electrophoresis (DGGE), and direct solid-phase dideoxy sequencing. Three missense mutations were identified: codon 175 (`CGC->CAC`, Arg->His), codon 173 (`GTG->ATG`, Val->Met), and codon 273 (`CGT->TGT`, Arg->Cys).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
**Strengths:**
- **Orthogonal Mutation Verification:** For the subset of samples screened by CDGE, aberrant bands were verified by perpendicular denaturing gradient profiles and directly sequenced on magnetic streptavidin beads, unambiguously establishing the nucleotide and amino acid substitutions in Cases 1, 3, and 6 (Table I, Figure 1).
- **Matched Normal Controls:** Frozen tumour tissue was paired with adjacent non-neoplastic gastric mucosa from the same patients to evaluate constitutional heterozygosity and LOH.

**Weaknesses & Methodological Audit:**
- **Severe Verification Bias (Two-Stage Conditional Screening):** Crucially, CDGE and sequencing were *only* performed on 9 of the 10 cases pre-selected for having either positive p53 IHC or 17p LOH (`pYNZ22.1`). The remaining 20/30 tumours (which were IHC-negative and either LOH-negative or uninformative at `pYNZ22.1`) were never screened for `p53` mutations. Because nonsense, frameshift, or splice mutations typically abolish p53 protein accumulation (IHC-negative) and can occur without large 17p13.3 deletions—and because 6/30 cases were uninformative for `pYNZ22.1`—the true mutation prevalence in the 30-patient cohort is unknown. Reporting the mutation prevalence as "three out of nine cases studied by CDGE, i.e., 33.3%" in the discussion conflates the conditional positive predictive value among pre-screened cases with the cohort-wide mutation rate (which is at least 3/29 = 10.3%, or unknown in the unscreened 20 tumours).
- **Locus Mismatch and Low Probe Informativeness:** LOH screening relied primarily on `pYNZ22.1` (17p13.3, telomeric to `TP53` at 17p13.1), while the `pBHP53` intragenic/proximal probe was uninformative (`NI`) in 6 of the 7 LOH cases tested (Table I). Thus, 17p13.3 LOH may reflect telomeric deletions not involving the `TP53` locus.
- **Underpowered Statistical Comparisons:** The comparison of p53 immunoreactivity between LOH-positive (3/9, 33.3%) and LOH-negative (1/15, 6.7%) cases yields $P = 0.099$ (test unspecified, matches one-tailed Fisher's exact test; two-tailed $P = 0.130$), which is severely underpowered due to $N = 24$ informative cases.
- **Table I Typographical Error:** In Table I, the bottom row is mislabelled as "Case 1" instead of "Case 10" (the sequence of rows is Case 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 1).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Specific missense `p53` mutations in hotspot B (codons 173, 175) and hotspot D (codon 273) occur in human gastric carcinoma. `[Well Supported]` by CDGE and direct sequencing (Table I).
- **Claim 2:** The prevalence of `p53` mutations in gastric carcinoma (stated as 13.3% by IHC and 33.3% by CDGE) is similar to breast cancer and recent gastric cancer reports, but lower than colon cancer. `[Fragile]` — The 33.3% (3/9) figure suffers from conditioning bias on IHC+/LOH+ pre-selection, and the small sample size ($N = 30$ total, $N = 9$ sequenced) yields wide confidence intervals (95% CI for 3/9 is 7.5%–70.1%; for 3/29 is 2.2%–27.4%).
- **Claim 3:** Discrepancies between `pYNZ22.1` LOH (7 cases without detected mutations) and CDGE results reflect physical distance between 17p13.3 and `p53` or mutations outside the four screened hotspots, while IHC+/mutation-negative cases (Cases 8 and 9) may reflect stromal contamination or non-hotspot mutations. `[Moderately Supported]` as plausible biological and technical explanations, though stromal cell percentage was not histologically quantified in the frozen DNA extracts.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Primer Sequences & CDGE Conditions:** While Figure 1 states the denaturant percentages (54% for fragment B, 46% for fragment D) and cites Børresen et al. for the CDGE protocol, the exact oligonucleotide primer sequences for the four hotspot PCR reactions and cycling parameters are not printed in the text.
- **Clinicopathological Metadata:** No patient-level or summary clinicopathological data (Lauren histological classification: intestinal vs. diffuse type, TNM stage, tumour grade, or stromal fraction) are provided for the 30 gastric carcinomas, limiting clinical interpretability.
