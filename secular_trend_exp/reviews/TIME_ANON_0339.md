### 1. Executive Summary
This cellular immunology study investigates why susceptibility to autoimmune insulin-dependent diabetes mellitus (IDDM) in nonobese diabetic (NOD) mice requires homozygosity for the unstable, poor-peptide-binding MHC class II molecule $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$. Utilizing a panel of MHC-congenic, F1 heterozygous, and transgenic mouse strains sharing the NOD non-MHC background (`NOD`, `NOD.I-A^k` [`NOD.H2^h4`], `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1`, `NOD I-A^k` transgenic, and `[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1`) alongside `B10.H2^g7` mice, the authors measure peripheral $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell "autoproliferation" (recall proliferation to endogenously presented self-antigens on naive irradiated APCs after priming with self-peptides `TCR Vβ8.2 38–60`, `MM 110–121`, or `MM 69–78` in CFA) and responses to foreign $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted (`SWM 110–121`) and $\text{I-A}^{\text{k}}$-restricted (`HEL 46–61`) peptides. They demonstrate that while `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` mice completely lose $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted autoproliferation and exhibit severely blunted in vivo priming to foreign `SWM 110–121`, both `NOD I-A^k` transgenic mice and hemizygous `[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1` mice retain robust $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted autoproliferation. Flow cytometry of peripheral $\text{B220}^+$ B cells and thymic $\text{CD11c}^+$ dendritic cells reveals that `NOD I-A^k` transgenic mice express 2- to 4-fold less $\text{I-A}^{\text{k}}$ (yielding a $\sim 4$-fold higher $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}:\text{I-A}^{\text{k}}$ surface ratio) than `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` mice. Crucially, irradiated `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` APCs present `SWM 110–121` to an established T-cell line at the expected ~50% semidominant efficiency in vitro, proving that the defect in `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` mice is central (thymic repertoire skewing away from $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ when a high-affinity competing MHC class II allele is co-expressed at equimolar levels) rather than peripheral APC failure.

The genetic dissection using hemizygous `[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1` and `B10.H2^g7` controls is elegant and provides compelling functional evidence for the thymic avidity/repertoire-skewing model of MHC class II homozygosity in autoimmune diabetes. The main shortcomings are the complete absence of formal statistical hypothesis tests despite a statement in the Methods, a tiny sample size ($n = 2$) in the hemizygous F1 experiment, and a media concentration typo in the Methods.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Statement of Statistical Analysis Without Reported Test Statistics or $p$-Values:** The final sentence of the Materials and Methods (line 49) states: "Statistical analysis was performed on Excel (Microsoft Corp., Redmond, WA)." However, not a single statistical test ($t$-test, ANOVA, Mann–Whitney), test statistic, or $p$-value appears anywhere in the Results text or figure legends. Moreover, Figures 1, 2, 3, and 4B–C display single representative experiments ("One representative experiment of four is shown") without summary means $\pm$ SEM across independent biological replicates or error bars on the thymidine incorporation curves.
- **Minimal Sample Size in Key Hemizygous Control (`[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1`):** The critical experiment ruling out a simple $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ gene-dosage effect (Figure 2) compares `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` ($n = 5$) against `[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1` using only **$n = 2$** `[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1` mice, without reporting replication across multiple litters or experiments.
- **1,000-Fold Concentration Typo for 2-Mercaptoethanol in T-Cell Media:** In Materials and Methods (line 43), the T-cell culture medium is described as supplemented with "`50 mM 2-ME`" (2-mercaptoethanol). Standard lymphocyte culture medium uses $50\,\mu\text{M}$ 2-ME; $50\,\text{mM}$ 2-ME is acutely cytotoxic and denatures proteins.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **[Well Supported]** Both $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ expression and non-MHC NOD background alleles ($Idd$ loci) are necessary—and neither alone is sufficient (`NOD.I-A^k` and `B10.H2^g7` are both negative)—to generate peripheral $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell autoproliferation.
- **[Well Supported]** The loss of $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted T-cell responses in `[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1` mice is not caused by an intrinsic inability of heterozygous F1 APCs to present $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted peptides (Fig. 4B) or by a simple 50% reduction in $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ gene dosage in the absence of a competing MHC class II molecule (`[NOD × NOD.I-A^null]F1`, Fig. 2).
- **[Well Supported]** The relative ratio of $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ to $\text{I-A}^{\text{k}}$ on thymic $\text{CD11c}^+$ dendritic cells and peripheral APCs governs whether the peripheral $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell repertoire is skewed away from $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$ (`[NOD × NOD.I-A^k]F1`) or permits positive selection and incomplete negative selection of $\text{I-A}^{\text{g7}}$-restricted autoreactive T cells (`NOD I-A^k` transgenic and `NOD`).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Mouse congenic generation numbers (`N6F16`, `N7F16`), antibody clones (`39J`, `10.3.6`, `AMS 32.1`), peptide residue coordinates, irradiation dose ($3,300\,\text{rads}$), and thymic dendritic cell isolation steps are well documented.
- **Deficiencies:** Exact amino acid sequences of the synthetic peptides (`MM 69–78`, `MM 110–121`, `SWM 110–121`, `HEL 46–61`, `TCR Vβ8.2 38–60`) are cited to prior work rather than listed; quantitative MFI summary statistics (mean $\pm$ SD across the 3 FACS experiments) are omitted.
