### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This landmark *Drosophila* genetics and cell biology study reports the isolation, molecular cloning, and ultrastructural/functional characterization of a female-sterile EMS mutation, *shi kong* (*sko*), demonstrating that it is allelic to *cheerio* (*cher$^1$*) and encodes *Drosophila* filamin (the ortholog of human nonmuscle $\alpha$-filamin / `ABP280`, mutated in human periventricular nodular heterotopia). After separating *sko* from an unlinked `Gl^1`-suppressor mutation by meiotic recombination on an isogenized third chromosome and mapping it to polytene interval `89E7–90A` (`89F`), the authors isolate a $3.2\text{-kb}$ ovary cDNA, two spanning cosmid clones ($\sim 30\text{-kb}$ transcription unit), and a full-length $7,536\text{-bp}$ cDNA (`GH12209`, encoding a 2,343-residue $\sim 250\text{-kDa}$ protein sharing $\sim 50\%$ identity with human $\alpha/\beta/\gamma$-filamins across an N-terminal actin-binding domain, 20 Ig-like rod repeats [lacking vertebrate repeats 6–9], two hinges, and a C-terminal dimerization tail). Using Northern blots and an affinity-purified rabbit polyclonal antibody (`4-3D`) raised against the C-terminal 90 residues, they show that the single-copy *filamin* gene produces a $7.5\text{-kb}$ mRNA ($\sim 250\text{-kDa}$ protein in ovaries, embryos, and adults) and a 3'-derived $3.0\text{-kb}$ mRNA ($\sim 97\text{-kDa}$ protein in embryos and adults lacking the N-terminal actin-binding domain); *sko/sko* eliminates both the $250\text{-kDa}$ and $97\text{-kDa}$ proteins, whereas *cher$^1$/cher$^1$* selectively eliminates the $250\text{-kDa}$ isoform. Mobilizing a $5'$ *P*-element (`EP(3)3715`, inserted $78\text{ bp}$ upstream of the cDNA) generates two targeted deletions (`EPSΔ5` and `EPSΔ12.1`) that fail to complement *sko* and phenocopy its ovarian defects. Confocal microscopy and thin-section transmission electron microscopy (TEM) establish that filamin localizes directly to germline ring canals from germarium regions 2–3 through stage 10b, and that loss of filamin spares cytokinesis, outer-rim assembly (anillin, phosphotyrosine, T-shaped plasma membrane junctions), and early microtubule/dynein-mediated oocyte polarity (*oskar*, *bicoid*, *gurken*, Staufen), but abolishes inner-rim F-actin bundle recruitment, Hts stabilization, Kelch accumulation, ring canal expansion, and nurse-cell plasma membrane integrity.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Unsequenced Molecular Lesions in the EMS-Induced *sko* and *cher$^1$* Alleles:**
  - While the authors prove beyond doubt that *sko* and *cher$^1$* are alleles of *filamin* (via non-complementation with each other and with two targeted *P*-element excision deletions `EPSΔ5` and `EPSΔ12.1` mapped by genomic Southern blotting, plus loss of the $250\text{-kDa}$ and/or $97\text{-kDa}$ bands on Western blots), the exact nucleotide mutations on the *sko* and *cher$^1$* chromosomes were not sequenced (`Line 138`). Because antibody `4-3D` recognizes the C-terminal 90 amino acids, a nonsense or frameshift mutation truncating the C-terminus in *sko* could still leave an N-terminal truncated polypeptide undetected by the C-terminal antiserum (a possibility the authors explicitly acknowledge in `Line 138`).
- **Single-Strand Sequencing of Portions of the $7.5\text{-kb}$ EST cDNA (`Line 49`):**
  - The authors transparently note in Materials and Methods (`Line 49`) that while the entire $7,536\text{-bp}$ `GH12209` sequence was manually proofread on an ABI377 sequencer, portions were sequenced on only one strand.
- **Uncharacterized $\sim 140\text{-kDa}$ Immunoreactive Polypeptide on Western Blots (`Figure 8a`):**
  - In addition to the $250\text{-kDa}$ and $97\text{-kDa}$ filamin isoforms that vanish in *sko/sko* mutants, the blot-affinity-purified `4-3D` antibody detects a minor $\sim 140\text{-kDa}$ band in ovary and adult extracts that persists in both *sko/sko* and *cher$^1$/cher$^1$* mutants (`Line 133`, `Line 138`). Testing `EPSΔ12.1` (full transcription-unit deficiency, if viable or in somatic clones) on Western blot would definitively distinguish a cross-reacting non-filamin protein from an alternative transcript.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality / STRENGTHS
- **[Well Supported] Gold-Standard Four-Pronged Genetic Proof That *sko* and *cheerio* Encode *Drosophila* Filamin:** Combining (1) meiotic and deficiency mapping + polytene *in situ* hybridization to `89F`, (2) non-complementation between *sko* and *cher$^1$* plus isoform-specific loss of the $250\text{-kDa}$ vs. $250\text{-kDa}+97\text{-kDa}$ polypeptides on Western blots, (3) loss of ring-canal immunostaining in *sko* ovaries, and (4) targeted generation of imprecise *P*-element excision deletions (`EPSΔ5`, `EPSΔ12.1`) that fail to complement *sko* and reproduce its exact ring-canal and actin phenotypes is a textbook model of rigorous *Drosophila* genetics.
- **[Well Supported] Ultrastructural (TEM) and Confocal Dissection of Outer-Rim vs. Inner-Rim Ring Canal Assembly:** Pairing multi-marker confocal microscopy (phalloidin, anillin, phosphotyrosine `PY20`, ring-canal-specific Hts, Kelch) with stage-matched thin-section TEM (`Figure 3`) cleanly demonstrates that the electron-dense outer-rim coat and T-shaped membrane junctions form with anillin in *sko* mutants, whereas circumferential inner-rim F-actin bundles fail to tether to the membrane, leading to ring canal collapse and nurse-cell membrane vesiculation/rupture by stages 5–6.
- **[Well Supported] Separation of Early Slow Microtubule Transport From Late Actomyosin Dumping:** Demonstrating that cytoplasmic dynein, Staufen, *oskar*, *bicoid*, and *gurken* localize normally to the anterior/posterior poles of *sko* oocytes (`Figure 4`) rules out a general transport block prior to stage 10b nurse-cell dumping.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reproducibility Assessment:** Full sequence data for the $7,536\text{-bp}$ *Drosophila* filamin cDNA are deposited in GenBank/EMBL/DDBJ under accession `GH12209` (alongside comparative accessions `113001`, `328597`, `4218955`, `1079404`), PCR primer sequences for the `6xHis` C-terminal antigen are provided verbatim (`Line 58`), fly stock genotypes and Bloomington/BDGP identifiers (`EP(3)3715`, `Df(3R)C4`) are listed, and antibody dilutions and staining protocols are completely specified.
