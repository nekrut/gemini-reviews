### 1. Executive Summary
This study conducts an *in silico* genome-wide cataloguing of the *Aux/IAA* gene family in hexaploid bread wheat (*Triticum aestivum* "Chinese Spring") and its diploid progenitors *T. urartu* (AA) and *Aegilops tauschii* (DD). Using HMMER (PF02309) and BLAST searches against early draft chromosome-arm assemblies, the authors identify 84 full-length *TaIAA* coding sequences clustered into 34 homoeologous groups across 20 chromosomes (absent on 2D), alongside 27 *TuIAA* and 28 *AetIAA* sequences. They perform Neighbor-Joining (NJ) phylogenetic clustering, MEME motif discovery (capped at 4 motifs), exon-intron structure display, microarray probe look-ups via PLEXdb/GeneVestigator across 19 organs, synteny/collinearity mapping via Circos, and pairwise $K_a/K_s$ estimation against a barley outgroup for 12 selected orthologous triads/pairs.

While providing a descriptive inventory of wheat *Aux/IAA* homoeologs, the study follows a formulaic commodity "gene family characterization" template and suffers from substantial methodological limitations: (1) uncritical reliance on unvalidated distance-based (NJ) trees without evolutionary model selection, (2) conflation of allopolyploid homoeology (whole-genome hybridization) with within-genome "segmental replication," (3) inability of legacy microarray probe sets to resolve sub-genome-specific homoeolog expression (all A, B, and D copies share a single probe, and several paralogous groups cross-hybridize to identical probes), and (4) an arbitrary, un-tested pairwise $K_a/K_s$ calculation on only 12 of the 34 groups without statistical tests for branch- or site-level selection or sub-genome rate asymmetry.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Phylogenetic Inference & Orthology Assignment:** All trees (84 *TaIAA* CDS in Fig 1A; 159 multi-species proteins in Fig 5; wheat–progenitor trees in Fig S2) are built exclusively via Neighbor-Joining (NJ) in MEGA 6.0 after ClustalW alignment. No amino acid or nucleotide substitution model (e.g., JTT, WAG, GTR+$\Gamma$) or gamma rate heterogeneity parameter is specified, nor is Maximum Likelihood (ML) or Bayesian inference used to verify deep monocot-dicot nodes. Note also an internal count discrepancy in Figure 5 caption ("85 wheat, 31 rice, 29 Arabidopsis, 7 tomato, 2 maize, 2 grape, 1 sorghum, and 1 pear" = 158, whereas the text states 84 *TaIAAs* and 159 total proteins).
* **Terminology & Evolutionary Model Conflation (Allopolyploidy vs. Segmental Duplication):** In Materials & Methods ("Expansion pattern analysis"), the authors state: *"Paralogs from different sub-genomes of wheat were regarded as segmental replication."* Biologically and evolutionarily, A, B, and D sub-genome copies in hexaploid wheat are **homoeologs** arising from allopolyploidization (interspecific hybridization of *T. urartu*, an *Ae. speltoides*-like B progenitor, and *Ae. tauschii*), not intra-genomic segmental duplications. Furthermore, inferring gene loss (e.g., *TaIAA14-B*, *TaIAA17-B*) or interchromosomal translocation (*TaIAA27-A*, *TaIAA32-A*) from an incomplete draft chromosome-arm shotgun assembly without checking assembly gaps or unanchored scaffolds is prone to false-positive deletion calls; notably, 43 initial HMM hits (127 non-redundant hits minus 84 full-length sequences) were discarded without reporting whether the "missing" homoeologs were simply truncated on draft contigs.
* **Microarray Cross-Hybridization & Homoeolog Resolution Failure:** To profile expression across 19 organs, the authors map the 34 *TaIAA* groups to PLEXdb/GeneVestigator microarray probes. Because A, B, and D homoeologs share high sequence identity, all sub-genome copies of a given *TaIAA* group map to the same probe. Worse, distinct paralogous groups share identical probes (*TaIAA2* and *9*; *TaIAA18*, *22*, and *33*; *TaIAA29* and *30*), reducing 84 genes (34 groups) to 28 non-specific probes. Claiming "functional redundancy" between *TaIAA29* and *TaIAA30* because of "identical expression patterns" (Discussion, line 205) when both genes query the exact same microarray probe (Results, line 154) is a circular technical artifact.
* **$K_a/K_s$ Selection Analysis:** The "Adaptive evolution analysis" computes pairwise $K_a/K_s$ via PAL2NAL/PAML (`codeml`) for only 12 of the 34 groups using barley (*Hordeum vulgare*) as an outgroup (Table 2). No rationale is given for why 22 groups were excluded, no likelihood ratio tests (LRTs) against neutral models ($\omega = 1$) or branch/site models are performed, and separate $K_a$ and $K_s$ values are omitted (preventing inspection for $K_s$ saturation or $K_a=0$ artifacts, e.g., *TaIAA22-A* $\omega = 0.0023$).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *"Thirty-four Aux/IAA genes were identified, 30 of which have duplicated genes on the A, B or D sub-genome, with a total of 84 Aux/IAA sequences."* — **[Moderately Supported]**. The bioinformatic screen of the draft assembly identifies 84 full-length ORFs, though only *TaIAA1-A* through *TaIAA15-A* (35 sequences) are actually printed in Table 1 (truncated in the main text), and 43 partial HMM hits were excluded without detailing how many represent assembly fragmentation of the "missing" homoeologs.
* **Claim 2:** *"Despite several genes, including TaIAA29 and TaIAA30, that may have a functional redundancy because of their identical expression patterns (Figure 6)..."* — **[Methodologically Flawed]**. As noted in Results (line 154), *TaIAA29* and *TaIAA30* share the exact same microarray probe. Their "identical expression pattern" in Figure 6 is a trivial consequence of plotting the same probe data twice (or cross-hybridization), not biological co-expression.
* **Claim 3:** *"Compared with TaIAA5-A, the motifs of TaIAA5-B and TaIAA5-D transform Motif 1 + 2 + 3 to Motif 2 + 3 + 4, which may bring out a novel function."* — **[Fragile]**. Without RT-PCR/RACE cloning or RNA-seq read validation of exon-intron boundaries, apparent N-terminal or C-terminal domain absences in draft wheat gene models (such as missing Domain I or IV, or *TaIAA5-D* having a 3,289 bp ORF vs. 1,959 bp in *TaIAA5-B*) frequently reflect automated gene-prediction boundary errors rather than genuine evolutionary neofunctionalization.
* **Claim 4:** *"The duplicated genes have undergone an evolutionary process of purifying selection..."* — **[Moderately Supported]**. Gene-wide pairwise $\omega < 1$ between wheat and barley is expected for conserved core hormone signaling transcription factors, though calling this an "adaptive evolution analysis" is a misnomer when only purifying constraint ($\omega \in [0.002, 0.544]$) is tested.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Sequence & Coordinate Reporting:** Table 1 lists Ensembl/URGI sequence IDs, scaffold numbers, and chromosome arm coordinates for *TaIAA1-A* through *TaIAA15-A*, but omits *TaIAA15-B* through *TaIAA34-D* from the main table. External database URLs (URGI, Ensembl Plants, GigaDB for *T. urartu* `100050` and *Ae. tauschii* `100054`) are provided.
* **Parameter & Pipeline Gaps:** Specific assembly release versions for URGI/Ensembl Plants, exact ClustalW gap penalties, MEGA 6.0 distance correction models (e.g., p-distance vs. Poisson vs. JTT), and PAML `codeml` control file settings (`runmode`, `CodonFreq`, `model`, `NSsites`) are not reported. No wet-lab validation is performed.
