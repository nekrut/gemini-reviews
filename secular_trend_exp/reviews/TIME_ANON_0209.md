### 1. Executive Summary
This detailed methodological protocol and validation paper describes a yeast transformation-associated recombination (TAR) reverse genetics pipeline for assembling, mutagenizing, and rescuing full-length infectious cDNA clones of human coronavirus OC43 (HCoV-OC43) and three fluorescent reporter viruses (`mClover3-H2B`, `mRuby3-H2B`, and `mCardinal`) in yeast centromeric plasmid/bacterial artificial chromosome (`YCpBAC`) shuttle vectors. The reporter cassettes are inserted into the intergenic region between the viral $M$ and $N$ genes under the control of the authentic $N$ transcription regulatory sequence (TRS), with a duplicated $N$ TRS (`TRS*`) driving $N$, thereby generating an eighth subgenomic mRNA without deleting any viral genes. Full-length DNA-launched clones (driven by a 5' CMV promoter and terminated by a 3' poly(A)–HDV ribozyme–BGH poly(A) cassette) are rescued via PEI transfection of HEK 293T cells (co-transfected with an HCoV-OC43 $N$ helper plasmid) followed by BHK-21 co-culture. Validation experiments in HEK 293A cells ($\text{MOI} = 0.1$, $n = 3$ biological replicates) demonstrate that all three reporter viruses replicate to infectious $\text{TCID}_{50}$ titers indistinguishable from wild-type HCoV-OC43 (ATCC VR-1558), express expected viral proteins (N, HE, S) by Western blot, and produce the predicted size-shifted 3'-coterminal sgRNAs by chemiluminescent Northern blot.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Protocol Granularity & Experimental Controls:** The protocol includes explicit quality-control checkpoints at every stage: (1) a "vector-only" linearized YCpBAC transformation control in *S. cerevisiae* VL6-48N spheroplasts to monitor non-homologous background; (2) *Sgr*AI restriction digestion of donor sub-clones (`OC43TAR456`) to destroy donor vector backbone without requiring gel extraction of large fragments; (3) phase-contrast microscopy verification of spheroplast integrity in 1 M sorbitol vs. 2% SDS lysis (Fig. 5); (4) multiplex colony PCR screening across recombination junctions followed by whole-plasmid sequencing (e.g., Plasmidsaurus) after *Stbl3 E. coli* electroporation; and (5) mock-plasmid transfection controls in 293T/BHK-21 co-culture to distinguish PEI transfection toxicity from viral CPE.
- **Validation Rigor (Figure 6):** Replication kinetics (6 and 24 hpi, $n = 3$ biological replicates) are evaluated by Spearman-Kärber $\text{TCID}_{50}$ assays and efficiency-corrected $2^{-\Delta\Delta C_q}$ RT-qPCR (normalized to 18S rRNA) with two-way ANOVA and Tukey post-hoc correction. Non-radioactive biotinylated oligonucleotide Northern blotting (`Chemi-Northern` targeting nt 469–512 of *OC43-N*) cleanly resolves the 1.7 kb $N$ sgRNA and the exact upward size shifts (+1,246 nt for mClover-H2B, +1,239 nt for mRuby-H2B, +912 nt for mCardinal) of the upstream *Rep*, *M*, and *E* sgRNAs.
- **Minor Quantitative & Recipe Typographical Errors:**
  - **Table 2 Molarity Calculation Error (line 693–695, 706):** In Table 2, row 1 (`CMVn-OC43-WT-Ribo-BGH-YCpBAC + PmeI/I-SceI`), the concentration is `[ng/µL] = 82` and `Size (bp) = 33,734`. The true molar concentration is $56 \div (11,595 \times 0.660) = 0.00732\text{ pmol}/\mu\text{L}$, whereas Table 2 lists `0.0415 pmol/µL` (an ~5.7-fold arithmetic error in the example table). Furthermore, the footnote formula `‡` on line 706 omits a factor of 2 (using $660\text{ pg/pmol}$ per nucleotide instead of per base pair, or dividing by nucleotides rather than base pairs).
  - **Recipe 21 (50$\times$ TAE buffer, line 457) & Recipe 29 (2$\times$ Laemmli buffer, line 553):** In Recipe 21, `93.05 g` of EDTA disodium dihydrate ($\text{MW} = 372.24\text{ g/mol}$) in $1\text{ L}$ is **250 mM** (or requires 100 mL of 0.5 M EDTA for 50 mM final), whereas the table column says `Final concentration: 50 mM` alongside `93.05 g` (and 93.05 g of EDTA will not dissolve in 80 mL of water at pH 8).
