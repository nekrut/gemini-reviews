### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This virology study establishes the first bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC)-based reverse genetics infectious cDNA clone (`pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC`, GenBank `MT361640`) and a red fluorescent protein (`tdTomato`) reporter derivative replacing `ORF5` (`pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC-RFP`) for a contemporary epidemic Clade B Middle East respiratory syndrome coronavirus (MERS-CoV) isolate (`KNIH002`, `KT029139`) from the South Korean nosocomial outbreak. The authors verify infectious virus rescue (`YKC P0`/`P1`) via cytopathic effect, Spike (`S`) and Nucleocapsid (`N`) immunoblotting, Northern blotting of genomic and 7 subgenomic RNAs, leader-TRS junction sequencing, and a silent `G7622C` signature marker in `nsp3`. Comparative multi-step growth curves ($\text{MOI} = 0.001$), `sgRNA8-N` RT-qPCR, and plaque morphology assays in Vero E6 cells show that both parental `KNIH002` (`P5`) and rescued `YKC` (`P1`) are significantly attenuated (~1.5-log$_{10}$ lower peak titer and smaller plaque size) relative to the prototype Clade A `EMC` strain (`JX869059`), and that `YKC` is further attenuated relative to its own parental `KNIH002` stock. Finally, Sanger sequencing of 6 cDNA clones from passage 0 (`P0`) and passage 3 (`P3`) of clone-rescued `YKC` demonstrates rapid *in vitro* accumulation of `ORF5` point, frameshift deletion, TRS deletion, and Spike-complementary insertion mutants during serial passage in Vero E6 cells. While the BAC infectious clone and `YKC-RFP` reporter represent valuable community tools and the demonstration of rapid *in vitro* `ORF5` mutation is convincing, the comparative phenotypic interpretations are tempered by three cell-culture-adaptive nonsynonymous mutations fixed inside the `YKC` clone, unmatched passage histories between `EMC` and `KNIH002`, reliance on IFN-deficient Vero E6 cells, and underpowered biological replication ($N = 2$ independent experiments) in the growth kinetic comparisons.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Incorporation of Three Cell-Culture-Acquired Nonsynonymous Mutations into the `pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC` Clone Confounds Comparison with Parental `KNIH002`:**
  - Because `KNIH002` was passaged 3 times in Vero E6 and 3 times in Huh7 cells (`P6`) prior to RNA extraction for cDNA cloning, the authors identified 8 point mutations fixed across their RT-PCR clones relative to the published `KNIH002` (`P3`) reference sequence (`KT029139`), including three nonsynonymous substitutions (`C6286T` $\rightarrow$ `A2003V` in Nsp3, `C11061T` $\rightarrow$ `P3595S` in Nsp6, and `T26109C` $\rightarrow$ `M6T` in the interferon antagonist `ORF4b`) and five synonymous mutations (Table 2). Rather than reverting these culture-acquired mutations to match the authentic `KT029139` reference genome, the authors retained all 8 mutations in `pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC`. Consequently, rescued `YKC` (`P1`) exhibits significant growth attenuation, lower N/S protein expression, and a ~60% reduction in plaque area ($P = 0.0051$) compared even to its own parental `KNIH002` (`P5`) stock (Fig. 4B–E), meaning `YKC` does not fully phenocopy wild-type `KNIH002`.
- **Unmatched Passage Histories and Exclusive Use of IFN-Deficient Vero E6 Cells for Clade A (`EMC`) vs Clade B (`KNIH002`) Phenotypic Comparisons:**
  - As acknowledged in the Discussion (Offset 46080, line 11), the `EMC` stock used for growth comparisons had an unknown passage history (`likely multiple more passages after sequencing analysis using a P6 stock`), confounding whether its 1.5-log$_{10}$ higher titer in Vero E6 cells reflects intrinsic Clade A vs Clade B biology or extensive laboratory adaptation of the `EMC` stock to Vero E6 cells.
  - Furthermore, all comparative growth curves, sgRNA quantifications, and plaque assays (Fig. 4) were performed exclusively in **Vero E6 cells** (African green monkey kidney cells carrying a chromosomal deletion of the type I interferon locus). In the Discussion, the authors speculate extensively that amino acid differences in viral IFN antagonists (`Nsp3`, `ORF4b`, `ORF5`, and `M`) may drive the growth rate differences between `EMC` and `KNIH002`/`YKC`; however, type I IFN-mediated growth differences cannot be evaluated in IFN-deficient Vero E6 cells without testing IFN-competent human respiratory epithelial cells (e.g., Calu-3 or primary human airway cultures).
- **Discrepancy Between Methods ($N \ge 3$) and Figure Legends ($N = 2$ or $N = 1$) for Biological Replication:**
  - The Statistical Analysis section (line 99) states: *"Unless otherwise specified, quantitative results were presented as mean ± standard deviation (SD) of at least three independent experiments."* However, the legends for both Figure 3B (`YKC` vs `YKC-RFP` growth curve) and Figure 4B (`EMC` vs `KNIH002` vs `YKC` growth curve) state: *"The results are the mean ± SD of two independent experiments with two replicates"*, and the legend for Figure 4D (`sgRNA8-N` copy number by RT-qPCR) states: *"The average and range of two technical replicates is shown"* ($N = 1$ independent experiment). Computing two-tailed unpaired Student's $t$-tests with `**** P < 0.0001` by pooling 4 wells across 2 experiments as if they were independent biological replicates is statistically improper.
- **Small Sample Size ($n = 6$ Clones) for Quasispecies Profiling and Minor Table 2 Footnote Error:**
  - The analysis of `ORF5` genetic instability in `P0` and `P3` (Fig. 5B) relies on Sanger sequencing of only 6 `pCR2.1-TOPO` clones per passage rather than deep amplicon sequencing. Notably, 3 of the 6 clones in `P0` already carried the `C61G` (`nt 27,020`) mutation immediately after rescue co-culture, suggesting either a mutation arising during the initial BHK-21/Huh7 rescue or plasmid heterogeneity.
  - In Table 2 footnote `d` (line 111), the text states *"the 'T → A' change at nt 26,109 results in a silent mutation at the ORF4a and a nonsynonymous substitution at the ORF4b"*, whereas Table 2 and the main text correctly list `nt 26,109` as a **`T → C`** substitution (`T → A` is at `nt 25,968`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality / STRENGTHS
- **Strengths:**
  - Thorough molecular validation of the rescued Clade B infectious clone (`YKC`) and `tdTomato` reporter virus (`YKC-RFP`), including full-genome Sanger verification (`MT361640`), Northern blotting resolving genomic RNA and all 7 subgenomic mRNAs (Fig. 2C), chromatogram verification of leader-TRS fusion sites (`sgRNA8-N` and `sgRNA5-RFP`) and the `G7622C` genetic marker (Fig. 2D–E, Fig. 3A), and demonstration that replacing `ORF5` with `tdTomato` does not impair viral replication in Vero E6 cells (Fig. 3B–C).
  - Clear demonstration that starting from a genetically homogeneous BAC clone (`YKC P0`), just three serial passages in Vero E6 cells generate multiple disruptive `ORF5` variants (including a 7-nt TRS deletion, 6-nt and 16-nt coding frameshift deletions, and an 11-nt insertion complementary to the Spike gene), providing an important cautionary lesson for MERS-CoV laboratory stock management.
- **Claim Proportionality:**
  - `[Well Supported]` Establishment of a functional BAC-based reverse genetics system (`pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC`) and replication-competent RFP reporter virus (`YKC-RFP`) for a Clade B Korean MERS-CoV isolate.
  - `[Well Supported]` `ORF5` is dispensable for *in vitro* replication in Vero E6 cells and rapidly accumulates deletion/insertion mutations within 3 passages of clone-rescued virus.
  - `[Moderately Supported]` `KNIH002` and `YKC` exhibit attenuated replication and smaller plaque morphology compared to `EMC` in Vero E6 cells (supported *in vitro*, though potentially confounded by passage history and the 3 culture-adapted mutations in `YKC`).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- The complete sequence of the `pBAC-MERS-CoV/YKC` clone is deposited in GenBank (`MT361640`), and all cloning/sequencing/RT-qPCR primers, restriction sites, antibody catalog numbers, and BSL-3 protocols are fully detailed in Table 1 and the Methods section.
