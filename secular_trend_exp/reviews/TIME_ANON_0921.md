### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This study presents a chromosome-scale haplotype-resolved/triploid genome assembly ($3x = 45$, $8.57\text{ Gb}$ anchored across 45 chromosomes, contig $\text{N50} = 8.76\text{ Mb}$, scaffold $\text{N50} = 178.9\text{ Mb}$, BUSCO $= 94.35\%$, 72,675 annotated protein-coding genes) of the early-diverging angiosperm *Chloranthus spicatus* using PacBio HiFi ($122.62\text{ Gb}$), Illumina short reads ($227.40\text{ Gb}$), and Hi-C ($510\text{ Gb}$). Using comparative phylogenomics across 25 seed plants (1,092 and 517 low-copy nuclear genes analyzed via RAxML concatenated ML and ASTRAL coalescent trees), $K_s$/4DTv synteny analysis, UPLC-ESI-QTRAP-MS/MS targeted metabolomics (identifying 49 coumarins across root, stem, leaf, stamen, and floral axis), and tissue-level RNA-seq co-expression ($n=3$), the authors propose a spatially compartmentalized biosynthetic pathway for isofraxidin involving an expanded coumarin synthase (`COSY`, 115 genes) family, `CYP71` hydroxylases, and $O$-methyltransferases (`OMT`). While the primary deliverable—a contiguous 8.57 Gb triploid genome assembly—is technically solid, the multi-omics pathway elucidation relies entirely on gene-metabolite Pearson correlations across 5 tissues without any in vitro or in planta enzymatic validation (despite falsely claiming "complemented by enzymatic verification" in the Conclusions), and the gene family expansion analysis (`CAFE` / `COSY` count of 115 vs. 29 in *Arabidopsis*) fails to account for the fact that all three subgenomes (45 chromosomes, 72,675 genes) of the autotriploid *C. spicatus* were input into OrthoMCL alongside monoploid assemblies of diploid comparators, artificially tripling *C. spicatus* gene counts!

### STRENGTHS
- **High-contiguity 8.57 Gb autotriploid chromosome-scale genome assembly:** Achieving an 8.76 Mb contig N50 and anchoring 99% of an 8.57 Gb repeat-rich (73.7% TE) triploid genome ($3x = 45$) into 45 pseudochromosomes using PacBio HiFi and 510 Gb of Hi-C data (validated by DAPI karyotyping and 94.35% BUSCO completeness) is a substantial genomic resource for an early-diverging angiosperm clade.
- **Comprehensive phylogenomic and metabolomic profiling:** Reconstructing the placement of Chloranthaceae + Magnoliids using both concatenated site-heterogeneous ML and ASTRAL coalescent models on 1,092/517 low-copy genes, alongside tissue-specific UPLC-QTRAP-MS/MS profiling of 49 coumarins across 5 organs, provides valuable evolutionary and chemotaxonomic data.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Severe ploidy confounding in OrthoMCL / CAFE gene family expansion and `COSY` copy-number comparisons:** The assembled *C. spicatus* genome is an **autotriploid** ($3x = 45$) containing all three homologous chromosome sets and **72,675** predicted protein-coding genes (~24,225 genes per monoploid complement, whereas diploid *Chloranthus* species have $2n=30$ and ~21,000–24,000 genes). In Materials and Methods ("Identification of whole-genome duplication", line 46), the authors explicitly state that they used "monoploid chromosome representatives" for $K_s$/4DTv WGD analysis, **but** for gene family clustering (`OrthoMCL`, line 151) and expansion analysis (`CAFE`, line 63) and `COSY` mining (line 86), they used the full unphased triploid annotation of 72,675 genes against monoploid reference proteomes of *Arabidopsis thaliana* and *Amborella trichopoda*. Specifically, the authors claim a "dramatic lineage-specific proliferation" of `COSY` with **115 members** in *C. spicatus* vs. **44** in *Amborella* and **29** in *Arabidopsis* (lines 86 and 109). Divided by 3 for the three allelic chromosome sets in the autotriploid assembly, $115 / 3 = 38.3$ `COSY` loci per monoploid genome—which is actually *fewer* than *Amborella* (44)! Similarly, the 138 "expanded" gene families in CAFE (Figure 2D) are heavily confounded by counting 3 allelic copies per locus in *C. spicatus*.
- **Direct contradiction regarding enzymatic verification and inconsistent enzyme nomenclature:**
  - In **Conclusions** (line 185), the authors state: *"Through integrated multi-omics analysis **complemented by enzymatic verification**, we have elucidated the core regulatory framework"* and Figure 3's legend (line 74) states *"the experimentally resolved pathway of isofraxidin"*. Yet no enzymatic assays, recombinant protein expression, or biochemical activity measurements were performed anywhere in the study—as the next paragraph of the Conclusions (line 187) and Discussion (line 115) admit: *"However, experimental validation of top candidate genes... remains a current limitation."*
  - In **Results** (line 91), the authors state: *"PAL-mediated conversion of phenylalanine to cinnamic acid exhibited peak catalytic activity in root tissues"*, when only transcript FPKM and metabolite peak areas (not enzyme catalytic activity) were measured.
  - In **Results** (lines 71, 86, 91), coumaroyl-CoA 2'-hydroxylase (`C2'H`) is repeatedly misidentified as **CYP74** (`C2'H, CYP74`). In plant biochemistry, `C2'H` (`F6'H1`) belongs to the **2-oxoglutarate-dependent dioxygenase (2-ODD)** superfamily, whereas `CYP74` enzymes are cytochrome P450 allene oxide synthases / hydroperoxide lyases (`HPL`/`AOS`) in the jasmonate oxylipin pathway!
  - In **Results** (line 96) and **Discussion** (line 113), the candidate methyltransferases are identified as `OMT_5/6/8`, whereas **Conclusions** (line 185) changes them to `OMT_5/6/7`.
- **Unadjusted Pearson correlation fishing across 267 candidate genes and 5 tissues:** Selecting gene–metabolite pairs with $r \ge 0.80, p < 0.05$ across 5 tissues without false discovery rate (FDR) correction or accounting for tissue autocorrelation guarantees high rates of coincidental co-expression among tissue-specific genes. Furthermore, proposing an obligatory inter-organ transport pathway where leaf `CYP71_1–4` makes esculetin, stamen `CYP71_11–15` makes fraxetin, and root/stem `OMT_5/6/8` methylates fraxetin to isofraxidin ignores the physiological implausibility of roots importing coumarin intermediates exclusively from flower stamens during vegetative growth.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1 (*Chloranthus spicatus* is an autotriploid [$3x = 45$] with an 8.57 Gb genome dominated by LTR retrotransposons [63.5%] and a single ancient Chloranthaceae-specific WGD [$K_s \approx 1.1$]): `[Well Supported]`** — Strongly supported by cytogenetics, $k$-mer spectra, HiFi + Hi-C assembly, and monoploid $K_s$/4DTv distributions.
- **Claim 2 (Chloranthaceae + Magnoliids form a clade sister to Eudicots): `[Well Supported]`** — Supported across 1,092 and 517 nuclear loci under both concatenated ML (site-heterogeneous models) and ASTRAL coalescent inference.
- **Claim 3 (The `COSY` family underwent dramatic lineage-specific expansion to 115 members in *C. spicatus* compared to 44 in *Amborella*): `[Methodologically Flawed]`** — Confounded by comparing the uncollapsed 3-haplotype autotriploid gene catalog (72,675 genes; $115 / 3 \approx 38$ per haplotype) against monoploid gene catalogs of diploid species.
- **Claim 4 (`CYP71_12/13/15` and `OMT_5/6/8` have been enzymatically verified to catalyze fraxetin and isofraxidin synthesis via obligatory stamen-to-root inter-organ shuttling): `[Overstated]`** — Purely correlative RNA-seq/metabolomics observations without biochemical or genetic validation, and claiming `CYP74` as coumaroyl-CoA 2'-hydroxylase is a biochemical annotation error.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
No public repository accession numbers (NCBI BioProject/SRA, CNCB/NGDC Genome Warehouse, or MetaboLights) are provided in the manuscript for the 8.57 Gb genome assembly, PacBio HiFi reads, Hi-C data, RNA-seq reads, or metabolomics datasets.
