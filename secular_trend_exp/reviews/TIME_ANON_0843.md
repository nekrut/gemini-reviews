### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This integrative evolutionary genomics and ultrastructural symbiosis study (`TIME_ANON_0843`) investigates the macroevolutionary replacement of obligate bacterial endosymbionts (*Candidatus* Karelsulcia muelleri and *Candidatus* Hodgkinia cicadicola) by *Ophiocordyceps*-allied yeast-like fungal symbionts (YLS) across 133 cicada species (65 newly sampled species comprising 236 dissected individuals plus 68 species from public databases, spanning all five subfamilies of Cicadidae). Combining diagnostic PCR and RFLP-cloning (~200 clones/replicate), fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) across adult bacteriomes, fat bodies, gonads, and staged embryos, transmission electron microscopy (TEM), metagenomic and whole-genome sequencing (assembling 38 cicada mitogenomes, 21 complete *Karelsulcia* genomes, 11 *Hodgkinia* scaffolds in *Chremistica nigra*, and 21 YLS fungal mitogenomes), BUSCO/USCO phylogenomics (`IQ-TREE` ML and `MrBayes` BI with `nRCFV` compositional heterogeneity filtering), and fossil-calibrated `MCMCTree` divergence dating (`7` fossil calibrations), the authors discover a remarkable transitional state in *Chremistica ochracea*. While 80% ($24/30$) of *Chr. ochracea* adults harbor only *Karelsulcia* in the bacteriomes and YLS in the fat bodies, 20% ($6/30$) additionally retain a severely degraded, cell-envelope-less *Hodgkinia* nested inside the cytoplasm of *Karelsulcia* cells (`endosymbiont-within-an-endosymbiont`) that is co-transmitted transovarially into embryos alongside *Karelsulcia* and YLS.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Rigorous Ruling Out of Cross-Contamination for the Unusual *Chr. ochracea* *Hodgkinia* 16S rRNA Placement:** Because the 16S rRNA sequence of *Hodgkinia* in *Chr. ochracea* grouped phylogenetically with *Hodgkinia* from the distantly related cicada genus *Tettigetta* (`Supplementary Fig. 1`), a critical forensic question is whether PCR contamination occurred. The authors address this directly and convincingly (lines 125, 155–168): (1) the *Chr. ochracea* RFLP clones were generated and sequenced prior to the collection of *Tettigetta* specimens (with raw clone electropherograms and RFLP gel images deposited in CNCB OMIX `OMIX009590`); (2) sequence-specific fluorescent probes (`Cy5-Hodgkinia`, `Cy3-Karelsulcia`, `FAM-YLS`) with no-probe and single-probe negative controls localized the exact *Hodgkinia* rRNA target inside *Karelsulcia* cells in tissue sections (`Fig. 6a–l`); and (3) TEM independently confirmed the physical presence of envelope-deficient *Hodgkinia* cells inside the cytoplasm of *Karelsulcia* (`Fig. 6d, h`).
- **Inability to Assemble the Metagenomic *Hodgkinia* Genome from *Chr. ochracea*:** While the authors assembled 11 *Hodgkinia* scaffolds (`588,504 bp`, including 2 PCR-verified circular chromosomes `CP178246` and `CP178247`) from the congeneric species *Chremistica nigra* (which lacks YLS and retains canonical bacteriome *Hodgkinia*) and complete *Karelsulcia* genomes from 21 species, they were unable to assemble the *Hodgkinia* genome from *Chr. ochracea* despite 10 Gb of Illumina metagenomic reads (`lines 56, 100`). As the authors transparently acknowledge, extreme sequence degeneration, low cellular titer inside *Karelsulcia*, and severe minicircle fragmentation in *Hodgkinia* make short-read assembly difficult without FACS enrichment or deep long-read sequencing; consequently, the metabolic repertoire of the intra-*Karelsulcia* *Hodgkinia* in *Chr. ochracea* could not be directly reconstructed.
- **Identification and Correction of a 40-kb Duplication Artifact in a Previously Published YLS Mitogenome (`PNEP01`):** In re-examining the reference YLS mitogenome of *Meimuna opalifera* (`PNEP01`, previously reported as `170 kb`), the authors identified a 40-kb assembly duplication artifact and showed that the corrected `129,780-bp` size matches their de novo reassembly (`line 108`), strengthening their comparative correlation between fungal mitogenome size and intron count (`Fig. 5a`).
- **Phylogenomic Compositional Heterogeneity Control (`nRCFV` and Completeness Matrices):** Ancient insect endosymbionts suffer from extreme AT bias and long-branch attraction; the authors appropriately applied `TrimAl`, normalized Relative Compositional Frequency Variation (`nRCFV > 0.0016` removal), multiple USCO completeness thresholds (`USCO50`, `USCO60`, `USCO80`, `USCO90`), `ModelFinder` edge-linked partition models, and both `IQ-TREE` (`5,000` ultrafast bootstraps + `1,000` SH-aLRT) and `MrBayes` (`ESS > 200`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality / STRENGTHS
- **Discovery of Ongoing Symbiont Replacement and Intra-*Karelsulcia* Nested Symbiosis in *Chr. ochracea* `[Well Supported]`:** Demonstrating that $6/30$ ($20\%$) of *Chr. ochracea* individuals retain envelope-less *Hodgkinia* inside *Karelsulcia* cytoplasm (verified by both TEM and 3-color FISH in adult bacteriomes and embryos) while $24/30$ ($80\%$) have completed the transition to *Karelsulcia* + YLS provides a rare population-level snapshot of endosymbiont replacement in action.
- **Macroevolutionary Reconstruction of 5 Symbiont Localization Categories Across Cicadidae `[Well Supported]`:** Mapping histological, FISH, and genomic data across 133 species onto a 7-fossil-calibrated timetree (`MRCA ~96.4 Ma`, earliest YLS replacement `\ge 42.2 Ma`, `Fig. 7`) provides a comprehensive synthesis of bacterial-to-fungal symbiont transitions.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Exceptional Data Availability:** All newly assembled genomes (`CP178246`, `CP178247`, plus 21 *Karelsulcia*, 21 YLS mitogenomes, 38 cicada mitogenomes), PCR amplicons, FISH probe sequences (`Supplementary Table 3`), fossil calibrations (`Supplementary Table 4`), and raw RFLP clone traces (`OMIX009590`) are deposited and cataloged (`Reproducibility: 1.6/2.0`).
