### 1. Executive Summary
This landmark clinical pathology and postmortem investigation examines 18 full autopsies of suspected Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) fatalities (8 confirmed as SARS-CoV positive and 10 unconfirmed/non-SARS controls, plus 6 healthy trauma controls), peripheral blood white blood cells from 22 acute confirmed SARS patients (3–29 days post-symptom onset, with 6 analyzed by transmission electron microscopy vs. 4 controls), and retrospective peripheral blood $\text{CD3}^+/\text{CD4}^+/\text{CD8}^+$ T-lymphocyte counts in 65 serologically confirmed versus 35 misdiagnosed SARS patients (stratified by glucocorticoid treatment). Using histopathology, immunohistochemistry (IHC for CD3, CD4, CD8, CD20, CD56, S100, CD68, and cytokeratins AE1/AE3), digoxigenin in situ hybridization (ISH, 154 bp probe from `AY274119`), immunogold EM-ISH, transmission EM, and TaqMan/molecular-beacon real-time RT-PCR, the authors demonstrate diffuse alveolar damage with multinucleated syncytia, severe lymphoid depletion across spleen, lymph nodes, and gut Peyer's patches, and extra-pulmonary SARS-CoV presence in respiratory epithelium, intestinal mucosa, renal distal tubules, hypothalamic/cortical neurons, macrophages, and circulating lymphocytes.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Comprehensive Multi-Organ Autopsy & Specificity Controls:** Performing 18 complete autopsies under high-consequence epidemic conditions and pairing confirmed SARS cases ($n = 8$) against both clinically misdiagnosed pneumonia/shock cases ($n = 10$, Table IV) and healthy trauma controls ($n = 6$), as well as stratifying the 100-patient clinical T-cell cohort by glucocorticoid exposure ($n = 50$ vs. $n = 20$ steroid-treated; $n = 15$ vs. $n = 15$ steroid-naive, Figure 4), is a major design strength. The steroid-naive comparison (Figure 4A–C) convincingly proves that early $\text{CD3}^+/\text{CD4}^+/\text{CD8}^+$ lymphopenia is intrinsic to SARS-CoV infection rather than solely a corticosteroid artifact.
- **Ultrastructural & Sense/Antisense ISH Limitations in Assessing Productive Lymphocyte Infection:**
  - In Table II ($n = 6$ acute SARS blood samples, 100 WBCs counted per patient by TEM), the authors report that $51.5\%$ of circulating lymphocytes ($5.5 \pm 0.7$ of $10.7 \pm 1.0\%$), $29.7\%$ of monocytes, and $3.0\%$ of granulocytes contained "coronavirus-like particles" (80–120 nm cytoplasmic vesicles). In transmission EM of peripheral blood leukocytes (especially in severely ill, viremic patients), clathrin-coated vesicles, multivesicular bodies, and rough ER cisternae frequently mimic 80–120 nm coronavirus particles unless verified by viral spike/nucleocapsid immunogold EM on the blood cells themselves.
  - Furthermore, the 154 bp digoxigenin RNA ISH probe (`AY274119`) detects positive-sense genomic/subgenomic viral RNA (with sense-strand and unrelated-probe negative controls), which demonstrates viral genome presence/uptake (e.g., phagocytosis by $\text{CD68}^+$ macrophages or abortive entry into lymphocytes) but does not prove productive viral replication (negative-strand replicative intermediates or infectious progeny release) inside T lymphocytes.
- **Statistical Reporting:** While Table I, Table II, and the text cite $P < 0.01$ or $P < 0.05$, the specific statistical tests used for cell count comparisons, longitudinal time-course comparisons in Figure 4, and morphometric macrophage size/splenic depletion comparisons are not specified in the Materials and Methods.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: SARS-CoV disseminates systemically beyond the lung to infect respiratory epithelium (type I/II pneumocytes, ciliated tracheal epithelium), intestinal mucosal epithelium, renal distal tubular epithelium, and brain neurons.** `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively supported by convergent LM-ISH, EM-ISH, TEM, cytokeratin/CD68 double-labeling, and real-time RT-PCR across 8 confirmed autopsies (with 10 non-SARS and 6 trauma autopsies all negative), providing a direct anatomical explanation for fecal/urinary viral shedding and neurological symptoms.
- **Claim 2: Severe early $\text{CD3}^+/\text{CD4}^+/\text{CD8}^+$ lymphopenia and splenic/mucosal lymphoid atrophy are hallmark pathological features of SARS independent of corticosteroid therapy.** `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated by splenic weight reduction (62% of control), white-pulp/germinal-center and Peyer's patch depletion (Figures 2–3), and the steroid-stratified 100-patient longitudinal T-cell analysis (Figure 4).
- **Claim 3: "SARS infection is similar to HIV infection that causes AIDS... SARS virus infects and destroys the immune cells in a much faster and more devastating fashion" and "the damage to the immune system, more than damage to the lungs, determines whether a patient recovers or dies."** `[Overstated]` — Claiming that over 50% of circulating peripheral blood lymphocytes harbor viral particles (Table II) and that lymphopenia is caused exclusively by direct lytic viral infection of T cells (analogous to HIV/AIDS) rather than cytokine/IFN-driven lymphocyte apoptosis, margination, or abortive infection overstates the ultrastructural and ISH evidence.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Transparency of Clinical & Molecular Data:** Table III provides complete IHC antibody clones, suppliers, dilutions, temperatures, and incubation times; Table IV provides individual-level clinical, radiographic, virological, steroid, and autopsy diagnosis data for all 18 postmortem cases; and the exact GenBank accessions (`AY274119`, `GI:30027616` nt 15,121–15,661) and all six nested real-time RT-PCR/ISH primer sequences are explicitly documented.
