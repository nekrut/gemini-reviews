### 1. Executive Summary
This canine genetics study investigates the genetic basis of primary non-congenital posterior polar cataract (CAT) in the Entlebucher Mountain Dog (EMD). The authors genotyped 88 purebred EMDs across 10 multi-generational pedigrees (36 paternal half-sib and 49 full-sib groups; 54 diagnosed with posterior polar cataract by DOK veterinary ophthalmologists, 23 unaffected, and 11 other lens opacifications coded as unknown phenotype) using 39 microsatellite markers flanking 31 known human/mouse cataract candidate genes. Initial non-parametric multipoint linkage analysis in MERLIN 1.1.1 identified suggestive linkage near *LIM2* and *FTL* on canine chromosome 1 (`CFA1`: 107.5–109.8 Mb, $Z_{\text{mean}} = 1.94$–$2.08$). Fine-mapping with 30 additional microsatellites on CFA1 localized a genome-wide significant linkage peak at 96.07 Mb (`ABGc006`, $Z_{\text{mean}} = 3.58$, Kong & Cox $\text{LOD} = 1.29$, chromosome-wide $p = 0.0002$, genome-wide Bonferroni $p_g = 0.004$) spanning a 1.3 Mb interval (`ABGc005` at 95.105 Mb to `FH3883` at 96.397 Mb), supported by a 4-marker haplotype association (`ABGc006–ABGc007–REN211B17–C01.643`, $\chi^2 = 20.85, p = 0.0133$). Resequencing all exons and flanking regions of *FTL* and *LIM2* in 37 EMDs (25 CAT cases, 12 controls) and cDNA verification of *LIM2* in lens tissue revealed no coding mutations and only 3 non-coding SNPs in *LIM2* that were not robustly associated with CAT, excluding *FTL* and *LIM2* as the causal gene. This is an honest, carefully executed linkage and candidate-gene exclusion study, with minor statistical weaknesses in using naive case-control $\chi^2$ association tests on related pedigree animals and right-censoring of age-at-onset in unaffected controls.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Non-Parametric Multipoint Linkage & Genome-Wide Error Correction:** The use of Whittemore & Halpern NPL $Z_{\text{mean}}$ and Kong & Cox exponential/linear LOD scores in MERLIN 1.1.1 is well suited for complex/recessive pedigree data without assuming fixed penetrance parameters. The genome-wide Sidak/Bonferroni scaling $p_g = 1 - (1 - p)^{1/r}$ where $r = 126\text{ Mb} / 2425\text{ Mb} = 0.05196$ (so $1/r = 19.25$) correctly converts the chromosome-wide $p = 0.0002$ to $p_g = 1 - (0.9998)^{19.25} = 0.00384 \approx 0.004$. However, note that while $Z_{\text{mean}} = 3.58$ is highly significant, the corresponding Kong & Cox LOD score is modest ($\text{LOD} = 1.29$, chromosome-wide $p = 0.007$, which scales to $p_g = 0.126$ genome-wide).
- **Unadjusted Relatedness in Case-Control & Haplotype Association Tests:** In addition to MERLIN family-based linkage, the authors ran SAS/Genetics `CASECONTROL` and `HAPLOTYPE` $\chi^2$ tests across the 88 dogs (and screened all 2-, 3-, and 4-adjacent-marker sliding haplotypes on CFA1, reporting $p = 0.0133$ for one 4-marker haplotype). Because the 88 dogs belong to 10 closely related half-sib/full-sib families, standard Pearson $\chi^2$ contingency tests violate the independence assumption and inflate Type I error, and no multiple-testing correction was applied across the sliding-window haplotype scans.
- **Age-of-Onset Right-Censoring in Unaffected Dogs:** CAT in EMDs is late-onset (mean age at diagnosis $5.46 \pm 2.64$ years), and the mean age at last examination across all 88 dogs was $6.00 \pm 2.79$ years. Because dogs examined under 4–5 years of age may be pre-symptomatic carriers/homozygotes, treating young clear dogs as definitively "unaffected" in case-control comparisons can reduce power (though MERLIN NPL affected-only allele sharing is robust to unaffected misclassification).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim: A 1.3 Mb interval on CFA1 (95.11–96.40 Mb, peaking at `ABGc006` at 96.07 Mb) shows significant non-parametric linkage to primary posterior polar cataract in EMDs** — `[Moderately Supported]`. The NPL $Z_{\text{mean}} = 3.58$ ($p_g = 0.004$) provides solid evidence of allele sharing on CFA1, though the modest Kong & Cox LOD ($1.29$) and wide haplotype span (96.07–102.04 Mb) indicate that dense SNP array mapping is needed to narrow the locus.
- **Claim: Coding mutations in *FTL* and *LIM2* (located ~11–13 Mb distal at 107.5–109.8 Mb) are excluded as the cause of CAT in EMDs** — `[Well Supported]`. Full genomic sequencing of *FTL* and exonic/flanking sequencing of *LIM2* in 37 dogs (25 cases, 12 controls), plus experimental cDNA confirmation in canine lens tissue that canine *LIM2* exon 3 is 126 bp shorter than human *LIM2*, convincingly rules out coding variants in these two genes. (Note: in Table 2, two non-coding *LIM2* SNPs have nominal $P_{\text{allele}} = 0.04$, which the authors prudently dismiss as non-significant given $P_{\text{genotype}} = 0.16$–$0.17$ and the 12 Mb physical distance from the refined linkage peak).
