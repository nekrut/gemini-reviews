### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This study (`TIME_ANON_0586`) investigates the physical interaction, maturation kinetics, intracellular trafficking, and higher-order assembly of the membrane (M) and spike (S) envelope glycoproteins of mouse hepatitis virus strain A59 (MHV-A59). By systematically screening cell lysis detergents, the authors discover that native heteromultimeric M/S complexes are disrupted by 1% NP-40 or 1% Triton X-100 alone, but are quantitatively preserved in a mixed micelle buffer of 0.5% NP-40 and 0.5% sodium deoxycholate. Using `[35S]methionine` pulse-chase labeling, reciprocal co-immunoprecipitations (`mAb J1.3` anti-M and `mAb J7.6` anti-S), endoglycosidase H (Endo H) digestion, Brefeldin A (BFA) blockade, sucrose velocity gradient sedimentation, indirect double immunofluorescence with the medial-Golgi marker $\alpha$-mannosidase II, and cell-surface vs. intracellular two-step immunoprecipitations, the authors demonstrate that:
1. Newly synthesized, unglycosylated $M_0$ rapidly associates with pre-existing folded S protein in pre-Golgi membranes, whereas newly synthesized S requires a $>20\text{ min}$ lag (rate-limited by oxidative folding and oligomerization) before entering M/S complexes;
2. Consistent with these asymmetric association kinetics, newly synthesized M is incorporated into extracellular budding virions significantly faster than newly synthesized S;
3. Co-expressed M and S form large ($>20\text{S}$) heteromultimeric complexes independent of other coronaviral proteins, and M specifically retains S in the Golgi complex (preventing S transport to the plasma membrane without impeding VSV-G trafficking), proving that M/S complexes alone do not arrest at the pre-Golgi intermediate compartment (IC) site of coronavirus budding.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
* **Rigorous Biochemical Controls Excluding Post-Lysis Aggregation and Non-Specific Retention:**
  - The study includes an exemplary set of biochemical controls:
    1. **Post-lysis mixing control (`mix`, Fig. 1B):** Mixing separate detergent lysates of cells expressing M alone and S alone yields zero co-precipitation of M by `mAb aS`, ruling out non-specific post-lysis micellar trapping.
    2. **Two-step cell-surface vs. intracellular immunoprecipitation with VSV-G internal control (Fig. 7):** Co-expressing VSV-G alongside MHV M demonstrates that VSV-G reaches the plasma membrane quantitatively while MHV S is selectively retained intracellularly by M, ruling out general secretory pathway blockade.
    3. **Sucrose velocity gradient sedimentation control (Fig. 8E):** Expressing S alone shows that mature `S/gp180` and `S/gp90` remain at the top of the 15–30% sucrose gradient, confirming that the $>20\text{S}$ sedimentation of co-expressed M/S (faster than 19.3S thyroglobulin) reflects specific M-driven higher-order multimerization.
* **Unresolved Mechanistic Difference in Brefeldin A (BFA) Sensitivity Between MHV-Infected and vTF7-3 Co-Transfected Cells (Fig. 6):**
  - In MHV-infected cells, BFA (6 $\mu\text{g/ml}$) does not inhibit M/S complex formation, whereas in vTF7-3-driven M+S co-expression, BFA completely prevents stable M/S co-precipitation (reversible upon BFA washout). In the Discussion (p. 346), the authors hypothesize that either another viral factor (such as N or the newly identified E protein) stabilizes early ER complexes or higher protein accumulation in infection overcomes a concentration threshold. Testing co-transfection of N or E alongside M and S in the presence of BFA, or titrating plasmid dosage to match infected-cell expression levels, would directly distinguish between these two hypotheses.
* **Single-Trace PhosphorImager Quantitation Without Replicate Error Bars (Fig. 3B):**
  - The virion incorporation curves in Fig. 3B present PhosphorImager radioactivity counts (cpm) from a single representative experiment without standard error bars or explicit reporting of the number of independent biological replicates.
* **Minor Text-Extraction Duplication:**
  - The Abstract is printed three times at the beginning of the extracted text file (lines 7, 9, and 11).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality (STRENGTHS)
* **`[Well Supported]` Detergent-sensitive heteromultimeric association of coronavirus M and S glycoproteins with asymmetric maturation kinetics:**
  - The demonstration that unglycosylated $M_0$ enters complexes immediately with pre-synthesized S while newly synthesized S lags by $>20\text{ min}$—and that this kinetic asymmetry directly translates into faster virion incorporation of newly made M than S (Fig. 2 and Fig. 3)—is a textbook demonstration of quantitative pulse-chase virology.
* **`[Well Supported]` Specific Golgi retention of S by M and exclusion of M/S complexes as the sole determinant of pre-Golgi intermediate compartment budding:**
  - Because M/S heteromultimers transit through the intermediate compartment and accumulate in the $\alpha$-mannosidase II-positive Golgi apparatus when co-expressed, the authors rigorously deduce that additional viral factors (such as E and/or nucleocapsid) are required to specify pre-Golgi budding.
