### 1. Executive Summary
This computational molecular evolution and paleobiology study reconstructs the evolutionary history and ancestral metal-cofactor specificity of the nitrogenase metalloenzyme family (Mo-dependent Nif, V-dependent Vnf, and Fe-dependent Anf). Using a curated dataset of 284 concatenated Nif/Vnf/AnfHDK sequences across 20 bacterial and archaeal phyla (plus 10 Bch/ChlLNB outgroup sequences), the authors evaluate 12 alignment/model combinations in PhyloBot/RAxML/CODEML, infer 25 maximum-likelihood and 2,500 Bayesian-sampled ancestral sequences across five deep nodes (`AncA`–`AncE`), train an active-site support vector classifier, and compute active-site pocket volumes across 3,080 Modeller homology structures using POVME 2.0. The study demonstrates two major findings: (1) contrary to a prior prominent study, modeled active-site pocket volumes overlap substantially across Nif-II, Vnf, and ancestral models and cannot reliably classify metal dependence; (2) the 30 active-site residues within 5 Å of the cofactor strongly distinguish extant Mo-nitrogenases from V/Fe-nitrogenases, and the deepest ancestors (`AncB`–`AncE`) consistently classify as Mo-like, whereas `AncA` (ancestor of Vnf/Anf) preserves the V-specific `355-TGGPRL-360` carbonate-binding loop. By mapping the stepwise absence of `nifN` and `nifE` in basal `F-Mc` and `Clfx` clades, the authors propose a parsimonious model of early proto-Mo-cofactor assembly preceding the `nifDK` $\rightarrow$ `nifEN` duplication.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Phylogenetic & Ancestral Sequence Robustness:** The authors systematically assess sensitivity to alignment algorithms (MSAProbs vs. MUSCLE) and substitution/rate models (CAT+LG, CAT+WAG, GAMMA+LG, GAMMA+WAG) across the top 5 likelihood-ranked trees, and further propagate site-level posterior uncertainty by drawing 100 random Bayesian sequence samples per node per tree (2,500 sequences). Reporting that active-site mean posterior probability is high (0.92–0.98 vs. 0.83–0.91 across full HDK) provides solid statistical grounding for downstream active-site inference.
- **Rigorous Negative Benchmarking of Homology-Modeled Pocket Volumes:** A standout methodological strength is the authors' transparent refutation of homology-modeled pocket volume as a predictor of metal specificity. By including newly available VnfD crystal templates (`5N6Y`), 10 modeling replicates per extant/ML sequence, 100 Bayesian variants per node, and control runs with both FeMo- and FeV-cofactors, they show that homology modeling introduces ~190 Å³ inflation relative to crystal structures (`3U7Q` crystal 994.8 Å³ vs. modeled 1,186.4 Å³) and that Nif-II and Vnf volume distributions completely overlap.
- **SVM Classifier Circularity / Phylogenetic Non-Independence:** In Section 2.2, extant sequences are labeled as Nif (240 sequences, excluding 16 uncharacterized Clfx/F-Mc/Mb-Mc), Vnf (14 sequences), or Anf (14 sequences) "according to their phylogenetic clustering" and split randomly 60%/40% into training and test sets. Because sequences within monophyletic clades share evolutionary history, a random 60/40 split does not test cross-clade generalization (hence the 100% test accuracy), and because `AncB`–`AncE` are phylogenetically connected to the Nif/basal backbone, an active-site classifier trained on extant sequences will naturally group internal nodes near the root with the ancestral/majority sequence state unless convergent physico-chemical features are isolated from phylogenetic signal. Nevertheless, the specific motif analysis of the `355-TGGPRL-360` carbonate loop in `AncA` provides independent biochemical evidence for the Vnf-like transition at `AncA`.
- **Minor Textual Duplication:** Paragraphs 49–55 of the second part of the Discussion (starting "It is evolutionarily unlikely for a portion of the FeMo-cofactor biosynthetic pathway to be lost...") are verbatim duplicates of text already appearing in the long paragraph 47 immediately above. This copy-editing error should be removed.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Homology-modeled active-site pocket volumes cannot reliably distinguish nitrogenase metal cofactor specificity.** `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated across 3,080 models with both FeMo and FeV templates and non-parametric statistical comparisons.
- **Claim 2: Deepest ancestral nitrogenase active sites (`AncB`–`AncE`) resemble extant Mo-nitrogenases, while `AncA` (ancestor of Vnf and Anf) resembles V-nitrogenases.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by sequence identity matrices (Figure 4a vs. 4b showing stronger divergence in the active site than across the whole HDK complex), SVM posterior probability classification, and the specific conservation of the Vnf carbonate-accommodating `355-TGGPRL-360` loop in `AncA`.
- **Claim 3: Basal `Clfx` (lacking `nifEN`) and `F-Mc` (lacking `nifN`) lineages reflect an ancestral simplified/alternative pathway for proto-Mo-cofactor synthesis rather than secondary gene loss.** `[Moderately Supported]` — This is a compelling evolutionary hypothesis that reconciles ~3.2 Ga isotopic fractionation signatures with the later origin of canonical `nifEN`, though the authors appropriately frame it as a proposed model requiring wet-lab biochemical characterization of `Clfx`/`F-Mc` enzymes and resurrected ancestors (especially since N₂ fixation has not yet been definitively proven in `Clfx`).
