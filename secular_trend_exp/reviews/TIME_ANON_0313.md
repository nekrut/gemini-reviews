### 1. Executive Summary
This cellular and molecular immunology study investigates how a single 9-amino-acid viral epitope (`D4.NS3.500-508`, `TPEGIIPTL`) from the nonstructural protein NS3 of dengue virus type 4 (`D4V`) is recognized by human `HLA-B35`–restricted $\text{CD8}^+\text{CD4}^-\text{CD3}^+$ cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) clones derived from a `D4V`-immune donor. Screening eight nonamer peptides carrying the `HLA-B35` anchor motif (`Pro` at position 2) across `D4V NS3 453–618`, the authors show that although six of the eight peptides bind `HLA-B*3501` on `RMA-S-B*3501` cells (`Table 3`), only `D4.NS3.500-508` induces specific PBMC proliferation (`Table 1`) and sensitizes autologous lymphoblastoid cell lines (`LCL`) to lysis by bulk `D4V`-stimulated CTLs (`Table 2`). Using limiting dilution cloning after 6 weeks of `D4.NS3.500-508` peptide stimulation, the authors isolate nine $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL clones (`Tables 4–8`) and demonstrate that a single stimulating peptide expands CTL clones exhibiting four distinct dengue serotype specificity profiles when tested against both recombinant vaccinia viruses expressing `D2V`, `D3V`, or `D4V` `NS3` (`Table 6`) and homologous synthetic serotype peptides (`Table 7`): (1) `D4V` serotype-specific (`clones 9, 14`), (2) `D2V + D4V` subcomplex-specific (`clone 12`), (3) `D1V/D3V + D4V` subcomplex-specific (`clones 2, 17, 26`), and (4) `D1V–D4V` serotype cross-reactive (`clones 20, 21, 22`). Finally, using an aspartic acid (`D`) positional scanning panel across all nine residues of `TPEGIIPTL` (`Tables 9–10`), the authors decouple `HLA-B*3501` anchor binding (`P2 Pro501`, `P6 Ile505`, `P7 Pro506`) from TCR contact (`P4 Glu503D` abolishes recognition by all clones despite preserving MHC binding, whereas `P5 Ile504D` and `P8 Thr507D` differentially ablate serotype-specific vs. cross-reactive clones).

The experimental design is rigorous and systematic: MHC class I peptide binding (`RMA-S-B*3501` stabilization) is explicitly separated from TCR recognition, synthetic peptide pulsing is cross-validated against endogenous viral antigen processing (`VV-NS3` recombinants and live `D2V`-infected LCLs), and `HLA-B35` restriction is proven using both allogeneic HLA-typed LCL panels and murine `P815-B*3501` transfectants. Two internal reporting inconsistencies in the text/tables should be corrected in revision.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Decoupling Peptide–MHC Binding from TCR Contact (`Tables 3, 9, and 10`):**
  - A major strength of this work is the quantitative `RMA-S-B*3501` flow cytometric stabilization assay across a 6-log concentration gradient (`0.01` to `1,000 μM`). In the aspartic acid (`D`) scan (`Table 9`), substitutions at positions `501 (P2)`, `505 (P6)`, and `506 (P7)` completely abrogate `HLA-B*3501` surface stabilization (MFI `62–83` vs. `69` no-peptide control), identifying them as primary/secondary MHC binding residues. Conversely, `503D` (`TPEDIIPTL`) binds `HLA-B*3501` even more strongly than wild-type `TPEGIIPTL` (MFI `373` vs. `316` at `1,000 μM`; `177` vs. `125` at `10 μM`), yet reduces cytolysis to `4–25%` across all seven tested CTL clones (`Table 10`), proving that `P4` (`Glu503`) is an essential TCR contact residue.
- **Orthogonal Antigen Presentation Controls (`Tables 4–8`):**
  - Every clone is tested not only on peptide-pulsed targets (`2.5 × 10^-6` to `2.5 μg/ml`), but also on autologous targets endogenously processing full-length `NS3` via recombinant vaccinia virus (`VV[D2:NS3]`, `VV[D3:NS3]`, `VV[D4:NS3]` vs. `VV[control]`) and live `D2V`-infected LCLs (`Table 5`), ruling out peptide-pulsing artifacts.
- **Internal Consistency Audit (Two Text/Table Discrepancies):**
  1. **Sequence of `D2.NS3.500-508` (`TPEGIIPSM` vs. `TPEGIIPSL`):** On page 857 (`Results`) and page 860 (`Discussion`), the text states that `D2.NS3.500-508` has the sequence `TPEGIIPSM` (*"Peptide D2.NS3.500-508 has two amino acid substitutions; S for T at amino acid 507 and M for L at amino acid 508"*). However, the footnote to `Table 7` (`page 859`) states: *"Sequences of NS3:500-508 are TPEGIIPSL for D2V, TPEGIIPAL for D3V, and TPEGIIPTL for D4V."* Furthermore, on page 861 (`Discussion`), the authors write *"because of the substitution of the amino acid at the 8th position"* when comparing `D3.NS3.500-508` (`TPEGIIPAL`) and `D4.NS3.500-508` (`TPEGIIPTL`)—which actually is the **8th position of the nonamer** (`aa 507`), consistent with `507` being P8 and `508` being P9. The `TPEGIIPSM` vs. `TPEGIIPSL` discrepancy in `Table 7` must be corrected.
  2. **Clone numbering and limiting dilution origin (`Methods` vs. `Discussion`):** In `Materials and Methods` (`page 855`), the authors list the seeding densities of eight of the nine clones (`Clones 2, 9, 12, 14` from `1 cell/well`; `Clones 20, 21, 22` from `3 cells/well`; `Clone 26` from `10 cells/well`), inadvertently omitting `Clone 17` (which appears in `Tables 4, 6, 7, 8, and 10`). In the `Discussion` (`page 860`), the authors state that two additional serotype cross-reactive clones (`"clones 2 and 10"`) were established from `1 cell/well` but `"did not grow enough to be used throughout the entire experiments"`, which collides with `Clone 2` already assigned to the subcomplex-specific (`D3V+D4V`) clone tested across all tables. (Note: the authors did verify monoclonality of `clones 20, 21, and 22` by PCR showing single $V_\alpha 8$ and $V_\beta 5.2$ TCR usage).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *"A single nine-amino acid peptide induces proliferation of a heterogeneous panel of dengue virus-specific CD8+ CTL clones that are all restricted by HLA-B35 but have a variety of serotype specificities."* `[Well Supported]`
  - Demonstrated across both endogenous `VV-NS3` expression (`Table 6`) and synthetic peptide titration (`Table 7`), with `HLA-B35` restriction confirmed across allogeneic targets (`Table 8`) and `P815-B*3501` cells.
- **Claim 2:** *"Peptides that contain a single amino acid substitution at each position of D4.NS3.500-508 were recognized differently by the T cell clones... indicating that the manner of recognition by these multiple CTLs is heterogeneous."* `[Well Supported]`
  - Directly supported by the combined `RMA-S-B*3501` binding (`Table 9`) and relative lysis (`Table 10`) matrices.
- **Claim 3:** *"Therefore, immunization with a subunit vaccine containing a single T cell epitope is not a realistic approach to inducing broad immunity in an outbred population."* `[Well Supported]`
  - The authors show admirable restraint in the Discussion (`page 861`), explicitly noting that their donor's T cell repertoire was primed in vivo by live attenuated `D4V` vaccination and cautioning against over-extrapolating single-peptide subunit vaccine strategies.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Protocol Completeness:** All 10 data tables provide complete numerical values (mean cpm, stimulation indices, MFIs across peptide concentrations, and specific $^{51}\text{Cr}$ release percentages across E/T ratios and peptide dilutions), and viral strains (`D2V New Guinea C`, `D3V H-87`, `D4V 814669`), donor HLA haplotype, and peptide sequences are fully specified. Resolving the `TPEGIIPSM`/`TPEGIIPSL` typo in `Table 7` and clarifying the clone IDs (`clone 17` and the duplicate label `clone 2` on page 860) will make the record fully consistent.
