### SUMMARY
### 1. Executive Summary
This cellular immunology and virology study investigates how receptor-mediated targeting of measles virus (MV) to its human cellular receptor CD46 affects MHC class II-restricted antigen processing and presentation. Using murine `M12.4.1` B lymphoma cells ($H\text{-}2^d$) and stable transfectants expressing human CD46 (`M12.CD46`), the authors measure IL-2 production by $I\text{-}E^d$-restricted hemagglutinin (H)-specific (`TH5.124`) and $I\text{-}A^d$-restricted nucleoprotein (NP)-specific (`TNP.408`) T cell hybridomas. They demonstrate that CD46 expression enhances MHC class II presentation of both external (H) and internal (NP) virion proteins by $>1,000$-fold. Through a series of mechanistic controls, they show that this enhancement (1) does not require viral transcription or protein neosynthesis (UV-inactivated MV is presented equally well), (2) is mediated by native conformation-dependent binding of MV H to CD46 (soluble and liposomal native H are enhanced $>100$- to $1,000$-fold, whereas SDS/2-ME-denatured H is presented equally poorly by `M12` and `M12.CD46`), (3) is independent of F-mediated plasma membrane fusion (unaffected by neutralizing anti-F mAb or the fusion-inhibitory tripeptide `Z-D-Phe-Phe-Gly`), and (4) is abolished by $50\ \mu\text{M}$ chloroquine, demonstrating that CD46-bound virions undergo endocytosis and acid-dependent endosomal/lysosomal processing alongside classical plasma membrane fusion.

### STRENGTHS
- **Clean Mechanistic Dissection of Entry vs. Processing Pathways:** The study systematically rules out viral replication (via 254-nm UV inactivation), plasma membrane fusion (via both anti-F mAb and `Z-D-Phe-Phe-Gly` peptide with `Gly-Phe-Phe` negative control, plus F-free H-liposomes), and non-specific clonal differences in APC function (via denatured H and soluble NP controls).
- **Simultaneous Tracking of Envelope (H) and Internal Nucleocapsid (NP) Antigens:** Demonstrating that an internal core protein (NP) from UV-inactivated virions is presented via a fusion-independent, chloroquine-sensitive pathway proves that whole virions are endocytosed via CD46 and degraded in acidic compartments.
- **Direct Comparison with $\text{Fc}\gamma\text{RIIb1}$-Mediated Opsonization:** Comparing CD46-mediated capture against anti-H/anti-F antibody opsonization on `M12` cells (Fig. 1c) contextualizes the high endocytic efficiency of CD46 relative to B-cell $\text{Fc}\gamma\text{RIIb1}$.

### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Absence of Anti-CD46 Blocking Antibody Control on `M12.CD46` Cells:** While the denatured-H control (Fig. 2c) demonstrates that `M12.CD46` and parental `M12` cells have equivalent baseline pinocytic presentation capacity, blocking CD46 directly with an anti-CD46 mAb on `M12.CD46` cells would have provided an even more direct receptor-specificity control on the exact same transfectant line.
- **Graphical Reporting Without Error Bars:** The dose-response curves in Figures 1–4 plot mean OD values (`OD 570–650 nm` in the CTLL-2 MTT bioassay) without error bars on the data points, relying on the statement in Materials and Methods that "the standard deviation between replicates was usually well below 5%."

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1 (Targeting MV to CD46 on APCs enhances MHC class II presentation of both H and NP by $>1,000$-fold independent of viral replication): `[Well Supported]`** — Directly demonstrated by infectious vs. UV-inactivated MV dose-response curves across `M12` and `M12.CD46` cells.
- **Claim 2 (Enhancement is mediated by direct interaction of native H glycoprotein with CD46): `[Well Supported]`** — Confirmed by the $>100$- to $1,000$-fold shift for native soluble and liposomal H and complete loss of the CD46 advantage upon H denaturation.
- **Claim 3 (CD46-targeted MV is processed via an endocytosis and chloroquine-sensitive endolysosomal pathway rather than plasma membrane fusion): `[Well Supported]`** — Strongly supported by insensitivity to `Z-D-Phe-Phe-Gly` and anti-F mAb and complete inhibition by $50\ \mu\text{M}$ chloroquine.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Experimental Detail:** Cell lines (`M12.4.1`, `M12.CD46`, `TH5.124`, `TNP.408`), viral strain (`MV Halle`), antibodies (`cl55`, `cl25`, `B22-F5`), peptide inhibitors, and assay concentrations are thoroughly documented.
