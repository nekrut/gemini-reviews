### 1. Executive Summary
This biochemical and structural study characterizes the oligomerization state and structure-function relationships of SARS-CoV non-structural protein 15 (Nsp15), a $\text{Mn}^{2+}$-dependent, uridylate-specific viral endoribonuclease (XendoU superfamily). The authors generate 22 alanine-scanning mutants (20 single, 2 double) targeting conserved histidine, lysine, aspartate, and glutamate residues, purifying the 19 soluble His-tagged proteins from *E. coli* via IMAC and Mono Q chromatography. Using a single-uridine radiolabeled Spike TRS oligoribonucleotide (`CAACUAAACGAAC`) and a real-time FRET-based tetranucleotide cleavage assay (`5'-FAM-ArUAA-3'-TAMRA`), coupled with size-exclusion chromatography (SEC), sedimentation velocity analytical ultracentrifugation (AUC), native pore-limit gradient gel electrophoresis, and negative-stain transmission electron microscopy (TEM) single-particle 3D reconstruction + 2D crystallography of Nsp15-K289A, the authors establish that:
1. Conserved active-site residues H234A, H249A, D272A, and K289A completely abolish endoribonuclease activity (even at 200-fold excess) and relieve *E. coli* expression toxicity (~1000-fold higher yield).
2. Wild-type Nsp15 exists in a concentration-dependent monomer–trimer–hexamer equilibrium (85–90% hexamer at 1–5 $\mu$M), whereas $\text{Mn}^{2+}$ addition only minimally alters hexamer fraction (63% to 66% at 0.1 $\mu$M).
3. An N-terminal mutant, E3A, shifts the equilibrium strongly toward monomers/trimers and exhibits impaired, right-shifted concentration-dependent cooperative activity, whereas active-site mutant K289A locks the protein predominantly in the hexameric state (98% hexamer/dodecamer).
4. Negative-stain TEM single-particle reconstruction (1.7–2.0 nm resolution by 0.5 FSC) and 2D crystal projection mapping ($p3$ plane group, $a=b=9\text{ nm}, \gamma=120^\circ$) reveal a $D_3$-symmetric dimer of trimers ($9\text{ nm} \times 11\text{ nm}$) containing cavities and a central core density.

This is a rigorous, multi-orthogonal biophysical investigation that cross-validates oligomerization states across four independent hydrodynamic/electrophoretic/microscopic modalities and directly links oligomerization to cooperative enzymatic catalysis.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Orthogonal Biophysical Cross-Validation:** The central structural claim—that Nsp15 forms a hexamer composed of two stacked trimers—is supported by four distinct techniques:
  * Superdex 200 SEC at $5\ \mu\text{M}$ showing peaks at $47.9\text{ kDa}$ (monomer, ~10%) and $252\text{ kDa}$ (hexamer, ~90%), with E3A shifting to 52% monomer plus a trimer shoulder and K289A shifting to 98% hexamer/dodecamer.
  * Sedimentation velocity AUC fitted via SEDFIT Lamm equation models at $0.1\ \mu\text{M}$ and $1\ \mu\text{M}$, resolving species at $1.8\text{ S}$ and $6.3\text{ S}$ and demonstrating concentration-dependent assembly (63% $\to$ 85% hexamer from $0.1$ to $1\ \mu\text{M}$) vs. minimal $\text{Mn}^{2+}$ effect (63% $\to$ 66%). (Note a minor arithmetic typo in line 56: $6 \times 40.8\text{ kDa} = 244.8\text{ kDa}$, whereas the text states "predicted hexamer molecular mass (285 kDa)"—this should be corrected).
  * Native 4%–20% pore-limit gradient electrophoresis capturing trapped trimer intermediates alongside monomers and hexamers across active and inactive mutant panels.
  * Negative-stain TEM single-particle reconstruction refined in $D_3$ point-group symmetry after independent symmetry validation via Fourier projection maps of 2D crystals ($p3$ plane group showing triangular mass arrangements with six density maxima per unit cell).
* **Enzymatic Kinetics & Concentration Titration:** The real-time FRET kinetic assay across $1$–$250\text{ nM}$ enzyme demonstrates non-linear (cooperative/oligomerization-driven) increases in initial cleavage velocity between $30$ and $150\text{ nM}$ for WT, and a clear right-shift in concentration threshold and reduced $V_{\max}$ for the oligomerization-defective E3A mutant (Fig 8). However, the authors do not fit a formal kinetic cooperativity (Hill) or monomer–hexamer mass-action dissociation ($K_d$) model to extract quantitative $K_{1/2}$ or $k_{\text{cat}}/K_m$ parameters with standard errors. Additionally, notice a minor discrepancy between the text (line 89: "1.2 $\mu$M substrate") and Figure 8 legend (line 92: "1.6 $\mu$M substrate").
* **Missing Circular Dichroism (CD) Data:** The Abstract (line 7) states: *"Two of the inactive mutants had unusual secondary structures compared to the wild-type protein, as measured by circular dichroism spectroscopy."* However, no CD spectra, methods, or specific mutant identities for those two misfolded inactive mutants appear anywhere in the Results or Materials and Methods sections (presumably omitted during manuscript revision or moved to unreferenced supplement). This discrepancy between the Abstract and main text must be resolved.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Claim 1:** *"Four mutants showed no activity, even when added at concentrations up to 200-fold higher than wild-type... H234A, H249A, D272A, and K289A... suggesting that they are directly involved in catalysis."* — **[Well Supported]**. Fully consistent with XendoU family conservation (two catalytic histidines and lysine) and validated by quantitative gel cleavage and fluorescence assays, alongside the striking biological observation that catalytic inactivation eliminates *E. coli* expression toxicity (~1000-fold yield increase).
* **Claim 2:** *"Wild-type protein exists in an equilibrium between hexamers and monomers in solution... A mutant in the N terminus of Nsp15 [E3A] was impaired in hexamer formation and had low endoribonuclease activity, suggesting that oligomerization is required for endoribonuclease activity."* — **[Well Supported]**. Demonstrated across SEC, AUC, native pore-gradient PAGE, and concentration-dependent initial rate titrations showing partial rescue of E3A activity at high enzyme concentration.
* **Claim 3:** *"Three-dimensional reconstruction of negatively stained single particles of Nsp15... suggested that the six subunits were arranged as a dimer of trimers..."* — **[Well Supported]**. Although negative-stain EM has a resolution limit of ~17–20 Å (0.5 FSC), the $C_3$ rotational symmetry was independently verified from 2D crystal Fourier transforms ($p3$ plane group) before imposing $D_3$ symmetry on side-on bilayers, avoiding symmetry-imposition circularity.
