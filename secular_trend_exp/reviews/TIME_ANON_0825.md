### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This evolutionary genomics and transcriptomics study characterizes the complete genome and host-partitioned gene expression of *Diachasmimorpha longicaudata* entomopoxvirus (DlEPV), a mutualistic heritable DNA virus maintained in the venom gland of the braconid parasitoid wasp *D. longicaudata* and injected into tephritid fruit fly hosts (*Anastrepha suspensa*). Using hybrid PacBio SMRT (HGAP3/Celera Assembler) and Illumina MiSeq polishing ($6.24 \times 10^6$ read pairs), the authors assemble the complete DlEPV genome into a single exogenous 253-kb linear dsDNA contig containing two 17-kb inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) and 193 ORFs (65.1% coding density, 30.1% GC content), including 45 of 49 conserved poxvirus core genes and a massive central tandem expansion of 27 baculovirus repeated ORF (*BRO*) virulence genes. Non-integration into the wasp nuclear genome is independently confirmed by normalized qPCR showing $<1$ viral copy per wasp genome across male wasps and non-venom-gland tissues (S1 Fig), and by absence of the 4 missing poxvirus core genes (`H3L`, `E6R`, `G6R`, `A29L`) in de novo assemblies of unmapped wasp venom-gland RNA-seq reads. Maximum-likelihood (RAxML LG+$\Gamma$) and Bayesian (PhyloBayes CAT-GTR) phylogenomics across 16, 44, and 10 (Asfarviridae-rooted) core genes, alongside synteny dot plots, demonstrate that DlEPV is sister to the *Drosophila melanogaster* pathogen Yalta virus (`MT364305`). Finally, using time-course RT-qPCR ($n=6$ biological replicates per time point) to match peak viral transcription stages (unemerged adult female wasp venom glands vs 72 hpp parasitized fly pupae) followed by stranded Illumina RNA-seq ($n=6$ biological replicates per host), the authors show that 91.2% (176/193) of DlEPV genes are differentially expressed (Cuffdiff FDR $q < 0.05$): 82.2% (37/45) of core replication genes are upregulated in wasp venom glands, whereas 79.1% (53/67) of putative virulence genes (including 22/27 *BRO* genes) are strongly upregulated in parasitized fly tissue ($>10\times$ higher on average).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Rigorous multi-platform genome assembly and endogenization falsification:** Combining long-read PacBio assembly with high-depth Illumina short-read polishing (correcting 140 indels/SNPs), custom profile-HMM searches (HMMER3) across both viral intergenic regions and unmapped wasp de novo transcriptomes (`GSE144541`), and normalized qPCR across developmental stages and sexes (S1 Fig) conclusively proves that DlEPV is an exogenous cytoplasmic viral symbiont rather than an integrated polydnavirus-like EVE.
- **Careful stage-matching and biological replication for comparative RNA-seq:** Rather than comparing arbitrary time points between two different insect species, the authors first established RT-qPCR developmental time courses (one-way ANOVA + Tukey HSD across $n=6$ biological replicates, Fig. 4) to identify plateau/peak viral transcription in wasp venom glands (unemerged adult) and parasitized fly hosts (72 hpp, with the wasp larva dissected out prior to RNA extraction), and sequenced $n=6$ biological replicates per group.
- **Robust phylogenomic design:** Evaluates 16-gene (including partial hymenopteran EPV `LHEV`), 44-gene (all complete EPVs), and 10-gene (rooted with *Asfarviridae* outgroups ASFV and Kaumoebavirus) datasets under both ML (`LG+G`) and site-heterogeneous Bayesian (`CAT-GTR`) models, complemented by chromosomal core-gene synteny dot plots (Fig. 3).

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Cross-tissue RNA-seq normalization bias (`FPKM` calculated on viral-mapped reads vs total library reads):** In Section "Transcriptome sequencing and analysis", the authors state that *"An average 38.9% of venom gland reads and 7.8% of parasitized fly reads aligned to the DlEPV genome"* and that *"Quality reads for each sample were separately mapped to the DlEPV reference genome using bowtie2 v2.2.4. Cuffquant from Cufflinks v2.2.1 was used to calculate the average fragments per kilobase of transcript per million mapped reads (FPKM) values for each DlEPV ORF in both wasp and fly tissues, and Cuffdiff was used to test for differential expression."* Because reads were mapped **only** to the 253-kb DlEPV reference genome (where total viral transcription is compositional and sums to 100% of mapped reads in Cuffquant/Cuffdiff), if a set of high-abundance structural/replication genes is massively expressed in the venom gland, compositional normalization (dividing by total viral mapped reads) mathematically forces all other genes to appear "downregulated" in wasp relative to fly (and vice versa), creating a bimodal yin-yang split where 91.2% (176/193) of genes are called differentially expressed (86 up, 90 down)! While the relative shift in transcriptional allocation between core replication genes (37/45 enriched in wasp) and *BRO*/early virulence genes (53/67 enriched in fly) is biologically real and striking (e.g., via Fisher's exact test on gene categories), no formal enrichment test statistic (e.g., hypergeometric / Fisher's exact test $p$-value comparing 37/45 vs 53/67) is reported, and compositional closure of viral-only mapping is not discussed.
- **Heuristic "Putative Virulence: Early Promoter" classification:** Classifying 34 unannotated ORFs as "putative virulence genes" solely because they contain the 10-bpdegenerate motif `TGAAAXXXXA` within 100 bp upstream of the start codon—in a genome that is 70% AT-rich—risks substantial false-positive motif calls (in a 70% AT genome, the probability of `TGAAA....A` occurring by chance at a given position is $(0.35)^5 \times (0.15) \approx 0.00079$, or $\sim 7.5\%$ per 100-bp window).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** DlEPV maintains an exogenous 253-kb linear dsDNA genome encoding 45/49 poxvirus core genes and is not integrated into the *D. longicaudata* genome. `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 2:** DlEPV is most closely related to the dipteran pathogen Yalta virus (`MT364305`), sharing 44 core genes, 27 additional orthologs, and a 50-kb syntenic block, indicating origin from a dipteran host pathogen. `[Well Supported]`
- **Claim 3:** DlEPV achieves functional partitioning between its mutualistic wasp host and parasitized fly host via differential transcriptional allocation (preferential expression of core replication genes in wasp venom gland vs *BRO* and early putative virulence genes in parasitized flies). `[Well Supported]`

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
