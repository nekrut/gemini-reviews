### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This landmark molecular and cellular biology study provides the first definitive *in vivo* genetic test of whether post-translational acetylation of $\alpha$-tubulin at the evolutionarily invariant Lysine-40 residue (`K40`) is essential for eukaryotic microtubule assembly, stability, or function. Exploiting the fact that the ciliated protozoan *Tetrahymena thermophila* assembles at least 17 distinct microtubule organelles from a single $\alpha$-tubulin gene and two identical-protein $\beta$-tubulin genes, the authors developed a positive-selection homologous gene replacement strategy using a taxol-hypersensitive, oryzalin-resistant host mutant (`E5`, carrying `Ala65 -> Thr65` in $\alpha$-tubulin). By microinjecting macronuclei with linear 3.2-kb *HindIII* genomic fragments carrying wild-type `Ala65`, a diagnostic silent *HaeIII* site at codon 281, and either wild-type `Lys40` (`pTUB100E3`, control `K1`) or an unacetylatable `Arg40` substitution (`pTUB-R40`, clones `R1–R7`), and driving phenotypic assortment across all ~45 macronuclear chromosomes over 50–60 generations in $15\ \mu\text{M}$ taxol, the authors achieved 100% replacement of endogenous $\alpha$-tubulin. Complete loss of K40 acetylation (`<2%` of wild-type levels, less than 1 gene copy per macronucleus) was verified by Southern blotting (`Figure 2B`), immunofluorescence with monoclonal antibody `6-11B-1` (`Figure 3`), serial-dilution Western blotting (`Figure 4`), and axonemal isoelectric focusing (`IEF`, `Figure 5`). Strikingly, mutants completely devoid of $\alpha$-tubulin K40 acetylation (`R3C`, `R7C`) are indistinguishable from isogenic `K40` controls (`K1`) in cortical/ciliary/mitotic microtubule morphology, vegetative growth across temperatures ($18^\circ\text{C}$, $30^\circ\text{C}$, $39^\circ\text{C}$) and osmotic/ionic stresses, ciliary swimming speed ($286 \pm 174\ \mu\text{m/s}, n=69$ vs. $290 \pm 142\ \mu\text{m/s}, n=52$), conjugation pairing, and sensitivity to microtubule-depolymerizing (`oryzalin`, `colchicine`) or stabilizing (`taxol`) drugs (`Figure 6`). Furthermore, the uniform basic shift of all ~5 axonemal $\alpha$-tubulin charge isoforms on IEF gels (`Figure 5`) proves that nearly all wild-type ciliary $\alpha$-tubulin is acetylated at K40, that no secondary lysine is acetylated in compensation, and that other charge-altering modifications (such as polyglutamylation) coexist on the same $\alpha$-tubulin molecules.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **Definitive Complete Gene Replacement Overcoming Prior Partial-Replacement Limitations:** Earlier work in *Chlamydomonas* (`Kozminski et al.`) achieved only ~70% replacement of endogenous $\alpha$-tubulin with a non-acetylatable mutant, leaving open the possibility that the remaining 30% wild-type tubulin sufficed for function. Here, phenotypic assortment of the ~45-copy *Tetrahymena* macronucleus combined with a `1/50` (`2%`) serial-dilution Western blot calibration (`Figure 4`) rigorously proves that fewer than 1 out of 45 macronuclear gene copies could remain wild-type.
- **Isogenic Control Transformation (`pTUB100E3` vs. `pTUB-R40`):** Rather than comparing `R40` transformants against untransformed wild-type `CU428` or the `E5` host, the authors generated parallel gene-replacement transformants (`K1`) in the exact same `E5` host using an identical plasmid (`pTUB100E3`) containing the silent *HaeIII* site and `Ala65` rescue marker, isolating the effect of `K40R` (`AAG -> AGA`) to a single amino acid change.
- **Rich Biochemical Insights from Axonemal Isoelectric Focusing (`Figure 5`):** Running isolated ciliary axonemes on high-resolution IEF gels demonstrates a clean shift of the entire 5-band $\alpha$-tubulin ladder toward the basic cathode in `R3`, simultaneously proving near-stoichiometric K40 acetylation in wild-type cilia, absence of compensatory lysine acetylation, and persistence of orthogonal charge-altering post-translational modifications.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Minor Textual Swap Between Clones `R3` and `R7` Regarding the Extra Flanking Insertion Copy (`page 1304` vs. `page 1305` and `Figure 2B`):**
  - On page 1304, the authors correctly write: *"four of the transformants contained an additional HaeIII fragment of ~3.1 kb (**Fig. 2 B, clones R1, 4, 5, 7**) that represents an additional copy of the transformed gene inserted into the flanking region."* Inspection of `Figure 2B` confirms that `R7` (and subclones `R7A, B, C`) has the `3.1-kb` band, whereas `R3` (and `R3A, B, C`) has only the `5.5-kb` and `3.3-kb` replacement bands.
  - However, on page 1305, the text reverses the two clones: *"R7 is a gene replacement transformant while R3 contained an additional integrated copy of the arg 40 gene (see above)."* Because both `R3C` and `R7C` were assayed in parallel across all Western, swimming, and drug experiments with identical results, this label swap in the text does not affect any scientific conclusion.
- **Extensive Reliance on "Data Not Shown" for Secondary Phenotypic Assays:** In accordance with journal space norms of the 1990s, `[3H]`-acetate pulse labeling in cycloheximide, growth curves at $18/30/39^\circ\text{C}$ and in sucrose/NaCl, SEM ciliary counts and oryzalin-induced deciliation kinetics, phagocytosis rates, and conjugation pairing kinetics are described qualitatively as `"data not shown"`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Complete replacement of all macronuclear $\alpha$-tubulin genes with a non-acetylatable `K40R` allele eliminates $\alpha$-tubulin K40 acetylation in Tetrahymena without affecting viability, growth, microtubule architecture, ciliary motility, conjugation, or drug stability.* `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated by Southern, Western, IF, IEF, swimming velocity, and drug-sensitivity assays across independent clones (`R3C`, `R7C`) and an isogenic `K40` replacement control (`K1`).
- **Claim 2:** *Virtually all wild-type ciliary axonemal $\alpha$-tubulin is acetylated at K40, no other lysine is acetylated in compensation in `K40R` cells, and acetylated $\alpha$-tubulin carries additional charge-altering modifications.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by the uniform basic shift of the multi-band $\alpha$-tubulin isoform ladder on axonemal IEF gels (`Figure 5`) and the SDS-PAGE mobility shift (`Figure 4`).
