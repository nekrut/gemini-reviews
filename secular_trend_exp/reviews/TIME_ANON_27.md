### 1. Executive Summary

This manuscript investigates the pre-integrative and catalytic roles of Prototype Foamy Virus (PFV-1) integrase (IN) using three point mutants—`M5` ($\text{His}^{42}\text{Leu}$ in the HHCC zinc-finger domain), `M8` ($\text{Asp}^{160}\text{Gly}$ in the catalytic $\text{D,D}_{35}\text{,E}$ triad), and `M9` ($\text{Ile}^{106}\text{Thr}$, previously reported to impair strand transfer more severely than 3'-processing)—alongside biochemical cleavage assays with purified recombinant wild-type PFV-1 IN. The authors show that (1) none of the three mutations alters nuclear localization of Flag-tagged IN in HeLa cells (Fig. 1) or late-stage virion release after proviral plasmid transfection (Fig. 2A), whereas `M5` and `M8` abolish single-cycle infectivity and `M9` reduces infectivity by $\sim 50\%$ (Fig. 2B); (2) in infected U373-MG cells, `M5` and `M8` show reduced total viral DNA (`gag` qPCR) and an increased ratio of 2-LTR junctions to total `gag` DNA (up to $35\%$ in `M8` vs. $9\%$ in WT, Fig. 3); (3) in vitro, purified PFV-1 IN cleaves a 62-bp double-stranded oligonucleotide spanning the 20-bp palindromic PFV-1 2-LTR circle junction to produce recessed 3'-OH ends with a $5'\text{-AT}$ overhang (Fig. 4); and (4) nested `Alu`-LTR qPCR divided by total `gag` DNA yields an invariant apparent integration ratio ($\sim 1\text{--}2\%$) across WT, `M5`, `M8`, and `M9` (Fig. 6). From these data, the authors propose that 2-LTR circles are the primary precursor of linear viral DNA and conclude that *"integrase is essential, integration is not"* for retroviral replication.

While the in vitro demonstration that recombinant PFV-1 IN can endonucleolytically cleave the palindromic 2-LTR junction into 3'-processed ends (Figure 4) is a well-controlled biochemical result that offers a plausible explanation for symmetrical U3/U5 processing, the manuscript's overarching in vivo model (Figure 7B) and radical claims in the Abstract and Discussion are contradicted by the authors' own mass-balance stoichiometry and qPCR data. Specifically, cleaving a 2-LTR circle into a linear molecule does not change total `gag` copy number (since both circular and linear genomes carry `gag`), $65\%$ of viral DNA in the catalytically dead `M8` ($\text{Asp}^{160}\text{Gly}$) mutant is *not* in 2-LTR circles, and observing an unchanged `Alu`-LTR/`gag` ratio in a catalytic-triad mutant (`M8`) that is non-infectious in single-round reporter assays reflects `Alu`-PCR background proportional to total viral DNA rather than proof that integration is dispensable.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit

1. **Mass-Balance Contradiction in the Proposed "2-LTR Circle $\to$ Linear DNA" Model (Figures 3 and 7B):**
   * Throughout the Abstract (lines 7–9), Results (lines 90, 98–100), and Figure 7B, the authors claim that IN controls *total viral DNA production* (`gag`) by cleaving 2-LTR circles into linear DNA, and that loss of IN cleavage explains why total viral DNA decreases while 2-LTR circles accumulate in `M5` and `M8`.
   * **Stoichiometric fallacy:** Total viral DNA in Figure 3A and Figure 6B was quantified by qPCR amplifying the $5'$ end of the `gag` gene (line 55). A 2-LTR circle is a full-length circularized viral genome that **contains the `gag` gene** just like a linear viral DNA molecule. Therefore, endonucleolytic cleavage of a 2-LTR circle at the LTR-LTR junction converts 1 circular `gag`-containing molecule into 1 linear `gag`-containing molecule—it **cannot** alter the total number of `gag` copies in the cell. The reduction in total `gag` DNA in `M5` and `M8` (Fig. 3A) must reflect a defect in reverse transcription or cDNA stability, not a failure to linearize 2-LTR circles.
   * **Quantitative refutation by the `M8` ($\text{Asp}^{160}\text{Gly}$) mutant:** If reverse transcription produced 2-LTR circles as the obligate precursor to linear DNA (Figure 7B, Steps 1–2), then in the catalytic-triad active-site mutant `M8`, cleavage at Step 2 would be blocked and **100% of synthesized viral DNA would remain as 2-LTR circles**, with absolute 2-LTR copy numbers in `M8` equaling the total `gag` copy number unless reverse transcription itself was impaired. Instead, Figure 3B shows that absolute 2-LTR copy numbers in the mutants do not exceed WT, and Figure 3C shows that even at 24 hpi, **$65\%$ of viral DNA in `M8` (and $75\text{--}85\%$ in `M5` and `M9`) does not carry a 2-LTR junction** (i.e., is already linear despite a mutated IN catalytic triad). Moreover, the elevated percentage in Figure 3C ($35\%$ vs. $9\%$) is primarily driven by the reduction in the denominator (`gag` DNA in Fig. 3A) rather than an absolute overproduction of 2-LTR circles in Fig. 3B.

2. **Unvalidated `Alu`-LTR Signal in Catalytically Dead `M8` ($\text{Asp}^{160}\text{Gly}$) Used to Claim Integration Is Non-Essential (Figure 6):**
   * In Figure 6B–C, the authors report that the ratio of `Alu`-LTR qPCR copies to total `gag` DNA copies at 24 hpi is identical ($\sim 1.2\text{--}1.7\%$) across WT, `M5` ($\text{His}^{42}\text{Leu}$), `M8` ($\text{Asp}^{160}\text{Gly}$), and `M9` ($\text{Ile}^{106}\text{Thr}$). From this constant ratio, they conclude that IN mutations do not affect integration efficiency per unit of viral DNA and that *"integrase is essential, integration is not"* (line 105).
   * **Internal contradiction with Figure 2B:** In Figure 3A and Figure 6B (lower panel), cells infected with `M5` or `M8` contain $\sim 15\text{--}25\%$ as much total `gag` DNA (and, according to Fig. 6B upper panel, $\sim 15\text{--}25\%$ as many `Alu`-LTR copies) as WT-infected cells at 24 hpi. Yet in the single-round FAB $\beta$-galactosidase reporter assay (Figure 2B), which measures viral Tas transactivation at 24 hpi, `M5` and `M8` infectivity is near **background ($<2\text{--}5\%$)**, whereas `M9` (which has similar or lower `gag` DNA than `M5` at 10–24 hpi in Fig. 3A!) achieves **$\sim 50\%$ of WT infectivity**. If `M5` and `M8` genuinely produced functionally integrated proviruses at the exact same per-molecule rate as WT and `M9`, their FAB reporter signal in Fig. 2B would be proportional to their DNA levels ($\sim 20\%$), not abolished.
   * **Incomplete `Alu`-PCR background control:** In the Methods (line 124), the authors subtract linear amplification measured in a parallel first-round reaction containing **only primer $\lambda\text{TSPA}$** (wait: line 124 first says *"The same mix, containing only primer λ TSPA, was prepared"*, and later says *"a parallel reaction with the Alu primers in the first-round PCR, in order to calculate the linear amplifications"*—an internal text contradiction). A single-primer control omitting `Alu` primers does not control for PCR recombination / template-switching between abundant single-stranded `Alu` amplicons and unintegrated viral LTR DNA during the first-round amplification, which naturally scales as a fixed small fraction ($\sim 1\%$) of total unintegrated viral DNA (`gag`) even in the complete absence of integrase catalytic activity (`M8`).

3. **Missing Biochemical Controls on Mutant IN Proteins & Technical-Duplicate Error Bars:**
   * In Figure 4, only wild-type recombinant PFV-1 IN was purified and tested on the palindromic 2-LTR junction substrate. The authors never purified or tested recombinant `M5`, `M8`, or `M9` IN proteins on the 2-LTR junction oligonucleotide to demonstrate that `M8` abolishes junction cleavage while `M9` retains partial junction cleavage matching its $\sim 50\%$ viral infectivity.
   * In Figures 3A–B and 6B, the error bars represent *"standard deviations representing variations between two quantifications of the same sample"* (technical qPCR duplicates of one extract, lines 55, 93) rather than biological variance across the four independent infection experiments.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality

* **Claim that PFV-1 IN specifically cleaves the 20-bp palindromic 2-LTR junction in vitro to generate cohesive $5'\text{-AT}$ overhangs matching 3'-processed LTR ends (Figure 4):** **Well Supported.** The denaturing PAGE cleavage mapping alongside a G+A sequencing ladder (Fig. 4B), comparison with blunt U5 and U3 3'-processing substrates (Fig. 4C), EDTA inhibition, and lack of cleavage on mutated palindromes or HIV-1/MLV junctions (Fig. 4D) are convincing and resolve how symmetrical 2-bp loss can occur without invoking asymmetrical U5-only processing.
* **Claim that 2-LTR circles are the primary in vivo precursor of linear viral DNA and that IN is required only for linear DNA production rather than chromosomal integration (Abstract, Figures 6–7, Discussion lines 98–107):** **Methodologically Flawed / Overstated.** As detailed above, this claim violates `gag` copy-number conservation, is contradicted by the presence of $65\%$ non-2-LTR viral DNA in the catalytic-triad mutant `M8`, and misinterprets a constant $\sim 1\%$ `Alu`-PCR background-to-`gag` ratio in catalytically dead `M8` as wild-type integration efficiency.

* **Positives:** All primer and probe sequences for `Alu`-LTR nested qPCR, 2-LTR junction cloning, QuickChange mutagenesis (`M5`, `M8`, `M9`), prokaryotic His-tag expression (`pET15bIN`), and in vitro cleavage oligonucleotides (`U5B`, `U5A`, `U3B`, `U3A`, `LTR-LTRB`, `LTR-LTRA`), as well as raw LightCycler copy numbers across all four independent experiments in Figure 6C, are explicitly provided in the text and figures.
