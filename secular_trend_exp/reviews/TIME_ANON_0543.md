### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This study performs a genome-wide bioinformatic survey of the *Aux/IAA* gene family in eggplant (*Solanum melongena*), reporting 35 *SmIAA* genes classified into Clades A (A1–A5) and B (B1–B4), followed by chromosomal mapping, MCScanX synteny, PlantCARE promoter *cis*-element counting, SOPMA/SWISS-MODEL structural modeling, STRING protein–protein interaction prediction, re-plotting of public RNA-seq FPKM values (`PRJNA328564`), and qRT-PCR of 9 genes in seedling roots under NaCl, PEG6000, and IAA treatments. While the manuscript follows a common descriptive gene-family template, forensic audit reveals a fundamental domain-classification error that invalidates its central evolutionary conclusion: because the Pfam `PF02309` (Aux/IAA–PB1) domain is shared by both Aux/IAA transcriptional repressors and Auxin Response Factor (ARF) transcription factors, screening solely for `PF02309` without filtering out proteins containing N-terminal ARF domains (`B3` DNA-binding domain `PF02362` and `Auxin_resp` `PF06507`) caused the authors to misclassify eggplant ARF transcription factors as a novel "Subgroup B4 of non-canonical Aux/IAA proteins." This error is compounded by SWISS-MODEL/PyMOL superposition artifacts, copy-pasted methods text, uncalibrated circadian-confounded qRT-PCR controls, and the absence of statistical hypothesis testing in the expression experiments.

### 2. STRENGTHS
- **Multi-Dataset Integration:** Combines comparative genomics across five Solanaceae species and *Arabidopsis* with public multi-tissue transcriptomics (`PRJNA328564`) and wet-lab qRT-PCR across three abiotic/hormonal treatments (200 mM NaCl, 20% PEG6000, 100 μM IAA) at five timepoints.
- **Clear Documentation of Public Accessions:** Provides the eggplant cultivar accession (`V06B1246`), BioProject ID (`PRJNA328564`), and genome database sources.

### 3. WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT (Methodological & Statistical Audit)
- **Fatal Domain Misclassification: ARF Transcription Factors Misidentified as "Subgroup B4 Non-Canonical Aux/IAA Proteins":** In Section 4.1, candidate genes were identified solely by the presence of the `PF02309` (Aux/IAA) domain in Pfam/CDD/InterPro/SMART. However, `PF02309` encodes the C-terminal Type I/II Phox and Bem1p (PB1) dimerization domain present in **both** *Aux/IAA* genes and *ARF* (Auxin Response Factor) genes; canonical *ARF* proteins are distinguished from *Aux/IAA* proteins by possessing an N-terminal B3 DNA-binding domain (`PF02362`) and a middle `Auxin_resp` domain (`PF06507`), having much larger molecular weights (70–125 kDa vs. 17–35 kDa for Aux/IAAs), and containing 10–14 exons. Because the authors did not exclude proteins carrying `PF02362`/`PF06507`, 12 large multi-exon proteins (up to 1,108 aa / 122.6 kDa and 14 exons, lacking Aux/IAA Domains I and II but containing five extra N-terminal motifs—Motifs 3, 4, 7, 8, and 9 in Figure 5D) were retained and misclassified as "Subgroup B4 non-canonical Aux/IAA proteins." In Section 3 (line 85), the authors inadvertently reveal this error by noting that their B4 member `SmIAA1` "is predicted to encode `SmARF1`" and citing potato B4 homologs that are actually unrelated proteins (`StPHYB`, `StCDPK1`, `StSUT4`, `StSP6A`).
- **SWISS-MODEL Template Reuse & PyMOL Outlier-Trimming Artifact (Section 2.6 & 4.6):** The authors claim that SWISS-MODEL templates were selected with "sequence identity > 70%, coverage > 70%" for all 35 proteins (150–1,108 aa) and that PyMOL `align` yielded `RMSD < 1 Å` for 50 inter-clade pairs. In reality, Aux/IAA proteins are 58–81% intrinsically disordered (random coil), and no full-length Aux/IAA structure exists in the PDB (only the ~100 aa C-terminal PB1 domain has been structurally resolved). SWISS-MODEL threads different paralogs onto the *same* short PB1 template, and PyMOL's `align` command performs sequence alignment followed by iterative $\text{C}\alpha$ outlier rejection—reporting `< 1 Å` RMSD over the shared template core while discarding unaligned regions. Interpreting this modeling artifact as "structural conservation extends beyond evolutionary clades" is invalid.
- **Uncontrolled Circadian Confounding & Missing Statistical Tests in qRT-PCR (Section 2.9 & 4.9):** Seedlings grown under an 8 h light / 16 h dark cycle were immersed in NaCl, PEG6000, or IAA and harvested at 0, 2, 6, 12, and 24 h, using only the `0 h` sample as the control instead of time-matched mock-treated (water) controls at 2, 6, 12, and 24 h. Because auxin signaling and *Aux/IAA* transcription oscillate strongly across the diurnal light/dark cycle (and 54% of *SmIAA* promoter elements are light-responsive; Section 2.5), fold changes at 6 h, 12 h (dark transition), and 24 h confound treatment effects with endogenous circadian rhythms. Furthermore, no statistical tests (ANOVA, *t*-tests, or *p*-values) are described or reported for the qRT-PCR data.
- **Copy-Paste Error in Collinearity Methods (Section 4.3, Line 115):** The authors state that `OneStep MCScanX-SuperFast` was run "based on the Nei–Gojobori (NG) model with 1000 bootstrap replicates"—a phrase erroneously duplicated from the `Simple Ka/Ks Calculator` sentence immediately following it (`MCScanX` detects syntenic blocks via dynamic programming on BLASTP hits, not Nei-Gojobori bootstrapping).

### 4. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **[Methodologically Flawed] Subgroup B4 represents a Solanaceae-specific expansion of non-canonical Aux/IAA proteins with 14 exons and 9 conserved motifs:** Artifact of failing to filter out ARF transcription factors (and chimeric annotations) that share the C-terminal `PF02309` PB1 domain.
- **[Methodologically Flawed] Tertiary structure conservation (`RMSD < 1 Å`) spans different phylogenetic clades independently of sequence phylogeny:** Artifact of homology modeling onto identical PDB templates and PyMOL's iterative outlier rejection.
- **[Fragile] Dynamic root transcriptional responses to NaCl, PEG6000, and IAA (Figure 10):** Lacks time-matched mock controls to separate stress/hormone induction from diurnal cycling and lacks statistical significance testing.

### 5. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
While database sources and primer tables (Supplementary Tables S1–S11) are referenced, the manuscript contains duplicated abstract text, a copy-pasted methods sentence in Section 4.3, and no raw or processed numerical qRT-PCR $\Delta\Delta C_t$ variance tables or statistical test outputs.
