### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **No Primary Research Design or Quantitative Analysis:** As a general-audience clinical overview (`Tables 1–4`), the text presents no original study design, patient cohort, or statistical methodology.
- **Factual and Molecular Virology Errors in the Text:**
  1. **HIV Protease Substrate Misidentification (line 38):** Under `Protease inhibitors`, the author writes: *"These undergo a process of maturation that is mediated by cleavage of some of the viral proteins, particularly the cleavage of gp160 into gp120 and gp41. Prevention of this cleavage renders the virion unable to infect susceptible cells. The viral protease that accomplishes this cleavage is therefore a potential target for antiviral drugs, and these form the class of protease inhibitors."* This is a fundamental textbook error: HIV-1 `gp160` is cleaved into `gp120` and `gp41` in the trans-Golgi network by **host cellular furin / proprotein convertases**, NOT by the HIV-1 aspartyl protease. HIV-1 protease cleaves the viral **Gag (`Pr55`) and Gag-Pol (`Pr160`)** polyproteins during virion maturation.
  2. **Order-of-Magnitude Error in HIV Genome Size (line 28):** Under `Antiretroviral Drugs`, the author states: *"since there are only $10^5$ nucleotide bases in the HIV genome, the rate at which mutants arise is very high."* The HIV-1 RNA genome is $\sim 9.7\text{ kb}$ ($\approx 10^4$ nucleotides), not $10^5$ nucleotides ($100\text{ kb}$, which is the size of a herpesvirus genome).
  3. **Origin of the 1976 Swine Influenza Vaccine (first half of manuscript):** In discussing the 1976 U.S. A/New Jersey/76 (H1N1) "swine flu" vaccination campaign and Guillain-Barré syndrome, the text states that the vaccine was *"prepared from a killed duck virus."* Inactivated influenza vaccines are propagated in **embryonated chicken eggs** from reassortant seed strains (A/New Jersey/8/76 $\times$ A/PR/8/34), not prepared from a duck virus.
  4. **Measles SSPE Epidemiology and Vaccine Strain Claims:** Several historical and epidemiological numbers (e.g.,vidarabine vs acyclovir CASG mortality rates, VAPP rates of 1 in 2.4 million doses) are cited narratively without primary citations or confidence intervals.
