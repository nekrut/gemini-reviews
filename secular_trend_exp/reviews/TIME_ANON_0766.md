### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This molecular virology and genome-editing study compares the antiviral efficacy and double-strand break (DSB) repair mutational spectrum of *Lachnospiraceae bacterium* CRISPR-Cas12a (`LbCas12a` / `Cpf1`, driven with a 3' HDV ribozyme for precise crRNA processing) against *Streptococcus pyogenes* Cas9 (`SpCas9`) for inactivating integrated HIV-1 (`pLAI`) proviral DNA in human T cells. Screening 23 crRNAs targeting conserved sense/antisense HIV-1 regions in transient HEK293T co-transfections and advancing 8 crRNAs (`LTR1–3`, `Gag1`, `Vpr2`, `Tat1`, `Tat2`, `TatRev`) into 60-day spreading-infection challenges across 6 replicate stably transduced SupT1 T-cell cultures per crRNA, the authors show that whereas single-gRNA `SpCas9` invariably allows NHEJ-assisted viral escape, single-crRNA `LbCas12a` achieves permanent 60-day sterilization ("cure in a bottle", verified by 30-day co-culture rescue with uninfected WT SupT1 cells) in 6/6 `Tat2`, 3/5 `TatRev`, 1/6 `Gag1`, and 1/6 `Tat1` cultures. Mechanistically, they explain this via the PAM-distal staggered cleavage architecture of Cas12a (cutting at nt 18–23 outside the 5-nt PAM-proximal seed, allowing iterative re-cleavage of point mutants until frame-disrupting indels accumulate, tracked from Day 30 to Day 60 in Table 1). Furthermore, by comparing 243 deduplicated Cas12a proviral repair alleles against 190 Cas9 repair alleles—and corroborating across 1,569 published Cas12a-edited sequences from 55 literature studies—they discover that Cas12a (which generates 5-nt 5'-overhangs) produces larger deletions (mean $-24.3\text{ bp}$ vs. $-17.5\text{ bp}$) and deletions accompanied by small insertions ("delins", 44.4%), but **zero pure insertions** (0% vs. 30.8% in Cas9).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **Longitudinal Proviral Evolution Tracking (Day 30 vs. Day 60) & WT Co-Culture Rescue:** Table 1 provides a compelling demonstration of iterative cleavage in the "cured" cultures: between Day 30 and Day 60, WT sequences drop from 56 to 2 and point mutations drop from 38 to 1 while inactivating indels rise from 106 to 186, coinciding with the transition from rescue-positive (`+` at Day 30) to rescue-negative (`-` at Day 60) in 30-day co-cultures with unprotected SupT1 cells.
- **Deduplication of Clonal PCR/Viral Expansion Reads & Cross-Literature Meta-Validation:** Recognizing that identical indel clones in TA-cloning could reflect viral replication or PCR jackpotting, the authors deduplicated identical alleles (leaving $n=243$ unique Cas12a and $n=190$ unique Cas9 alleles) and validated the 0% pure-insertion signature across 1,569 sequences from 55 independent Cas12a studies across animals and plants (Supplementary Table S3).
- **Clear Mechanistic Synthesis of End-Processing Biochemistry:** Linking the 5-nt 5'-overhang generated by Cas12a to 5'$\to$3' exonucleolytic/endonucleolytic resection (Artemis / Mre11-CtIP) and increased microhomology-mediated end joining (MMEJ: 70/175 [40.0%] in Cas12a vs. 16/78 [20.5%] in Cas9) provides a coherent biochemical explanation for why staggered 5'-overhangs eliminate blunt-end fill-in (+1/+2 bp pure insertions characteristic of Cas9) and shift the repair spectrum toward larger deletions and delins.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Historical (Non-Simultaneous) Comparison Against Cas9 at Non-Identical Target Loci:** As the authors acknowledge, the Cas9 indel dataset (harvested at Day 12 and Day 110 using two gRNAs) was taken from their prior publication (`[10]`), and because `SpCas9` (`NGG` PAM) and `LbCas12a` (`TTTN` PAM) recognize different PAM sequences, the target sites in HIV-1 are not identical (though overlapping in regions like `TatRev`). Local sequence context (particularly PAM-proximal dinucleotides and flanking microhomologies) strongly influences Cas9 +1 insertion frequency (`[42, 43]`).
- **Low Per-Culture Clone Sampling Depth via TA-Cloning Instead of Deep Amplicon Sequencing (NGS):** Mutational spectra at Days 30 and 60 were profiled by conventional PCR, TA-cloning, and Sanger sequencing of ~28–38 clones per culture across 6 cultures (Table 1), rather than Illumina deep amplicon sequencing ($>10,000$ reads/sample), which would have quantified rare variants and exact clonal dynamics with much higher statistical precision.
- **Lack of Formal Statistical Hypothesis Tests on Indel Size and Class Distributions:** In Figures 5, 6, and 7, the authors report percentages (e.g., 0% vs. 30.8% pure insertions; 70/175 vs. 16/78 MMEJ) and mean deletion lengths ($24.3\text{ bp}$ vs. $17.5\text{ bp}$ regular deletions; $28.1\text{ bp}$ vs. $16.0\text{ bp}$ delin deletions;p. 124 claims *"The deletion component in Cas12a-induced delins is significantly larger than that of Cas9 delins"*), but no $p$-values from Fisher's exact test, $\chi^2$ test, or Mann-Whitney $U$ test are reported in the text or figure legends! (For example, 0/114 vs. 38/125 pure insertions gives Fisher's exact $p = 2.4 \times 10^{-12}$, and 70/175 vs. 16/78 MMEJ gives $p = 0.0025$—computing these explicitly would strengthen the presentation).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Single-crRNA LbCas12a can achieve complete inactivation of infectious HIV-1 in stably transduced SupT1 T-cell cultures (especially with `crTat2`, 6/6 cultures cured by day 60).* `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated by 60-day syncytia monitoring, 30-day WT SupT1 rescue assays, and longitudinal loss of WT and point-mutant proviruses between Day 30 and Day 60.
- **Claim 2:** *LbCas12a editing produces regular deletions and deletion-insertions ("delins") with larger mean deletion sizes than SpCas9, but essentially no pure insertions.* `[Well Supported]` — Consistently demonstrated in the HIV-1 proviral dataset ($n=243$ vs. $n=190$ unique alleles) and replicated across 1,569 published Cas12a target sequences in 55 external studies.
- **Claim 3:** *PAM-distal cleavage by Cas12a spares the PAM and 5-nt seed sequence from immediate mutation, permitting repeated re-cleavage of point-mutated proviruses until frame-inactivating indels occur.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by the disappearance of Day-30 point mutations by Day 60 in Table 1.
