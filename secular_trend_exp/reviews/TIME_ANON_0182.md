### 1. Executive Summary
This study presents a comparative evolutionary, structural, and expression analysis of 34 Trehalose-6-phosphate synthase (`TPS`) family genes across *Populus trichocarpa* (12 genes: `PtTPS1`–`PtTPS12`), *Arabidopsis thaliana* (11 genes: `AtTPS1`–`AtTPS11`), and *Oryza sativa* (11 genes: `OsTPS1`–`OsTPS11`). The authors show that the family splits into Class I (7 genes, typically with 16 introns inside the coding region and catalytically active TPS residues conserved in `PtTPS1`/`AtTPS1`/`OsTPS1`) and Class II (27 genes, consistently harboring 2 coding introns and rapid substitutions at substrate-binding pockets). They estimate synonymous ($d_S$) and nonsynonymous ($d_N$) distances using `PAML` (`YN00` and `CODEML` branch models), map segmental duplications, build a homology model of the TPS domain against *E. coli* OtsA (`1GZ5`), and evaluate tissue and stress-response expression of `PtTPS` genes by RT-PCR. While the exon-intron conservation analysis and duplication history are informative, the evolutionary statistics suffer from severe phylogenetic pseudoreplication (applying an independent two-sample $t$-test to all pairwise $d_N/d_S$ ratios across the tree), an under-specified `CODEML` two-ratio branch model on unrooted subtrees without site or branch-site models, and non-quantitative 35-cycle endpoint RT-PCR.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Phylogenetic Pseudoreplication in Pairwise $d_N/d_S$ $t$-Tests (Figure 5, line 63):** To test whether $d_N/d_S$ differs between the TPS domain and the TPP domain, the authors compute pairwise $d_N$ and $d_S$ values using `YN00` across gene pairs and apply a standard two-sample $t$-test ($t\text{-test}, P < 0.0001$). Pairwise evolutionary distances among $N = 34$ sequences ($\binom{34}{2} = 561$ pairs) share internal phylogenetic branches and are strongly non-independent; treating all pairwise ratios as independent observations drastically underestimates variance and produces invalid $p$-values. A likelihood-ratio test partitioning the alignment by domain in `CODEML` (fixed vs separate $\omega$ parameters on the tree) should have been used instead.
- **Ambiguous Unrooted Two-Ratio `CODEML` Models and Absence of Site/Branch-Site Tests (Table 1):** In Table 1, the authors split the 34 genes into 4 small subtrees (`Tree 1`: 7 Class I genes; `Tree 2`: 7 Class IIb1 genes; `Tree 3`: 9 Class IIb2 genes; `Tree 4`: 11 Class IIa genes), divide each subtree into "Clade A" ($\omega_1$) and "Clade B" ($\omega_0$), and run `CODEML` One-ratio vs Two-ratio branch models. On an unrooted tree split at its root into two clades (Clade A and Clade B), the basal branch connecting Clade A and Clade B cannot be uniquely assigned to Clade A or Clade B without an outgroup. Furthermore, averaging $\omega$ across all sites in a clade (giving $\omega \approx 0.05\text{--}0.27$) has zero power to detect episodic positive selection or site-specific functional divergence (`Branch-Site` models or `DIVERGE` Type I/II coefficients), yet the authors speculate extensively about positive selection and neofunctionalization at catalytic sites.
- **Saturated 35-Cycle Endpoint RT-PCR (Figure 7, line 101):** Expression of the 12 `PtTPS` genes across 5 tissues and 4 abiotic stress treatments ($\text{H}_2\text{O}_2$, NaCl, salicylic acid, and drought) was assayed by endpoint RT-PCR using **35 amplification cycles** (`94°C 30s, 55°C 30s, 72°C 30s` for 35 cycles) visualized by ethidium bromide agarose gel electrophoresis, with no biological replicate counts, densitometry, or quantitative real-time PCR (qRT-PCR) reported. At 35 cycles, abundant transcripts (`PtTPS1`, `PtTPS5`, `PtTPS10`, `18S` internal control) are in the PCR plateau phase, rendering claims of lack of stress induction insensitive to 2–5-fold transcriptional changes.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Plant `TPS` genes divide into two ancient clades with conserved intron-exon structures (Class I with 16 coding introns and Class II with 2 coding introns) that predated the monocot-eudicot split, followed by extensive salicoid segmental duplications in Populus.* `[Well Supported]` — Clearly demonstrated by the phylogenetic topology (Fig. 2a), conserved intron phase/position mapping (Fig. 2b), and syntenic chromosomal block analysis (Fig. 3).
- **Claim 2:** *Only `PtTPS1` (along with `AtTPS1` and `OsTPS1`) retains all conserved OtsA active-site residues (`Arg9`, `Trp40`, `Tyr76`, `Trp85`, `Asp130`, `His154`, `Arg262`, `Asp361`, `Glu369`), whereas `PtTPS2` and Class II `PtTPS` proteins have accumulated non-conservative substitutions at the catalytic pocket.* `[Well Supported]` — Supported by sequence alignment and structural mapping onto *E. coli* OtsA (`1GZ5`, Fig. 6).
- **Claim 3:** *The TPP domain evolves under stronger purifying selection than the TPS domain ($t\text{-test}, P < 0.0001$), and specific clades (`Tree 1` and `Tree 3`) underwent asymmetric evolutionary rates driven by functional divergence.* `[Fragile]` — While the direction of domain conservation (`TPP` vs `TPS` sequence identity) is biologically plausible, the pairwise $t$-test is pseudoreplicated and the two-ratio branch model lacks outgroup rooting and site-specific selection tests.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Positive:** Gene models, chromosome coordinates, and `CODEML` log-likelihoods ($\ln L$, $\omega_0, \omega_1$, $2\Delta\ln L$) are tabulated in Table 1 and Supplementary Table 1.
- **Missing:** The RT-PCR primer sequences for `PtTPS1`–`PtTPS12` and `18S` rRNA are relegated to an external supplementary table (`Supplementary Table 3`) not included in the main manuscript text, and the exact age and biological replicate numbers ($n$) of the *Populus deltoides* seedlings used in the stress treatments are not reported.
