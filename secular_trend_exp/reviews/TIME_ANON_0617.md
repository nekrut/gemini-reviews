### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This molecular biology study characterizes the genomic organization, transcriptional start site, minimal core promoter, transcription factor binding, and epigenetic regulation of the human high-affinity leukotriene $\text{B}_4$ receptor gene (`BLT1`), and reports the discovery that the functional promoter of `BLT1` resides entirely inside the open reading frame (ORF) of the newly identified low-affinity $\text{LTB}_4$ receptor gene (`BLT2`) located `~4 kb` upstream ("promoter in ORF"). Using a `5.5-kb` human genomic phage clone (`LambdaNOK`, GenBank `AB008193`), the authors map the `BLT1` transcription initiation site by 5'-RACE in HL-60 granulocytes, perform 5'-deletion luciferase reporter analyses across myeloid (`THP-1`) and non-myeloid (`HeLa`) cells to localize basal promoter activity to the `-76` to `-31` region, demonstrate by EMSA, antibody supershift, and site-directed mutagenesis (`m1` at `-52/-47`) that Sp1 binds and activates the core GC box, and show via `HpaII`/`MspI` Southern blotting and in vitro `SssI` CpG methylase reporter assays that cell-type-specific CpG methylation of the `BLT1` promoter correlates with transcriptional silencing in non-expressing cells (`HeLa`, `Raji`) vs active transcription in `BLT1`-expressing myeloid cells (`THP-1`, `U937`, retinoic acid-differentiated `HL-60`).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
- **Transfection of Unpurified Bulk Ligation Mixtures in the In Vitro Methylation Assay (Methods line 61, Figure 6B):** To test whether CpG methylation selectively silences the `BLT1` promoter without methylating the `pGL3-basic` luciferase backbone, the authors excised the `-123/+91` `KpnI-HindIII` promoter fragment (`214 bp`), treated it with or without `SssI` methylase + `SAM`, phenol-extracted and ethanol-precipitated the fragment, ligated `200 ng` of the methylated or unmethylated fragment into `1.5 ug` of `KpnI/HindIII`-digested `pGL3-basic`, and transfected the ethanol-precipitated ligation mixture directly into `HeLa` cells (normalizing to co-transfected `pRL-TK`). Because `SssI` treatment and subsequent `HpaII` verification/precipitation steps can alter DNA recovery or cohesive-end ligation efficiency, transfecting raw ligation mixtures without gel-purifying and quantifying closed-circular ligated product (or verifying equal plasmid copy number uptake by Southern/qPCR) leaves open the possibility that part of the `85%` drop in normalized luciferase activity reflects lower ligation yield in the `SssI`-treated reaction.
- **Restriction Enzyme (`HpaII`/`MspI`) Southern Blotting vs Bisulfite Sequencing:** Methylation status in `THP-1`, `HL-60`, `U937`, `HeLa`, and `Raji` cells (Figure 5C) was assayed exclusively by `HpaII` (methylation-sensitive) and `MspI` (methylation-insensitive) digestion at two `CCGG` recognition sites (`-90` and `-63`) within the `365-bp` `PstI` fragment. While the Southern blot clearly demonstrates complete protection from `HpaII` cleavage in `HeLa` and `Raji` cells and demethylation in `THP-1` and `U937` cells, `HpaII` probes only 2 of the 17 CpG dinucleotides in the `-123/+91` promoter region (and does not directly assay the CpG inside the `-52/-47` Sp1 binding site `GGGCGG`). Sodium bisulfite genomic sequencing would provide single-CpG resolution across all 17 sites.
- **Strengths of Promoter & EMSA Controls:** The transcriptional characterization is exceptionally thorough: 5'-RACE (`15/19` clones mapping to `+1`, `4/19` to `-18`), RNase protection references, matched sense (`p(-1062/+91)`) vs antisense (`p(+91/-1062)`) promoter constructs in both `THP-1` and `HeLa` cells, cold wild-type vs mutant (`m1`, `m2`, `m3`) oligonucleotide competition in EMSA, specific anti-Sp1 supershift vs anti-Sp2/Sp3/Sp4/Egr-1 negative controls, and matching `m1`/`m2` site-directed point mutations in the `-123/+91` luciferase reporter (showing `>70%` loss of activity upon mutating the `-52/-47` Sp1 box `m1`, vs no loss upon mutating the `-85/-80` site `m2`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: The human `BLT1` gene lacks a TATA box and relies on Sp1 binding at a `-52/-47` GC box for basal transcription** — `[Well Supported]`. Demonstrated concordantly by 5'-deletion luciferase series, EMSA competition with wild-type and mutated probes, specific anti-Sp1 antibody supershifts, and site-directed mutagenesis of the `-52/-47` motif.
- **Claim 2: The functional promoter of `BLT1` is embedded within the coding ORF of the upstream `BLT2` receptor gene ("promoter in ORF")** — `[Well Supported]`. Genomic sequencing (`AB008193`) and alignment with the `BLT2` ORF place the `BLT1` core promoter (`-76` to `-31`) directly inside the protein-coding region of `BLT2`, representing a rare mammalian example of overlapping coding/promoter architecture.
- **Claim 3: Cell-specific CpG methylation of the `BLT1` core promoter represses basal transcription in non-myeloid cells (`HeLa`, `Raji`) whereas demethylation permits expression in myeloid cells (`THP-1`, `U937`)** — `[Well Supported]`. Supported by `HpaII`/`MspI` Southern blots paired with Northern blots across five cell lines and by in vitro `SssI` promoter methylation reducing reporter activity by `85%` in `HeLa` cells. Notice also that the authors appropriately note the nuance in `HL-60` cells (where the `HpaII` sites are largely methylated despite inducible `BLT1` mRNA expression upon retinoic acid differentiation, which they reasonably attribute to Either monoallelic/subpopulation demethylation or methylation at sites outside the two `HpaII` recognition sequences).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Sequence & Reagent Transparency:** The `5.5-kb` human genomic sequence spanning `BLT2` and `BLT1` is deposited in DDBJ/EMBL/GenBank under accession `AB008193` (and cDNA `D89078`). Complete sequences for 5'-RACE primers, EMSA oligonucleotides (`wt`, `m1`, `m2`, `m3`), and restriction boundaries for all luciferase deletion constructs are reported in the text and figures.
