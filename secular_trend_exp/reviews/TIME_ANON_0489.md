### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This manuscript reports the molecular cloning, complete cDNA nucleotide and deduced amino acid sequence (4,652 bp; 1,051 amino acids), structural domain analysis, and eukaryotic cell-surface expression of the human integrin $\alpha_3$ subunit of the VLA-3 ($\alpha_3\beta_1$) extracellular matrix receptor. Starting from immunoaffinity-purified placental $\alpha_3\beta_1$ (using mAb J143) to raise and affinity-enrich rabbit polyclonal anti-$\alpha_3$ antibodies, the authors screened a human endothelial cell $\lambda\text{gt11}$ expression library to isolate initial 3' cDNA clones (3.22, 3.24) and subsequently walked 5' across HepG2 hepatocarcinoma (3.122, 3.285) and endothelial (3.410, 3.520) cDNA libraries. Authenticity of the cloned $\alpha_3$ cDNA is rigorously established by four independent lines of evidence: (1) reciprocal immunoaffinity purification between $\beta$-galactosidase fusion proteins and native placental $\alpha_3$ (Fig. 1); (2) reciprocal immunodepletion of surface $^{125}\text{I}$-labeled LOX melanoma cell extracts using mAb J143 and an antiserum raised against the deduced COOH-terminal peptide (Fig. 2); (3) exact identity between the deduced mature $\text{NH}_2$-terminal sequence (`FNLDTRFLVVKEAG`) and previously determined Edman peptide sequence; and (4) cell-surface expression of the reconstructed cDNA in transfected CHO cells verified by FACS with mAb J143 (Fig. 5). Comparative sequence analysis demonstrates that human $\alpha_3$ lacks an I-domain, contains 18/19 conserved cysteines, three $\text{DX(D/N)X(D/N)GXXD}$ divalent cation-binding motifs within seven $\text{NH}_2$-terminal repeats, a dibasic heavy/light-chain cleavage site, and shares 89% identity with hamster galactoprotein b3 (Gap b3), 70% similarity with chicken CSAT antigen band 2, and 37% overall identity (43% cytoplasmic identity) with human integrin $\alpha_6$.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Multi-Pronged Biochemical & Immunological Validation:** The authors do not rely solely on sequence homology or antibody screening of phage plaques (which can yield false positives). Instead, they perform reciprocal affinity selection (using immobilized clone 3.22 fusion protein to affinity-purify antibodies from crude serum that then recognize native placental $\alpha_3$, Fig. 1C) and reciprocal immunodepletion between mAb J143 and an anti-C-terminal synthetic peptide serum on radiolabeled cell lysates (Fig. 2, including negative control antibody lanes a–c, f, i).
- **Internal Quantitative Consistency:** The deduced mature polypeptide of 1,019 residues (1,051 minus the 32-residue signal peptide) has a calculated molecular mass of 113,505 Da; accounting for 13 canonical N-glycosylation sites ($\text{Asn-X-Ser/Thr}$, average ~2,500 Da per glycan chain) yields ~146,000 Da, matching the empirical SDS-PAGE mobility ($M_r$ 145,000–150,000 non-reduced, and 110,000 heavy + 30,000 light chains upon reduction at the dibasic cleavage motif between residues 839–864).
- **Functional Surface Expression & Northern Controls:** Reconstructed full-length $\alpha_3$ cDNA in CDM8 drives surface expression in ~20% of transiently electroporated CHO cells by FACS against a clean vector-only control (Fig. 5), and Northern blotting shows the expected 5.0 kb transcript abundant in MRC fibroblasts and low in leukemic T-cell lines (MOLT-4, HSB) with an actin loading control (Fig. 6).

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Chimeric cDNA Reconstruction Across Tissue/Tumor Sources:** The 3.47 kb expression construct (`pBSKSII+` / CDM8) was spliced together from clones originating from two distinct cell types: human endothelial cells (`3.410` BamHI/NdeI 0.9 kb 5' fragment and `3.520` HindIII/SalI 1.08 kb 3' fragment) and the HepG2 hepatocarcinoma line (`3.285` NdeI/EcoRI 1.66 kb central fragment). While restriction maps overlapped consistently (Fig. 3), potential coding polymorphisms or tissue-specific alternative splicing between endothelial and hepatoma transcripts are not explicitly evaluated.
- **Incomplete Double-Stranded Sequencing Coverage:** The Methods state that "Clones 3.24 and 3.285, and 5'-1 kb of clone 3.410 were sequenced in both directions." Because the full cDNA sequence spans 4,652 bp (and clone 3.410 is >2 kb while 3.24 is at the 3' end), a portion of the coding or 3' untranslated region was sequenced on only one strand. In addition, minor typesetting/OCR dropouts appear in the printed nucleotide figure (e.g., around nucleotides 1030–1040, 1210–1230, 1390–1410 in Fig. 4 where boxed metal-binding domains obscured characters in print), though the sequence was deposited in EMBL/GenBank/DDBJ (`M59911`).
- **Absence of Direct Adhesion/Ligand-Binding Assays on Transfected Cells:** While the title and introduction highlight $\alpha_3\beta_1$ as a receptor for fibronectin, laminin, and collagen, the CHO transfectants were only assayed for surface immunoreactivity with mAb J143 (Fig. 5) and were not tested in functional cell adhesion assays on purified ECM substrates.
- **Heuristic Similarity Tree Construction:** The integrin $\alpha$-subunit similarity tree (Fig. 9) is constructed from averaged pairwise Smith & Smith similarity scores without evolutionary substitution models, branch-length confidence intervals, or bootstrap resampling.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** The cloned 4.6 kb cDNA encodes the authentic human integrin $\alpha_3$ subunit. `[Well Supported]` — Unambiguously proven by $\text{NH}_2$-terminal peptide identity, reciprocal fusion-protein affinity purification, reciprocal C-terminal peptide immunodepletion, and mAb J143 surface FACS staining in transfected CHO cells.
- **Claim 2:** Hamster galactoprotein b3 (89% identical, 100% identical in the C-terminal 40 residues) and chicken CSAT antigen band 2 (70% similar over 170 C-terminal residues) are species homologues of human integrin $\alpha_3$. `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by full-length and C-terminal sequence alignments (Fig. 7) and cross-hybridization/cross-reactivity.
- **Claim 3:** Human $\alpha_3$ is evolutionarily closest to $\alpha_6$ (37% overall identity; 43% cytoplasmic tail identity, $13/30$ residues), which may underlie their shared recognition of the E8 fragment of laminin. `[Moderately Supported]` — The sequence conservation between $\alpha_3$ and $\alpha_6$ (including their cytoplasmic domains) is striking and quantitatively documented, and the functional hypothesis linking this homology to laminin E8 fragment binding is appropriately framed as a testable proposal.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Accession & Sequence Availability:** High. The complete 4,652 bp cDNA sequence and 1,051 aa translation are printed in full (Fig. 4) and deposited in EMBL/GenBank/DDBJ under accession number `M59911`.
- **Reagent & Protocol Detail:** Cloning vectors, restriction sites used for full-length reconstruction (`BamHI`, `NdeI`, `EcoRI`, `HindIII`, `SalI`, `XhoI` to `XbaI` linker conversion), antibody affinity purification buffers (0.2 M and 3.5 M KSCN elution), and synthetic peptide sequence (`CRIQPSETERLTDDY`) are specified in full detail enabling independent replication.
