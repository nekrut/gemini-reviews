### 1. Executive Summary
This study provides a rigorous clonal and functional characterization of HIV-1 envelope-specific $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{CD8}^+$ cytolytic T lymphocytes (CTL) isolated from HIV-1-seronegative, vaccinia-naive human volunteers immunized in a Phase I prime-boost trial using a live recombinant vaccinia vector expressing HIV-1 LAI *env* (`vac-env`) followed by purified recombinant gp160 protein. By stimulating PBMCs with *env*-transfected autologous EBV-transformed B lymphoblastoid cell lines (B-LCL) and performing limiting-dilution T-cell cloning, the authors demonstrate that this prime-boost regimen induces potent class I MHC-restricted $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL alongside class II-restricted $\text{CD4}^+$ CTL. Crucially, comparative functional assays using acutely HIV-1-infected and gp120-pulsed autologous $\text{CD4}^+$ T lymphoblasts reveal that $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL are 3–10-fold more potent per cell than $\text{CD4}^+$ CTL and selectively lyse productively infected cells, whereas $\text{CD4}^+$ CTL also lyse uninfected bystander $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells that have endocytosed shed gp120 via surface CD4.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Experimental Controls & Assay Design:** Every Chromium-51 ($^{51}\text{Cr}$) release cytotoxicity assay includes well-matched negative and specificity controls: mock-infected targets, wild-type/control vaccinia vector (`vSC8`)-infected targets, and `vac-env` (`vPE16` / `vPE8`)-infected targets, alongside PBMC cultures from a wild-type vaccinia control recipient (volunteer A41) and unimmunized seronegative donors ($n=2$).
- **Monoclonality Assurance:** Limiting dilution cloning was restricted to plates seeded at cell densities yielding $<10\%$ growth-positive wells, ensuring a $>95\%$ Poisson probability of monoclonality.
- **Quantification of Productive Infection vs. Bystander Lysis:** In autologous PHA-activated polyclonal $\text{CD4}^+$ T-cell lines (>99% pure after two sorting cycles) infected with HIV-1 LAI, the fraction of productively infected cells (20–30%) was independently validated by two orthogonal flow cytometric methods: intracellular p24/gag staining of permeabilized cells and downregulation of surface CD4 using an epitope-distinct mAb (`12T4D11`). The plateau of target lysis by $\text{CD8}^+$ CTL clones (`A42.52`, `A42.53`) matched the exact productively infected fraction (Fig. 5 and Fig. 7), whereas $\text{CD4}^+$ CTL clone `A42.45` lysed a substantially larger fraction (Fig. 7) and lysed uninfected gp120-pulsed $\text{CD4}^+$ blasts (Fig. 6).
- **Sample Size Limitations:** The primary limitation is the small number of immunized human subjects evaluated ($n=2$ `vac-env`/gp160 recipients: A42 with a strong response and A52 with a modest response), with most clonal comparisons derived from donor A42 (20 *env*-specific clones out of 56 screened; 10 characterized in depth) and one clone (`A52.161`) from donor A52. While appropriate for an intensive clonal mechanistic study within a Phase I trial, inter-individual variability in CTL induction cannot be statistically generalized from $n=2$. Additionally, figure plots report mean specific lysis (stating $\text{SEM} < 5\%$ in Methods) without plotting individual error bars on the curves.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *"The vac-env/gp160 regimen can induce env-specific CD8+ CTL in seronegative humans that are class I MHC-restricted and 3–10-fold more active on a per cell basis than CD4+ CTL."* — `[Well Supported]`
  - Directly demonstrated by bulk stimulated PBMC assays (Fig. 2), limiting dilution clonal panels (Fig. 3), single-allele-matched B-LCL restriction mapping showing HLA-A3.1 and HLA-B35 restriction (Table 1), and E:T titration curves down to 0.03:1 (Fig. 4).
- **Claim 2:** *"In contrast to CD4+ CTL, gp120-specific CD8+ CTL can correctly distinguish between productively infected cells and noninfected T cells that have acquired gp120 via CD4."* — `[Well Supported]`
  - Elegantly proven both by pulsing uninfected autologous $\text{CD4}^+$ T lymphoblasts with $30\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ recombinant gp120 (where 3/3 $\text{CD4}^+$ clones lyse targets at 60–80% while 7/7 $\text{CD8}^+$ clones show near-zero lysis, Fig. 6) and in acute HIV-1 infection cultures where shed gp120 coats 100% of cells (Fig. 7).
- **Claim 3:** *"Some vaccine-induced CD8+ CTL clones showed complete crossreactivity with targets expressing the env genes of the divergent MN and RF isolates."* — `[Well Supported]`
  - Directly supported by Fig. 8 using recombinant vaccinia vectors `vMN-462` and `vRF-222`, showing that HLA-A3.1-restricted gp120-specific $\text{CD8}^+$ clones (`A42.9`, `A42.29`, `A42.46`) lyse LAI, MN, and RF targets equally well, whereas $\text{CD4}^+$ clones and the HLA-B35 gp41-specific $\text{CD8}^+$ clone `A42.52` are strain-specific.
