### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **Strict one-mutant-per-donor sampling (`n = 63` donors) eliminates clonal expansion bias:** A major statistical flaw in early in vivo T-cell `HPRT` mutation databases was sequencing multiple `6-TG^r` colonies from the same individual without `TCR` Southern blotting, which inflated hotspots whenever an in vivo T-cell clone expanded. Sampling exactly 1 mutant from each of 63 independent donors guarantees statistical independence across all 63 mutational events.
- **Combined cDNA (RT-PCR) and genomic DNA analysis:** Pairing cDNA sequencing with genomic exon amplification allows the authors to distinguish true genomic deletions from point mutations in intronic splice donor/acceptor sites that manifest as exon skipping in `HPRT` mRNA.
- **Balanced epidemiological stratification (`34` smokers vs. `29` non-smokers; `45` males vs. `18` females):** The cohort size (`n = 63`) is well balanced for testing smoking-associated transversion (`G:C -> T:A`) enrichment or age effects.
