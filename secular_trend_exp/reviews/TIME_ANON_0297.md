### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular phylogenetics study resolves the polyphyletic nature of the enigmatic holoparasitic angiosperm order Rafflesiales by sampling representatives of all nine genera across all four traditionally recognized families: Rafflesiaceae *s. str.* (*Rafflesia*, *Rhizanthes*, *Sapria*), Mitrastemonaceae (*Mitrastema*), Cytinaceae (*Bdallophyton*, *Cytinus*), and Apodanthaceae (*Apodanthes*, *Berlinianche*, *Pilostyles*). Analyzing three datasets—mitochondrial *matR* (106/77 taxa, 2,177 bp), mitochondrial *atp1* (71 taxa, 1,265 bp), and a 3-gene nuclear SSU rDNA + plastid *rbcL* + *atpB* matrix (103/77 taxa, 4,646 bp, where holoparasites are represented by nuclear SSU rDNA)—under Maximum Parsimony (MP), Maximum Likelihood (ML), and partitioned Bayesian Inference (BI), coupled with `Seq-Gen` parametric simulations and Siddall–Whiting taxon-removal tests, the authors demonstrate that: (1) Rafflesiales comprises 3–4 independent holoparasitic lineages (Rafflesiaceae in Malpighiales, Mitrastemonaceae in Ericales, Cytinaceae in Malvales, and Apodanthaceae in Malvales or Cucurbitales); (2) MP on nuclear SSU rDNA is severely misled by long-branch attraction (LBA), clustering long-branch parasites into an artifactual clade in 79–83% of simulated datasets, whereas ML and BI recover the *matR*-congruent placements; and (3) incongruent placements in mitochondrial *atp1* (and possibly Apodanthaceae *matR*) reflect single-gene horizontal gene transfer (HGT) or formidable host/pollen DNA contamination.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Exemplary Diagnosis of Long-Branch Attraction via Parametric Simulations & Taxon Removal:** Rather than merely speculating about LBA in hyper-accelerated holoparasite nuclear 18S/SSU rDNA, the authors execute two rigorous tests: (a) Siddall–Whiting single-parasite-clade inclusion analyses under MP (showing that when analyzed one at a time without other long-branch parasites, MP places *Mitrastema* in Ericales, *Rafflesia*/*Rhizanthes*/*Sapria* in Malpighiales, and *Cytinus*/*Bdallophyton* and *Pilostyles* in Malvales, matching the simultaneous ML/BI tree); and (b) 100 `Seq-Gen` parametric simulations of 1,766 bp alignments on both 20-taxon and 77-taxon GTR+$\Gamma$+I ML model trees (Figure 5), demonstrating that increasing non-parasite taxon sampling from 20 to 77 taxa fails to rescue MP (79% vs. 83% false LBA clades) because adding distantly related long-branch parasites attracts them to Cytinaceae rather than breaking up the terminal parasite branches.
- **Forensic Transparency on Host & Environmental Contamination:** Section "Formidable contamination issues" candidly documents how initial PCR amplifications from *Rafflesia tuan-mudae* attached buds and *Berlinianche* 5-mm flowers amplified host mitochondrial *matR* (*Tetrastigma* and *Julbernardia*, plotted directly in Figure 1 as proof), and how sticky papillate styles of *Pilostyles* initially yielded wind-borne grass pollen and fungal 18S sequences until verified across independent geographic accessions (Texas and California).
- **Incomplete Taxon/Gene Coverage in Apodanthaceae & *atp1*:**
  - In the nuclear SSU rDNA / 3-gene dataset, only *Pilostyles* was sequenced for Apodanthaceae (*Apodanthes* and *Berlinianche* are `N/A` in Table 1), and for *Berlinianche* no authentic *matR* sequence could be amplified.
  - In the *atp1* matrix, eudicot sampling from GenBank was sparse (32 non-parasitic eudicots, completely lacking Cucurbitales), which limits the ability to test whether Apodanthaceae *atp1* groups with Cucurbitales (as in *matR*) or represents legume host-to-parasite HGT (sister to *Pisum* for *Pilostyles*, while *Apodanthes* pairs on a long branch with an anomalous *Polemonium* sequence with BI PP = 0.54).
  - For the 3-gene matrix, coding plastid *rbcL* and *atpB* (which are lost in holoparasites) as ~2,880 bp of missing data (`?`) for all 14 Rafflesiales taxa while keeping them for photosynthetic taxa stabilizes the non-parasite backbone, which the authors properly verify by running backbone-constrained SSU-only analyses (Figure 4).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Traditional Rafflesiales is polyphyletic, comprising three or four independent origins of extreme holoparasitism across Malpighiales (Rafflesiaceae *s. str.*), Ericales (Mitrastemonaceae), Malvales (Cytinaceae), and Malvales/Cucurbitales (Apodanthaceae).** `[Well Supported]` — Strongly supported by cross-compartmental congruence between nuclear SSU rDNA (under ML/BI and single-clade MP) and mitochondrial *matR*, alongside critical re-evaluation of floral morphological characters.
- **Claim 2: Adding long-branch parasite taxa exacerbates rather than cures MP long-branch attraction in nuclear SSU rDNA.** `[Well Supported]` — Directly proven by the 20-taxon and 77-taxon `Seq-Gen` simulation experiments and single-clade removal tests.
- **Claim 3: Incongruences between mitochondrial (*atp1*, *matR*) and nuclear SSU rDNA trees in Apodanthaceae and *Mitrastema* reflect single-gene horizontal gene transfer (HGT).** `[Moderately Supported]` — The grouping of *Pilostyles* (a legume parasite) with *Pisum* in *atp1* is consistent with host-to-parasite mitochondrial HGT, whereas the *Mitrastema* + *Beta* (*atp1*) and *Apodanthes* + *Polemonium* (*atp1*, PP = 0.54) pairings are weakly supported and confounded by sparse eudicot *atp1* sampling—caveats the authors explicitly acknowledge in the Abstract and text.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **TreeBASE Deposition & Model Parameters:** All sequence alignments (`matR`, `atp1`, 3-gene, SSU) are publicly deposited in TreeBASE (`Study S1177`, matrix accessions `M2034`–`M2037`), and PAUP* `LSET RCLASS`, ModelTest 3.06 hLRT/AIC p-values, MrBayes 3.0b4 partitioning schemes, and `Seq-Gen 1.2.7` simulation settings are thoroughly documented. (GenBank accession numbers for the newly sequenced amplicons are referenced via the supplementary files/author website but should ideally be listed in a main-text table.)
