### 1. Executive Summary
This multi-omics study investigates early molecular alterations in gonadal white adipose tissue (gWAT) preceding the onset of type 2 diabetes (T2D) in 10-week-old female New Zealand Obese (NZO) mice stratified by hepatic fat content and early blood glucose trajectories into diabetes-prone (DP, $n=15$) and diabetes-resistant (DR, $n=15$) groups. The authors integrate RNA-seq, small RNA-seq ($n=29$), Illumina Mouse Methylation BeadChip arrays ($n=6$/group), and TIMS-TOF DIA proteomics ($n=30$, 6,032 proteins) using Weighted miRNA Co-expression Network Analysis (WMCNA), identifying differential expression of imprinted miRNA clusters at the *Dlk1-Gtl2* (chr12, upregulated in DP) and *Mest* (*miR-335-5p*, chr6, downregulated in DP) loci. They then seek translational corroboration across two human cohorts—adipose tissue from 14 monozygotic (MZ) twin pairs discordant for T2D ($n=12$ pairs for miRNA array, $n=5$ female pairs for *miR-335*) and plasma from 99 high-risk female participants in the TÜF cohort—followed by functional mimic transfection in murine 3T3-L1 and human SGBS preadipocytes and dual-luciferase reporter assays in HeLa cells targeting *Gna12* and *Gga3*.

While the breadth of multi-omic profiling and the deposition of raw sequencing/proteomic datasets (`GSE310455`, `GSE310456`, `GSE310457`, `GSE310390`, `PXD057291`) are substantial strengths, the statistical threshold choices across omics layers are inconsistent and frequently rely on unadjusted nominal $p < 0.05$, the human female MZ twin sub-analysis ($n=5$ pairs) is severely underpowered, and in vitro target validation is partially discordant between species and reporter assays.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Inconsistent Multiple-Testing Correction Across Omics Layers:** While bulk mRNA-seq in NZO gWAT applies an adjusted $p < 0.05$ (`DESeq2`, yielding 5,046 DEGs), the small RNA-seq (`edgeR`), DNA methylation (`limma` on MM285 array, $n=6$/group), and DIA proteomics (6,032 proteins) analyses define differential features using unadjusted nominal $p < 0.05$ without false discovery rate (FDR) control. Specifically, the 437 differentially expressed small RNAs (150 miRNAs), the 1,606 differentially abundant proteins, and the differentially methylated CpGs annotated to 70/150 miRNAs (Table 2, where most CpG $p$-values range from $0.004$ to $0.049$) are all reported at unadjusted $p < 0.05$. Across $\sim 285,000$ CpG probes and $>6,000$ proteins, filtering at nominal $p < 0.05$ guarantees a high fraction of false positives.
- **Underpowered Human Twin Subgroup Analysis:** The human validation cohort comprises 14 MZ twin pairs discordant for T2D, of which only 12 pairs had small RNA array data. Because *miR-335* was not significant across all 12 pairs, the authors restrict the analysis to $n = 5$ female twin pairs, reporting a one-tailed or borderline Wilcoxon signed-rank $p = 0.043$ and a non-significant Spearman correlation with $\text{HbA}_{1\text{c}}$ ($\rho = -0.588, p = 0.074$; Fig. 8C–D). For $n = 5$ pairs, the minimum possible two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank $p$-value (when all 5 pairs change in the same direction) is $2/32 = 0.0625$ (or $p \approx 0.043$ using normal approximation without continuity correction). No multiple-testing correction is applied for screening all orthologous DE miRNAs and CpGs across the twin arrays.
- **Discrepancy in Promoter Window Definition for TFBS Enrichment:** In the Methods section ("Enrichment of the transcription factors"), the authors state that upstream sequences of "500 bp upstream from the start of the pre-miRNAs" were interrogated using JASPAR and the Crawford-Garthwaite one-sample t-test at a threshold of $10^{-6}$. However, the title of Table 1 explicitly states "within 500 kb upstream of differentially expressed miRNAs in the gWAT of DP mice." A 500 bp vs. 500 kb window represents a 1,000-fold difference in genomic search space.
- **Reagent Catalog Error in Methods:** In the "Cell culture, transfection, differentiation and migration assay" section, the miRCURY LNA miR-335-5p mimic and the non-targeting (NT) control mimic are assigned the exact same Qiagen catalog number (`#YM00473600-ADA`), omitting the distinct catalog identifier for the negative control mimic.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **[Well Supported]** Pre-diabetic 10-week-old DP female NZO mice exhibit higher gWAT insulin resistance (adipo-IR index) and broad transcriptional/proteomic remodeling (downregulation of *Slc2a4*/GLUT4 and *Irs2*, coordinated upregulation of the maternal *Dlk1-Gtl2* chr12 miRNA cluster) independent of gWAT depot weight.
- **[Moderately Supported]** *miR-335-5p* directly represses murine *Gga3* via its 3'-UTR and accelerates early (day 3) triglyceride accumulation and transcriptional maturation markers in 3T3-L1 adipocytes. However, the parallel claim that *Gna12* is a direct functional target of *miR-335-5p* is **[Fragile]**: *Gna12* 3'-UTR luciferase activity was completely unchanged by *miR-335* mimic in HeLa cells (Additional file 1: Fig. S4), and conversely *GGA3* mRNA was not significantly reduced by *miR-335-5p* mimic in human SGBS cells ($p = 0.172$, Fig. 10D).
- **[Overstated]** Positioning circulating plasma *miR-335-5p* as a female-specific clinical biomarker of prediabetes based on a modest unadjusted correlation ($\rho = -0.25, p = 0.01$ in $n=99$ TÜF females; $\rho = -0.30, p = 0.01$ in BMI $> 26$) and $n=5$ female twin pairs over-interprets noisy exploratory associations that lacked a male comparator arm in the TÜF plasma cohort.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Raw murine transcriptome (`GSE310456`), small RNAome (`GSE310457`), methylome (`GSE310390`), 3T3-L1 RNA-seq (`GSE310455`), and DIA proteome (`PXD057291`) datasets are deposited in public repositories. Software versions (`FastQC v0.11.5`, `Trim Galore v0.6.4`, `HISAT2 v2.1.0`, `SortMeRNA v2.1b`, `Bowtie2 v2.3.5.1`, `DESeq2 v1.24.0`, `edgeR v3.26.8`, `limma v3.40.6`, `WGCNA v1.70.3`, `Spectronaut v14.10`) are thoroughly documented.
- **Deficiencies:** No custom analysis scripts for the WMCNA integrative scoring or Crawford-Garthwaite JASPAR scanning are deposited; the NT mimic catalog number is duplicated; and human twin and TÜF cohort data require author request.
