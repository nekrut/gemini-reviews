### 1. Executive Summary
This candidate-gene association study genotypes five polymorphisms in the apolipoprotein B (*APOB*) gene—the exon 1 signal peptide 9-bp insertion/deletion (`rs11279109`), exon 26 codon 2488 *Xba*I (`rs693`), exon 26 codon 3611 *Msp*I (`rs1801701`), exon 29 codon 4154 *Eco*RI (`rs1042031`), and the 3' hypervariable minisatellite (`3'VNTR`)—in $n=795$ Kuwaiti subjects to test for associations with fasting serum lipid traits (TC, TG, HDL-C, LDL-C) and family history of dyslipidemia/hypertriglyceridemia. Forensic audit of the manuscript and its eight tables reveals multiple severe internal contradictions, statistical errors, and an outright inversion of the primary odds ratio interpretation:
1. **Inverted Odds Ratio Interpretation for the *Msp*I (`rs1801701`) `M-M-` Genotype:** In Table 4, the logistic regression outcome variable is explicitly defined as high triglycerides (`0 = <= 1.7 mmol/L` and `1 = > 1.7 mmol/L`), and Table 5 shows that 2 of 4 (`50.0%`) `M-M-` homozygotes have high TG (`> 1.70 mmol/L`) compared to only 85 of 579 (`14.7%`) `M+M+` homozygotes and 4 of 82 (`4.9%`) `M+M-` heterozygotes. Consequently, the logistic regression in Table 4 yields an adjusted **Odds Ratio of 8.80 (95% CI: 1.13–68.46, p = 0.038)** for `M-M-` vs. `M+M+`—indicating an **8.8-fold INCREASED odds of hypertriglyceridemia** (driven by just $n=4$ homozygous individuals). Yet throughout the Abstract (line 13), Results (line 222), and Discussion (line 276), the authors invert this finding, claiming `M-M-` confers an *"8.8 fold decrease in the risk of high TG levels"* / *"8.8 fold increased probability of having lower TG levels (<1.70 mmol/L)"* and designating `M-` as a *"protective allele"*!
2. **Contradictory Cohort Age Reporting & Systematic Mislabeling of SEM as SD:** In Materials and Methods (`Sample description`, line 32), the cohort ($n=795$) is described as having *"a mean of 57.1 years (±13.0 SD)"*, whereas Table 1 reports `Age (Years) ± SD` for the exact same $n=795$ cohort as `32.63 ± 0.78` (males, $n=324$), `30.62 ± 0.66` (females, $n=471$), and `31.45 ± 0.68` (total, $n=795$). Furthermore, the Dispersion values labeled `± SD` in Table 1 (`Age ± 0.68`, `TC ± 0.04`, `TG ± 0.03`, `HDL-C ± 0.02`, `LDL-C ± 0.04`) and Table 7 are clearly Standard Errors of the Mean (SEM), not Standard Deviations (an SD of 0.68 years across 795 subjects spanning ages 18–69 is mathematically impossible).
3. **Impossible Sample Size Summation in Haplotype Table 7:** For a cohort of $n=795$ diploid individuals ($1,590$ total chromosomes), Table 7 reports `Non-carrier` plus `Carrier` counts for every haplotype that sum to **2,356** (`e.g., Haplotype A: 1675 Non-carriers + 681 Carriers = 2,356`, which is $\approx 3 \times 795$), an impossible number of either subjects or chromosomes.
4. **Scrambled dbSNP `rs` Identifiers Between Abstract and Methods/Tables:** In the Abstract (line 7), codon 2488 *Xba*I is assigned `rs1042031`, codon 3611 *Msp*I is assigned `rs693`, and codon 4154 *Eco*RI is assigned `rs1801701`, whereas in Methods (line 63) and Tables 2–5 they are assigned `rs693`, `rs1801701`, and `rs1042031`, respectively.
5. **Failure to Apply Multiple-Testing Correction:** The authors explicitly state (line 118) that *"Bonferroni correction for multiple testing was taken into consideration but was not applied."* None of the borderline nominal associations ($p = 0.021, 0.031, 0.038, 0.048$) survive correction across 5 polymorphisms, 8 haplotypes, and 4 lipid traits.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Mathematical Inversion of the Primary Logistic Regression Result (Table 4 vs. Table 5 & Text):**
  - In Table 5 (`MspI rs1801701`), among subjects with normal TG (`<= 1.70 mmol/L`), genotype counts are `M+M+ = 494`, `M+M- = 78`, `M-M- = 2`. Among subjects with elevated TG (`> 1.70 mmol/L`), genotype counts are `M+M+ = 85`, `M+M- = 4`, `M-M- = 2`.
  - The unadjusted odds ratio of elevated TG (`> 1.70 mmol/L`) for `M-M-` relative to `M+M+` is $\frac{2/2}{85/494} = \frac{1.0}{0.17206} = 5.81$ (reported in Table 4 as unadjusted `OR = 5.81, 95% CI: 0.81–41.80, p = 0.048` and adjusted `OR = 8.80, 95% CI: 1.13–68.46, p = 0.038`).
  - By Contrast, for the `Ins/Del` polymorphism, `Del` carriers (`ID` and `DD`) have *higher* rates of elevated TG (`22.4%` and `21.1%` vs. `12.6%` in `II`), and because Table 4 sets `DD` as the reference category (`OR = 1.0`), `ID` has `OR = 0.52` for high TG. The authors appear to have confused the coding direction of `MspI` (where `M+M+` is the reference category, `OR = 1.0`) with `Ins/Del`, leading them to falsely report that `M-M-` has an 8.8-fold *decreased* risk of high TG.
  - Moreover, basing a primary genetic risk claim on a homozygous recessive cell containing only $n=4$ individuals (`2 vs. 2`) is statistically fragile and violates standard minimum minor-homozygote count thresholds.
- **Cohort Age Contradiction & SEM-as-SD Misreporting (Line 32 vs. Table 1 vs. Lines 213 & 222):**
  - Line 32 states mean age is `57.1 ± 13.0` years; Table 1 states mean age is `31.45 ± 0.68` years.
  - In line 213, mean TG for insertion carriers is written as `0.95 ± 0.0` (truncating the SEM/SD to zero), and in line 222, mean TG for `M-` heterozygotes is written as `0.88 ± 0.9 mmol/L` whereas Table 3 reports `M+M-` mean TG as `0.88 ± 0.44 mmol/L`.
- **Table 7 Haplotype Sample Size Inflation ($N = 2,356$):** In Table 7, every column sums to `2,356` (`1675 + 681 = 2356`; `2032 + 324 = 2356`; `2094 + 262 = 2356`; etc.). Since the study genotyped $n=795$ individuals (`1,590` chromosomes), a total $N$ of `2,356` is mathematically irreconcilable with the study design.
- **Uncontrolled Multiple Hypothesis Testing:** Testing 5 markers across 4 quantitative lipid traits (Table 3), binary TG status (Tables 4–5), binary family history of hypercholesterolemia (Table 6), and 8 haplotypes across 4 lipid traits (Table 7) involves $>60$ statistical tests. With the smallest reported nominal p-value in the entire paper being $p = 0.021$, applying any standard multiple-testing correction (Bonferroni or Benjamini-Hochberg FDR) renders all reported genetic associations non-significant ($p_{adj} > 0.50$).
