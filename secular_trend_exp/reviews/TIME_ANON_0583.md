### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
* **Missing Main Text, Cohort Sizes, and Statistical Tests:**
  - The main body (`pp. 2239–2245`) was not extracted from the scanned images. Consequently, the number of biopsies per diagnostic group (normal, OLP, dysplasia, primary SCC, lymph node metastasis), patient clinical staging, and statistical tests are completely absent.
* **Inability of Immunohistochemistry to Distinguish Pro-MMP Zymogens from Catalytically Active Enzymes:**
  - As described in the Abstract, the study relies entirely on immunohistochemical staining of MMP-1, MMP-2, and MMP-9 and TIMP-1, -2, and -3. Standard IHC antibodies generally recognize both latent pro-enzymes and active cleaved forms (as well as MMP-TIMP inactive complexes). Without gelatin/casein zymography or in situ zymography, the Abstract's claim that *"there is increased production of MMPs and TIMPs inside the dynamic process of invasions and metastases"* conflates protein accumulation (including inhibited TIMP-bound complexes) with net proteolytic activity.
* **Subjective Semi-Quantitative IHC Scoring Across Heterogeneous Compartments:**
  - Comparing stromal fibroblast/inflammatory staining in OLP (an inflammatory mucocutaneous disease with dense band-like T-cell infiltrate) against desmoplastic tumour stroma in SCC and lymphoid parenchyma in lymph node metastases requires blinded, Compartment-stratified scoring and multiple-testing correction across $6 \text{ markers} \times 5 \text{ tissue groups}$, none of which can be verified from the stub.
