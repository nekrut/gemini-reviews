### 1. Executive Summary
This primary experimental immunology and virology study demonstrates that an HIV-1 NL4-3–derived lentiviral vector (`HDV`, deleted for *env*, *vif*, *vpr*, *vpu*, and *nef*, pseudotyped with VSV-G, and expressing EGFP or IRES-EGFP) efficiently and stably transduces not only TCR/mitogen-activated human $\alpha\beta$ and $\gamma\delta$ T cells, IL-2–activated NK cells, macrophages, and monocyte-derived dendritic cells (up to 4 months in culture), but also purified resting ($\text{HLA-DR}^-\text{CD69}^-$) human $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{CD8}^+$ T cells (both $\text{CD45RA}^+\text{RO}^-$ naive and $\text{CD45RO}^+\text{RA}^-$ memory subsets) when cultured with common $\gamma_c$-chain cytokines (IL-2, IL-4, IL-7, IL-15) or IL-6 in the absence of TCR stimulation. Continuous 8-day BrdU labeling proves that >50% of transduced cytokine-stimulated resting T cells never enter S-phase (whereas an MLV-based vector `pMX.EGFP` fails to transduce <0.5% of these cells), and naive $\text{CD45RA}^+$ cells retain their naive phenotype for 30 days post-transduction. Furthermore, cytokine stimulation renders resting $\text{CD4}^+$ T cells permissive to replication-competent R5-tropic HIV-1 (`BAL-GFP`), accompanied by cytokine-induced CCR5 upregulation on memory T cells. Finally, the authors validate the system for primary human T-cell structure–function genetics by expressing wild-type versus cytoplasmic-tail-truncated (`Asp188` frameshift) murine CD28 (`mCD28`) in human $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{hCD28}^-\text{CD8}^+$ T cells, demonstrating strict dependence on the cytoplasmic domain for anti-mCD28 costimulated proliferation and 50-fold IL-2 production.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Stringent Purification & Phenotypic Verification of Resting T Cells:** To ensure that apparent transduction of "resting" T cells was not an artifact of contaminating pre-activated blasts, PBMCs were monocyte-depleted, positively selected for CD4 or CD8, detached with Detachabead, and negatively depleted of $\text{HLA-DR}^+$ and $\text{CD69}^+$ cells (achieving 99.5% CD4 purity, <0.3% baseline HDV transduction in media alone, and >95% $\text{CD69}^-\text{HLA-DR}^-$ status among $\text{GFP}^+$ cytokine-transduced cells).
- **Elegant Cell-Cycle & Retroviral Specificity Controls:** Pairing continuous 8-day BrdU incorporation flow cytometry (Figure 5A–B) with a parallel VSV-G–pseudotyped gammaretroviral MLV vector control (`pMX.EGFP`, which transduced ~100% of cycling Jurkat cells but <0.5% of cytokine-treated primary T cells) directly proves that $\gamma_c$-cytokine–permissive lentiviral integration occurs in non-dividing $G_0/G_1$ primary T cells without requiring S-phase progression or nuclear envelope breakdown.
- **Clean Orthogonal Structure–Function Assay in Primary Human T Cells:** Using xenogeneic murine CD28 (`WT` vs. `$\Delta$cyto` Asp188 frameshift) in human T cells—and specifically in repeatedly stimulated $\text{hCD28}^-\text{CD8}^+$ human T cells to rule out mCD28/hCD28 heterodimerization (Figure 10B), alongside an anti-mCD4 clustering control—provides a rigorous, artifact-free demonstration of costimulatory signaling dissection in primary lymphocytes.
- **Quantitative Reporting Limitations:** In keeping with classical flow cytometry and cellular immunology conventions, figures display single representative FACS dot plots/histograms (Figures 1–8) and single representative $[\text{}^3\text{H}]$thymidine / CTLL-2 IL-2 titration curves (Figures 9–10) without summary bar graphs showing mean $\pm$ SD across $n$ independent blood donors or formal statistical $p$-values, and several control experiments (e.g., IFN-$\alpha$ pre-integration block, 3-gene co-transduction, RCR assay, CXCR4 upregulation) are cited as "data not shown."

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Multiply attenuated VSV-G–pseudotyped HIV-1 vectors (`HDV`) stably transduce primary human $\text{CD4}^+/\text{CD8}^+/ \gamma\delta$ T cells, NK cells, macrophages, and dendritic cells, whereas primary resting or T-cell–stimulated B cells are refractory.** `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated with long-term (4-month) longitudinal FACS tracking and lineage-gated transduction panels (Figures 1–2).
- **Claim 2: Survival/$\gamma_c$-chain cytokines (IL-2, IL-4, IL-7, IL-15, and to a lesser extent IL-6) overcome the pre-integration block in resting naive and memory T cells without requiring S-phase entry or inducing naive-to-memory differentiation.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by subset-sorted transduction (Figures 3–4), continuous 8-day BrdU co-staining (Figure 5), 30-day CD45RO tracking (Figure 6), post-infection cytokine rescue, and replication-competent R5 HIV-1 infection (Figures 7–8).
- **Claim 3: HDV enables clean structure–function genetic dissection of signaling receptors (mCD28 WT vs. $\Delta$cyto) in primary human T cells.** `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively shown by dose-dependent anti-CD3 + anti-mCD28 proliferation and CTLL-2 IL-2 bioassays in $\text{CD4}^+$ and $\text{hCD28}^-\text{CD8}^+$ primary human T cells (Figures 9–10).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Experimental Detail:** Plasmid constructions (`pHIV-PLAP` backbone, `HDV-IRES.EGFP`, `BAL-GFP`, `pMX.EGFP`, `mCD28` WT and `Asp188` frameshift), bead-sorting ratios, cytokine concentrations (`IL-2` 200 U/ml, `IL-4` 20 ng/ml, `IL-6` 400 U/ml, `IL-7` 20 ng/ml, `IL-15` 10 ng/ml, `TNF-$\alpha$` 50 ng/ml), viral titers/MOIs (10–20 for VSV-G HDV, 1–2 for R5 HIV-1), and antibody clones/sources are thoroughly documented in the Materials and Methods.
