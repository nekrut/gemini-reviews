### 1. Executive Summary
This study provides definitive molecular and genetic proof that cytoplasmic actin is the primary *in vivo* target of cytochalasin B (CB) in mammalian cell motility and cytokinesis. Building on their prior isolation of a CB-resistant human KB cell mutant (`Cyt 1`) that expresses an acidic variant of $\beta$-actin ($\beta'$-actin) and lacks wild-type $\beta$-actin, the authors clone and sequence the full-length $\beta'$-actin and $\gamma$-actin cDNAs from `Cyt 1` cells (deposited under EMBL/GenBank/DDBJ accession `X63432`). They identify two missense substitutions in $\beta'$-actin—`Val139 -> Met` (`GTG -> ATG`) and `Ala295 -> Asp` (`GCC -> GAC`)—alongside a completely wild-type $\gamma$-actin sequence. To achieve high-level exogenous expression competing against endogenous cytoplasmic actin (the most abundant cellular protein), they engineer a plasmid vector (`pTKSV\beta'-act 1`) coupling the SV40-driven $\beta'$-actin cDNA to a $5'$-truncated herpes simplex virus thymidine kinase (`\Delta 1 tk`) gene that produces only $\sim 40\%$ normal TK levels, forcing HAT-selected transformants in `KB100 TK-` cells to integrate high-copy tandem arrays. Across 8 independent transformants, stepwise CB-selected sublines (`Tf\beta'-17(9)`), drug-free revertants (`Tf\beta'-17(40)`), and restriction-fragment single-mutant chimeras (`pTKSV\beta'-act 2` and `3`), the authors demonstrate that $\beta'$-actin expression quantitatively confers resistance to CB-induced growth inhibition ($D_{10}$), stress-fiber disruption, and cell-surface zeiosis, and that *both* `Val139Met` and `Ala295Asp` substitutions (which flank opposite sides of the inter-domain cleft between actin subdomains 1 and 3) are required for high-level resistance.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Ingenious Selectable Marker Engineering for High-Copy Stoichiometric Expression:** Because actin constitutes $5\text{--}10\%$ of total cellular protein and CB resistance exhibits incomplete dominance (requiring a substantial fraction of the cellular actin pool to be mutant $\beta'$-actin), standard single-copy stable transfections fail to alter cellular phenotype. By deleting the $5'$ leader and first 10 codons of HSV *tk* (forcing translation initiation from the second internal AUG at $\sim 40\%$ efficiency), the authors selected in HAT medium specifically for high-copy tandem integrants, achieving $\beta'$-actin levels of $7\text{--}32\%$ of total actin in primary clones and $41\%$ after stepwise selection (`Tf\beta'-17(9)`).
- **Quantitative Dose-Response, Reversion, and Chimeric Dissection of Double vs. Single Mutations (Table I):**
  - Across 8 independent transformant clones (`Tf\beta'-11` through `Tf\beta'-18`), the fraction of $\beta'$-actin (quantified by densitometric integration of vertical-slab isoelectric focusing [IEF] gels) correlated monotonically with the $10\%$ survival dose ($D_{10}$), rising from $1.4\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ in parental `TK-` cells ($0\%$ $\beta'$-actin) to $1.5\text{--}1.6\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ at $7\text{--}8\%$ $\beta'$-actin, $2.0\text{--}2.6\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ at $24\text{--}28\%$ $\beta'$-actin, $3.2\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ at $32\%$ $\beta'$-actin, and $9.7\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ at $41\%$ $\beta'$-actin (approaching $12.0\ \mu\text{g/ml}$ in `Cyt 1` cells at $50\%$ $\beta'$-actin).
  - Crucially, passaging the unstable clone `Tf\beta'-17(9)` for 40 generations without drug (`Tf\beta'-17(40)`) caused $\beta'$-actin expression to drop from $41\%$ to $10\%$ and $D_{10}$ to revert from $9.7$ to $1.9\ \mu\text{g/ml}$, proving that resistance tracks dynamically with $\beta'$-actin dosage rather than secondary host mutations.
  - Chimeric cDNAs separating `Val139Met` (`\beta'-Actin 2`) and `Ala295Asp` (`\beta'-Actin 3`) confirmed that `Ala295Asp` accounts for the $-1$ IEF charge shift (Figure 5) and showed that either single mutation alone at $20\text{--}34\%$ of total actin confers only minimal resistance ($D_{10} = 1.6\text{--}1.8\ \mu\text{g/ml}$), proving synergistic requirement for both residues.
- **Southern Blot Exclusion of Insertional Mutagenesis:** `BamHI` restriction digestion and Southern hybridization across 6 clones (Figure 6) confirmed distinct host-junction fragments in every clone plus a spontaneous plasmid-loss revertant (`Tf\beta'-113`) that simultaneously lost hybridizing plasmid bands, $\beta'$-actin protein on IEF, and CB resistance.
- **Minor Reporting Note:** Table I reports point estimates for `% of total actin` and $D_{10}$ ($\mu\text{g/ml}$) without listing standard errors across replicate clonogenic survival dishes or densitometry scans, although the consistency across 14 independent clonal lines and conditions is exceptionally tight.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Actin Is the Primary *In Vivo* Target of Cytochalasin B on Cell Proliferation, Stress Fibers, and Zeiosis:** `[Well Supported]` Transferring the cloned $\beta'$-actin cDNA into CB-sensitive cells reproduces the complete cellular resistance phenotype—clonogenic survival, preservation of rhodamine-phalloidin stress fibers (Figure 7), and suppression of zeiotic surface blebbing within 10 minutes of $20\ \mu\text{M}$ CB exposure (Figure 8).
- **Structural Localization of the Cytochalasin Binding Pocket to the Cleft Between Actin Subdomains 1 and 3:** `[Well Supported]` Mapping `Val139` ($\alpha$-helix 137–144 in subdomain 1) and `Ala295` (adjacent to helix 288–294 in subdomain 3) onto the $2.8\text{ \AA}$ actin–DNase I crystal structure (`Kabsch et al.`) provides a compelling structural rationale placing the CB-binding site at the hinge base of the subdomain 1/3 cleft.
