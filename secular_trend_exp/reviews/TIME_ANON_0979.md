### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This molecular pathology study investigates the mechanism of transcription factor AP-2$\alpha$ (*TFAP2A*, chromosome 6p22.3-pter) downregulation in primary human cutaneous melanoma ($n = 52$ primary tumors). By comparing spatially matched immunohistochemistry (IHC) for AP-2$\alpha$ protein with *in situ* hybridization (ISH) for *TFAP2A* mRNA on consecutive tissue sections across 52 primary melanomas, and performing microsatellite loss of heterozygosity (LOH) analysis using markers `D6S470` and `D6S263` on microdissected areas of 13 informative primary tumors, the authors test whether AP-2$\alpha$ protein loss arises from genomic deletion/transcriptional silencing or post-transcriptional failure. Among 27 strongly AP-2$\alpha$ protein-positive tumors, 25 ($92.6\%$) were concordantly mRNA-positive. Among 25 tumors containing AP-2$\alpha$ protein-negative areas, 9 ($36\%$) lacked both mRNA and protein, whereas 16 ($64\%$) retained *TFAP2A* mRNA expression throughout the consecutive protein-negative section. Microsatellite LOH or chromosome 6 monosomy was detected in $4/5$ ($80\%$) informative mRNA-/protein-negative tumor areas, but also in $5/13$ ($38\%$) informative mRNA-positive tumor areas. The authors cautiously conclude that while 6p LOH/monosomy occurs frequently, post-transcriptional or translational failure accounts for a substantial fraction ($64\%$) of AP-2$\alpha$ protein loss in primary cutaneous melanoma. *(Note: The archived manuscript file contains only the Abstract and Reference list because the historical full text was archived as a scanned PDF only).*

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **Spatially matched consecutive-section design (IHC + ISH + microdissected LOH):** Rather than bulk homogenates that obscure intratumoral heterogeneity and stromal admixture, the authors compare AP-2$\alpha$ protein (IHC) and mRNA (ISH) across consecutive histological sections in $n = 52$ primary melanomas, using the 27 protein-positive tumors ($92.6\%$ mRNA concordance) as an internal positive control for ISH sensitivity.
- **Exemplary claim proportionality on LOH findings:** Observing LOH at `D6S470`/`D6S263` in both mRNA-negative ($4/5$, $80\%$) and mRNA-positive ($5/13$, $38\%$) tumor areas, the authors explicitly state that *"this chromosome region is thus suggestive of harbouring a putative tumour suppressor gene of cutaneous melanoma, but this referring specifically to TFAP2A could not be completely verified in this analysis."*

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Small informative sample size in the microsatellite LOH sub-cohort:** Only 13 of the 52 tumors were analyzed for LOH at `D6S470` and `D6S263`, yielding just 5 informative mRNA-/protein-negative areas ($4/5 = 80\%$) and 13 informative mRNA-positive areas ($5/13 = 38\%$). By Fisher's exact test, $4/5$ vs. $5/13$ yields $p = 0.28$, which is underpowered to establish a statistically significant enrichment of 6p LOH in mRNA-negative areas.
- **Alternative explanations for ISH+/IHC- discordance not excluded in abstract:** Retention of *TFAP2A* mRNA in AP-2$\alpha$ protein-negative regions ($16/25$, $64\%$) could reflect not only impaired translation initiation or accelerated proteasome degradation, but also expression of alternatively spliced dominant-negative *TFAP2A* isoforms (e.g., AP-2B, cited in the reference list: `Buettner et al.`, `Meier et al.`, `Ohtaka-Maruyama et al.`) or somatic point mutations/truncations that hybridize with the ISH riboprobe while abolishing the C-terminal antibody epitope.
- **Archival text truncation:** The full experimental text, IHC/ISH photomicrographs, and LOH electropherograms are locked in a 303.2 KB scanned PDF, preventing inspection of probe coordinates and antibody clone specifications.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim that $64\%$ ($16/25$) of AP-2$\alpha$ protein-negative primary melanoma regions retain *TFAP2A* mRNA on consecutive sections, while $36\%$ ($9/25$) lose both mRNA and protein:** `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by paired IHC and ISH across 52 primary melanomas with $92.6\%$ ($25/27$) positive concordance in protein-positive controls.
- **Claim that chromosome 6p LOH (`D6S470`, `D6S263`) alone does not fully verify *TFAP2A* as the sole 6p tumor suppressor target:** `[Well Supported]` — Transparently reflects the $5/13$ ($38\%$) LOH rate in mRNA-positive regions.
- **Claim that failure in post-transcriptional processing/translation is a possible inactivation mechanism of AP-2$\alpha$ in cutaneous melanoma:** `[Moderately Supported]` — Strongly motivated by the ISH+/IHC- discordance in $16/25$ cases, though alternative splicing or epitope-disrupting point mutations remain alternative explanations.
