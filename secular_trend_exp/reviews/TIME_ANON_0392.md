### 1. Executive Summary
This mechanistic cellular virology and immunology study demonstrates that intracellular (cytoplasmic) expression of the ov-serpin plasminogen activator inhibitor type 2 (PAI-2) protects HeLa cells from the rapid lytic cytopathic effect (CPE) of alphaviruses (Ross River virus [RRV] by up to $10^5$-fold in MOI, and Sindbis virus) and adenovirus 5, converting acute lytic RRV infection into a persistent productive infection (<1% infected cells). Using stably transfected sense PAI-2 clones (S1a, S1b) compared against antisense PAI-2 (A2/7, A2/17), chloramphenicol acetyltransferase (CAT), and empty vector (CMV) controls, the authors systematically rule out inhibition of viral structural polyprotein cleavage (p95/pE2 Western blot), inhibition of apoptosis (EM morphology, Bcl-2 levels, lack of protection against influenza/vaccinia, antioxidant insensitivity), and extracellular/cell-surface uPA inhibition (recombinant PAI-2 up to 8,000 ng/mL and exogenous uPA blockade). Instead, through six orthogonal lines of evidence—poly(I:C) IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ bioassays, RT-PCR for *IFNA*/*IFNB*, 15$\times$–30$\times$ concentrated supernatant transfer, long-term (18–25 d) neutralizing anti-IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ antibody reversal, ISRE EMSA/supershift and nuclear ISGF3$\gamma$ immunoblotting, and rapid (4 h) *2'-5'-OAS* Northern blot induction—they prove that intracellular PAI-2 induces constitutive sub-threshold autocrine IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ production that primes ISGF3$\gamma$ expression and antiviral gene activation. Finally, Northern blotting in MonoMac6 macrophages shows that viral exposure (even UV-inactivated or antibody-neutralized RRV and poly(I:C)) strongly induces endogenous *PAI-2* mRNA.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Rigorous Elimination of Alternative Hypotheses and Multi-Pronged Mechanistic Proof:** The experimental design is exemplary in testing and falsifying competing hypotheses before establishing the autocrine IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ priming mechanism:
  1. Sense clones (S1a, S1b) are consistently benchmarked against four negative control lines (antisense A2/7 and A2/17, irrelevant protein CAT, and empty CMV vector).
  2. Extracellular PAI-2 is excluded both by adding up to 8,000 ng/mL purified active PAI-2 (150,000 IU/mg) to A2/7 cells and by adding uPA (which forms an irreversible 1:1 complex with surface/extracellular PAI-2) to S1b cells without loss of protection.
  3. Because IFN priming in HeLa cells is known to persist for ~18 days via stable ISGF3$\gamma$ pool maintenance, the authors astutely passaged S1b cells at low density in neutralizing anti-IFN-$\alpha$ + anti-IFN-$\beta$ antisera (vs. normal rabbit/sheep sera) for 18, 21, and 25 days to successfully reverse the antiviral state.
* **Absence of Formal Error Bars / Statistical Tests in Textual Reporting:** While survival assays were performed in duplicate, triplicate, or quadruplicate (e.g., supernatant transfers in quadruplicate; antibody neutralization in triplicate), the text reports percentage survival and densitometric trends without formal $p$-values or variance metrics in the prose, and several confirmatory controls (EM micrographs, mutant ISRE competition EMSAs, EMSA/immunoblot supershifts, NAC/PDTC antioxidant pretreatments) are cited as "(data not shown)".
* **Clonal Selection Caveat in HeLa Transfectants:** Although two independent sense clones (S1a, S1b) and four control lines were tested for viral CPE and time-course survival (Figs. 1–2), the downstream mechanistic assays (Figs. 3–7) focus primarily on comparing clone S1b against clone A2/7.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Intracellular (not extracellular) PAI-2 protects HeLa cells from alphavirus CPE via autocrine IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ priming of ISGF3:** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by concordant functional bioassays, concentrated supernatant transfer (44% and 100% protection at 15$\times$ and 30$\times$), 18–25 day neutralizing antibody reversal, constitutive nuclear ISGF3$\gamma$ and ISRE binding, and rapid 4 h *OAS* mRNA induction upon RRV infection.
* **Selective protection against IFN-sensitive viruses (RRV, Sindbis, Adenovirus) vs. IFN-evading viruses (Vaccinia, Influenza):** `[Well Supported]` — The virus panel results align with known viral antagonism of the IFN-$\alpha/\beta$ pathway in HeLa cells.
* **Induction of endogenous *PAI-2* in MonoMac6 macrophages by viral RNA / virions:** `[Well Supported]` — Northern blotting demonstrates robust *PAI-2* transcript induction within 4 h of exposure to infectious, antibody-neutralized, or UV-inactivated RRV as well as poly(I:C).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Detailed Experimental Protocols:** Oligonucleotide sequences for the ISRE EMSA probe and RT-PCR primers (*IFN-$\alpha$*, *IFN-$\beta$*, *$\beta$-actin*), antibody clones and concentrations, viral strains and stock titers, and bioassay standardization against recombinant IFN-$\alpha$2B (Intron A) are fully specified in the Materials and Methods.
