### 1. Executive Summary
This study characterizes the tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum* cv. Ailsa Craig) Elongator complex subunit 2-like gene (*SlELP2L*, `XM_004240442`) using RNA interference (RNAi). Selecting two independent T1 transgenic lines (RNAi-1 and RNAi-8) with 92–94% knockdown of *SlELP2L* transcripts, the authors report pleiotropic developmental phenotypes including reduced leaf rachis length, lower leaf gibberellin (GA) and indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) levels, accelerated leaf and fruit sepal senescence, darker-green immature/mature-green fruits with elevated pericarp chlorophyll, slightly reduced carotenoid accumulation at B+4, reduced expression of ethylene-biosynthetic (*ACS2*, *ACS4*, *ACO1*, *ACO3*), ripening-regulator (*RIN*, *NOR*, *E8*), and cell-wall-modifying (*PG*, *PE1*, *CEL2*, *XTH5*) genes, upregulated DNA methyltransferase transcripts (*SlDRM7*, *SlDRM8*, *SlMET1*), and increased seedling root sensitivity to exogenous $10\,\mu\text{M}$ abscisic acid (ABA). While the phenotypic characterization across two independent RNAi lines is consistent, the study relies on non-specific commercial sandwich ELISA kits for phytohormone quantification, lacks direct epigenetic (histone acetylation or bisulfite DNA methylation) or ethylene gas measurements, and omits a dedicated statistical analysis section specifying how multi-group/multi-timepoint comparisons were tested.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Unvalidated Commercial Sandwich ELISA for Small-Molecule Phytohormones (ABA, IAA, GA):** In Materials and Methods (line 145), endogenous ABA, IAA, and GA in crude $80\%$ methanol leaf extracts were quantified using a *"one-step double-antibody sandwich method enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA)"* measured at $450\,\text{nm}$ from a generic supplier (`wksubio.com`). Biochemically, small-molecule phytohormones ($\text{MW} \approx 175\text{--}346\,\text{Da}$) are haptens that are too small to simultaneously bind two antibodies in a "double-antibody sandwich" format (authentic hormone immunoassays require competitive RIA/ELISA after solid-phase extraction and methylation, or LC-MS/MS with deuterated internal standards). Using unpurified $80\%$ methanol plant extracts directly in commercial "sandwich" ELISA kits is notorious in plant physiology for yielding cross-reactive matrix artifacts rather than authentic hormone concentrations.
- **Spectrophotometric Chlorophyll Equation Mismatch:** On line 6 (part 2), pericarp pigments were extracted in **hexane:acetone (60:40, v/v)**, and total chlorophyll was calculated using the equation $\text{Total Chl (mg mL}^{-1}) = 8.02(\text{OD}_{643}) + 20.2(\text{OD}_{647})$. In standard Arnon/Lichtenthaler spectrophotometry, the coefficients $8.02$ and $20.2$ (in $\mu\text{g mL}^{-1}$, not $\text{mg mL}^{-1}$!) apply to $80\%$ aqueous acetone at $663\,\text{nm}$ and $645\,\text{nm}$, whereas the authors used a non-polar hexane:acetone (60:40) biphasic/mixed solvent and measured absorbance at $643\,\text{nm}$ and $647\,\text{nm}$ (two closely spaced wavelengths in the Chl *b* region, omitting the Chl *a* red peak at $\sim 663\,\text{nm}$).
- **Absence of Statistical Methods Specification:** The Methods section contains no description of the statistical tests used across Figures 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 (e.g., whether Student's $t$-test or one-way/two-way ANOVA with post-hoc multiple comparison correction was applied when comparing WT against RNAi-1 and RNAi-8 across 3–5 developmental stages).
- **Unstressed Control Design in Hormone Time-Course (Figure 2):** In the hormone treatment experiment (line 123, Figure 2), 3-week-old WT seedlings were sprayed with ABA, $\text{GA}_3$, or ACC, enclosed in plastic bags for 3, 6, 12, and 24 h, and compared only to a single `0 h` unstressed baseline rather than time-matched mock-sprayed (water/solvent + plastic enclosure) controls at 3, 6, 12, and 24 h. Because enclosing plants in plastic for 24 h alters humidity, $\text{CO}_2$, and circadian gene expression, comparing 24 h enclosed plants to 0 h unenclosed plants confounds circadian/enclosure effects with hormone response.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Silencing SlELP2L causes reduced leaf growth, accelerated leaf and sepal senescence, dark-green immature fruits, and seedling hypersensitivity to exogenous ABA.* `[Well Supported]` — Reproducibly demonstrated across two independent severe knockdown lines (RNAi-1 and RNAi-8, with 92–94% silencing and verified lack of off-target knockdown of *SlELP1L/3L/4L/6L*) in Figures 3, 4, 9 and Table 1.
- **Claim 2:** *SlELP2L-silenced fruits have down-regulated ethylene- and ripening-related genes, affecting fruit ripening.* `[Moderately Supported]` — While qRT-PCR shows 30–80% reductions in *ACS2/4*, *ACO1/3*, *RIN*, *NOR*, *E8*, and cell-wall genes (*PG*, *PE1*, *CEL2*, *XTH5*), Table 1 shows that actual ripening time (days from anthesis to breaker: $42 \pm 3.2$ in WT vs. $42 \pm 2.1$ and $41 \pm 4.2$ in RNAi lines) and visual red coloration at B+4/B+7 (Figure 4A) were virtually unchanged, and neither ethylene evolution rate nor fruit firmness was measured.
- **Claim 3:** *SlELP2L modulates DNA methylation and histone modification in tomato.* `[Overstated]` — The authors measured only mRNA transcript levels of four DNA methyltransferase genes (*SlDRM5/7/8*, *SlMET1*) by qRT-PCR (showing a ~1.5–2-fold increase in *SlDRM7/8*), without performing any bisulfite sequencing, 5-mC quantification, or histone H3 acetylation ChIP assays.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- Gene accession numbers (`XM_004240442` for *SlELP2L* and accessions for *SlELP1L/3L/4L/6L*) and cloning/T-DNA primers are fully specified in the text, with qRT-PCR primers referenced in Table S2.
- However, the commercial ELISA kit URL (`http://www.wksubio.com/product/5794959.html`) is non-archival and provides no antibody clone or cross-reactivity validation data, and exact statistical test details are omitted.
