### 1. Executive Summary
This is a comprehensive, rigorously designed molecular virology study that resolves conflicting reports regarding which host cyclophilin isoform (`CypA` vs. `CypB`) is essential for Hepatitis C Virus (HCV) replication and uncovers a striking mechanistic link between `CypA` dependency, resistance to the non-immunosuppressive cyclosporine analogue `DEBIO-025`, and proteolytic processing kinetics at the `NS5A–NS5B` cleavage site. By establishing stable lentiviral shRNA knock-downs targeting the 3' UTR of `CypA` or `CypB` across two permissive hepatoma subclones (`Huh7-Lunet` and `Huh7.5`), the authors demonstrate that `CypA` depletion reduces subgenomic JFH1 (genotype 2a) and Con1 (genotype 1b) replicon RNA replication by 50- to 1,000-fold while sparing Dengue virus 2 (`NGC`) replicons, whereas `CypB` knock-down has no effect. Crucially, HCV replication in `CypA` knock-down cells is fully rescued by ectopic expression of shRNA-resistant wild-type `CypA`, but not by the peptidyl-prolyl isomerase (`PPIase`)-defective mutant `CypA-H126Q`. Furthermore, full-length `Jc1` genomes and subgenomic replicons containing `NS2` (`sgNS2/JFH1-Luc`) exhibit even greater sensitivity to `CypA` depletion than minimal `NS3–5B` replicons, implicating `NS2` as an additional `CypA`-dependent factor. Finally, by selecting for `DEBIO-025` resistance in `sgNS3/JFH1-neo/RFP` replicon cells and performing `[35S]` pulse-chase immunoprecipitation and reverse genetics across engineered `P7–P1` cleavage-site variants (`V2440A`, `V2440L`, `2a-5AB`, `2b-5AB`, and double mutants), the authors prove that mutations delaying `NS5A–NS5B` cleavage (`V2440A`, `V2440L`) confer `DEBIO-025` resistance and reduce `CypA` dependence, whereas mutations accelerating cleavage (`2a-5AB`, `2b-5AB`) cause hyper-sensitivity to `DEBIO-025` and heightened `CypA` requirement.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Genetic Specificity, Off-Target Exclusion, and Viral Controls:**
  - Stable 3'-UTR shRNA knock-downs of `CypA` and `CypB` were validated by both immunoblotting and quantitative immunofluorescence across two independent cell backgrounds (`Huh7-Lunet` and `Huh7.5`), showing no cross-depletion between `CypA` and `CypB` (Fig. 1, Fig. 4B).
  - Off-target shRNA effects were definitively excluded by restoring replication and `DEBIO-025` resistance via stable transduction of shRNA-resistant wild-type `CypA`, controlled in parallel against empty vector and the active-site mutant `CypA-H126Q` (Figs. 4 and 5C).
  - Polymerase-defective active-site mutants (`Con1/GND` and `JFH1/DeltaGDD`) were included in every transient replication experiment to define the input-RNA decay baseline, and a flavivirus control (`Dengue virus 2 NGC` subgenomic replicon, Fig. S1) excluded non-specific cellular toxicity or general translation defects in `CypA`-depleted cells.
- **Kinetic Normalization & Exponential Doubling-Time Analysis:** Transient luciferase replicon assays were normalized to the 4-hour post-electroporation reading to control for transfection efficiency and analyzed across early exponential time points (`4, 24, 48, 72 h`), enabling accurate calculation of apparent RNA doubling times (`3.7 h` in control vs. `11.4 h` in `Huh7-Lunet sh-CypA`; `3.6 h` vs. `7.4 h` in `Huh7.5 sh-CypA`) before host-cell saturation plateaus occur at 72 h.
- **Bidirectional Perturbation of `NS5A–NS5B` Cleavage Kinetics (Figs. 8–9, Table 2):** A major strength of the study is that the authors did not stop at characterizing the drug-selected resistance substitution (`V2440A`) and assembly-enhancing substitution (`V2440L`), which both slow `NS5A–NS5B` processing in `[35S]` pulse-chase assays (Fig. 8); they also engineered consensus genotype `2a-5AB` and `2b-5AB` `P7–P3` cleavage sites that *accelerate* `NS5A–NS5B` cleavage and showed that these hyper-processing mutants exhibit the exact opposite phenotype (~10-fold increased `DEBIO-025` sensitivity and impaired replication in `CypA`-depleted cells), which is then epistatically reversed by introducing `V2440L` (`2a-5AB V2440L` and `2b-5AB V2440L`).
- **Balanced Mechanistic Interpretation:** The authors thoughtfully note in the Discussion (line 129) that because active-site `PPIase` mutations like `H126Q` simultaneously impair substrate binding to the hydrophobic pocket of `CypA`, genetic loss of rescue by `H126Q` demonstrates requirement for the intact catalytic/substrate-binding pocket but cannot strictly separate catalysis from chaperone binding.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`CypA`, and not `CypB`, is essential for HCV genotype 1b (`Con1`) and 2a (`JFH1`) RNA replication and infectious virus production, and is the primary cellular target of `DEBIO-025`.* `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated by shRNA knock-down, `wt` vs. `H126Q` rescue, and bidirectional shifts in `DEBIO-025` $IC_{50}$/$IC_{90}$ curves upon `CypA` depletion versus overexpression (Figs. 1–5).
- **Claim 2:** *Full-length `Jc1` and `sgNS2` replicons are more sensitive to `CypA` depletion than `sgNS3–5B` replicons, implicating `NS2` as an additional viral target of `CypA`.* `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by side-by-side comparison of `sgNS2/JFH1-Luc` and `sgNS3/JFH1-Luc` in `CypA` knock-down cells (Fig. 3C).
- **Claim 3:** *Processing kinetics at the `NS5A–NS5B` cleavage site inversely correlate with `CypA` dependence and `DEBIO-025` sensitivity.* `[Well Supported]` — Established via `[35S]` metabolic pulse-chase immunoprecipitation and dose-response/replication kinetics across 6 engineered single and combinatorial `NS5A–NS5B` cleavage-site mutants (Figs. 6–9, Table 2).
