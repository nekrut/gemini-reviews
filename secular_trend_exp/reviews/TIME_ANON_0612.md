### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This experimental study investigates the functional and physical roles of human Argonaute isoforms (AGO1–AGO4) in duplex antigene RNA (agRNA)-mediated transcriptional gene silencing (`PR-9` duplex targeting positions `-9` to `+10` in high-PR-expressing T47D breast cancer cells) and transcriptional gene activation (`PR-11` duplex targeting positions `-11` to `+8` in low-PR-expressing MCF7 breast cancer cells) of the progesterone receptor (*PR*) promoter. By combining isoform-specific siRNA knockdowns, biochemical subcellular fractionation, native RNA immunoprecipitation (RIP) using recently characterized monoclonal antibodies (`4B8`, `4G8`, `11A9`, `5A3`, `6C10`) as well as Flag/HA-tagged AGO1–4 vectors, RNA polymerase II ChIP, and 5'-RACE cleavage assays, the authors demonstrate that AGO2 knockdown reverses both `PR-9` transcriptional silencing and `PR-11` transcriptional activation. Furthermore, native RIP experiments indicate that both `PR-9` and `PR-11` recruit AGO2 (and to a lesser extent AGO1 during silencing, but not AGO3 or AGO4) onto the noncoding antisense transcript `AT2` spanning the *PR* promoter without inducing endonucleolytic cleavage of `AT2`. The manuscript is mechanistically thorough and appropriately cross-validates endogenous antibody RIP against epitope-tagged AGO1–4 expression constructs, though several biochemical and statistical caveats temper the quantitative strength of the conclusions.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
- **Baseline Confounding in Two-Step Knockdown-Activation Assays (Figure 3B,D vs Figure 5B):** In MCF7 cells, depleting endogenous AGO2 (`siAGO2`) or Dicer (`siDicer`) alone without adding any promoter-targeted agRNA causes a 2- to 3-fold baseline increase in *PR* mRNA and protein expression (Figure 3B,D), likely by relieving constitutive microRNA repression of the *PR* 3'-UTR. In the subsequent two-transfection reversal assay (Figure 5B), the authors normalize the `siAGO2 + PR-11` group (`~1.5-fold`) relative to the `siAGO2 + MM` group (`1.0`). Because `siAGO2` already elevates basal *PR* transcription/translation 2–3 fold, it is ambiguous whether the blunted fold-induction upon adding `PR-11` reflects true loss of AGO2-dependent promoter activation or a ceiling/saturation effect relative to the elevated baseline. Reporting unnormalized absolute RT-qPCR expression levels (relative to `GAPDH` across all conditions simultaneously) alongside the pairwise fold-change ratios would resolve this ambiguity.
- **Non-Crosslinked Native Nuclear Lysis and Post-Lysis Reassortment Risk in RIP:** Nuclear RIP was performed without formaldehyde or UV crosslinking; isolated nuclei were suspended in `0.15 M KCl, 20 mM Hepes, 0.5% NP-40` and ruptured via three freeze-thaw cycles followed by sonication. Because synthetic duplex agRNAs are transfected at `25 nM` (`50 nM` in 5'-RACE), free nuclear pools of AGO2-loaded guide strands could associate with the `AT2` antisense transcript in vitro after nuclear lysis. While the mismatch duplex control (`MM`) and the differential behaviour of AGO1 vs AGO2 provide reassuring specificity controls, native RIP is inherently more vulnerable to post-lysis re-association than UV-CLIP or formaldehyde-crosslinked RIP.
- **Semi-Quantitative End-Point PCR for RIP Detection:** While RT-qPCR was used to quantify *PR*, *AGO1–4*, and *AT2* transcript abundance in knockdown experiments, all RIP enrichments (Figures 6, 7, S6, S7) were evaluated purely by 36-cycle end-point PCR followed by agarose gel electrophoresis and UV ethidium bromide imaging, without quantitative real-time PCR (`% input` or fold-enrichment over IgG across biological replicates).
- **Statistical Testing:** All bar graphs report `mean ± SD` of triplicates analyzed via unadjusted two-tailed Student's *t*-tests (`*P < 0.05`, `**P < 0.01`, `***P < 0.005`), without ANOVA or multiple-comparison corrections across multi-group isoform panels (e.g., testing `siAGO1`, `siAGO2`, `siAGO3`, `siAGO4`, and `siMM` simultaneously).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: AGO2 expression is required for both agRNA-mediated transcriptional silencing (`PR-9`) and activation (`PR-11`) of the progesterone receptor gene** — `[Well Supported]`. Reversal of both silencing in T47D cells and activation in MCF7 cells upon `siAGO2` knockdown is demonstrated at both the protein (Western blot) and mRNA (RT-qPCR) levels, and supported by matching RNAP2 promoter occupancy shifts by ChIP (Figures 4C and 5C).
- **Claim 2: Promoter-targeted agRNAs guide AGO2 to the overlapping antisense noncoding transcript (`AT2`) without cleaving it** — `[Moderately Supported]`. Native RIP with both endogenous monoclonal antibodies and Flag/HA-tagged AGO constructs detects `AT2` enrichment only in the presence of complementary `PR-9` or `PR-11` duplexes, and 5'-RACE detects no cleavage products across the target region (in contrast to a robust *Luciferase* positive control cleavage product in Figure S8). However, the absence of a positive cleavage control targeting the nuclear `AT2` transcript itself (for example, a phosphorothioate antisense oligonucleotide recruiting RNase H) leaves open the minor possibility that cleaved nuclear `AT2` fragments are degraded too rapidly for 5'-RACE capture.
- **Claim 3: Functional dominance of AGO2 over AGO1, AGO3, and AGO4 is not merely a reflection of relative cellular isoform expression levels** — `[Moderately Supported]`. While the authors show that *AGO2* is the most abundant mRNA in T47D cells (`~45%` of total *AGO* transcript pool) and second-most abundant in MCF7 cells (`~30%`, behind *AGO3*), comparison across four different *AGO1–4* TaqMan/qPCR primer sets without plasmid standard curves assumes identical amplification efficiencies (`2^{-\Delta Ct}`) across distinct loci, and mRNA levels do not directly measure nuclear protein concentrations. Nonetheless, the Flag/HA-tagged overexpression RIP assays (where all four isoforms are driven by the same vector backbone) corroborate preferential association of AGO2 (and AGO1 in silencing) with `AT2`.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Sequence & Reagent Transparency:** Complete sense and antisense RNA sequences for all duplex siRNAs (`siAGO1–4`, `siDicer`, `MM`), agRNAs (`PR-9`, `PR-11`), RT-qPCR primers, RIP primers, ChIP primers, and 5'-RACE adapter/gene-specific primers are explicitly tabulated in Supplementary Table S1, along with antibody clones, dilutions, and commercial catalog numbers.
- **Quantitative Data Availability:** Raw cycle threshold ($C_t$) tables and densitometric gel quantifications are not deposited in an external repository, and RIP outputs are presented qualitatively as gel images.
