### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This manuscript reports the molecular cloning, complete cDNA and deduced amino acid sequence (3,195 bp; 918 residues; 103 kDa; EMBL `Z38132`), biochemical domain mapping, and structural fold assignment of scruin, the 102-kDa equimolar actin-cross-linking protein from the crystalline acrosomal process bundle of *Limulus polyphemus* sperm, alongside the cloning of three *Limulus* testicular actin isoforms (`ACT3`, `ACT5`, `ACT11`) and identification of `ACT5` as the authentic acrosomal bundle actin. Sequence analysis reveals that scruin has no homology to canonical actin-binding proteins ($\alpha$-actinin, fimbrin, gelsolin) but instead consists of a tandem duplication of two homologous halves (32% identity over 390 residues) separated by a central linker. Each half comprises six tandem ~50-residue repeats (12 repeats in total: repeats 1–6 in residues 73–381 and repeats 7–12 in residues 581–889) homologous to *Drosophila* kelch, poxvirus ORFs, mouse MIPP, and *Dactylium* galactose oxidase. Pairwise comparisons show that corresponding repeats between the N- and C-terminal halves (e.g., repeat 2 vs. 8, 42% identity) are much more conserved than repeats within the same half (11–23%), demonstrating evolution via duplication of a six-repeat unit. Drawing on crystal structures of galactose oxidase and sialidases, the authors map each six-repeat domain to a four-stranded antiparallel $\beta$-sheet "superbarrel" ($\beta$-propeller) fold, directly explaining the bilobed globular structure of scruin observed in 13-Å cryo-EM helical reconstructions. Limited proteolysis of native acrosomal bundles with four proteases (trypsin, chymotrypsin, V-8, Endo-Asp) and Edman sequencing confirm that the two $\beta$-propeller domains are compact and protease-resistant (47 kDa N-terminal and 56 kDa C-terminal fragments), connected by a 5-kDa protease-sensitive neck (residues 420–462).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Seamless Integration of Biochemistry, Sequence Evolution, and Cryo-EM Structural Biology:** The study directly bridges 13-Å cryo-EM reconstructions of the *Limulus* acrosomal bundle with primary sequence and proteolytic domain mapping. Showing that corresponding repeats across the two halves ($i$ vs. $i+6$) share 23–42% identity whereas adjacent repeats within a half share 11–23% identity provides compelling quantitative evidence that scruin evolved by tandem duplication of an ancestral 6-bladed $\beta$-propeller rather than independent expansion of single 50-aa motifs.
- **Rigorous Protein Chemistry & Isoform Identification:** Native scruin and actin were separated by size-exclusion chromatography in 6 M guanidine HCl after 4-vinylpyridine cysteine modification, cleaved with CNBr or *Achromobacter* protease I, sequenced by gas-phase Edman degradation, and analyzed by MALDI-MS with internal insulin calibration. Using six diagnostic internal peptides, the authors definitively identify `ACT5` (out of three cloned testicular actin transcripts) as the specific isoform in the acrosomal bundle, verify 92.1% identity with rabbit skeletal actin (with only 1 substitution in each helix-loop-$\beta$ motif, validating prior cryo-EM docking models), and detect putative methylation of His-74 by mass spectrometry.
- **Biochemical Domain Mapping by Multi-Protease Footprinting:** Limited proteolysis across four proteases with distinct cleavage specificities in both $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ and EGTA, paired with Edman sequencing of every C-terminal fragment (Fig. 5), precisely localizes the inter-domain hinge to residues 420–462 (`KYKK...DSSG`) and shows slower cleavage kinetics in the presence of $\text{Ca}^{2+}$.
- **Transparent Reporting of Negative/Artifactual Recombinant Results:** The authors candidly report that full-length and N-terminal scruin constructs were unstable in *E. coli* when the 5' UTR was removed, and that refolded C-terminal constructs (`454C` and `590C`) self-assembled into worm-like filaments (Fig. 7) that sedimented in the absence of actin, precluding in vitro F-actin cosedimentation assays.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Ambiguity Between Native Self-Association and Refolding Aggregation of `454C`/`590C`:** The recombinant C-terminal constructs (`454C` and `590C`) were purified from *E. coli* inclusion bodies in 8 M / 6 M urea and dialyzed into low-salt buffer, whereupon they formed detergent-insoluble worm-like filaments and amorphous aggregates in high salt or $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ (Fig. 7). While the Discussion suggests this self-assembly may reflect native inter-filament scruin-scruin cross-linking interactions, no biophysical folding controls (such as circular dichroism spectroscopy to verify native $\beta$-sheet content or proteolytic resistance comparison against native 56-kDa C-terminal domain) were performed to exclude non-native $\beta$-strand intermolecular aggregation after urea denaturation.
- **Lack of Direct Experimental Proof that the Kelch $\beta$-Propeller Motif Suffices for Actin Binding:** Because recombinant domain binding assays were thwarted by self-pelleting and nicked native scruin remained bound within the intact bundle (sedimentation data mentioned as "data not shown"), the paper does not directly demonstrate isolated single-domain actin binding or test whether other kelch-family proteins (e.g., *Drosophila* kelch or poxvirus ORFs) bind F-actin.
- **Minor Accession Typo and Unprinted Actin Sequences:** The reported EMBL accession numbers for the three *Limulus* actin clones (`ACT3`, `ACT5`, `ACT11`) are listed on p. 56 as `Z38130`, `Z39131`, and `Z38129`—where `Z39131` appears to be a typographical error for `Z38131` in the consecutive block `Z38129–Z38132`. Additionally, the amino acid sequences and peptide alignments for `ACT3`, `ACT5`, and `ACT11` are not shown in any figure.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Scruin consists of two homologous 6-repeat kelch domains (predicted 6-bladed $\beta$-propeller superbarrels) connected by a 5-kDa protease-sensitive linker, corresponding to the two globular domains seen in 13-Å EM reconstructions. `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by cDNA sequence, internal repeat homology matrix, limited proteolysis with four enzymes, and Edman cleavage-site mapping.
- **Claim 2:** `ACT5` is the authentic actin isoform of the *Limulus* acrosomal process and preserves the surface helix-loop-$\beta$ motifs modeled from vertebrate skeletal actin. `[Well Supported]` — Proven by 6 isoform-specific internal peptides, MALDI-MS, and sequence conservation (92.1% overall identity; 1 conservative substitution per helix-loop-$\beta$ motif).
- **Claim 3:** Other kelch-repeat / $\beta$-propeller family members (*Drosophila* kelch in ring canals, poxvirus ORFs during microvillar induction) may also function as actin-binding proteins. `[Moderately Supported]` — Appropriately presented as a hypothesis ("present the possibility" / "tempting to speculate") that motivates future functional testing.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Sequence & Accession Completeness:** High. Full nucleotide (3,195 bp) and amino acid (918 aa) sequences of scruin are printed in Fig. 2 and deposited in EMBL (`Z38132`), along with the three *Limulus* actin isoforms (`Z38129`, `Z38130`, `Z38131` [printed as `Z39131`]).
- **Experimental Protocols:** Degenerate PCR primer sequences (`S4`, `S2`), thermal cycling profiles, protease ratios (wt/wt), buffer compositions, and inclusion-body purification steps are fully documented.
