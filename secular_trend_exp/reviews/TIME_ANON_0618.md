### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This multi-author conference report summarizes selected late-breaking and oral presentations from a Conference on Retroviruses and Opportunistic Infections (CROI) meeting across six thematic sections: (1) antiretroviral treatment interruption (TI) and biomarkers of post-treatment viraemic control (`ACTG`, `SPARTAC`, `VISCONTI` NK cells, dendritic cell vaccination); (2) latency reversal and viral biogenesis modulators (`ABX464` Rev-splicing modulator and an oral `TLR7` agonist in SIV-infected rhesus macaques); (3) antigen- and LRA-driven clonal expansion of HIV-1-infected CD4+ T cells ("shock without kill"); (4) defective proviral transcription and early ART initiation in primary infection (`PRIMO` cohort); (5) infrared matrix-assisted laser desorption electrospray ionisation (`IR-MALDESI`) mass spectrometry imaging of efavirenz tissue biodistribution; and (6) Phase II/III clinical trial results for tenofovir alafenamide (`TAF`), the maturation inhibitor `BMS-955176`, and the gp120 attachment inhibitor `BMS-663068` (fostemsavir). While the biological synthesis of HIV reservoir dynamics and defective proviral transcription is well-informed and balanced, the manuscript is a secondary conference commentary rather than primary peer-reviewed research and contains several glaring numerical, unit, and heading errors.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS, WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
- **Arithmetic Contradiction in Total Randomised Sample Size for the Pooled Phase III TAF Trials (`Abstract 113LB`, line 124):**
  - On line 124, the text states: *"In the studies, **1073** antiretroviral-naïve adults were randomised to start either a TAF- (**n = 866**) or TDF-based (**n = 867**) combination treatment in two identical international Phase III trials..."*
  - Summing the two randomized arms yields $866 + 867 = 1,733$ participants, not `1,073` (a `660`-participant typographical error in the reported total sample size).
- **Inconsistent and Biologically Implausible Concentration Ratios in the `IR-MALDESI` Efavirenz Biodistribution Section (line 111):**
  - On line 111, the summary of Abstract 25 states that efavirenz concentrations in brain tissue were *"lowest in the basal ganglia (167 pg/g tissue). This concentration increased by approximately two-fold in most other tissues (cerebrum, lymph nodes, spleen, testes and most GALT) with highest concentrations in the rectal tissue (3.6-fold)."* Immediately afterward, it claims: *"In particular, efavirenz concentration in CNS tissue was **6 log 10 higher** and for the remaining tissues and **2 log 10 higher** than plasma."*
  - A tissue concentration of `167–334 pg/g` (`0.167–0.334 ng/g`) is roughly four orders of magnitude *lower* than steady-state therapeutic plasma efavirenz concentrations (`~1,000–4,000 ng/mL`), and it is mathematically impossible for CNS tissue (`167–334 pg/g`) to be `6 log10` (`1,000,000-fold`) higher than plasma while rectal tissue (`3.6-fold` higher than basal ganglia, i.e., `~600 pg/g`) is only `2 log10` (`100-fold`) higher than the same plasma sample.
- **Duplicated Drug Code in Section Headings (`BMS-955176` vs `BMS-663068`, lines 135 and 140):**
  - Line 135 is titled `## Maturation inhibitor BMS-955176`, and line 140 is erroneously titled `## Attachment inhibitor BMS-955176`, mislabeling the attachment inhibitor `BMS-663068` with the maturation inhibitor's compound identifier.
- **Secondary Nature of Conference Abstracts:** Because the article compiles summaries of conference oral/poster presentations (`Abstracts 47, 52, 104LB, 108, 110LB, 111LB, 113LB, 114LB, 143LB, 354`), primary experimental protocols, inclusion/exclusion criteria, statistical adjustments, and raw data tables are not available for independent verification.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Cell-associated HIV RNA, residual plasma viraemia, and T-cell exhaustion markers (`PD-1`, `Lag-3`, `Tim-3`) predict time to viral rebound after treatment interruption, whereas defective proviruses and clonal expansion confound reservoir measurements** — `[Well Supported]`. The synthesis accurately captures the distinction between total integrated HIV DNA, transcriptionally active defective proviruses, and intact replication-competent proviruses expanded by cognate tumour/vaccine antigens or latency-reversing agents.
- **Claim 2: TAF is virologically non-inferior to TDF (`92%` vs `90%` VL $\le 50$ copies/mL at 48 weeks) with statistically significant but clinically modest reductions in eGFR decline and bone mineral density loss** — `[Well Supported]`. The commentary provides a commendably critical appraisal of the FDA snapshot non-inferiority margin (`2.0%, 95% CI: -0.7% to 4.7%`) and explicitly questions whether the small clinical difference in serum creatinine (`0.08` vs `0.11 mg/dL`) justifies the higher cost of TAF over soon-to-be-generic TDF.
- **Claim 3: `IR-MALDESI` mass spectrometry imaging shows CNS efavirenz concentrations are `6 log10` higher than plasma** — `[Methodologically Flawed]`. Internally inconsistent with the reported `167 pg/g` basal ganglia concentration and `2- to 3.6-fold` non-CNS tissue ratios in the preceding sentences.
