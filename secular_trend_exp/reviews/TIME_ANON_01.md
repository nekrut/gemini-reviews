### 1. Executive Summary
This manuscript reports the design and analytical validation of real-time quantitative TaqMan RT-PCR assays for measuring feline glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase (GAPDH) and six feline cytokines (IL-4, IL-10, IL-12 p35, IL-12 p40, IFN$\gamma$, and IL-16) on an ABI Prism 7700 system. The authors cloned and sequenced feline GAPDH (`AF097177`), designed exon-exon junction-spanning TaqMan probes to prevent genomic DNA (gDNA) co-amplification, validated linear dynamic range over eight orders of magnitude down to 10 plasmid copies, verified equal amplification efficiencies between target cytokines and GAPDH to satisfy the assumptions of the comparative $\Delta\Delta C_T$ method, and compared random hexamers vs. oligo(dT) priming as well as two-tube vs. single-tube RT-PCR formats. Finally, they applied the assay to fresh vs. 5-year frozen feline peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs), mitogen-stimulated PBMCs (ConA and *Staphylococcus aureus* Pansorbin), and three feline cell lines (CrFK, NLFK, and 3201).

The experimental validation of the assay's core molecular mechanics—specifically the empirical test of species-specific vs. heterologous GAPDH primers, the 100 ng gDNA cross-reactivity test including a single-exon positive control (`IFNγ No. 1`), and the dilution-slope verification of amplification efficiency—is thorough and well-executed. However, several mathematical and methodological issues require correction: (1) an inverted sign definition in the $\Delta\Delta C_T$ equations in the Methods section, (2) a numerical discrepancy between the Results and Discussion regarding GAPDH mRNA loss in frozen cells along with a broken reference to a non-existent "Tab. 3", (3) a confounded comparison between the one-tube and two-tube RT-PCR systems where enzymes, cycling parameters, and primer concentrations were changed simultaneously, and (4) the unvalidated assumption that GAPDH transcription per cell remains invariant during strong mitogenic stimulation or across distinct cell lineages.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit

* **Verification of the $\Delta\Delta C_T$ Equal-Efficiency Assumption:** The comparative $C_T$ method requires the amplification efficiencies of the target cytokine and reference housekeeping gene (GAPDH) to be approximately equal so that $\Delta C_T$ is invariant to template dilution. The authors explicitly tested this across 6 to 7 serial cDNA dilutions run in triplicate, demonstrating that the absolute slope of $\Delta C_T$ versus log input RNA was $\le 0.10$ for all six cytokines (IL-4: $0.09$; IL-10: $0.10$; IL-12 p35: $0.06$; IL-12 p40: $0.06$; IFN$\gamma$ No. 2: $0.05$; IL-16: $0.08$). This is a rigorous validation of the $\Delta\Delta C_T$ prerequisite.
* **Algebraic Sign Error in the $\Delta\Delta C_T$ Formula (Section "The comparative $C_T$ method"):** The text defines $\Delta C_T = C_{T,\text{target}} - C_{T,\text{GAPDH}}$ and then states: *"The $\Delta C_T$ for each experimental sample was subtracted from the $\Delta C_T$ of the calibrator. This difference is called the $\Delta\Delta C_T$ value. Finally, the amount of target, normalized to an internal control and relative to the calibrator, was calculated by $2^{-\Delta\Delta C_T}$."* If $\Delta\Delta C_T = \Delta C_{T,\text{calibrator}} - \Delta C_{T,\text{sample}}$, then an upregulated transcript (which has a lower $C_{T,\text{target}}$ and thus a smaller $\Delta C_{T,\text{sample}}$) yields a positive $\Delta\Delta C_T$, making $2^{-\Delta\Delta C_T} < 1$. Standard Livak/PE Biosystems Bulletin #2 notation defines $\Delta\Delta C_T = \Delta C_{T,\text{sample}} - \Delta C_{T,\text{calibrator}}$ so that fold-change is $2^{-\Delta\Delta C_T}$. Additionally, the subsequent sentence states that *"2^{\Delta C_T} would give a predicted copy number difference between GAPDH and a given cytokine mRNA,"* which should specify $2^{-\Delta C_T}$ for the ratio of cytokine to GAPDH copies (since GAPDH is more abundant and has a lower $C_T$).
* **Numerical Discrepancy Between Results and Discussion & Missing Table 3:** In the Results section ("Quantitation of GAPDH transcription in frozen cells..."), the authors report GAPDH $C_T$ values of $18.03 \pm 0.76$ for freshly isolated PBMCs versus $20.01 \pm 1.14$ for frozen cells ($n = 7$, citing a non-existent "Tab. 3"), and state this indicates a 3.3-fold decrease. However, $20.01 - 18.03 = 1.98$ cycles, which corresponds to $2^{1.98} = 3.94$-fold (assuming 100% efficiency), whereas in the Discussion the authors state that fresh and frozen cells *"showed a difference of 1.71 $C_T$ values (3.3-fold difference)"* ($2^{1.71} = 3.27$). The authors must reconcile whether the true mean difference was $1.71$ or $1.98$ cycles and provide the missing Table 3. Additionally, the $n=7$ comparison between fresh and 5-year stored PBMCs appears to be an unpaired comparison of different blood samples rather than longitudinal paired aliquots from the same donors, so biological inter-individual variance is confounded with storage degradation.
* **Confounded One-Tube vs. Two-Tube RT-PCR Comparison:** The manuscript attributes superior sensitivity to separating RT and PCR into two tubes compared to a single-tube RT-PCR system (Fig. 5). However, the one-tube protocol simultaneously changed the DNA polymerase (*Thermus flavus* Tfl polymerase vs. AmpliTaq Gold), buffer composition (2 mM $\text{MgSO}_4$ vs. 5 mM $\text{MgCl}_2$), RT temperature ($48^\circ\text{C}$ vs. $42^\circ\text{C}$), cycling profile (3-step with $64^\circ\text{C}/68^\circ\text{C}$ vs. 2-step at $60^\circ\text{C}$), and primer concentration (reported as "1.2 mM"—almost certainly a typographical error for $1.2\ \mu\text{M}$—versus $400\text{ nM}$). Furthermore, the Methods section for the one-tube RT-PCR system erroneously states that the reaction contained *"10 $\mu$l of cDNA"* when the first sentence of that paragraph states that cellular total RNA was directly subjected to RT and PCR. Because reagent chemistry and thermal profiles were not held constant, the sensitivity difference cannot be attributed solely to the one-tube vs. two-tube format.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality

* **Claim 1 — Exon-exon junction probes selectively amplify feline cytokine cDNA without gDNA cross-reactivity: `[Well Supported]`.**  
  By testing 100 ng of purified feline gDNA alongside cDNA and no-RT RNA controls, the authors demonstrated complete absence of gDNA amplification for all intron-spanning assays (GAPDH, IL-4, IL-10, IL-12 p35, IL-12 p40, IFN$\gamma$ No. 2, and IL-16), while their deliberate intra-exonic control system (`IFNγ No. 1`) amplified both gDNA and cDNA (Fig. 2).
* **Claim 2 — Species-specific primers and probes are essential because heterologous mammalian GAPDH assays suffer severe efficiency losses on feline templates: `[Well Supported]`.**  
  Testing feline PBMC cDNA with cat ($C_T = 17.01$), rat ($C_T = 21.45$, 22-fold loss), equine ($C_T = 24.89$, 235-fold loss), and human ($C_T = 29.03$, 4,153-fold loss) GAPDH TaqMan systems quantitatively demonstrates the penalty of primer/probe sequence mismatches.
* **Claim 3 — Analytical sensitivity and linear dynamic range of cytokine assays match those of the GAPDH plasmid standard: `[Moderately Supported]`.**  
  The 8-log linear range ($10$ to $10^8$ copies) and 10-molecule limit of detection were directly established only for the cloned GAPDH plasmid in carrier DNA (Fig. 3). While the cDNA dilution curves (Fig. 4) confirm matching amplification efficiencies within the detectable range, absolute limit-of-detection standard curves were not constructed for the six individual cytokine plasmids.
* **Claim 4 — CrFK feline kidney cells constitutively express 10,000-fold more IFN$\gamma$ than unstimulated PBMCs, and ConA/Pansorbin induce 50- to 130-fold IFN$\gamma$ upregulation: `[Moderately Supported]`.**  
  All fold-change claims rely on normalizing to a single housekeeping gene (GAPDH) via $2^{-\Delta\Delta C_T}$. In mitogenic T-cell activation (72 h ConA) and when comparing immortalized epithelial kidney lines (CrFK, NLFK) to primary resting lymphocytes, glycolytic flux and cellular GAPDH mRNA abundance per cell can vary substantially, which may inflate or compress relative $\Delta C_T$ ratios.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
Reproducibility is high: Table 1 provides complete $5'\to 3'$ sequences and amplicon lengths for all 20 primers and 10 TaqMan probes, GenBank/EMBL accession numbers are supplied for all target sequences including the newly sequenced feline GAPDH (`AF097177`), and reagent concentrations and cycling parameters are fully specified. To make the manuscript completely self-consistent, the authors should: (1) correct the typographical errors in the Methods ("10 $\mu$l of cDNA" $\to$ total RNA and "1.2 mM" $\to$ $1.2\ \mu\text{M}$ in the one-tube RT-PCR section, and the sign of $\Delta\Delta C_T$), (2) reconcile the $1.71$ vs. $1.98$ $\Delta C_T$ values for frozen cells, and (3) include the missing Table 3 (or remove the broken citation if those data were merged into the text) and report exact biological replicate numbers ($n$) and error bar values in the text for Figures 1, 2, 5, and 6.
