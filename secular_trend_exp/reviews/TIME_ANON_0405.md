### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates an unusual evolutionary and functional duplication of the essential peptidoglycan biosynthesis gene *murC* (encoding UDP-$N$-acetylmuramate:L-alanine ligase) across nitrogen-fixing root-nodule-inducing actinobacteria of the genus *Frankia*. By analyzing published genomes and newly assembled metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) spanning the four *Frankia* phylogenetic clusters (clusters 1–4), the authors show that whereas cluster-1 (7 genomes) and non-symbiotic cluster-4 (4 genomes) strains harbor only a single *murC* gene (*murC2*), all examined cluster-2 (12 genomes) and cluster-3 (6 genomes) strains carry two distinct paralogs in conserved syntenic loci: *murC1* (which clusters phylogenetically with canonical Actinobacteria *murC*) and *murC2* (which forms a well-supported clade sister to land plant plastid-targeted MurC proteins). Structural homology modeling (`SWISS-MODEL`, `Dali` against *Haemophilus influenzae* `1P31`, and `ConSurf`) confirms that both MurC1 and MurC2 retain all three catalytic domains and essential active-site residues, while RT-qPCR of root nodules (and pure culture of cluster-3 strain BCU110501) demonstrates that *murC2* is expressed at significantly higher levels than *murC1* in cluster-2 *Frankia* symbioses (*Datisca glomerata*, *Coriaria myrtifolia*, *Coriaria papuana*). The combination of comparative synteny, structural modeling, and Difficult-to-Culture *Frankia* nodule RT-qPCR is valuable, though the evolutionary analysis relies on heuristic consensus-sequence similarity matrices rather than formal phylogenetic hypothesis testing, and RT-qPCR sample sizes and exact test statistics are under-reported in the main text.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Consensus-Based Similarity Matrix vs. Evolutionary Distance Models (Section 2.4 & Figure 2):** To compare sequence divergence across taxonomic groups (Actinobacteria, Firmicutes, Cyanobacteria, Proteobacteria, Chlamydia, Plantae, *Frankia* MurC1, and *Frankia* MurC2), the authors generated a single 75%-threshold consensus amino acid sequence for each clade in `Geneious` and computed pairwise sequence similarities (`Blosum62` with threshold 1) among the group consensus sequences (Fig. 2). Collapsing diverse phyla into thresholded consensus strings containing degenerate/ambiguous positions and computing pairwise similarity scores is a non-standard heuristic that discards within-clade branch lengths and substitution rate heterogeneity; reporting mean patristic distances from the Maximum Likelihood (`RAxML` `PROTGAMMALG`) tree and conducting formal topological constraint tests (e.g., Approximately Unbiased [AU] tests) would be substantially more rigorous.
- **Interpretation of *murC2* Evolutionary Origin (HGT vs. Duplication + Convergence):** The ML phylogeny (Fig. 1) places *Frankia* MurC2 + Plantae MurC inside a lineage nested near Chlamydia and Verrucomicrobia, completely separated from Actinobacteria MurC (which contains *Frankia* MurC1). In the Discussion, the authors argue against horizontal gene transfer (HGT) of *murC2* because `IslandViewer 4` found no anomalous GC content or mobility genes, and instead favor an ancient gene duplication in the *Frankia* ancestor followed by either differential loss or "convergent evolution" with land plants. However, sequence composition ameloriates over hundreds of millions of years (renderings tools like `IslandViewer 4` insensitive to ancient HGT), and convergent sequence evolution across hundreds of amino acids in an intracellular cytoplasmic/stromal enzyme to produce 63% sequence similarity between *Frankia* MurC2 and plant MurC is evolutionarily implausible compared to an ancient inter-phylum HGT event (e.g., from a chlamydial/bacterial donor into early streptophytes and/or ancestral *Frankia*).
- **Incomplete Statistical Reporting for Root Nodule RT-qPCR (Section 3.5 & Figure 6):** In Section 2.6, the authors state that statistical significance between *murC1* and *murC2* expression (`$\Delta C_T$` normalized to initiation factor IF-3 gene *infC*) was determined by two-sample $t$-tests in `R`. However, neither Section 3.5 nor the Figure 6 caption states the number of biological replicates ($n$ independent root nodules or plants per strain), degrees of freedom, $t$-statistics, or exact $p$-values beyond asterisk thresholds (`* p < 0.05`, `** p < 0.01`, `*** p < 0.001`).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Frankia* cluster-2 and cluster-3 genomes maintain two distinct *murC* loci (*murC1* and *murC2*) in conserved syntenic neighborhoods, whereas cluster-1 and cluster-4 genomes retain only *murC2*. `[Well Supported]` — Thoroughly documented across 29 *Frankia* genomes/MAGs via synteny mapping (Fig. 4) and ML phylogenetics (Fig. 1, Fig. S1).
- **Claim 2:** Both MurC1 and MurC2 encode structurally intact, catalytically competent UDP-$N$-acetylmuramate:L-alanine ligases, and *murC2* is the predominantly transcribed copy in cluster-2 root nodules. `[Well Supported]` — Grounded in 3D homology modeling (`Z-score ~ 56` against PDB `1P31`, conserving all substrate-binding residues) and direct RT-qPCR across five cluster-2 inocula (Dg1, Dg2, Cm1, Cppng1, BMG5.1) and cluster-3 strain BCU110501.
- **Claim 3:** The close phylogenetic clustering of *Frankia* MurC2 with plant MurC likely reflects ancestral duplication in *Frankia* or convergent evolution rather than ancient horizontal gene transfer. `[Overstated]` — Relying on the absence of recent genomic-island signatures (`IslandViewer 4`) to rule out ancient HGT overstates the time-horizon of compositional HGT detectors and proposes an unparsimonious sequence-level convergence scenario.
