### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates the oncogenic role of the nucleosome assembly protein NAP1L1 in pancreatic neuroendocrine neoplasms (pNENs). Using the human pNEN cell line BON in vitro, an orthotopic pancreatic tail xenograft model in mice ($n = 4$ control vs. $n = 5$ shRNA knockdown), and a clinical cohort of 10 normal pancreas and 43 pNEN specimens (38 primaries, 5 metastases), the authors report that: (1) siRNA/shRNA knockdown of *NAP1L1* reduces BON cell proliferation (~40-fold drop in BrdU incorporation), inhibits mTOR/p-mTOR, S6K1, 4E-BP1, and ribosomal *RPS24*/*RPL28* expression, upregulates $p57^{Kip2}$ (*CDKN1C*) mRNA and protein, and decreases orthotopic tumor volume/weight and liver metastasis; (2) NAP1L1 binds directly to the $p57^{Kip2}$ promoter ($-164$ to $+21$) by ChIP-qPCR in proliferating BON cells and maintains $p57^{Kip2}$ promoter methylation; and (3) *NAP1L1* is overexpressed and inversely correlated with $p57^{Kip2}$ in human pNEN primaries and metastases. While the multi-tier integration of in vitro, orthotopic in vivo, and clinical tissue analyses is a strength, the central epigenetic mechanism (methylation-specific PCR primer design) is technically flawed, the orthotopic tumor volume vs. weight numbers are physically incompatible by nearly two orders of magnitude, and all functional perturbations rely on a single cell line (BON) and a single siRNA sequence.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Physically Impossible Tumor Volume vs. Wet Weight Discrepancy in the Orthotopic Mouse Model (Figure 3C–D):** On line 48, the authors report that 6 weeks after orthotopic injection, pancreatic tumors in the control group had a volume of $51.75 \pm 19.23\text{ mm}^3$ ($\approx 0.052\text{ cm}^3$) and a wet weight of $3.14 \pm 0.95\text{ g}$, whereas knockdown tumors had a volume of $17.48 \pm 4.12\text{ mm}^3$ ($\approx 0.017\text{ cm}^3$) and a wet weight of $1.57 \pm 0.29\text{ g}$. Because soft tissue has a density of $\approx 1.0\text{ g/cm}^3 = 0.001\text{ g/mm}^3$, a $51.75\text{ mm}^3$ tumor weighs $\approx 0.052\text{ g}$ (52 mg), **not** $3.14\text{ g}$ (which would be >10–15% of an entire adult mouse's body weight and correspond to $\sim 3,140\text{ mm}^3$). Either the volume formula/units ($\text{mm}^3$ vs. $\text{cm}^3$ or caliper dimension scaling) or the wet weight measurements are off by a factor of ~60–90×.
- **Flawed Bisulfite Methylation-Specific PCR (MSPCR) Primer Design (Figure 4A & Line 140):** To measure $p57^{Kip2}$ promoter methylation after sodium bisulfite conversion (which converts all unmethylated cytosines to uracil/thymine), the authors report using primers `CGCCAATCGCCGTGGTGTTG` (forward 1), `ACTACATTATGCTAATCGCG` (forward 2), and `GCCAGGCCTGAGCGAGCGAG` (reverse) on reference sequence `NG_008022.1`. Notice that the reverse primer `GCCAGGCCTGAGCGAGCGAG` is 80% G+C and contains multiple non-CpG cytosines (`GCC`, `CCT`, `AGC`) that have **not** been converted to `T` (or `A` on the reverse strand) for bisulfite-treated DNA, and the assay lacks paired Methylated (M) and Unmethylated (U) primer sets required for valid MSPCR. Quantifying "low PCR products" with unconverted/partially converted primers without an unmethylated amplification control or bisulfite sequencing does not validly measure CpG methylation.
- **Single Cell Line & Single siRNA Sequence Without Rescue:** All in vitro mechanistic experiments were conducted in a single cell line (BON) using a single siRNA (`GGUAGAAACACCAACAGGAUACAUU`) without a second independent siRNA or cDNA rescue to exclude off-target toxicity, and without testing whether co-silencing $p57^{Kip2}$ rescues proliferation or mTOR signaling in *NAP1L1*-depleted cells.
- **Orthotopic Xenograft Selection Protocol & Small Sample Size:** In the Methods (line 118), because stable *NAP1L1*-shRNA cells could not be expanded, BON cells were transfected overnight in flasks, selected with $3\text{ }\mu\text{g/mL}$ puromycin for only 48 hours (yielding 77–90% viability), and injected into $n = 4$ control vs. $n = 5$ knockdown mice. With $n = 4$ vs. $n = 5$, a two-tailed non-parametric Mann–Whitney $U$ test has a minimum possible exact $p$-value of $p = 0.0159$ (when completely non-overlapping), and in the knockdown group 3 of 5 mice had no palpable tumors at 4 weeks, yet Figure 3C–D reports tumor volume and weight means across the group without clarifying whether zero-tumor mice were scored as $0$ (or whole pancreas weight) or excluded.
- **Normal Pancreas Control Confounder in Clinical Cohort:** Comparing 43 neuroendocrine tumors (38 primaries, 5 metastases) against 10 bulk "normal pancreas" tissues (Figure 5) is confounded by tissue composition: bulk normal pancreas is >95% exocrine acinar/ductal tissue and only 1–2% endocrine islets, whereas pNENs are 100% neuroendocrine cells. While the primary ($n = 38$) vs. metastasis ($n = 5$) comparison is valid, normal bulk exocrine pancreas is not a matched cell-of-origin control for islet tumors.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: *NAP1L1* expression is elevated in pNEN metastases relative to primary tumors and correlates with Ki67 and tumor size.** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by RT-qPCR and Western blotting across 38 primaries and 5 metastases (Figure 5), though the metastasis sample size ($n = 5$) is small and bulk normal pancreas is an imperfect baseline.
- **Claim 2: *NAP1L1* silencing inhibits BON cell proliferation and mTOR pathway activity in vitro and reduces orthotopic tumor growth in vivo.** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by BrdU ELISA, cell counts, and Western blots for mTOR/S6K1/4E-BP1, though limited to one cell line/siRNA and requiring correction of the physically impossible volume-vs-weight figures in the mouse model.
- **Claim 3: NAP1L1 epigenetically silences $p57^{Kip2}$ by directly binding its promoter and inducing promoter CpG methylation.** `[Fragile]` — While ChIP-qPCR shows NAP1L1 enrichment at the $p57^{Kip2}$ promoter ($-164$ to $+21$) in proliferating vs. serum-starved BON cells, NAP1L1 has no intrinsic DNA methyltransferase domain (no DNMT interaction was tested), and the MSPCR assay used to claim promoter demethylation lacks bisulfite-converted U/M primer pairs or bisulfite sequencing validation.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagent & Protocol Reporting:** The siRNA sequence, ChIP/MSPCR primer sequences, NCBI RefSeq ID (`NG_008022.1`), drug concentrations (`GSK1120212` at $10^{-7}\text{ M}$, `BEZ235` at $10^{-8}\text{ M}$), and STR authentication of BON cells are reported, but Applied Biosystems TaqMan/Assays-on-Demand catalog IDs for *NAP1L1*, *CDKN1C*, *MEN1*, *RPS24*, *RPL28*, and *ALG9*, as well as specific antibody catalog numbers and dilutions, are omitted.
