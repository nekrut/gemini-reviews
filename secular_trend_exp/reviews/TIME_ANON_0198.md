### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular and cellular immunology study reports the isolation and functional characterization of *SRG3*, a murine *SWI3*-related gene cloned via thymus-minus-spleen subtractive cDNA hybridization. *SRG3* encodes a 1,100-amino-acid protein (155 kDa) with 91% identity to human BAF155 and 33–47% regional identity to yeast SWI3, containing an acidic N-terminus, a SANT/myb-like tryptophan repeat domain, a leucine zipper motif, and a proline/glutamine-rich C-terminus. Using rabbit polyclonal antisera against a C-terminal GST-SRG3 fusion, the authors show that SRG3 co-immunoprecipitates with a 195-kDa murine SWI2 homolog in thymocytes, is expressed ~3–5-fold higher in thymus (as well as brain and testis) than in peripheral splenic/lymph node T and B cells, and fractionates on glycerol gradients both within the 2-MDa SWI/SNF complex and as a predominant ~300-kDa subcomplex. Finally, stable antisense RNA knockdown of SRG3 (~50% and ~70% protein reduction in two S49.1 thymoma clones) markedly suppresses hydrocortisone-induced DNA fragmentation and subdiploid apoptosis (from 46% to 4%).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Biochemical & Molecular Controls:** The characterization of SRG3 includes appropriate negative controls (pre-immune serum IP in Fig. 3B; empty vector pRc/CMV transfectants in Fig. 6; $\beta$-actin loading control and *TCF-1* purity control in Northern blots in Fig. 4B; yeast *SWI3* positive and empty vector negative controls in CY165 *HO-lacZ* complementation assays).
- **Antisense Knockdown Clonal Selection (`[Statistical/Design]`):** The functional apoptosis result rests on two stable antisense-transfected S49.1 clones (clone A with ~50% SRG3 protein and clone B with ~30% SRG3 protein) compared against parental S49.1 and a single vector-only transfectant (Fig. 6). While the dose-dependent concordance between Clone A and Clone B in DNA laddering (Fig. 6B) is reassuring, clonal selection in S49.1 thymoma cells can inadvertently select for spontaneous glucocorticoid-receptor (GR) or apoptotic pathway variants unless rescued by re-expression or validated across multiple vector clones and GR-level controls (i.e., verifying that GR expression itself was unchanged in Clones A and B).
- **Quantitative Reporting & Typographical Discrepancy:**
  - In Materials and Methods (line 75), hydrocortisone treatment is stated as $10\ \mu\text{M}$ for 72 h, whereas the legend to Figure 6B (line 106) states "10 mM of hydrocortisone" (a 1,000-fold unit typo in the figure legend).
  - Densitometric estimates ("expressed about three times higher in thymocytes", "about 20% of that expressed in thymocytes", "$\sim 50$ and $30\%$ SRG3 protein", "46% vs 4% subdiploid") are reported as point estimates without replicate standard deviations or formal hypothesis test $p$-values.
  - Cross-reactivity of the anti-SRG3 C-terminal polyclonal serum with a 170-kDa protein (putative murine BAF170) complicates interpretation of total immunoreactivity, though SDS-PAGE separates the 155-kDa and 170-kDa bands and Fig. 6A shows selective reduction of the 155-kDa band.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *SRG3* is the murine homolog of human BAF155 (yeast SWI3) and physically associates with a murine SWI2 homolog in thymocytes. `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated by full-length cDNA sequencing (1,100 aa, 91% identity to BAF155) and co-immunoprecipitation with anti-hSWI2 (Fig. 3B).
- **Claim 2:** A substantial fraction of SRG3 in thymocytes exists in ~300-kDa complexes distinct from the canonical 2-MDa SWI2-containing SWI/SNF complex. `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by glycerol density gradient fractionation (Fig. 5), though partial dissociation of peripheral subunits during high-salt/ultracentrifugation extraction cannot be completely ruled out without cross-linking or reciprocal IP of the 300-kDa fractions.
- **Claim 3:** SRG3 is required for glucocorticoid-induced apoptosis in S49.1 thymoma cells and may regulate thymocyte development. `[Moderately Supported]` — Antisense knockdown in Clones A and B strongly reduces GC-induced apoptosis, and the authors appropriately frame the in vivo thymic development role as a hypothesis requiring further study.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- The complete nucleotide and deduced amino acid sequence of *SRG3* was deposited in EMBL/GenBank/DDBJ under accession number `U85614`.
- Yeast strain genotypes (CY165), plasmid vectors (pGEX4T-2, pRS316GU, pRc/CMV), restriction fragments used for Northern probing (1.8-kb *Hind*III fragment, bases 653–2361) and antisense expression (2.8-kb *Xba*I fragment, bases 1–2829), and buffer formulations are described in sufficient detail to reproduce the experiments.
