### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular epidemiology and assay-development study designs and validates a 178-SNP targeted amplicon-sequencing barcode on the Illumina MiSeq platform (22 $\times$ 8–12-plex primary PCRs followed by indexed secondary PCR, combined with `McrBC` methylation-dependent restriction digestion of host human DNA and Phi29 whole-genome amplification) tailored to *Plasmodium vivax* populations in Papua New Guinea (PNG). Candidate biallelic SNPs were selected from 20 high-coverage PNG *P. vivax* whole-genome sequences (filtered for $\text{MAF} > 0.10$, pairwise $\text{LD} < 0.2$ in 0.5 kb windows, $|\text{Tajima's } D| < 0.5$, $H_e > 0.4$). After pilot validation on 6 isolates (3 $\text{MOI}=1$, 3 $\text{MOI}=2$) with and without WGA across 20 SNPs, the full 178-SNP panel was applied to 94 field isolates from four PNG north-coast catchment areas (East Sepik collected in one survey; Malala, Mugil, and Utu in Madang Province collected in another survey), yielding 146 high-quality SNPs ($\ge 50\times$ depth, median $563\times$) in 83 successfully genotyped isolates. Benchmarking against a previously published 10-microsatellite panel on the same samples showed that the 146-SNP barcode detected higher polyclonality (83.2% $\text{MOI} \ge 2$ via `THEREALMcCOIL`), stronger geographic clustering (STRUCTURE $K=3$, DAPC, NJ trees), and higher pairwise $F_{ST}$ ($0.021$–$0.121$). While the laboratory assay (especially `McrBC` + WGA validation) and marker density represent a clear practical advance for *P. vivax* surveillance, the statistical comparison with microsatellites contains arithmetic errors, an invalid 4-point Mantel test, temporal confounding between the East Sepik and Madang collections, and synthetic chimeric haplotype reconstruction in polyclonal infections.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Arithmetic Error in Sample Counts (Line 113):** In Results line 113, the authors write: *"The Real McCOIL analysis indicated that out of the 83 successfully genotyped P. vivax samples, 69 samples (83.2%) have at least two clones and 24 samples (16.8%) were confirmed monoclonal infections."* Note that $69 + 24 = 93 \ne 83$! Since $69 / 83 = 83.13\%$ and $14 / 83 = 16.87\%$, the number of confirmed monoclonal infections is **14**, not **24** (a typographical/arithmetic error of 10 samples). Similarly, Figure 3 caption (line 131) states *"86 P. vivax isolates"* whereas Results line 108 states **83** isolates were successfully genotyped.
- **Invalid 4-Location Mantel Test & Temporal Confounding:**
  - In Results line 146 and Figure 5, the authors perform a Mantel test between pairwise $F_{ST}$ and geographic distance across the **4 catchment areas** ($N = 4$ locations $\implies \binom{4}{2} = 6$ pairwise points). For $N = 4$ spatial locations, there are only $4! = 24$ possible permutations of the distance matrix, meaning the minimum possible non-parametric permutation p-value of a Mantel test is $1/24 = 0.0417$ (one-sided) or $2/24 = 0.0833$ (two-sided), and no p-value or $r$-statistic is actually reported in the text!
  - Furthermore, the distant East Sepik population was collected in a different calendar year/survey (`[YEAR]/13`, line 38) than the three Madang populations (`[YEAR]`, line 38). Thus, the largest geographic distance (East Sepik vs. Madang villages, $F_{ST} = 0.086$–$0.121$) is completely confounded with temporal sampling separation.
- **Ascertainment Skew in the 20 Discovery Genomes:** The 20 WGS isolates used to ascertain SNPs with $\text{MAF} > 0.10$ and $|\text{Tajima's } D| < 0.5$ were heavily skewed toward Madang ($n = 17$) versus East Sepik ($n = 2$) and Milne Bay ($n = 1$) (line 98). Moreover, in Table 1 (line 139), the upper and lower triangular matrices of pairwise $F_{ST}$ are unlabeled—presumably one triangle is SNP $F_{ST}$ (lower triangle: East Sepik vs. Malala/Mugil/Utu $= 0.09, 0.086, 0.121$; within Madang $= 0.021$–$0.040$) and the other is microsatellite $F_{ST}$ (upper triangle: $0.025, 0.033, 0.12, 0.067, 0.045, 0.083$), which should be explicitly labeled in the table footnote.
- **Chimeric "Dominant Haplotype" Reconstruction in Polyclonal Infections:** Because 83.2% (69/83) of successfully genotyped samples were polyclonal ($\text{MOI} \ge 2$), taking the major-read-count allele at each of the 146 unlinked amplicon loci (line 113) constructs a synthetic chimeric haplotype when two clones are present at similar proportions (e.g., 55:45), which can distort NJ phylogenies and pairwise allele sharing ($P_S$).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim: `McrBC` digestion + Phi29 WGA + multiplexed MiSeq amplicon sequencing provides unbiased, deep coverage of 146 *P. vivax* SNPs from field blood samples** — `[Well Supported]`. The pilot experiment comparing paired WGA vs. non-WGA templates in monoclonal ($p = 0.13$) and polyclonal ($p = 0.92$) infections with zero genotype discordance, followed by median $563\times$ coverage in 83/94 field isolates, solidly validates the wet-lab protocol.
- **Claim: The 146-SNP barcode provides higher resolution for detecting polyclonality and sub-national population structure than 10 microsatellites** — `[Well Supported]`. Comparing 146 genome-wide biallelic loci across all 14 chromosomes to 10 microsatellites naturally increases statistical power to detect both heterozygous mixed infections and subtle allele-frequency differentiation (DAPC and STRUCTURE $K=3$), though as the authors fairly note in the Discussion (line 170), this reflects marker number/density (146 vs. 10) and microsatellite homoplasy/saturation ($H_e = 0.82$) rather than an intrinsic flaw of microsatellite loci per se.
- **Claim: Significant isolation-by-distance (IBD) at the sub-provincial level proving SNPs can pinpoint geographic origin of infections** — `[Overstated]`. With only 4 sites (3 clustered in Madang and 1 in East Sepik sampled in a different year), the 6-pair regression is driven entirely by the between-province (East Sepik vs. Madang) contrast; within Madang province (Malala, Mugil, Utu), SNP $F_{ST}$ is low ($0.021$–$0.040$) and DAPC ellipses overlap substantially.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagent & Pipeline Transparency:** The `McrBC` + WGA protocol, PCR cycling conditions, GATK/bwa-mem/samtools parameters, and SNP coordinates/primers (Additional file 3: Table S2) and final dominant haplotypes (Additional file 4: Table S3) are well documented.
- **Raw Sequencing Data:** Raw MiSeq FASTQ files are not deposited in NCBI SRA/ENA ("available from the corresponding author on reasonable request," line 188), though processed genotype tables are included in the supplementary files.
