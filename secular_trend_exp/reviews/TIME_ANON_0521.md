### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This study integrates public transcriptomic datasets to systematically identify constitutive promoters in *Streptomyces coelicolor* M145 and validates a subset for heterologous metabolic engineering across *Streptomyces* species. Using 8 time-series microarray datasets (`361` array chips from NCBI GEO across diverse media and stress conditions) and a 24-hour SMM-medium RNA-seq dataset (`GSE44415` / `[ACCESSION_REDACTED]`), the authors filter 7,825 *S. coelicolor* genes down to `941` stably expressed genes (`|log2FC| <= 1`, i.e., within 0.5- to 2-fold of the sample mean on `>80%` of arrays), narrow these to `537` genes in essential COG categories (excluding unknown category `S`), retain `314` first-gene-in-operon/isolated genes via `DOOR2` and `Rockhopper`, and select `166` putative constitutive promoters covering a wide dynamic range of RNA-seq RPKM values. Eight candidate promoters (`SCO0552p`, `SCO1897p`, `SCO1998p`, `SCO2488p`, `SCO4485p`, `SCO4508p`, `SCO4543p`, `SCO5014p`) are cloned upstream of green fluorescent protein (`gfp`) in the integrative vector `pSET152` and evaluated against `ermE*p` via fluorescence and RT-qPCR in *S. coelicolor* M1146, *S. venezuelae* WVR2006, and *S. albus* J1074, followed by driving the cryptic jadomycin B biosynthetic gene cluster (`jadJ`) in *S. venezuelae* ISP5230. While the pipeline provides useful genetic parts, the experimental design suffers from an unaddressed confounding of transcriptional promoter activity with native 5'-UTR/RBS translational efficiency and mRNA stability, as well as a direct internal contradiction in using *hrdB* as the RT-qPCR housekeeping reference after arguing in the Background that *hrdB* is not constitutively transcribed.

### 2. STRENGTHS
- **Multi-Dataset Transcriptomic Filtering Pipeline:** Leveraging `361` microarray chips spanning distinct media (`R5`, `SMM`, `MG`, `MM`, `MS`, `YEME`) and environmental perturbations (`160` conditions) is a logical strategy to identify genes with low expression variance across developmental stages.
- **Cross-Species and Functional Metabolic Engineering Validation:** Testing the 8 selected regulatory sequences across three *Streptomyces* hosts (*S. coelicolor* M1146, *S. venezuelae* WVR2006, and *S. albus* J1074) and demonstrating time-course jadomycin B activation via `jadJ` overexpression in *S. venezuelae* ISP5230 (`Fig. 4`) provides practical proof-of-concept utility.

### 3. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Confounding of Transcriptional Promoter Strength with 5'-UTR/RBS Sequence Variation:** As described in Methods (Line 156) and Results (Line 73), each "promoter" construct was cloned as a `500 bp` genomic fragment directly upstream of the native translational start codon (`ATG`/`GTG`) fused to `gfp`. Consequently, each construct carries a completely different native 5'-untranslated region (5'-UTR) and ribosome binding site (RBS). In bacteria, 5'-UTR secondary structure and RBS affinity profoundly govern both ribosome loading (translation initiation rate) and ribonuclease degradation (mRNA half-life). No RiboJ insulator or standardized synthetic RBS was used, meaning that differences in GFP fluorescence and even `gfp` steady-state mRNA levels cannot be attributed purely to transcriptional promoter initiation rate.
- **Self-Contradictory Use of *hrdB* as the RT-qPCR Internal Reference Gene:** In the Background (Line 24), the authors explicitly critique the field's reliance on *hrdB* by writing: *"Previously, a couple of streptomycete promoters were cloned from house-keeping genes, such as hrdB encoding the principal sigma factor. However, the transcription of hrdB is not constant during the lifecycle of Streptomyces."* Yet in the Methods (Line 175), the authors normalize all time-course RT-qPCR measurements (`24, 48, 72, and 96 h`) to *hrdB* (`"The hrdB was used as the internal control"`), directly undermining their own claims of temporal stability across fermentation time points.
- **Flawed Premise of Correlating Native Endogenous ORF RPKM with Heterologous `gfp` Expression:** In `Fig. 2d–e`, the authors correlate single-time-point (24 h SMM medium) RNA-seq RPKM values of the 8 native endogenous *S. coelicolor* genes (`SCO0552`, `SCO4508`, etc.) against `gfp` mRNA (`R^2 = 0.25`) and GFP protein fluorescence (`R^2 = 0.0155`) in `R5` medium. Even under identical promoter initiation rates, endogenous ORFs of varying lengths and codon compositions have vastly different mRNA degradation rates than `gfp`, and the media differed (`SMM` for RNA-seq vs. `R5` for GFP assays).
- **Plasmid Nomenclature Inconsistency and Missing Error-Bar Definitions:** Line 158 refers to `"primers GFP-F/R from plasmid pLN1"`, whereas Table 1 lists only `pLNermE*` (`pSET152` derivative containing `ermE*p` and `gfp`). Furthermore, `Fig. 2`, `Fig. 3`, and `Fig. 4` state `"Mean values of three independent experiments with SD are indicated by error bars"`, but no statistical hypothesis tests or p-values are reported for any comparison, nor is it clarified whether the three experiments represent independent integrative transconjugant clones or technical flask replicates.

### 4. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Bioinformatics screening of `361` microarray chips and RNA-seq data identifies `166` candidate constitutive regulatory regions in *S. coelicolor*. `[Well Supported]` — The bioinformatic filtering criteria (`|log2FC| <= 1` in `>80%` of arrays, COG categories, operon head filtering) are clearly described.
- **Claim 2:** The 8 tested 500-bp upstream regions drive relatively stable reporter expression across time points and across three *Streptomyces* species compared to `ermE*p`. `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported by fluorescence and RT-qPCR panels (`Fig. 2–3`), though temporal stability in RT-qPCR is partially confounded by normalizing to *hrdB*.
- **Claim 3:** `SCO4508p` and `SCO5014p` are "strong promoters" that outperform `ermE*p` in activating cryptic jadomycin B biosynthesis. `[Moderately Supported]` — Technically they act as strong *500-bp promoter + native 5'-UTR/RBS regulatory cassettes* rather than isolated transcriptional promoters; their superior jadomycin B yield (`110.2 ± 9.7 µg/ml` vs. `55.6 ± 8.3 µg/ml` for `ermE*p`) is well demonstrated (`Fig. 4`).

### 5. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Availability:** Public GEO accession numbers (`GSE18489`, `GSE2983`, `GSE30569`, `GSE30570`, `GSE31068`, `GSE44415`, and `[ACCESSION_REDACTED]`) are fully listed in Additional file 1: Table S1, and strains/plasmids are detailed in Table 1.
- **Missing Details:** The exact genomic coordinates of the 500-bp cloned regions and primer sequences (referenced as Additional file 1: Table S2–S4) are not embedded in the main text, and the undefined plasmid `pLN1` (Line 158) requires correction to match Table 1 (`pLNermE*`).
