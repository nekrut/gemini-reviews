### 1. Executive Summary
This brief genetic mapping study characterizes the genomic organization and chromosomal location of the murine actin capping protein $\beta$-subunit gene (`Cappb1`). Using Southern blot hybridization, screening of a 129SV lambda FIXII genomic library, genomic-to-cDNA sequence comparison, and PCR/allele-specific oligonucleotide dot-blot genotyping across the Jackson Laboratory interspecific `(C57BL/6J x SPRET/Ei)F1 x SPRET/Ei` (BSS) backcross panel ($n=94$ animals), the authors confirm that mice harbor a single `Cappb1` gene undergoing alternative splicing of a 113-bp exon/intron and map `Cappb1` to distal mouse Chromosome 4 between `Cdc42` (co-segregating with `Afar`) and `D4Mit312`, in a region of conserved synteny with human Chromosome 1p36.1. They further evaluate 95% confidence intervals of recombination fractions to test colocalization with three mouse neurological/morphological mutant loci (`snubnose`, `curly tail`, and `cribriform degeneration`).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Interspecific Backcross Linkage Mapping:** Genotyping two 3'-UTR insertion/deletion polymorphisms across $n=94$ BSS backcross progeny using four allele-specific oligonucleotide probes provided clean haplotype segregation (Fig. 1), placing `Cappb1` $1.06 \pm 1.06\text{ cM}$ distal to `Cdc42` (1/94 recombinant) and $4.25 \pm 2.08\text{ cM}$ proximal to `D4Mit312` (4/94 recombinants), and 5/94 recombinants relative to anchor marker `D4Mit13`.
- **Binomial Confidence Interval Comparison for Candidate Loci:** Rather than relying solely on composite Mouse Genome Database (MGD) positions, the authors appropriately calculated 95% binomial confidence limits from the raw recombinant counts of individual crosses relative to shared anchor loci (`D4Mit13` for `Cappb1` [5/94; 95% CI: 2.5–12.5 cM] and `ct` [26/272; 95% CI: 6.6–13.7 cM]; `Tyrp1` for `sno` [92/535; 95% CI: 14.0–20.5 cM]). This statistically excludes `sno` while leaving `ct` (overlapping CIs) and `cri` (raw recombinant counts unavailable in the literature) as unexcluded positional candidates.
- **Minor Technical / Textual Errors:**
  - In line 23, the authors write: *"The mouse intron is also 113 bp in length with the exon sequence and surrounding intron sequence containing the canonical splice donor (AGA) and acceptor (AGG) sites at either end of the intron."* First, in the preceding sentence they stated that $\beta1$ cDNA contains a 113-bp *exon* absent in $\beta2$ cDNA (meaning the 113-bp sequence is a cassette exon rather than a constitutive intron); second, canonical eukaryotic splice donor and acceptor dinucleotides are `GT` and `AG`, whereas `AGA`/`AGG` include flanking exonic/intronic nucleotides without clearly delineating the exon–intron boundary.
  - The gene symbol for *snubnose* is inconsistently written as `sn` (line 31) and `sno` (lines 33, 35, 46).
