### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This classic somatic cell genetics study maps a non-classical MHC-linked gene required for MHC Class II antigen processing and peptide loading (later identified as HLA-DM) to a ~1 Mb homozygous deletion within the MHC Class II region on human chromosome 6p. The authors characterize a newly isolated EMS-mutagenized B-lymphoblastoid cell line (B-LCL) mutant, `5.2.4` (derived from hemizygous progenitor `8.1.6`), which carries a homozygous deletion of the DR/DQ-to-complement interval while retaining one haplotype copy of HLA-DP (`DP4.1`) and Class I genes. Through Southern blot deletion mapping, DP4-restricted T-cell proliferation assays (with native intact HBsAg and tetanus toxoid vs. synthetic peptides), SDS-PAGE DR dimer stability assays, conformation-sensitive mAb `16.23` flow cytometry, and pairwise somatic cell hybrid complementation crosses (`5.2.4`, `9.5.3`, `7.9.6`, `7.2.6`, and `T2-DR`), the authors prove that the Class II antigen-presentation defect in the `16.23`-selected point mutants falls into a single recessive complementation group mapping inside the `5.2.4`/`T2` MHC Class II deletion, and is genetically separable from the Class I peptide transporter `PSF1` (`TAP1`).

### 2. STRENGTHS
- **Rigorous Somatic Cell Complementation Design:** The authors systematically verify that each mutation (`9.5.3`, `7.9.6`, `7.2.6`, `5.2.4`) is recessive in hybrids with wild-type/DR-null lines (`3.1.3`, `9.22.3`, `8.1.6`), and then perform all-pairs mutant $\times$ mutant crosses plus an independent cross with the unrelated homozygous MHC deletion line `T2-DR` to rule out unlinked second-site EMS mutations.
- **Gene Dosage Control for Hemizygous DP Expression:** Because `5.2.4` is hemizygous for `DP4.1` whereas progenitor `8.1.6` is dizygous, the authors explicitly include the DP-hemizygous line `9.28.6` as a dosage-matched control in all antigen presentation and peptide titration assays (Figure 2a,b).
- **Clean Genetic Dissociation of Class I and Class II Peptide Loading:** By assaying surface HLA-A2 (`PA2.1`) and HLA-B27 (`ME-1`) alongside peptide rescue (HIV gag 265–279 vs. HBsAg S1a negative control), the study demonstrates that while `5.2.4` lacks `PSF1` (`TAP1`) and has reduced Class I surface expression, the point mutants (`9.5.3`, `7.2.6`, `7.9.6`) have normal Class I expression, proving that Class I peptide transport (`PSF1`) and Class II peptide loading are governed by distinct MHC-linked genes.

### 3. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Incomplete Complementation Matrix Entry in Table 1:** In Table 1, the `5.2.4 x 7.9.6` hybrid is scored as `-` for `16.23` mAb binding, but its SDS-PAGE DR dimer stability is listed as `ND` (Not Determined), unlike the other hybrids where both assays were completed.
- **Summary Reporting of Proliferation Replicates:** T-cell proliferation data in Figures 2a and 3c are plotted as normalized percentages of control (`% of 9.28.6` or `% of 8.1.6`) reporting only "median values from triplicate cultures of a representative experiment; SEM <15% in at least three experiments" rather than plotting individual replicate variance or error bars directly on the bar charts and peptide titration curves.
- **Residual Response to Intact Antigen in `5.2.4`:** In Figure 2a, `5.2.4` exhibits ~15–20% residual T-cell stimulation to native TT and HBsAg. The authors transparently discuss three plausible explanations (leakiness of the `5.2.4` pathway, trace pre-fragmented peptide in the native antigen prep, or alternative processing for a subset of polyclonal TT T cells), though protease-inhibitor/fixed-APC controls were not run in this study to distinguish trace peptide contamination from HLA-DM-independent presentation.

### 4. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Mutant `5.2.4` has a homozygous deletion in the DR/DQ region while retaining one haplotype of DP and Class I:** `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by Southern blotting with `DPA1`, `DPB1`, `DQA1`, `DQB1`, `DRA`, `DRB3`, `C4`, and `HLA-1.3` probes (Figure 1c).
- **A single MHC-linked gene within the `5.2.4`/`T2` deletion is required for normal Class II processing/presentation of intact proteins and SDS-stable dimer formation:** `[Well Supported]` — Established via non-complementation across `9.5.3`, `7.9.6`, `7.2.6`, `5.2.4`, and `T2-DR` (Figure 3, Table 1).
- **Class II molecules on presentation-defective mutants are underoccupied by self-peptides, enhancing exogenous peptide binding (~15-fold):** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by dose-response peptide titration curves comparing `5.2.4` vs. `9.28.6` and `9.5.3/7.9.6/7.2.6` vs. `8.1.6` (Figure 2b,c).
- **The Class II presentation gene is distinct from `PSF1` (`TAP1`):** `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated by normal HLA-A2 and HLA-B27 surface levels on `9.5.3`, `7.2.6`, and `7.9.6` (Figure 4).

### 5. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagent and Cell Line Documentation:** All mutant derivations, mAb hybridoma specificities (`16.23`, `VI.15`, `ME-1`, `PA2.1`, `HB10.A`), peptide sequences/concentrations (HBsAg 14–33, TT 947–969, HIV gag 265–279), and Southern blot cDNA probes are thoroughly documented with literature citations. A few secondary observations (e.g., TT peptide titration enhancement in `5.2.4`, tetraploidy FACScan profiles, and `Ii`/`B2m` loading control Southern blots) are cited as "data not shown" per era conventions.
