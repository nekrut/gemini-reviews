### 1. Executive Summary
This study (`TIME_ANON_0127`) investigates the expression of lysyl oxidase (`LOX`), collagen type IV, and advanced glycation end-product (`AGE`) signaling effectors (`p-ERK`, `p-c-Jun`, and `NF-κB p65`) in archived ovarian wedge-resection tissues from $N=6$ women with polycystic ovary syndrome (`PCOS`) compared with normal ovarian tissues from $N=4$ normo-ovulatory control women undergoing oophorectomy for benign disease. Using semi-qualitative immunohistochemistry (`IHC`), Western blotting, electrophoretic mobility shift assays (`EMSA` with `LOX` promoter restriction fragments encompassing `NF-κB` [`nt 5–94`] and `AP-1` [`nt 2094–2190`] motifs), and RT-PCR, the authors report increased `LOX`, collagen type IV, `p-ERK`, `p-c-Jun`, and `NF-κB p65` expression in `PCO` ovaries, correlated `AGE`/`NF-κB` and `LOX` staining in granulosa cells, and increased binding of `NF-κB p65` and `p-c-Jun` from `PCO` tissue extracts to the human `LOX` promoter. While the hypothesis linking ovarian AGE accumulation to `LOX`-mediated extracellular matrix cross-linking in PCOS is biologically interesting, a forensic audit reveals mathematically impossible Fisher's exact test $p$-values for $N=6$ vs $N=4$ subjects (indicative of unacknowledged histological section pseudoreplication), multiple direct numerical contradictions between the main text and figure captions, absence of Western blot loading controls, and purely observational/correlative data that do not test whether AGEs causally induce `LOX`.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Mathematical Impossibility of Reported Fisher's Exact Test $P$-Values for $N=6$ vs $N=4$ Patients (Pseudoreplication):**
  - In `Materials and methods` (`Ovarian specimens`, line 41), the cohort consists of **$n_1 = 6$ women with PCOS** and **$n_2 = 4$ control women** ($N_{\text{total}} = 10$).
  - In `Statistical methods` (line 79), the authors state: `"Comparisons between the expression levels among patients with PCOS and controls were performed using Fisher's exact test. ... All results with a two-sided P-value <= 0.05 were considered significant."`
  - However, in `Results` (line 89), the authors report Fisher's exact test $p$-values of **$P = 0.002$** (`"vessels of PCO tissue compared to normal (extent, P = 0.002; intensity, P = 0.002)"`) and $P = 0.004$.
  - **Combinatorial Proof of Impossibility:** For a sample of $n_1 = 6$ cases and $n_2 = 4$ controls, the total number of distinct ways to assign 6 cases and 4 controls across any contingency table with fixed marginals is:
    $$\binom{6 + 4}{4} = \binom{10}{4} = \frac{10!}{6!\,4!} = 210$$
    Even under the most extreme possible separation (e.g., 6/6 PCOS patients in the highest staining category and 4/4 controls in the lowest category), the hypergeometric probability of that single extremal table is $1 / 210 = 0.004762$, so the minimum possible two-sided Fisher's exact test $p$-value is **$P = 0.0048$** (or $2/210 = 0.0095$). It is **mathematically impossible** to obtain $P = 0.002$ from $N=6$ vs $N=4$ independent subjects!
  - How did STATA 9.0 output $P = 0.002$ and Kendall's $\tau = 0.2857$ with $P = 0.0412$ (line 113, where $\tau = 0.2857$ on $N=10$ subjects would yield $P \approx 0.25$, not $0.0412$)? The captions of **Fig 1** and **Fig 2** reveal the error: `"Pictures are representative of 20 sections."` Treating 20–30 multiple histological sections from the same 10 ovaries as independent statistical observations in Fisher's exact test and Kendall's $\tau$ is severe **pseudoreplication** that invalidates every IHC $p$-value in the paper.
- **Direct Internal Contradictions Between Main Text, Methods, and Figure Captions:**
  1. **Contradictory Western Blot Fold-Changes:** In `Results` (line 118), the text states: `"The expression levels of p-ERK, p-c-Jun and NF-kB p65 were increased 2.5 to 3-fold in the PCO tissue (Fig. 2B)." ` Yet the caption of **Fig 2B** (line 97) states: `"Protein levels of p-ERK are increased by 3-fold, of p-c-Jun 1.5-fold and of NF-kB p65 2-fold relative to control."`
  2. **Contradictory Protein Extract Type and Loading Mass:** In `Materials and methods` (`Western blot analysis`, line 53), the text specifies `"Whole-cell protein extracts (20 ug)"`, whereas the captions of **Fig 1D** and **Fig 2B** specify `"40 ug of whole-cell extract"`. Similarly, `Materials and methods` (`Electrophoretic mobility-shift assay`, line 69) states `"Nuclear extracts from control or PCOS ovarian tissue (30-40 ug)"`, whereas `Results` (line 125) and the **Fig 3** caption (line 64) state `"whole-cell ovarian protein extract ... Increasing concentrations (10, 20, 40 ug) of protein were used"`.
  3. **Contradictory RT-PCR Quantification Method & End-Point Protocol:** `Materials and methods` (line 74) states `"relative quantification was established with the \Delta\Delta Ct method"`, whereas `Results` (line 134) and **Fig 4A** state `"Using the Pfaffl equation"` (which requires primer-specific amplification efficiencies that are never reported). Moreover, the cycling protocol in line 74 (`"30 cycles each of 94°C for 30 sec, 55°C for 1 min and 72°C for 30 sec, followed by an extension at 72°C for 5 min"`) is a standard block-cycler end-point PCR program, with no real-time qPCR instrument, SYBR Green/TaqMan reagent, or `GAPDH` primer sequences reported.
- **Missing Western Blot Loading Controls and Replicate Quantification:** Neither **Fig 1D**, **Fig 2B**, nor **Fig 4B** includes a housekeeping loading control (such as $\beta$-actin or GAPDH) or total unphosphorylated ERK1/2 and c-Jun, and no standard deviations, sample sizes ($n$), or statistical tests are reported for the Western blot or qPCR fold-changes.
- **Incorrect Molecular Biology Terminology:** In lines 36 and 58, the authors refer to the $2,241\text{-bp}$ 5'-flanking regulatory sequence of the human *LOX* gene in `pGL2` as `"The human LOX promoter cDNA"` and `"part of the cDNA of the human LOX promoter"`. A gene promoter is genomic DNA, not complementary DNA (`cDNA`) reverse-transcribed from mRNA.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`NF-κB p65` and `AP-1` (`p-c-Jun`) present in human ovarian extracts specifically bind to `NF-κB` (`nt 5–94`) and `AP-1` (`nt 2094–2190`) elements of the human `LOX` promoter in vitro.* **`[Moderately Supported]`** — Supported by dose-dependent EMSA complex formation, $100\times/200\times$ cold probe competition, and antibody-mediated complex inhibition (**Fig 3**), though without a non-specific competitor oligonucleotide or mutant binding-site probe control.
- **Claim 2:** *`LOX`, collagen type IV, `p-ERK`, `p-c-Jun`, and `NF-κB p65` are significantly upregulated in PCOS ovaries ($P = 0.002\text{–}0.048$).* **`[Methodologically Flawed]`** — The reported Fisher's exact test $p$-values ($P = 0.002$) are mathematically impossible for $N=6$ vs $N=4$ patients and reflect pseudoreplication across histological sections, while the Western blots lack loading controls and replicate statistics.
- **Claim 3:** *Excess collagen deposition and cystogenesis in PCOS tissue are due to AGE-mediated stimulation of `LOX` promoter activity via `NF-κB` and `AP-1`.* **`[Overstated]`** — No experiment in the manuscript tests whether exogenous AGEs (or RAGE blockade) induce `LOX` mRNA/protein or `NF-κB`/`AP-1` binding in ovarian cells; because `NF-κB` and `AP-1` are activated by numerous inflammatory cytokines, oxidative stress, and hyperandrogenism in PCOS, attributing their activation in whole-ovary biopsies specifically to AGEs is purely correlative.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagent & Primer Reporting:** Forward and reverse primers for `LOX` are provided (line 74), along with restriction enzymes (`ApaI`/`MscI` and `BsgI`/`BpmI`) and Santa Cruz/Zymed/Chemicon antibody catalog numbers. However, `GAPDH` internal control primer sequences, the qPCR instrument/chemistry, the exact GenBank accession number defining coordinates `1–2241` of the human `LOX` promoter, and raw patient-level IHC scoring tables ($N=6$ vs $N=4$) are missing.
