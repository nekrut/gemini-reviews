### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This preclinical vaccine study expresses a secreted, N-glycosylated N-terminal polypeptide of the SARS-CoV (Urbani strain) Spike glycoprotein spanning amino acids 14–762 (`nS`, corresponding to the S1 subunit containing the ACE2 receptor-binding domain, fused to a honeybee melittin signal peptide and C-terminal $\text{His}_6$ tag) in recombinant baculovirus-infected High Five insect cells. Groups of 7 female BALB/c mice were immunized subcutaneously on days 0, 28, and 56 with $10\ \mu\text{g}$ of Ni-NTA affinity-purified `nS` formulated with either saponin adjuvant (`QS21`) or Ribi adjuvant (`MPL + TDM`), alongside matched negative-control groups immunized with baculovirus-expressed soluble vaccinia virus `L1R` protein in the same adjuvants. Both `nS` regimens induced antibodies that bound native membrane-associated full-length S on MVA/S-infected HeLa cells (Fig. 2), reached high endpoint ELISA titers (up to 1:409,600 with QS21, Fig. 3A) and Vero microneutralization titers (mean 1:1,269 with QS21 vs. ~4.6-fold lower with MPL+TDM, Fig. 3B), and conferred a $\ge 10^6$-fold reduction in lung viral titers ($P = 0.0017$, Mann–Whitney U test; 6/7 mice below detection in both groups, Fig. 4A) and a $10^3$- to $>10^4$-fold reduction in nasal turbinate viral titers ($P = 0.0017$ vs. control; QS21 significantly superior to MPL+TDM at $P = 0.0250$ with 7/7 mice below detection, Fig. 4B) two days after intranasal challenge with $10^5\ \text{TCID}_{50}$ SARS-CoV. The study is well-controlled and concisely presented, with a minor panel-label swap in the Figure 4 legend and pooled-serum ELISA measurements.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Matched Recombinant Antigen Negative Control:** Uses a identically produced, Ni-NTA affinity-purified baculovirus-secreted viral glycoprotein (vaccinia virus `L1R`) formulated in the exact same adjuvants (`QS21` or `MPL + TDM`) as the negative control, rigorously ruling out non-specific innate immune protection from insect-cell contaminants, His-tags, or adjuvants.
- **Conformational Validation and Appropriate Non-Parametric Statistics:** Verifies that antibodies raised against insect-cell `nS` recognize native, ER-folded full-length Spike on the surface of unpermeabilized MVA/S-infected HeLa cells (Fig. 2), and uses exact Mann–Whitney U tests corrected for ties to compare censored/left-truncated viral titers in the lungs and nasal turbinates ($n=7$/group).
- **Balanced Discussion of Animal Model Limitations:** Explicitly notes in the Discussion (line 48) that young BALB/c mice support transient viral replication without clinical disease, and discusses the need for testing in ferrets and non-human primates (citing the MVA-S hepatitis warning in ferrets by Weingartl et al.).

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Swapped Panel Labels `(A)` and `(B)` Within the Figure 4 Legend:** In the Figure 4 legend (line 39), the first sentence states that virus titers *"were measured in the lower (A) and upper (B) respiratory tract"* (matching the Results text on lines 36 and 41, where Fig. 4A is lung and Fig. 4B is nasal turbinates). However, the immediately following sentence reverses `(A)` and `(B)`: *"The dotted line represents the lower limit of detection corresponding to 1.8 log10 TCID50 per g tissue for upper respiratory tract (A) and 1.5 log10 TCID50 per g tissue for lower respiratory tract (B)."*
- **Pooled Sera Used for ELISA Kinetics and Early Neutralization Timepoints:** In Figure 3A (all timepoints) and Figure 3B (days 28 and 56), endpoint ELISA titers and IgG2a/IgG1 ratios were measured on *pooled* sera from the 7 mice per group rather than individual animals, precluding inter-animal variance estimates and statistical comparison of binding titers or IgG subclass ratios across individual mice (individual mouse sera were only assayed on day 78 in Fig. 3B).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Baculovirus-expressed secreted SARS-CoV S1 (`nS`, aa 14–762) is N-glycosylated (~110 kDa reducing to ~85 kDa after PNGase F) and elicits antibodies that bind native cell-surface Spike and neutralize SARS-CoV.** `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by SDS-PAGE/silver stain, PNGase F digestion, surface immunofluorescence on MVA/S-infected cells, and Vero microneutralization assays.
- **Claim 2: Subcutaneous immunization with `nS` + `QS21` outperforms `nS` + `MPL+TDM`, providing complete protection against SARS-CoV replication in both the upper (7/7 undetectable) and lower (6/7 undetectable, $\ge 10^6$-fold reduction) respiratory tracts of BALB/c mice.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by statistically significant Mann–Whitney U comparisons ($P = 0.0017$ vs. L1R control; $P = 0.0250$ QS21 vs. MPL+TDM in nasal turbinates).
