### 1. Executive Summary
This comprehensive study presents a chromosome-level de novo reference genome assembly of a male Dezhou donkey (*Equus asinus*, 2.432 Gb, contig N50 = 7.92 Mb, scaffold N50 = 93.37 Mb across 30 autosomes + X, Y, and mtDNA) built using 211× Illumina short-insert/mate-pair reads, PacBio Sequel long reads, and 56× Hi-C chromatin contact data, annotated with 13-tissue RNA-seq. Combining 85 newly resequenced genomes (~10.9× coverage) with 48 public genomes (totaling 126 domestic donkeys and 7 wild asses across 9 countries), the authors characterize global donkey population structure (Tropical Africa, North Africa & Eurasia, and Australia), demographic histories (PSMC and SMC++), and discordant maternal (mtDNA divergence 0.303–0.910 Mya) versus paternal (Y-chromosome divergence 14.7–17.9 kya) histories. Finally, using genome-wide selection scans ($F_{ST}$, XP-EHH, ROD, $\pi$, Tajima's $D$) across Dun (ancestral diluted gray) and non-Dun (recessive black/chestnut) donkeys, combined with croup skin histology, RNA-seq, RT-qPCR, immunohistochemistry, and immunofluorescence, they identify a causal 1 bp deletion (`chr8:g.42742556CT>C-`) ~18.6 kb downstream of *TBX3* that abolishes asymmetric cortical keratinocyte *TBX3* expression and drives convergent coat-color evolution with horses.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **High-Contiguity Reference Assembly & Sex Chromosome Validation:** The hybrid assembly (SOAPdenovo + DBG2OLC + Pilon + BESST + PBJelly + Juicer/3d-dna Hi-C) achieves a 6-fold to 24-fold improvement in scaffold N50 over prior donkey assemblies. Particularly commendable is the assembly and depth-based validation of the non-recombining Y chromosome (NRY) across all 126 individuals (confirming zero depth in all females and half autosomal depth in $\ge 80\%$ of jacks).
- **Population Genomics & Demographic Modeling:** The variant calling pipeline (GATK HaplotypeCaller + hard filtering + bootstrapped BQSR/IndelRealigner across 17.28M SNPs and 1.5M indels) is rigorous. Population structure (NJ tree, PCA, ADMIXTURE $K=2\text{–}10$ with 20 replicates, $D$-statistics, TreeMix) and coalescent demography (PSMC with 100 bootstraps, SMC++, and 70 `ms` coalescent simulations validating the ~40–60 kya PSMC admixture hump) are carefully executed and interpreted without overclaiming single vs. dual domestication centers given the lack of ancient DNA.
- **Multi-Modal Functional Validation of the *TBX3* Causal Variant:** The mapping of the Dun/non-Dun locus integrates three complementary selection statistics (XP-EHH, $F_{ST}$, ROD), fine-scale haplotype phasing (`SHAPEIT`) and median-joining networks, perfect recessive cosegregation of `chr8:g.42742556CT>C-` (`C-/C-` in all non-Dun vs. `CT/CT` or `CT/C-` in all Dun donkeys and wild asses), croup skin RNA-seq and RT-qPCR ($n = 3$ vs. $n = 3$, showing 1.8–2.3× *TBX3* downregulation and coordinate upregulation of 9 melanogenic genes: *DCT*, *KIT*, *KITLG*, *MC1R*, *MLANA*, *OCA2*, *SLC24A5*, *TRPM1*, *TYR*), and H&E/IHC/IF microscopy demonstrating loss of asymmetric cortical keratinocyte TBX3 expression in anagen hair follicles.
- **Minor Sample Count Inconsistency in Text:** In the Results (line 90), the Dun vs. non-Dun scan is described as comparing "25 Dun donkey samples and 23 non-Dun donkey samples" ($n = 48$), whereas in the Methods (line 158) it is described as "44 donkeys, which were divided into two groups, i.e., Dun (23 gray donkeys) and non-Dun (21 black donkeys)." Similarly, line 53 states "two Asian wild asses (two *E. hemionus* and one *E. kiang*)" (which sums to 3, whereas line 99 says two new plus three public plus one NGDC kiang = 6 Asian wild asses + 1 Somali wild ass = 7 wild asses total). These text numbers should be harmonized.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Domestic donkeys originated in Africa from African wild ass ancestors, with Tropical African donkeys closest to the Somali wild ass and strong paternal (Y-chromosome) bottlenecking relative to deeply diverged maternal (mtDNA) clades.** `[Well Supported]` — Strongly supported by autosomal IBS trees, PCA, ADMIXTURE, $D$-statistics, PSMC/SMC++, and calibrated BEAST2 Y-chromosome vs. mtDNA phylogenies. The authors appropriately note that ancient DNA is needed to resolve whether domestication occurred at one or two African centers.
- **Claim 2: The recessive non-Dun coat color in domestic donkeys is caused by a 1 bp deletion (`chr8:g.42742556CT>C-`) ~18.6 kb downstream of *TBX3* that downregulates cortical *TBX3* expression and relieves inhibition of melanosome deposition.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by genome-wide sweep scans, 100% genotype-phenotype cosegregation, comparative homology to the horse `non-Dun1`/`non-Dun2` enhancer region (predicting disruption of an NFIC binding motif `CTGGC` $\rightarrow$ `CGGC`), skin transcriptomics/RT-qPCR, and hair follicle histology/immunofluorescence.
- **Claim 3: Independent regulatory deletions downstream of *TBX3* in horses and donkeys represent convergent molecular selection during equine domestication.** `[Well Supported]` — Sequence alignment (Supplementary Fig. 19) proves that the donkey 1 bp deletion lies 25 bp downstream of the horse `non-Dun1` SNP and inside the 1,609 bp horse `non-Dun2` deletion region, demonstrating independent mutational origins targeting the same cis-regulatory element.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data & Code Availability:** Public accessions for previously sequenced individuals (`PRJCA001131`, `SRR1562345`, `ERR650932`–`ERR650969`, `ERR650540`–`ERR650703`, `SRA082086`), software versions, command-line parameters for Hi-C (`HiC-Pro`, `Juicer`, `3d-dna`), GATK filtering thresholds, PSMC/SMC++ parameters, `ms` simulation parameters (Supplementary Table 23), Sanger/qPCR primer sequences, and antibody catalog numbers (`Bioss bs-10266R`) are thoroughly documented, alongside extensive Supplementary Data 1–12 and Source Data files.
