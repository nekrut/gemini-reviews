### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This molecular surveillance study evaluates antimalarial drug resistance markers (*Pfcrt*, *Pfmdr-1*, and *PfK-13*) via Sanger sequencing and parasite genetic diversity markers (*Pfmsp-1*, *Pfmsp-2*, and *Pfglurp*) via allele-specific nested PCR and agarose gel sizing in *Plasmodium falciparum* clinical isolates collected from symptomatic patients in Tripura, Northeast India. Out of 242 febrile patients screened, 84 were malaria-positive (~71 *P. falciparum* mono-infections and 4 mixed infections). Because of variable amplification and sequencing success across loci, downstream sample sizes fluctuate substantially: $n = 64$ for *Pfmdr-1*, $n = 46$ for *Pfcrt*, $n = 48$ for *PfK-13*, $n = 52$ for *Pfmsp-1*/*Pfmsp-2*/*Pfglurp*, and $n = 35$ for the combined polyclonality-versus-resistance correlation. While the primary GenBank accessions are provided and the baseline surveillance data are useful for regional tracking, the manuscript suffers from severe tabular corruption, internal statistical contradictions in its contingency table tests, low statistical power due to sample attrition, and low-resolution agarose gel fragment sizing.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
* **Severe Tabular Corruption and Misaligned Columns (Table 1 & Table 2):** In Table 1, the header defines 9 columns (`Genes`, `Allele frequency (n = 52)`, `Fragment size range (bp)`, `Number of genotypes`, `Monoclonal infection (%) n = 52`, `Polyclonal infection (%) n = 52`, `chi-square p-value`, `MOI (n = 52)`, `H_E`), but the row for *Pfmsp-1* only contains 6 cells (`Pfmsp-1 | 30 (57.7%) | 22 (42.3%) | 0.000086 | 1.56 | 0.89`), placing monoclonal/polyclonal counts under allele frequency and fragment size headers, and omitting the family-level summaries. Furthermore, the $\chi^2$ $p$-value ($0.000086$) is reported without specifying whether it tests goodness-of-fit across the three genes or 50:50 binomial proportions within *Pfmsp-1* (a 30 vs. 22 split on $n=52$ yields $\chi^2 = 1.23, p = 0.27$, whereas comparing monoclonal vs. polyclonal across the three loci $30/22$, $36/16$, $49/3$ yields $\chi^2 = 19.3, p = 0.000064$).
* **Invalid Statistical Testing in Age-Stratified MOI Comparisons (Table 2):** Table 2 purports to test the "Difference in Multiplicity of infection (MOI) between adults and children" ($n = 31$ children vs. $n = 21$ adults), yet reports $p$-values for individual allelic families (`K1` $p = 0.5512$, `MAD20` $p = 1.0$, `RO33` $p = 0.3753$) rather than comparing the continuous/ordinal MOI distributions (e.g., via Mann-Whitney U or Poisson regression). Worse, for *Pfglurp*, children have 34 alleles across 31 isolates ($\text{MOI} = 1.10$, i.e., 28 monoclonal and 3 polyclonal isolates) and adults have 21 alleles across 21 isolates ($\text{MOI} = 1.00$, i.e., 21 monoclonal and 0 polyclonal isolates). A Fisher's exact test comparing 3/31 vs. 0/21 polyclonal infections gives $p = 0.264$, yet Table 2 reports $p = 0.0053$, which is mathematically impossible for a 1.1 vs. 1.0 MOI difference on $n=52$ and appears to stem from mistakenly feeding raw allele counts (34 vs. 21) into a 1-way goodness-of-fit test without normalizing by group size (31 vs. 21).
* **Underpowered Subgroup Analysis & Over-Interpretation of Non-Significant Trends (Table 3):** Only 35 of 75 *P. falciparum*-containing isolates had complete data across both diversity and resistance markers (53% attrition). In Table 3, *Pfcrt* mutations occur in 21/22 (95.5%) polyclonal vs. 10/13 (76.9%) monoclonal isolates ($p = 0.265$), and *Pfmdr-1* mutations occur in 3/22 (13.6%) vs. 1/13 (7.7%) ($p = 1.0$). Despite these completely null results, the Discussion claims that "The current study documented a high rate of polyclonal infections with comparatively higher rates of mutation in *Pfcrt* and *Pfmdr-1* genes as compared to that observed in monoclonal infections."
* **Haplotype Diversity and LD Calculation Flaws:** The Results state that *Pfcrt* had 40 CVIET and 6 CVMNK sequences across 46 isolates, reporting haplotype diversity $H = 0.232$. Because all 4 SNPs in *Pfcrt* (codons 72, 74, 75, 76—or 4 polymorphic sites) are completely linked in just two haplotypes (40 CVIET vs. 6 CVMNK), all pairwise intragenic $r^2$ values within *Pfcrt* are trivially $r^2 = 1.0$. Moreover, since Sanger sequencing was performed directly on bulk PCR products when 53.85% of isolates were polyclonal, mixed resistance/wild-type chromatograms were either uncalled or collapsed into a single dominant call without phasing, biasing haplotype and LD estimates.
* **Low Resolution of Agarose Gel Sizing:** Using EtBr-stained agarose gels with 20 bp (*msp1*/*msp2*) and 50 bp (*glurp*) binning intervals systematically underestimates true allelic diversity and MOI compared to capillary electrophoresis or amplicon sequencing.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality (STRENGTHS)
* **Strengths:** The study provides primary molecular surveillance data from an under-sampled border state (Tripura, Northeast India), employs nested PCR and Sanger sequencing for three major antimalarial resistance loci (*Pfcrt*, *Pfmdr-1*, *PfK-13*), and deposits all 158 sequence records in NCBI GenBank.
* **Claim 1 (High prevalence [87%] of *Pfcrt* CVIET triple-mutant chloroquine-resistant genotypes despite official CQ withdrawal):** `[Well Supported]` — Directly supported by Sanger sequencing of 46 isolates (40 CVIET, 6 CVMNK) and consistent with persistent fixation or informal CQ use in the region.
* **Claim 2 (Absence of *PfK-13* propeller domain mutations in the sampled cohort):** `[Moderately Supported]` — Supported in the 48 sequenced isolates, though sample size ($n=48$) is only powered to rule out mutations at $\ge 6\%$ prevalence (with 95% confidence) and no ring-stage survival assay or clinical follow-up was performed.
* **Claim 3 (Higher polyclonality in adults vs. children and higher *Pfglurp* MOI in children):** `[Fragile]` — The overall adult vs. child polyclonality comparison ($15/21$ vs. $13/31$, $p = 0.0494$ by $\chi^2$ without Yates continuity correction; Fisher's exact test two-tailed $p = 0.0501$) is borderline on $n=52$, and the *Pfglurp* $p = 0.0053$ in Table 2 is a statistical artifact of unnormalized count testing.
* **Claim 4 (Polyclonal infections exhibit higher rates of *Pfcrt* and *Pfmdr-1* drug resistance mutations):** `[Overstated]` — Directly contradicted by Table 3 ($p = 0.265$ and $p = 1.0$ on $n = 35$).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Data Availability:** Good for sequence data; GenBank accession ranges are explicitly provided for *Pfmdr-1* (`MG641959.1–MG642022.1`, $n=64$), *Pfcrt* (`MH823820.1–MH823865.1`, $n=46$), and *PfK-13* (`MG366541.1–MG366588.1`, $n=48$).
* **Metadata & Raw Gel Data:** Individual isolate-level metadata linking patient age/sex, gel fragment sizes, and sequencing accessions are not provided, preventing independent verification of the $n=35$ subset in Table 3.
