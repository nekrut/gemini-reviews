### 1. Executive Summary
This study combines genome-wide association mapping (Illumina OvineSNP50 BeadChip), Bayesian haplotype phasing (PHASE), targeted candidate gene and 6 kb promoter resequencing, large-scale cohort validation (KASPar genotyping), and in vitro functional signaling assays (BRE-luciferase reporter in human granulosa COV434 cells) to identify the causal mutations underlying hyperprolificacy in two sheep breeds: French Grivette (litter size, LS) and Polish Olkuska (ovulation rate, OR). Initial case-control GWAS in 39 Grivette ewes (28 cases, 11 controls; 47,290 SNPs) and 63 Olkuska ewes (29 cases, 34 controls; 46,451 SNPs) localized chromosome-wide and genome-wide association peaks to a 1.3 Mb haplotype on the X chromosome (`OARX`: 48.24–49.53 Mb) adjacent to *BMP15*. Sequencing *BMP15* identified two novel non-conservative missense variants in conserved mature-peptide motifs: `c.950C>T` (p.T317I, named $FecX^{Gr}$) in Grivette and `c.1009A>C` (p.N337H, named $FecX^O$) in Olkuska. Validation in 360 Grivette ewes (plus 27 with laparoscopy OR) and 137 Olkuska ewes (103 with laparoscopy OR) demonstrated that homozygous mutant ewes ($FecX^{Gr}/FecX^{Gr}$ LS $= 2.50 \pm 0.65$ vs. $1.93 \pm 0.42$ heterozygous; $FecX^O/FecX^O$ OR $= 3.28 \pm 0.85$ vs. $2.02 \pm 0.47$ heterozygous, $p < 10^{-3}$) are fertile and hyperprolific—a striking departure from all six previously known ovine *BMP15* mutations, which cause primary ovarian failure/sterility in homozygotes. In vitro BRE-luciferase assays in COV434 granulosa cells confirmed that p.T317I abolishes and p.N337H reduces (~50%) BMP15 homodimer signaling. This is an exemplary forward-genetics and functional validation study, with only minor omissions in kinship adjustment during the discovery GWAS and a typographical error in the Results description of the N337H co-transfection control.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Two-Stage Genetic Discovery & Independent Replication:** While the initial discovery GWAS used small extreme-phenotype case-control cohorts ($N = 39$ Grivette, $N = 63$ Olkuska) analyzed via Fisher's exact test with Bonferroni correction (and $n = 1,000$ $\max(T)$ permutations for 20-SNP sliding-window haplotypes) without a mixed-model kinship matrix, the authors did not rely solely on the discovery scan. Instead, they sequenced the full coding region and 6 kb 5' regulatory region of *BMP15* and genotyped much larger validation cohorts ($N = 360$ Grivette ewes across 9 flocks; $N = 103$–$137$ Olkuska ewes with $\ge 3$ repeated laparoscopies), providing unambiguous statistical confirmation ($p < 10^{-3}$ by one-way ANOVA with Newman–Keuls post-hoc test).
- **In Vitro Functional Assay Rigor:** Site-directed mutagenesis of full-length human *BMP15* cDNA (hBMP15 T316I and N336H, homologous to ovine T317I and N337H) was tested across 3 independent experiments in triplicate in human granulosa-derived COV434 cells stably expressing a BRE-luciferase reporter, alongside empty vector negative controls, rhBMP15 (100 ng/mL) positive controls, WT hBMP15, and 1:1 WT/mutant co-transfections mimicking heterozygotes.
- **Minor Textual Typo in Results (Line 102):** In describing the $FecX^O$ (N337H) rescue experiment, line 102 states: *"Concerning the BMP15 N337H mutation, it altered also the BMP15 signaling by half-reducing its activity (p<1E−02) and this was totally rescued by the BMP15 T317I/WT cotransfection."* Referring to Figure 5, this should clearly read *"BMP15 N337H/WT cotransfection"* rather than *"T317I/WT"*.
- **Minor Sample Count Discrepancies in Table 3:** In Table 3, the Olkuska Control genotype counts sum to $28 + 7 + 0 = 35$ (and $8+27=35$, $3+32=35$, $34+1=35$, $16+19=35$), whereas the GWAS text (lines 42, 164) states that 63 Olkuska ewes (29 cases, 34 controls) passed SNP chip QC (out of 64 initially genotyped: 29 cases, 35 controls, line 150). Thus, Table 3 includes the 1 control ewe that was excluded from the SNP50 chip scan due to call rate $<0.98$ but successfully Sanger-sequenced for *BMP15*.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim: Two novel *BMP15* missense mutations ($FecX^{Gr}$ T317I and $FecX^O$ N337H) cause hyperprolificacy in homozygous ewes without inducing sterility** — `[Well Supported]`. The convergence of X-chromosome linkage/association, haplotype clustering, coding/promoter sequencing, large-cohort genotype-phenotype segregation for both LS and laparoscopic OR, evolutionary conservation, and in vitro granulosa cell signaling assays establishes causality beyond reasonable doubt.
- **Claim: Impaired BMP15 signaling in COV434 granulosa cells demonstrates loss/reduction of BMP15 homodimer activity** — `[Well Supported]`. The BRE-luciferase results clearly show complete abrogation of SMAD1/5/8-mediated BRE activation by T317I and ~50% reduction by N337H.
- **Claim: Preservation of BMP15/GDF9 heterodimer signaling or TAK1 signaling explains why $FecX^{Gr}/FecX^{Gr}$ ewes remain fertile despite null BRE-luciferase activity** — `[Moderately Supported]`. The authors appropriately frame this (Figure 6 and Discussion lines 125–130) as a mechanistic hypothesis to be tested in future GDF9 co-expression/secretion and Western blot experiments.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reagent & Sequence Completeness:** All PCR, sequencing, KASPar genotyping, and site-directed mutagenesis primers are provided in Table S3; 5' regulatory polymorphisms are listed in Table S2; protein accessions and OARv2.0 genomic coordinates are explicitly stated.
- **Data Availability:** Raw OvineSNP50 BeadChip genotype files are not deposited in a public repository, though summary statistics and genotype counts across all discovery and validation cohorts are fully reported in the manuscript and figures.
