### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This study reports the complete chloroplast (cp) genome sequence of the economically important neotropical woody bamboo *Guadua angustifolia* (Bambusoideae: Bambuseae: Guaduinae; $135,331\text{ bp}$, GenBank `KM365071`), obtained by Sanger sequencing of overlapping PCR amplicons generated with 138 universal grass cpDNA primer pairs, and compares it with the neotropical herbaceous bamboo *Cryptochloa strictiflora* (Olyreae) and the paleotropical woody bamboo *Dendrocalamus latiflorus* (Bambuseae). The authors further assemble a 62-taxon Poaceae complete cp genome alignment (`89,529 bp`) for Maximum Likelihood (`RAxML`), Maximum Parsimony (`PAUP*`), and Bayesian (`MrBayes`) phylogenomic reconstruction, and perform a 46-taxon `BEAST` relaxed molecular clock analysis to estimate divergence times. The phylogenomic trees robustly resolve Bambusoideae as sister to Pooideae (100% bootstrap, 1.00 PP) rather than Ehrhartoideae, confirm *G. angustifolia* as sister to paleotropical woody bamboos (Bambuseae monophyly under plastid history), and estimate a recent divergence ($\sim 2.94\text{ Mya}$) between *G. angustifolia* and *C. strictiflora*. While the phylogenomic analysis across 62 grasses is well executed, the manuscript contains multiple internal numerical contradictions between the main text and tables regarding SNP counts (`110` vs. `130`), total gene counts (`131` vs. `128`), and intergenic spacer lengths (`46,938` vs. `46,846 bp`), and relies on low-depth (`1.7-fold`) Sanger sequencing to call rare species-specific SNPs.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Multiple Internal Numerical Contradictions Between Text and Tables:**
  - **Contradictory SNP Counts in Consecutive Sentences (Line 65):** Under *"Comparative study of chloroplast genomes in bamboos"*, the text states: *"Comparison of sequence differences of the two native American bamboos G. angustifolia and C. strictiflora yielded 99.6% sequence identity with a total of 153 sequence variations (**110 SNPs**, 41 indels and two inversions). Amongst the **130 SNPs** detected, 57% corresponded to transitions and 43% to transversions."* Reporting `110 SNPs` in one sentence and `130 SNPs` in the immediately following sentence (for the exact same pairwise comparison) is a glaring editing/accounting error.
  - **Contradictory Total Gene Counts (Line 63 vs. Table 1):** Line 63 states *"yielding a total of **131 genes** in this newly determined chloroplast genome"*, whereas Table 1 (`GA` column) lists `No. of genes = 128` (`83` protein genes + `37` tRNA genes + `8` rRNA genes = `128`). Even adding the 2 pseudogenes (`ycf1` and `rps19`) yields `130`, not `131`.
  - **Contradictory Intergenic Spacer Lengths (Line 99 vs. Table 1):** In the Discussion (Line 99), the authors state that intergenic spacer lengths are *"46,938 bp for G. angustifolia and 46,846 bp for C. strictiflora"*, whereas Table 1 reports `Spacer length (bp) = 46,846` for `GA` (*G. angustifolia*) and `46,893` for `CS` (*C. strictiflora*)—swapping the `46,846` value between the two species and introducing an unexplained `46,938` figure!
- **Low Sequencing Coverage (`1.7-fold`) for Variant Discovery:**
  - The entire $135.3\text{-kb}$ plastome was assembled from Sanger reads with an average coverage depth of only $1.7\times$ (Line 61; minimum $1\times$, maximum $5\times$). In regions covered by only a single unidirectional Sanger read ($1\times$) amplified by non-proofreading or standard *Taq* PCR, PCR polymerase errors or base-calling artifacts cannot be distinguished from genuine species-specific SNPs (such as the 110–130 SNPs reported against *C. strictiflora*).
- **Circularity / Secondary Calibration in `BEAST` Divergence Dating:**
  - In Methods (Line 132), the `BEAST` molecular clock calibration uses normal prior distributions ($47 \pm 4\text{ Mya}$ for the BEP crown and $29 \pm 4\text{ Mya}$ for Bambusoideae) derived from secondary molecular estimates rather than primary fossil constraints with lognormal offset priors, and estimates a recent divergence of $2.94\text{ Mya}$ between a woody bamboo (*G. angustifolia*, tribe Bambuseae) and an herbaceous bamboo (*C. strictiflora*, tribe Olyreae) while placing *G. angustifolia* closer to paleotropical woody bamboos ($19.04\text{ Mya}$ in Text Line 108 vs. $2.94\text{ Mya}$ pairwise comparison)—reflecting a confusion in the text between the crown divergence of Bambuseae vs. Olyreae ($\sim 28\text{ Mya}$ in Figure 5) and a much younger node! Indeed, looking at Line 86 vs. Line 108, the authors state in Line 86 that *"Estimated divergence time between the two neotropical bamboos G. angustifolia and C. strictiflora was fairly recent at 2.94 Mya"*, which is topologically impossible if *G. angustifolia* is sister to *D. latiflorus* (`DL`, paleotropical woody bamboo) and *C. strictiflora* (`CS`, Olyreae) is in the sister tribe! In a rooted tree where `((GA, DL), CS)` holds, the divergence time $T(\text{GA}, \text{CS})$ must be strictly **older** than $T(\text{GA}, \text{DL})$, yet the authors report $T(\text{GA}, \text{CS}) = 2.94\text{ Mya}$ and $T(\text{GA}, \text{DL}) = 19.04\text{ Mya}$! This indicates a misreading of the `BEAST` node ages or taxon labels in Figure 5.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality / STRENGTHS
- **[Well Supported] Sister Relationship of Bambusoideae and Pooideae Within the BEP Clade:** The 62-taxon complete plastome alignment (`89,529 bp`) provides decisive support (100% ML/MP bootstrap, 1.00 Bayesian posterior probability) for `(Ehrhartoideae, (Bambusoideae, Pooideae))`, resolving long-standing single-gene conflicts.
- **[Well Supported] Plastome Structural Conservation Across Woody and Herbaceous Bamboos:** The demonstration of conserved gene order, IR boundaries (including the Poaceae-specific `ndhH` 198-bp IR extension), and repetitive structure across neotropical and paleotropical bamboos is solid.
- **[Methodologically Flawed] Pairwise Divergence Time Reporting Between `GA`, `DL`, and `CS`:** As audited above, reporting that *G. angustifolia* (`GA`) and *C. strictiflora* (`CS`, in sister tribe Olyreae) diverged $2.94\text{ Mya}$ while `GA` and *D. latiflorus* (`DL`, its sister taxon in tribe Bambuseae) diverged $19.04\text{ Mya}$ violates ultrametric tree topology (`((GA, DL), CS)` cannot have $T(\text{GA}, \text{CS}) < T(\text{GA}, \text{DL})$).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Reproducibility Assessment:** The annotated *G. angustifolia* chloroplast genome is deposited in GenBank (`KM365071`), and all 61 comparative GenBank accession numbers are listed in Supplementary Table S2. Primer sequences (`Supplementary Table S1`) and phylogenetic parameters (`RAxML`, `PAUP*`, `MrBayes 3.1.2`, `BEAST 1.8.0`) are well documented.
