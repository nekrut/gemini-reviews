### 1. Executive Summary
This study characterizes an *Arabidopsis thaliana* nuclear RNA-binding protein (`AtRRM`, `At5g10350`, a `PABPN`-family member containing coiled-coil and RNA recognition motif domains) initially identified in a yeast two-hybrid (Y2H) library screen as an interactor of the C-terminal extension (`CTE`, aa 958–1123) of the telomerase catalytic subunit `AtTERT` (`At5g16850`). The authors examine `AtRRM` homodimerization and interaction with `AtTERT` constructs via Y2H and bimolecular fluorescence complementation (BiFC) in *Arabidopsis* leaf and tobacco BY-2 protoplasts, screen an *Arabidopsis* cDNA `cYFP` library by BiFC to identify five putative partner proteins (`MT2A`, and fragments of `MOS1`, `G2p`, `OZF2`, and `HSP70-1`), analyze two T-DNA insertion alleles (`rrm-1` `SALK_096285` null and `rrm-2` `SALK_116646C` partial knockdown) for telomere length (TRF) and telomerase activity (TRAP), and perform RT-qPCR and public expression co-regulation queries across 34 candidate genes harboring promoter `telobox` motifs (`AAACCCT`). While the characterization of `AtRRM` homodimerization via its C-terminal region (`RRM-3`, aa 170–217) and the honest reporting of negative telomere phenotypes in `rrm` mutants are commendable, the study suffers from high assay discordance between BiFC and Y2H/co-IP, reliance on only two biological replicates without multiple-testing correction across 34 RT-qPCR targets, and speculative claims regarding non-telomeric functions of `AtTERT`.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Assay Discordance Between BiFC, Y2H, and Co-IP:** Although full-length `AtRRM` interacts with `AtTERT` `CTE` and `RRM-3` in protoplast BiFC (Figure 1B), all Y2H pairings between `AtRRM` constructs (`RRM`, `RRM-1`, `RRM-2`, `RRM-3`) and `AtTERT` fragments failed to grow even on low-stringency `-LW` or `-LWH` plates, and in vitro TNT rabbit reticulocyte lysate co-immunoprecipitation also failed to detect direct binding. Similarly, among the five BiFC library screen hits (`MT2A`, `MOS1`, `G2p`, `OZF2`, `HSP70-1`), Y2H only confirmed `OZF2-AD` + `RRM-BD` (`MT2A` and `G2p` were negative in Y2H, `OZF2-BD` autoactivated, `HSP70-1-AD` failed to express in yeast, and `MOS1` could not be cloned full-length). Because split-YFP reconstitution in transient protoplast overexpression is irreversible and prone to trapping transient or non-specific bystander encounters inside the nucleus/nucleolus, unconfirmed BiFC positives without co-IP or endogenous pull-down validation remain fragile.
- **T-DNA Mutant Allele Limitations & Underpowered TRF Analysis:** `rrm-1` (`SALK_096285`, intron 2 insertion) is a complete transcriptional knockout, whereas `rrm-2` (`SALK_116646C`, intron 1 insertion) retains ~25–30% residual `AtRRM` transcript in G1 and G2 plants (`p < 0.01`, `p < 0.05`) and shows high plant-to-plant heterogeneity in G3 (`p = 0.0987`, non-significant knockdown). Terminal restriction fragment (TRF) telomere length analysis in G3 was performed on only $n = 3$ biological replicates per genotype, yielding borderline two-tailed unpaired Student's $t$-test $p$-values (`rrm-1` $p = 0.0575$, `rrm-2` $p = 0.0656$; mean shifts $< 200\text{ bp}$ out of $2.5\text{–}4.5\text{ kb}$). With $n = 3$, statistical power to rule out subtle telomere maintenance defects is low.
- **Multiple Testing & Replication in RT-qPCR Screening:** In Section 2.4 (`Table S3`), the authors test 34 candidate genes across multiple genotypes (`rrm-1`, `rrm-2`, `tert`) and tissues (7-day seedlings and 6-week leaves) using *"at least two biological replicates in three technical replicates"* (`n >= 2`) and unadjusted unpaired two-tailed $t$-tests ($\Delta\text{Ct}$ normalized to `AtUbi10`). Running >130 unadjusted two-sample $t$-tests at $n = 2$ biological replicates guarantees numerous false-positive $p < 0.05$ calls and explains why transcript shifts between `rrm-1` and `rrm-2` are frequently discordant (e.g., in leaves, only `AT2G45740`/`PEX11D` and `AT5G62190`/`PRH75` change significantly in the same direction across both alleles).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **`[Well Supported]` `AtRRM` (`At5g10350`) is a nuclear/nucleolar protein that homodimerizes via its C-terminal domain (`aa 170–217`):** Subcellular localization with `RFP` nuclear markers (`Fib1`, `SRp30`, `H2B`) and Y2H/BiFC domain deletion mapping (`RRM-1`, `RRM-2`, `RRM-3`) consistently show that the C-terminal 48 residues mediate homodimerization and are required for nuclear retention.
- **`[Well Supported]` Loss of `AtRRM` does not measurably alter bulk telomere length or in vitro telomerase TRAP activity:** Both `rrm-1` and `rrm-2` exhibit normal morphology, fertility, telomere repeat arrays, and telomerase extension activity across three generations.
- **`[Fragile]` `AtRRM` forms physiologically relevant in vivo complexes with `AtTERT`, `MT2A`, `MOS1`, `G2p`, `OZF2`, and `HSP70-1`:** With the exception of `OZF2` in one Y2H orientation, these interactions rely strictly on transient protoplast split-YFP complementation, where four of the library hits were isolated as truncated fragments (`MOS1`, `G2p`, `OZF2`, `HSP70-1`).
- **`[Overstated]` `AtRRM` and `AtTERT` co-regulate non-telomeric ribosome biogenesis and stress pathways via promoter `telobox` motifs:** Inferring a shared non-telomeric regulatory function from mild (`1.3`–`2.5`-fold), largely non-overlapping RT-qPCR fluctuations in a hand-picked set of 34 `telobox`-containing genes—without ChIP-qPCR or RNA-IP demonstrating `AtRRM` or `AtTERT` occupancy at those loci—over-interprets noisy transcriptomic correlations.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Strengths:** Locus identifiers (`At5g10350`, `At5g16850`, and all 34 target genes), SALK T-DNA stock numbers (`SALK_096285`, `SALK_116646C`), domain boundaries (`aa 1–64`, `1–169`, `170–217`), BiFC/Y2H vector systems (`pSAT1/pSAT4`, `pGBKT7/pGADT7`), and RT-qPCR cycling parameters are clearly documented.
- **Deficiencies:** All primer sequences (`Table S1`), Y2H autoactivation/expression summaries (`Table S2`), numerical $\Delta\Delta\text{Ct}$ values (`Table S3`), and GENEVESTIGATOR co-expression matrices (`Table S4`) are relegated to unbundled supplementary files not included in the main text. Exact sample sizes ($n$) for individual RT-qPCR bars should be stated explicitly rather than as *"at least two biological replicates."*
