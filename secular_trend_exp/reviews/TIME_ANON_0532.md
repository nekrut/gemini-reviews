### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This molecular and cellular biology study investigates the post-transcriptional translational control of the two major mRNA isoforms of the human ribonucleotide reductase small subunit (`RR M2` / `RRM2`), which are transcribed from two alternative promoters yielding a short (`63-nt`, `SM2`) and a long (`222-nt`, `LM2`) 5'-untranslated region (`5'-UTR`). Examining the `222-nt` `LM2` 5'-UTR reveals four upstream `AUG` triplets (`uAUG1–4`) that lie in the same open reading frame as the canonical physiological `AUG` start codon without intervening stop codons. Using custom peptide antibodies against the putative N-terminally extended `49-kDa` isoform (`antiLM2`) and internal `43-kDa` M2 sequence (`antiSM2`), cell-free rabbit reticulocyte lysate (`RRL`) translation, and cationic liposome-mediated transfection of capped, polyadenylated (`A30`) firefly luciferase reporter mRNAs (`pCRL-Luc-A30` vs. `pCRS-Luc-A30`, co-transfected with `β-galactosidase` control mRNA) in HeLa cells, the authors show that no `49-kDa` extended M2 protein is detectable in vivo (`Fig. 1B`) and that the `222-nt` 5'-UTR strongly represses translation (`Fig. 1E`, `Fig. 2C`). Serial 5'-truncation (`LM2D1–D6`, `Fig. 3`) and site-directed point mutagenesis of the four `uAUG` codons to `UUC` (`LM2U1–U4`, `Fig. 4`)—paired with `SYBR Green` RT-qPCR verification of equal reporter mRNA stability at 8 h post-transfection (`Fig. 3D`, `Fig. 4D`)—demonstrate that the four in-frame `uAUGs` act as potent cis-acting translational repressors independent of overall 5'-UTR length or `ΔG`. Finally, cleaving `eIF4G` via transient poliovirus `2A^pro` expression (`pCMV2A`) or 4-hour `TRAIL` (`250 ng/ml`) treatment in HeLa cells derepresses `LM2`-luciferase translation `20–30`-fold (`Fig. 5–6`) and increases endogenous de novo `[35S]methionine`-labeled M2 protein synthesis without altering `M2` mRNA levels (`RNase protection assay`, `Fig. 7–8`).

### 2. STRENGTHS
- **Direct mRNA Transfection and Systematic Point Mutagenesis With Stability Controls:** By transfecting in vitro synthesized, `m7GpppG`-capped, polyadenylated (`A30`) reporter transcripts directly into HeLa cells along with capped `β-galactosidase` control mRNA and quantifying intracellular reporter RNA persistence at 8 hours by RT-qPCR (`Fig. 3D`, `Fig. 4D`, `Fig. 6`), the authors cleanly uncouple translational efficiency from cryptic promoter splicing, nuclear export artifacts, and mRNA decay.
- **Orthogonal Validation of Endogenous M2 De Novo Translation:** Combining luciferase reporter assays (`Fig. 5`) with endogenous Western blotting, `GAPDH`-controlled RNase protection assays (`Fig. 7B`, `Fig. 8B`), and `[35S]methionine` pulse-labeling immunoprecipitation (`Fig. 7C`, `Fig. 8C`) under two independent `eIF4G`-cleaving perturbations (`poliovirus 2A^pro` and `TRAIL`) provides strong evidence that endogenous `M2` synthesis increases when cap-dependent scanning is impaired.

### 3. Methodological & Statistical Audit / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Direct Cleavage of `eIF4G` Not Shown by Immunoblotting (`"data not shown"`):** A central mechanistic premise of `Figures 5–8` is that poliovirus `2A^pro` transfection and 4-hour `TRAIL` (`250 ng/ml`) treatment relieve `uAUG`-mediated inhibition specifically by proteolytically cleaving `eIF4G`. However, the authors never show an anti-`eIF4G` Western blot confirming the extent or kinetics of `eIF4G` cleavage in their HeLa cells (citing `"2A^pro cleavage of eIF4G (data not shown)"` in Line 108 and relying on `[35S]methionine` global incorporation drop in `Fig. 5A` and literature citation `(32)` for `TRAIL`). Because both `2A^pro` and `TRAIL` cleave multiple cellular substrates (e.g., `PABP`, nuclear pore proteins, caspases, `eIF4B`), attributing `LM2` derepression exclusively to `eIF4G` cleavage without direct `eIF4G` Western blots or cleavage-resistant `eIF4G` rescue is indirect.
- **Error-Prone `ΔCt` Formula Notation and Absence of Statistical Tests:** In Materials and Methods (Line 80), relative luciferase RNA level is written as `"The relative luciferase RNA level = 2^ΔCt"` (without a minus sign, `2^-ΔCt`, or `ΔΔCt` reference to control). Furthermore, throughout `Figures 2–6`, bar graphs of luciferase activity and RT-qPCR RNA levels are presented without stating the number of biological replicates (`n`), whether error bars represent SD or SEM, or any statistical hypothesis tests / p-values.
- **RNA (`U`) vs. DNA (`T`) Base Mixed Notation in PCR Primers:** In Materials and Methods (Line 44), twelve PCR mutagenesis oligodeoxynucleotide primer sequences are printed containing uracil (`U`) bases (`"...AGGCAUGGCA-3'"`, `"...CAGCCAAUGGGA..."`, `"...TCCGAAUGGCTG..."`) mixed with thymine (`T`) bases in the same DNA primers.
- **Editorial Typo in Figure 2 Text:** Line 94 states `"In vitro transcripts with both 5'-caps and 5'-poly(A) tails were generated"`, where `"5'-poly(A) tails"` is a typo for `"3'-poly(A) tails"`.

### 4. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** The `63-nt` short 5'-UTR (`SM2`) of human `RRM2` is translated with high efficiency, whereas the `222-nt` long 5'-UTR (`LM2`) is translationally repressed by four in-frame upstream `AUG` codons without producing a detectable `49-kDa` N-terminally extended protein in HeLa cells. `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated by `antiLM2`/`antiSM2` immunoblotting (`Fig. 1B`), RRL translation (`Fig. 1E`), RNA-transfection reporter assays (`Fig. 2`), deletion mapping (`Fig. 3`), and `uAUG -> UUC` point mutagenesis (`Fig. 4`).
- **Claim 2:** Expression of poliovirus `2A^pro` or `TRAIL` treatment relieves `uAUG`-mediated inhibition of the long `M2` 5'-UTR (`20–30`-fold) and stimulates endogenous `M2` protein synthesis without changing `M2` mRNA abundance. `[Well Supported]` — Supported by reporter assays (`Fig. 5–6`), Western blots, RNase protection assays, and `[35S]methionine` pulse-IP (`Fig. 7–8`), though direct proof that `eIF4G` cleavage alone is sufficient awaits `eIF4G` rescue experiments.
- **Claim 3:** Fine-tuning translation from the long 5'-UTR regulates `M2` expression during the S phase of normal cell proliferation. `[Overstated]` — The study never examines cell-cycle synchronized cells or S-phase translation of `LM2`; extrapolating from poliovirus `2A^pro` and `TRAIL`-induced apoptosis to normal S-phase DNA replication in the Abstract (Line 7) and Discussion (Line 139) is speculative.

### 5. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
