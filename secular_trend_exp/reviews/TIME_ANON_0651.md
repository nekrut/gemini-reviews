### 1. Executive Summary (SUMMARY)
This study isolates and sequences a full-length cDNA clone encoding the $M_r$ 78,000 intermediate chain (`IC78`, predicted 683 amino acids, 76.5 kDa) of the *Chlamydomonas reinhardtii* flagellar outer arm dynein—a subunit previously shown by zero-length crosslinking to sit at the base of the dynein particle and contact $\alpha$-tubulin in situ. Comparative sequence analysis demonstrates that `IC78` is homologous in its C-terminal half to the $M_r$ 69,000 outer arm dynein intermediate chain (`IC69` / `ODA6`) and the $M_r$ 74,000 cytoplasmic dynein intermediate chain (`IC74`), while their N-terminal halves diverge. Crucially, the authors discover that the conserved C-terminal half of `IC78` (as well as `IC69` and `IC74`) comprises six imperfect WD-40 ($\beta$-transducin-like) repeats implicated in protein-protein interactions. Using the `IC78` cDNA to screen slow-swimming insertional mutants, they identify one mutant with a large insertion and seven with complete deletions of the `IC78` gene; electron microscopy of three mutants confirms complete loss of the outer dynein arm. RFLP linkage mapping places `IC78` on the left arm of linkage group XII/XIII at the `oda9` locus, and non-complementation in stable vegetative diploids establishes `ODA9` as the structural gene for `IC78`.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit (STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT)
**Strengths:**
- **Convergent Biochemical, Ultrastructural, and Genetic Proof:** The study links a biochemically defined polypeptide (`IC78`) to a classical flagellar motility locus (`oda9`) through four independent lines of evidence: (1) full-length cDNA cloning and sequence characterization (683 aa, 76.5 kDa), (2) insertional mutagenesis yielding 8 independent alleles (1 insertion, 7 deletions) with ultrastructural verification by transmission electron microscopy (TEM) across 3 alleles showing absence of outer dynein arms, (3) RFLP genetic mapping to the left arm of chromosome XII/XIII cosegregating with `oda9`, and (4) formal genetic non-complementation in stable heterozygous diploids.
- **Comparative Structural Bioinformatics:** By aligning `IC78`, `IC69`, and cytoplasmic `IC74` (using VAX/GCG sequence analysis and dot-matrix/motif alignment tools cited: Devereux et al., Vingron & Argos, Lupas et al., van der Voorn & Ploegh, Neer et al.), the authors identify six WD repeats across all three dynein intermediate chains, providing a unifying structural model (propeller/repeat scaffold in the C-terminus, cargo/microtubule-specific N-terminus) for both axonemal and cytoplasmic dynein intermediate chains.

**Weaknesses & Methodological Audit:**
- **Archival Text Truncation in Markdown:** The main text body and figures (referred to as a 1.6 MB PDF in line 12) are absent from the plain-text extraction, preventing direct line-by-line inspection of the sequence alignment tables, RFLP segregation ratios, or GenBank accession numbers in the body text.
- **Lack of Direct Transformation Rescue Reported in Abstract:** While non-complementation in stable diploids between `ic78` insertional deletions and `oda9` strongly supports allelism (and eliminates dominant negative artifacts), direct transformation rescue of `oda9` with the wild-type `IC78` genomic clone (analogous to the cited rescue of `oda6` by Mitchell & Kang) is not described in the abstract summary, leaving a minor formal possibility that the deletions span an adjacent tightly linked gene, although the 1-insertion + 7-deletion allelic series makes this unlikely.
