### 1. Executive Summary
This biochemical and molecular virology study systematically dissects the functional role of the disordered 18-amino-acid C-terminal tail (residues 271–288) of HIV-1 integrase (IN)—the only segment of the C-terminal domain (CTD) unresolved in two-domain crystal structures. The authors construct six progressive 3-residue C-terminal truncation mutants (`1–285`, `1–282`, `1–279`, `1–276`, `1–273`, `1–270`), an SH3-fold $\beta 5$-strand truncation (`1–266`), an active-site catalytic mutant (`D64N`), an `HIV-1/2` chimera replacing residues 271–288 with the 23-residue HIV-2 IN tail, and double mutants pairing the C-terminal truncations with the catalytic core domain solubilizing mutation `F185K`. Using C-terminally $\text{His}_6$-tagged recombinant proteins (which prevent co-purification of C-terminally proteolyzed fragments in *E. coli*, followed by thrombin tag cleavage), the authors quantify $\text{Mg}^{2+}$-dependent 3' processing, oligonucleotide strand transfer, and LEDGF/p75-dependent concerted integration in vitro. In parallel, they introduce the mutations into the single-round `pNLX.Luc(R-)` viral vector (incorporating three stop codons in the overlapping *vif* reading frame and producing VSV-G pseudotyped virions in APOBEC3G-negative 293T cells) to measure infectivity, virion IN incorporation (using NTD/CCD-specific mAb `8G4`), TaqMan late reverse transcription (LRT at 7 h), 2-LTR circle formation (24 h), and nested Alu-R qPCR chromosome integration (24 h).

The experimental design is exceptionally thorough, anticipating and controlling for every major technical pitfall in HIV-1 IN mutagenesis (bacterial C-terminal proteolysis, overlapping *vif* reading frame, CTD antibody epitope loss, plasmid DNA carryover in qPCR, and Class I vs. Class II pleiotropy). The only minor limitation is the small number of independent replicates ($n = 2$) in several biochemical and qPCR assays.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Exemplary Biochemical and Virological Controls:**
  1. *Elimination of C-terminal proteolysis artifacts:* By placing a thrombin-cleavable $\text{His}_6$ tag at the C-terminus rather than the N-terminus, the authors ensure that only intact, unproteolyzed C-terminal tail variants bind Ni-NTA (verified to $\ge 90\%$ homogeneity by Coomassie densitometry and Exonuclease III-free controls in Fig. 2A and 3A), and further verify that the 4-residue `LVPR` thrombin scar does not alter relative 3' processing activity.
  2. *Unconfounded viral genetics and immunodetection:* Stop codons after Vif residue Asn-19 in `pUCWTpol3stop` prevent mutant Vif polypeptides from confounding replication in APOBEC3G-deficient 293T/HeLa-T4 cells; mAb `8G4` (recognizing discontinuous NTD/CCD epitopes) ensures unbiased Western blot detection across CTD truncations (verified against 2 ng purified recombinant protein standards in Fig. 7); and TURBO DNase treatment plus parallel heat-inactivated ($65^\circ\text{C}$, 30 min) virus infections subtract plasmid carryover in LRT, 2-LTR, and Alu-R qPCR assays.
- **Limited Replicate Numbers ($n = 2$) in Selected Assays:** While 3' processing ($n = 4$ for deletion mutants) and viral luciferase infectivity ($n = 5$ independent experiments in duplicate) are well replicated, the oligonucleotide strand transfer (Fig. 4B), LEDGF/p75 concerted integration (Fig. 5B–C), and viral LRT / 2-LTR / Alu-PCR (Fig. 8A–C) datasets are based on $n = 2$ independent experiments (with error bars representing mean $\pm$ SEM of $n = 2$, which equals the half-range).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **[Well Supported]** The distal 9 amino acids of the HIV-1 IN C-terminal tail (residues 280–288) are dispensable for $\text{Mg}^{2+}$-dependent 3' processing, strand transfer, LEDGF/p75-mediated concerted integration, and single-round HIV-1 infectivity.
- **[Well Supported]** Progressive truncation of residues 271–279 (`IN 1–276` and `IN 1–273`) causes a specific Class I-like integration defect (50% and 80% loss of infectivity and Alu-PCR integration with normal LRT and 2-LTR circles), whereas complete tail removal (`IN 1–270`) or swapping the HIV-2 tail (`HIV-1/2`) abolishes concerted integration and infectivity while unveiling a 3- to 4-fold Class II reverse transcription defect.
- **[Well Supported]** Truncation into the fifth $\beta$-strand of the CTD SH3 fold (`IN 1–266`) severely impairs virion IN incorporation and reduces reverse transcription by 95%.
- **[Well Supported]** The `1–279/F185K` and `1–276/F185K` variants retain $\sim 50\text{--}75\%$ of wild-type enzymatic and LEDGF/p75-dependent concerted integration activity while removing half of the disordered C-terminal tail, making them well-validated candidates for X-ray crystallography.
