### 1. Executive Summary
This methodological note evaluates whether aligning short Illumina RNA-seq reads directly to an isolated single-gene genomic locus or isolated transcript isoform sequences (using web-based Galaxy tools: `Minimap2`, `BWA-MEM2`, `Bowtie2`, and `STAR`) can serve as a rapid, lightweight qualitative screen for mRNA isoform expression without mapping to a full reference genome. Using the four-exon homeobox gene *Otx* (encoding *Otx$\alpha$*, *Otx$\beta$*, and *Otx$\beta3$* via alternative first exons/TSSs) in public RNA-seq libraries of the sea urchin *Strongylocentrotus purpuratus* (`PRJNA81157`: ovary, 10 hpf cleavage, 30 hpf gastrula, and adult radial nerve) and validating isoform patterns by RT-qPCR in corresponding stages/tissues of the congeneric species *Strongylocentrotus intermedius*, the authors show that *Otx$\alpha$* is expressed in ovary, cleavage, and gastrula, whereas *Otx$\beta$* is expressed in gastrula and adult radial nerve, and that the annotated 5' TSS of *S. purpuratus* *Otx$\alpha$* lies downstream of the current GenBank prediction. While the paper is honest and proportionate about the severe limitations of single-gene mapping, the proposed bioinformatic shortcut introduces well-known decoy/off-target alignment artifacts, lacks biological replication and statistical testing in the RT-qPCR experiments, and suffered from template truncation where Tables 1 and 2 and Figure legends are missing from the manuscript text.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Fundamental Decoy-Depletion Artifact in Single-Gene Mapping:** When mapping whole-transcriptome RNA-seq reads against a single gene locus (~20–50 kb) or single transcript (~2–4 kb) instead of the full genome/transcriptome decoy index, reads originating from anywhere else in the genome that share micro-homology or low-complexity/repetitive motifs are forced to align to the single target sequence because no true genomic target is present to compete for the alignment score. The authors observe this directly—`Minimap2` and `BWA-MEM2` generated massive non-specific intronic coverage spikes on 20 bp and 76 bp repeats that dwarfed exonic coverage in IGV—and note in Discussion (line 71) that "the runtimes for whole-genome and single-gene mappings are comparable." Since full-genome alignment on Galaxy (`STAR` or `Minimap2` against a pre-indexed genome) takes the same runtime and eliminates off-target decoy artifacts, aligning to an isolated single-gene FASTA is bioinformatically inferior to standard genome alignment followed by BAM region slicing (`samtools view chr:start-end`) to produce small desktop-friendly files.
- **Inability to Resolve Nested TSS Isoforms (*Otx$\beta$* vs. *Otx$\beta3$*) or Quantify Expression:** Because *Otx$\beta3$* initiates inside the *Otx$\beta$*-specific first exon, neither short-read coverage visualization nor standard RT-qPCR can distinguish *Otx$\beta3$* when *Otx$\beta$* is expressed. Furthermore, as the authors acknowledge, unnormalized read depth on a single transcript without library-size scaling or EM multi-mapping resolution (`Salmon`/`Kallisto`/`RSEM`) is strictly qualitative.
- **RT-qPCR Replication and Statistical Deficiencies:**
  - In Section 2.2 (line 39), *S. intermedius* mature ovary RNA was isolated from "one animal" ($n = 1$ biological sample), radial nerves were pooled from 2–3 animals, and embryos were pooled from 2–3 females.
  - In Section 2.4 (line 49), RT-qPCR was analyzed "in at least three technical replicates" using the $\Delta\Delta\text{Ct}$ method normalized to *Ubb*, with no statistical hypothesis testing, error bars definition, or primer efficiency validation reported in the text.
- **Missing Tables and Figure Legends in the Manuscript Text:** Although the text cites `Table 1` (RT-qPCR primers, line 49), `Table 2` (aligner comparison, line 57), and `Figures 1–4`, none of the tables or figure captions are present in the manuscript body.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: *Otx$\alpha$* is maternally and zygotically expressed in ovary, cleavage, and early gastrula, whereas *Otx$\beta$* is expressed in early gastrula and adult radial nerve in both *S. purpuratus* and *S. intermedius*.** `[Well Supported]` — Concordant between whole-genome/transcript RNA-seq mapping in *S. purpuratus*, RT-qPCR in *S. intermedius*, and prior developmental literature in sea urchins.
- **Claim 2: The transcription start site (TSS) of *S. purpuratus* *Otx$\alpha$* is located downstream of the annotated 5' end in GenBank `Gene ID: 373400`.** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by sharp, consistent 5' read-coverage drop-offs across ovary, cleavage, and gastrula RNA-seq libraries, matching published 5' RACE data in *S. purpuratus* and *H. pulcherrimus* and de novo assembled *S. intermedius* egg transcripts.
- **Claim 3: Single-gene/transcript mapping is only suitable for qualitative inspection of simple non-overlapping genes and should not be used for complex multi-isoform genes or quantitative expression without orthogonal confirmation.** `[Well Supported]` — The authors are commendably forthright about every limitation of their approach (intronic repeat artifacts, nested UTR masking, multi-mapping inflation, and complex splicing in kinesin light chain).

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Data Accessions:** Public *S. purpuratus* SRA accessions (`PRJNA81157`: `SRR532046`, `SRR531958`, `SRR531949`, `SRR532074`), reference genome (`GCA_000002235.4`), and newly deposited *S. intermedius* egg RNA-seq reads (`PRJNA686841`) and genomic reads (`PRJNA391452`) are clearly provided, along with exact software versions (`FastQC v0.12.1`, `Minimap2 v2.28`, `BWA-MEM2 v2.3`, `Bowtie2 v2.5.4`, `STAR v2.7.11b`, `BAMTools v2.5.2`).
- **Missing In-Text Tables:** `Table 1` (containing the *S. intermedius* RT-qPCR primer sequences) and `Table 2` were omitted from the formatted manuscript text and must be restored for wet-lab reproducibility.
