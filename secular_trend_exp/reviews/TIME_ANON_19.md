### 1. Executive Summary
This study provides a comprehensive, whole-genome longitudinal analysis of how clinical isolates of human cytomegalovirus (HCMV) mutate and adapt during serial collateral passaging (CP) across three distinct human cell types: fetal foreskin fibroblasts (HFFF-2), retinal pigmented epithelial cells (RPE-1), and primary umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs). By isolating three clinical strains (AF1, U8, U11) free of multi-strain coinfections and passaging them for $\ge 50$ passages alongside an extensively passaged fourth strain (VR1814, up to p214 in fibroblasts and p199 in endothelial cells, plus its BAC-cloned derivatives FIX-BAC / RVFIX), the authors sequence ten complete ~235 kb HCMV genomes at the terminal passages and track the emergence kinetics of mutations across intermediate passages and original clinical specimens. They demonstrate that: (1) `RL13` mutates rapidly via frameshifts, nonsense codons, or deletions across *all* strains and *all* three cell types (fibroblast, epithelial, and endothelial); (2) `UL128L` (`UL128`, `UL130`, `UL131A`) loss-of-function mutations arise reproducibly in fibroblasts (always appearing later than `RL13` mutations) but are selected against in epithelial and endothelial cells; (3) the `UL145–UL140` region mutates in three of four strains; and (4) multistep growth-yield assays of passage-derived `RL13+UL128L+`, `RL13-UL128L+`, and `RL13-UL128L-` virus stocks across all three clinical strains show that `RL13` and `UL128L` independently and additively suppress infectious virion release and plaque formation in fibroblasts.

This is an exceptionally thorough, carefully controlled experimental evolution and viral genomics study. The authors anticipate nearly every potential experimental confound—screening 14 clinical samples to ensure single-strain clonality, running independent re-isolation passage series (U11 Series II and III) plus 96 plasmid clones from the unpassaged clinical specimen to rule out pre-existing >2% minority variants, normalizing viral inocula by immediate-early immunofluorescence (IF) infectious units rather than plaque-forming units, and transparently discussing the limitations of initial fibroblast isolation and mixed-genotype stocks.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Rigorous Control of Clinical Clonality and Pre-Existing Standing Variation:**
  * Because clinical HCMV specimens frequently harbor multiple coinfecting strains (which would confound longitudinal mutation calls with strain sorting during passaging), the authors pre-screened 14 clinical isolates by PCR-cloning and sequencing hypervariable loci `UL146` and `UL139`, selecting only single-strain isolates (AF1, U8, U11).
  * When identical `RL13` and `UL128` mutations appeared in both the fibroblast and epithelial lineages of strain U11 (Series I), the authors did not simply assume pre-existing clinical polymorphism; instead, they conducted two additional experimental evolution series—Series II (branched from p4 isolation stock) and Series III (independent re-isolation from the original clinical urine specimen)—and sequenced 48 plasmid clones each of `RL13` and `UL128` directly from the clinical sample, demonstrating that independent *de novo* frameshift/nonsense mutations arose in Series III and bounding any pre-existing mutant frequency in the clinical sample to $< 2\%$.
* **Careful Normalization in Growth-Yield Assays:**
  * Because `RL13+UL128L+` viruses form plaques ~100-fold less efficiently per IF-positive infected cell than `RL13-UL128L-` viruses, infecting cells at equal PFU would massively overdose the wild-type culture with physical virions. The authors properly titrated and equalized the multiplicity of infection (1 infectious unit/cell) using immediate-early (`IE1/IE2` and `UL44`) immunofluorescence at 3 days p.i., and pelleted/returned detached cells during supernatant sampling so cell-released virus (CRV) was not confounded with detached infected cells.
* **Minor Quantitative Reporting Details:**
  * In Fig. 2 (growth kinetics of CRV and CAV across AF1, U8, and U11), the trajectories are plotted as point estimates without error bars or replicate counts ($n$ of biological or technical titration replicates per timepoint), although the consistency of the $>10$-fold (`RL13-UL128L-` vs. `RL13-UL128L+`) and $>100$-fold (`RL13-UL128L+` vs. `RL13+UL128L+`) separation across three independent genetic backgrounds (AF1, U8, U11) provides strong biological replication.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Universal Selection Against `RL13` Across Fibroblast, Epithelial, and Endothelial Cells vs. Fibroblast-Specific Selection Against `UL128L`: [Well Supported].** Demonstrated across 10 complete genome sequences, 4 independent clinical strains, and multiple collateral passage series (Tables 1–4), with targeted intermediate passage sequencing showing `RL13` inactivation consistently preceding `UL128L` inactivation in fibroblasts.
* **Additive/Independent Suppressive Effects of `RL13` and `UL128L` on HCMV Replication and Release in Fibroblasts: [Well Supported].** Directly demonstrated by the multistep CRV and CAV yield curves and IF-to-plaque ratios across AF1, U8, and U11 (Fig. 2). Moreover, the authors appropriately caveat that passage-derived stocks contain minor proportions of other mutations and note corroboration in a genetically defined Merlin BAC system.
* **A Missense Substitution in `UL130` (`nt 177250`) Attenuates Selection for `UL128L` Ablation in VR1814 / FIX-BAC: [Moderately Supported].** Provides a compelling, parsimonious mechanistic explanation for why VR1814 retained endotheliotropism until p95–p121 and why engineered `UL128L` knockouts in FIX-BAC previously showed muted growth enhancement in the literature; appropriately framed as a hypothesis.
* **Pre-Existing `RL6` and `UL9` Null Mutations in Unpassaged Clinical Samples (AF1 and U11): [Well Supported].** Directly sequenced from the original clinical specimens prior to cell culture adaptation, confirming that RL11-family gene disruptions circulate in vivo.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Strengths:** Tables 1–4 provide exhaustive nucleotide coordinates (referenced to strain Merlin `AY446894`), gene locations, exact nucleotide changes/deletion sizes, coding effects, passage numbers of first detection/predominance, and all intermediate passages screened. Cell line catalog numbers (`HFFF-2 ECACC 86031405`, `RPE-1 Clontech C4000-1`), growth media, centrifugation conditions, and antibodies are meticulously documented.
* **Deficiencies:** While reference accession numbers (`AY446894`, `GQ222018`) are cited in the text, the GenBank accession numbers for the 10 newly sequenced complete terminal-passage genomes (AF1Fp60, AF1Rp50, AF1Ep50, U8Fp65, U8Rp51, U8Ep51, U11Fp63, U11Rp52, VR1814Fp214, VR1814Ep199) are not listed in the main text (likely due to formatting/footnote stripping at the bottom of the manuscript or pending deposition).
