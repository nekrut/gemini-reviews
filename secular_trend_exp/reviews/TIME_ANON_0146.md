### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular oncology study tests the hypothesis that epigenetic silencing via promoter CpG island hypermethylation accounts for the inactivation of the retained *BRCA2* allele in sporadic breast and ovarian cancers (which frequently exhibit chromosome 13q12–q13 loss of heterozygosity [LOH] of $30\text{--}50\%$ despite a near-complete absence of somatic *BRCA2* coding mutations). The authors identify a 1.1 kb CpG island ($-380$ to $+700\text{ bp}$ relative to the transcription start site, $>60\%$ GC content, CpG:GpC ratio $\approx 1:1$) at the 5' end of *BRCA2* (`GenBank Z73360`) and use a `HpaII`/`MspI` methylation-sensitive restriction endonuclease digestion-PCR assay across three `CCGG` sites, multiplexed with an uncut internal control amplicon in *BRCA2* exon 11 and validated against a methylated positive control `CCGG` site in exon 23. Across 64 primary sporadic breast carcinomas and matched peripheral blood leukocytes (49 confirmed by histology to have $\ge 50\%$ tumor cellularity and $30\%$ known to have 13q LOH), 18 breast and ovarian cancer cell lines, an SV40-immortalized normal breast epithelial line (`HB4a`), and four normal tissue types, no methylation is detected at any of the three promoter CpG sites. Competitive RT-PCR (*BRCA2* vs. *actin*) in the cell lines further shows comparable *BRCA2* expression between 13q-homozygous and 13q-heterozygous lines (with one low-expressing ovarian line still unmethylated). This is a clear, well-controlled negative study with candid discussion of its technical scope.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Assay Controls for Restriction Digestion and PCR Efficiency:** Every `HpaII`, `MspI`, and undigested reaction included a co-amplified internal control primer pair targeting a segment of *BRCA2* exon 11 lacking `CCGG` sites (confirming DNA template integrity and PCR amplification in every lane, Figs. 2–3), and assay sensitivity to 5-methylcytosine was positively verified using a constitutively methylated `CCGG` site in *BRCA2* exon 23 (Fig. 3B). Furthermore, histological review verified $\ge 50\%$ tumor cell content in 49/64 primary tumors, and because `HpaII` cleaves unmethylated DNA while leaving methylated alleles intact for exponential PCR amplification, the assay is inherently sensitive to even a minority of methylated tumor alleles within a heterogeneous biopsy.
- **Incomplete Coverage of the CpG Island (`3 / >50` CpGs Assayed):** As the authors transparently acknowledge in the Results and Discussion, the 1.1 kb *BRCA2* CpG island contains over 50 CpG dinucleotides and seven `HpaII`/`MspI` (`CCGG`) sites, of which four failed PCR optimization due to extreme GC content, leaving only three `CCGG` sites ($6\%$ of CpGs) interrogated. Sodium bisulfite genomic sequencing or methylation-specific PCR across the broader promoter was not performed, meaning focal methylation outside these three `CCGG` sites cannot be formally excluded (though CpG island hypermethylation in tumor suppressor genes typically spreads across most CpGs in the island).
- **Incomplete Reporting of Competitive RT-PCR Cell Line Identities:** In the competitive RT-PCR section (line 11) and Figure 4, 18 breast and ovarian cancer cell lines are plotted (and one ovarian cancer cell line is noted to have 10- to 50-fold reduced *BRCA2* transcript using downstream exon primers), but the specific names of the 18 cell lines were corrupted by OCR in the Figure 4 axis labels and are not listed in Materials and Methods.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *"Methylation was not detected in any of the tissues examined, suggesting that this mechanism of transcriptional repression is unlikely to explain the absence of somatic mutations in sporadic cancers."* — `[Well Supported]`. Within the resolution of the three `HpaII`/`MspI` sites tested across 64 primary tumors and 18 cell lines, the data cleanly refute widespread *BRCA2* promoter methylation and match the concurrent competitive RT-PCR finding that 13q LOH cell lines do not silence the remaining *BRCA2* allele.
- **Claim 2:** *"In one ovarian cancer cell line, reduced BRCA2 expression must be due to factors other than promoter methylation at the tested sites."* — `[Moderately Supported]`. Supported by the unmethylated `HpaII` digest in that cell line, though bisulfite sequencing would be needed to rule out methylation at non-`CCGG` sites.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- Sequence accession (`GenBank Z73360`), complete nucleotide sequence of the 1.1 kb 5' CpG island (Figure 1), and all PCR/RT-PCR primer and hybridization probe sequences (except the exon 23 positive-control primer pair) are printed in full.
- Listing the identities of the 18 breast and ovarian cancer cell lines in the text and providing the exon 23 control primer sequences would improve completeness.
