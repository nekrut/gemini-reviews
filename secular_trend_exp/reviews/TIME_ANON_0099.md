### 1. Executive Summary
This molecular oncology study characterizes an antisense promoter-associated noncoding RNA (`pncRNA_YAP1-1`, annotated as `AP001527.2` / `ENST00000614441.1`, a 631-nt single-exon transcript at `chr11:102,109,827–102,110,457` upstream of the `YAP1` TSS) in Ewing sarcoma (`ES`) cell lines (`SK-N-MC`, `TC-71`, `SK-ES1`, `RD-ES`). Through transient plasmid overexpression (`pcDNA3.1_pncYAP1-1`) and siRNA knockdown (`siPncRNA_YAP1-1#A/#B`) across `SK-N-MC` and `TC-71` cells, the authors report that `pncRNA_YAP1-1` leaves `YAP1` mRNA levels unchanged while reducing `YAP1` protein stability (`CHX` chase and `MG-132` proteasome inhibition), promoting `YAP1` `Ser127` phosphorylation and cytoplasmic retention, downregulating `YAP1/TEAD` target genes (`CTGF`, `CYR61`, `CCND1`), and attenuating cell viability (`MTS`), scratch wound closure, and laminin transwell invasion. Using `AnnoLnc` CLIP-seq predictions, biotinylated-RNA pull-down in nuclear extracts, `FUS` siRNA knockdown, and `TEAD` co-immunoprecipitation, they propose that `FUS` binds and stabilizes nuclear `pncRNA_YAP1-1`, which in turn binds `TEAD` to competitively displace `YAP1` from `TEAD`, leading to `YAP1` cytoplasmic export, phosphorylation, and proteasomal degradation. While the experimental assays are replicated in triplicate ($n=3$) across two ES lines with consistent directional readouts, the study exhibits several notable methodological and conceptual gaps: missing negative-control RNA baits in the biotin pull-downs, unaddressed stoichiometric implausibility of a low-abundance cis promoter RNA (`pncRNA_YAP1-1`) acting as a competitive stoichiometric decoy against abundant nuclear `TEAD` and `YAP1` proteins, and lack of causal rescue proof for the drug-treatment correlations (`Fig. 8`).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Missing Antisense / Scrambled RNA Negative Controls in Biotin-RNA Pull-Downs (`Figs. 6B & 7C`):**
  - In `Figure 6B` and `Figure 7C`, in vitro-transcribed biotinylated `pncRNA_YAP1-1` is incubated with `SK-N-MC` nuclear extracts to pull down `FUS`, `TEAD`, and `YAP1`. However, the Methods (`Biotin-RNA pull-down`, line 92) and Figure legends (`Figs. 6B, 7C`) describe no sense/antisense reverse-complement RNA control, scrambled RNA control, or poly(A)/unrelated lncRNA control (only beads-alone or `CSTF2`/`TAZ` negative-blot controls). Because `FUS` is an abundant, promiscuous nuclear RNA-binding protein that binds G/U-rich motifs across thousands of nascent RNAs in vitro, a sequence-scrambled or reverse-complement biotinylated RNA control (or native `FUS` / `TEAD` RIP-qPCR showing endogenous enrichment of `pncRNA_YAP1-1` over `GAPDH`/`U6`/`MALAT1`) is essential to prove binding specificity.
- **Stoichiometric Paradox of Low-Abundance `pncRNA_YAP1-1` Competitively Sequestering `TEAD` (`Fig. 7D`):**
  - The authors explicitly note in the Discussion (line 198) that *"LncRNAs are low abundant RNA species. Consistently, we found low level of pncRNA_YAP1-1 expression in ES cells"* (and `Fig. 1B` shows endogenous `pncRNA_YAP1-1` expression is $\sim 0.005\text{--}0.01$ relative to `GAPDH`, whereas `YAP1` mRNA in `Fig. 2A` is $\sim 0.15\text{--}0.30$ relative to `GAPDH`).
  - While transient `pcDNA3.1` overexpression or addition of $1\ \mu\text{g}$ of in vitro-transcribed `pncRNA_YAP1-1` to cell lysates (`Fig. 7D` legend states *"in the presence or not of in vitro-transcribed pncRNA_YAP1-1"*, whereas line 175 text says *"pncRNA_YAP1-1 overexpression"*, creating an ambiguity between in vitro RNA spike-in vs. cellular transfection!) can flood the nucleus with supraphysiological RNA copies, the authors also claim that knocking down endogenous `pncRNA_YAP1-1` with siRNA substantially increases total `YAP1` protein (`Fig. 3I`). No absolute copy-number quantification (droplet digital PCR or standard-curve molecules/cell of `pncRNA_YAP1-1` vs. `TEAD`/`YAP1` protein molecules/cell) is provided to explain how a low-copy promoter-associated transcript could stoichiometrically titrate the cellular `TEAD` pool in trans without acting in cis.
- **Baseline Positive Correlation vs. Functional Negative Regulation Across Cell Lines (`Fig. 1B` vs. `Fig. 2A–B`):**
  - Across the four ES cell lines, endogenous `pncRNA_YAP1-1` (`Fig. 1B`) and endogenous `YAP1` mRNA and protein (`Fig. 2A–B`) are **positively correlated**: both are high in metastasis-derived `TC-71` and `SK-N-MC` and low in primary-tumor-derived `SK-ES1` and `RD-ES` (likely because bidirectional transcription at the active `YAP1` promoter co-produces both `YAP1` pre-mRNA and the divergent promoter RNA `pncRNA_YAP1-1`).
- **Correlative Drug Treatments Without Epistatic Knockdown Rescue (`Fig. 8`):**
  - In `Figure 8`, the authors treat `SK-N-MC` cells with serum starvation, `BEZ-235` (PI3K/mTOR inhibitor), `RAD-001` (everolimus), or `etoposide`, showing that all four treatments increase `pncRNA_YAP1-1` ($\sim 2\text{--}4$-fold) and decrease `YAP1` protein. However, these broad cytotoxic/growth-arrest stimuli activate canonical Hippo kinase (`LATS1/2`) and general protein turnover pathways independently; the authors did not test whether transfecting `siPncRNA_YAP1-1` prevents the reduction of `YAP1` protein upon `BEZ-235`, `RAD-001`, or `etoposide` treatment.
- **Statistical Testing Details:** In `Figures 3H`, `5B`, `5C`, `7B`, and `8A–C`, multi-group comparisons (3 to 5 treatment groups) are analyzed by pairwise Student's $t$-tests rather than one-way or two-way ANOVA with post-hoc multiple-comparison adjustment (e.g., Dunnett's or Tukey's test).

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Overexpression of `pncRNA_YAP1-1` reduces `YAP1` protein stability (via `Ser127` phosphorylation and proteasomal degradation) without altering `YAP1` mRNA levels, thereby reducing `YAP1` target gene expression, proliferation, migration, and invasion in `SK-N-MC` and `TC-71` cells.* `[Moderately Supported]` — Consistently demonstrated across overexpression and siRNA knockdown experiments with `CHX` and `MG-132` controls (`Figs. 3–5`).
- **Claim 2:** *`FUS` specifically stabilizes `pncRNA_YAP1-1` and the `FUS/pncRNA_YAP1-1` complex directly binds `TEAD` to competitively disrupt endogenous `YAP1–TEAD` complexes.* `[Fragile]` — Lacks scrambled/antisense RNA specificity controls in the biotin pull-downs, lacks native `RIP-qPCR` validation, has an unresolved discrepancy between the text (line 175: *"pncRNA_YAP1-1 overexpression"*) and legend (`Fig. 7D`, line 173: *"in the presence or not of in vitro-transcribed pncRNA_YAP1-1"*), and does not address copy-number stoichiometry.
- **Claim 3:** *Anticancer agents (`BEZ-235`, `RAD-001`, `etoposide`) downregulate `YAP1` protein by inducing `pncRNA_YAP1-1`, making `pncRNA_YAP1-1` an actionable therapeutic target in Ewing sarcoma.* `[Overstated]` — Purely correlative in `Fig. 8` (no `siPncRNA_YAP1-1` rescue under drug treatment) and based entirely on 2D in vitro cell culture without in vivo xenograft validation.
