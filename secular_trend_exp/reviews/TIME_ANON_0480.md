### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This classical molecular immunology study reports the cDNA cloning, full-length Sanger sequencing, 5'-end RACE/PCR verification, recombinant expression, and partial epitope mapping of the human 100-kD nucleolar PM/Scl autoantigen (`EXOSC10` / PM/Scl-100), a major target of autoantibodies in polymyositis-scleroderma (PM/Scl) overlap syndrome. Using affinity-purified anti-100-kD patient antibodies to screen a HeLa $\lambda\text{gt11}$ expression library (isolating clone A16/2) followed by digoxigenin-labeled DNA hybridization of a human placenta $\lambda\text{gt11}$ library, the authors isolate a 2,797-bp full-length cDNA (clone H2/8, EMBL accession `X66113`) encoding an 885-amino-acid polypeptide with a predicted molecular mass of 100.8 kD. They verify the authenticity of the cloned autoantigen by showing that: (i) human antibodies affinity-purified on the recombinant $\beta$-galactosidase fusion protein recognize exclusively the native 100-kD nucleolar band on HeLa immunoblots and reproduce characteristic nucleolar immunofluorescence in HEp-2 cells; (ii) rabbit antisera raised against recombinant His-tagged fragment A reproduce identical nucleolar staining and 100-kD immunoblot reactivity; and (iii) immunoblot screening of 6 overlapping 6xHis-tagged recombinant fragments (A–F) against 15 PM/Scl patient sera and 3 control sera localizes a major linear antigenic region to residues 153–324 (recognized by 15/15 patient sera) and a minor antigenic region to residues 492–703 (4/15 sera). While sequence homology over a 12-amino-acid stretch (`LHAKNIIIRPQL`, 9/12 identity to the catalytic loop motif `LHAKNIIHRDLK` of Raf/PKC serine/threonine kinases) is noted speculatively without biochemical kinase assays, all experimental claims are rigorously supported and deposit full sequence data.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS / WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Bidirectional Immunological Validation of cDNA Authenticity:** Proves that clone H2/8 encodes the bona fide 100-kD nucleolar PM/Scl autoantigen via two reciprocal experiments: affinity-eluting human autoantibodies from the recombinant fusion protein (showing specific recognition of the native 100-kD HeLa nucleolar protein and nucleolar HEp-2 immunofluorescence, Figs. 2–3) and raising rabbit polyclonal antibodies against recombinant peptide A (reproducing the exact nucleolar immunofluorescence and 100-kD immunoblot reactivity, Fig. 7).
- **Systematic Epitope Mapping Across 15 Patient Sera with Specificity Controls:** Tests six overlapping 6xHis-purified fragments (A–F) spanning the 885-aa ORF against 15 independent PM/Scl patient sera, 1 normal human serum, and 2 primary biliary cirrhosis (PBC) control sera (Table 2, Figs. 5–6), while explicitly acknowledging in the Discussion that SDS-immunoblotting detects linear but not conformational/discontinuous epitopes.
- **5'-End Transcript Verification:** Supplements $\lambda\text{gt11}$ library cloning with gene-specific reverse transcription, homopolymer tailing, asymmetric PCR, and direct sequencing from HeLa S3 mRNA to map nucleotides $-37$ to $-1$ upstream of the Kozak-consensus `ATG` start codon.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Speculative Serine/Threonine Kinase Homology Based on a Single 12-Residue Window Without Catalytic Domain Conservation:** In Table 1, the authors highlight a 9/12 amino acid match between residues 175–186 (`LHAKNIIIRPQL`) and the subdomain VIB catalytic loop (`LHAKNIIHRDLK`) of Raf/PKC serine/threonine kinases. However, the critical catalytic aspartate (`D` in `HRDLK`) and arginine (`R` in `HRDLK`) are substituted by isoleucine (`I`) and proline (`P`) (`IIRPQL`) in the 100-kD PM/Scl protein, and none of the other conserved eukaryotic protein kinase subdomains (e.g., the GxGxxG ATP-binding loop or DFG motif) are present. Although the authors frame this cautiously ("suggests a possible function"), no statistical E-value correcting for database size or biochemical kinase assay is presented.
- **Absence of Northern Blot Transcript Size Verification:** While 5'-RACE/PCR mapped $-37$ to $-1$ and no upstream in-frame stop codon was present, a Northern blot on HeLa or placenta poly(A)+ RNA was not included to confirm that the 2.8-kb cDNA matches the full endogenous transcript length.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: Clone H2/8 (2,797 bp, 885 aa, 100.8 kD) encodes the human 100-kD PM/Scl nucleolar autoantigen distinct from the 75-kD PM/Scl protein.** `[Well Supported]` — Conclusively demonstrated by full-length sequencing, reciprocal affinity purification of human patient autoantibodies, and de novo rabbit immunization.
- **Claim 2: A major linear autoimmune epitope domain resides within the N-terminal third (aa 153–324, fragment C) and a minor determinant resides within aa 492–703 (fragments E–F).** `[Well Supported]` — Supported by 15/15 patient sera reacting with fragments A, B, and C and 4/15 reacting with E/F, with 0/3 control sera reacting (Table 2).
- **Claim 3: Local sequence similarity (9/12 residues at aa 175–186) to serine/threonine kinases suggests a possible kinase-related regulatory function.** `[Fragile]` — Framed cautiously as a hypothesis, but the substitution of the invariant catalytic `HRD` triad residues (`HRDLK` $\to$ `IIRPQL`) and absence of other kinase subdomains argue against bona fide protein kinase activity.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- The complete 2,797-bp nucleotide sequence and 885-aa translation are printed in full in Figure 4b and deposited in the EMBL Data Library under accession number `X66113`.
- Exact amino acid coordinates of all six recombinant fragments (A–F) and per-serum immunoreactivity scores across all 18 human sera are tabulated in Figure 5a and Table 2.
