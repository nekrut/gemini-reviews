### 1. Executive Summary
This study investigates antibody-independent complement activation by human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (`HIV-1`, strains `HTLV-IIIB` and `HTLV-RF`) in normal HIV-seronegative human serum and its role in enhancing viral infection of `CD4+`/complement receptor 2 (`CR2` / `CD21`)-positive target cells (`MT2` T-lymphoblastoid cells and normal human peripheral blood T lymphocytes/PBMCs). The authors demonstrate that fresh HIV-seronegative human serum activates complement via the alternative pathway (depositing C3 fragments onto the viral envelope without causing virolysis) and enhances low-multiplicity-of-infection (`MOI`) HIV-1 infection of `MT2` cells and normal T cells when target cells are pre-incubated with complement-opsonized virus and washed after 1–3 h. This complement-dependent enhancement requires an intact alternative complement pathway (`C3`- and factor `B`-dependent) and is blocked by a monoclonal antibody (`OKB7` or equivalent) plus $\text{F(ab')}_2$ anti-immunoglobulin directed against the C3d binding site of `CR2`, whereas blocking the `gp120`-binding site of `CD4` inhibits standard unopsonized infection without abolishing `CR2`-mediated complement-enhanced entry. ### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Pathway Specificity and Receptor Blocking Controls (Strengths visible in Abstract):** The experimental design uses well-defined classical vs alternative complement pathway depletion controls (confirming dependence on `C3` and factor `B` in seronegative serum, ruling out antibody-dependent classical pathway activation) and receptor-specific antibody blockade (`anti-CR2` C3d-binding-site mAb + $\text{F(ab')}_2$ cross-linking vs `anti-CD4` `gp120`-binding-site mAb) across two divergent HIV-1 strains (`HTLV-IIIB` and `HTLV-RF`) and two target cell systems (`MT2` cell line and primary human PBMCs/T cells).
- **Target Cell Restriction (`MT2` HTLV-I-Transformed Line vs Primary T Cells):** `MT2` cells express high levels of `CR2` (`CD21`) due to HTLV-I transformation, whereas resting peripheral blood CD4+ T lymphocytes express much lower surface density of `CR2` than B cells or follicular dendritic cells. Without the full-text figures and tables, the magnitude of complement-mediated infectious enhancement in primary human T cells vs `MT2` cells and the donor-to-donor variability across primary PBMC preparations cannot be quantitatively verified.
- **Unverifiable Quantitative Kinetics and Statistical Testing:** Because the Methods, Results, Figures, and Tables are omitted from the text conversion (residing only in the external scanned print PDF), exact `MOI` titrations, `p24` or reverse transcriptase (`RT`) activity units, biological replicate counts ($n$), and error bars/statistical tests cannot be audited.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *HIV-1 (`HTLV-IIIB` and `HTLV-RF`) activates the alternative complement pathway in HIV-seronegative human serum without undergoing complement-mediated lysis.* `[Moderately Supported]` — Biologically consistent with C3b deposition and factor H/decay-accelerating factor host membrane regulators protecting budding retroviral envelopes, though raw C3 deposition and lysis datasets are in the unextracted scan.
