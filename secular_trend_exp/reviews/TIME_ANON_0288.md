### 1. Executive Summary
This study evaluates the ability of phosphatidylserine (PS) and related dietary lipid supplements (PS-DHA conjugate, GPC, Krill oil) to elevate *IKBKAP* (IKAP) mRNA and protein levels in hTERT-immortalized fibroblast lines derived from three Familial Dysautonomia (FD) patients (`FDA`, `FDB`, `FDD`), one heterozygous carrier, and four healthy controls. The authors report that PS increases total *IKBKAP* transcription (both exon 20 included and skipped isoforms) without altering the splicing inclusion/skipping ratio, increases IKAP protein levels by 2.3-fold after 14 days in `FDB` cells, alters the expression of 877 genes on an Affymetrix Human Gene 1.0 microarray (enriched for cell-cycle and DNA metabolism genes), and shifts FD fibroblasts out of G1 arrest into S+G2 phases. While the observation that PS acts via global transcriptional up-regulation and cell-cycle release rather than splicing modulation is an important mechanistic distinction, the manuscript suffers from severe experimental design flaws: dropping 1 of 3 microarray replicates per group (`n = 2` final), confounding serum concentrations between control (10% FCS) and FD/heterozygous cultures (20% FCS), using chloroform/methanol (95:5) as the vehicle solvent in cell culture, and selective cell-line reporting across time points and assays.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Confounded Culture Media Conditions Between Genotypes:** In the Materials and Methods, control fibroblasts and 293T cells are cultured in DMEM with 10% FCS, whereas heterozygous and FD cell lines are cultured in DMEM with 20% FCS. Comparing baseline *IKBKAP* mRNA, protein, and especially G1 vs. S+G2 cell-cycle distributions (Figure 1 and Figure 5A–B) across cell lines grown in 10% vs. 20% serum introduces a major mitogenic and metabolic confounder.
- **Toxic Organic Vehicle Solvent (Chloroform/Methanol 95:5):** PS and Sharp-PS GOLD powders were dissolved in 95:5 chloroform/methanol at 20 mg/mL stock concentration. At 100–300 µg/mL treatment doses, cells were exposed to 0.5%–1.5% v/v chloroform/methanol in culture media. The authors themselves note at 200–300 µg/mL that "a possible explanation for lower efficacy at higher PS concentration is the toxicity of the solvent at high volumes," yet dose-response experiments (Figure 2B–C) did not match vehicle volume at every dose or quantify cell viability/cytotoxicity.
- **Microarray Under-Replication and Post-Hoc Outlier Removal:** Three Affymetrix Human Gene 1.0 chips were hybridized per condition (PS vs. solvent in `FDB` cells), but "one chip from each assay was omitted from the analysis because of unsatisfying clustering results." Running SAM (Significance Analysis of Microarrays) on `n = 2` vs. `n = 2` arrays lacks statistical power and valid permutation structure, and no raw or processed microarray data accession (e.g., GEO/ArrayExpress) is deposited.
- **Selective Time-Point and Cell-Line Reporting:** In Figure 2, `FDA` is shown only at 24 hr (where 5–100 µg/mL gave ~5-fold increase) and `FDB` is shown only at 48 hr (where 100–200 µg/mL gave ~3-fold increase), while `FDD` had "variable effects" relegated to Figure S1. Long-term treatment (Figure 3), microarray (Figure 4), other supplements (Figure 6), and kinetin/tocotrienol comparisons (Figure 7) were performed solely in `FDB` cells.
- **Antibody Switching and Exposure Manipulation in Western Blots:** Figure 1D uses Santa Cruz D-17 anti-IKAP, whereas Figure 3C switches to a BD Biosciences anti-IKAP antibody and reduces the exposure of the 293T control lane "to one fourth of its original amount" while quantifying bands by ImageJ on X-ray film without a linear standard curve.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1: PS increases wild-type IKAP mRNA and protein levels in FD fibroblasts via transcriptional upregulation rather than splicing correction.** `[Moderately Supported]` — RT-PCR and qPCR in `FDA` and `FDB` show elevated *IKBKAP* levels without changing the exon 20 inclusion-to-skipping ratio (unlike kinetin in Figure 7A, which shifts splicing ratio). However, the non-monotonic dose response in `FDA` (flat 5-fold increase from 5 to 100 µg/mL, dropping at 200–300 µg/mL due to chloroform/methanol toxicity) and inconsistent response in `FDD` weaken the generalizability across FD patient lines.
- **Claim 2: FD cells exhibit G1 cell-cycle arrest that is rescued by PS treatment, driving global cell-cycle gene upregulation and RNA Pol II transcription.** `[Fragile]` — Baseline G1 enrichment in FD cells vs. controls (Figure 5A–B) is confounded by different serum concentrations (20% vs. 10% FCS) and independent hTERT immortalization histories. Furthermore, stimulation of fibroblast proliferation by exogenous phospholipid supplementation in vitro may nonspecifically elevate housekeeping and metabolic gene transcription (including *IKBKAP*) in dividing fibroblasts without translating to post-mitotic sensory and autonomic neurons in vivo, which are the primary target tissue in FD.
- **Claim 3: PS and its omega-3 conjugate (Sharp-PS GOLD) are promising therapeutic agents for immediate testing in FD patients.** `[Overstated]` — Extrapolating from in vitro hTERT-immortalized dermal fibroblasts treated with 5–100 µg/mL PS dissolved in chloroform/methanol to clinical efficacy in a congenital sensory/autonomic neuropathy—where the tissue-specific defect is exon 20 skipping in post-mitotic neurons—overstates the translational readiness of a non-specific mitogenic/transcriptional effect.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Primer Sequences & Cell Line IDs:** NIGMS repository IDs (`GM00850`, `GM02342`, `GM04664`) and RT-PCR/qPCR primer sequences for *IKBKAP* and *HPRT* are explicitly provided in the text.
- **Missing Microarray Accession:** No public repository accession (GEO or ArrayExpress) is provided for the six Affymetrix Human Gene 1.0 arrays; only a supplementary gene list of 877 genes (Figure S2) is referenced.
- **Statistical Reporting:** Figure legends state "repeated independently three times, and the results shown are representative of an average experiment; QPCR experiments were amplified in triplicate; results shown are mean values ± SD," indicating that error bars in Figures 1C, 2, 3A, 4, 6, and 7 represent technical qPCR triplicates from a single representative experiment rather than biological replicates, and no p-values or statistical tests are reported for any qPCR or Western blot comparisons.
