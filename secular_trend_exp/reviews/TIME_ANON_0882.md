### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This manuscript is a conference meeting report summarizing highlights from the eighth annual meeting of the Human Genome Organization (HUGO / HGM2003), held in Cancun, Mexico, 27–30 April. It provides a qualitative overview of plenary and parallel session talks spanning the completion of the human genome sequence, medical genomics (e.g., calpain-10 in diabetes, *PDCD1* Runx-1 intronic polymorphism in SLE, chromosome 7 disease gene mapping, Quebec founder haplotype mapping, *Schistosoma japonicum* EST mimicry), and functional genomics/bioinformatics (yeast cell-size homeostasis via *SFP1* and ribosome biogenesis, a regulatory point mutation in *IGF2* underlying a muscle growth/fat deposition QTL in pigs, ATP-dependent nucleosome sliding, MBD proteins in Rett syndrome, divergent yeast promoter regulation of *GLT1*/*UGA3* by Abf1p, Pol II elongation coupling to alternative splicing, mouse stem-cell cDNA catalogs, Gene Ontology, and *E. coli* regulatory network congruence).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Broad, accurate synthesis of landmark functional and medical genomics findings:** Accurately summarizes diverse experimental paradigms ranging from Leif Andersson's identification of the single regulatory nucleotide polymorphism in *IGF2* driving a domestic pig muscle/fat QTL to Alberto Kornblihtt's kinetic coupling model of RNA Pol II elongation and alternative splicing.
- **Clear contextualization of early post-genome-completion challenges:** Highlights both the successes of mendelian/founder chromosome mapping and the emerging difficulty of finding large haplotype blocks in multifactorial diseases (Hudson's Quebec cohort) or reconciling microarray profiles with known *E. coli* regulatory networks (40–80% congruence).
