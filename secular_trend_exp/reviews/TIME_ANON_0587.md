### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This comprehensive full-text ultrastructural study maps the steady-state and stress-induced subcellular distribution of the mammalian KDEL receptor (KDEL-R / p23) across primary tissues (mouse early spermatids, rat pancreatic acinar cells, liver, kidney) and cultured cell lines (HeLa, HeLa-SA:48, NRK, Vero, and mouse L cells) using quantitative cryo-immunogold electron microscopy. Using an affinity-purified rabbit antibody against the cytoplasmic 21-residue COOH-terminal tail of p23 (`CDFFYLYITKVLKGKKLSLPA-COOH`) and streptolysin O (SLO) permeabilization to remove cytosolic masking factors and improve ultrastructural membrane visualization, the authors demonstrate that:
1. `rab1` is a high-density marker of the ER-Golgi intermediate compartment (IC), colocalizing with `p58` and mouse hepatitis virus (MHV) budding profiles (extending to 1–2 *cis*-most Golgi cisternae).
2. In SLO-treated MHV-infected L cells ($n=24$ systematic micrographs per compartment, Table I), KDEL-R density is highest in the IC ($5.3 \pm 0.9\text{ gold}/\mu\text{m}$ membrane) and Golgi stack ($4.2 \pm 0.7\text{ gold}/\mu\text{m}$), lower in the rough ER ($1.6 \pm 0.2\text{ gold}/\mu\text{m}$) and nuclear envelope ($0.8 \pm 0.2\text{ gold}/\mu\text{m}$), and $0\text{ gold}/\mu\text{m}$ on the plasma membrane or BSA-gold-loaded early/late endosomes.
3. KDEL-R colocalizes extensively in the IC and *cis*-most Golgi cisterna with `rab1` and endogenous KDEL ligands (PDI, CaBP1, CaBP2, calreticulin/CaBP3, and a generic `-K-X5-KDEL` peptide antibody).
4. In normal tissues and unstressed HeLa-SA:48 cells stably expressing a P5D4-epitope-tagged $\alpha\text{-2,6-sialyltransferase}$ TGN marker, only $16.6\%$ ($30/181$ gold particles across $n=28$ Golgi stacks, Table II) of Golgi KDEL-R resides on the *trans*/TGN side; however, cellular stress—vaccinia virus infection ($62.0\%$, $72/116$ gold particles across $n=22$ stacks), VSV-ts045 at $20^\circ\text{C}$, $20^\circ\text{C}$ temperature block in uninfected cells ($56\%$), or $43^\circ\text{C}$ heat shock ($46\%$)—triggers a dramatic shift of KDEL-R toward the *trans*-Golgi/TGN, whereas vacuolar $\text{H}^+$-ATPase inhibition with 500 nM bafilomycin A1 has no effect ($18\%$ *trans*).
5. Upon $\text{GTP}\gamma\text{S}$ ($50\,\mu\text{M}$) arrest in SLO-permeabilized cells, $24\%$ of $\beta\text{-COP}$-coated buds/vesicles ($n=200$ counted) label for KDEL-R compared to $4\%$ background with irrelevant anti-vaccinia antibodies.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT

#### STRENGTHS
- **Stereological Line-Intersection Quantitation & Multi-Marker Cryo-Immuno-EM:** Unlike purely qualitative EM studies, the authors apply systematic stereological sampling ($n=24$ micrographs at $22,000\times$ in Table I; $n=28$ and $n=22$ perpendicularly sectioned Golgi stacks at $28,000\times$ in Table II; $n=200$ $\beta\text{-COP}$ buds) and report normalized linear membrane densities ($\text{gold}/\mu\text{m} \pm \text{SEM}$).
- **Clever Unmasking Control via SLO Permeabilization:** Recognizing from their prior biochemical work that a cytosolic factor masks the COOH-terminal tail of KDEL-R, the authors use SLO permeabilization to wash out cytosol prior to fixation, unmasking both rough ER and IC KDEL-R pools while validating that the qualitative distribution matches unpermeabilized cells.
- **Rigorous Endocytic Exclusion and Bafilomycin A1 Controls:** Dual pulse-chase loading with 16-nm BSA-gold (late endosomes/lysosomes) and 4/5-nm BSA-gold (early endosomes), paired with acridine orange verification of bafilomycin A1 pH neutralization, cleanly rules out post-TGN/endosomal spillover and tests the pH-dependence hypothesis *in situ*.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Arbitrary Geometric Bisection of the Golgi Stack in Table II:** To quantify *cis* vs. *trans* KDEL-R distribution in Table II, the authors state: "An arbitrary line was then drawn through the stack to split the stack into *cis* and *trans* sides." Because viral infection (vaccinia or VSV at $20^\circ\text{C}$) and temperature blocks are explicitly noted in the text to cause *trans*/TGN enlargement and *cis*/medial stack shrinkage ("the TGN, which enlarges considerably while the Golgi stack shrinks"), bisecting the stack with a geometric midline without normalizing for cisternal membrane length on each side (`gold/um` as done in Table I) could inflate the apparent percentage of particles on the *trans* side simply due to *trans*/TGN membrane hypertrophy.
- **Lower Total Gold Particle Count per Stack in Vaccinia-Infected vs. Uninfected Cells in Table II:** In Table II, $n=28$ uninfected Golgi stacks yielded $151 + 30 = 181$ total gold particles ($6.46$ gold/stack), whereas $n=22$ vaccinia-infected stacks yielded $44 + 72 = 116$ total gold particles ($5.27$ gold/stack). Thus, *cis*-side gold per stack dropped from $5.39$ ($151/28$) to $2.00$ ($44/22$) while *trans*-side gold per stack rose from $1.07$ ($30/28$) to $3.27$ ($72/22$). Also, no formal statistical test (e.g., $\chi^2$ or Mann-Whitney test across individual stacks) or per-stack variance is reported in Table II or for the $20^\circ\text{C}$ ($56\%$) and $43^\circ\text{C}$ ($46\%$) stress experiments.
- **Minor Typo in Antibody Epitope Name in Methods vs. Text:** On page 1559 (Methods, Quantitation of Immunogold Labeling), the VSV-G cytoplasmic epitope tag is written as `P4D5`, whereas everywhere else in the text (page 1559 left column, page 1566) it is correctly written as `P5D4`.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** Mammalian KDEL-R is highly concentrated in both the `rab1`/`p58`-positive intermediate compartment (IC) and the *cis*-Golgi stack, with lower (~3-fold less) density in the rough ER and none at the plasma membrane or endosomes. `[Well Supported]` — Directly demonstrated by stereological quantitation (Table I) and double-immunogold colocalization with `rab1`, `p58`, MHV budding profiles, and endogenous KDEL ligands across multiple cell types and native tissues.
- **Claim 2:** Viral infection (vaccinia, VSV) and thermal stress ($20^\circ\text{C}$, $43^\circ\text{C}$), but not bafilomycin A1 pH neutralization, shift KDEL-R distribution toward the *trans*-Golgi/TGN independently of host protein synthesis. `[Well Supported]` — Supported by double-labeling with the P5D4-tagged $\alpha\text{-2,6-sialyltransferase}$ TGN marker and viral TGN markers, though geometric midline division in Table II may partially reflect TGN membrane expansion alongside receptor redistribution.
- **Claim 3:** KDEL-R is incorporated into $\beta\text{-COP}$-coated buds/vesicles originating from the IC ($24\%$ positive vs. $4\%$ background after $\text{GTP}\gamma\text{S}$ arrest). `[Well Supported]` — Quantified across 200 $\beta\text{-COP}$ buds/vesicles with two negative control antibodies.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Peptide & Reagent Precision:** Exact synthetic peptide sequences (`CDFFYLYITKVLKGKKLSLPA-COOH` for p23; `-K-X-X-X-X-X-K-D-E-L` degenerate mix for KDEL ligands), fixative concentrations (4% PFA + 0.1% GA in 200 mM Hepes pH 7.4, or 1% GA for SLO-treated cells), and gold particle sizes are thoroughly documented (1.6/2.0).
