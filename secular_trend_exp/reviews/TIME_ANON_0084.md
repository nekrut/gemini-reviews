### 1. Executive Summary
This is an outstanding, mechanistically rigorous *Drosophila* genetics and molecular biology study identifying and characterizing `Protein Partner of Sans-fille` (`PPS`, encoded by `CG6525`), a 2,016-amino-acid protein containing PHD, BRK, TFS2M, and SPOC domains that physically links the U1 snRNP protein SNF and the master female sex-determination regulator Sex-lethal (`SXL`) to co-transcriptional *Sxl* exon 3 skipping. Starting from a yeast two-hybrid screen of $9.8 \times 10^7$ embryonic cDNA clones with SNF bait, the authors validate direct binding between the C-terminal SPOC-containing fragment of PPS and SNF in vitro (GST pull-down) and in vivo (RNase-insensitive co-immunoprecipitation with SNF and phosphorylated U1-70K, plus RNase-sensitive co-IP with SXL in embryonic nuclear extracts). Using FRT-bearing piggyBac elements (`PBac{RB}CG6525^{e02263}` and `PBac{RB}Scg-beta^{e02063}`), they engineer a precise genomic deletion (`pps^1`) and use separate genomic rescue transgenes (`P{pps+}` vs. `P{Scg-beta+}`) to prove that loss of `pps` alone causes larval lethality ($90\%$ rescue with `P{pps+}`, $n=554$). Taking advantage of an hypomorphic single-copy `P{pps+}` rescue line and dominant maternal genetic interaction assays, they demonstrate that `pps` is required for female germline survival, oogenesis, and *Sxl* autoregulatory splicing, exhibiting potent synergistic female-specific lethality with `da^2` and spliceosomal alleles `fl(2)d^2`, `U2af38^{DeltaE18}`, and `spf45^{Delta}` (Table 1). Finally, native RNA immunoprecipitation (RIP) and chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP) across wild-type, female-only (`C(1)DX` / `Sxl^{7BO}`), and SXL-null (`da^1/da^1`) embryos reveal that PPS loads at the *Sxl* maintenance promoter (`SxlPm`) and across the *Sxl* transcription unit independently of SXL, where it facilitates recruitment of SNF and SXL to nascent transcripts near the first poly(U) tract.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Genetic Dissection & Rescue Controls:** Because the FRT-directed deletion `pps^1` removes the overlapping 5' ends of both `CG6525` (`pps`) and `Scg-beta`, the authors constructed two non-overlapping genomic rescue transgenes (`P{pps+}` and `P{Scg-beta+}`) and verified by Western blot and complementation that `P{pps+}` fully restores the 220 kDa PPS protein and rescues `pps^1/Df(3R)Exel7308` lethality ($90\%, n=554$), whereas `P{Scg-beta+}` fails to rescue, cleanly ruling out confounding from `Scg-beta` loss.
- **Sample Sizes & Transgenic Rescue of Maternal-Effect Lethality (Table 1):** All genetic cross sample sizes are explicitly reported and well powered (ranging from $n=105$ to $n=975$ reference siblings per cross). In `da^2/+; pps^1/+` mothers, survival of `Sxl^{f1}/+` daughters drops from $57\%$ ($n=275$) to $7.2\%$ ($n=222$) and is rescued back to $26\%$ ($n=517$) by `P{pps+}`. Similarly, dramatic synergistic lethality with `fl(2)d^2` ($0.2\%, n=450$), `U2af38^{DeltaE18}` ($24\%, n=525$), and `spf45^{Delta}` ($5.8\%, n=975$) is rescued to $60\%\text{--}100\%$ by `P{pps+}`. (Adding formal $\chi^2$ or Fisher's exact test p-values to Table 1 would be a minor statistical enhancement, though the effect sizes are unambiguous.)
- **Biochemical & ChIP Specificity Controls:**
  - Co-IP experiments include pre-immune serum controls, western blot stripping/reprobing for non-interacting spliceosomal proteins (`U2A'` and `Snf^{E21D}` mutant protein incapable of U1/U2 incorporation), and explicit RNase A digestion controls to distinguish protein-protein (`PPS–SNF`, `PPS–U1-70K`) from RNA-tethered (`PPS–SXL`) complexes.
  - ChIP assays include five tiled amplicons across `Sxl` (`Pm` promoter, `E1`, `I1`, `I2`, `E3`), an active housekeeping splicing control (`tra`), an untranscribed silent negative control (`CG14230`), unlinked euchromatic negative controls (`tll` and `hkb`), and Pol II phosphorylation isoform controls (`8WG16` for unphosphorylated initiation-poised Pol IIa vs. `H14` for Ser5-P elongating Pol IIo).
  - Crucially, the authors perform ChIP in two independent genetic backgrounds lacking SXL (`da^1/da^1` maternal-effect embryos and `Sxl^{7BO}` deletion embryos) as well as female-only embryos (`C(1)DX`), proving that PPS and SNF associate with `Sxl` chromatin independently of SXL, whereas efficient SXL recruitment near the intron 1 poly(U) tract (`I1`) is abolished in `snf^{J210}` mutants.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *`CG6525` (`pps`) encodes an essential nuclear protein that directly binds the U1 snRNP protein SNF and associates in an RNase-sensitive complex with SXL.* `[Well Supported]` — Supported by Y2H, GST pull-down, RNase A-treated vs. untreated embryonic nuclear extract co-IPs, and failure of PPS to co-IP mutant `Snf^{E21D}` protein.
- **Claim 2:** *`pps` is required in vivo for *Sxl* female-specific splicing and germline development, and genetically collaborates with `snf`, `fl(2)d`, `U2af38`, and `spf45`.* `[Well Supported]` — Demonstrated by tumorous ovaries, male-exon (`Exon 2-3-4`) RT-PCR inclusion in `1X P{pps+}; pps^1/Df(3R)Exel7308` ovaries, and dominant maternal synergy rescued by `P{pps+}` in Table 1.
- **Claim 3:** *PPS loads at the `SxlPm` promoter and along the transcription unit to bridge transcription and SXL/SNF recruitment to nascent `Sxl` pre-mRNA.* `[Well Supported]` — Supported by high-resolution ChIP across `Pm`, `E1`, `I1`, `I2`, and `E3` in WT, female-only, `da^1`, and `snf^{J210}` embryos.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Genetic Stocks, Primers, and Antibodies:** All piggyBac insertion alleles, deficiency stocks, genomic rescue construct boundaries (`7.2 kb` `StuI–BglII` fragment for `P{pps+}`, `2.5 kb` PCR fragment for `P{Scg-beta+}`), antibody sources and dilutions, and ChIP/RT-PCR primer sequences (`SxlPm`, `E1`, `I1`, `I2`, `E3`, `tra`, `CG14230`) are thoroughly documented in Materials and Methods.
