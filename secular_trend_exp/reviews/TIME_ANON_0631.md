### SUMMARY / 1. Executive Summary
This text is a descriptive book chapter or narrative review covering the taxonomy, genomic organization, spike (S) glycoprotein structure, replication cycle, and interspecies transmission of *Coronaviridae*, with sections on SARS-CoV, MERS-CoV, and avian Infectious Bronchitis Virus (IBV). It contains no original experimental data, computational analyses, or primary methodology. Worse, the manuscript is plagued by severe factual errors, garbled machine-translated prose (including untranslated French figure captions and text blocks), verbatim duplicate paragraphs, and misattributed figures (e.g., inserting a detailed TGEV RNA-protein pull-down western blot figure from another study in the middle of the MERS-CoV epidemiology section without explanation).

### STRENGTHS
- **Broad introductory scope:** Attempts to cover both human betacoronaviruses (SARS-CoV, MERS-CoV) and veterinary gammacoronaviruses (IBV), including classical embryonated egg and tracheal ring isolation protocols for IBV.

### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT / 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Multiple severe factual fabrications and errors:**
  - **Fabricated/garbled epidemics:** Twice (lines 63 and 120), the authors assert the existence of a "SARS pandemic in [YEAR]–16 in Russia and Ukraine (Berger, [YEAR])." No SARS-CoV pandemic occurred in Russia and Ukraine.
  - **Incorrect taxonomy and receptor biology:**
    - Line 127 states: "MERS-CoV is a pathogen that infects most mammals and higher animals, as well as humans... The infection is mainly due to the interaction with the DPP4 receptor for mammals and the ACE2 in bats." MERS-CoV uses DPP4 (not ACE2) in bats as well as camels/humans, and mice/rats are naturally resistant to MERS-CoV due to DPP4 sequence divergence unless transgenic.
    - Line 144 states: "a second ORF2 exists after the S gene and before the E gene codes for 3 nonstructural proteins which are 3a, 3b, 3c, and 3d" — four proteins are listed while claiming "3 nonstructural proteins," and MERS-CoV accessory genes between S and E are ORF3, ORF4a, ORF4b, and ORF5.
    - Line 146 refers to "protein 3C-like serine protease (3CLpro)" after calling it a "type 3C cysteine protease" on line 111.
    - Line 148 claims: "the subunit S1 which ensures the fusion with the host cell at the amino-terminal and the subunit S2 which ensures the fusion at the carboxyl-terminal" — S1 mediates receptor attachment, not membrane fusion.
    - Line 65 claims coronaviruses infect "fish" and "woodpeckers" indiscriminately, confusing *Coronavirinae* with *Bafinivirus* (Torovirinae).
- **Severe machine-translation artifacts and garbled scientific terminology:**
  - Throughout the text, standard virological terms are mangled by literal French-to-English machine translation: "spicules (S)" or "tip" or "peak gene sequences" (from French *spicule* / *pic* for Spike), "palm tree crops in live animal markets" (line 99, mistranslating *civettes palmistes* / masked palm civets as "palm tree crops"!), "camel camels" (line 198), "prototype meadows" (line 140), "pulley tests" (line 129, mistranslating *pull-down assays*), "epidermal intermediate S-glycoproteins" (line 101, mistranslating *epidemic*), and "Following genome takeoff" (line 46).
  - Unedited French text remains in Figure 7.5 ("Construction génomique des Coronaviridae"), Figure 7.9 ("Plus de 27 pays des cinq continents..."), and Figure 7.10 ("Coloration immunofluorescente de la section de paraffine rénale du rein 5 jours après la fièvre...").
- **Duplicated paragraphs and incoherent figure integration:**
  - Lines 38–45 repeat the bullet points from line 36 verbatim; lines 196–197 repeat lines 194–195 verbatim.
  - In line 101, the authors copy first-person prose from a cited paper ("we deduced that the epidemic strain retained growth ability... (Sheahan and Baric)") as if they performed the experiments.
  - Figure 7.6 (a complex 3-panel experimental figure mapping the GAIT-like RNA motif at the 3' end of TGEV interacting with EPRS and RRS from Marquez-Jurado et al.) is pasted into the MERS-CoV epidemiology section (line 129) with zero relevance or mention in the text.
