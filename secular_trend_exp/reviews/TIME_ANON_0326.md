### 1. Executive Summary
This study conducts a genome-wide association study (GWAS) and linkage disequilibrium (LD) haplotype block analysis for seed oleic acid (C18:1) content across 203 Chinese semi-winter rapeseed (*Brassica napus* L.) inbred lines grown in three years in Chongqing, genotyped with the 60K Brassica Infinium SNP array (24,338 filtered SNPs). Combining GWAS hits with $5\times$ whole-genome resequencing of 50 accessions and silique RNA-seq (15 days after pollination) of 13 accessions, the authors focus on two haplotype blocks on chromosomes A02 (`6,311,853–6,435,462 bp`) and C02 (`997,000–1,277,438 bp`), proposing that three linked genes on A02 (*BnMTACP2-A02*, *BnABCI13-A02*, *BnECI1-A02*) and two linked genes on C02 (*BnFAD8-C02*, *BnSDP1-C02*) co-regulate oleic acid accumulation via a WGCNA co-expression network. While the integration of array GWAS, resequencing, and transcriptomics addresses an important breeding trait, the manuscript contains glaring internal contradictions in chromosome assignments, a statistically anti-conservative significance threshold disguised as FDR $< 0.05$, mathematically flawed model equations, and underpowered transcriptomic/co-expression analyses ($n = 13$ samples using DEGSeq).

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Direct Contradiction in Identified Chromosomes (Text vs. Figure 3):** Throughout the Abstract (line 9), Results (line 48), and Conclusion (line 92), the authors state that the six significant haplotype regions mapped to chromosomes **A02, A07, A08, C01, C02, and C03**, and the entire downstream analysis focuses on **A02** and **C02**. However, the caption for Figure 3 (lines 48 and 54) explicitly states: *"Fig. 3 Six haplotype regions from chromosomes **A07, A08, C01, C03, C07 and C09** carrying candidate genes that are significantly associated with the oleic acid content"*, omitting both A02 and C02 entirely! Furthermore, for chromosome A02, the gene is alternately named *BnECI1-A02* and *BnECHIC-A02* within the same paragraph and Figure 4/5 captions.
- **Misrepresentation of GWAS Multiple Testing Threshold:** On line 116, the authors claim: *"An FDR < 0.05 was used to identify significant associations with the oleic acid content at a cut-off value of $-\log_{10}(P) = 4$."* With $M = 24,338$ tested SNPs and 51 SNPs passing $P < 10^{-4}$ across the three environments (~17 SNPs per environment), the expected number of false positives under the global null at $P = 10^{-4}$ is $24,338 \times 10^{-4} = 2.43$ per environment (corresponding to a Benjamini-Hochberg FDR of $\sim 14\text{--}15\%$, not $< 0.05$). In reality, $-\log_{10}(P) = 4$ is an arbitrary suggestive threshold that is more than an order of magnitude more lenient than the standard Bonferroni/effective-marker threshold for 24,338 SNPs ($-\log_{10}(0.05/24,338) = 5.69$).
- **Mathematical Typo in Mixed Linear Model Residual Variance:** On line 114, the residual vector $e$ of the $\text{Q}+\text{K}$ mixed linear model is defined as $e \sim N(0, R\sigma_g^2)$ instead of $R\sigma_e^2$ (confusing genetic variance $\sigma_g^2$ with residual error variance $\sigma_e^2$). Additionally, field trials used an *unreplicated* complete randomized design in each year (line 100), so genotype-by-year interaction variance ($\sigma_{ge}^2$) is completely confounded with residual error ($\sigma_e^2$) in the heritability formula $H^2 = \sigma_g^2 / (\sigma_g^2 + \sigma_e^2/n)$.
- **Flawed Transcriptomic & WGCNA Design ($n = 13$ accessions, DEGSeq):**
  1. Only 13 accessions (4 low, 4 medium, 5 high oil content—note they were selected on total *oil content*, not *oleic acid content*, line 136!) were profiled by RNA-seq at a single time point (15 DAP). When split across 2 or 3 haplotype alleles in Figure 4c and Figure S1c, the rare haplotype groups in $n = 13$ accessions can contain as few as 1–3 observations, making two-sample $t$-tests highly unstable.
  2. The authors used `DEGSeq v1.22.0` (line 136) for differential expression screening, a tool designed in 2010 for technical replicates using a Poisson/binomial model that ignores biological variance and produces extreme false-positive rates on biological accessions.
  3. Constructing a WGCNA network on $n = 13$ samples (below the WGCNA author-recommended minimum of 15–20 samples) and then filtering edges simply by raw Pearson correlation $\text{PCC} \ge 0.55$ (line 141, which has a nominal $p \approx 0.05$ before correcting for thousands of gene pairs) yields dense false-positive co-expression clusters.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *Strong selection created tightly linked multi-gene functional haplotypes on A02 (BnMTACP2, BnABCI13, BnECI1) and C02 (BnFAD8, BnSDP1) that co-regulate oleic acid accumulation.* `[Overstated]` — No population genetic test of selection (e.g., Tajima's $D$, $\pi$ ratio, XP-EHH, or $F_{\text{ST}}$) was performed anywhere in the manuscript! Extensive LD blocks spanning 120–280 kb are a standard feature of Chinese semi-winter *B. napus* due to its recent breeding bottleneck and allotetraploid history; finding multiple annotated lipid genes within a 280-kb LD block does not prove epistatic co-selection of all genes in the block rather than hitchhiking with a single causal variant.
- **Claim 2:** *Downregulation of BnFAD8-C02 and BnSDP1-C02 in Hap1 increases oleic acid accumulation.* `[Moderately Supported]` — *FAD8* (plastidial $\omega$-3 fatty acid desaturase) and *SDP1* (triacylglycerol lipase) have well-established biochemical roles in Arabidopsis lipid metabolism, though the major determinant of seed C18:1 vs. C18:2 in *B. napus* is endoplasmic reticulum *FAD2* rather than plastidial *FAD8*.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- Neither the 50-accession whole-genome resequencing reads nor the 13-accession silique RNA-seq datasets have public repository accession numbers (e.g., NCBI SRA/BioProject) reported in the manuscript.
- On line 81 of the Discussion, an unedited placeholder/scrubbing artifact states *"We performed a preliminary screen of at least [YEAR] genes related to the fatty acid/lipid metabolic processes"* (likely a 4-digit number like 1800 or 2000 that matched a year regex during anonymization, though the surrounding sentence also says "(data not shown)").
