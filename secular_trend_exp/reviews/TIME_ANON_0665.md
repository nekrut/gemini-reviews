### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This manuscript re-analyzes a previously generated RNA-seq dataset of HepG2 cells transduced with an adenovirus overexpressing Genotype IV Swine Hepatitis E Virus (SHEV) ORF3 (`AD-ORF3` vs. `AD-GFP`, $n=3$ per group) to filter for differentially expressed lncRNAs annotated to the Gene Ontology term "viral process" (`GO: 0016032`). The authors highlight two lncRNAs (`AL137002` / `MSTRG.7478` and `AL049840` / `MSTRG.8427`), predict cis-target mRNAs within 100 kbp (`CUL4A` and `KLC1` transcripts), run `IntaRNA 2.0` to predict RNA-RNA base-pairing energies, and run the AlphaFold 3 web server to model hypothetical interactions. Forensically, this manuscript is severely compromised: it contains an **unedited LLM conversational meta-response and internal artifact UUID pasted verbatim into the Discussion section**, misuses AlphaFold 3 (a biomolecular *protein*–RNA structure predictor) while conflating RNA–mRNA base-pairing with RNA–protein complexes, applies unadjusted $p \le 0.05$ filtering without multiple-testing correction, and performs zero wet-lab validation (while promising EMSA, RIP-seq, and FISH in the future tense).
