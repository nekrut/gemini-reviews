### SUMMARY
### 1. Executive Summary
This classical immunogenetics and molecular cloning study identifies an autosomal dominant genetic locus, *Nka*, that controls in vivo Natural Killer (NK) cell-mediated alloreactivity in crosses between alloreactivity-competent PVG rats and alloreactivity-deficient DA rats. Using 53 F2 and 201 `(DA x PVG)F1 x DA` backcross rats, the authors demonstrate Mendelian 3:1 and 1:1 segregation of in vivo allorejection of $^{51}\text{Cr}$- and $^{111}\text{In}$-labeled AO and LEW thoracic duct lymphocytes (TDL). By RFLP linkage mapping (223 backcross rats across 40 restriction enzymes) and pulsed-field gel electrophoresis (PFGE), they map the rat Natural Killer Gene Complex (NKC) to rat chromosome 4 (~39.5 cM from *Igkc*, 7.1 cM from *Cd9*, 0.4 cM from *Kap*, and 9.5 cM from *Pthlh*), containing *Nkrp1*, *Ly49*, and a rat *NKG2D* homologue. They clone the first four rat *Ly-49* cDNAs (*rLy-49.9*, *rLy-49.12*, pseudogene *rLy-49.19*, and *rLy-49.29*) and show by Northern blotting of IL-2-activated NK cells from DA, PVG, F1, and MHC-congenic PVG.1AV1 strains that high *Ly-49* transcript expression correlates with the dominant *Nka* responder phenotype independently of host MHC haplotype.

### STRENGTHS
- **Rigorous Classical Linkage & Physical Mapping:** The backcross cohort ($n = 223$ genotyped, $n = 210$ phenotyped) and F2 cohort ($n = 53$) provide strong statistical power to establish single-locus dominant inheritance and fine-map *Nka* within the NKC using informative intra-NKC recombinants and PFGE long-range restriction fragments.
- **MHC-Congenic Control (`PVG.1AV1`):** Using the `PVG.1AV1` strain (which carries the non-responder DA MHC haplotype $RT1^{av1}$ on a responder PVG background) cleanly rules out MHC-driven repertoire calibration/shaping as the cause of low *Ly-49* expression in DA NK cells.
- **Dual-Isotope Co-Injection Control:** Simultaneous injection of $^{51}\text{Cr}$-labeled AO and $^{111}\text{In}$-labeled LEW lymphocytes in 19 backcross recipients demonstrates complete co-segregation of alloreactivity against two distinct MHC allotypes.

### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Post-Hoc Exclusion / Discounting of Discordant Recombinants:** In Figure 1B and Figure 5, three backcross rats were homozygous `DA/DA` across the surrounding chromosome 4 markers but displayed high alloreactivity (attributed to "double crossover, gene conversion, or false positive alloreactivity results" from extravasation during tail-vein injection). Furthermore, two critical recombinants between *Nka* and *Kap* (marked with asterisks in Fig. 5) gave conflicting gene order (`NKC - Nka - Kap` vs. `NKC - Kap - Nka`); the authors discount the conflicting high-reactivity rat post hoc by citing suspected tail-vein extravasation. Because extravasation of labeled donor TDL mimics allorejection (low lymph node uptake), injection quality should have been recorded and excluded prospectively prior to genotyping.
- **Ad-Hoc Linear Normalization of Alloreactivity Ratios:** In Figure 1, the cervical lymph node/kidney ($\text{cpm/mg}$ tissue) ratios across separate experiments were normalized by multiplying each experiment by $40 / (\text{median}_{\text{high}} + \text{median}_{\text{low}})$. While bimodal separation is clear, assuming a bimodal split in advance to compute the normalization factor can artificially sharpen the boundary between high and low responders.
- **cDNA Chimera/Splicing Reconstruction of `rLy-49.12`:** Neither the library clone (`rLy-49.12a`, which had frame-shifting internal deletions at nt 250–260 and 331–493) nor the PCR clone (`rLy-49.12b`, which had an internal deletion at nt 801–954) encoded a full-length open reading frame; the reported `rLy-49.12` sequence is a deduced composite consensus. Without isolating an intact full-length transcript or genomic exon structure, it remains possible that `rLy-49.12` primarily produces alternatively spliced or defective transcripts in F344 NK cells.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1 (NK alloreactivity in PVG x DA crosses is controlled by an autosomal dominant locus *Nka* within the rat NKC on Chromosome 4): `[Well Supported]`** — Strongly backed by Mendelian segregation in F2 ($43:10 \approx 3:1$) and backcross ($96:105 \approx 1:1$) cohorts and tight RFLP linkage ($198/201$ concordant in AO assays).
- **Claim 2 (Low *Ly-49* transcript expression in DA NK cells is independent of host MHC class I haplotype): `[Well Supported]`** — Directly demonstrated by the MHC-congenic `PVG.1AV1` Northern blot (Fig. 7).
- **Claim 3 (*Nka* encodes an activating *Ly-49* receptor or a transcriptional regulator of *Ly-49* family genes): `[Moderately Supported]`** — The authors appropriately note in the Discussion that bulk Northern blots of IL-2-activated LAK cells with cross-hybridizing probes cannot distinguish a cis-acting locus control region/regulator from an individual activating receptor, nor completely rule out a localized maturation defect in the Ly-49 subset.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- **Sequence & Accession Completeness:** GenBank accession numbers are provided for all newly cloned sequences (`U56863` for `rLy-49.9`, `U56822` for `rLy-49.12`, `U56823` for `rLy-49.19`, `U56824` for `rLy-49.29`, and `U56936` for `rNKR-P1B`), along with exact PCR primer sequences and hybridization stringency conditions.
