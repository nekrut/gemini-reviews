### 1. Executive Summary
This study profiles host microRNA (miRNA) expression in the spleen and lungs of 15-day-old specific-pathogen-free (SPF) embryonated chicken eggs (ECEs) inoculated with avian infectious bronchitis virus (IBV strain M41, $100\,\text{EID}_{50}$) versus PBS mock controls at 48 and 72 hours post-infection (hpi), using Ion Torrent small-RNA sequencing and follow-up RT-qPCR of five predicted target genes (*NFATC3*, *NFAT5*, *SPPL3*, *TGFB2*, and *JUN*). The authors report 17 upregulated miRNAs in the spleen and 7 (or 5/6; see inconsistencies below) in the lungs at 72 hpi, and report mRNA downregulation of *NFATC3*, *NFAT5*, *SPPL3*, and *TGFB2* in the spleen but not the lungs. However, the experimental design suffers from fatal statistical underpowering ($n = 2$ biological replicate embryos per group per time point), an invalid differential expression pipeline (applying Fisher's exact test to pooled/mean normalized read counts of duplicates), severe internal numerical contradictions across the Abstract, Results, and Figure legends, and a 24-hour post-harvest $4^\circ\text{C}$ cooling protocol that risks severe post-mortem RNA degradation.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
- **Critically Underpowered Sample Size ($n = 2$ per condition):** As stated in Materials and Methods (line 28), only eight fertilized eggs were used in the entire experiment: four M41-infected and four PBS-control eggs, split across two time points (48 hpi and 72 hpi). Thus, each treatment group at 48 hpi and 72 hpi consists of only **$n = 2$ embryos** (2 infected vs. 2 uninfected at 48 hpi; 2 infected vs. 2 uninfected at 72 hpi).
- **Invalid Non-Parametric Testing on $n = 2$ (Mann-Whitney Test Impossible to Reach $p < 0.05$):** In the Statistical Analysis section (line 63) and Figure 3 legend (line 105), the authors state that RT-qPCR target gene expression means between mock and IBV groups at 72 hpi were compared using *"multiple Mann-Whitney tests"*, and they plot `*` ($p < 0.05$) and `**` ($p < 0.01$) on Figure 3A! Mathematically, a two-sample Mann-Whitney U test (Wilcoxon rank-sum test) with $n_1 = 2$ and $n_2 = 2$ has only $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ possible rank permutations; the minimum achievable two-sided $p$-value is $2/6 = 0.333$ (and one-sided is $1/6 = 0.167$). Even if technical PCR duplicates ($2 \times 2 = 4$ pseudoreplicates) were illicitly treated as independent biological replicates, the minimum possible two-sided exact Mann-Whitney $p$-value for $n_1 = 4, n_2 = 4$ is $2/\binom{8}{4} = 2/70 = 0.0286$, which *still* cannot produce $p < 0.01$ (`**` in Figure 3A)! This proves that the authors either treated technical replicates/dilutions as independent $n$ or misapplied the statistical test in GraphPad Prism.
- **Flawed Small-RNA Differential Expression Pipeline:** Instead of using negative binomial generalized linear models that account for biological variance across replicates (such as DESeq2 or edgeR), the authors normalized read counts to total reads, averaged the two biological duplicates, and applied *Fisher's exact test* on the mean normalized counts (line 43). Applying Fisher's exact test (or Poisson contingency table testing) to read counts collapses biological variance into technical sampling variance, yielding massive false-positive rates in high-throughput sequencing. Note also the typographical error on line 43 stating `(p-value < 0.5)` and `(Q value < 0.5)` instead of $0.05$.
- **24-Hour Post-Mortem Cooling Prior to RNA Extraction:** At 48 and 72 hpi, embryos were *"transferred to $4^\circ\text{C}$ for 24 h prior to collection of the spleen and lungs"* (line 28). Leaving dead or dying chicken embryos at $4^\circ\text{C}$ for 24 hours before dissecting spleens and lungs introduces severe post-mortem autolysis and differential mRNA vs. miRNA decay (since 22-nt miRNAs are far more stable during post-mortem cold ischemia than full-length mRNAs like *NFATC3* or *TGFB2*), which could directly confound the observed mRNA downregulation in infected spleens.
- **Internal Numerical Contradictions:**
  1. **Number of DE miRNAs in spleen and lung:** The Abstract (line 7) states *"13 and six miRNAs were upregulated in the spleen and the lungs"*. The Results text (line 81) states *"seventeen DE miRNAs in the spleen (Fig. 2A) and seven DE miRNAs in the lung (Fig. 2B)"*. The Figure 2 legend (line 84) and Discussion (line 112) state *"Seventeen DE miRNAs were identified in the spleen (A). Five DE miRNAs were identified in the lungs (B)"*.
  2. **Loose BLAST matching of 1–4 nt mismatches:** Allowing up to 4 nucleotide mismatches out of ~22 nt ($\sim 18\%$ divergence) when BLASTing against miRBase (line 76) will cross-map distinct miRNA family paralogs (e.g., miR-200a/b/429 or miR-1a/1b/206) that differ by only 1–3 bases outside or inside the seed region.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *IBV M41 infection upregulates specific host miRNA clusters in the embryonic spleen and lungs at 72 hpi.* `[Fragile]` — Derived from $n = 2$ embryos per group after 24-hour $4^\circ\text{C}$ post-mortem storage, analyzed via Fisher's exact test on averaged read counts without biological dispersion modeling.
- **Claim 2:** *Upregulated miRNAs functionally downregulate NFATC3, NFAT5, SPPL3, and TGFB2 mRNA expression in the spleen ($p < 0.05$ and $p < 0.01$).* `[Methodologically Flawed]` — Statistical significance ($p < 0.01$ by Mann-Whitney test) is mathematically impossible with $n = 2$ biological replicates, and no direct miRNA-mRNA reporter assay or Ago2-RIP was performed to establish causality over non-specific viral or post-mortem transcriptional shutoff.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
- RT-qPCR primer sequences are fully listed in the text (line 58), though `NFAT3C_FW1` is a typo for `NFATC3`.
- Table 1 ("DE miRNAs in spleen and lung, the corresponding target gene, and gene functions") is completely empty in the manuscript text (line 92).
- No raw sequencing data accession number (e.g., NCBI SRA, GEO, or ENA) is provided in the manuscript or the empty Associated Data section (line 166).
