### 1. Executive Summary
This hybrid bioinformatic and functional plant molecular biology study identifies 29 cyclin-dependent kinase family genes (15 *CDKs* and 14 *CDKLs* across 8 subfamilies, CDKA–CDKG and CDKL) in the model legume *Medicago truncatula* and investigates their role in the mitosis-to-endoreduplication switch required for symbiotic nitrogen fixation in indeterminate root nodules. Integrating public transcriptomic atlases (MtExpress and Symbimics) with RT-qPCR and promoter-*GUS* fusions in *Agrobacterium rhizogenes* composite plants inoculated with *Sinorhizobium*, the authors show that *CDKB1;1* and *CDKB2;2* are strongly expressed in mitotically active root tips, nodule primordia, and the nodule apical meristem (Zone I) but sharply downregulated in the infection and fixation zones (Zones II–III) where host cells undergo endoreduplication up to 64C, whereas *CDKL13* is root-specific and absent throughout nodules. Ectopic overexpression of *CDKB1;1* or *CDKB2;2* driven by the nodule-specific *DNF1* promoter (*pDNF1*) significantly reduces high-ploidy (16C–64C) nuclei via DAPI flow cytometry, downregulates endoreduplication markers (*CCS52A*, *Topo VIA*, *APC2*), increases white non-fixing nodules, and impairs rhizobial *pnifH::GUS* activity, whereas *pDNF1::CDKL13* overexpression has minimal impact.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit
* **Targeted Zone-Specific Ectopic Expression Design:** Driving *CDKB1;1* and *CDKB2;2* under the infection/differentiation-zone-specific *DNF1* promoter (*pDNF1*) rather than a constitutive *35S* promoter is a well-conceived experimental strategy: it avoids pleiotropic arrest of root growth or initial nodule meristem formation while directly testing whether preventing the natural downregulation of mitotic B-type CDKs in the infection/fixation zones blocks the 8C-to-16C endoreduplication switch.
* **Control Definition in Composite Hairy Root Assays:** In Figure 6 (legend line 146), the authors state: "dsRed served as a transformation marker. Control represents negative roots from the same seedlings as the positive roots." Using untransformed (dsRed-negative) roots from the same chimeric seedling rather than roots transformed with an empty *pDNF1::GUS/empty-pKGW-RR* vector does not control for the physiological effects of T-DNA integration or high dsRed protein accumulation in transgenic nodules, although the parallel *pDNF1::CDKL13* dsRed-positive construct serves as an effective internal negative control showing minimal phenotype.
* **Genomic vs. Coding Sequence Ambiguity in Overexpression Constructs:** Under "Molecular cloning" (line 43), the Methods state: "For constructing *pDNF1-CDKB1;1/CDKB2;2/CDKL13*-pKGW-RR, we amplified the full-length genomic/coding sequences of these three genes (3267 bp, 1743 bp, and 2913 bp, respectively)." Since the encoded proteins are ~300–350 amino acids (~900–1,050 bp CDS), fragments of 3,267 bp and 2,913 bp are full-length intron-containing genomic sequences rather than cDNAs; the authors should clarify whether genomic DNA was used for all three constructs.
* **Statistical Reporting and Multiple Comparisons:** Pairwise Student's $t$-tests are applied across multiple ploidy classes (2C, 4C, 8C, 16C, 32C, 64C in Fig. 7C–F) and multiple marker genes without multiple-testing correction, and the exact number of independent transgenic plants/nodules ($n$) scored for the pie charts in Fig. 6C,F and flow cytometry in Fig. 7 is omitted from the main text and figure legends.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
* **Spatial restriction of *CDKB1;1* and *CDKB2;2* to the nodule meristem and downregulation in endoreduplicating zones:** `[Well Supported]` — Public laser-capture microdissection RNA-seq data (Symbimics), RT-qPCR (roots vs. 21 dpi nodules), and *pCDKB1;1::GUS* / *pCDKB2;2::GUS* histochemical staining all show strong meristematic activity that vanishes in the infection and fixation zones.
* **Sustained expression of *CDKB1;1* or *CDKB2;2* in the infection zone blocks 8C-to-16C endoreduplication and impairs nitrogen fixation:** `[Well Supported]` — Flow cytometry of *pDNF1::CDKB1;1* and *pDNF1::CDKB2;2* nodules demonstrates accumulation of 2C–8C nuclei and depletion of 16C–64C nuclei, accompanied by reduced *CCS52A*/*Topo VIA*/*APC2* expression and loss of leghemoglobin and *nifH::GUS* expression (52.3% and 40.2% white/GUS-negative nodules, respectively).
* **Predicted STRING PPI interactions for *CDKL13*:** `[Moderately Supported]` — While the structural modeling and STRING network predictions for *CDKB1;1* and *CDKB2;2* match canonical plant cell-cycle biology, the *in silico* PPI network for *CDKL13* (CycT1, Pol II CTD, SPT4, DDB1) is purely computational and appropriately treated as secondary since *CDKL13* is not expressed in nodules.

### 4. Reproducibility & Data Completeness
* **Bioinformatic and Experimental Documentation:** Database sources (MtExpress, Symbimics), reference gene ID (`MtPDF2`, `MtrunA17_Chr6g0484701`), bacterial strains (*S. meliloti* Rm1021 *nifH::GUS*, *S. medicae* ABS7 *hemA::LacZ*, *A. rhizogenes* ARqua1), and flow cytometry buffer composition (Galbraith buffer) are clearly specified, with gene IDs and primer sequences referenced in Supporting Tables S1–S4.
* **Missing Sample Counts in Figure Legends:** Exact counts of transgenic roots and nodules examined in the phenotypic pie charts (Fig. 6C,F) and nuclei gated per biological replicate in flow cytometry (Fig. 7A,B) should be explicitly stated in the figure legends.
