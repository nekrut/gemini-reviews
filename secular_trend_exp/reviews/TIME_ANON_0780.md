### 1. Executive Summary / SUMMARY
This molecular and developmental biology study investigates the cell-type and tissue specificity of the human fibroblast growth factor 2 (`FGF-2`) internal ribosome entry site (IRES, nucleotides 1–539 of the `FGF-2` 5' leader) ex vivo across 14 mammalian cell lines and in vivo in transgenic mice carrying a `CMV`-driven bicistronic *Renilla*/firefly luciferase transgene (`CMV-LucR-FGF2IRES-LucF`, lines `RFL12` [28 copies] and `RFLD` [1 copy]) compared against transgenic mice carrying an encephalomyocarditis virus (`EMCV`) IRES control (`CMV-LucR-EMCVIRES-LucF`, lines `RELA` and `RELB` [12–15 copies]). The authors find that while `FGF-2` IRES is broadly active across cultured cell lines (highest in `p53-/-` `Saos-2` osteosarcoma and `SK-N-BE`/`SK-N-AS` neuroblastoma cells), its in vivo activity in transgenic mice is developmentally regulated—high in E11 whole embryos and E16 heart and brain (but absent in placenta and liver)—and becomes strikingly restricted in adults, where brain exhibits up to 400-fold higher `LucF/LucR` activity than stomach or other somatic organs, in sharp contrast to the ubiquitous activity of the viral `EMCV` IRES. While the parallel comparison of two independent transgenic founder lines per construct against `EMCV` IRES is a major strength, the RNA integrity control (`Figure 5`, separate 25-cycle RT-PCRs of internal `LucR` and `LucF` amplicons) is insufficient to rigorously exclude tissue-specific cryptic splicing or cryptic promoter initiation within the 539-nt `FGF-2` 5' leader in adult brain.

### 2. Methodological & Statistical Audit / STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES

#### STRENGTHS
- **In Vivo Transgenic Comparison Against Viral `EMCV` IRES Across Multiple Founder Lines:** Establishing two independent transgenic mouse lines for `FGF-2` IRES (`RFL12` with 28 copies and `RFLD` with a single copy) and two independent lines for `EMCV` IRES (`RELA` and `RELB`) controls for chromosomal integration-site position effects and demonstrates that the dramatic adult brain enrichment of `LucF/LucR` is specific to the 539-nt `FGF-2` 5' leader rather than the `CMV` promoter or luciferase protein stability.
- **Broad Cross-Species and Cross-Lineage Ex Vivo Screen:** Calibrating `pCRFL` (`FGF-2` IRES) against `pCRHL` (intercistronic hairpin negative control) across 14 primary and transformed mammalian cell lines cleanly shows higher cap-independent translation in neuroblastoma and `p53-/-` osteosarcoma lines relative to non-transformed cells.

#### WEAKNESSES & METHODOLOGICAL AUDIT
- **Insufficient Exclusion of Cryptic Splicing or Cryptic Promoter Activity in the DNA Bicistronic Assay (`Figure 5`):** A well-known pitfall of DNA-transfected or transgenic `CMV-LucR-IRES-LucF` bicistronic cassettes containing long GC-rich cellular 5' leaders (539 nt in `pCRFL`) is that tissue-specific cryptic promoter activity inside the 539-nt sequence or a cryptic 3' splice acceptor site upstream of `LucF` can generate capped monocistronic `LucF` mRNAs that are translated with high efficiency by standard cap-dependent ribosome scanning. To rule this out, the authors performed separate 25-cycle RT-PCR amplifications of internal fragments of `LucR` and `LucF` (`Figure 5`). Because `LucR` and `LucF` were amplified separately with internal primer pairs rather than across the full `LucR–IRES–LucF` intercistronic junction (and without Northern blotting, RNase protection, or a promoterless `LucR-FGF2-LucF` control), a spliced or internally initiated monocistronic `LucF` transcript accounting for even 5–10% of `LucF` RNA in brain could produce the extreme `LucF/LucR` luminescence ratios (`191–454`) without changing the apparent ethidium-bromide band ratio of separate 25-cycle PCR products.
- **Omission of Sample Sizes ($n$) and Statistical Hypothesis Testing in In Vivo Organ Profiling:** Neither the Materials and Methods nor the Results specify how many embryos or adult mice ($n$) were assayed per organ and transgenic line, nor are any statistical tests ($p$-values) reported for the cell-line or organ comparisons.

### 3. Biological / Experimental Grounding & Claim Proportionality
- **Claim 1:** *The 539-nt human `FGF-2` 5' leader drives strong embryo- and adult-brain-enriched `LucF/LucR` expression in transgenic mice, whereas the picornaviral `EMCV` IRES drives quasi-ubiquitous expression across adult organs.* `[Well Supported]` — Replicated across independent 1-copy (`RFLD`) and 28-copy (`RFL12`) transgenic lines vs `RELA`/`RELB`.
- **Claim 2:** *The high `LucF/LucR` ratio in adult transgenic mouse brain is exclusively mediated by internal ribosome entry on intact bicistronic mRNAs.* `[Moderately Supported]` — Strongly suggestive given the neuroblastoma cell-line concordance and known brain HMW FGF-2 expression, but separate internal RT-PCR amplicons (`Figure 5`) do not definitively exclude a brain-specific cryptic splice isoform or internal promoter transcript.
